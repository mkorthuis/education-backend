# Money and Governance From Lee's Side of the Oyster River Cooperative

Part of the Lee research (town/Lee). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered.** Lee's side of the Oyster River Cooperative School District's money and governance, building on `town/Durham/money-and-governance.md` (cited by topic and fact number, not repeated): how fast Lee's school taxes have grown against Durham's and Madbury's, and Lee's rising share of students and taxes; the district's projected and actual school tax rates for each town since 2013; the FY27 estimate for a Lee home; Lee's 2021 revaluation; how Question 2 was counted across the three towns; the district's yearly budget increases since FY15 and its inflation-adjusted cost per pupil; what the "$1.3 million" in the FY27 budget was (the amount above the default budget) and where it went; the finance committee's 4.5–5% starting target for FY28; Mast Way's age, condition and ten-year repair plan; the high school gym and fields in the 2024 assessment, with a derived estimate of Lee's share of a turf bond; what the 2026 superintendent posting asks for; Lee's seat on the school board; the middle school's state test results before and after its 2022 building; and where Lee voters find plain-language explanations of the town and school ballots.

**Counts.** 7 topics (all shared local topics), 45 facts, 2 number tables.

**Main sources.** ORCSD's FY26 public budget hearing slides (January 15, 2025), its 2025 budget trifold, the 2026 mandatory report graphs, the internal superintendent posting (October 23, 2026), the November 12, 2026 finance committee minutes, the December 2024 facilities assessment (Mast Way and high school sections), the December 2022 workshop with state legislators, and the budget videos and town information pages; the Town of Lee's March 2025 Voter's Information Guide; and earlier raw copies in `town/Durham/raw/` (FY27 hearing and deliberative session slides, budget FAQ, 2026 annual report, board minutes). Derived figures use the site's NH DRA, NH DOE and enrollment data.

**Main gaps.** The Town of Lee's website refused automated requests (a bot check), so Lee's 2026 tax rate, its 2026 voter guide and town election results, and its annual report were not retrieved; NH DRA's and NH DOE's apportionment pages were blocked for the Durham research and were not retried. Oyster River's articles of agreement and the current apportionment formula weights were still not found. The board had not chosen a superintendent or adopted its FY28 budget as of November 21, 2026. The district posts only combined three-town vote totals, so how Lee alone voted on the March 2026 school articles is not known from official sources.
