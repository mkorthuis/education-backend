# Lee Residents' Questions

The questions the Lee research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Lee's students attend the Oyster River Cooperative School District (SAU 5), shared with Durham and Madbury. Lee's elementary children attend Mast Way School (K–4) in Lee, or Moharimet School in Madbury; grades 5–8 attend Oyster River Middle School and grades 9–12 Oyster River High School, both in Durham. Oyster River is researched in `../Durham/`; the files here add Lee's side. Several questions contain premises (the tax cap not passing, a superintendent search, a $1.3 million budget increase, competency-based grading at the high school, Barrington's tuition students crowding the high school, the Mast Way building's age, a late bus at 4:30, Lee paying a different share) that the research should check rather than assume.
