# Canaan Elementary School and Families

Part of the Canaan research (town/Canaan). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Canaan Elementary School (CES, preschool to grade 4, 31 School Street) and what Canaan families with young children ask about it: the earliest drop-off, the after-school program and early-release days, where to see what a class is learning, the half-day preschool, the kindergarten cutoff, speech and other evaluations before enrollment, class sizes and reading scores, enrichment for advanced readers, Title I, translation, the buses on dirt roads, guardianship papers, summer programs, the school's communication channels, snow-day calls, and how Canaan Elementary compares with Enfield Village School.

- **14 topics, 55 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 20 MB): the CES 2025-26 handbook (PDF and text); CES website pages (home, daily schedule, calendar, handbook, Title I, music, library, technology links, staff list, news, principal); the district's Curriculum, Student Support Services, Special Education, 504, Title I, Community, Enrollment and policy pages; the 2026-27 district calendar (PDF and a text copy that scrambles the dates); grade 2 report card rubric and "I Can" standards; the "Progress Reporting Information" page; the 2026-27 preschool and kindergarten registration flyer; the Title I family compact; the special education Child Find and Section 504 notices; the "Summer resources" flyer; policies EBCD, EEA, EEAB, EEAC, EG, IHBAB, IHBB, IHBBA, JEB-R, JFAA, JLP, JRA and KB (PDF and text); the district's November 2026 update and curriculum newsletters and CES's November 2026 newsletter; the 2026-27 MS-27 budget form and the FY28 superintendent's proposed budget; excerpts of the 2026 annual report and of the October 13, 2026 board minutes; the October 1, 2026 enrollment count and a scanned proficiency chart with a transcription; sanitized text copies of the five 2026-27 bus route sheets that serve CES (house-number stops reduced to street names, drivers' names removed; the route PDFs were not saved); Canaan town pages (Recreation Commission with its 2022-23 after-school flyer, Highway Department, library pages); the Mascoma Valley Parks & Recreation 2017-18 brochure; federal Civil Rights Data Collection rows for CES (gifted and talented program; staffing); a saved set of read-only queries of the site's database; and OpenStreetMap and OSRM lookups for school and child care centers.
