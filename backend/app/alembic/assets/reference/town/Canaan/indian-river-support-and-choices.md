# Indian River School: Buses, Meals, Sports, Support and Choices for Canaan Families

Part of the Canaan research (town/Canaan). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Canaan families asked about Indian River School (IRS, grades 5–8, 45 Royal Road in Canaan, beside Mascoma Valley Regional High School at 27 Royal Road) and the support and choices around it: the bus for a 6th and a 9th grader, free lunch and negative meal balances, the school's culture and the "School for girls" idea, free and reduced-price meal applications and sports physicals, homeschooled and EFA students in sports and band, sports practices with no late bus, athletic fees, concussions, school counselors, psychologists and the social worker, reading help and out-of-district programs, 5th grade class size, who checks the curriculum and what parents can say, building safety at IRS and Canaan Elementary, whether the schools are "good enough", and Cardigan Mountain School's financial aid. Enfield's file (`town/Enfield/indian-river-support-and-choices.md`) already covers IRS in depth, so this file cites it by topic and fact number and writes only what Canaan's questions add.

- **15 topics, 49 facts, 3 tables.** All 15 topics are shared slugs from `shared-topics.md`: `district-transportation`, `district-meals`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-student-support`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-parent-rights-and-materials`, `district-goals-and-performance-reporting`, `district-safety-and-security`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-fees`, `district-homeschool-access`, `town-family-help`, `town-school-options-nearby` and `district-school-results-compared`. The tables are `canaan_middle_high_bus_runs_2026_27`, `canaan_school_support_staff_2023_24` and `canaan_proficiency_compared_2026`. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 14 MB): five sanitized bus sheets, 20 board policies, the IRS handbook and athletic handbook, the Canaan Elementary handbook, reports and minutes from the 2026 board packets, excerpts of the 2026 annual report, CRDC rows, Cardigan's and the health center's pages, GreatSchools profiles and the site-database queries.
- **Places used:** `district Mascoma Valley Regional` for the schools, policies and budget; `town Canaan` for the health center and Cardigan Mountain School. No new state rules were written; state rules are cited from the library and earlier towns by file, topic and fact number.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Buses:** the five runs that serve Canaan (M4, M5, M8, M-7/9 and the M10 van) are each headed CES/IRS/HS, so a 6th grader and a 9th grader at the same stop ride the same bus at the same time. The buses reach the IRS/HS stop at 7:45 to 7:55 a.m. (rides of 45 to 65 minutes from the first stop) and leave it together at 3:05 p.m.; no later run was found.
  - **Meals:** under policy EFAA no student is refused a school breakfast or lunch because of a low balance; families get weekly balance notices, a letter at $15 and a meeting at $30. A household may apply for free or reduced-price meals at any time, using current income if hours were cut.
  - **"School for girls":** IRS is a public, coeducational school (138 boys and 153 girls in 2023-24). The boys' school in Canaan is Cardigan Mountain School (grades 6–9).
  - **Sports:** no late or activity bus exists; practices are limited to 2 hours but no end times are posted. The athletic participation fee is in the district's policy (with a waiver) but no amount is posted anywhere read. The concussion rules (policy JLCJ and the handbook) require immediate removal, written clearance and a parent's written permission.
  - **Support staff:** IRS has its own school counselor (and two family outreach counselors) and a share of a district social worker; the 2023-24 federal data show no school psychologist at IRS and 0.5 at each of the other three schools.
  - **5th grade:** 72 students in 4 classrooms on the first day of 2026-27 (average 18). The handbook doesn't say whether students move between the four teachers.
  - **Building safety:** IRS's front entrance is the only door open to visitors, and the handbook says all other doors are "locked or secured"; the handbook describes a school resource officer and the budget has a contracted officer line for IRS. Canaan Elementary's handbook says only that visitors sign in and wear a badge.
  - **Good enough?** In 2026, 54% of Canaan Elementary's tested students were proficient in math and 50% in reading; at IRS, 39% and 47%. IRS's 2025 annual report mentions "Focus School requirements"; IRS students with IEPs were 10% proficient in math and 15% in reading.
  - **Cardigan:** day tuition is $48,900 for 2026-27 and aid is need-based: "over $3 million" to "more than 30%" of students, but not every qualifying family. Cardigan publishes nothing about day students or Canaan families.
- **Main gaps:** the athletic fee amount; practice end times; any free or low-cost sports physical program; whether paraprofessional and psychologist time is full at IRS; whether students rotate among 5th grade teachers; Canaan Elementary's door and officer arrangements; NH DOE's school designation list (HTTP 403); and Cardigan's aid for day families.
- **Proposed shared topic:** none.
