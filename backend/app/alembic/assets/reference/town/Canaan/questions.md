# Canaan Residents' Questions

The questions the Canaan research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Canaan's students attend the Mascoma Valley Regional School District (SAU 62), a cooperative of five towns that runs Canaan Elementary School and Enfield Village School (preschool to grade 4), Indian River School in Canaan (grades 5–8) and Mascoma Valley Regional High School in Canaan, with the Hartford Area Career and Technical Center across the river in Vermont. Several questions contain premises (an Indian River School "for girls", a $21.5 million high school renovation bond, a five-town district, the SAU 62 office in Enfield, Butler buses, three apps for school news) that the research should check rather than assume.
