# Canaan: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Canaan, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the third ToLoad batch. Canaan is in the Mascoma Valley Regional School District (SAU 62), a five-town cooperative (Canaan, Dorchester, Enfield, Grafton and Orange) with its office in Enfield, which runs:
- **Canaan Elementary School** and **Enfield Village School** (preschool to grade 4);
- **Indian River School** (grades 5–8) and **Mascoma Valley Regional High School**, side by side on Royal Road in Canaan;
- the Hartford Area Career and Technical Center in Vermont, which about 24% of Mascoma juniors and seniors attended in 2024-25.

Enfield's research (Enfield Village School, Indian River, Mascoma High and the district's money) was already done; these files cite it and write Canaan's side.

## Premises checked

| Question says | What the sources show |
|---|---|
| Indian River "School for girls" | Wrong: it is coeducational (138 boys, 153 girls); the boys' school in Canaan is Cardigan |
| Early drop-off for a 7 a.m. shift | None before 7:30 |
| After-school care "every other Wednesday" | Wrong: the 2026-27 calendar has 6 early dismissals (5 Wednesdays and 1 Thursday) and none on a Friday |
| A full-day preschool | Half-day, 2 or 3 days a week |
| A 6th and 9th grader on the same bus | Yes: the same bus and pickup time |
| A late bus for sports | None for any town |
| Butler buses down dirt roads | Butler's Bus is the contractor; policy EEAC covers closed roads; no road-by-road list |
| Three apps (PlusPortals, email, Facebook) | PlusPortals appears on a district page ("Plus Portals"); Alma is the current system; CES has a Facebook page and a monthly newsletter |
| Locked entryways and an officer | Indian River's handbook says visitors use the front door and other doors are locked, and budgets a contracted officer; Canaan Elementary's handbook mentions only sign-in; federal data shows 1 law enforcement officer at CES in 2023-24 |
| Only a third proficient in math at Mascoma | About right for 2025 (33%); 25% in 2026 (state 31% and 30%) |
| Electrical or welding at a career center during the school day | Welding yes (the Hartford center); no day-school electrical program, only an adult evening course |
| Credit recovery after school | Not stated in any source |
| An hour on the bus from the Dorchester line | Confirmed: route M-8 is about 65 minutes in the morning and 67 in the afternoon (derived); classes start at 8:00 |

| A $21.5 million bond "paid off yet" | No: approved March 11, 2014 and opened in 2016; level payments of $1,358,455 a year; the last year wasn't found |
| A four-hour deliberative session | The January 31, 2026 session ran 1 hour 52 minutes (derived) |
| Lake towns get a tax break | No separate break found |
| EFAs taking money from Canaan's schools | Not shown: no source says how much of the aid change comes from EFAs |
