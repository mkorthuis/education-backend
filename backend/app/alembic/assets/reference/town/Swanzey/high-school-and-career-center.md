# Monadnock Regional High School, the Cheshire Career Center, College Courses and Adult Education

Part of the Swanzey research (town/Swanzey). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Monadnock Regional High School (MRHS, grades 9–12, in the Monadnock Regional Middle High School building in Swanzey Center) and its side of the Cheshire Career Center (CCC) at Keene High School: course levels and AP courses, college-credit courses and the district's payment for college courses, graduation requirements and course loads, summer school and other ways to make up credit, the school's own trade courses (including welding, which the CCC doesn't teach), how CCC students fit into the MRHS day, the CCC's 2027-28 enrollment form (deadline, lottery and each SAU's share of seats), work habits ("Husky Habits") and workplace skills grading, counseling and college and scholarship help, the school's own follow-up of graduates and SAT averages, ApprenticeshipNH's high school program, and adult education and HVAC training for adults. The CCC's programs, credentials and college credit are in Keene's file (`town/Keene/middle-high-and-career-center.md`); this file cites it rather than repeating it.

- **7 fact topics, 42 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 25 MB): the 2026-27 MRMHS Program of Studies (PDF, 53 pages, with the Husky Habits rubric images transcribed), the 2026-27 MRMHS Family/Student Handbook (PDF, 78 pages), the MRMHS school profile (two images, transcribed), the MRMHS guidance, scholarship, terminology, ELO, program of studies and about pages, the 2026-27 MRMHS bus routes, the district's 2026 annual report (with the DRA's FY2026 appropriations), the CCC home page, its 2027-28 CTE enrollment form and its 2026-27 Program of Studies (Google Doc as text), ApprenticeshipNH's western hub page, CCSNH's Early College contacts page, River Valley's site search for "hvac", and Manchester Community College's three HVAC program pages.
- **Places:** 36 facts use `district Monadnock Regional`, 3 use `district Keene` (CCC facts Keene's files lack) and 3 use `town Swanzey` (adult training options).
- **New data concepts:** the CCC's seat shares by SAU (SAU 93 has 20%) and its March 31 application deadline and lottery for 2027-28; which CCC first-year courses are still open to a student starting in grade 12; how long CCC students are away from MRHS each day; the district's $300-a-year payment toward college courses; the school's own 11-year graduate follow-up series and junior SAT averages; MRHS's AP list against Keene High's; Husky Habits scores; the district's $0 adult education appropriation; HVAC programs in the community college system.
