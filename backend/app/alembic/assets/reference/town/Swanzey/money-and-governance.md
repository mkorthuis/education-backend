# Swanzey: Money, Taxes and Governance of the Monadnock Regional School District

Part of the Swanzey research (town/Swanzey). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 6 topics, 79 facts and 7 number tables on how the Monadnock Regional School District (SAU 93) is governed and paid for, and what that means for Swanzey taxpayers: the sale of the former Cutler School and the talk of a new SAU office building; the six-town cost-sharing formula, its history and what each town paid for 2025-26; the 2026-27 (FY2027) budget, its drivers and default budget, and six years of budget votes; Question 2's tax cap and the cuts the administration expects for 2027-28; support staff pay against Keene, Jaffrey-Rindge and other districts; the school board's seats, weighted votes and budget committee; how to follow meetings from home and the 2027 voting calendar; Swanzey's 2026 tax rate, exemptions and home sales.

- **Cutler:** grades 3–6 moved to Mount Caesar (MTC) for 2026-27. The board is selling the building: appraisals of $1.9 million (February 2026) and $1.2 million (August and October 2026), a probate court (cy pres) petition approved September 15, 2026 because the land is in a charitable trust, and the charter school right of first refusal. The only interest reported so far is "at the town level" in Swanzey. Voters will be asked in March 2027 what to do with the money; the administration wants a new, accessible SAU building; the Finance & Facilities Committee recommended investing it or an SAU project, not returning it to taxpayers. Keeping Cutler costs about $40,000 a year until it sells.
- **The formula:** operating costs are split 50% by pupils (average daily membership) and 50% by equalized property value, capital costs 100% by value. Voters switched to 25/75 in 2013 and back to 50/50 in 2019. For 2025-26 Swanzey had 54.4% of the pupils and 48.7% of the value, so it paid 51.5% of operating costs; its local school tax came to $12,649 per pupil, against $25,542 in Fitzwilliam and $9,055 in Troy (**derived**).
- **Budget and taxes:** the FY2027 budget of $40,874,456 (+3.36%) passed 1,560–477, $232,056 below the default. In 2025 voters rejected a budget cut at the deliberative session to $36.0 million, so the $38.8 million default applied. Swanzey's 2026 rate, set November 10, 2026, is $21.11: local school $10.63 (down from $11.03), state school $1.12, town $6.91, county $2.45.
- **The tax cap:** the cap passed district-wide (61.2%, site data, unofficial) though Swanzey alone voted 57.0%. On November 10, 2026 the superintendent said cost drivers of about $1.5 million meet an allowed increase of $884,572, and the administration will look for $600,000–$800,000 in cuts; no list was public by November 21.

**Main sources:** MRSD's 2026 budget and voting documents (MS-27, MS-DSB, plain-language warrant, deliberative presentation and minutes, official results), the 2025 and 2026 annual reports (with NH DOE's FY2025 and FY2026 cooperative apportionments), school board, budget committee and Finance & Facilities Committee minutes (May 2025 to November 2026), the 2026–2029 MESSA contract, the 2013 green sheet and the 2013, 2018, 2019 and 2021–2025 ballots and results, the NH Supreme Court's 2015 Appeal of Town of Troy order, RSA 195:7 and 194:61, the Town of Swanzey's tax collector, assessing and Selectboard pages, NH REALTORS® October 2026 market updates, and the site's database (Question 2, tax rates, enrollment).
