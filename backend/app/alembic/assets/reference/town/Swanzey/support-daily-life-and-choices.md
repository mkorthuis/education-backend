# Support, Daily Life and Choices at Monadnock Regional Middle and High School

Part of the Swanzey research (town/Swanzey). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

This file covers support, daily life and choices for Swanzey's students at Monadnock Regional Middle High School (MRMHS, grades 7–12, in Swanzey Center), with the district-wide special education and homeschool pieces the questions raise: who to call for extra help in 7th grade, counseling, mental health referrals and returning after a crisis, speech-language staff and contracted services, 1:1 paraprofessionals and the new support staff contract, help with IEP disagreements, homeschooled and EFA students on MRMHS teams, what home education and EFA families must file, daytime homeschool classes and co-ops near Swanzey and Keene, private school tuition help, hall passes and the hall monitor proposal, the 7:30 a.m. bell and its history, vaping and bullying rules, the phone policy, sports, middle school teams and cooperative teams, athletic trainers, team buses and fees, clubs and music, and foster children's school stability. Facts about the district use `district Monadnock Regional`; Keene programs use `town Keene`; Swanzey's EFA count and its co-ops use `town Swanzey`.

**Counts:** 13 topics, 75 facts, 1 number table (`mrsd_iep_students_out_of_district_and_charter`). Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (31 MB, most of it the district's 2026 annual report).

**Main sources:** the MRMHS 2026-27 Family/Student Handbook and Student-Athlete Handbook, the 2026-27 Program of Studies, MRSD's 2026 annual report (superintendent, student services, MRMHS principal and nutrition reports, and DRA's 2025-26 appropriations), the district's 2025–2027 Suicide Prevention Plan, the 2026–2029 MESSA (support staff) contract redline, the 2025 and 2026 election results and the February 2026 deliberative session, MRSD board minutes from December 2017, September 2018 and July 2024 to March 2025, the district's job site, Cheshire Medical Center's sports medicine pages, NH DOE's 2024-25 foster care contact list (saved by the Concord research), the Keene Family YMCA's fall 2026 program guide, Trinity Christian School's Homeschool Connect page, Granite State Home Educators' enrichment directory, and an NHPR republication of a Keene Sentinel story.
