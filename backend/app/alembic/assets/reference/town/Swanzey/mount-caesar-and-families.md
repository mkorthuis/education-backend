# Mount Caesar Elementary and Young Families

Part of the Swanzey research (town/Swanzey). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Mount Caesar Elementary School (Mt. Caesar, "MTC"), the Monadnock Regional School District's preschool-to-grade-6 school in Swanzey, for the young-family questions: Cutler Elementary's closing and the move of grades 3–6 to Mount Caesar, enrollment and class sizes, the addition and its safety upgrades, bus routes and ride times from West Swanzey, school hours and the 2026-27 calendar, storm notices, the free Beyond the Bell after-school and summer program, middle school late buses, the preschool, kindergarten entry, early entrance and registration papers, meals and the meal application, Spanish-language materials and English learner services, counselors, guardianship papers, teacher prep time, sixth grade and homework, and summer camps in Swanzey and Keene.

**Counts:** 13 topics, 64 facts, 1 number table (`swanzey_mtc_bus_ride_minutes_2026_27`).

**Main sources:** the district's 2026-2027 Elementary Parent and Student Guide (with its board-approved 2026-27 calendar, transcribed from image); the 2026-27 MRMHS Family/Student Handbook; the 2026-2027 elementary bus routes (as of August 13, 2026); school board minutes from March 2025 to November 10, 2026; the March 2023 warrant articles and bond hearing minutes; Mount Caesar's FY26 Title I schoolwide plan; the 2026-27 meal letter (scanned; transcribed) and applications in English and Spanish; policy EFAA; the 2025–2028 teachers' contract (MDEA); Beyond the Bell's 2022 21st Century grant application and 2024 summer handbook; district ESOL, Title I and staff directory pages; Swanzey Recreation's 2026 summer camp handbook and School's Out page; Keene Parks and Recreation's 2026 camp flyer; the Keene YMCA's summer camp page.

**Main gaps:** no 2026-27 class-size figures for Mount Caesar (the board asked for a "classroom makeup" report on September 15, 2026, but the minutes give only school totals); no morning arrival or drop-off time for students is posted, and no before-school program exists; the preschool tuition amount is not posted; kindergarten registration dates for 2027-28 are not posted; no source says whether Beyond the Bell runs on delay or early-release days; no source describes text alerts from First Student or the district; no source says whether report cards, storm alerts or permission slips are offered in Spanish, or how often the ESOL teacher is at Mount Caesar; the project site with the renovation details (mrsdproject.com) returned an error, so heating, ventilation and air-quality upgrades could not be confirmed; Swanzey Lake Summer Camp fees are not in its handbook.

**Library gaps filled:** the library's district-practices file lists Monadnock's kindergarten day length and registration as not found and has only the September 30 cutoff (kindergarten-entry-cutoff fact 67) and a thin preschool fact (district-preschool fact 73). This file adds the full-day kindergarten, the October 1–November 1 early-entrance rule, registration papers and the Mount Caesar preschool's tuition and makeup (kindergarten-day-length, kindergarten-entry-cutoff, kindergarten-registration, district-preschool below).

**No new topics proposed.** All facts use shared or library slugs.
