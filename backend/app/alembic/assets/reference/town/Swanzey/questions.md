# Swanzey Residents' Questions

The questions the Swanzey research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Swanzey's students attend the Monadnock Regional School District (SAU 93), shared with Fitzwilliam, Gilsum, Richmond, Roxbury and Troy. In Swanzey it runs Mount Caesar Elementary School (preschool to grade 6), since Cutler Elementary closed, and Monadnock Regional Middle School (grades 7–8) and High School (grades 9–12), which share a building. Students may take career programs at the Cheshire Career Center at Keene High School. Several questions contain premises (Cutler closed and West Swanzey's children moved to Mount Caesar, renovations at Mount Caesar, contract staff replacing district hires, locked bathrooms, very early middle and high school start times, a six-town cost formula, a default budget rising with enrollment) that the research should check rather than assume.
