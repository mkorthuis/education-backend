# Stevens High School and the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center

Part of the Claremont research (town/Claremont). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Stevens High School (grades 9–12) and the Claremont campus of the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center (SRVRTC-Claremont): the trades programs and what was cut, credentials and college credit, the Region 10 agreement with Newport and Sunapee, work-based learning, counseling, honors and early college courses, VLACS, sports and activities through the 2025-26 budget crisis, fees, safety and discipline, and accreditation. Sources are the Stevens 2026-27 handbook and program of studies, the 2024 school profile, the SRVRTC site, Claremont School Board minutes and packets (June 2025 to November 2026), NH DOE's CTE monitoring reports (2024, 2025, 2026), the Region 10 agreement, board policies, NEASC's directory data and HB 1399 (2026).

**Counts:** 11 topics, 66 facts, 3 number tables.
