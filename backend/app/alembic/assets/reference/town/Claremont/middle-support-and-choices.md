# Claremont Middle School, Student Support and School Choices

Part of the Claremont research (town/Claremont). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Claremont Middle School (CMS, grades 6–8, 107 South Street) and the choices and supports around it for Claremont families in SAU 6: special education staffing and the district's own account of gaps in services, the posting for evening and weekend special education staff, bullying and behavior rules, counseling and mental health support, the phone rule, CMS staffing, vacancies and substitutes, bus routes and storm closings, school hours, homeschooled and EFA students in district programs, returning to CMS for grade 6, Claremont Christian Academy, EFA providers in Sullivan County, and the alternatives to CMS (online courses, VLACS, hardship transfers and the open enrollment vote).

- **11 topics, 89 facts, 2 tables.** Ten topics are shared slugs placed at `district Claremont`; `town-school-options-nearby` has facts at `district Claremont Christian School` (the academy's own facts) and `town Claremont`. No new state-rule facts; state rules are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **Main sources:** the CMS 2026-27 Parent & Student Handbook (in the September 2, 2026 board packet; the board approved it 5–2 that night), the superintendent's written reports to the board (September 2 to November 18, 2026), the October 7, 2026 "Special Education Management Update" and the August 19 interim special education plan, school board minutes (January to November 4, 2026), Claremont board policies (JJJ, JJAA, JJIB, IHBG, IHBG-R, JICJ, JICK, JLDBA, JLD, JLDBB, EEA, EBCD, EBCE, IMBA, IHBI, JEC, JG, JFAA), SAU 6 and CMS web pages and news posts, SAU 6's job board (AppliTrack), the 2026-27 bus route sheets, the 2026 district warrant, the EFA marketplace feed, Claremont Christian Academy's site and VLACS's site.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Special education:** by the district's own October 7, 2026 account, "We don't have enough capacity to meet all our students' SDI needs at any of the schools"; it needs 15 more certified staff, has no special education director (the superintendent fills the role with consultants), and found 63% of IEPs out of compliance at the start of the year. It would add two special education teachers at CMS. It sent families an October 1 letter about gaps, and case managers are telling families about "completed, missed, and compensatory services."
  - **Outside-of-hours staff (P5.2):** confirmed. On October 21 the superintendent reported a posting for licensed educators available "for limited evening or weekend hours," as "an alternative option" for families whose children can receive services outside school hours, because some services have been hard "to fit into student and teacher schedules." On November 18 he reported a new CMS student support classroom and teacher reassignments.
  - **CMS staffing:** CMS had 20 open positions in June 2026, reached 24 certified or certification-eligible classroom teachers by August 5, and on November 18 had been without a full-time certified 8th grade math teacher for about five weeks; the district was weighing virtual instruction from Imagine Learning.
  - **Hours and buses:** CMS runs 7:35 to 2:15 (opening bell 7:38). In 2026-27 Disnard starts at 8:30 and Maple Avenue at 8:50. The same seven numbered buses run a 6:10–7:25 run for Stevens and CMS and then the elementary run. The transportation department has 15 staff against 17.6 budgeted positions and no substitute driver, so an absent driver means doubled-up routes. No decision to cut routes was found; a study of running buses in-house versus outsourcing is still on the board's list.
  - **Phones:** banned bell to bell under policy JICJ (September 2025). The CMS handbook adds a third-offense plan to keep the phone in the office and refers to Yondr pouches. No CMS figures on violations or social media problems were found.
  - **Choices:** Claremont Christian Academy's 2026-27 tuition is $7,300 (K5–5) and $7,900 (6–12). Its grades 6–12 campus is in Charlestown. It is an EFA marketplace provider, and a base 2026-27 EFA ($4,351) would cover about 55% of grades 6–12 tuition (derived). In March 2026 Claremont voters approved an open enrollment article that sets the share of Claremont students who may attend open enrollment schools elsewhere at 0%, 1,255 to 622.
