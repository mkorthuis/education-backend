# Claremont Residents' Questions

The questions the Claremont research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Claremont is a city and SAU 6. The Claremont School District runs Disnard Elementary School and Maple Avenue Elementary School (since Bluff Elementary closed), Claremont Middle School (grades 6–8) and Stevens High School (grades 9–12), with a regional technical center. Cornish and Unity send high school students to Stevens under tuition contracts. Several questions contain premises (a $5 million deficit and a state loan to repay, Bluff closing and third-grade classes combined, the state trying to overturn the Claremont rulings, Stevens's accreditation, Cornish and Unity pulling back from SAU 6, a 7:30 middle school start, outside-of-hours special education staff) that the research should check rather than assume.
