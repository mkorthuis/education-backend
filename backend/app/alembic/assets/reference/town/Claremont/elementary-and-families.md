# Disnard, Maple Avenue and Young Families in Claremont

Part of the Claremont research (town/Claremont). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Claremont's two elementary schools after Bluff closed: the 2026-27 grade-level alignment (Disnard Pre-K to grade 2, Maple Avenue grades 3 to 5), class sizes and staffing since the move, the school day and buses, meals and the end of Bluff's free-meal program, Title I reading help and reading screening results, retention policy, English learner services and translation, the district's preschool cuts and plans, and the city's recreation and library programs.

- **14 fact topics, 80 facts, no number tables.** Sources are SAU 6 and school web pages, the 2026-27 elementary handbook, board policies, Claremont School Board minutes (November 2025 to November 2026) and packet documents, the elementary bus route sheets, NH DOE's fall 2025 school designations, FRAC's CEP fact sheet, and the city's Parks and Recreation and Fiske Free Library pages. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 20 MB).
- **Main findings:** there are no longer Disnard or Maple Avenue zones. The board voted on September 16, 2026 to put all Pre-K to grade 2 at Disnard and grades 3 to 5 at Maple Avenue, though policy JCA (2016) still describes three neighborhood zones. Free meals for every student ended with Bluff, the only school on the federal Community Eligibility Provision. The district cut its general preschool program for 2025-26 and now serves only preschoolers with IEPs. Reading screening (DIBELS) at the start of 2026-27 found half or more of students in grades 1 to 5 below benchmark.
- **Main gaps:** crossing guards near Disnard on Hanover Street (no city or school source found); whether the Claremont Savings Bank Community Center runs any after-school program (its pages list none); whether ParentSquare translation is turned on; how many English learners Maple serves now and who teaches them; any elementary gifted or advanced math program (none found); a current children's summer reading program at the Fiske Free Library.
- **Spot-checked** against raw copies: the September 16, 2026 vote ("Disnard Elementary School hosting grades PreK-2 and Maple Avenue Elementary School hosting grades 3-5", `board/minutes/9.16.26-approved-csb-meeting-minutes.txt`); the June 3, 2026 note on Bluff's "community eligibility program" (`board/minutes/6.3.26-approved-csb-meeting-minutes.txt`); and the DIBELS row "1st Grade 27% 21% 48% 37% 28% 65%" (`board/packets/cia-report-2026-11-18.txt`).
