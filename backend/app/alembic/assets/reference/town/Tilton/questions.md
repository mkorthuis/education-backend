# Tilton Residents' Questions

The questions the Tilton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Tilton's students attend the Winnisquam Regional School District (SAU 59), shared with Northfield and Sanbornton. It runs the Winnisquam Early Learning Center (preschool), Southwick School (K–4) in Northfield and Sanbornton Central School (K–4), Winnisquam Regional Middle School (grades 5–8) and Winnisquam Regional High School (grades 9–12) in Tilton, with the new Winnisquam Career Center. Several questions contain premises (Union Sanborn closing, full-day pre-K at the Early Learning Center, low WRHS math proficiency, about $23,700 per student, the outlets' commercial taxes, Compass Classical Academy, Tilton School's aid, a morning class at Huot, Tilton children going to school in Northfield, three apps) that the research should check rather than assume.
