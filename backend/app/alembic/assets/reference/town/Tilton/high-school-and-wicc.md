# Winnisquam Regional High School, the Winnisquam Career Center and the Huot Center

Part of the Tilton research (town/Tilton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Winnisquam Regional High School (WRHS, grades 9–12, 435 West Main Street, Tilton), which serves Tilton, Northfield and Sanbornton, for the questions assigned to agent 3: the Winnisquam Career Center (WiCC) next door and what WRHS students get from it (programs, credit, enrollment, schedule); the Huot Center in Laconia for programs WiCC lacks, and how WRHS students get there; WRHS's diplomas, honors, AP and Early College courses and their cost; extended learning opportunities (ELOs) and internships; online credit recovery, summer recovery, the Diploma Academy, alternative learning plans and HiSET; counseling and college and financial aid help; technology, media and STEM electives and clubs; the phone policy at WRHS and WRMS; and the school resource officer. Facts are placed with `district Winnisquam Regional` so Northfield's and Sanbornton's research can cite them.

**Counts:** 10 topics, 47 facts and 4 number tables.

**Main sources:** the WRHS 2026-27 Comprehensive Guide (handbook, Program of Studies, Activities and Athletic handbooks in one Google Doc; its red text marks what is new for 2026-27); the WRHS 2025-26 School Profile; the counseling office's September–November 2026 newsletters and its FAFSA and Xello sheet; WiCC's website and 2025-26 Program of Studies; Winnisquam Regional School Board minutes (August 2025 to the November 16, 2026 draft) and the September–November 2026 board packets (enrollment reports with WiCC rows, principals' reports, draft policy EEA); district policies IHCD, IHCD-R, IHBH, IHBI, IHCA, IKFG and JICJ; the WRMS 2026-27 handbook; Huot's application page; the site's own data (grade 11 scores, graduation cohorts, graduates' plans); and OpenStreetMap/OSRM driving routes. WiCC's five programs and terms are in `town/Belmont/belmont-high-and-huot.md` (cte-center-programs 9–10) and `town/Franklin/franklin-high-and-pathways.md` (cte-center-programs 5–8); Huot's programs, schedule and admission are in `town/Laconia/high-school-and-huot.md`. They are cited, not repeated.

**Main gaps:** WRHS does not post how many credits a Huot course earns, or Huot bus return times. No list of colleges WRHS graduates attend or are admitted to was found (the profile gives only percentages). No count of students in the Diploma Academy, credit recovery or ELOs, and no internship host list. No WRHS course in computer science, programming or video production is listed, and nothing found explains why. The SRO's hours split between WRMS and WRHS isn't posted.

**Topic note:** no new topics proposed.

4. At Winnisquam Regional High School, a student who takes a two-year WiCC program in grades 11 and 12 uses 4 of the 32 semester-long class periods in a four-block schedule over four years and earns 5.0 credits for them, leaving 28 periods for the other 19 of the 24 credits the WRHS diploma requires, before counting study halls (**derived**).
   - citation: WiCC Program of Studies 2025-26, p. 1; WRHS School Profile 2025-26; WRHS 2026-27 Comprehensive Guide, bell schedule and graduation requirements
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/12xvoZtiHhy3d-mrZM-fvdUNWytInyQjzxKunSAEziBs/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Winnisquam Regional
   - note: **Derived:** 4 blocks × 2 semesters × 4 years = 32; two years of one daily WiCC class = 4 semester periods earning 2 × 2.5 = 5.0 credits (fact 3); 24 − 5 = 19; 32 − 4 = 28. Assumptions: a daily semester course earns 1.0 credit (profile: "Courses that meet daily for one semester also earn 1.0 credit"), the student passes everything, and the WiCC class replaces one WRHS block. WRHS's 2026-27 blocks (Block 2 8:55–10:10, Bear Block 10:13–10:48, Block 3 10:51–12:06, lunch block 12:09–1:11, Block 4 1:15–2:31) don't line up exactly with WiCC's (9:00–10:30, 10:33–12:03, 12:30–2:00; Franklin file cte-center-programs 6), so which WRHS block a WiCC class displaces isn't stated in any source.
