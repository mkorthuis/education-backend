# Tilton: Money, Taxes, Governance and the Winnisquam Cost Split

Part of the Tilton research (town/Tilton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 11 topics, 52 facts and 4 number tables on the Winnisquam Regional School District (SAU 59), which Tilton shares with Northfield and Sanbornton, and on Tilton's own tax bill. Northfield's research can cite the district-wide facts directly; they are placed with `district Winnisquam Regional`, and Tilton-only facts with `town Tilton`.
- **Budget:** the 2026-27 budget article ($32,174,588; general fund up 3.12%) and its drivers (health insurance up 34.6%, contracts, special education); the 2025-26 vote (216 to 41); the administration's first 2027-28 budget (up 6.95%); special education spending; year-end surpluses returned to taxpayers; the WiCC's tuition revenue; where budget documents are posted.
- **Taxes and the cost split:** the formula (70% students/30% property value until 2024, moving a point a year to 75/25 by 2028-29, voted 186 to 32); the full 2025-26 apportionment by town (table) and the share history (table); why Tilton's local school tax rose 24.8% in 2025 (**derived**); the 2026-27 estimate (+$0.70 per $1,000 in Tilton at most); Tilton's 2025 bill with the Tilton–Northfield Fire District's $2.04, which the site's total leaves out; Tilton's commercial tax base (42.4% of value, **derived**) and the Tanger Outlets parcel ($24,950,000, about 2.2%); the 2024 revaluation; Tilton's elderly exemption; the tax cap as the board discussed it.
- **Governance and votes:** nine board seats, three per town, elected at large; the new separately voted Central Office (SAU) budget ($1,150,519 proposed for 2027-28); the 2027-28 calendar (budget hearing February 9, 2027; annual meeting Saturday, March 20, 2027 at 9:00 AM); where agendas, packets and minutes are.
- **Buildings and bonds:** no bonded debt since the last heating-project payment in 2024-25; a roof bond of up to $7 million planned for the March 2027 vote; how the WiCC was paid for.
- **Enrollment, housing, ratings, plans and volunteers:** district enrollment 1,343 (2020-21) to 1,126 (October 1, 2026) (table); 400–500 new Tilton housing units expected; home sale prices; straight-line distances from Tilton places to the schools (table, **derived**); WRHS's GreatSchools rating and what it is built from; the new strategic plan (approved October 19, 2026, not posted); the volunteer background check.

**Main sources:** the district's 2026 Annual Report, 2026-27 budget presentation, warrant, MS-27, budget hearing handout and November 5, 2026 Central Office budget hearing slides; school board minutes from January to November 2026 and board packets (September, October 2026; March 2026); the 2024 annual meeting minutes and Formula Study notice; the district's state-aid ledger and volunteer letters; the Town of Tilton's 2025 and 2024 annual reports (tax rate breakdown, MS-1, apportionment), its 2024 revaluation report, assessing FAQ, exemption pamphlet and the outlet parcel's tax card; NH REALTORS' October 2026 local market updates; GreatSchools; one Laconia Daily Sun article (2022 bond votes); OpenStreetMap geocoding; the site's database.
