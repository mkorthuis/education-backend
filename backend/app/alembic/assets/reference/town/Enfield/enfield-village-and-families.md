# Enfield Village School and Young Families

Part of the Enfield research (town/Enfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Enfield Village School (EVS, preschool to grade 4, 271 US Route 4) and what young Enfield families ask about it: the school day and after-school program, the 2026-27 bus runs (including the long Route 4A run and why all grades now ride together), storm alerts, meals and the yearly meal application, changing a child's bus stop, the public preschool, the kindergarten cutoff and early entry, child care and drive times, how teachers communicate, which school a family on the Grafton side of Route 4A attends, home internet, English learner support, who attends the school, holidays, and moving records in and out.

- **12 topics, 46 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 10 MB): the EVS 2026-27 handbook (PDF and text, 189 pages including the district policies it reprints); district policies EEA, EEAB, EBCD, IC, IMBA, IHBBA, ACD, JEB, JEB-R, KB and JFAA (PDF and text); the 2026-27 district calendar; the 2026-27 free and reduced-price meal letter; the district's enrollment, bus routes, food services, Title I, technology, community, policies, staff and board pages; sanitized text copies of the nine 2026-27 bus route sheets (house-number stops reduced to street names, drivers' names removed; the route PDFs were not saved); redacted excerpts of six 2026-27 school board minutes; the Indian River handbook text; a 2017-18 Mascoma Valley Parks & Recreation brochure; federal (NCES) enrollment-by-race files; OpenStreetMap and OSRM lookups for intersections and drive times; an extract of New Hampshire's BEAD broadband location files; and a saved query of the site's child care data.
- **Topics:** shared slugs `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-transportation`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff`, `district-preschool`, `district-meals`, `district-family-communication`, `district-student-support`, `district-volunteers-and-partners`, `town-recreation-and-summer`, `town-child-care` and `town-broadband`. - **Main gaps:** no current source gives the EVS after-school program's hours, fees or open places (only a 2017-18 brochure: Monday to Friday until 6 p.m.); nothing was found on PTA after-school clubs, their sessions or sign-up; no source states what time storm alerts go out; no tuition amount for the preschool is posted; no English learner teacher or English learner services page was found.
