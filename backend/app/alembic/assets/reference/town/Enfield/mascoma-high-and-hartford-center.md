# Mascoma High and the Hartford Center: Career Programs, AP, College Courses, Credits, Buses and Outcomes

Part of the Enfield research (town/Enfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Mascoma Valley Regional High School (MVRHS, in Canaan) for Enfield families: how Mascoma students reach the Hartford Area Career and Technology Center (HACTC) in White River Junction, Vermont, and how its credits count; AP courses that run every other year and who pays AP exam fees; River Valley Community College (RVCC) courses at the high school, Early College Online and their fees; Dartmouth's Community High School Program; extended learning opportunities (ELOs); credit recovery and graduation credits; credit for homeschooled students; PE credit for sports and NHIAA's rule on 8th graders on high school teams; Butler's Bus Service, the driver shortage and whether there is a late bus; outdoor, forestry and hands-on courses; and the school's own college and career readiness figures.

- **7 fact topics, 70 facts and 3 number tables.** All topics are shared town topics: `cte-center-programs`, `district-transportation`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `district-homeschool-access`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `high-school-outcomes` and `district-student-support`. Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 16 MB): MVRHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies and Student Handbook, the 2025-26 Student Athlete Handbook, MVRHS web pages, seven board policies (IHCD/LEB, IHBH, IKFB, IK, IHBG, EEA, JJIC), NHIAA's 2026-27 by-laws on classification and below-grade-9 play, the superintendent's August 11, 2026 report, school board minutes for July 28 and August 11, 2026 (redacted text: residents' names and house-number addresses removed), Butler's September 2026 credit memo, MVRHS's fall 2026 quarter report (AP and readiness data), the FY28 budget proposals, October 19, 2026 budget committee minutes, HACTC's Natural Resources page and two OSRM route results. PDFs were read with macOS PDFKit; scanned pages were rendered to images and transcribed (scripts in the session scratchpad).
- **Places:** all 70 facts use `district Mascoma Valley Regional`. HACTC is in Vermont, which the site has no place type for; its facts about Mascoma students sit with Mascoma, as Lebanon's and Hanover's files did.
- **New data concepts:** Mascoma's HACTC bus and tuition budget lines (FY26–FY28); the 2026-27 driver shortage, combined K–12 routes and Butler's route credit; which AP courses run in 2026-27 and which in 2027-28; Mascoma's AP results 2022–2026 and its 2025-26 college and career readiness counts; the ELO approval steps and timelines; Mascoma's credit recovery contract and deadline; the PE half-credit for two sports; NHIAA's Division III rule applied to Mascoma 8th graders; drive times from MVRHS to HACTC and Dartmouth (**derived**).

| Question | Premise | What the sources show |
|---|---|---|
| P2.1 | The school buses students to HACTC in White River Junction | **Yes.** The handbook says bus transportation is provided for all HACTC students, and policy EEA has the district provide and pay for it. Students must ride the bus unless given permission. HACTC is about 14 miles and 28 minutes from MVRHS (**derived**). Mascoma budgets about $101,000–$107,000 a year for the HACTC bus (district-transportation 1–4). |
| P2.2 | Half the day at the tech center | **About right.** HACTC programs take 138 minutes a day and earn 3 credits a year. MVRHS requires 23 credits, more than the state's 20 (cte-center-programs 2–3; school-programs-after-grade-6 21–23). |
| P7.1 | AP Biology and Chemistry run only every other year | **Correct.** The program of studies marks AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP European History and AP US History as alternating. AP Biology and AP European History run in 2026-27; AP Chemistry and AP US History in 2027-28 (school-programs-after-grade-6 1–3). |
| P7.2 | A "Community High School program" at Dartmouth | **It exists.** Dartmouth's Community High School Program is listed in MVRHS's program of studies. Dartmouth's rules are in Hanover's file. No count of Mascoma applicants or approvals was found (school-programs-after-grade-6 16). |
| P7.3 | The district covers AP exam fees | **Yes, for AP courses taken at MVRHS.** The program of studies says "The school district covers all associated costs." Students taking AP courses online through VLACS or VHS pay for their own exams. RVCC credit for AP English Literature costs "a nominal fee." The CCSNH scholarship can cover two courses a year (school-programs-after-grade-6 5–6, 11–13). |
| P10.3 | Mascoma will "accept" homeschool credits | **Not as transfer credit.** Policy IK's list of "approved schools" for transfer credit doesn't include home education. Home-educated students instead show the required competencies through a portfolio or assessments, and the principal places students who re-enroll (district-homeschool-access 1–3). |
| P11.2 | 8th graders can try out for JV | **Only with an NHIAA waiver.** With 317 students, Mascoma falls in NHIAA's Division III range. In Division III, 8th graders may play sub-varsity only when they are needed to keep the program going and NHIAA grants a waiver. No weight-room rule for 8th graders was found (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 7–9). |
| P15.1 | Two varsity sports count toward PE so a student can take another elective | **Partly.** Two varsity or JV sports in one year earn a ½ PE credit, once in four years, with paperwork from the counselor. The other ½ credit must come from a PE course at MVRHS (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 1–3). |
| P15.3 | "Online Running Start" classes | **Two different programs.** Running Start (now "Early College at Your High School") courses are taught in the high school. The online program is Early College Online (formerly eStart). Whether a college accepts the credit is up to the college (school-programs-after-grade-6 11–15). |
| P17.1 | A student must wait for summer school after failing a class | **No.** The program of studies' "Summer Credit Recovery" section has students apply within 10 days of being told they failed and finish by the end of the next quarter (school-programs-after-grade-6 18). |
| P17.2 | Butler's Bus Service is changing and combining routes because it is short of drivers | **Correct as of fall 2026.** In August 2026 the district was short three full-time drivers. It returned to carrying all grades on the same buses and removed three buses. Butler credited $7,299 for a missed or combined route in September 2026 (district-transportation 5–9). |
| P17.3 | A late bus for high schoolers staying after school | **None found.** The 2026-27 route sheets list one morning run and one afternoon run per route, and no handbook or policy describes a late bus (district-transportation 11–12). |
| P19.2 | Outdoor skills are mostly "PE electives like Outdoor Pursuits" | **Partly.** Outdoor Pursuits is a PE course. MVRHS also teaches Environmental Science, The Science of Survival, woods and building construction. Forestry and horticulture are at HACTC's Natural Resources program. No agriculture course or FFA chapter at MVRHS was found (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 4–6; cte-center-programs 9). |
