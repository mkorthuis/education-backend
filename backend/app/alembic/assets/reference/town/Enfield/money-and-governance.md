# Enfield: Money, Taxes and Governance in the Mascoma Valley Regional School District

Part of the Enfield research (town/Enfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 6 topics, 59 facts and 4 number tables on how the Mascoma Valley Regional School District (SAU 62) budget becomes Enfield's school tax, how the cooperative's costs fall on its five towns, the 2026-27 budget and its higher default budget, what is driving costs while enrollment falls, the 2027-28 budget now being built, board and budget committee seats, how Mascoma's spending compares with Lebanon's and Hanover's, and where agendas and budget numbers are posted.
- **Taxes:** Enfield's 2026-27 rate is $18.89 per $1,000 (local school $10.03, state school $1.15, town and county $7.71), so about 59.2% of the bill is for schools (**derived**). Enfield raises the largest dollar share of the five towns' local school tax (42.4% in 2025) but has 51.3% of their property value, and its school tax per $1,000 of equalized value is the second lowest of the five (**derived**). The towns' shares of returned surplus money track their shares of pupils (Enfield 39.8%), not property value.
- **Budget and default:** voters approved the $32,974,071 operating budget on March 10, 2026 (1,305 to 502). The default budget was higher ($33,209,497) because it starts from last year's budget plus the support staff contract and adds required salary, benefit, transportation, insurance and debt increases; the board and budget committee cut items to keep the proposed increase at 0.96% after a 26.7% health insurance increase.
- **Costs and enrollment:** from 2014-15 to 2024-25 the district's school spending rose 40.0% while the five towns' resident students fell 16.1%; benefits rose 65.8%, special education 57.8% and transportation 111.3% (**derived**, not inflation-adjusted).
- **2027-28:** the superintendent's proposal (November 16, 2026) is $32,149,618 for the district (+1.4%) plus a separate $1,660,501 SAU budget article (+2.8%); Question 2 (the tax cap) was not adopted for Mascoma (55.42% yes), so no cap applies. No program closures or layoffs were proposed in the documents read.
- **Governance:** seven board seats (Canaan 2, Enfield 2, one each for Dorchester, Grafton and Orange), all elected at large by voters of all five towns; Enfield cast 44.4% of the March 2026 ballots (**derived**).

**Main sources:** the district's 2025 and 2026 annual reports, the 2026 warrant, MS-27 and MS-DSB forms, the March 10, 2026 vote results, budget committee and school board minutes (November 2025 to November 2026), the superintendent's proposed 2027-28 budgets, the superintendent's November 17, 2026 report, board policies BBB, BEDB and BEDH, RSA 195:19-a and 195:19-b, Valley News (January 7, 2026), and the site's database (tax, enrollment, Census and DOE-25 lines, read-only).

## gov-cooperative-district: Cooperative School Districts

- kind: term
- question_type: governance_money
- description: What a cooperative school district is and how it shares costs among its towns.

1. Under RSA 195:19-a, a cooperative school board's size, makeup, method of selection and terms are set by the cooperative's bylaws or articles of agreement, limited to these choices: all members elected at large; single-member districts of nearly equal population; multimember districts that most nearly give proportional representation; members who each live in and represent a member district but are all elected at large; or another one-person, one-vote method approved by the State Board of Education; terms may not exceed 3 years, and cooperatives that existed on August 22, 1971 need not conform unless they vote to.
   - citation: RSA 195:19-a
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/195/195-19-a.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-22
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "IV. The members of the cooperative school board shall each be domiciled in and represent a pre-existing district with each pre-existing district having at least one such resident representative but all members of the cooperative school board shall be elected at large". Elections use the non-partisan ballot (RSA 669). Mascoma's board follows this pattern (district-sau-governance facts 2–3). Raw: raw/money/rsa-195-19-a.html, .txt
2. Under RSA 195:19-b, any cooperative school district may vote at a regular or special meeting to change the number, makeup, method of selection and terms of its board members and how seats are apportioned among its member districts, as long as the board has no more than 15 members and terms are no longer than 3 years.
   - citation: RSA 195:19-b
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/195/195-19-b.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-22
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "Any cooperative school district organized under any of the provisions of RSA 195 or pursuant to any special act may at any regular or special meeting vote to change the number, composition, method of selection, and terms of office of members on the board of the district, provided that in no event shall the board exceed 15 members nor terms exceed 3 years; and may change the apportionment of the board in relation to the pre-existing school districts."
