# Enfield Residents' Questions

The questions the Enfield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Enfield's students attend the Mascoma Valley Regional School District (SAU 62), shared with Canaan, Dorchester, Grafton and Orange: Enfield Village School (preschool to grade 4), Indian River School (grades 5–8) and Mascoma Valley Regional High School (grades 9–12), the last two in Canaan. Career programs are at the Hartford Area Career & Technology Center in Vermont. Several questions contain premises (hour-long bus rides on Route 4A, about 40% math proficiency, a $32.97 million budget with a higher default, AP courses every other year, the Dartmouth Community High School program, MAPs pathways) that the research should check rather than assume.
