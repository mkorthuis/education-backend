# Moultonborough: Money, Taxes, Buildings and Governance (Moultonborough School District, SAU 45)

Part of the Moultonborough research (town/Moultonborough). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 86 facts and 4 number tables on the Moultonborough School District's budget and what drives it, its taxes and state aid, the "donor town" history and risk, its tuition agreements with Tamworth and Inter-Lakes, its buildings and capital plans, how residents can follow and vote on the budget, talk of sharing or consolidation, the enrollment outlook, local housing costs, and how its spending and results compare with Inter-Lakes, Gilford and Governor Wentworth (Kingswood).
- **Budget:** voters approved a 2026-27 operating budget of $21,111,300 on March 14, 2026, up $605,979 (2.96%), with no positions added or cut (2.4 FTE fewer through attrition). The 40 health insurance lines rose $537,558 (23.3%) and special education's other lines $168,876, together more than the whole increase (**derived**). The district's total appropriations rose about 54% from 2019-20 to 2026-27 while its October enrollment went from 499 to 484 (table). The ABC's December 2025 report lists $500,000 of possible savings and urges cost control; the board answered that cuts mean people and programs.
- **Taxes and state aid:** school taxes were $2.90 of Moultonborough's $5.33 rate in 2025 (about 54%, **derived**). The district estimated $2.96 for 2026 ($30 more on a $500,000 home) before the board returned $700,000 of surplus to lower taxes. Moultonborough keeps about $4.9 million a year of statewide education tax above its cost of an adequate education (FY2026, **derived** from the site's data); it sent $4,033,580 to the state in FY2005 as a "donor" town. Question 2's tax cap passed in Moultonborough (62.6%, unofficial), so it applies from the 2027-28 budget.
- **Sharing:** Tamworth students attend Moultonborough Academy under a yearly agreement (2026-27 rate $20,221); Inter-Lakes and Moultonborough exchange students tuition-free under an MOU (9 and 7 students in 2026-27); football remains a co-op with Inter-Lakes but lacrosse ended. No consolidation or tuition-out study was found.
- **Buildings:** a $7,344,000 energy bond (5.10%, to 2040) paid for two summers of Central School work; the track ($350,000) is planned for FY2030, Moultonborough Academy has no working science lab, the gym's air handler fan is broken, and a 20-year capital plan was ordered in October 2026.
- **Support staff:** the 2025-2029 MEST contract covers paraprofessionals; layoffs go by reverse seniority; para pay for 2026-27 runs $18.87 to $27.96 an hour on the Para I and II scales.

**Main sources:** the district's 2026 Annual Report (MS-26 budget form, enrollment, staff, special education spending), 2026-27 proposed budget detail (V.3), February 10, 2026 budget hearing packet (estimated tax impact), 2025 audit, Mandatory Report to Voters, MEST contract, school board, hearing, Buildings & Grounds and annual meeting minutes (October 2025 to November 2026), joint Select Board–School Board minutes; the town's Advisory Budget Committee (ABC) reports (February 2025, December 2025, January 2026), Tax Rate History and draft Housing Needs Assessment (April 2025); a Coalition Communities list of FY2005 donor towns; Inter-Lakes' November 2026 board packet; and the site's database (adequacy aid, tax rates, Question 2, cost per pupil, test results, graduates' plans).
