# Moultonborough Residents' Questions

The questions the Moultonborough research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Moultonborough is SAU 45. The Moultonborough School District runs Moultonborough Central School (preschool to grade 5) and, on Blake Road, the Moultonborough Middle Level (grades 6–8) with Moultonborough Academy (grades 9–12). Students may take career programs at the Lakes Region Technology Center in Wolfeboro. The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting. Several questions contain premises (universal preschool, Blizzard Bag days, a 'donor town' return, an Advisory Budget Committee warning about small electives, a shared psychologist and social worker) that the research should check rather than assume.
