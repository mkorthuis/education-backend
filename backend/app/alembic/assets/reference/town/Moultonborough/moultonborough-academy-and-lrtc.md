# Moultonborough Academy and the Lakes Region Technology Center

Part of the Moultonborough research (town/Moultonborough). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Moultonborough Academy (MA, grades 9–12, site school id 22120, on Blake Road with the Middle Level) and the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC) in Wolfeboro, where MA students take career programs. It answers residents' questions about the LRTC bus, shop and marine classes, help with trades, AP and honors, extended learning opportunities (ELOs), college counseling, ties to trades and emergency services, sports co-ops, small classes and electives, school climate, a small school for an anxious student, reduced schedules and the school store, new practical courses, stress, local scholarships and dual enrollment costs.

Much was already gathered for Moultonborough Academy by the Tamworth research (`town/Tamworth/high-school.md`, 21 facts placed `district Moultonborough`: the AP list and AP rules, honors and weighting, the $150 Early College fee, the schedule and competency grading, the Inter-Lakes reciprocal agreement, VLACS and the Independent Learning Center, the Apprentice and Internship courses, the guidance calendar, advisory and PAWS, LRTC's programs for MA students, the counseling team and college attendance). This file cites those by fact number and adds what they lack.

- **7 fact topics, 44 facts and 1 number table.** 38 facts use `district Moultonborough`, 1 uses `district Governor Wentworth Regional` (an LRTC rule), 4 use `town Moultonborough` (community scholarships and the Adele Taylor trust fund) and 1 has no place (how a course earns the AP label).
- **Raw copies** are in `raw/high/` (about 50 MB): MA's 2026-27 Program of Studies and Student Handbook; MA's home, counseling, College and Career Center, athletics and Local Scholarships pages; the district's 2026 annual report; school board packets for August 11, October 13 and November 10, 2026 (with Vision OCR text); the 2026-27 board goals; Athletic, Instructional, Budget and Mental Health & Wellbeing committee minutes (August 2025 to October 2026); the Advisory Budget Committee's February 2025 school report and December 18, 2025 preliminary 2026-27 report (scanned; OCR and page images); policies IHCD, IMBC, IIB and the IHBH-R ELO form; the 2020 MA school climate survey summary; the district's social-emotional programs page; the town's Adele Taylor trust fund record, December 2025 trust fund report and January 2026 trustees' minutes; the College Board's AP Course Audit page for online providers; OSRM routing between MA and LRTC; excerpts of LRTC's 2026-27 handbook and Governor Wentworth's LRTC principal reports; and the CRDC 2023-24 rows for MA.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Small classes, by the numbers.** In June 2025, 31 MA classes for 2025-26 were scheduled with 8 or fewer students (9 with 4 or fewer), from Child Development (1) and Latin II (2) to AP English Literature (8). District policy IIB requires board approval for any high school section of 8 or fewer. The Advisory Budget Committee (ABC) asked the board in December 2025 to hire a consultant on reducing small classes, but named no electives to cut. The district itself merged two business electives into one course for 2026-27 so the teacher can teach more social studies (district-classrooms-and-staff 1–6; table moultonborough_academy_small_classes_2025_26).
  - **AP and college courses are taught live.** The 2025-26 AP sections in the June 2025 list (AP World History, AP English Literature, AP Calculus, AP Computer Science Principles) are regular in-school blocks of 6–8 students. Early College at MA is large for a small school: 75 students completed 131 courses in 2024-25, and 66 were enrolled in 132 in 2025-26. Online courses come through VLACS only when MA doesn't offer a course or it won't fit (school-programs-after-grade-6 1–4).
  - **Sports co-ops are with Inter-Lakes, not Kingswood.** Football (varsity, JV and middle level) and unified basketball are joint teams with Inter-Lakes. Joint boys' and girls' lacrosse ended after 2025-26 for lack of players. JV volleyball was not expected for 2026-27, boys' tennis depends on middle-schoolers being approved to play, and coed varsity cross country did not run in fall 2026 (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 1–5).
  - **LRTC:** 17 MA students attended LRTC in 2025-26, up from 6. LRTC is about 19.9 road miles and 41 minutes by car from MA (derived). No bus schedule is posted (cte-center-programs 1–4).
  - **Scholarships:** at the June 2026 senior banquet, 82 scholarships from 31 organizations went to 23 seniors. No dollar total is published (town-family-help 1–4).
  - **Dual enrollment costs:** district policies IHCD and IMBC say families pay college course tuition unless the board approves otherwise. The state-funded free courses and the $150 fee are what keep most Early College courses low-cost (school-programs-after-grade-6 11–13).
