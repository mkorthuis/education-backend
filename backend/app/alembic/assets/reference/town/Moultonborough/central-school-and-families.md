# Moultonborough Central School and Young Families

Part of the Moultonborough research (town/Moultonborough). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Moultonborough Central School (MCS, preschool to grade 5) and the daily life of young families: the free preschool and its schedule, Child Find, enrollment by grade and class sizes, school hours, tardiness and dismissal changes, the 2026–27 calendar and half days, Blizzard Bag days, buses, stops and the morning ride from Black Point, closing notices, reading instruction and Title I help, special education and dyslexia questions, meal prices and free or reduced-price applications, the phone and device rules, Alma and other family communication, translation and English learner staff, help for new families, the PTA and volunteers, and the town's after-school (Beyond the Bell), summer camp and library summer reading programs.

12 topics, 78 facts and 1 number table. Sources are the MCS and SAU 45 SchoolBlocks sites (pages and the Google Drive files they link), the MCS Family Handbook 2026–27, the Middle Level Student Handbook 2026–27, the 2026–27 district calendar, school board policies IIB, EEA, JICJ, IHBBA-R and EFAA, the 2026–27 meal application and family letter, the March 2026 district annual report, the 2026–27 school board goals, the October 13 and November 10, 2026 school board packets (enrollment by month and grade, the K–5 report card project, the library report), SAU 45's 2026–27 ESEA consolidated application, the MCS Weekly of November 20, 2026, the Town of Moultonborough's 2025 annual report and Recreation Department pages, the Moultonborough Public Library's summer reading page, OpenStreetMap routing (derived drive times) and the U.S. Naval Observatory (January sunrise). No new shared topic is proposed.

Main gaps: preschool session times and whether preschoolers are bused (no source says); Beyond the Bell's fees and registration (the Recreation Department's registration site loads only by script); actual bus route and stop times (the district's "Bus Routes" folder requires a Google sign-in); whether MCS reading staff are trained in Orton-Gillingham (no source names it, and the Sonday System publisher's site refused automated requests); the text of policy IHBBA (English learner instruction), which requires a sign-in; whether MCS translates teacher notices or report cards; summer day camp fees; any child care on Blizzard Bag days. Web searching stopped partway through because the session's search limit was reached; the rest of the work used pages already found and direct site navigation.

Notes for reviewers: the MCS Family Handbook is titled 2026–2027 and its staff list matches 2026–27, but its cover says "Updated 8/8/2023" and some sections keep older dates (conference days November 10 and March 20, teacher workdays "2025-2026") and older meal prices; facts use the 2026–27 calendar and meal letter for those. Facts name roles, not staff.
