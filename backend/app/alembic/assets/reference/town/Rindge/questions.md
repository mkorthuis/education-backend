# Rindge Residents' Questions

The questions the Rindge research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Rindge's students attend the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District (SAU 47), shared with Jaffrey. Rindge's elementary children attend Rindge Memorial School; grades 6–12 attend Conant Middle High School in Jaffrey. The cooperative is researched in `../Jaffrey/`; the files here add Rindge's side. Several questions contain premises (a free pre-K lottery at Rindge Memorial, an unfilled special education position and one school psychologist, paraprofessionals paid $17 an hour, 83% of special education on local taxes, Conant's 2025 award, the Little Orioles child care center, scores near 41% and 42%) that the research should check rather than assume.
