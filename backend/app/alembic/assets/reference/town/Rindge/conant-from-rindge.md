# Conant Middle High School and Career Programs From Rindge

Part of the Rindge research (town/Rindge). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file adds Rindge's side of Conant Middle High School (CMHS, grades 6–12, in Jaffrey), which the Jaffrey research already covers in depth in `town/Jaffrey/conant-and-cte.md` (cited below as "Jaffrey file"). New here: the 2026-27 Conant bus schedule for Rindge (six buses, first pickups at 6:23 a.m., stops near Lake Monomonac among the earliest), the cooperative's Articles of Agreement promise to bus all grades, the end of any published late bus, how athletes get home, the academic and attendance eligibility rules for sports and activities, what the district's February 2025 and February 2026 annual reports say about the 2025 award, AP pass rates, math interventions and test preparation, graduation and college-going, library staffing at Conant and Rindge Memorial, middle school and Rindge Memorial STEM courses and clubs, whether Conant students can take Franklin Pierce University courses, a New Hampshire rule on teen drivers carrying passengers, and Census estimates of young adults in Rindge.
