# Student Support and School Choices for Rindge Families

Part of the Rindge research (town/Rindge). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

This file covers support and choices for Rindge's students in the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District (SAU 47): who staffs special education at Rindge Memorial School (RMS) in 2026-27 and which lines are vacant, the district's evaluators and how many evaluations they do, paraprofessional vacancies and how para pay compares with local fast-food and retail pay, out-of-district placements and the transportation to them, counseling, break spaces and social-emotional supports at RMS, the move from RMS to Conant Middle School (Fly-Up Day and the WEB orientation), returning to RMS from home education, RMS's visitor and emergency rules and policing, military families, Hampshire Country School and Heritage Christian School in Rindge, what an EFA covers against their tuition, and why Massachusetts schools near Lake Monomonac aren't an easy option.

Much of the shared district was already researched in `town/Jaffrey/`. Its facts placed with `district Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative` apply to Rindge and are cited by file, topic and fact number instead of being written again.

**Counts:** 10 topics, 54 facts, 5 number tables (`jrcsd_rms_student_services_positions`, `jrcsd_evaluations_and_evaluator_staff`, `jrcsd_out_of_district_placements`, `rindge_area_hourly_wages_2025`, `monomonac_drive_to_schools`). ## cross-state-school-tuition-rules: Massachusetts School Choice and Students From New Hampshire

1. Massachusetts' inter-district school choice law (General Laws c. 76, § 12B) defines both the "sending district" and the "receiving district" as districts "within the commonwealth," and pays the receiving district's tuition (75% of its per-pupil spending, up to $5,000, or the full cost for special education) by deducting it from the sending district's state aid.
   - citation: Massachusetts General Laws, Part I, Title XII, Chapter 76, Section 12B(a), (b) and (f)
