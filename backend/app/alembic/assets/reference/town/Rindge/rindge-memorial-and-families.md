# Rindge Memorial School and Young Families

Part of the Rindge research (town/Rindge). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Rindge Memorial School (RMS, preschool to grade 5) in the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District (SAU 47) from the side of Rindge's young families: the preschool and its lottery, the 2026-27 bus schedule, class sizes and staffing, the school day, absences and make-up work, meals and the privacy of meal accounts, field trip costs and help with them, how the school reaches families, registering a child (including by guardians) and proof of residency, the town's before- and after-school program and summer options, child care near Rindge, help from the town, Franklin Pierce University's student teachers and programs at RMS, and reading help at RMS and the middle school. It builds on the Jaffrey research (`town/Jaffrey/jaffrey-grade-school-and-families.md`), which already holds most district-wide facts (the pre-K cuts and waitlist, Little Orioles, delays and remote days, meal prices, Alma and SchoolMessenger); this file cites those facts and adds Rindge's side.
