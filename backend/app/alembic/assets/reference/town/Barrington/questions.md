# Barrington Residents' Questions

The questions the Barrington research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Barrington is SAU 74, a one-district SAU. The Barrington School District runs the Early Childhood Learning Center (preschool and kindergarten), Barrington Elementary School (grades 1–4) and Barrington Middle School (grades 5–8). Barrington has no high school; its students choose among Coe-Brown Northwood Academy, Dover High School and Oyster River High School under tuition contracts. Several questions contain premises (a preschool lottery, a new Dover High contract, Coe-Brown's deposit, a choice deadline, guaranteed seats at Oyster River, a three-year teachers' contract, an annual homeschool filing) that the research should check rather than assume.
