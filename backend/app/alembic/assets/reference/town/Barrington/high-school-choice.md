# High School Choice for Barrington Students

Part of the Barrington research (town/Barrington). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Barrington's high school choice works and what each school offers Barrington students: the Oyster River lottery and its seat limits, Coe-Brown's registration dates and deposit, how Dover takes every Barrington student who chooses it, switching schools and arriving mid-year, where Barrington's 9th–12th graders actually go (by school and year), the three high school buses and how long they ride, late buses, special education at each school and who represents Barrington at IEP meetings, career and technical education access, nursing and health courses, AP and college results, robotics and STEM clubs, sports teams and eligibility, and what Barrington students and boards have said about fitting in. Coe-Brown Northwood Academy is researched here for the first time (its programs, policies, results, fees, special education and athletics).

- **9 fact topics, 69 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 34 MB): SAU 74's High Schools and transportation pages and Drive folder listings; the 2027-28 Oyster River lottery packet, the 2027 residency affidavit and policies JFABA-R6, R7, R8 and JCA (`policies/`); the Oyster River, Dover and Coe-Brown agreements (`contracts/`, OCR text from scanned PDFs); the 2026-27 bus route sheets (`buses/`); excerpts of nine Barrington school board packets, December 2025 to November 2026 (`board/`, OCR text); Oyster River's April 2026 presentation on Barrington students and its January 2025 joint meeting minutes (`orcsd/`); and Coe-Brown's admissions, about, academics, athletics, counseling, special education, ELO and bus pages, its 2026-27 Program of Studies, 2026-27 student handbook, 2025-26 school profile and 2026-27 club list (`coebrown/`). Scanned pages were read by OCR and checked against page images (`img/`).
- **Places:** 46 facts use `district Barrington` (including Barrington students' results and experience at the three schools), 21 `district Coe-Brown Northwood Academy`, 1 `district Oyster River Coop` (its clubs and computer courses, which Durham's files lack) and 1 `district Dover` (its team levels, which Dover's files lack).
- **New data concepts:** Barrington students by high school and grade, 2024-25 to 2026-27; each high school bus run's first pickup, arrival and longest ride; the Oyster River lottery procedure (sibling preference, February drawing, wait list, transfers); the share and counts of Barrington students in Oyster River's sports, clubs, AP, Early College and CTE; Barrington-only SAT and plans at Oyster River and SAT benchmark rates at Dover; Barrington's LEA representative for each high school; Coe-Brown's AP, dual enrollment, health science, vocational and robotics courses, its transfer-student credit rules, fees, sports levels and eligibility rule.
