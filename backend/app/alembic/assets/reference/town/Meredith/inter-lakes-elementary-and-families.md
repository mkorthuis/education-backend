## Summary

Covers Inter-Lakes Elementary School (ILES, preschool to grade 6, 21 Laker Lane, Meredith) and Sandwich Central School (SCS, K–6) in the Inter-Lakes Cooperative School District (SAU 2), and the questions of families with young children: the school day and the 2026-27 calendar; before- and after-school care (the Inter-Lakes Day Care Center's "7-Upper Club" and Meredith Parks and Recreation's After School Program and school-break camps); the 2026-27 bus routes, ride times on the back roads and Route 3 stops; how storm delays work and are announced; school nurses, medicine and inhaler paperwork; full-day kindergarten, class sizes and twins; school meals, the yearly application and the district's new meal pilot for families up to 300% of the poverty level; the reading program and reading help; enrichment; registration and how students are assigned to ILES or Sandwich Central.

11 topics, 64 facts and 1 number table (`inter_lakes_bus_routes_2026_27`). Sources are the ILES 2026-27 family handbook, the SCS 2026-27 handbook and welcome letter, the district's 2026-27 calendar, all 22 of its 2026-27 bus route sheets, its food service page, August 2026 meal notice and pilot application, its 2025-26 Growth & Proficiency report, the ILES Title I page, ten district policies (EBCD, EEA, EFAA, IC, IFA, IIB, JCA, JLC, JLCD and the policy list), school board minutes from December 2025 to October 2026 (October 13 is a draft), the Inter-Lakes Day Care Center's website and Meredith Parks and Recreation's 2026-27 flyers and parent guide. No new shared topic is proposed; kindergarten facts are added to the library's `kindergarten-day-length` topic.

Main gaps: what time of morning a delay or closing is decided or announced; whether the 7-Upper Club has a waitlist, what it charges and where it meets now; Wild Roots' and Inter-Lakes Day Care Center's tuition; whether any child care center carries children to or from the ILES half-day preschool; whether a Meredith child may choose Sandwich Central; kindergarten class sizes by section and how Inter-Lakes places twins; any gifted program; whether a bus enters any apartment complex off Route 3 (policy EEAB, which sets stops, is not posted).

Privacy note for reviewers: the bus route sheets list stops at house numbers and name drivers; facts give road names and times only. Board minutes name members of the public who commented; facts don't name them. Staff are named only where families are told to contact them.
