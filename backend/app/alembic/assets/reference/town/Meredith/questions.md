# Meredith Residents' Questions

The questions the Meredith research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Meredith's students attend the Inter-Lakes Cooperative School District (SAU 2), shared with Center Harbor and Sandwich. Inter-Lakes runs Inter-Lakes Elementary School (preschool to grade 6) in Meredith, Sandwich Central School (K–6), and Inter-Lakes Middle Tier and Inter-Lakes High School on one campus in Meredith. Inter-Lakes students may take career programs at the Huot Technical Center in Laconia. Several questions contain premises (Meredith paying over 70% of the district's bill, enrollment under 1,000, cuts to two paraeducators, an inclusion specialist and robotics camp teachers, $359,000 for field lights, a 10-to-1 ratio, about 45% proficient in math, "Meredith Bay" addresses, votes on a Wednesday) that the research should check rather than assume.
