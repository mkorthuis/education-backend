# Meredith: Money, Taxes and Governance (Inter-Lakes Cooperative and SAU 2)

Part of the Meredith research (town/Meredith). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 55 facts and 3 number tables on how the Inter-Lakes Cooperative School District's 2026-27 (FY2027) budget was built and adopted, what drove its 5.71% increase while enrollment fell, how the district's cost is split among Meredith, Center Harbor and Sandwich (half by property value, half by students), how that share reaches Meredith's tax rate and tax bills, why Meredith keeps its statewide education tax and gets no adequacy grant, the $359,000 field lights article and the capital plan, the robotics program, students per teacher, how the board and the annual meeting work (and why officer elections and the budget vote fall on different days), SAU 2's cost split with Ashland, Meredith's elderly exemption, and the "Meredith Bay" development in Laconia.
- **Budget:** $37,295,119 operating budget (up $2,015,282, 5.71%) plus a one-year teachers' contract ($458,794); special education and its transportation ($915,694) and health and dental insurance ($557,654) made up most of the rise; 2.93 fewer FTE; the 10-year budget, special education and enrollment series (table).
- **Cost split:** Meredith paid 71.9586% of the 2025-26 assessment (down from 73.6644% in 2023-24); the 1961/1967 formula, each town's valuation and student shares (table) and the derived equalized-value shares for 2023-2025 (table).
- **Taxes:** the DRA's 2025 rate calculation for Meredith (one rate on all property; the statewide education tax kept locally, "not retained $0"); the district's per-$1,000 estimates for each 2026 article; the about $800,000 of FY2026 fund balance returned to lower 2026-27 taxes; Meredith's update years and falling ratio; how bills are issued; the town's parcel and tax base mix.
- **Governance:** the seven-member board (two seats per town plus one at-large, all elected by the whole district), officer elections by ballot on town election day since 1981, the Wednesday annual meeting, every 2026 article's outcome, livestreams and comments, the 2026-27 meeting and budget schedule, and SAU 2's 88.645% / 11.355% split.
- **Facilities:** the lights article (total $359,000, $226,163 from donations, $132,837 from taxes, board split 3-3, installed by August 2026) and the FY2027 capital plan (ILES paving and sidewalks kept at $50,000; $491,000 of projects deferred).

**Main sources:** the Inter-Lakes School District's 2026 Annual School District Meeting Materials, its 2025 and 2024 Annual Reports, school board minutes (September 2025 to October 2026), the March 4, 2026 annual meeting minutes, the 2026 election warrant, the 1963 annexation and reorganization minutes, the board's 2026-27 schedule and November 10, 2026 packet; the Town of Meredith's DRA 2025 tax rate breakdown, tax rate and ratio history, 2025 town report, tax collector page, elderly exemption sheet and 2026 town warrant; Meredith Bay's own pages; the LakerBots' camp page; and the site's database (school taxes, tax rates, staff, class sizes, adequacy aid).
