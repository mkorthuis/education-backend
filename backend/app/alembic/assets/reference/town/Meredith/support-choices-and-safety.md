# Student Support, School Choices and Safety in the Inter-Lakes Schools

Part of the Meredith research (town/Meredith). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Meredith residents' questions on special education and 504 plans in the Inter-Lakes Cooperative School District (SAU 2): the 2026–27 paraeducator cuts, who to call about an IEP, speech and occupational therapy staffing, who trains teachers on behavior and sensory needs, summer services, and teachers' planning time. It also covers moving to Laconia in mid-year, home-educated students in Inter-Lakes sports and band, EFAs, the home education paperwork that was repealed in 2026, coming back from a private school, bullying on the halls and buses, school resource officers and building security on Laker Lane, and grandparents' access to the parent portal.

- **10 topics, 84 facts and 4 tables.** All topics are shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`. Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 28 MB): district policies in `raw/support/policies/`, school board minutes from August 2025 to October 2026 in `raw/support/minutes/`, and job descriptions in `raw/support/jobs/`.
- **Places used:** `district Inter-Lakes Cooperative` for the district, its schools, policies, budget, staff and board; `sau 2` for the SAU-wide Director of Student Services job description and the SAU 2 parent resource guide; `town Meredith` for the Meredith Police Department's report and EFA providers in Meredith; `town Sandwich` for EFA providers in Sandwich and Center Harbor.
- **Main findings:**
  - **No "inclusion specialist" was cut.** The 2026–27 budget voters approved on March 4, 2026 cut 2.0 FTE health office paraeducators, a regular education paraeducator and a library paraeducator at Inter-Lakes Elementary, and two never-filled paraeducator positions at Sandwich Central, while adding a special education teacher at Sandwich Central, a third school nurse and a classroom teacher moved to Inter-Lakes Elementary. No source names an inclusion specialist position. Special education paraeducator FTE went from 56.53 to 54.73.
  - **In June 2026 the board added behavior staff at Inter-Lakes Elementary.** It approved 6–0 a full-time Behavior Specialist and a full-time Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) for ILES, who work directly with teachers and paraeducators. A contracted BCBA serves the middle/high school and Sandwich Central. A behavioral consultant is training staff to build a Multi-Tiered System of Supports. The ILES WINN Room stays as a regulation space open to all students, alongside a new Student Support Center.
  - **Speech is partly contracted.** The district lists four speech-language pathologists and two occupational therapists on staff, with a contracted physical therapist. One speech-language pathologist position has been unfilled "for multiple years" and is covered by contracted services; in 2024–25 speech contracted services cost $191,072 against $10,000 budgeted.
  - **There is a school resource officer at both Laker Lane schools.** Meredith police officers serve at the middle/high school and at Inter-Lakes Elementary, with two traffic officers at arrival and dismissal; the district pays for the school year. NEASC's 2025 visiting team described a full-time SRO, camera monitoring and a buzz-in front entrance at the middle/high school.
  - **Inter-Lakes' home education policy is out of date.** Policy IHBG (amended 2018) still describes the notice, portfolio and annual evaluation that state law repealed on July 1, 2026. Even under that policy, parents kept the evaluation themselves; nothing was sent to SAU 2 each year.
  - **A family moving out of the district gets up to 45 days of free attendance.** Policy JFAB gives a family moving out before the end of the year a tuition waiver of up to 45 days; after that, staying requires board approval and tuition, set at "just over $33,000" a year.
- **Main gaps:** whether Inter-Lakes gives grandparents or other relatives without guardianship an Alma account; whether students with 504 plans get any summer services (the district's extended school year is for IEPs; the federal 504 guidance page blocked automated requests); the ILES Behavior Support Plan 2026–27 in the June 23 board packet (the agenda portal loads it by script); how the Laker Lane SROs' days and hours are set (no memorandum of understanding was found).
- **Earlier files:** No question was answered wholly by earlier files. The library's rules on special education, 504 plans, FERPA, EFAs and home education, and Laconia's, Chatham's and Albany's files, are cited in the table.
