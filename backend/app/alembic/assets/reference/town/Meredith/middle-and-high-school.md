# Inter-Lakes Middle/High School and the Career Centers

Part of the Meredith research (town/Meredith). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Inter-Lakes Middle/High School (ILMHS, grades 7–12, 1 Laker Lane, Meredith) for Meredith, Center Harbor and Sandwich families: how students get to the Huot Center in Laconia and the Winnisquam Career Center, and what credit they earn there; AP, honors and Early College (LRCC) courses and what they cost; business, personal finance and shop courses; internships and job shadows; competency-based grading and reassessment; the phone policy; sports, co-op teams, physicals, concussion baseline tests, eligibility and the new field lights; how grades 7 and 8 are kept on their own schedule; late buses; graduates' plans, SAT and AP results; a derived comparison of 2026 state test results with other Lakes Region schools; and adult, marine and diesel training near Meredith.

**Counts:** 11 topics, 81 facts and 3 number tables.

**Main sources:** ILMHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies, 2026-27 Student Handbook and 2026-27 School Profile; the district's Design Principles (adopted June 9, 2020) and Learning & Reassessment Guidelines; Inter-Lakes policies JICJ, JJA-1, JJA-2, JJIC, JLCJ, JLCJA, IHCD, IHBH, IMBC and EEA; the athletics, counseling, Career Partnership, Huot/Winnisquam and Lights Campaign pages; school board minutes from March 2024 to October 2026 and the March 4, 2026 annual meeting; the district's 2025-26 Growth & Proficiency slides; the NEASC visiting team report (April 2025); LRCC's and WMCC's program pages; RSA 193-C:6; and the site's own 2026 test results (for the derived comparison). The Huot Center's own programs, admission rules and costs are in `town/Laconia/high-school-and-huot.md` and are cited, not repeated.

**Main gaps:** No source says whether ice hockey and lacrosse are cooperative teams with Moultonborough Academy in 2026-27 (the NHIAA's pages refused automated requests). No late or activity bus is described anywhere; nothing says one does not exist. No rule was found on whether middle school teams use the turf field or the new lights. The fall 2026 tryout and physicals letter is no longer posted (the page now shows winter). No source gives where graduates enrolled (only acceptances), or any results for Laconia Christian Academy or other private schools that can be set beside the state tests.

**Places used:** `district Inter-Lakes Cooperative` for ILMHS, its policies, buses and board; `town Meredith` for derived drive times from the school; `town Laconia` for Lakes Region Community College (as in Laconia's files); no place for the White Mountains Community College program and the state law fact.
