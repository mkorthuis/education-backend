# Milton Residents' Questions

The questions the Milton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Milton is SAU 64. The Milton School District runs Milton Elementary School (preschool to grade 5) and Nute Middle High School, which holds Nute Junior High (grades 6–8) and Nute High (grades 9–12) in one building. Several questions contain premises (math proficiency around 23%, a graduation rate around 75%, a default budget higher than the proposed one, a feasibility study looking at closing Nute High and tuitioning students out, an open enrollment warrant article) that the research should check rather than assume.
