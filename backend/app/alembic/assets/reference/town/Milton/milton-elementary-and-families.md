# Milton Elementary and Young Families

Part of the Milton research (town/Milton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Milton Elementary School (MES; preschool to grade 5, 8 School Street) for the young-family questions P1, P4, P7, P10, P11.2 and P17: the preschool and what is known about its hours and buses; morning drop-off and dismissal; after-school care; the 2026-27 calendar, early releases and snow make-up days; storm notices and closing rules; bus eligibility, routes and a missed bus; reading instruction, the reading specialist and dyslexia screening; speech and other special education services; free and reduced-price meal applications; how the school reaches families (Apptegy, Rooms, newsletters, paper notices) and whom to email; kindergarten and grade 1 class sizes and the class-size policy; the kindergarten day and entry age; fall 2026 staffing under the district's budget freeze; and walking routes, crosswalks and crossing guards near the school.

**Counts:** 14 topics, 72 facts, 1 number table (`milton_enrollment_by_grade_2024_2025`).

**Main sources:** the MES Family Handbook 2026-27 (rev. August 20, 2026); the SAU 64 Student and Parent Transportation Handbook (2024-25, rev. July 18, 2024); the district's 2026-2027 calendar (approved January 21, 2026); three fall 2026 issues of the MES newsletter, *The Ram Jam*; the FY27 deliberative session handout and the February 7, 2026 deliberative session minutes; the School Board-approved FY27 budget detail (December 17, 2025); SAU 64's job postings (November 21, 2026); the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24; the Strafford Regional Planning Commission's 2025 Milton Sidewalk Audit (a posted draft); the Granite YMCA's school-age child care page; 34 CFR 300.34; an OSRM drive-time estimate; the site's own database. **Added November 22, 2026 from SAU 64's document library** (index in raw/browser/sau64-documents-index.tsv; files from files-backend.assets.thrillshare.com, which has no bot check): board policies JEB, IIB, IKB, EBCD, IC, EEA, EEAB, EEAE-R, IHBA, IHBAB and JLCA; the December 2025 special education and January 2026 MTSS program review summaries; school board minutes from July 2024 to October 2026 (the scanned 2024-25 and 2025-26 sets, 41 documents, were read by OCR on this computer and key passages checked against the page images), including the MES principal's reports, the 2025-26 welcome flyer and the fall 2026 finance hearings; and the 2025-26 bus route sheet (times and street names only).

**Main gaps:** the preschool's hours, schedule and tuition are still not posted anywhere reached; the 2026-27 bus routes are not in the document library; no source mentions after-school care at MES, a homework club, a crossing guard or a speech screening of all students. sau64.org and nhsau64.gov web pages (preschool page, news items, live feed) still return a bot check, and the town site, UNH's copy of the 2025 town report, Alma Start, NH DOE and LINQ Connect returned 403.

**Library gaps filled:** the library's `district-preschool` fact 72 found no Milton preschool details; this file adds the CRDC 2023-24 make-up of the preschool, the district's October counts, its contact and the 2025 review's call to re-examine the preschool program. The library lists Milton's kindergarten day length and entry cutoff as "not found"; this file adds both (`kindergarten-day-length` 1, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff` 1).

**No new topics proposed.** All facts use shared or library slugs. One rule fact on related services (transportation, speech-language) is added under the library's `special-ed-iep-basics`.

**Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 16. P4.2 and P11.2 lean on the library's `school-staffing-and-class-size` 1 and 4; P7.1 on `school-meals-eligibility` 2–9; P17.2 on `special-ed-evaluation-request` 1–5 and Albany's `dyslexia-screening-and-intervention` 1–2; P4.3 on Rochester's `remote-learning-and-snow-day-rules` 1–4; P10.1 on Hampstead's `statewide-online-tutoring` 1–2.
