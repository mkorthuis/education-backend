# Nute High School: Career Programs, Advanced Courses, College and Careers, Sports and the Arts

Part of the Milton research (town/Milton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

- **What's covered:** Nute High School (grades 9–12, in the Nute Middle High School & Library building at 22 Elm Street): access to the Tri-City career centers and the CTE bus; shop, computer, art, music and drama courses; AP, honors, VLACS, Edmentum and Great Bay Early College; extended learning opportunities (ELOs), internships and work-based learning; credit recovery and summer school; counseling, FAFSA and college-application help; graduation pathways; outcomes the school itself reports; sports teams and cooperative teams with Farmington; JROTC options; and the NEASC accreditation review.
- **10 fact topics, 78 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 8 MB): Nute's 2025-26 Program of Studies and Family Handbook (Google Docs linked from the school's home page), the NEASC visiting team report (March 2025), the SAU 64 transportation handbook (2025-26), the school counseling Google site (8 pages), the ELO Google site, the counseling office's CTE guides and the 2026-27 Early College flyer, three Milton School District annual reports (2022, 2024 and 2025, text only, graduates' names removed), VLACS's and Great Bay Community College's own pages, NHIAA 2026-27 handbook alignments (copied from other towns' saved files), federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24 rows for Nute and nearby high schools, and drive routes from Nute to the three career centers.
- **Other agents' raw copies cited:** the FY2027 proposed budget and the February 7, 2026 deliberative session minutes (`raw/money/`), and Milton School Board minutes, the Nute athletics and student activities pages and the November 2026 staff directory (`raw/middle/`).
- **New data concepts:** drive times from Nute to each career center; which career programs Nute students may reach and the "nearest center" rule; the district's vocational bus and tuition budget lines; a count of honors-level courses; Nute's own graduate-outcome figures beside NH DOE's; counts of college applications at ApplyNH! Day and trades-fair attendance; CRDC counts of AP, dual enrollment, computer science, credit recovery and sports at Nute and four nearby high schools.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 17. Earlier files supply the state rules (CTE admission and cost, dual enrollment, NHIAA cooperative teams, youth work hours, registered apprenticeships, UNH's admission expectations, the FAFSA, Granite Edvance, employer surveys) and are named in the question table.
- **Main gaps:** how many Nute students attend each career center and the CTE bus times; which electives actually ran in 2025-26 and 2026-27 (the posted Program of Studies is 2025-26; the board added three electives for 2026-27 and cut one secondary elective position, unnamed); the 2026-27 handbook; roster sizes and whether any team besides volleyball has a JV squad; how many Nute graduates apply to or enter UNH (not published by high school); where students sit while taking VLACS courses during the day; and anything on www.sau64.org beyond the home pages, which began returning a bot check.
- **Proposed new topic:** none; all facts fit shared topics.
