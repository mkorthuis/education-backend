## What you need to know about Conway

- **Conway** is in Carroll County. It is the largest town in the Mount Washington Valley, with villages including Conway Village, North Conway, Center Conway, East Conway, Redstone and Kearsarge; Intervale straddles the Bartlett line. Site town id 113.
- **Conway School District** (site district id 113) is SAU 9's largest district. It votes by official ballot (SB2), with a deliberative session in March. Its schools, with site school ids:

  | School | Grades | Site id | Students, 2024-25 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Pine Tree Primary School | K–2 | 29272 | 210 |
  | Conway Intermediate School | 3–5 | 29271 | 251 |
  | A. Crosby Kennett Middle School (KMS) | 6–8 | 27010 | 308 |
  | Kennett High School (KHS) | 9–12 | 20345 | 641 |

  The Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center (MWVCTC) is at Kennett High.
  - The site also lists the older names Pine Tree Elementary (20330) and Conway Elementary (20340), and John H. Fuller School (20335), which has closed.
  - The schools were reorganized by grade level recently. Check the dates and the Lakeside/Mountainside naming episode yourself.
- **Students from other towns:**
  - Kennett High takes sending-town students from Albany, Bartlett, Eaton, Jackson and others under tuition contracts.
  - Madison and Freedom (SAU 13) students are said to join KMS in grade 7. Check this.
- **Charter schools nearby:** Northeast Woodland Chartered Public School (site school 29231) and Robert Frost Charter School (site schools 29070 and 29256). Check where Robert Frost is and whether it is local at all.
- **Site data:**
  - Cost per pupil was $26,469 in FY2025, against $22,699 statewide.
  - District-wide proficiency for all students was 32% math and 51% reading in 2024-25. The newest results, for 2025-26, are 37% math and 48% reading.
  - The site also has class size, staff counts, salaries, safety, free and reduced meals, English learners and graduation.
- **SAU 9** (https://www.sau9.org/) serves Albany, Bartlett, Chatham, Conway, Eaton, Hart's Location and Jackson. Conway's pages are under https://www.sau9.org/conway-home. School sites follow the pattern khs.sau9.org.
