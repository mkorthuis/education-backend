# Conway Residents' Questions

The questions the Conway research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Conway's schools are Pine Tree Primary School (K–2), Conway Intermediate School (3–5), A. Crosby Kennett Middle School (6–8) and Kennett High School (9–12), where the Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center also sits. John H. Fuller School has closed. Several questions contain premises (figures, names, dates) that the research should check rather than assume.
