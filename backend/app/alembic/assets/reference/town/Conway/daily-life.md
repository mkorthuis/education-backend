# Daily Life for Conway Families: Buses, Care, Meals, Attendance, Devices and Family Communication

Part of the Conway research (town/Conway). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the day-to-day side of school for Conway families: who runs Conway's buses, the 2026-27 routes and derived ride times (North Conway to Pine Tree and Conway Intermediate, East Conway to Kennett, the earliest middle school pickups), late buses, driver hiring and the board's "one bus route" discussion; Project SUCCEED before- and after-school care; school hours at Pine Tree Primary (K–2), Conway Intermediate (3–5) and Kennett Middle (6–8); conferences; snow days, remote days and early releases for young children; attendance and family travel under Conway's revised policy JH; free and reduced-price meals when income drops and summer meals; Chromebooks and home internet; notices, PowerSchool and ParentSquare; interpreters and translation; and enrollment and transportation for children in foster care.

It builds on the Jackson and Bartlett files and `town/sau9/kennett-and-sau9.md`, which already cover SAU 9's storm procedure, Kennett late-bus rules and bell times, SAU 9 meal prices and free and reduced applications, 2026 summer home delivery, ParentSquare's translation features and PowerSchool. Those are named in the question table, not repeated.

- **Main sources:** SAU 9's 2026-27 route sheets; the 2026-27 Pine Tree, Conway Intermediate, Kennett Middle and Kennett High handbooks; Project SUCCEED's website and 2026-27 parent handbook; Conway School Board minutes (July 2024 to November 5, 2026: 60 sets searched, 12 scanned sets read by OCR); Conway policies EEA, JH, JFABE, JICL and IMBA; the 2026 school election results; SAU 9's Conway free and reduced packet; 7 CFR 245; 20 U.S.C. 6311–6312; the US Education and HHS November 2024 foster care guidance and NH DOE's September 2024 guidance (via the Wayback Machine); SchoolSpring job postings; the NH Food Bank; the Conway Public Library.
- **Main findings:**
  - Conway runs its own buses with district-employed drivers on a two-tier schedule: each driver does a Kennett run and then an elementary run, and every elementary run stops at both Pine Tree and Conway Intermediate. Scheduled elementary rides from a route's first listed stop run 28 to 55 minutes in the morning; afternoon runs reach their last stops 25 to 62 minutes after leaving a school (derived). In September 2024 the superintendent reported all rides under one hour, most 40 to 50 minutes.
  - Kennett Middle School starts at 7:35 a.m., not 7:25 (the building opens at 7:25). The earliest morning pickups on routes titled for North Conway or the West Side are 6:21, 6:30 and 6:40 a.m.
  - Project SUCCEED is real and runs 7:30 a.m. to the start of school and 3:15 to 5:15 p.m. at Pine Tree and Conway Intermediate, "when staff is available", and is closed on early release days. Pine Tree's fall 2026 session was full, with a waiting list. It is funded by federal 21st Century grants, an $88,000 warrant article (approved 1,193 to 445 in April 2026), donations and fees; no fee schedule is published.
  - Conway's 2025 revision of policy JH dropped the family-vacation language; vacations now fall under the principal's "other good cause". The Intermediate and Middle school handbooks still ask for a planned-absence form two weeks ahead.
  - SAU 9's 2026-27 Conway meal FAQ tells families who lost a job or had hours cut to "use your current income", and federal rules let a school use projected annual income when one month does not reflect the year.
  - Conway adopted a foster care policy (JFABE) in February 2026; federal guidance encourages enrollment, records and transportation within three business days. No Conway foster care point of contact is named publicly, and SAU 9 is not on NH DOE's 2024-25 list.
- **Main gaps:** Project SUCCEED fees; whether Project SUCCEED or other care runs on PLC Wednesdays; 2026-27 late-bus stops; any route-level bus-delay alert; any hotspot lending; the Conway foster care contact; actual (not scheduled) winter ride times.

## school-meals-eligibility: Free and Reduced-Price School Meals: Who Qualifies

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: NH's school meal requirement and the federal income limits and categorical eligibility for free or reduced-price meals for July 2026–June 2027.

1. Under federal school meal rules, "current income" means the household's income in the month before it applies, but if that month does not reflect the household's yearly rate of income, as with seasonal work, the school uses projected annual income, and it may use the prior year's income as the base when that is accurate.
   - citation: 7 CFR 245.2, definition of "Current income"
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-245.2
   - as_of: 2026-11-13
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "Current income means income, as defined in § 245.6(a), received during the month prior to application. ## foster-care-school-stability: School Stability for Children in Foster Care

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Federal and New Hampshire rules for keeping children in foster care in their school of origin, best-interest decisions, immediate enrollment, records and transportation, and the timelines in federal and state guidance

1. Under the federal Every Student Succeeds Act, a child in foster care stays in the school of origin unless it is decided that this is not in the child's best interest, a decision based on all factors relating to the child's best interest, including the appropriateness of the current school and its distance from the new placement.
   - citation: 20 U.S.C. 6311(g)(1)(E)(i) (ESEA section 1111(g)(1)(E)(i))
   - source: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title20/html/USCODE-2024-title20-chap70-subchapI-partA-subpart1-sec6311.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-13
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "any such child enrolls or remains in such child's school of origin, unless a determination is made that it is not in such child's best interest to attend the school of origin, which decision shall be based on all factors relating to the child's best interest, including consideration of the appropriateness of the current educational setting and the proximity to the school in which the child is enrolled at the time of placement". The provision is written as state plan assurances. Raw copy: raw/daily/foster/usc-20-6311.txt
