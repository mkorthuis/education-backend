# Charter Schools, School Choice and Help for Conway Families

Part of the Conway research (town/Conway). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the choice and family-help side of the Conway residents' questions: getting into Northeast Woodland Chartered Public School, Robert Frost Charter School and how both compare with Pine Tree Primary School's own description, Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs) and local EFA providers, Conway's home education policy after the July 2026 law change, outside evaluations and therapy near Conway, local scholarships for trades and two-year programs, and SAU 9's homeless and foster care contacts.

- **5 topics, 68 facts and 5 tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/community/` (charters, efa, minutes, policies, providers, sau9, scholarships), about 33 MB.
- **It builds on** the Jackson file `community-and-families.md` (Northeast Woodland basics, private schools, child care, the July 2026 home education law) and the Bartlett file `daily-life-and-community.md` (EFA program-manager rules, 31 valley EFA marketplace entries). Those facts are named in the question table, not repeated.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Robert Frost Charter School is local.** It is a K–12 public charter at 110 Main Street in Conway, using a Montessori and project-based model in mixed-age classrooms. Its own charter says 49 students; the site's data shows 36 for 2024-25. It admits on a rolling basis and holds a lottery only when applicants exceed space.
  - **Northeast Woodland's chances can't be computed.** It publishes no applicant or seat counts by grade. It reported a waitlist of 15 for 2025-26, an enrollment cap of 225 and a practical maximum of 193. Its board planned two kindergartens of 13 for 2026-27, said in March 2026 that most grades had waitlists, and runs early lotteries for 15 kindergarten seats in each of 2027-28 and 2028-29. Its March 2026 lottery policy puts staff children ahead of siblings, which the website's lottery page doesn't yet show.
  - **The EFA premise is right.** RSA 194-F:1, VI has had no income limit since June 10, 2025. A cap still applies (12,500 for 2026-27, reached in August 2026), with families at or below 350% of poverty uncapped. The CSF feed showed 23 Conway students eligible for 2026-27, all in a priority group.
  - **Conway's home education policy predates the new law.** Conway's policy is IHBG, recoded from IGBG with no change on July 13, 2026, after the July 1 law change. It still describes participating agencies, notices, portfolios and annual evaluations. Conway's IGBJ is a different policy, on paying for independent educational evaluations.
  - **No homeless liaison or foster contact is named on any public page.** Conway has a homeless policy (JFABD, revised September 28, 2026) and a new foster care policy (JFABE, adopted February 9, 2026). The superintendent names the homeless liaison and the foster care point of contact, but no page names them. NH DOE's 2025-26 statewide liaison list is blocked and not archived. Conway reported 40 students in homeless situations in 2023-24.
  - **Outside therapy and evaluations.** Children Unlimited in Conway runs a children's clinic (speech, occupational and physical therapy, play therapy) that takes insurance and has a sliding fee. MaineHealth Memorial Hospital's speech therapists serve children. Northern Human Services runs early supports for children under 3 from Conway.
  - **Scholarships for trades and two-year programs.** Kennett's standard and non-standard local lists include 18 that are open to, or limited to, trades, two-year or technical students. The NH Charitable Foundation's certificate and two-year programs have an open deadline of December 11, 2026.
- **Main gaps:**
  - the names of SAU 9's homeless liaison and foster care point of contact;
  - Northeast Woodland's 2026-27 applications and seats by grade, and its board minutes after April 2026;
  - Robert Frost's board minutes (the folder is private) and any NH DOE report for it;
  - therapy waitlists (none published);
  - union scholarships.
- **Proposed shared topics:** therapy and evaluation providers, and local scholarships, have no shared topic, so both sit under `town-family-help` here. I propose:
  - `town-health-and-therapy-providers` (local, help): "Outside speech, occupational and physical therapy, evaluations, early supports and mental health providers near a town";
  - `local-scholarships` (local, after_high_school): "Local and regional scholarships for a high school's graduates, including trades and two-year programs."
