# Conway: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Conway, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`; the people are invented. The research is in the reference library's format (`../../additional-data/README.md`) and uses the shared topics in `../shared-topics.md`. It isn't loaded into the Ask yet; see `../recommended-improvements.md`.

Gathered November 13, 2026. This is the third SAU 9 town, after Jackson and Bartlett. Conway is SAU 9's largest district and runs Kennett High, so its Kennett research went into the shared `../sau9/` folder.

## Possible problem in the site's own data

The money agent compared the site's salary-band rows with Conway's 2025-26 salary schedule (annual report p. 42, `raw/money/annual-reports/ar-fy2025-p45.png`). The page image and the database confirm three problems:

- Conway's **BA** band in the site ($40,442–$83,192) is the schedule's **non-degree** column. The bachelor's column runs $42,571–$87,571.
- Conway's **MA** band shows **464 steps**; the schedule has 46.
- Jackson's **BA minimum** shows **$4,600**; the schedule says $46,000. `additional-data/state-issues.md` notes the same figure in NH DOE's own file.

These likely come from NH DOE's source data rather than the site's import code, but that hasn't been checked. They affect the site's salary pages and any Ask answer about starting pay.

## Notable findings

- **The default budget took effect in both 2024 and 2025.** In 2024 the budget failed 859–876, which moved the reorganization and the Fuller closure up to fall 2024. In 2025 it cut 7.6 positions, including the career center's computer science teacher. The budget committee recommended against the operating budget in 2023, 2024 and 2025.
- **The Fuller building:** the board voted 5-1 in January 2026 not to sell it, against an appraisal of about $1.88 million. A housing request for proposals was approved in August 2026, but the committee stalled in October. Any sale or lease triggers a 60-day right of first refusal for charter schools.
- **Kennett's students by town:** in March 2026, 313 of Kennett's 600 students lived in Conway. Madison, Freedom and Tamworth have no 2027 contracts yet; SAU 13 presented a Governor Wentworth contract in November 2026.
- **The renaming:** the board adopted Lakeside and Mountainside 4-3 in June 2024. Voters passed a petition article 1,142–794 in April 2025, and the board restored the old names 5-2 in May 2025.
- **Pay:** starting paraprofessional pay ($19.92 an hour) is above the BLS May 2025 averages for local retail, cashier, waiter, desk clerk and housekeeper jobs (derived). Teachers start at $46,071 under the 2026–29 contract.
