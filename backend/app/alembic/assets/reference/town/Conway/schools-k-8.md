# Conway's Schools Below High School: Pine Tree Primary, Conway Intermediate and Kennett Middle

Part of the Conway research (town/Conway). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Conway School District's own schools below high school: Pine Tree Primary School (K–2, Center Conway), Conway Intermediate School (3–5, Conway Village) and A. Crosby Kennett Middle School (KMS, 6–8, Conway Village). It covers the 2024 regrouping of grades and the closing of John H. Fuller School, the 2022–2024 votes on moving grade 6, the 2024–2025 renaming to Lakeside and Mountainside and the vote to restore the old names, staffing and the 2025-26 and 2026-27 cuts, kindergarten, reading and English learner support, intervention blocks, accelerated math, special education and IEP moves, counseling and behavior support, KMS's grade 6 team and the Madison and Freedom students who join in grade 7, bullying and social-emotional programs, winter recess, and homeschooled students' access.

- **10 fact topics, 101 facts and 1 number table.** All topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; no new topic is proposed. Every fact uses `place: district Conway`. Raw copies are in `raw/schools/` (about 33 MB): 38 Conway School Board minutes (2022–2026, scanned ones read by OCR), the three 2026-27 handbooks, both Title I plans, 8 Conway policies, three school election results (with page images), the Madison and Freedom grades 7–8 tuition contracts (OCR), KMS newsletters and one Conway Daily Sun article.
- **Main sources:** the Pine Tree, Conway Intermediate and KMS 2026-27 handbooks; Conway School Board minutes, July 2022 to the draft of November 5, 2026; the April 2024, 2025 and 2026 school election results; Conway policies JJJ, IHBG, IHB, IMAH, IHBBA, JLDBA, JICK and IGDK; the schools' published Title I plans; SAU 9's Fall 2024 Report Card and Fall 2025 newsletter (already saved in `town/sau9/raw/sau9/`); and the site's class-size data.
- **Premises checked:** grade 6 did move to KMS (September 2024, voted April 17, 2024, after a 2022 vote to move it was rescinded). The 2025-26 staffing reductions were real: the April 2025 budget failed and the board cut four elementary classroom teaching positions in all, two elementary paraprofessionals and KMS's alternative-program teacher, among others. The Lakeside/Mountainside names were adopted June 24, 2024 and dropped by a 5-2 board vote on May 21, 2025, after voters passed a petitioned article 1,142–794; the cost to change back was estimated at $14,450, and no separate cost of the first renaming was reported. Madison and Freedom students do join KMS in grade 7, under 2003 tuition contracts for grades 7–8.
- **Main gaps:**
  - No public record shows whether KMS delivers every IEP's service hours.
  - No record shows how teachers are managing larger classes.
  - No written rule on outdoor recess in cold weather.
  - No gifted program or gifted policy exists.
  - No written procedure for moving an IEP from Conway Intermediate to KMS.
  - No district analysis explains proficiency beyond the statements quoted.
  - Kindergarten is never called "full-day" in writing; the facts show it runs the full school day.
  - Conway's revised policy JJJ does not mention EFA students.
  - No record of which schools relocating families ask about.
