# Danville Residents' Questions

The questions the Danville research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Danville's students attend the Timberlane Regional School District (SAU 106), shared with Atkinson, Plaistow and Sandown. In Danville it runs Danville Elementary School (preschool to grade 5); grades 6–12 attend Timberlane Regional Middle School and High School in Plaistow. Timberlane is researched in `../timberlane/`, `../Atkinson/` and `../Plaistow/`; the files here add Danville's side. Several questions contain premises (special education hiring trouble, under 300 children at Danville Elementary, early bus pickups, the Performing Arts Center's funding, a strong wrestling program, First Student's reliability, a budget committee cutting millions, the other towns outvoting Danville, the SAU office in Plaistow) that the research should check rather than assume.
