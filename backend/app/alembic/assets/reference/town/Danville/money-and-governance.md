# Danville: School Taxes, Timberlane's Budget and Danville's Voice

Part of the Danville research (town/Danville). Agent 4 of 4. Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 53 facts and 6 number tables on Danville's side of the Timberlane Regional School District's money and governance. Much of the district-wide picture (the four-town cost formula, the budget and its drivers, fund balance, the tax caps, the building lease, the SAU) is already in `town/timberlane/timberlane-money-and-governance.md`; this file cites those facts and adds what is Danville's own.
- **Danville's 2026 tax bill.** NH DRA set Danville's 2026 rate at $18.50 per $1,000 on October 30, 2026 (local education $12.93), after a town-wide revaluation that raised assessed values 54.79% on average. The rate fell, but the school tax Danville raises rose 28.4% (to $11,961,362), the largest increase of the four towns, mainly because Timberlane used far less fund balance to cut FY2027 taxes. For a home that rose by the residential average, the 2026 bill is about 23% higher, and because the summer bill is half of last year's total, the December bill carries the whole increase.
- **How Danville's share is figured and where it goes.** DRA's worksheet (Danville's share of Timberlane's costs, less its own adequacy grant and the state education tax kept locally), Danville's share and cost per student since 2017, why the same district increase raises Danville's rate most, what share of a Danville bill goes to schools (about 70% local plus 6% state), the split by school level, and a derived DOE-25 comparison of central office spending (5.1% of current spending, mid-range among similar districts).
- **Danville's votes and voice.** Danville's operating-budget votes since 2010 (its majority matched the district result in 14 of 17 votes), its March 2026 tallies on every Timberlane article (no on all but the budget and one petition), its share of ballots and deliberative-session voters, its two of nine board seats, public comment rules and the move to a Saturday deliberative session.
- **Danville Elementary's building, community use, homes and enrollment.** Built 1963; what the lease, CIP and summer work have done there compared with other schools; the 2026 lead notice; gym fees; Danville home prices; NESDEC's Danville enrollment history and projection.

**Main gaps:** a budget or per-pupil spending figure for Danville Elementary by itself (the district doesn't publish one); a building condition report for Danville Elementary; district-wide results for the March 2026 articles other than the operating budget; Danville's 2025 town report (bot check); teacher turnover data; how much school ratings move Danville home prices (no local study).

**Topic proposal:** none; the shared topics fit.
