# Danville Elementary School and Young Families

Part of the Danville research (town/Danville). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Danville Elementary School (the Timberlane Regional School District's preschool-to-grade-5 school at 23 School Street, Danville) from a young family's side: the half-day preschool and kindergarten options, the Southern District YMCA's before- and after-school program inside the school, class sizes and staffing, how reading help is found and given (i-Ready, MTSS and Title I), advanced learners, bus pickup times and First Student's record, delays and notices, help with the parent portal, a guardian's enrollment papers, and building security at Danville and the Plaistow campus. It reuses the Atkinson, Plaistow and shared Timberlane files, which already hold most district-wide rules, and adds Danville's own side.

- **13 topics, 41 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 49 MB): Danville Elementary's home page, staff directory and admin page; its March, October and November 2026 newsletters (text of all pages, cited pages kept as PDF); the 2026-27 elementary handbook; the district's enrollment, Title I and transportation pages and the Title I brochure and Danville compact; 20 sets of school board, Budget Committee, CIP and Early Childhood Committee minutes (2023–2026) and the November 19, 2026 board packet with NESDEC's Danville report; eight district safety and closing policies, plus IIB; the MTSS handbook; four First Student bus-stop lookups; staff-directory searches for a school resource officer; the Southern District YMCA's child care pages, FAQ and 2024-25 parent handbook; the Colby Memorial Library's pages; and pages 1, 73 and 89 of Danville's 2024 town report.
- **Topics:** nine shared district slugs (`district-preschool`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-transportation`, `district-family-communication`, `district-student-support`, `district-safety-and-security`), the library topic `kindergarten-day-length` (with its own kind and description), and three town slugs (`town-child-care`, `town-library-services`, `town-community-profile`). No new topic is proposed.
- **Tables:** `danville_elementary_grade_sizes_2026_27` (derived class averages), `danville_elementary_bus_stop_times_2026` (bus lookups) and `danville_nesdec_enrollment_pk5_2017_2037`.
- **Places:** `district Timberlane Regional` for Danville Elementary and district facts; `town Danville` for the YMCA program, the Colby Memorial Library and NESDEC's count of Danville children.
- **Main gaps:** the result of the March 2026 tuition-free full-day kindergarten article (Article 14) is still not posted; whether half-day kindergarten is offered at Danville in 2026-27; the time of morning the superintendent decides on delays; why Danville's pickup times are set where they are; Danville's yearly gifted-and-talented report to NH DOE (NH DOE refused automated requests); Danville's 2025 town report (UNH's repository refused automated requests); the YMCA's current monthly rates (its 2026-27 handbook is "coming soon").
- **Spot checks:** district-classrooms-and-staff fact 3 (Danville's 18.5–22.5 class averages), town-child-care facts 2–3 (YMCA rates and weather rules) and the bus table (all four stop times, buses, bell times and road distances) were checked word for word against the raw copies, as was the 11-drill figure in district-safety-and-security fact 8. District-transportation fact 5 rests partly on the November 5, 2026 minutes, still a draft in the November 19 packet.
- **Privacy:** board minutes and directories name residents, staff and students; none are named in the statements, and staff appear in notes only as roles. Outside services used: the district's and school's sites, Google Docs (the handbook), the Southern District YMCA, the Colby Memorial Library, the Town of Danville, UNH's repository, NH DOE and the NH Secretary of State (both refused), and First Student's bus-stop lookup, which was sent only the addresses of two public buildings and two businesses. All requests used the identifier `nhfacts-research` (`webmaster@nhfacts.com`); no personal information was sent.
