# Timberlane Regional Middle and High School From Danville

Part of the Danville research (town/Danville). Gathered November 21, 2026 by research agent 3. Timberlane Regional Middle School (TRMS, grades 6–8) and Timberlane Regional High School (TRHS, grades 9–12) are already researched in `town/timberlane/` (`timberlane-middle-school-and-student-life.md`, the "MS file", and `timberlane-high-school.md`, the "HS file"); this file cites them and adds Danville's side and what they lacked.

## Summary

This file covers what a Danville family meets after Danville Elementary: how many Danville students move up to TRMS and how they are introduced to it, the morning and afternoon buses between Danville and the Plaistow campus and the Danville late bus route, how far Danville is from the Pinkerton and Salem career centers, TRMS's in-school suspension program and the district's recent discipline and bullying-policy steps, federal data on Algebra I at TRMS, TRMS math staffing, when each TRHS AP course and shop course opens to a student, community programs TRHS students join, a college application event, and a grade-by-grade comparison of 2026 test results, growth and family income with Hampstead, Windham and Pinkerton.

- **7 fact topics, 23 facts and 3 number tables.** Every fact uses `place: district Timberlane Regional`. Tables: `danville_trms_trhs_bus_stop_times_2026_27` (district Timberlane Regional), `trms_hampstead_windham_algebra1_crdc_2023_24` and `timberlane_neighbors_test_results_2026` (district Timberlane Regional, district Hampstead, district Windham, district Pinkerton Academy and NH).
- **New data concepts:** Danville's resident students by grade in 2026-27 and its share of the TRMS sixth grade (NESDEC); stop times, bell times and derived ride lengths for Danville's middle and high school buses (First Student's route lookup at two public buildings); the Danville late bus route; road distances from Danville to Pinkerton and Salem (OSRM); TRMS's In-School Support Specialist; the board's extended-suspension authorization; the 2026 bullying-policy stakeholder review; federal Algebra I counts for TRMS, Hampstead Middle and Windham Middle; the grade at which each AP and shop course opens; single-grade test, growth and income comparisons.
- **Main sources:** First Student's Edulog WebQuery; NESDEC's 2026-27 enrollment reports and the draft November 5, 2026 minutes (both in the School Board's November 19, 2026 packet); approved School Board minutes of May 2025, September 4, 2025, March 5, March 19, June 4 and August 13, 2026; the district's late bus routes page; TRHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies; the TRMS and TRHS home pages and the TRMS staff directory; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24 (Algebra I file); OpenStreetMap Nominatim and OSRM; the site's own NH DOE data (test results, free and reduced-price meals, enrollment).
