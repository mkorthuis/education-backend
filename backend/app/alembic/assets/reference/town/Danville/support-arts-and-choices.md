# Special Education, Support, Arts and School Choices for Danville Families

Part of the Danville research (town/Danville). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file adds Danville's side of special education, student support, the arts, sports and school choice in the Timberlane Regional School District (SAU 106). Most district-wide answers were already in the shared Timberlane files (`town/timberlane/`) and the Atkinson and Plaistow files, so the question table cites those first. New here: Danville Elementary's special education, counseling and extra-help staff; the district's elementary special education coordinator and the eliminated Director of Special Education; the superintendent's October 2026 account of which therapists are contracted; the district's 2025-26 compliance on evaluation timelines; the music staff for Danville and the Private Lesson Program's place, hours and price; the high school theater courses and the Timberlane Players; what athletics, activities and the Performing Arts Center (PAC) cost in the general fund; the FY2028 reduction targets; Timberlane's state finals in sports other than wrestling since 2010; school-day costs worked out for meals; and the PAC's rental rates.
