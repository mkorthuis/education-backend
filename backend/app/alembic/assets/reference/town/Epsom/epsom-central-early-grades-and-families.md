Part of the Epsom research (town/Epsom). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Epsom Central School's kindergarten day and registration, the Boys & Girls Club care at the school, the 2026-27 bus routes and morning ride times, bus changes for families with two homes, storm closings and delays, how the school reaches families (Alma, One Call Now, the newsletter), reading and phonics instruction, early reading screening, Title I reading and math help, the math program and recent state test results, how a learning disability evaluation starts, summer reading, class sizes and sections, building security and drills, and preschool and child care options near Epsom.
