# Epsom Central's Upper Grades, Support and Choices

Part of the Epsom research (town/Epsom). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Epsom Central School's middle grades and the support and choices around them: hands-on and STEM learning (there is no shop class), advanced math and VLACS, sports and clubs, getting home after activities (there is no late bus), homework help, counseling, bullying and cyberbullying, special education staffing and whom to contact at SAU 53, homeschooled and EFA students' access, what an EFA or private school choice means for Epsom's state aid, the "Cornerstone Christian Academy on Black Hall Road" premise, nearby private schools, after-school spaces and open enrollment. Sources are the Epsom Central 2026–2027 Student Handbook; the school's site, menu, newsletters (November 2025 to September 2026), athletics, counseling and flag football sites and the 2026 sports schedules; Epsom School Board policies (JJJ, IHBG, JJA, JJA-R, JJI, IHAB-A.M., IMBD, IKB, JICK, JLD, JCA, JFAB, KE, KED, ACE-504, IHBA-R, IFA, JG, EEA); Epsom School Board minutes from October 2025 to November 2026, the February 3, 2026 deliberative session minutes, the 2026 warrant and budget packet and the election results; SAU 53's special education page, staff directory and job board; the Boys & Girls Club, EYAA and Epsom Parks & Recreation pages; GreatSchools, the federal private school search and Epsom Bible Church's site; and the site's own data, from which a drive-time table and an EFA provider count are derived.

**Counts:** 12 topics (all shared slugs), 81 facts and 1 number table (drive times from Epsom Central to nearby private schools, derived).

**New data concepts:** the 2026 middle school sports schedules and league; the bullying policy's off-campus and social media rules; Epsom's newly revised JJJ, which names EFA students; drive times to private K–8 schools; EFA marketplace providers within 10 miles; the 2026 open enrollment article and the pending Supreme Court appeal.

**Main findings:**
- There is no Cornerstone Christian Academy in Epsom today. Directory listings place one at 398 Black Hall Road (Epsom Bible Church) using 2012 federal data; the federal 2023–24 private school search finds no private school in Epsom, and NH DOE's private school counts in the site's data (2012–2026) list none. Epsom Central itself is on Black Hall Road (282). The Cornerstone Christian Academy in the state's data is in Ossipee, about 69 minutes away.
- An EFA is paid from the state education trust fund, not the town's budget. Epsom pays no excess statewide property tax, so a resident student who leaves Epsom Central for an EFA lowers Epsom's adequacy grant (about $4,351 base aid per pupil in FY2027, derived); a child already in a private school or home education doesn't change it.
- Epsom Central offers no shop or technology education class; its grade 6–8 unified arts are PE, health, music, art and digital literacy. Hands-on work comes through science engineering labs, grant-funded VEX IQ robotics teams, three January mini-course days and a summer leathercraft activity.
- There is no late or activity bus: the seven buses run morning and dismissal routes only, and the handbook makes students responsible for rides to and from practices and games. The Boys & Girls Club runs at the school until 5:30 p.m. for grades K–8 (members pay no more than $85 a week).
- The school fields boys' and girls' soccer and basketball, cross country, cheerleading, track, golf, softball and baseball in the Southeast League; the girls' soccer team won the 2026 league championship.
- Policy JJJ (revised July 2026, adopted October 13, 2026) gives home-educated, EFA, charter and private school residents the same access to classes, sports and band as enrolled students.
- Two school counselors (2 FTE, about 189 students each, derived) and a school psychologist serve the school. The August 2026 bullying policy covers off-campus and online conduct that disrupts school, with parents told within 48 hours.
- In August 2026 the principal said the school would need to contract for part-time speech services. SAU 53's November 21, 2026 job board lists no Epsom Central openings.
- Voters didn't make Epsom Central an open enrollment school in March 2026 (the article would also have kept Epsom students from leaving under open enrollment). The superintendent says Epsom students can still attend other districts' open enrollment schools, with Epsom paying up to 80% of tuition. Twelve 7th and 8th graders take algebra or a foreign language through VLACS.

**Main gaps:** whether IEP services start late or are missed (no source reports it); how many students play on each team; whether the 1998 advanced math program still runs; the school results for Articles 2 and 3 (the scanned results are cropped); the Epsom Public Library's teen offerings (blocked); and how families feel about private schools against Epsom Central, which no data measures.
