# Epsom Residents' Questions

The questions the Epsom research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Epsom is in SAU 53. The Epsom School District runs Epsom Central School (kindergarten to grade 8). Its high school students attend Pembroke Academy under an AREA agreement, and may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. The district decides its budget by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (about 380 students, an 850-student Pembroke Academy, attending Coe-Brown instead, low math scores, paying for out-of-town transport, Cornerstone Christian Academy, vouchers costing the town) that the research should check rather than assume.
