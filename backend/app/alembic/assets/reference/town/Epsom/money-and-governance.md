# Epsom: Money, Taxes, Tuition and Governance

Part of the Epsom research (town/Epsom). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 82 facts and 1 number table on the Epsom School District's money and governance.
- **Taxes:** how the FY27 budget became the school tax estimate ($14.13 per $1,000 for the school portions, up $0.97), the 2025 revaluation that cut the rate while the levy rose 17.7% (**derived**, site data), the levy's growth since 2014 against falling resident counts (**derived**), the state aid in the district's revenue estimate, and what the school tax cap means locally. The tax year 2026 rate was not yet set on November 10, 2026.
- **Budget:** the FY27 voted budget ($16,297,889) and its drivers (tuition +$413,071, special education +$233,798), the FY28 proposal ($16,600,452, +1.86%) and its function-by-function shares, special education's size (22.43% of students identified), the SchoolCare assessment that took the 2025 surplus, surplus returned to taxpayers each year since FY2017, the budget vote record (passed 2024, 2025 and 2026; default four of five years before), and where the plain-language budget papers are.
- **SAU 53:** Epsom's share (17.4% for FY27, 15.7% for FY28) and the state's half-pupils, half-valuation formula behind it, with a table for all five districts; Epsom's portion of the top administrators' salaries ($81,091.27 in 2025-26, **derived**); two superintendents; the SAU budget as a separate article from March 2027.
- **The AREA agreement and tuition:** Pembroke Academy as Epsom's "AREA High School" (agreement dated to 1969 at the 2026 deliberative session), the FY28 tuition estimate ($21,159.22, up 14.5% **derived**; 151 students), how tuition is reconciled, the Prospect Mountain open enrollment dispute now before the NH Supreme Court, the failed 2026 open enrollment article, and what HB 1374 did and did not change.
- **Building:** no bonded debt; the 1980s roof section replaced in summer 2026 for $124,000 from surplus; the remaining roof sections' life; $1,049,217.88 in trust funds; propane heat and boiler repairs; no HVAC project or bond found.
- **Transport, staff, board:** special education transport up $145,750 for FY28 (ride sharing, monitors, distance), McKinney-Vento costs; 92.85 staff FTE, para wages ($17.55–$22.53 an hour in 2026-27), $100-a-day substitutes, unfilled para posts, hiring difficulty; public comment rules, live-streaming, and board votes.

**Main sources:** the Epsom School District page and its Google Drive folders on sau.sau53.org: the 2026 deliberative session packet (warrant, DRA MS-27 and default budget forms, line-item budget, revenue and tax impact sheets), the FY27 voted budget, the November 10, 2026 FY28 budget packet, the 2026 deliberative session minutes, school board minutes from March 2024 to November 2026, the 2026 election results, the "Mandatory Report to Voters" (RSA 189:76) posting, the support staff master agreement, SAU 53's 2024 climate survey and Pembroke Academy's 2025 AREA presentation; HB 1374's text from gc.nh.gov; and the site's database, read only.
