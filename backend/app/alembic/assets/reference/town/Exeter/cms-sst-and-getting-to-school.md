# Cooperative Middle School, the Seacoast School of Technology, Getting to School and Home Education

Part of the Exeter research (town/Exeter). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Cooperative Middle School (CMS, grades 6–8 in Stratham, for all six towns of the Exeter Region Cooperative School District), the Seacoast School of Technology (SST, the regional career and technical center on Linden Street in Exeter), buses and late buses, home education and EFA students' access to district programs, and adult and evening training. Facts about CMS, SST and the cooperative's buses are placed `district Exeter Region Cooperative`; SAU-wide documents `sau 16`; the Exeter School District's own home-education policies `district Exeter`; Exeter Adult Education and nearby evening training `town Exeter`; UNH's credit rules have no place.

- **12 fact topics, 92 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 65 MB).
- **Main sources:** SST's website (13 program pages, schedule, sending schools, credentials, college credit, applications), its 2026-27 student handbook, 2026-2028 course guide, 2026-27 calendar and dual-credit list; SST's monthly reports to the cooperative board (September and October 2026: enrollment by sending school, 1,159 industry certifications and 431 dual-enrollment courses in 2025-26); Exeter High's 2026-27 program of studies and bell schedule; CMS pages (daily schedule, teams, Encore, STEAM, experiential education, athletics, counselors, incoming 6th grade, clubs, late buses) and the CMS student/caregiver handbook; the cooperative's policies JJJ, IHBG, JICJ, JICK, ADC, JICH, EEA and EEAE, and the Exeter School District's IHBG and JJJ; cooperative board minutes (September 2025–October 2026) and packets; SAU 16's transportation, closings and First View pages; Exeter Adult Education's pages and its October 2026 board report; Great Bay Community College's Business & Training Center class list; UNH's FY27 advanced standing flyer and registrar transfer-credit page; the 2022 Successful Middle School assessment of CMS; and the 2017-18 federal Civil Rights Data Collection.

- **Premises checked:**
  - "Plumbing or HVAC through SST" (P2.1): **no such program.** SST's 13 programs (Animal & Plant Science, Automotive, Biomedical, Building Construction, Business/Finance/Marketing, Careers in Education, Computer Science, Culinary, Digital Media Arts, Electrical Technologies (new in 2026-27), Engineering, Health Science, Welding) include neither plumbing nor HVAC. The closest are Building Construction, the new Electrical Technologies, and Automotive's vehicle heating and air conditioning unit (2 ASE Heating and Air Conditioning certificates earned in 2025-26).
  - "Certifications ready for an apprenticeship" (P2.2): SST students earned 1,159 industry certifications in 2025-26, 713 of them in Automotive; OSHA 10 (28 across Building Construction and Welding), LNA (15) and ServSafe (33) are among them. None is a plumbing, HVAC or electrical license; state rules still require an apprentice license and thousands of supervised hours.
  - "The bus ride from downtown Exeter to Stratham" (P7.1): CMS is about 3.0 road miles and a 10-minute drive from Exeter's Town Offices on Front Street (**derived**). SAU 16 posts no current route sheets or ride times; the 2025-26 route pages a search engine lists are no longer on its transportation page.
  - "Is there a late bus at CMS?" (P7.2): **yes.** Two late buses leave CMS between 4:10 and 4:15 p.m.; Bus 1 stops at Main Street School, the Tuck Learning Center on Linden Street and the Exeter Senior Citizens Center on Court Street.
  - "Routes combined or canceled for driver shortages" (P9.3): **not found.** The cooperative board's 2025-26 and 2026-27 minutes record no route consolidation, and SST reported in September 2026 that bus schedules from all six sending schools were "running smoothly." No count of late, combined or canceled runs is published.
  - "Annual homeschool paperwork with SAU 16" (P16.2): **out of date.** Since July 1, 2026, New Hampshire requires no yearly filing or evaluation; both districts' policy IHBG (revised September 2026) asks for notice only when a child withdraws or wants to use district programs. SAU 16's posted "Home Education Information" sheet still cites the repealed RSA 193-A:6.
  - "SST evening adult classes" (P20.1): **none posted.** SST's site lists no adult or evening classes. SAU 16's Exeter Adult Education (Tuck Learning Center) runs evening diploma courses, enrichment classes and a ParaPro workforce program built with SST.
  - "Six different towns at CMS" (P3.2): confirmed. In 2025-26 Exeter residents were about 43% of the six towns' public school students in grades 6–8 and 46% of 6th graders (**derived**).
