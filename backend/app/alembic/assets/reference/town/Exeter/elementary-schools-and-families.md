# Exeter's Elementary Schools, the Early Years and Family Support

Part of the Exeter research (town/Exeter). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Exeter School District's preschool (the Exeter Developmental Preschool, EDP), Main Street School (kindergarten to grade 2) and Lincoln Street School (grades 3 to 5): where the preschool is and what it costs, full-day kindergarten and the school day, drop-off and pickup, before- and after-school care and its costs (Great Bay Kids at Main Street, the YMCA at Lincoln Street), the 2026-27 free school meals, reading and math programs and screening, advanced learners, special education staffing and programs, English learners, the schools' racial makeup and belonging work, translation, the PTO, how Exeter's setup compares with Stratham's and Kensington's, and Great Bay Charter School's admission and lottery.

- **15 topics, 97 facts, 2 number tables.** All topics are shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`, plus the library's own `kindergarten-day-length` (with its kind, question type and description). No new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 47 MB; Exeter School Board minutes in `raw/elementary/board/`, policies in `raw/elementary/policies/`, NCES data in `raw/elementary/nces/`).
- **Sources:** the 2026-27 Main Street/EDP and Lincoln Street handbooks; EDP, Main Street, Lincoln Street, Stratham Memorial and Kensington pages; Exeter School Board minutes (September 2025 to October 2026) and the School Meals Advisory Committee's minutes; Exeter policies IHBBA, EFAA, IFA, IHBA and KB; SAU 16's November 2026 enrollment report, food service pages and 2026-27 meal application packet; Great Bay Kids' and the Southern District YMCA's program and tuition pages; Exeter Parks and Recreation's registration site; the town's 2018 Master Plan and School Street project page; the Exeter Historical Society's columns; Phillips Exeter Academy's 2026-27 calendar; Great Bay Charter School's pages and 2027-28 admission calendar; RSA 194-B:2 and 194-B:9; NCES enrollment by race (via the Urban Institute portal); and the site's own data.
- **Main gaps:** any EDP waitlist or deadline; how long Great Bay Kids' and the YMCA's waitlists are, and the YMCA's price (its registration site refused the request); why Exeter splits K–2 and 3–5 (no source says); when English learners are pulled from class; whether ParentSquare translation is on or interpreters are offered for conferences; Exeter's state gifted-and-talented report; whether Stratham and Kensington run full-day kindergarten; Great Bay Charter's applicant and lottery counts; and any measure of how welcome families feel or how responsive the special education team is.
