# Exeter Residents' Questions

The questions the Exeter research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Exeter is in SAU 16. The Exeter School District runs Main Street School (preschool to grade 2) and Lincoln Street Elementary School (grades 3–5). Grades 6–12 attend the Exeter Region Cooperative School District, which Exeter shares with Brentwood, East Kingston, Kensington, Newfields and Stratham: Cooperative Middle School (in Stratham) and Exeter High School, which hosts the Seacoast School of Technology, a regional career and technical center. Both districts vote by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (a 2026 tax rate of $19.54, almost $13 per $1,000 for local schools, Exeter subsidized by or subsidizing the other co-op towns, a 7:30 start at Exeter High, the state capping special education aid increases, annual homeschool paperwork, an open enrollment debate in the co-op, preschool at Main Street School) that the research should check rather than assume.
