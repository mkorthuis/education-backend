# Exeter High School: Courses, Clubs, Counseling and the School Day

Part of the Exeter research (town/Exeter). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Exeter High School (EHS), the Exeter Region Cooperative School District's grades 9–12 school at 1 Blue Hawk Drive: its AP, honors and science courses and course levels (including the plan to merge the Academic and College Prep levels), dual enrollment, online courses and extended learning opportunities (ELOs); scheduling rules for electives and independent study; placing students who return from home education; clubs, robotics and coding; the parent portals (Infinite Campus and Canvas) and records rules; the Commons model, school counselors, social workers, the RISE Center and academic help; college and career planning, SchooLinks and the school profile's college-going and SAT figures; the 7:30 bell schedule; buses, parking and the board's question about the school's intersection; how the school describes itself; and what Phillips Exeter Academy (PEA) opens to the public.
