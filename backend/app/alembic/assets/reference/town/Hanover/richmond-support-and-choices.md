# Richmond Middle School, Student Support and Choices in Hanover

Part of the Hanover research (town/Hanover). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Richmond Middle School (RMS, grades 6–8, Dresden School District) and the support and choices around it: how the move from Ray School works and when Norwich students join; how special education and 504 plans are organized across SAU 70; homeschooled students in Richmond sports and band; Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs) near Hanover; moving from homeschool, or from out of state, into Hanover High School for 9th grade and when 9th-grade courses are chosen; Richmond's and Hanover High's phone policy; supports for students with ADHD or who need a quiet place or a counselor; bullying and the social climate as the school and parents describe it; fees, fee waivers and the 8th-grade trips; getting home after clubs and sports; the parent portals; what guardians need; and the Ray School's counselors.

## school-programs-after-grade-6: Middle and High School Programs a District's Students Attend

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice

1. At Frances C. Richmond Middle School in Hanover (grades 6–8, Dresden School District), the 6th graders are Hanover residents; students from Norwich, Vermont, join them in 7th grade, after finishing grade 6 at Norwich's Marion Cross School, and all students complete 8th grade together.
