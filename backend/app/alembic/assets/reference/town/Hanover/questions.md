# Hanover Residents' Questions

The questions the Hanover research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hanover is in SAU 70, an interstate SAU with Norwich, Vermont. The Hanover School District runs the Bernice A. Ray School (K–5); the Dresden School District, an interstate district of Hanover and Norwich, runs the Frances C. Richmond Middle School (grades 6–8) and Hanover High School (grades 9–12), which take Norwich's students after its Marion Cross School and tuition students from Lyme and other towns. Several questions contain premises (Marion Cross having pre-K while Ray starts at kindergarten, the Dresden assessment climbing every year, Hanover paying more than its share against Norwich, a budget book cutting about 6.7 FTEs, K–2 children needing an adult at the bus stop) that the research should check rather than assume.
