## What you need to know about Derry

- **Derry** is in Rockingham County, between Londonderry, Windham, Hampstead, Chester, Auburn and Hudson. Site town id 131. The town has a town council; the school district is separate.
- **Derry Cooperative School District** (site district id 131) is **SAU 10** (https://www.sau10.nh.gov). It votes on its budget by official ballot (SB2): a deliberative session in February and ballot voting in March (2026: February 7 at West Running Brook, March 10 at Pinkerton's Hackler Gymnasium). Use `district Derry Cooperative` for SAU-level facts too.
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - **Elementary, kindergarten to grade 4:** East Derry Memorial (20370), Ernest P. Barka (28465), Grinnell (20390), South Range (20405).
  - **West Running Brook Intermediate School** (29268, grades 5–6) and **Gilbert H. Hood Middle School** (20420, grades 7–8). West Running Brook was a middle school until 2024; the site's data holds no enrollment for the old West Running Brook Middle School (27065) or Derry Village School (20380), both closed.
  - **Derry Early Education Program (DEEP)** (29156), the preschool, at Hood.
- **Pinkerton Academy** (site district id 999, school 20395) is a private academy in Derry that serves as the high school for Derry and, under tuition contracts in `places.yaml`, Auburn (2013), Candia (2018–2038), Hooksett (2016), Hampstead, Chester (to 2034) and Fremont (2026–2036). Derry's contract dates from 2002. Pinkerton had 2,891 students in 2024-25 in the site's data. Its site is https://www.pinkertonacademy.org.
- **Next Charter School** (site district id 740, school 29105), a charter high school in Derry, had 79 students in 2024-25 (https://nextcharterschool.org).
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - Enrollment: the site's school totals for Derry are wrong before 2024-25, because the closed West Running Brook Middle School has no rows. Use NH DOE's counts by town of residence (the site's town enrollment): 5,297 Derry students in 2013-14, 4,624 in 2023-24 and 4,407 in 2025-26, all grades including Pinkerton. In 2024-25 the district's own schools had 3,009 students.
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Derry Cooperative $21,491 (its K–8 schools; Pinkerton tuition isn't in it); state $22,699; Windham $19,690; Salem $19,752; Londonderry $21,196; Timberlane $23,256; Hampstead $29,374.
  - The site also has every school's enrollment by grade, test scores (with growth where NH DOE reports it), class sizes, staff and salary schedules, free and reduced-price meals, English learners, safety counts, Pinkerton's graduation and plans, licensed child care in the town, EFAs by town, Census estimates, adequacy aid (Derry got about $1.02 million in FY2027 fiscal capacity disparity aid, among the largest amounts), special education aid, tax rates and school taxes. Point to these; don't restate them.
