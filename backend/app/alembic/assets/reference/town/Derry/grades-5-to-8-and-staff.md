# West Running Brook, Hood and Staff: Grades 5 to 8, Support and Staffing

Part of the Derry research (town/Derry). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Derry's two schools for grades 5 to 8, West Running Brook Intermediate School (grades 5–6) and Gilbert H. Hood Middle School (grades 7–8), and district staffing: STEM and computer science, math help and intervention, homeschooled students' access and Derry's home education policy, advanced classes (PACE), devices and the new generative AI policy, paraprofessional and bus driver pay and hiring, counselors, recess and physical activity, and what the district says about teacher hiring. Sources are SAU 10's website, its 2026-27 handbooks, school board policies, curriculum documents, board minutes from August 2025 to October 2026, the education assistants' union contract, the morning announcements both schools post, the district's list of approved software, and First Student's own job posting for Derry.

**Counts:** 9 topics, 75 facts, 3 tables.

**Main findings:**
- **Advanced classes (PACE).** Derry's Program for Acceleration and Curriculum Enhancement offers accelerated math in grades 4–8, taught a grade level above, and accelerated language arts in grades 6–8. Grade 8 PACE math is Algebra I. West Running Brook and Hood each have a PACE math and a PACE language arts teacher. There is no accelerated science track, and no AP or "pre-AP" course at either school. The district hasn't posted how students are chosen for PACE.
- **STEM and coding.** Both schools have computer teachers. West Running Brook also has a STEM teacher, and Hood a technology education teacher. In 2025-26 Hood added a Digital Literacy class. The grades 6–8 computer science curriculum (Computer Science 1 and 2) covers HTML and CSS web pages and programming with loops, conditions and variables. West Running Brook runs an after-school Coding Club, limited to 20 students. SAM Labs kits are being used for STEM in grades K–6 in 2026-27.
- **Math help.** Hood lists a math intervention teacher and has "intervention blocks" in its schedule. The district tests students in NWEA MAP and regroups them every 4 to 6 weeks. Hood runs a free drop-in homework club Monday to Thursday, 2:30–3:00. Title I math tutoring is only at South Range and Grinnell. No district after-school math tutoring for grades 5–6 was found.
- **Homeschooled students.** Hood's athletics page says homeschooled Derry residents in grades 7–8 may play on its teams. Derry's home education policy, IHBG (revised January 2022), still describes the annual evaluation that state law dropped on July 1, 2026. Board minutes through October 13, 2026 don't show it being revised.
- **AI.** The school board adopted policy EHAG, Generative Artificial Intelligence, on October 13, 2026. It doesn't ban AI. Students may use only tools the school or district has approved, for instructional purposes, and must label AI-generated work.
- **Paraprofessionals and drivers.** Under the 2024-2027 contract, education and special education assistants start at $18.10 an hour in 2026-27, and the district pays 25% of single health coverage for those working 27.5 or more hours a week. The schools were still hiring paraprofessionals in March and September 2026. Drivers work for First Student, whose Derry posting offers $27.00–$30.00 an hour. The superintendent reported driver shortages for grades 5–6 athletic buses in September 2026.
