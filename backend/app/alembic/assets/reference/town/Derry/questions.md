# Derry Residents' Questions

The questions the Derry research is meant to answer. Thirty resident profiles, three questions each (90 in all, half again the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Derry is SAU 10. The Derry Cooperative School District runs four elementary schools (East Derry Memorial, Ernest P. Barka, Grinnell and South Range, kindergarten to grade 4), West Running Brook Intermediate School (grades 5–6), Gilbert H. Hood Middle School (grades 7–8) and the Derry Early Education Program (preschool). Its high school students attend Pinkerton Academy, a private academy in Derry that also takes students from Auburn, Candia, Chester, Hampstead, Hooksett and, from 2026-27, Fremont, under tuition contracts. Next Charter School, a charter high school, is in Derry. The district votes on its budget by official ballot (SB2). Several questions contain premises (budget totals, start times, Pinkerton's size, school configurations, programs and homeschool rules) that the research should check rather than assume.
