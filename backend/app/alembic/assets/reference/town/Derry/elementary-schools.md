# Derry's Elementary Schools and the Early Years

Part of the Derry research (town/Derry). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Derry Cooperative School District's four elementary schools (East Derry Memorial, Ernest P. Barka, Grinnell and South Range, kindergarten to grade 4) and its preschool, the Derry Early Education Program (DEEP): getting into DEEP and what it costs, class sizes and reading help, how report cards work, homework and Chromebooks, early release days, clubs, art and music, English learner services, counseling and behavior support, the bullying policy, playgrounds, how a home address decides the school, and how the schools' test results compare with each other and with Windham and Londonderry.

- **11 topics, 80 facts and 3 number tables.** All topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; no new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 23 MB): `docs/` (handbook, policies, calendars, the bond and capital plan summary, the music grant, the October 2026 enrollment report, the teachers' contract and the FY2028 proposed budget, the last two read by OCR), `minutes/` (19 school board minutes, 2025–2026), `pages/` (SAU 10 pages, staff directories and the ESOL site) and `data/` (NH DOE test-result rows and site database extracts).
- **Sources.** Nearly everything comes from SAU 10's own site (sau10.nh.gov), the Google Docs and Drive files it links (the 2026-27 Student & Family Handbook, policies, calendars, board minutes and attachments) and the district's ESOL site. Test results come from NH DOE's public assessment files as copied in the site's backend; meal eligibility and class sizes from the site's database. No news source was needed.
- **Main gaps:** NH DOE's iReport (state school report cards and any improvement designations) could not be reached; the web search budget for the session was used up before this agent could search. Not found anywhere: DEEP's waitlist or number of tuition seats, and its 2026-27 tuition; how many minutes a week elementary students spend in art, music and PE; whether 4th graders take Chromebooks home; current Title I school list on an NH DOE source (the district's pages disagree); the PreK-8 behavior framework document; anything about Barka's fields.
- **Spot-checked** against raw copies: the calendar vote "Vote: Unanimous (7/0)" and "There will now only be two Early Release (ER) days" (`minutes/2026-02-10-board-minutes.txt`); Barka's playground "$150,000 … 4" (`docs/bond-cip-summary-2026-p3.png`); and South Range's October 1, 2026 grade counts 59, 61, 66, 46, 66 (`docs/enrollment-oct-2026-p1.png`). All match.

## district-curriculum-and-assessment: Report Cards, Reading Instruction, Local Tests and Homework in Derry's Elementary Schools

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: The curriculum programs a district uses (math, reading, science), how reading is taught and supported, its local tests, and how teachers fit local content into lessons

1. Since 2024-25 the Derry Cooperative School District reports learning in grades K–8 on a 4-point scale instead of letters: 4 means the student regularly and independently shows understanding and extends it, 3 that the student shows understanding with little or no support, 2 that the student is acquiring the basics with moderate to high support, 1 that the student has difficulty even with high support, and "IE" that there is not enough evidence to score.
   - citation: Derry Cooperative School District, Grading Practices Update 2024-2025 (linked from the Academics – Grading System page)
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lAC8L4iaCGwubiU61EuY9EpnzPBOhbmruDLMzx2FChE/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-11-15
   - place: district Derry Cooperative
   - note: Text: "We have created a consistent 4-point scale to be used for the assessment of academic standards in grades K-8.
