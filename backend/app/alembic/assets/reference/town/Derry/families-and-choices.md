# Families, Support and Choices in Derry

Part of the Derry research (town/Derry). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how the Derry Cooperative School District (SAU 10) reaches families (PowerSchool, ParentSquare, conferences, translation), school meals and meal charges, fees and supply lists, PTAs and gifts, school board meetings and public comment, moving between elementary schools, school nurses, substitute teachers, and Next Charter School as a high school option (admission, its ties to Pinkerton Academy, and rides). Nearly everything comes from the district's own pages: the 2026-27 Student & Family Handbook, school board policies, ten sets of 2026 board minutes, the board's "At a Glance" summaries, staff directories and meal documents. The Next Charter facts come from Next's own site, handbook, enrollment policy, 2023 charter renewal and 2025-26 and 2026-27 board minutes.

**Counts:** 12 topics (all shared slugs), 105 facts and 3 number tables (2026-27 meal prices, speakers during public comment at ten 2026 board meetings, and Next Charter's reported enrollment).

**New data concepts:** meal-charge limits and the district's unpaid meal balances; the board's public comment rules and how many people used them in 2026; Next Charter's entry rules (Derry preference, the 90% rule, grade 9–10 entry) and its partnership with Pinkerton; nurse staffing by school; the federal four-week notice about uncertified teachers in Title I schools.

**Main findings:**
- School meals in Derry are not free for everyone. Lunch costs $3.10 (elementary) or $3.30 (grades 5–8 and Next), breakfast $1.80; free and reduced-price meals need an application each year, and last year's approval ran out in early October 2026. Students may charge up to $25. Unpaid balances reached $24,369.65 in June 2026.
- Each Derry school is "staffed with a full-time registered nurse" (handbook). Directories list one nurse at each elementary school, two at West Running Brook and one at Hood, and Hood added a one-year nurse position in 2026 for high-need students and DEEP.
- Moving between elementary schools takes a written request to the superintendent (by July 1), and for a school inside Derry the only test is whether it has room. East Derry Memorial was the school with the least kindergarten room in 2026, and Grinnell and South Range had the most.
- Public comment comes at the start of each board meeting: 3 minutes a person, 30 minutes in all, and the board doesn't answer on the spot. Between March and October 2026, 0 to 6 people spoke at each meeting.
- Next Charter is a tuition-free public charter high school inside Hood Middle School. Derry residents get admission preference (at least 90% of seats), entry is normally in grade 9 or 10, and enrollment was 85 in October 2026. Its partnership with Pinkerton opens some Pinkerton electives, CTE, athletics and activities. Next doesn't run buses, and Derry's posted bus routes include no Next route.

**Main gaps:**
- Whether Derry teachers hold conferences by phone or video, and whether flyers and permission slips are actually translated.
- The text of Next's agreement (MOU) with Pinkerton, and whether Next students may take Pinkerton AP classes or play varsity sports on the same terms as Pinkerton students.
- Whether Derry students at Next may ride Hood or Pinkerton buses.
- How much PTAs spend on playgrounds and field trips, and field trip costs.
- How often classes have long-term substitutes.
