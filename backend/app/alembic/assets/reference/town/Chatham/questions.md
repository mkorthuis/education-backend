# Chatham Residents' Questions

The questions the Chatham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Chatham runs no schools. Its students attend schools in Fryeburg, Maine: Maine School Administrative District 72 (MSAD 72) for the younger grades and Fryeburg Academy, a private academy that takes town-tuitioned students, for high school. Several questions contain premises (school names, times, programs, figures) that the research should check rather than assume.
