# Chatham Community and Families

Part of the Chatham research (town/Chatham). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the community and family side of the Chatham residents' questions: Chatham's population, ages, income and school-age children; its housing and seasonal homes; town government and emergency services; private child care and preschool in the Fryeburg–Conway area; home education and Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs) for a town whose students attend school in Maine; scholarships for Chatham graduates of a Maine academy; papers for grandparents raising a grandchild; and recreation, music and ski programs near Chatham.

- **10 topics, 74 facts and 6 tables.** On November 14, 2026, facts and table columns that only repeated the Census Bureau's American Community Survey (ACS) summary figures were removed, since the site's Census data now has them for the Chatham district. Raw copies are in `raw/community/` (Census JSON, OPD population reports, town web pages and selectmen minutes, the town's master plan, Maine child care licensing records, the Mulford Trust's tax return, scholarship pages, statutes, the EFA marketplace feed, Pleasant Mountain and Mountain Top Music Center pages, OSRM routes), about 38 MB.
- **Places used:** `town Chatham` for community, housing, emergency services and town programs; `district Chatham` for the district's homeschool and EFA facts; `maine MSAD 72` and `maine Fryeburg Academy` for those schools' rules; no place for general NH or Maine rules.
- **It builds on** the Jackson file `community-and-families.md` (licensed child care in the Conway villages, home education in SAU 9, Fryeburg Academy on the EFA marketplace), the Bartlett file `daily-life-and-community.md` and the Conway file `community-and-choice.md` (EFA rules for curriculum and co-ops, valley EFA providers). Those facts are named in the question table, not repeated.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Chatham has about 340 residents.** The 2020 Census counted 341 and OPD estimates 347 for 2025. The Census counted 45 residents aged 5 to 17 in 2020; Chatham's 2026 district report lists 40 students tuitioned (26 in grades K–8, 14 at Fryeburg Academy).
  - **About a third of homes are seasonal** (81 of 241 in 2020). Income, poverty, home values and other survey estimates are in the site's Census data.
  - **Police, fire and ambulance come from outside the town.** The town plan names the Carroll County Sheriff and State Police Troop E for police, the Saco Valley Fire Department in North Fryeburg, Maine for fire, and Fryeburg Rescue for ambulance. The selectmen's 2025 and 2026 minutes still refer to the sheriff, Saco Valley Fire and Fryeburg Rescue. The town has no noise ordinance and in October 2026 discussed possibly creating a hazard mitigation plan.
  - **The Clarence E. Mulford Trust gives no scholarships to individuals.** Its 2025 tax return says it funds organizations, almost all in Fryeburg and the eight towns next to it. Its largest 2025 grant was $548,511 to Fryeburg Academy.
  - **The Pleasant Mountain locals pass is the ski area's product, not MSAD 72's.** It is for people living in MSAD 72, MSAD 61 or MSAD 17. The 2025-26 town lists did not include Chatham. The 2026-27 student pass requires enrollment in "the local school district".
  - **Maine's homeschool access law and MSAD 72's policy cover Maine residents.** MSAD 72's policy is for home-schooled children who live in MSAD 72 and are in a Maine home instruction program. No page read addresses NH home-educated or EFA students from Chatham.
  - **NH has no caregiver authorization affidavit.** HB 1377 (2026), which would have created one, died in the House. Grandparents use court guardianship, filed electronically in the Circuit Court's Family Division.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Fewer than 400 residents": **correct.** The 2020 Census counted 341 and OPD estimated 347 for 2025. The ACS estimate and its margin are in the site's Census data.
  - "The Clarence E. Mulford Trust" with scholarships for Chatham graduates: **the trust exists** (Fryeburg, tax-exempt since 1965), **but it makes no awards to individuals.** It funds organizations, including Fryeburg Academy.
  - "The Pleasant Mountain Locals Pass through MSAD #72": **the pass is sold by Pleasant Mountain**, for residents and students of MSAD 72, MSAD 61 and MSAD 17. Chatham was not on the 2025-26 town lists.
  - "MSAD #72 sports or band" for Chatham homeschoolers: **Maine law and MSAD 72 policy provide access for home-schooled students who live in MSAD 72.** They say nothing about NH residents.
  - "File our annual NH homeschool notification and portfolio evaluation": **out of date.** Since July 1, 2026, NH requires no notice, portfolio or evaluation, except a declaration when leaving a public school or using public school programs.
  - "NH's caregiver authorization affidavit": **no such NH law.** HB 1377 (2026) would have created one; it died when the session ended.
  - "NH state scholarships (like the NH Charitable Foundation)": **the Charitable Foundation is a private foundation**, open to any NH resident, including students at out-of-state schools. The state's Governor's Scholarship rule ties eligibility to NH residency, not to an NH high school.
- **Main gaps:**
  - posted prices and waitlists for infant, toddler and preschool care in Fryeburg (none published; the Conway programs' rates and wait-list status as listed with NH DHHS are in the site's data);
  - Mountain Top Music Center's lesson prices (shown only in a script-driven registration system);
  - the 2026-27 Pleasant Mountain locals pass price and town list;
  - Chatham's town reports after 2015 (not on the town site; the UNH archive refused downloads);
  - emergency alert systems and cell coverage in Chatham;
  - whether MSAD 72 or Chatham's tuition arrangement lets NH homeschoolers join MSAD 72 activities.
- **Proposed shared topics:**
  - `maine-school-rules` (rule, rules): "Maine laws and Maine Department of Education rules that apply to students attending Maine public schools, such as home instruction, attendance and tuition." It holds the Maine home instruction facts here.
  - `guardianship-of-minors` (rule, rules), for the library: "How New Hampshire courts appoint a guardian for a child, which court division hears it, and what a guardian may decide." The NH guardianship facts sit under `town-family-help` here for now, with no place, as the Conway file did for statewide scholarship facts.
