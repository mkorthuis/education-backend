# Support, Daily Life and Choices in Litchfield

Part of the Litchfield research (town/Litchfield). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

- **Main findings:**
  - **Start times:** LMS's late bell is 7:22 a.m. (buses unload from 7:10–7:15), Campbell's first block starts at 7:35 and Griffin starts at 8:25. LMS and Campbell share eight morning bus routes whose first stops are at 6:40–6:47 a.m.; the same buses then run Griffin's routes, arriving at 8:15. The board combined the middle and high school routes in June 2021, when it lengthened the LMS day; in April 2022 the superintendent put the School Start Time Task Force on hold until a new elementary school was approved, saying the bigger question was synchronizing all three schools.
  - **Home-educated and EFA students:** policy JJJ (September 2026) opens district courses, sports and activities to every resident non-enrolled student, EFA students included, on the same terms as enrolled students. In August 2026 the board was told HB 1817 bars charging EFA students for courses, which matches district practice, and that an EFA student who goes over 50% of instructional time must choose between full enrollment and the EFA. The district counted 78 EFA students in August 2026.
  - **Ratings:** GreatSchools rates Campbell 7 of 10, Bedford High 8 and Hollis-Brookline 9; for all three the rating rests entirely on "College Readiness," built mostly from 2021 federal course data (Campbell: 12% in AP and under 1% in dual enrollment, against 19% and 10% at Bedford) and 2024 graduation rates (Campbell's 99% is the highest of the three). The board reported in August 2026 that U.S. News ranked Campbell 19th in New Hampshire in 2026, up from about 78th of 85 in 2023.
  - **Air conditioning:** the GMS project's architect lists Griffin as having heating only and LMS and Campbell as having heating and cooling; Griffin's 2024 assessment finds cooling only in the portables, the IT room and one classroom. Campbell had cooling failures in summer 2026, and the board spent $190,000 in October 2026 on two failed units.
  - **Security:** Litchfield police staff the schools with a school resource officer (Campbell's handbook lists one); LMS got a secure vestibule in 2020-21, while in May 2025 the superintendent said Griffin lacks one, which the March 2027 project vote would add.
  - **Lunch:** the district runs its own kitchens and reported 50–52% participation in July 2025, lower at the high school.
- **Main gaps:** no measure of FirstView's reliability in Litchfield; Niche's and U.S. News's own pages (blocked); no named interpreter or contracted translation service; no survey of why families choose private schools; no record of how Campbell has treated credits from a private school; no state traffic data (NHDOT blocked automated requests).
