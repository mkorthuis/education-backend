# Litchfield Residents' Questions

The questions the Litchfield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Litchfield is SAU 27, a one-district SAU. The Litchfield School District runs Griffin Memorial School (preschool to grade 4), Litchfield Middle School (grades 5–8) and Campbell High School (grades 9–12). Campbell students may take career programs at Hudson's Palmer Center at Alvirne. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (a 2:50 dismissal, a 7:00 a.m. middle school start, about 340 students at Campbell, Sophomore and Senior Projects, SNHU dual-credit courses, about 60% of Griffin students proficient in math, a low online rating for Campbell, 5:30 board meetings) that the research should check rather than assume.
