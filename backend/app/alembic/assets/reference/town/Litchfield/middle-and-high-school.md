Part of the Litchfield research (town/Litchfield). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Litchfield Middle School (grades 5–8) and Campbell High School (grades 9–12), and Campbell's side of career and technical education (CTE) at Hudson's Wilbur H. Palmer CTE Center at Alvirne. It answers questions about welding and electrical training, shop classes, math and science courses compared with Merrimack and Londonderry, accelerated math, VLACS, the Sophomore and Senior Projects, SNHU and community college credit, school size, sports, late buses, phones, the move to LMS in fifth grade, counselors and bullying, college help, early release, class rank and world languages.
