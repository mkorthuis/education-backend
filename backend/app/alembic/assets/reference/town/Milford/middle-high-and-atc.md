# Milford Middle School, Milford High School and the Applied Technology Center

Part of the Milford research (town/Milford). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Milford Middle School (MMS, grades 6–8), Milford High School (MHS, grades 9–12) and the Applied Technology Center (ATC), the regional career and technical education (CTE) center inside MHS: the ATC's programs, enrollment, credentials, college credit and work-based learning; the CTE renovation bond votes of 2025 and 2026 and what has happened since; MHS graduation rules and how CTE courses count; AP and dual enrollment; counseling, college planning and scholarships; graduates' plans; homeschooled and EFA students at the ATC; phones and bullying rules at MMS; sports; arts and music; and the moves from Heron Pond to MMS and from MMS to MHS.

- **10 fact topics, 90 facts, 4 number tables** (milford_mhs_ap_participation_2022_2026, milford_atc_enrollment_by_program_2025_26, milford_atc_sending_students_2025_26, milford_students_at_other_cte_centers_2025_26). All topics are shared slugs; no new topics proposed.
- **Places:** every fact is `district Milford` (SAU 40 is a one-district SAU). Palmer Center and Nashua Technology Center programs are cited from Hudson's and Nashua's files, not repeated.
- **Sources:** the ATC's pages and its 2026-27 CTE Program of Studies; the MHS 2026-27 Program of Studies, school profile, bell schedule, counseling and scholarship pages (a Google Site); the MMS 2026-27 Family Handbook and school pages; the MHS Athletic Handbook 2025-26 and winter sports information-night slides; the district's FY27 Proposed Budget Book (December 15, 2025); 20 sets of school board minutes (July 2025 to October 2026); the March 2025 and March 2026 school ballot results; the district's CTE renovation website (milfordcterenovation.com) and its presentation; the district's home education page and form; and the site's database (graduates' plans).

## cte-center-programs: Milford's Applied Technology Center: Programs, Enrollment, Credentials and Work-Based Learning

1. Milford High School & Applied Technology Center (ATC) says it is one of 28 official career and technical education centers in New Hampshire, that Milford students and grade 10–12 students from Souhegan and Wilton-Lyndeborough Cooperative may enroll, that admission is open to all MHS students and to applicants from area schools on a first-come, first-served basis with space reserved for out-of-district students in each program, and that homeschool students from local and surrounding towns are welcome to explore its programs.

   - citation: Milford ATC, Program of Studies 2026-2027, program pages
