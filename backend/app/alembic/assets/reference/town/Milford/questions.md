# Milford Residents' Questions

The questions the Milford research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 16, 2026, under the heading "MILTON NH"; every profile describes Milford (the Oval, Jacques Memorial, Heron Pond, SAU #40, Milford High School and its Applied Technology Center), so this is the Milford research. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Milford is SAU 40, a one-district SAU. The Milford School District runs the Milford Early Education Experiences preschool, Jacques Memorial Elementary (kindergarten and grade 1), Heron Pond Elementary (grades 2–5), Milford Middle School (grades 6–8) and Milford High School (grades 9–12) with the Applied Technology Center, a regional career and technical center. Mason sends its grades 6–12 to Milford under a tuition contract. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (a budget over $52 million, more than $22,000 a pupil, about a third of students proficient in math, a 6% tax cap question in November 2026, a CTE renovation bond, a default budget higher than the proposed budget, the Sage School at Bales, enrollment growth or decline) that the research should check rather than assume.
