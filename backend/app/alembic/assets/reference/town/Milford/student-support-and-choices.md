# Special Education, Student Support, School Choices and Safety in Milford

Part of the Milford research (town/Milford). Gathered November 16, 2026.

- **Main findings:**
  - **"The Sage School at Bales" is Project DRIVE.** No district page uses the name "Sage School." The Bales School building at 5 Elm Street holds Project DRIVE (the Milford Alternative Program) and the Office of Student Support Services. Students reach Project DRIVE by referral from a school's student study team or the Director of Special Services, followed by an observation, an intake interview, a visit and a 30-day trial placement; for a student with an IEP, the IEP team (which includes the parents) decides placement. The board plans March 2027 warrant articles to buy 119 West Street for Project DRIVE and the special education offices and to sell Bales, which board members said needs about $8 million of work and is not wheelchair accessible.
  - **One full-time SRO for the whole district.** The 2026-27 police MOU (signed September 14–15, 2026) assigns one full-time school resource officer to the district; a 2026-27 board goal is a March 2027 warrant article for a second SRO. MHS keeps all exterior doors locked during the day, and the district received over $1 million in state security grants in three rounds plus $589,000 in a fourth.
  - **Buses:** the board renewed Butler's Bus Service for 2026-27 through 2028-29 for regular and special education routes. Butler's said it was "aware of staffing concerns." No record of canceled routes was found. The 2026-27 MMS/MHS route sheet lists no late or activity bus; all 13 afternoon runs leave between 2:24 and 2:56 PM, and the MMS Homework Help Club requires students to walk or be picked up.
  - **Spanish-speaking families:** the district uses Talking Points, a texting app that translates messages both ways; the home language survey is printed in English, Spanish and Portuguese; interpreters are promised for IEP meetings and ESOL complaints. One ESOL teacher serves both MMS and MHS, and ESOL is given inside the school day, by pull-out or push-in, never during art, music or PE.
  - **Counseling and mental health:** a social worker in every building (the fourth position was restored after voters added $125,000 in February 2026), a licensed mental health worker at Project DRIVE, MTSS-B behavior staff in every building, and agreements with Greater Nashua Mental Health and Harbor Care. MHS has four counselors for 655 students (about 164 each, derived).
