# Milford's Early Years, Elementary Schools and Daily Life

Part of the Milford research (town/Milford). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Milford children start school and what their days look like through grade 5. It covers how to enroll in the Milford Early Education Experience (MEEE) preschool and what happened to this year's sign-up forms, and full-day kindergarten at Jacques Memorial. It covers class sizes and staffing at Jacques and Heron Pond, why grades K–1 and 2–5 are in separate buildings, the enrichment teacher and gifted policy, and reading and math programs (95% Group phonics, Eureka Math², Acadience screening, Title I). It also covers Chromebooks, school hours at all four schools, two-hour delays, the shared elementary bus runs, meals and meal applications, fees, kindergarten registration paperwork, nurses, and the Boys & Girls Club's before- and after-school programs with their transport from the schools.
