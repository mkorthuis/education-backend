# Gilford: Money, Taxes, Governance and the Superintendent Search

Part of the Gilford research (town/Gilford). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics, 62 facts and 2 number tables on the Gilford School District (SAU 73, a one-district SAU). They cover the 2026-27 (FY2027) budget and what drove it; the budget's growth since 2021-22 while enrollment fell (table); the 2026 votes; the teachers' contract; the district's Mandatory Report to Voters (10 years of cost per pupil, teacher pay and administrator pay, before and after inflation); how the budget turns into taxes; fund balance returned to taxpayers; the Gilford–Gilmanton AREA plan (the tuition formula, bond charge, special education billing, Gilmanton's seats on the board, the 2035 term and phase-out, and the 2025–26 renegotiation); the FY2028 SAU budget hearing; the superintendent search; staffing and enrollment since 2017-18 (table); capital reserves and building projects; and how residents can follow and take part in board meetings and the annual vote.
- **Budget:** $32,185,627 operating budget for 2026-27, up $866,757. The superintendent called it 2.81% (the general fund), or 4.26% counting the separate teacher contract. Voters approved it 684–327 and the contract 733–277 on March 10, 2026.
- **Drivers:** support staff, administrator and non-union raises ($448,322), a new bus contract (+$184,935), K–8 reading curriculum ($100,000) and a middle school technology position ($34,343). Health insurance was budgeted slightly lower. The teacher contract ($485,810) was a separate article.
- **Trend:** the operating budget grew 20.4% from 2021-22 to 2026-27 while October 1 enrollment fell 8.6% (**derived**). Certified staff went from 119 to 116.11 between 2017-18 and 2026-27, while enrollment fell 10.3% (**derived**).
- **Gilmanton:** Gilford budgets $4,471,465 of tuition revenue for 2026-27. The AREA formula charges Gilmanton the high school's net cost per student, set from the budget and then adjusted to actual costs, plus a separate bond charge. Gilmanton's per-student rate for recent years is not posted.
- **Superintendent:** the district is hiring a superintendent to start July 1, 2027 (applications due December 11, 2026). The board approved the job description and timeline on October 5, 2026, and two board members sit on the search committee. No posted source mentions a retirement, or says whether finalists will meet the public.

**Main sources:** SAU 73's Budget Information folders (the 2026-27 proposed budget presentation, MS-27 and warrant, MS-DSB default budget, the deliberative session packets for 2025 and 2026, the 2026 ballot proof and marked results, the Mandatory Report to Voters, and earlier years' budget presentations); school board agendas and minutes from September 2025 to October 2026; the AREA plan and the AREA subcommittee agendas; the 2026–2029 teachers' agreement; the board's 2026-27 goals; the SAU 73 home page (the SAU budget hearing notice and the superintendent hiring poster); SchoolSpring's Gilford listing; Lakes Region Public Access TV; and the site's database.
