# Gilford Elementary School and Young Families

Part of the Gilford research (town/Gilford). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Gilford Elementary School (GES, kindergarten to grade 4) and the daily life of young families: school hours and early arrival, GES Extended Day and the Gilford Youth Center's before- and after-school programs (Rise and Shine, the After School Club), the 2026–27 calendar and early release days, delays and early dismissals, buses, stops and weather notices, ParentSquare and Alma, translation, meal prices and free or reduced-price applications, Gilford Got Lunch, class-size goals, reading instruction and Title I help, special education referral and evaluation timelines, preschool special education, kindergarten entry, English learner policy, and clubs, enrichment and summer programs for elementary children.

16 topics, 83 facts and no number tables. Sources are the GES and SAU 73 websites (SchoolBlocks pages and the Google Drive files they link), the 2026–27 GES Student and Parent Handbook, the 2026–27 calendar, meal price sheet, free and reduced-price application and Extended Day brochure, Gilford School Board policies (IIB, IHBBA, IHBAA, JEB, EEA, EEAB, EEAE-R, EFAA, KB), the Special Education Procedures Plan updated October 1, 2026 (a scanned PDF, read by OCR), the Title I plan and 2026–27 ESEA consolidated application, the 2026–27 bus route sheets, board minutes (November 3, 2025; January 5, April 6, September 14 and October 5, 2026), the Gilford-Gilmanton AREA agreement, the Gilford Youth Center, Gilford Got Lunch, Lakes Region Elite Basketball and Gilford PTA sites, and ParentSquare's own site. Three facts add Gilford to the library's `kindergarten-entry-cutoff`, `kindergarten-day-length` and `kindergarten-registration` topics, with those topics' own kind, question type and description. No new shared topic is proposed.

Main gaps: whether GES kindergarten is full-day (no Gilford page says so outright); actual kindergarten and second-grade class sizes for 2026–27 (NH DOE's enrollment reports refused automated requests, and the GES staff directory loads only by script); 2025–26 meal prices, so the "prices up" premise could not be checked; any winter practice for stops on steep roads; how long evaluations actually take (only the plan's deadlines are published); whether ParentSquare translation is turned on for Gilford families, and whether report cards can be translated; who teaches English to English learners at GES; the Town of Gilford's site (Parks and Recreation, road maintenance), which refused automated requests.

Privacy note for reviewers: the saved copy of the Gilford Got Lunch home page had a photo caption naming students, and the Gilford PTA home page named a raffle winner. Those names were removed from the raw copies (`raw/elementary/ggl-home.*`, `raw/elementary/gilford-pta-home.*`). Facts name roles, not staff, except where a page's quoted text does.
