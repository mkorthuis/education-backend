# Gilford Residents' Questions

The questions the Gilford research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Gilford is SAU 73, a one-district SAU. The Gilford School District runs Gilford Elementary School (K–4), Gilford Middle School (grades 5–8) and Gilford High School (grades 9–12), which also takes Gilmanton's high school students under a tuition contract. Gilford High students may take career programs at the Huot Technical Center in Laconia. Several questions contain premises (the Good Morning Club at the Youth Center, GES Extended Day, a budget up 2.8% with falling enrollment, the superintendent retiring and a search, higher lunch prices, graduation at the Bank of NH Pavilion) that the research should check rather than assume.
