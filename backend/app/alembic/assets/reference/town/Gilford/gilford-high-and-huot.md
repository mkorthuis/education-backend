# Gilford High School and Its Side of the Huot Technical Center

Part of the Gilford research (town/Gilford). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Gilford High School (GHS, grades 9–12, serving Gilford and Gilmanton) on the questions assigned to agent 3: how Gilford students reach the Huot Technical Center in Laconia and the Winnisquam Career Center (WiCC) in Tilton, and what GHS says about credit for them; what changes for a student whose family moves from Gilmanton to Gilford; AP and Early College courses and how they fit a trimester schedule; extended learning opportunities (ELOs), internships and open campus; course selection and graduation credits for new families (including the 26-credit diploma starting with the Class of 2030); counseling and mental health staffing; where the Class of 2026 went and where it graduated; sports teams, cooperative teams, eligibility and costs; performing arts and instruments; and what the record shows about students and the community.

**Counts:** 11 topics, 58 facts and 3 number tables.

**Main sources:** the GHS 2026-27 Program of Studies (approved January 5, 2026), 2026-27 Student and Parent Handbook and 2026-27 School Profile; GHS web pages (counseling, registration, performing arts, clubs, Superfund, health services, ELOs); the GHS "Post High School Planning Virtual Room" slide deck; SAU 73 policies EEA, IHBH, IHCD, IKFG, JJIB and JLD; the district's 2024 Suicide Prevention and Behavioral Health Response Plan, 2025 strategic plan and 2026-27 goals; the 2026-27 Gilford and Gilmanton bus route sheets; the Gilford–Gilmanton AREA agreement; Gilford School Board minutes from September 2025 to October 2026; the NHIAA's 2026-27 handbook by-laws; federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) school-support files for 2017-18 to 2023-24; the GMS 2026-27 handbook (for band); and one Laconia Daily Sun article (graduation).

**Main gaps:** No graduation ticket limit is posted anywhere (P11.3). No GHS athletic participation fee, and no instrument rental or loan rule at GMS or GHS, was found (P17.2, P18.2). No away-game bus return times are published (P17.3). GHS does not post how many credits a Huot or WiCC course earns, how many Gilford students attend Huot, or Huot bus runs other than one morning trip. There are no data on counseling wait times or how counselors spend their time. The 2025 Gilford town report (UNH Scholars) and the town website refused automated requests.

**Topic note:** No new shared topic is proposed. The NHIAA transfer and scholastic rules already in `Keene/middle-high-and-career-center.md` (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 4–5) and the co-op rule in `Epping/support-safety-and-choices.md` (school-sports-rules 5) are pointed to, not rewritten.
