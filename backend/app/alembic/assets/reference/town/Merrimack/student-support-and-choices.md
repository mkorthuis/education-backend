# Student Support, Families and School Choices in Merrimack

Part of the Merrimack research (town/Merrimack). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how the Merrimack School District (SAU 26) serves students with IEPs at Merrimack High School (MHS), its paraeducators, counselors, social workers and psychologist, attendance rules and absences, translation and how the schools reach families (the apps and portals), school meal prices and applications, home-educated and EFA students in district classes, sports and band, open enrollment at MHS, Gate City Charter School for the Arts, moving from Gate City or home education to MHS, buses for charter school students and town youth sports.

- **13 topics, 80 facts, 3 tables.** All 13 topics are shared slugs from `shared-topics.md`: `district-special-education-services`, `district-student-support`, `district-conduct-policies`, `district-family-communication`, `district-meals`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-homeschool-access`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `district-transportation`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `town-school-options-nearby` and `town-recreation-and-summer`. Tables: `merrimack_support_staff_directory_2026`, `merrimack_students_with_ieps_oct1`, `merrimack_race_ethnicity_2024_25`. Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 22 MB): 15 sets of 2026 board minutes, 12 policies and the attendance brochure, the MHS and MMS handbooks, the 53-page staff directory, the October 19, 2026 special education report, the special education procedures plan, the May 2026 Lau Plan, meal forms, the job board, NCES race counts, RSA 194-B:2 and town recreation pages.
- **Places used:** `district Merrimack` (SAU 26, its schools, policies and staff), `district Gate City Charter School For the Arts` (Gate City), `town Merrimack` (Parks and Recreation, the Merrimack Youth Association). No general rules were written; state rules are cited from the library by slug.
- **Main findings:**
  - **IEPs:** 697 Merrimack students had IEPs on October 1, 2026 (22%, against a 20.1% state average). NH DOE rated the district "needs assistance" overall every year from 2022 to 2026, with "meets requirements" on compliance each year. 78% of parents in the state's family survey said schools facilitated their involvement. The district's procedures plan says every teacher who carries out an IEP gets a copy, progress is reported to parents at least as often as report cards, and a requested IEP meeting is held (or refused in writing) within 21 days.
  - **Paraeducators:** the staff directory lists 167 paraeducators (32 at MHS, 41 at Reeds Ferry). On November 15, 2026 the job board had regular paraeducator openings at MHS, JMUES, Reeds Ferry and district-wide, and three long-term substitute para jobs at Reeds Ferry at $12.00 an hour. No source measures unfilled one-on-one hours.
  - **Counselors:** MHS has four caseload counselors (about 253 students each, derived), a director, a social worker, a 504 coordinator and a Student Assistance Program coordinator; Merrimack Middle has two counselors (about 257 each). The directory lists one district-wide school psychologist. The K–8 social worker cut in the 2026-27 budget was brought back in August 2026 by repurposing a vacant position. No source gives wait times.
  - **Open enrollment:** 2026 Article 7 made MHS the only open enrollment school, capped at **5** non-resident students, and set Merrimack students' share at other districts' open enrollment schools at 0%. It passed (unofficial count 2,553 to 1,372, Patch).
  - **Charter students:** Merrimack residents who attend Gate City, which is in Merrimack, are entitled to rides on Merrimack's regular routes (policy EEA, RSA 194-B:2, V) and get absolute admission preference at Gate City (RSA 194-B:2, IV). New policy JJJ (October 5, 2026) opens district classes, sports and band to home-educated, EFA, charter and private school residents on the same terms and fees.
  - **Apps:** the district names PowerSchool, Canvas, Google Classroom, SchoolMessenger, MySchoolBucks, FinalForms, ClassLink and GoGuardian, with PowerSchool text messages added in October 2026; its own pages call both Canvas and Google Classroom its learning system.
- **Main gaps:** Gate City's own site (its domain redirects to an unrelated site), so nothing on its lottery dates, applicants or how many Merrimack applicants get in; how many non-resident students applied to or enrolled at MHS under Article 7; the "paraeducator report" mentioned in April 2026; wait times for counselors; any record of racial incidents at MHS beyond the site's harassment counts; the town's official election results.
