# Merrimack Residents' Questions

The questions the Merrimack research is meant to answer. Thirty resident profiles, three questions each (90 in all, half again the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. The profiles came with first names only. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Merrimack is SAU 26. The Merrimack School District runs three elementary schools for preschool to grade 4 (James Mastricola Elementary, Reeds Ferry and Thorntons Ferry), James Mastricola Upper Elementary School (JMUES, grades 5–6), Merrimack Middle School (grades 7–8) and Merrimack High School. Its preschool is the Merrimack Early Education Program (MEEP). Gate City Charter School for the Arts (K–8) is in Merrimack, and Merrimack students can attend the Nashua Technology Center. The district votes on its budget by official ballot (SB2), with a default budget if the proposed one fails. Several questions contain premises (budget figures, a reinstated teacher, trust and reserve deposits, a health curriculum pilot, state standards, a rating, programs) that the research should check rather than assume.
