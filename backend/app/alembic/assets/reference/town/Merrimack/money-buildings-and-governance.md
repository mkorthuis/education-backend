# Merrimack: Money, Buildings and Governance

Part of the Merrimack research (town/Merrimack). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 6 topics, 62 facts and 10 number tables on how the Merrimack School District (SAU 26) sets, votes on and pays for its budget, what the default budget is and why it was higher than the proposed budget in 2026, what happens to leftover money, the buildings and capital plan, athletics costs, complaints to the board and changes in district leadership.
- **The budget.** The 2026-27 operating budget ($96,917,871, approved 3,101–827 on April 14, 2026) was $46,453 below its default budget ($96,964,324) and $213,425 below 2025-26. The default budget's own calculation, the operating budget and default for every vote from 2021 to 2026 (the default was higher only in 2021 and 2026; voters rejected the budget in 2024 and 2025), what the district cut under the 2024 and 2025 default budgets, and the 21.75 staff positions cut for 2026-27.
- **Costs.** Health insurance up 15.5% (over $2 million) for 2026-27; benefits are 30.9% of the request. The teachers' contract (3.99% a year; salaries said to be $6,000–$10,000 below area districts). Cost per pupil against ten nearby districts and staff against enrollment, 2015 to 2026 (**derived** from the site's data). The district's own inflation-adjusted cost per pupil (up 9.9% in nine years) and average teacher pay (down 11.3%).
- **Taxes and state aid.** How the 2026-27 local school tax was built; the adequacy grant falling $326,994 for 2026-27 and below its 1999-2000 level in dollars; special education aid; how the unassigned fund balance has been used each year (about $4.38 million applied to the December 2026 bill, $1.3 million kept); the 2026 town-wide revaluation and new $15.08 rate ($9.83 local school); Litchfield's 2026 rate; what the default budget would have meant per $1,000 (**derived**); the board's estimate of the new tax cap's effect.
- **Buildings.** No bonds; one $4 million ventilation lease note ($2.66 million left in 2025); roofs paid by yearly warrant articles; the $150,000 roof reserve deposit and deferred special education reserve deposit; the 2024 five-year capital plan (MHS turf field, locker room study, MHS roofs and boilers); the plan cut back during default budgets and the May 2026 six-year plan ($12.5 million through 2031-32, no turf field, fields and air conditioning listed without costs); aging HVAC (Honeywell audit, 2026); the MHS gym floor; athletic fields.
- **Governance.** The Budget Committee's role and what it tried to cut in 2025 and 2026; failed petitions to abolish it or give it the default budget; the board's complaint policies (KE, KEB) and public comment rules (BEDH); superintendent and central office changes from 2023 to 2026.

**Main sources:** SAU 26's finance pages, warrants (2021–2026), DRA default budget and MS-27 forms, budget presentation and hearing handout (2026-27), budget by unit, 2025 audit, 2026 mandatory report to voters, voting results; school board minutes (2024–2026), Budget Committee and Planning & Building Committee minutes (2025–2026); board policies KE, KEB, BEDH; MHS athletics pages; the superintendent's entry report; the Town of Merrimack's 2026 tax rate release, assessing and CIP pages; Litchfield's 2026 tax rate sheet; the site's database (cost per pupil, staff, enrollment, tax rates, adequacy aid, Question 2).
