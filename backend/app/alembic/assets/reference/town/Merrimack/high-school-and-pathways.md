# Merrimack High School and Pathways After High School

Part of the Merrimack research (town/Merrimack). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Merrimack High School (MHS) and the routes from it: its AP, honors, computer science and technology education courses, Early College (Nashua Community College) and SNHU dual enrollment, extended learning opportunities (ELOs) and internships, how Merrimack students reach the Nashua Technology Center (NTC) and the Wilbur H. Palmer Career & Technical Education Center at Alvirne High School in Hudson, the FIRST Robotics team and science clubs, school counseling for college, careers and financial aid, SAT and AP results, the district's Adult Diploma Program, HiSET help and adult education classes, and the shops, labs and equipment the school describes.
