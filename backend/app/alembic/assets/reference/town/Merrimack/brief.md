## What you need to know about Merrimack

- **Merrimack** is a town in Hillsborough County, between Nashua, Amherst, Bedford, Litchfield, Hudson and Londonderry, along the Everett Turnpike and Route 3. Site town id 351. It has a town council; the school district is separate.
- **Merrimack School District** (site district id 351) is **SAU 26** (https://www.sau26.org; school sites such as https://rfs.sau26.org and https://tfs.sau26.org). It votes on its budget by official ballot (SB2), with a default budget if the proposed budget fails; the 2026 deliberative session was March 3, 2026. Use `district Merrimack` for SAU-level facts too. (Merrimack Valley is a different district, around Penacook; don't confuse them.)
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - **Preschool to grade 4:** James Mastricola Elementary (MES, 21020), Reeds Ferry (21015), Thorntons Ferry (21035). The **Merrimack Early Education Program (MEEP)** is the preschool.
  - **James Mastricola Upper Elementary School** (JMUES, 21025, grades 5–6), **Merrimack Middle School** (28375, grades 7–8), **Merrimack High School** (21040, grades 9–12).
- **Nearby schools families use:** Gate City Charter School for the Arts (K–8, in Merrimack; site district name `Gate City Charter School For the Arts`), the Academy for Science and Design (charter, Nashua), Bishop Guertin High School (Nashua), and the Nashua Technology Center for CTE.
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - Enrollment by town of residence (the site's town enrollment): 3,905 Merrimack students in 2013-14, 3,541 in 2023-24 and 3,417 in 2025-26. In 2024-25: Merrimack High 1,049, JMUES 533, Reeds Ferry 504, Thorntons Ferry 504, Merrimack Middle 480, MES 375.
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Merrimack $23,849; state $22,699; Amherst $23,894; Litchfield $21,147; Hudson $19,704; Bedford $19,650; Nashua $18,376.
  - The site also has every school's enrollment by grade, test scores (with growth where NH DOE reports it), class sizes, staff and salary schedules, free and reduced-price meals, English learners, safety counts, graduation and plans, licensed child care in the town, EFAs by town, Question 2 results, Census estimates, adequacy aid, special education aid, tax rates and school taxes (including Bedford's, Amherst's and Litchfield's). Point to these; don't restate them.
