# Hooksett: Money, Tuition Contracts and Governance

Part of the Hooksett research (town/Hooksett). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 83 facts and 7 number tables on how the Hooksett School District (SAU 15) builds, votes on and pays for its budget, what drives it, its staff pay and turnover, health insurance, the new school tax cap, the tax bill, its Pinkerton Academy contract and its five other high school agreements, the history of its high school arrangements (including the 2012 look at building its own high school), SAU 15's budget and cost split, its buildings and bond, the board's meetings and the 2027 vote.

- **Budget.** The 2026-27 operating budget is $45,851,643 (gross, including grants and food service), approved March 10, 2026, after voters rejected the proposed budget four times in eight years. Tuition is 32% of the general fund, salaries 26%, support services 16%, benefits 15% and transportation 8%. The November 17, 2026 draft for 2027-28 proposes $46,653,373.73 in the general fund (up 3.61%).
- **Health insurance.** The superintendent reported a 23% SchoolCare premium increase for 2027-28 on November 17, 2026; the draft budget carries it, and the district is seeking competing quotes. In 2026-27 the plan rate rose 3.75%, after a $553,000 SchoolCare assessment in fall 2025.
- **Tax cap.** Hooksett voters approved Question 2 on November 3, 2026, 4,275 to 2,215. It caps the school district's local property tax levy (not the town's budget) for 2027-28 and 2028-29; overrides need 60% on the March ballot.
- **Pinkerton.** Tuition is figured from Pinkerton's operating costs plus debt for buildings (capital outlay and debt service were $2,443.16 of the $16,923.81 general education cost per student in 2024-25). Hooksett commits to a minimum of 63% of its 8th graders; 115 of 139 in the Class of 2027 chose Pinkerton. The 2027-28 rate was not yet set (budgeted at +4.5%; Pinkerton projected 6–8%).
- **SAU 15.** The SAU's budget is 2.1% of the three districts' budgets. Hooksett's share of SAU salaries (about 58%, **derived**) matches its share of the three districts' students and property value under the state formula (about 58%, **derived**).
- **Votes.** Deliberative session Saturday, January 30, 2027, 2:00 p.m., and ballot vote Tuesday, March 9, 2027, both at Cawley. In 2024 the deliberative session cut the budget $750,000 and reduced a $13.3 million lease to $0.
