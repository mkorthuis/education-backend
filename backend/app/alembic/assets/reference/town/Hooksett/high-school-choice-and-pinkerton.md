# Hooksett's High School Choice and Pinkerton Academy

Part of the Hooksett research (town/Hooksett). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hooksett's side of high school: how the choice works (Pinkerton Academy as the school of record, the 63% rule, the petition schools and their caps), the agreements with Pinkerton, Manchester, Bow, Goffstown, Londonderry and Pembroke, what families may pay, private school tuition, how many Hooksett students attend each school, the Pinkerton buses from Hooksett (times, delays, no late run), Hooksett students with IEPs at Pinkerton and the petition schools, and Hooksett students' own results at Pinkerton: graduation and dropout rates, AP exams, dual enrollment and grade 11 science. Pinkerton's own programs (counselors, the Freshman Academy, CTE, AP, phones, clubs, start time) are in the shared Pinkerton files (`town/pinkerton/`), which this file cites rather than repeats; it adds only Pinkerton facts those files lack (the AP math and science list, the course load rule, culinary and business details, VLACS credit, tryouts and team buses).

- **10 fact topics, 71 facts and 8 number tables.** Places: `district Hooksett` 54, `district Pinkerton Academy` 16, no place 1 (VLACS's own terms). Tables: `hooksett_high_school_enrollment_by_school`, `hooksett_grade8_high_school_choices`, `hooksett_mou_enrollment_caps`, `hooksett_pinkerton_bus_times_2026_27`, `hooksett_pinkerton_graduation_rates`, `hooksett_pinkerton_ap_exam_totals`, `hooksett_pinkerton_ap_math_science_exams`, `hooksett_pinkerton_nhsas_science`.
- **Main sources:** Hooksett School Board policies JCB, JCB-R, JCA, EEA and EE; the Pinkerton agreement (2015, amended 2021), the 2016 Manchester agreement and the four 2023–2028 memorandums (scanned; read by OCR and checked against page images); the Cawley letter and timeline for the class of 2027; the 2026-27 Pinkerton route sheets and storm-delay letter; the 2023–2028 First Student contract; the School Board's September, October and November 2026 packets and minutes, including the Pinkerton data on Hooksett students (graduation, dropouts, AP, dual enrollment, NH SAS science) and the June and October 2026 high school enrollment counts; the board chair's 2025-26 annual report; the FY2027-28 budget of November 17, 2026; the Town and School Reports for FY23-24 and FY25-26; Pinkerton's August 18, 2026 presentation to the Hooksett board, its athletic handbook and winter registration page; VLACS's pages; RSA 193:3, 194:22 and 194:27.
- **New data concepts:** Hooksett high schoolers by school and grade; each 8th-grade class's high school choices; the petition caps per grade; Hooksett-only graduation, dropout, AP, dual enrollment and grade 11 science results at Pinkerton, beside all of Pinkerton; Pinkerton bus pickup, arrival and drop-off times by Hooksett route; the storm-delay rules for Pinkerton riders; what parents may owe at a petition school.

### Premises checked

| Question | Premise | What the sources show |
|---|---|---|
| P8.1 | Hooksett is "locked into Pinkerton for free" | **Partly.** Pinkerton is the school of record and every 8th grader is assigned there, but families may petition for Londonderry, Goffstown, Bow, Pembroke or Manchester's Central, West or Memorial, as long as at least 63% (63.26% in 2026-27) of the class still goes to Pinkerton. The district pays Pinkerton's tuition; at a petition school it pays an amount equal to Pinkerton's rate, and parents owe anything above that and, outside Londonderry's own bus, the transportation (high-school-tuition-options 1–5; district-tuition-contracts 9–11). |
| P5.2, P8.2, P16.3 | A 6:15 bus; a late bus home from Pinkerton | **6:15 is typical; no late bus.** The six Hooksett routes pick up from 5:57 to 6:41 a.m. and reach Pinkerton at 7:00–7:05; the earliest stop is on Smyth Road. Buses leave Pinkerton at 2:00 p.m., with one afternoon run per route and no late or activity run posted (district-transportation 2–4). |
| P8.3, P13.1 | "Over 3,000" or "3,200+" students at Pinkerton | **Over 3,000 is about right; 3,200 is high.** Pinkerton's 2026-27 profile counts 2,988 in grades 9–12 and says it serves about 3,100 (Pinkerton file `pinkerton-academics-and-pathways.md`, high-school-outcomes 1). Hooksett had 478 students there on October 1, 2026 (table hooksett_high_school_enrollment_by_school). |
| P5.3 | Pinkerton against Manchester's vocational programs for the trades | **Both are open in principle; "better" can't be measured.** Hooksett's Manchester agreement covers Central and West (Memorial at Manchester's choice) with the same opportunities as Manchester students, and the Pinkerton agreement exempts students who want vocational training Pinkerton doesn't offer. MST lists HVAC, plumbing, collision repair and aviation manufacturing, which Pinkerton's program list lacks; Pinkerton has welding and animal science, which MST's lacks. No Hooksett student was at MST in June or October 2026 (cte-center-programs 1–3). |
| P7.2 | Whether Hooksett or Pinkerton manages an IEP | **Both, with different jobs.** Pinkerton writes and carries out the IEP through a case coordinator; Hooksett stays the responsible district, sends its representative, approves changes and pays all special education costs and special transportation (district-special-education-services 1–4; Pinkerton file `pinkerton-student-life.md` district-special-education-services 1–4, 8). |
| P19.1 | Hooksett might cover Derryfield or Trinity tuition | **Not for a family's own choice.** Hooksett's policy on schools outside its agreements covers only public schools and academies. A private school can be paid only after a best-interest or hardship reassignment, and even then the private school may bill the parents; transportation is the parents' (high-school-tuition-options 7–10). Education Freedom Accounts are the state's separate route (library `efa-uses-and-requirements` 2). |
| P13.2 | Hooksett would pay for a VLACS class | **No payment is needed:** VLACS courses are free to New Hampshire residents. Pinkerton counts them as "alternative credit," up to three credits, with a counselor's approval (school-programs-after-grade-6 9–11). |
| P6.3 | Dual enrollment "through SNHU or the community colleges" | **Yes, mostly at Pinkerton.** Most college-credit courses are taught at Pinkerton through Manchester Community College; one 2025-26 course was through SNHU. Hooksett's students were counted as enrolled in college-credit courses 164 times in 2025-26 (school-programs-after-grade-6 6–8). |
