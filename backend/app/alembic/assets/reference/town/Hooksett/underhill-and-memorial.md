# Underhill and Memorial Schools and Young Families in Hooksett

Part of the Hooksett research (town/Hooksett). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Hooksett School District's two elementary schools, Fred C. Underhill School (preschool to grade 2) and Hooksett Memorial School (grades 3–5), from a young family's side: the preschool lottery and tuition, kindergarten's school day, buses and bus stops, before- and after-school care, the two schools' hours, storm delays and how families are told, special education staffing, translation, moving within town, returning from home education, class sizes, summer programs, and child care in town.

- **10 topics, 64 facts, no number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 38 MB): the 2026-27 district handbook, the 2026-27 elementary bus route sheets, the 2023–2028 bus contract (scanned; pages 1–9 transcribed), the revised 2026-27 calendar and the K–8 storm letter, three school board packets (August 18, September 15 and November 17, 2026) and two sets of minutes (September 15 and October 27, 2026), the April 2026 kindergarten information night slides, the Hooksett Special Education Policies and Procedures Manual (2021), SAU 15's LAU (English learner) plan (2021), twelve board policies (EBCD, EE, EEA, EEAC, IHBBA, IHBG, IHBG-R, IHCA, IJB, JCA, JECD, JG), the district, Underhill, Memorial and preschool pages, New Morning Schools' and the Granite YMCA's pages, and the SAU 15 job postings list for November 21, 2026.
- **Topics:** nine shared slugs (`district-preschool`, `kindergarten-day-length`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-transportation`, `district-family-communication`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `town-recreation-and-summer`) and `town-child-care`.
- **Places:** `district Hooksett` for the district, its schools, its preschool, its buses and programs run in its buildings (including New Morning's program at Underhill); `town Hooksett` for the YMCA day camp, New Morning's off-site camps and the town's other child care options.
