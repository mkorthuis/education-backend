# Hooksett Residents' Questions

The questions the Hooksett research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hooksett is in SAU 15 with Auburn and Candia. The Hooksett School District runs Fred C. Underhill School (preschool to grade 2), Hooksett Memorial School (grades 3–5) and David R. Cawley Middle School (grades 6–8). Hooksett has no high school; its students attend Pinkerton Academy under a tuition contract or choose among other high schools the district contracts with (Manchester, Bow, Goffstown, Londonderry, Pembroke). The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (half-day kindergarten at Underhill, a 23% health insurance increase, a $45 million school budget, a town tax cap, over 3,000 students at Pinkerton, Hooksett being locked into Pinkerton) that the research should check rather than assume.
