# David R. Cawley Middle School and Home Education in Hooksett

Part of the Hooksett research (town/Hooksett). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers David R. Cawley Middle School (grades 6–8) and Hooksett's rules for home-educated and EFA students: the move from Hooksett Memorial School into 6th grade (Step Up Day, placement, teams, the earlier day, grading and homework); math levels, Algebra I and the Advancing Learning Program (ALPs); phones and bullying; counselors, the school psychologist and the absence of a social worker; Section 504 practice; clubs and sports; the racial and ethnic makeup of Hooksett's schools and the district's nondiscrimination and inclusion statements; STEM and coding at Cawley and Memorial; and the SAU 15 homeschool notice, Hooksett's home education policies and the new access policy JJJ the board adopted on November 17, 2026.
