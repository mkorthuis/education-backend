# Wolfeboro Residents' Questions

The questions the Wolfeboro research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Wolfeboro's students attend the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (SAU 49), shared with Brookfield, Effingham, New Durham, Ossipee and Tuftonboro. In Wolfeboro it runs Carpenter School and Crescent Lake School; Kingswood Regional Middle School and Kingswood Regional High School, both in Wolfeboro, serve the whole district, and the Lakes Region Technology Center at Kingswood offers career programs. Kingswood also takes tuition students from other towns. The district decides its budget by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (switching buildings in 3rd grade, a 900-student high school, new tuition students from SAU 13, bus rides over an hour, low aide pay, petition articles for playgrounds) that the research should check rather than assume.
