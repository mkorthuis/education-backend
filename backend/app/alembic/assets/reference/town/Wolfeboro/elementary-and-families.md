# Carpenter, Crescent Lake and Young Families in Wolfeboro

Part of the Wolfeboro research (town/Wolfeboro). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Wolfeboro's two elementary schools in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49) from a young family's side: Carpenter School (kindergarten to grade 2 since 2025-26) and Crescent Lake School (grades 3 to 6), why third grade moved to Crescent Lake after the May 2025 ceiling collapse at Carpenter and the board's April 2026 vote to keep it there, the planned Carpenter preschool, preschool and after-school care at the Children's Center (Wolfeboro Area Children's Center) and its Magic Moments Preschool at the Lakes Region Technology Center, what each does on snow days, staffing for special education, speech and reading, IEPs when a child moves from Carpenter to Crescent Lake, class size policy, the competency-based (1–4) report card, math and reading support, enrichment for advanced students, volunteering, summer programs, the town's population outlook, and the 2026 petition article that created a playground capital reserve fund.

## special-ed-moving-with-iep: Moving to a New District or State With an IEP

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: What a new school must do when a child with an IEP transfers within New Hampshire or from another state.

1. Under federal rules, a reevaluation of a child with an IEP is done when the school decides the child's needs warrant one or when a parent or teacher asks, no more than once a year unless the parent and school agree otherwise, and at least once every 3 years unless they agree it is unnecessary.
   - citation: 34 CFR 300.303
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.303
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "(1) May occur not more than once a year, unless the parent and the public agency agree otherwise; and (2) Must occur at least once every 3 years, unless the parent and the public agency agree that a reevaluation is unnecessary." A move to another school is not among the triggers listed. Raw: raw/elementary/rules/ecfr-34cfr-300.303.txt
2. Under federal rules, a child with a disability's placement is decided by a group that includes the parents, is set at least once a year, is based on the child's IEP and is as close as possible to home, and the child attends the school he or she would attend without a disability unless the IEP requires another arrangement.
   - citation: 34 CFR 300.116
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.116
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "(b) The child's placement— (1) Is determined at least annually; (2) Is based on the child's IEP; and (3) Is as close as possible to the child's home; (c) Unless the IEP of a child with a disability requires some other arrangement, the child is educated in the school that he or she would attend if nondisabled."
