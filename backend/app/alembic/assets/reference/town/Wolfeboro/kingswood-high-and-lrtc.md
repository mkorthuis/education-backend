# Kingswood Regional High School and the Lakes Region Technology Center

Part of the Wolfeboro research (town/Wolfeboro). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Kingswood Regional High School (KRHS, grades 9–12, site school id 22425) and the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC) beside it in Wolfeboro, both run by the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (site district id 208, SAU 49) for Brookfield, Effingham, New Durham, Ossipee, Tuftonboro and Wolfeboro, with Middleton tuitioned in. It answers a Wolfeboro family's questions about playing sports while taking LRTC classes, LRTC's equipment and money, whether new SAU 13 tuition students will crowd the school or the shops, AP, college credit and the arts, college results, local scholarships, the Arts Center and evening classes, Brewster Academy as a day school, the school's real size, the calendar, and AI and phone rules. Ossipee's research will cite the district-placed facts here.

- **Main findings:**
  - **LRTC classes sit inside the Kingswood day.** LRTC is joined to KRHS by a corridor and uses the same bell schedule (A block 7:25 to D block ending 2:20), so a Kingswood student's LRTC program doesn't overlap after-school practice. Athletes must be in school bell to bell and carry three blocks a quarter; LRTC courses count (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 1–4).
  - **Not crowded so far.** KRHS had 712 students on October 1, 2025 (729 a year earlier; 804 in 2016-17 in the site's data) and its 2026-27 profile says 703, not 900. Tuition students in grades 9–12 fell from about 88 to 84. On October 1, 2026 two Freedom students were at KRHS; the board accepted two Madison students in August 2026; a Tamworth agreement was still being negotiated in October; and an "SAU13 Tuition Agreement" was on the November 2, 2026 agenda, whose minutes weren't posted yet (district-classrooms-and-staff 1–2, district-tuition-contracts 1–2).
  - **LRTC seats:** LRTC had 244 students in 2025-26, 173 of them from Kingswood (up from 147). NH DOE's counts for LRTC have stayed between 229 and 275 since 2014. When a program is oversubscribed, second-year students come first, then grades, attendance and GPA decide, and lab class size is set by the number of workstations. No waitlist counts are published (cte-center-programs 2–4, 6).
  - **AP and college credit:** 7 AP subjects in 2026-27 (AP Literature and AP French are not offered this year) and 15 Early College courses, which are tuition-free when the course counts as STEM and carry a fee otherwise (school-programs-after-grade-6 1–4).
  - **Calendar:** the board voted on December 8, 2025 to revise the 2026-27 calendar, "establishing a pre-Labor Day start date": school starts Tuesday, September 1, three days before the holiday weekend. A 2026 bill to require post-Labor Day starts (SB 403) went to interim study (district-school-day-and-calendar 1–3, school-calendar-rules 1).
  - **AI:** the board adopted a generative AI policy (EHAG) on January 5, 2026 and revised its academic honesty policy (IKL) on November 2, 2026. AI may be used only with approved tools and teacher permission, and AI use must be stated and cited. Phones have been banned bell to bell since August 2025 (district-technology-and-phones 1–6).
  - **Brewster as a day school:** Brewster lists no local-resident tuition rate, but 79% of its day students receive aid (average $28,700), and Lakes Region freshmen can be considered for the need-based Curvey Scholar Program (town-school-options-nearby 1–4).
