# Mont Vernon Residents' Questions

The questions the Mont Vernon research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Mont Vernon's students attend Mont Vernon Village School (kindergarten to grade 6), then Amherst Middle School (grades 7–8) under a tuition contract with Amherst, then Souhegan Cooperative High School in Amherst, all in SAU 39 with its office in Amherst; career programs are at the Milford and Nashua tech centers. Several questions contain premises (four school boards under SAU 39, a 6% SAU administrative cap, one open-enrollment spot at MVVS, Mont Vernon outvoted by Amherst on Souhegan's budget, Souhegan as a Coalition of Essential Schools school, a Spanish class at MVVS that lines up with Amherst's, a robotics team and band at MVVS) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Jenna (34) — Mom of a 4-year-old, just moved from Nashua

- **P1.1** My kid turns five right before the cutoff—is kindergarten at Mont Vernon Village School full-day, and what’s the class size usually like?
- **P1.2** Since MVVS starts at kindergarten, where do most Mont Vernon families send their kids for preschool?
- **P1.3** Do they have before- and after-school care right at the school, or do parents have to figure out a carpool down to Amherst or Milford?

- **P2.3** Does Mont Vernon’s Spanish class in elementary school actually line up with what they teach in 7th grade at Amherst, or do the kids end up behind?
