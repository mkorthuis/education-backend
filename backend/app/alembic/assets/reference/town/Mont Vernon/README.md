# Mont Vernon: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Mont Vernon, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the fourth ToLoad batch, after Amherst's research on the same SAU. Mont Vernon is in SAU 39 (with Amherst and Souhegan):
- **Mont Vernon Village School** (kindergarten to grade 6; 224 students on October 1, 2026), run by the Mont Vernon School District;
- grades 7–8 go to **Amherst Middle School** (grades 5–8, 604 students in October 2026) under a tuition agreement with the Amherst School District (July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2030, with an extension option to 2034);
- grades 9–12 go to **Souhegan High School**, run by the Souhegan Cooperative School District (Amherst and Mont Vernon), with career programs at Nashua and Milford.

Amherst's research (Clark-Wilkins, Amherst Middle, Souhegan, the SAU and Amherst's money) was already done; these files cite it and write Mont Vernon's side.

## Corrections and additions to earlier files

Applied:
- **Amherst `money-and-governance.md`, district-tuition-contracts 1:** it said tuition includes a "$400 per pupil facility charge." The signed agreement says the rental charge begins in the fiscal year after the current long-term debt payments end (Amherst's June 30, 2025 audit shows $765,000 of 2009 renovation bond debt maturing in 2029). The fact and its note now say so.
- **Amherst `raw/elementary/ams-shs-bus-routes-2026-27.txt` and `cw-bus-routes-2026-27.txt`:** the agent found stops with house numbers still in the copy ("307 RT 101", numbered Francestown Turnpike stops). My earlier pass missed them because "RT" and "TPK" weren't in the tool's street-suffix list. The main session removed the numbers (17 and 3 lines) as part of a wider pass; see the final report.

Not applied (additions for Amherst's files):
- **`amherst-middle-school.md`, premises P7.1 and district-curriculum-and-assessment 6:** AMS's spring 2026 results as 65% math and 71% reading (grade 6 math 71%, grade 8 math 61%); the site's database on November 22, 2026 shows 64%, 72% and 60% (grade 6 math 72%), possibly a reload of the state data.
- **`district-special-education-services` 3:** the 2024 manual's named MVVS coordinator and the MVVS page's list differ for two of three names.
- **`district-sau-governance` 1 and Not retrieved:** the SAU 39 board's roster is the three district boards' members; Souhegan's seven seats are 5 Amherst voters and 2 Mont Vernon voters, so "elected by Amherst voters" applies to Amherst's own five seats. The voting weights are in Mont Vernon's file (RSA 194-C:7–8).
- **`district-tax-and-state-aid` 6:** the Souhegan sheet labels the FY27 shares "NHED override"; Mont Vernon's share fell from 16.091% to 15.4464% while its resident students were 16.7% of the cooperative's membership.
- **Amherst's Souhegan file, cte-center-programs 9:** the old Region 16 arrangement dissolved in 2023; a copy of the new Nashua Region 16 agreement in Hollis's raw folder names Souhegan as a sending district (unsigned copy). Souhegan's CTE Supplement and Nashua's file differ on Construction Technology grade levels (grades 10–11 and 11–12 against 11–12 and 12), and Carpentry II's prerequisite looks like a slip.
- **Meal prices:** MVVS's handbook gives breakfast $2.00 and lunch $3.15, the November 20, 2026 newsletter lunch $3.25; Amherst's file has Clark-Wilkins at $1.75 and $3.00.
- **Milford `elementary-schools-and-daily-life.md`, town-recreation-and-summer 4:** the Boys & Girls Club's handbook says transportation for Mont Vernon members is "on a limited basis"; MVVS's newsletter says the Club provides a bus from MVVS for grades K–12; the Club's kindergarten after-care pricing doesn't list Mont Vernon.
