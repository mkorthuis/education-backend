# Mont Vernon Grades 4–6, Amherst Middle School and Choices

Part of the Mont Vernon research (town/Mont Vernon). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the upper grades of Mont Vernon Village School (MVVS, grades 4–6), the move to Amherst Middle School (AMS) for grades 7–8, and the choices around them: how AMS helps Mont Vernon 6th graders get ready, the bus to AMS and the lack of a posted late bus, Spanish, special education staffing and who to ask first, the IEP handoff, homeschooled and EFA students' access to clubs and classes, test scores against Clark-Wilkins, math enrichment and gifted programs, a mid-year transfer, teacher retirements and hiring, and rides home from track and band.

- **13 topics, 58 facts, 5 tables.** All topics are shared (`district-transportation`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-student-support`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-homeschool-access`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `district-school-results-compared`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-budget-and-cost`, `town-school-options-nearby`, `town-recreation-and-summer`). No new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/`.
- **Places used:** `district Mont Vernon` (MVVS, the tuition arrangement, the board and its contract), `district Amherst` (AMS programs and teams), `sau 39` (SAU-wide buses and athletics), `town Mont Vernon` (EFA counts and a Milford teen program).
- **Main findings:**
  - **The ride to AMS is long for the first stops.** On SAU 39's 2026-27 sheets, Mont Vernon's three morning runs take 44 to 52 minutes from the first stop to the school loop (18 to 22 minutes from the last Mont Vernon stop), and the afternoon runs leave the loop at 3:20–3:26 p.m. No late or activity bus is posted. Clubs run 3:45–4:45 p.m.
  - **AMS runs a set of transition steps for Mont Vernon 6th graders.** The Mont Vernon School Board's April and November 2024 minutes list a counselor visit, AMS students visiting MVVS, an evening at AMS with a panel of Mont Vernon 7th graders, a summer "Flight School," a shared Ecology School trip and September one-on-one check-ins. Only 12 of 38 Mont Vernon 7th graders came to Flight School in 2024.
  - **Test scores:** MVVS's 2026 state test results are above Clark-Wilkins' in grades 3 and 4 and well above the state's, with 30–40 students tested per grade, so one student is about 3 points. Mont Vernon's own October 2026 slides show its 7th graders at AMS scored 65% proficient in math and 66% in reading in spring 2026, which is 7 and 3 points below the same year group's results as 6th graders and, in reading, below all AMS 7th graders (77%) (derived). The school board discussed a drop at 7th grade in February 2024.
  - **Special education:** SAU 39's manual says MVVS handles most needs inside classrooms and uses "private out of district providers" when a child needs a self-contained setting. Board minutes show the speech pathologist's retirement for 2025-26 and a 3rd grade teacher's retirement request that was rescinded.
  - **Clubs and homeschoolers:** Policy IHBG-R (2018) lets homeschooled and other non-enrolled children living in Mont Vernon ask the MVVS principal in writing to join clubs, band and chorus. The board approves robotics and other clubs one at a time; band was offered after school only "in some years."
- **Main gaps:**

  - NH DOE's gifted and talented report page refused automated requests (HTTP 403), so MVVS's yearly gifted report wasn't read.
  - No source says how MVVS's weekly Spanish lines up with AMS's 7th grade world language, whether a 6th grader at MVVS may take 7th grade math, or how a mid-year arrival from a Montessori school is placed in reading and math groups.
