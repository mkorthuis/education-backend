# Money and Governance: Mont Vernon's School Budget, the Tuition Deal With Amherst, Its Souhegan Share and SAU 39

Part of the Mont Vernon research (town/Mont Vernon). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

What's covered: the Mont Vernon School District's FY27 budget and what drove it, its last three budget votes and the FY28 drafts, the tax-impact estimates and what lies behind the "jumps", a derived split of what Mont Vernon taxpayers' school money pays for (Mont Vernon Village School, Amherst Middle School tuition, SAU 39 and Souhegan), the new four-year tuition agreement with Amherst (formula, cap, rental charge, exits, votes), Mont Vernon's say at Amherst Middle School, the four boards and how SAU 39's board votes, Mont Vernon's share of and votes on Souhegan's budget, open enrollment at Mont Vernon Village School, enrollment projections and room, reserve funds, debt and the building study, how permanent the arrangement is, and where minutes and budget drafts are posted.

Counts: 10 topics (9 shared slugs and 1 library slug, `gov-sau`), 50 facts, 7 number tables.

Main sources: Mont Vernon's FY27 voter guide, budget hearing, deliberative session minutes and budget workbook, the clerk's results ballots for March 2024 to 2026, the FY28 draft presentations, the 2026 Amherst-Mont Vernon tuition agreement and its FAQ, NESDEC's November 2025 Mont Vernon report, the FY2025 audit, 2024 board and facility committee minutes, SAU 39's board pages and budget folders, the Town of Mont Vernon's website, and RSA 194-C:7 and 194-C:8. ## gov-sau: School Administrative Units (SAUs)

- kind: term
- question_type: governance_money
- description: What an SAU is and what it does for its member districts.

1. When an SAU has more than one district, each district that runs a school has 3 votes on the SAU's joint board plus one more vote for every 16 of its resident pupils enrolled in the SAU's schools (a remainder of 8 or more adds a vote, using last June's average daily membership in residence), the votes of a district's members present are divided equally among them, and the extra weighted votes count only when a majority of the members present and voting demand it.
   - citation: RSA 194-C:7; RSA 194-C:8
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/194-C/194-C-8.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-22
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: RSA 194-C:7: "Every school district maintaining one or more public schools shall be entitled to 3 votes on the joint board of school administrative units, plus additional votes as provided in RSA 194-C:8 ... the total votes per district shall be equally divided among the district's board members present." RSA 194-C:8: "each district shall be entitled to one vote for each 16 pupils residing in that district and enrolled in schools under the administrative unit. A balance of 8 or more students shall entitle that district to an additional vote ... Weighted votes shall only be used upon the demand of a majority of the members of any board present and voting".
