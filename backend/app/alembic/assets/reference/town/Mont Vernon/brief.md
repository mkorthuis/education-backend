## What you need to know about Mont Vernon

- **Mont Vernon** is a town in Hillsborough County north of Milford and west of Amherst, on Route 13, with Purgatory Falls and its village. Site town id 367.
- **The Mont Vernon School District** (site district id 367) is in **SAU 39** (with Amherst and the Souhegan Cooperative; the SAU office is in Amherst) and runs Mont Vernon Village School (MVVS; 21790; kindergarten to grade 6; 234 students in 2024-25). Grades 7–8 go to **Amherst Middle School** under a tuition contract (the places file has it from 2026-07-01 to 2030-06-30, since 2011, approved by voters in March 2026, with an option to extend to 2034). Grades 9–12 go to **Souhegan Cooperative High School** (site district id 493; 26860; Amherst), a cooperative of Amherst and Mont Vernon. Check how costs are split, the number of boards, and the votes.
