# Souhegan High School From Mont Vernon: Career Centers, the Founding Model, AP and College Courses, ELOs, Homeschool Entry and Support

Part of the Mont Vernon research (town/Mont Vernon). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Souhegan Cooperative High School (Amherst) from a Mont Vernon family's side: the automotive and construction programs at the Nashua and Milford career centers, who gets a seat and how far Mont Vernon is from them; sports and graduating on time with a career center course; what "Coalition of Essential Schools" means at Souhegan today; AP, dual enrollment and SAT results beside Hollis-Brookline and Bedford; weighted GPA and VLACS; entering ninth grade from home education; mental health supports, mental health absences and summer credit recovery; ELOs and part-time jobs; graduating early; and the engineering, media and computer classes taught at the school itself. Amherst's research already covers Souhegan in depth (`town/Amherst/souhegan-high-school.md`); this file cites it by topic and fact number and writes only what Mont Vernon's 14 questions needed beyond it.
