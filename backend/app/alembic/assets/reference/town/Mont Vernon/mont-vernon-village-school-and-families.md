# Mont Vernon Village School and Young Families

Part of the Mont Vernon research (town/Mont Vernon). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Mont Vernon Village School (MVVS, kindergarten to grade 6, 1 Kittredge Road) and young families' daily life in Mont Vernon: the school day, the 2026-27 calendar and its early-release days, the three school bus routes and their times and roads, snow delays and closings, kindergarten and class sizes, preschool and child care nearby, before- and after-school care (the YMCA's Y Academy at the school and the Boys & Girls Club of Souhegan Valley), registration papers, specials, homework, recess and bullying, volunteering and Grandparent/Special Friend Day, and robotics, band and clubs.

- **15 topics, 63 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 21 MB): the MVVS 2026-27 family-student handbook; the 2026-27 Mont Vernon School District calendar; the MVVS bus route sheets as a house-number-free text copy; the SAU 39 new student and transportation pages and the Certification of Address; the district's FY27 voter guide, January 2026 budget hearing, two excerpts of the February 2026 deliberative session minutes and the October and November 2026 FY28 budget presentations; the NESDEC enrollment report; the YMCA and Boys & Girls Club pages, flyers and handbooks; the Clark-Wilkins school closing page (which names Mont Vernon); a cleaned excerpt of MVVS's ParentSquare feed; RSA 189:13-a; the 2022 Master Plan; and the site-database queries and drive-time results.
