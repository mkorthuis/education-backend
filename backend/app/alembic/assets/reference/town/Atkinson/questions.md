# Atkinson Residents' Questions

The questions the Atkinson research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Atkinson's public students attend the Timberlane Regional School District, a four-town cooperative of Atkinson, Danville, Plaistow and Sandown (SAU 106 in the site's data; the questions say SAU 55): Atkinson Academy (preschool to grade 5) in Atkinson, then Timberlane Regional Middle School (6–8) and Timberlane Regional High School (9–12) in Plaistow. Timberlane students may take career programs at Salem High's or Pinkerton Academy's CTE centers. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (portable classrooms at Atkinson Academy, Atkinson paying a disproportionate share, enrollment around 3,200, budget cap votes, a $25 million facilities and energy contract, a 360-student Atkinson Academy, about 52% math and 60% reading proficiency, SAU 55, pay-to-play fees, annual homeschool portfolio evaluations) that the research should check rather than assume.
