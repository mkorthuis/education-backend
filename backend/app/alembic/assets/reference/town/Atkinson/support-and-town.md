# Atkinson Families and Town: Meals, Fees, Fields, Home Education, Taxes, Exemptions, the Library and Enrollment

Part of the Atkinson research (town/Atkinson). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Timberlane's 2026-27 meal prices, application and meal-charge rules; what the district's posted policies say about trip, damage and device costs; who schedules and maintains school fields and town fields; home-educated and EFA students' access to Timberlane's classes and activities, and the district's home education policy after the July 2026 law change; Atkinson's 2026 tax rate after the 2026 revaluation and how the bill splits between the town, the county and the schools; Atkinson's elderly, disabled, blind and veterans' exemptions and credits and its tax deferral; what the state's low and moderate income relief is worth in Atkinson; the Kimball Library's early literacy and after-school programs; town recreation; enrollment documents for a guardian; and Timberlane's policies for families whose first language isn't English.

10 topics, 60 facts, no number tables. Sources: the Town of Atkinson's assessing, tax collector, recreation and human services pages, its 2025 annual report and March 2026 election results; the Kimball Library's site and policies; Timberlane Regional School District (SAU 106) policies, its enrollment and food service pages, the 2026-27 meal application, April 2026 school board minutes and the November 5, 2026 Policy Committee packet; and RSA 198:57.

Main gaps: no posted Chromebook insurance or device fee, and no posted rule that free or reduced-price meal approval waives trip or activity fees (Timberlane's middle and high school handbooks weren't found online); no agreement between the town and Timberlane on Atkinson Academy's own fields, and no spring field schedule; no program the Kimball Library runs jointly with Atkinson Academy for early literacy; no survey of how welcome families from other countries feel; NH DRA's relief page and NH DOE's enrollment data pages refused automated requests (HTTP 403).
