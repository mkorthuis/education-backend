## What you need to know

- **Atkinson** is a town in Rockingham County on the Massachusetts line (Haverhill), along Routes 111 and 121, with Big Island Pond and the Atkinson Resort & Country Club. Site town id 27.
- **The Timberlane Regional School District** (site district id 534) is a cooperative of four towns: Atkinson, Danville, Plaistow and Sandown. The site's data put it in **SAU 106**; residents still call it SAU 55, and Hampstead is SAU 55 in the site's data. Check whether and when Timberlane became its own SAU (premise).
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - Atkinson Academy (22740): preschool to grade 5 in Atkinson; 364 students in 2024-25 (the site labels it 2025).
  - Pollard Elementary (22765, Plaistow): preschool to grade 5; 434 students.
  - Danville Elementary (22745); Sandown Central (22775, preschool and K) and Sandown North (27910, grades 1–5).
  - Timberlane Regional Middle School (22760): grades 6–8, in Plaistow; 723 students.
  - Timberlane Regional High School (22770): grades 9–12, in Plaistow, with the Performing Arts Center; 932 students.
  - In total, about 3,212 students in 2025-26.
- **Career and technical education:** Timberlane students may apply to Salem High's CTE center and to some Pinkerton Academy programs. Both are researched (see below); write only Timberlane's side.
- **Starting pages:** the district and SAU, https://www.timberlane.net (schools, board, budget committee, budgets and warrants, policies, calendar, athletics); the town, https://www.town-atkinson.nh.us (tax rate, assessing, exemptions, recreation, the library).
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - Atkinson's resident public students: 889 (2014-15), 751 (2019-20), 703 (2026-27).
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Timberlane $23,256; state $22,699.
  - Atkinson's 2025 tax rates per $1,000 (NH DRA): total $12.82, local school $7.95, state education $1.33, municipal $2.84, equalization ratio 74%.
  - Question 2 (November 2026): Atkinson 67.0% yes. Across the district, 72.0% yes, so the tax cap is adopted for Timberlane (unofficial).
  - Math proficiency, 2026: Timberlane 42% (district), against 66% in Windham, 50% in Hampstead and 44% statewide. P17.2 says Atkinson Academy is "around 52% math and 60% reading"; the site has school-level scores.
  - The site also has every school's enrollment by grade, test scores, class sizes, staff and salary schedules, meals, English learners, safety, graduation and plans, child care, EFAs by town, Census estimates, adequacy aid, special education aid, tax rates and school taxes. Point to these; don't restate them.
