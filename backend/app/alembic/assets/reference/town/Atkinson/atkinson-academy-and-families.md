# Atkinson Academy and Young Families

Part of the Atkinson research (town/Atkinson). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Atkinson Academy, the Timberlane Regional School District's preschool to grade 5 school in Atkinson, from a family's side: its staff and specialists, reading support and the interventionist, special education (including the contracted autism program), the multiage preschool, full-day and half-day kindergarten and the 2025 and 2026 ballot articles, arrival, dismissal, PickUp Patrol and bus changes, storm closings and how families are told, counseling, social work and English learner services, unified arts and music, the 1803 building and its capital projects, how Atkinson children are assigned to Atkinson Academy, test results compared with Windham and Hampstead, and the enrollment outlook for Atkinson.

- **14 topics, 79 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 43 MB): the 2026-27 elementary Parent & Student Handbook (a Google Doc linked from the district's Canva handbook) and the district handbook, the Atkinson Academy home page and staff directory, the MTSS handbook, the multiage early childhood flyer, Early Childhood Committee minutes, the 2026 warrant, the 2025 and 2026 election results, the Articles of Agreement, 22 sets of school board minutes and two board packets (2024–2026), the 2025 and 2026 deliberative session minutes, CIP documents, 10 district policies, the posted 2013 special education manual, the elementary music page, and CSV extracts of the site's test data.
- **Topics:** twelve shared district slugs (`district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-preschool`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-transportation`, `district-family-communication`, `district-student-support`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-facilities-and-capital`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `district-school-results-compared`), one town slug (`town-community-profile`) and one library topic with its own kind and description (`kindergarten-day-length`). No new topic is proposed.
- **Places:** `district Timberlane Regional` for Atkinson Academy and the district; `town Atkinson` for the NESDEC enrollment report on Atkinson's resident students.
- **Main sources:** aa.timberlane.net and timberlane.net pages and their linked PDFs, Google Docs and Canva pages; board, CIP and Early Childhood Committee minutes read through the district's own document listings; and the site's database (test scores, child care, staff counts, English learners), used for one derived comparison table and to point to what the Ask already has.
- **Main gaps:** the result of the March 2026 full-day kindergarten article (Article 14) is not in the district's posted results; no source mentions portable classrooms, shared art or counseling rooms, music rooms or carts at Atkinson Academy; no preschool lottery or peer-model tuition is published; no source says whether the library media specialist is full-time; Atkinson Academy has no published before- or after-school program of its own (the site's child care data lists a licensed program at its address); no school summer reading or math program for elementary students was found.
