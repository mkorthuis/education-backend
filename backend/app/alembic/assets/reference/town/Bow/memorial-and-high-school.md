# Bow Memorial School and Bow High School: Academics, the CRTC and Life After High School

Part of the Bow research (town/Bow). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Bow Memorial School (BMS, grades 5–8) and Bow High School (BHS, grades 9–12): Bow's side of the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC) and CRTC+, the morning bus to the CRTC and what Bow budgets for it; how CRTC courses, internships and other outside learning count toward a Bow diploma; local employer and community partnerships; math results and what the schools are doing about math; middle school math and the step from 8th to 9th grade; AP, honors, Early College and online courses; counselors and college planning; world languages and enrichment; NCAA eligibility help; junior and senior campus privileges and attendance rules; graduates' plans; and how dual-enrollment credit moves to NHTI.

- **13 fact topics, 72 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 14 MB): the BHS 2026-27 Program of Studies (printable PDF, and the linked Google Doc version with the Class of 2030 requirements), the 2026-27 school profile, the CRTC's 2026-27 dates for Bow, the Alternative Learning Options, Flex Time, campus privileges, attendance and junior college-planning documents, the 2026-27 bell schedule and odd/even calendar, BHS counseling, graduation, After Bow High, career exploration, scheduling and athletics pages, the 2026 athletic handbook, the BMS 2026-27 handbook, counseling, clubs and grade 6 and 8 welcome letters, the BES 2026-27 handbook (for world languages), the SAU's Bow bus routes and the Bus 12 route sheet, SAU 67's Future Ready page, Bow School Board minutes (September 2025, March, May and November 2026), excerpts of the June, October and November 2026 board packets (the NEASC accreditation report, principals' reports, the FY2026 expense report), the FY2026-27 proposed budget, two CRTC pages and an excerpt of the NCAA's 2026-27 guide.
- **Places:** 66 facts use `district Bow`; 6 have no place (NHTI's transfer rules, CCSNH's Early College grades, NCAA rules). No facts were written for Dunbarton, Concord or the SAU.
- **New data concepts:** the morning bus run from Bow to the CRTC; Bow's CTE tuition and CTE transportation budget lines, FY2025–FY2027; the CRTC's 2026-27 calendar as Bow posts it; BHS's dual-enrollment course list with fees, and participation and credits by year; AP exam results; school-day SAT averages against the state's proficiency cut scores (**derived**); the end of a gifted-and-talented teacher line in Bow's budget; BHS's 2026 NEASC accreditation findings; the junior and senior campus privilege rules (no off-campus lunch from 2026-27); NHTI's transfer-credit rules; CCSNH's share of Early College grades of C or better; NCAA initial-eligibility rules for 2026-27 set against the older rules still printed in BHS's Program of Studies.

## ncaa-initial-eligibility: NCAA Initial-Eligibility Rules for College Athletes

- kind: how_it_works
- question_type: after_high_school
- description: NCAA initial-eligibility standards for college athletes: core courses, core-course GPA, the Division I 10/7 rule, the end of the test-score requirement and the Eligibility Center (proposed from the Bow research)

1. The NCAA's 2026-27 Guide for the College-Bound Student-Athlete says NCAA Divisions I and II removed standardized test scores from their initial-eligibility requirements in January 2023, and tells students to check with each college whether it uses test scores for admission or scholarships.
   - citation: NCAA Eligibility Center, 2026-27 Guide for the College-Bound Student-Athlete, "Test Scores," p. 12
   - source: http://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/CBSA.pdf
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - note: Text: "In January 2023, NCAA Divisions I and II adopted legislation to remove standardized test scores from initial-eligibility requirements. Check with the NCAA school you plan to attend regarding whether standardized test scores are necessary for admissions or scholarship requirements." The NCAA's legislative database page refused automated requests (Not retrieved). Raw: raw/high/ncaa-guide-college-bound-2026-27-excerpt.pdf (.txt, source page 14)
2. To practice, compete and receive an athletics scholarship in the first year, the NCAA's 2026-27 guide requires 16 NCAA-approved core-course credits for both Divisions I and II, a minimum core-course GPA of 2.3 for Division I and 2.2 for Division II, and, for Division I only, the "10/7" rule: 10 core courses, seven of them in English, math and science, completed before the seventh semester of high school.
   - citation: NCAA, 2026-27 Guide for the College-Bound Student-Athlete, "Division I Academic Standards" (p. 14) and "Division II Academic Standards" (p. 16)
   - source: http://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/CBSA.pdf
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - note: Division I: "Complete 16 NCAA-approved core-course credits in four consecutive academic years (eight semesters) from your initial start of ninth grade"; "Meet the 10/7 requirement by completing 10 of your 16 NCAA-approved core-course credits, including seven in English, math and science, before the start of the seventh semester"; "Earn a minimum 2.3 core-course GPA." Division II: "Earn a minimum 2.2 core-course GPA." Division III schools set their own standards (not covered here). Raw: raw/high/ncaa-guide-college-bound-2026-27-excerpt.txt (source pages 16, 18)
3. The NCAA's guide tells students to register with the Eligibility Center, check their high school's list of NCAA-approved courses, and ask their high school counselor to upload transcripts, including the final transcript with proof of graduation.
   - citation: NCAA, 2026-27 Guide for the College-Bound Student-Athlete, pp. 14, 16
   - source: http://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/CBSA.pdf
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - note: Text: "Register for an Academic and Athletics Certification account"; "Ask your high school counselor to upload your final official high school transcript with proof of graduation to your Eligibility Center account"; "Receive academic and athletics certifications from the Eligibility Center." Bow High's marking of approved courses is in district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 1. Raw: raw/high/ncaa-guide-college-bound-2026-27-excerpt.txt (source pages 16, 18)
