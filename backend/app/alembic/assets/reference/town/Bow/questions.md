# Bow Residents' Questions

The questions the Bow research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Bow is in SAU 67 with Dunbarton. The Bow School District runs a preschool at Bow High School, Bow Elementary School (kindergarten to grade 4), Bow Memorial School (grades 5–8) and Bow High School (grades 9–12); Dunbarton sends its high school students to Bow under a tuition contract. Bow High students may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting. Several questions contain premises (math proficiency in the low 40s at Bow High, CRTC+ classes, a Bow Rec Center before- and after-school program, a special education preschool at Bow High, a building bond coming at Bow Elementary) that the research should check rather than assume.
