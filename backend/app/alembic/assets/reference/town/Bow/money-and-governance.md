# Bow: Money, Taxes, the Dunbarton AREA Agreement and SAU 67

Part of the Bow research (town/Bow). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics, 62 facts and 3 number tables on the Bow School District's money and governance, its AREA (tuition) agreement with Dunbarton, and SAU 67.
- **Budget:** the FY27 (2026-27) budget voters approved on March 13, 2026 ($40,850,771), what it buys by function and object (47% salaries, 27% benefits), why it rose (health insurance about 20%), the district's own cost-per-pupil comparisons, the teachers' contract and its insurance terms, and staff departures recorded in board minutes.
- **Taxes and state aid:** the FY27 revenue and tax estimate (local school taxes +$2,072,710 or 8.26%; estimated local rate $13.62), what moved it (investment earnings down $675,000, adequacy aid up), the 2024 revaluation effect, the EFA phase-out grant, and Bow's and Dunbarton's Question 2 (HB 1300) outcomes with the district's estimate of what the cap would have meant.
- **Dunbarton:** the 2024–2039 AREA agreement (cost formula, capital fee, exclusions, minimum payment, transport, input rights), Dunbarton's 2026-27 tuition budget ($4,550,290, **derived**), student counts since 2014, and how the capital fee paid part of the 2026 BHS roof.
- **SAU 67:** board make-up, the 50/50 cost formula and each district's share (Bow 80.65% for FY27, 81.94% proposed for FY28), the FY28 SAU budget and the new HB 564 ballot vote.
- **Board and public input:** meeting schedule, YouTube recordings, where every budget document is posted, the budget process, and how warrant articles are voted.
- **Buildings and reserves:** the September 2026 capital improvement plan ($18.5 million to 2041 and beyond; $6.2 million in the next five years, **derived**), reserve balances and planned deposits, outstanding bonds, the 2023 BES renovation and addition bond, the 2026 BHS roof, and fields.
- **Enrollment:** October 1 enrollment 2013–2025 and the district's projections to 2028-29.

**Main sources:** the Bow School Board pages on sau67nh.gov and their Google Drive folders (2026 warrant, revenue and tax estimate, line-item budget, MS-27, budget hearing packet, mandatory report to voters, capital improvements plan, bond statement, board minutes and packets 2023–2026, the BEA contract), the Dunbarton board's files (AREA agreement, 2026-27 budget packet, enrollment report), the SAU 67 November 2026 packet, and the site's database. One Concord Monitor article (March 14, 2026) is the only source found for the 2026 meeting results.

| Brief | Bow "votes by official ballot" | Not so. The local school levy rose 40.4% from tax year 2020 to 2025 while resident students fell 1.6% (**derived**, site data). It leaves out debt service, SAU costs and transportation, which each district pays itself. Dunbarton budgeted $4,550,290 for 2026-27 (**derived**). No source measures a surplus or subsidy. |
| P7.2 | A "massive building bond or addition" coming at Bow Elementary | No. The BES addition already happened: voters approved a $12.4 million bond in March 2023 for an eight-classroom addition and renovation, built in 2023–2024. | P16.2 / P19.3 | Fewer kids enrolling as vouchers expand | Not so far.
