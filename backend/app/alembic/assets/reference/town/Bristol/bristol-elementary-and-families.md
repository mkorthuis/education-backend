# Bristol Elementary and Young Families

Part of the Bristol research (town/Bristol). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Newfound Elementary School at Bristol (the school's name since August 2026; the site's database still lists it as Newfound Elementary School at Bristol, formerly Bristol Elementary School) and the families of young children in Bristol: the preschool and how a 3-year-old is evaluated, kindergarten day length and registration, reading support, class size and staffing, how Bristol's school differs from the New Hampton school and the other elementary schools nearby, the school's morning arrival, doors and drop-off, buses (including the Tapply-Thompson Community Center stop), what happens if a child misses the bus, after-school care, water, asbestos, structural repairs and air at the building, STEM and gifted programs, and licensed child care and Head Start near Bristol.

16 topics, 77 facts and 6 number tables (`newfound_bes_morning_bus_arrival_2026_27`, `newfound_elementary_classrooms_2026_27`, `bristol_nearest_head_start_drive_minutes`, `newfound_elementary_free_reduced_meal_share_2025_26`, `newfound_elementary_state_test_proficiency`, `bristol_elementary_enrollment_k4_2024_25`). The main sources are the 2026-27 Newfound Area elementary handbook, SAU 4's and the two elementary schools' pages, Project Promise's and the Tapply-Thompson Community Center's (TTCC's) own pages, the district's 2026-27 bus sheets (scanned; school stops only, transcribed from image), School Board packets and minutes (June to November 2026), Facilities and Building Committee minutes (January 2025 to October 2026), the September 2025 lead test letters (handwritten; transcribed from image), the April 2026 asbestos and structural reports, the 2025-2030 strategic plan's year 1 report, the March and May 2026 consolidation and fifth-grade presentations, and the Town of Bristol's 2025 water quality report. The site's own database was queried read-only for class size, enrollment, free and reduced-price meals, test results and licensed child care; those are marked **derived** where a comparison was built from them. Distances and drive times are **derived** from OpenStreetMap data (Nominatim for the two school locations and the town centers, OSRM for routes; only school addresses and town names were sent).

Main gaps: the 2027-28 kindergarten registration dates are not posted; no source says whether kindergarten is full-day or half-day (the handbook gives one set of school hours and puts kindergarten in the lunch and recess schedule); no time is posted for when the doors open in the morning; whether the district buses preschoolers to New Hampton and who may get off at the TTCC stop are not stated; no district carpool or missed-bus backup was found; no indoor air quality test report was found (the district did install carbon dioxide detectors in kitchens and boiler rooms); no gifted and talented program was found and the district's state report on it wasn't reached; the two licensed Bristol child care programs have no website of their own that was found; and the NH Department of Environmental Services' lead results page blocked automated requests.

Privacy notes for reviewers: the saved bus-sheet copy keeps only school stops and the TTCC stop (the original sheets list home stops by house number and aren't saved); the TTCC's after-school staff names are removed from the saved copy; saved minutes are excerpts with attendee lists and resident names removed. Staff named in sources are not named in the facts' statements; they are counted by role, and a few quoted passages in notes show a title in brackets where the minutes give a name.
