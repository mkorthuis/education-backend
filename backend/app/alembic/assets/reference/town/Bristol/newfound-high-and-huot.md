# Newfound Regional High School, the Huot Center and the Winnisquam Career Center

Part of the Bristol research (town/Bristol). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Newfound Regional High School (NRHS, Bristol; grades 9-12) and how its students use career centers: the 2026-27 schedule and calendar, graduation credits and diploma options, AP, Early College and dual enrollment, extended learning opportunities (ELOs) and early graduation, shop and technology courses, the Huot Career and Technical Center in Laconia and the Winnisquam Career Center (WiCC) in Tilton (applications, buses, class time, credentials), study hall and Flex, business partnerships and internships, sports and clubs, the Class of 2026's plans, outside ratings, home-educated students' access, and the legal ages for a commercial driver's license (CDL).

**Counts:** 14 topics, 51 facts and 6 number tables.

**Main sources:** NRHS's 2026-27 student handbook, bell schedule, school profile, live course catalog (a published spreadsheet, read November 22, 2026), posted 2024-25 Program of Studies and athletics handbook; Newfound Area School Board policies IC, IHBG, IHBH, IHCD, IKF, IKFA, IKFG and IMBC; board minutes from January 2025 to October 2026 and the September 3, 2026 transportation committee minutes; the district's 2026-27 calendar; Huot's 2026-27 handbook, Program of Studies and program pages; WiCC's web pages; the NHIAA 2026-27 handbook alignments; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24; GreatSchools pages; NH statutes, NH administrative rules and federal rules; routing estimates (OpenStreetMap geocoder and OSRM, school addresses only).

**Main gaps:** No Newfound course list from 2017-18 or 2018-19 was found, so "half the shop classes cut since 2018" can't be checked. Newfound doesn't post its Huot bus times or which NRHS class blocks a Huot student misses, any half-day senior schedule, who pays AP exam fees, or how many students transfer out. The ELO page and the counseling pages for Huot and home schooling are not readable (editor links or empty pages). Niche, U.S. News, SchoolDigger, NH DOE (403) and the NH DMV and OSHA pages (403) were not retrieved. No source says which NRHS rooms have air conditioning.

## cdl-age-rules: Commercial Driver's License: Minimum Ages and Training Rules

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Minimum ages for a commercial driver's license (CDL) under New Hampshire and federal rules, and the entry-level driver training that applies to people applying for one.

1. New Hampshire law says no commercial driver license (CDL) may be issued to anyone under 18, so a student can't hold a CDL before turning 18.
   - citation: RSA 263:27
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XXI/263/263-27.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-22
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Statute text: "A commercial driver license shall be issued to any person who has passed a commercial driver's examination. No such license shall be issued to any person less than 18 years of age." The NH DMV's CDL pages refused automated requests (see Not retrieved). Raw: raw/high/rules/rsa-263-27.txt
2. Under the federal motor carrier safety rule on driver qualifications, a person is qualified to drive a commercial motor vehicle only if he or she is at least 21 years old.
   - citation: 49 CFR 391.11(b)(1)
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-391/subpart-B/section-391.11
   - as_of: 2026-11-22
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "(b) Except as provided in subpart G of this part, a person is qualified to drive a motor vehicle if he/she— (1) Is at least 21 years old." This federal rule governs drivers covered by the federal motor carrier safety rules; whether a New Hampshire CDL holder aged 18 to 20 may drive within the state only is a state matter that the NH DMV's pages would show, and those pages were not retrieved. Raw: raw/high/rules/ecfr-391.11.txt
3. Federal entry-level driver training rules apply only to people who apply for a CDL, a CDL upgrade or an endorsement, and set the minimum training content for the theory and behind-the-wheel parts.
   - citation: 49 CFR 380.601
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-380/subpart-F/section-380.601
   - as_of: 2026-11-22
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "This subpart establishes training requirements for entry-level drivers ... applies only to those individuals who apply for a commercial driver's license (CDL) or a CDL upgrade or endorsement and does not otherwise amend substantive CDL requirements in part 383." Neither Newfound nor the Huot Center lists a CDL course (cte-center-programs 5). Raw: raw/high/rules/ecfr-380.601.txt
