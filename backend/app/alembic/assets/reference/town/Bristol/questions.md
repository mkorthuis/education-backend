# Bristol Residents' Questions

The questions the Bristol research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Bristol's students attend the Newfound Area School District (SAU 4), which serves Bristol and its neighbors on Newfound Lake: Newfound Elementary School at Bristol (kindergarten to grade 4), Newfound Memorial Middle School (grades 5–8) and Newfound Regional High School (grades 9–12), all in Bristol, with a second elementary school and preschool at New Hampton. Several questions contain premises (a school tax bill that keeps jumping, a start before Labor Day in rooms with no air conditioning, lead and asbestos reports at Bristol Elementary, an energy audit, voting at the high school, an enrollment of about a thousand) that the research should check rather than assume.
