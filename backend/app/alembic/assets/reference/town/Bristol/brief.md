## What you need to know about Bristol

- **Bristol** is a town in Grafton County at the foot of Newfound Lake, home of Speare Memorial Hospital's local clinics and the Tapply-Thompson Community Center. Site town id 67.
- **The Newfound Area School District** (site district id 388) is **SAU 4**, a multi-town district (check the member towns: Alexandria, Bridgewater, Bristol, Danbury, Groton, Hebron and New Hampton, and how costs are split). It runs Newfound Elementary School at Bristol (20090; kindergarten to grade 4), Newfound Elementary School at New Hampton (20105; preschool to grade 4), Newfound Memorial Middle School (26680; grades 5–8) and Newfound Regional High School (20085; grades 9–12). It votes by deliberative session and ballot (check). This is the first research on Newfound.
