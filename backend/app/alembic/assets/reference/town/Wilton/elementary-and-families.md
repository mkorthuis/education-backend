# Florence Rideout, Lyndeborough Central and Young Families in Wilton

Part of the Wilton research (town/Wilton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Wilton children start school and what their days look like through grade 5 in the Wilton-Lyndeborough Cooperative School District (SAU 63). It covers the small Pre-K at Lyndeborough Central School (LCS), kindergarten at LCS in Lyndeborough and the move to Florence Rideout Elementary (FRES) in grade 1, the bus runs that reach LCS, school hours, storm-day calls and the 2026-27 calendar. It covers before- and after-school options (the Boys & Girls Club of Souhegan Valley's Wilton pricing, Wild Rose Farm's afternoon program, child care near FRES), FRES's grades 3–5 test results next to Milford, Amherst and the state, the accelerated math position and BEAST Academy enrichment groups, reading instruction and screening, special education staffing and out-of-district placements, how the schools reach families (including translation), meals and meal charges, recess, devices, moving within Wilton or to Milford, and the Wilton Public & Gregg Free Library.

- **Premises checked:**
  - "Public pre-K, or pay for private preschool" (P1.1): partly. The district runs a Pre-K at LCS, but it posts no tuition, schedule, eligibility rules or application. Its teacher is listed as "Preschool/Pre-K Teacher/Case Manager," and NH DOE counted 4 preschoolers at LCS on October 1, 2026 (library `district-practices.md`, district-preschool fact 117 and the statewide count).
  - "Kindergarten at Lyndeborough Central, then Florence Rideout in first grade" (P1.2, P20.2): correct. LCS (192 Forest Road, Lyndeborough) holds Pre-K and kindergarten; FRES in Wilton holds grades 1–5. LCS is about 4.1 miles by road from FRES (derived).
  - "Before- and after-school care" (P1.3): no district-run care was found. The Boys & Girls Club's after-school program serves Wilton students ($275 a month, transport $50 extra); its before-school program is for Milford students only.
  - "FRES scores against Milford and Amherst" (P3.2): in 2025-26, FRES's grades 3–5 proficiency was above Milford's in 5 of 6 grade-and-subject results, below Amherst's in all six (by 13 to 30 points), and above the state in 2 and below in 4 (derived from the site's data).
  - "An accelerated track or just extra worksheets" (P3.3): partly. FRES's 2026-27 directory lists an "Accelerated Math Program AOP" position, and Title I staff run BEAST Academy enrichment math groups for grades 2–5. No description of who qualifies is published.
  - "Moving across the line into Milford mid-year" (P8.1): Milford is a separate district. A family that moves there becomes Milford's responsibility. Staying at FRES would need the WLC school board's approval with tuition and no district bus (Policy JFAB). A move within Wilton doesn't change schools: FRES is the district's only grades 1–5 school.
  - "How early do they call a delay or snow day" (P8.3): the superintendent aims to decide by 5:30 a.m.; early releases are decided by 11:00 a.m.; if a delay turns into a closing, the handbook says the announcement comes by 8:30 a.m.
  - "Someone who can translate for Spanish-speaking grandparents" (P11.1): no interpreter service, Spanish-speaking staff or translated notices are posted. The Home Language Survey lets families ask for translated school notices and tells staff to "provide an interpreter when necessary." The schools' messaging service, ParentSquare, says it can translate messages into 190+ languages.
  - "Kindergarten classes small enough" (P20.2): LCS forms three kindergarten classes. With 31 kindergartners on October 1, 2024, that is about 10 per teacher (derived), with two instructional aides. No 2026-27 count is posted.
