# Wilton: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Wilton, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the eighth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Wilton and Lyndeborough form the Wilton-Lyndeborough Cooperative School District (WLC), SAU 63:
- **Lyndeborough Central School** serves preschool and kindergarten, and **Florence Rideout Elementary** in Wilton grades 1–5.
- **WLC Middle School (6–8) and Senior High (9–12)** share one building on School Road.
- **Career programs:** the Milford Applied Technology Center (MATC). **High Mowing School**, a private Waldorf school, is in Wilton.

The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting. Milford and Souhegan were researched in `../Milford/` and `../Amherst/`.

## Corrections and additions to earlier files
