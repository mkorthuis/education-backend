# Wilton Residents' Questions

The questions the Wilton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Wilton's students attend the Wilton-Lyndeborough Cooperative School District (SAU 63), shared with Lyndeborough: Lyndeborough Central School (preschool and kindergarten), Florence Rideout Elementary in Wilton (grades 1–5) and the Wilton-Lyndeborough Cooperative Middle and High School (grades 6–12). Students may take career programs at the Milford Applied Technology Center. The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting. High Mowing School, a private Waldorf school, is also in Wilton. Several questions contain premises (kindergarten in Lyndeborough, a graduating class under 40 or 50, tuitioning into Souhegan or Milford, lagging para pay, a coming bond) that the research should check rather than assume.
