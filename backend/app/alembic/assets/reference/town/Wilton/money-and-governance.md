# Wilton: Money and Governance

Part of the Wilton research (town/Wilton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 13 topics (all local), 67 facts and 7 number tables on the Wilton-Lyndeborough Cooperative School District's money and governance: the 2026-27 budget and what drove it, the first 2027-28 draft, unspent funds, special education costs, the district's own cost-per-pupil figures, how Wilton and Lyndeborough split costs under the Articles of Agreement (and the bond exception), the district's tax-impact estimates and 2026 levy estimate, Wilton's 2026 revaluation, the Question 2 recount, the buildings, capital reserve funds, the 2014 bond and heating, the new support staff contract and its pay scales, substitute pay, the shift from paraprofessionals to ABA therapists, staff turnover and vacancies in 2025-26, leadership changes, the draft strategic plan's staff-retention goal, how the board and Budget Committee work, enrollment trends, and the district's federal-grant consultation with High Mowing School.

- **Budget:** voters approved $16,578,477 for 2026-27 (+3.55%); $456,792 of the $568,269 increase was employee benefits (health insurance up an average 34.6%). The first 2027-28 draft is $17,538,054 all-in (+4.17%); excluding special education it is up 1.1%.
- **Cost sharing:** costs are split 50% by each town's resident students and 50% by equalized property value; in 2025-26 Wilton had 74.69% of students and paid 72.31% (2026-27: 69.74%). Lyndeborough pays a fixed $96,564 a year toward the 2014 elementary bond and Wilton the rest.
- **Buildings:** no new bond is on the table as of November 2026. WLC's heating units are original to the building and "heating system modernization" is a named future priority; an outside energy firm began a facility analysis in October 2026.
- **Staff:** the 2026–2029 support staff contract raised the uncertified paraprofessional starting rate from $15.76 to $17.50 an hour; substitutes earn $120 a day. Paraeducator positions fell from 20.5 (2019) to 12.0 (2026) while ABA therapists rose from 12 to 19. In 2025-26, seven of 26 WLC teaching positions turned over; by November 2026 board members said staffing "has stabilized."

**Main sources:** the 2024-25 Annual Report (with the 2026 warrant, special education report and bond schedule), the March 7, 2026 district meeting minutes and budget presentation, the Articles of Agreement (2014), the FY26 tax-rate impact sheet, the FY27 line-item budget, the FY28 first draft and staffing sheet, Budget Committee minutes (February–November 2026), School Board minutes and reports (February–November 2026), the 2026–2029 support staff contract, the capital reserve and CIP documents, the Question 2 recount results and HB 1300 forum slides, SAU 63's substitute and job pages, the Town of Wilton's assessor, tax rate and emergency notification pages, and the site's database (cost per pupil, ADM, teacher salaries, town enrollment, tax rates).
