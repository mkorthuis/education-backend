# WLC High School and the Milford Applied Technology Center

Part of the Wilton research (town/Wilton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Wilton-Lyndeborough Cooperative (WLC) High School, grades 9–12, in the middle/high school building in Wilton, and how its students reach career programs at the Milford Applied Technology Center (MATC) and other centers: getting to career classes, the bell schedule, hands-on electives at WLC, credit for apprenticeships and other extended learning, AP and dual enrollment, grading and class size, counseling, FAFSA and scholarship help, sports teams and shared programs, late buses, open enrollment and tuitioning to Milford or Souhegan, graduates' plans and financial literacy.

All topics are shared slugs; one is a statewide rule topic (`youth-employment-rules`, no place). No new topics proposed.
