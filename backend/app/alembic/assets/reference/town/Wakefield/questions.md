# Wakefield Residents' Questions

The questions the Wakefield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Wakefield is SAU 101. The Wakefield School District runs Paul School in Sanbornville (preschool to grade 8). Its high school students attend Spaulding High School in Rochester under an AREA plan, and the board may approve and pay tuition elsewhere (Kingswood in Wolfeboro) for up to 10% of its high schoolers each year. Several questions contain premises (a Kingswood lottery, an Area Agreement Committee renegotiating Rochester's tuition, Paul School staying under 400 students, $200,000 a year into trust funds, an interim principal) that the research should check rather than assume.
