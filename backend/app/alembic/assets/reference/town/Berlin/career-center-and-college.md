# Berlin's Career and Technical Center and College Courses

Part of the Berlin research (town/Berlin). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Berlin Regional Career & Technical Center at Berlin Middle High School (its programs, grade levels, credits, credentials, enrollment and Gorham students), White Mountains Regional's career center in Whitefield, Early College and dual enrollment with White Mountains Community College (WMCC), WMCC's articulation agreements, ELOs, JAG and work credit, computer science and personal finance at Berlin, welding certification and apprenticeships near Berlin, and the state rules on career centers, extended learning opportunities (ELOs) and required high school courses.

- **10 fact topics, 56 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 15 MB, including `browser-pass/`). Scripts are in the session scratchpad folder `berlin-high/`.
- **Places:** `district Berlin` (career center, Berlin Middle High, budget book), `district Gorham Randolph Shelburne Cooperative` (Gorham's own profile), `district White Mountains Regional` (its career center), `town Berlin` (WMCC and job training around it), and no place for state and federal rules and the ASE credential.
- **Topic `industry-credentials-explained`** (how_it_works, after_high_school) was added to shared-topics from the Plymouth and Berlin research; used here for ASE's student G1 rule.
- **Sources:** scripted requests to SAU 3's website (https://www.sau3.org) got a "Client Challenge" bot check. The main session then read the guidance page and the 2026-27 Berlin Middle High School Program of Studies in Chrome (`raw/high/browser-pass/`, `raw/middle/browser-pass/`); the program, Early College, ELO, JAG, computer science and personal finance facts now come from it. The career center's Google site, WMCC, Gorham's profile, NH DOE and the budget book (saved by the money agent) fill in the rest.
- **Corrections from the Program of Studies:** the introductory career courses are open to grades 9–12 (the center's website shows woodworking for grades 9–10 only); the website's Digital Electronics and Civil Engineering & Architecture are not in the 2026-27 course list; LNA Test Prep is 1 credit, not 0.5; the year-one and year-two courses are now named "1" and "2".
- **Source conflict:** the NH Jump$tart Coalition's 2024 survey lists Berlin High as requiring a stand-alone personal finance course, but the 2026-27 Program of Studies puts personal finance inside the required economics course for the classes of 2027–2029 (school-programs-after-grade-6 10–11).
- **Gaps:** the full ELO policy (IHBH) and application, Berlin's gift and donation policy, whether Berlin's automotive program is ASE-accredited, what local shops think of the credentials, how Gorham students travel to Berlin, and any grade 6–8 technology or coding courses.
