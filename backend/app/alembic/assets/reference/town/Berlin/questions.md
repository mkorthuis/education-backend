# Berlin Residents' Questions

The questions the Berlin research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Berlin is a city and SAU 3, a one-district SAU. The Berlin School District runs Berlin Elementary School (preschool to grade 5), Berlin Middle School (grades 6–8) and Berlin Senior High School (grades 9–12), with a regional career and technical center. Dummer and Milan send grades 7–12 to Berlin under tuition contracts, and White Mountains Community College is in the city. Several questions contain premises (Project Youth ending at 5:45, grades 6–12 in one building on Willard Street, about a quarter of middle schoolers proficient, free meals for everyone, $150 dual-enrollment classes, a WMCC early childhood agreement, paraprofessionals quitting for better pay, the City Council voting on the school budget, vacant school buildings, annual homeschool portfolios) that the research should check rather than assume.
