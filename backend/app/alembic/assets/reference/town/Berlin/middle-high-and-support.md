# Berlin Middle High School: Academics, Student Support and School Choice

Part of the Berlin research (town/Berlin). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Berlin Middle School (grades 6–8) and Berlin Senior High School (grades 9–12), which share one building at 550 Willard Street and are run as "Berlin Middle High School." It covers the building's grade grouping, middle school sports, AP courses, attendance and truancy rules, bullying procedures, homeschooled and EFA students' access, out-of-district placements (the Enriched Learning Center and Northern Human Services), North Country Charter Academy's Berlin site, adult diploma options, and private and Catholic schools near Berlin.
