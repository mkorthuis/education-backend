# Woodbury and School Choices

Part of the Salem research (town/Salem). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the middle-school years and the choices around them for Salem families: the move from a neighborhood elementary school to Woodbury School (grades 6–8), Woodbury's teams, counselors, clubs, sports, arts, maker and engineering classes, phone and bullying rules, and grading; choosing ninth-grade courses at Salem High School; computer science, arts and performing arts at Woodbury and Salem High; The Birches Academy's kindergarten lottery, sibling preference and waitlists; St. Joseph Regional Catholic School's tuition, class sizes, EFA listing and activities; how private, charter and home-educated students can join Woodbury programs; and what two Massachusetts private high schools (Central Catholic and Austin Prep) ask of applicants and cost.

- **9 topics, 84 facts, 2 tables.** All nine topics are shared town topics. Raw copies are in `raw/woodbury/` (about 28 MB). Places: `district Salem`, `district The Birches Academy of Academics & Art A Public Charter Sch`, `district Diocese of Manchester` (St. Joseph), `town Salem` (EFA statistics and the Massachusetts high schools).
- **Main findings:**
  - Woodbury had 781 students in 2025-26 by the district's count (762 in 2024-25 in the site's data), not 900; 900 is the number the renovated building was designed for, and roughly Woodbury's enrollment a decade ago (892 in 2015-16). Each grade is split into three teams of about 85 students, with one counselor per grade.
  - The Birches holds a lottery only for kindergarten; other grades use waitlists. Siblings are enrolled before the lottery when there is space. For 2026-27 its board was told of 20+ kindergarten siblings in February, and in August of waitlists of 19 (K), 36 (grade 1) and 26 (grade 2), but no waitlist and 8 open seats in grade 6.
  - St. Joseph's 2026-27 tuition is $7,645 (K–5) and $7,720 (grades 6–8). It is listed in the EFA marketplace; the 2026-27 base EFA ($4,351) equals about 56% of its junior high tuition (**derived**). Salem had 16 EFA applicants on the 2026-27 waitlist on November 15, 2026.
  - Salem policy IHBG-R lets private, charter and home-educated Salem children join Woodbury sports, clubs and classes on written request, with conditions; the policy (2016) predates the 2026 EFA and home-education laws, and the Woodbury handbook says only current Woodbury students may attend social events.
  - The school board approved an AP Cybersecurity course for 2027-28 on October 27, 2026. Woodbury posts no course list; its own posts show grade 7 "Innovators and Makers" and "Engineering and Design" classes, but no maker space or robotics lab is described on district pages.
  - Woodbury's phone rule bans phones, earbuds and smart watches from 7:25 a.m. to 2:10 p.m.; the bullying policy JICK was revised October 27, 2026.
