## What you need to know about Salem

- **Salem** is in Rockingham County on the Massachusetts border (Methuen, Haverhill), next to Windham, Pelham, Atkinson and Derry, along I-93 and Route 28. Site town id 473. The town has a town council and town manager; the school district is separate.
- **Salem School District** (site district id 473) is **SAU 57** (https://sau57.org). It votes on its budget by official ballot (SB2), with a default budget if the proposed budget fails; the 2026 deliberative session was February 5, 2026. Use `district Salem` for SAU-level facts too.
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - **Elementary, kindergarten to grade 5:** William T. Barron (22865, https://barron.sau57.org), Mary A. Fisk (22875), William E. Lancaster (22860), North Salem (22890), Dr. L. F. Soule (22895). Each school has its own `<school>.sau57.org` site.
  - **Salem Preschool** (29225, https://preschool.sau57.org), in the former Walter F. Haigh School building (26555, no longer an elementary school).
  - **Woodbury School** (22850, grades 6–8).
  - **Salem High School** (22880, https://shs.sau57.org, grades 9–12) and its **career and technical education center**, which also takes students from other towns.
- **Charter and private schools in Salem:** The Birches Academy (K–8, district name in the site's data `The Birches Academy of Academics & Art A Public Charter Sch`), the Arts Academy of New Hampshire Chartered Public High School (9–12), St. Joseph Regional Catholic School (K–8, operator `Diocese of Manchester`) and Shalom Christian Academy (K–8).
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - Enrollment by town of residence (the site's town enrollment): 3,870 Salem students in 2013-14, 3,539 in 2023-24 and 3,532 in 2025-26. In 2024-25 Salem's schools had Salem High 1,098, Woodbury 762, Barron 377, North Salem 353, Lancaster 294, Fisk 272, Soule 269 and the preschool 113.
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Salem $19,752; state $22,699; Windham $19,690; Pelham $20,212; Londonderry $21,196; Derry Cooperative $21,491.
  - The site also has every school's enrollment by grade, test scores (with growth where NH DOE reports it), class sizes, staff and salary schedules, free and reduced-price meals, English learners, safety counts, graduation and plans, licensed child care in the town, EFAs by town, Question 2 results, Census estimates, adequacy aid, special education aid, tax rates and school taxes. Point to these; don't restate them.
- **Saved pages:** `additional-data/raw/districts/batch-7/salem-*`: the annual meeting, Barron's home page, enrollment FAQs, policy JEB, the "join us" page, policy documents, and Salem Preschool's home, about, programs and hours, and registration pages.
- **Library facts already loaded** (don't repeat them; cite them as existing): the kindergarten cutoff (five years old before October 1, not September 30 as in most districts); registration through an online application; Salem Preschool at 24 School Street (integrated, ages 3 to 5); the SB2 budget form and the 2026 deliberative session.
