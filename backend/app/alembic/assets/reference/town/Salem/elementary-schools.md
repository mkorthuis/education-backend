# Salem Elementary Schools and Salem Preschool

Part of the Salem research (town/Salem). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Salem's five elementary schools (Barron, Fisk, Lancaster, North Salem and Soule) and Salem Preschool in the former Haigh School: the preschool's waitlist and registration, class sizes and before- and after-school care at each school, full-day kindergarten, how the schools grew from six to five and what that did to classroom space, the reading and math programs (Wit & Wisdom, Fundations, Heggerty, Eureka Math², ST Math, i-Ready), report cards and reading help, advanced math and the absence of a gifted program, strings, band and STEM, behavior support at North Salem, recess and physical education, and how Fisk's math results compare.

- **13 topics, 82 facts, 2 number tables.** Twelve topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; one adds Salem facts to the library's own `kindergarten-day-length` topic, where Salem was a gap. No new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 35 MB, 114 files).
- **Sources:** the district's and schools' own pages, the five 2026-27 elementary handbooks and the preschool handbook, the 2024-25 and 2025-26 district annual reports, the October 2026 enrollment report and 2027-28 budget documents, superintendent's goal reports (2016, 2018, 2019), the 2012 and 2017 enrollment studies, board policies IFA, JG and JLCF, board agendas, the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (Salem rows of the extracts the Concord research saved), RSA 188-E:24-a, the site's own database (derived comparisons only), and the pages of the Boys & Girls Club of Greater Salem, Main Street Learning Center, Wilson Language Training, Heggerty and Music & Arts.
- **Main gaps:** how long Salem Preschool's waitlist is, or how fast the 2026-27 sessions filled; any elementary math enrichment or acceleration (none is posted); how Woodbury places students in Algebra I; any elementary math or STEM competition team; which school received the 2026 state robotics grant; the school board's student achievement presentations (not posted; the board portal needs a password); Create and Discover's own pages; a published measure of daily screen time or of teacher workload and turnover at North Salem.
- **Spot-checked against raw copies:** North Salem's handbook line "Each student in grade K-5 has access to his/her own Chromebook for the year" (`northsalem-handbook-2026-27.txt`); the October 2026 class sections for Fisk's kindergarten, 10, 10 and 9 (`budget-2027-28-enrollments-p1.png`); and policy JLCF's "All elementary schools will offer at least 20 minutes of recess on all days during the school year" (`policy-jlcf.txt`). All three match.

Salem Preschool's pre-registration for 2027-28 is first come, first served: each online submission is time-stamped, sessions and classrooms are assigned strictly by the registration log and availability, requests for a particular session or teacher aren't accepted, and a place is confirmed only by an email from the preschool's main office (allow 3 to 5 business days), not by the form's automatic confirmation.

## kindergarten-day-length: Kindergarten Day Length by District
