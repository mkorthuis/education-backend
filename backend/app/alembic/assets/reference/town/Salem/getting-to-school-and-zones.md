## twins-classroom-placement: Classroom Placement of Twins and Other Multiples

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: New Hampshire's law on placing twins and other multiples in the same or separate classrooms: parents' requests, deadlines and the principal's decision

1. New Hampshire law lets a parent or guardian of twins or other multiples (triplets or more) in elementary school ask that they be placed in the same classroom or in separate classrooms, no later than 60 days before the first day of each school year or at registration for children enrolling in a new school, and the request "shall be granted" unless the principal, after meeting with the parents and carefully considering their reasons and the best interests of their children and other children affected, decides a different placement is necessary.
   - citation: RSA 193:3-a, II
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-3-a.htm

## school-transportation-rules: School Bus and Transportation Rules

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: When NH districts must provide transportation, and who is responsible when a student attends school outside the assigned school or district.

1. New Hampshire law requires a driver meeting or overtaking, from either direction, a marked school bus that has stopped to pick up or drop off children to stop at least 25 feet from the bus and not move until the bus moves or its flashing red lights stop (except on a divided highway when the bus is on the other side), forbids passing a stopped school bus on the right, and until January 1, 2027 makes a first offense a violation with a $150 fine.
   - citation: RSA 265:54, I, I-b, III and IV
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XXI/265/265-54.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "The driver of a vehicle upon a way upon meeting or overtaking from either direction any school bus ... which has stopped on the highway for the purpose of receiving or discharging school children shall stop his or her vehicle before reaching such school bus at least 25 feet away from such school bus. The driver shall not proceed until such school bus resumes motion, or until flashing red lights cease to operate." I-b (until January 1, 2027): "shall be guilty of a violation and shall be fined $150 plus penalty assessment for a first offense, and shall be fined not less than $250 nor more than $1,000 for a subsequent offense." IV: passing on the right, $500 fine. Raw: raw/daily/rsa-265-54.txt

2. Starting January 1, 2027, New Hampshire makes passing a stopped school bus a class B misdemeanor with a $300 fine and a 7-day license suspension for a first offense, a class A misdemeanor with at least a $750 fine and at least a 30-day license revocation for a second, and at least 15 days in jail and revocation for at least a year for a third, and lets bus camera footage be used to identify the vehicle.
   - citation: RSA 265:54, I-a to I-d (as amended by 2026, 163:1, effective January 1, 2027)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XXI/265/265-54.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text (effective January 1, 2027): "shall be guilty of a class B misdemeanor and shall be fined $300 plus penalty assessment for a first offense. In addition, the director shall suspend the person's license ... for a period of 7 days for the first offense." I-c: "a second or subsequent time shall be guilty of a class A misdemeanor and shall be fined not less than $750. Unless the court determines that exceptional circumstances exist, the person's driver's license ... shall be revoked for a period of not less than 30 days." I-d: "a third or subsequent time ... a mandatory sentence of not less than 15 consecutive days in the county correctional facility. The person's driver's license ... shall be revoked indefinitely and shall not be restored for at least one year." I-a: "The camera footage from the bus may be used as evidence to identify the vehicle owner or operator". Salem's buses carry video and audio cameras (district-transportation 15). Raw: raw/daily/rsa-265-54.txt
