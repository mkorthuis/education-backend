# Student Support and Families in Salem: Special Education, Counseling, Meals, Absences and Family Help

Part of the Salem research (town/Salem). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers support for students and families in the Salem School District (SAU 57): educational assistant (paraprofessional) openings and pay, how special education services are staffed and what the district says about meeting IEP service hours, the IEP move from Woodbury to Salem High, special education referrals and evaluations (including speech), 504 plans and the district's 504 grievance steps, behavior support staff and programs, counseling and mental health staff and resources, English learner services and translation, guardians' paperwork and parent portal access, meal applications and privacy, menus and lunch lines, vegetarian and religious diets, classroom party food, religious holiday absences, vaping and drug rules and prevention lessons, summer catch-up options, quiet study space at Salem High, and help with coats, food and other needs.

- **Main sources:** SAU 57 policies JH, JLD, JLDBA, JICG, JICH, JLCF, JLCF-R, EFC, EFAA, JFAA, JRA, KED, AC and IHCA; the Special Education Procedures Plan (approved September 1, 2021) and the district's Special Education Overview for Parents; the 2026-27 handbooks for Barron, Fisk, Lancaster, North Salem, Soule, Woodbury and Salem High; the Salem High 2026-27 Program of Studies; the Student Services, ESOL, Title I, Homeless Youth, Nutrition, Allergy Guidelines, Meal Account, Free and Reduced Meal, ParentSquare language, Parent Portal and Annual Update pages; Woodbury and Salem High counseling, special education and family resources pages; the Frontline (AppliTrack) job board; the proposed 2027-28 budget presentation (October 24, 2026) and its new-program section; school board agendas; MealViewer menu data; End 68 Hours of Hunger's Salem page; the Town of Salem Human Services page; 34 CFR 300.323 and 7 CFR 245.8.
- **Main findings:**
  - Every Salem school had a full-time special education educational assistant posting open on November 15, 2026 (all posted July 1, 2026), at $19.56 an hour for 6.5 hours and 183 days (about $23,267 a year, **derived**). Barron posted another para opening for its new autism program on November 9, 2026.
  - The district's 2027-28 budget proposal says building staff could not meet some reading IEP services in 2025–26, so it spent $70,245 on 3 outside tutors for 7 secondary students. No district page describes how missed IEP minutes are made up when a room is short-staffed.
  - When two students with autism moved up to Salem High for 2026-27 and none entered Woodbury's program, the district moved Woodbury's ABC special educator position to Salem High. Its procedures plan says IEPs cover moves from school to school, and every teacher and provider gets a full copy of the IEP.
  - Woodbury and Salem High's 2026-27 handbooks list "Observation of a Bona fide Religious Holiday" as an excused absence. Board policy JH (last revised 2010) and the Barron and Soule handbooks don't list it, and JH says unexcused absences get a grade of "0".
  - Salem's meal guidelines say vegetarian items are offered at every level and marked on online menus, but vegan, kosher and halal meals are not offered. Every student pays at the register through the same account system, and policy EFC requires "all possible measures" to protect families' anonymity.
  - Salem High has six counselors assigned by last name (about 183 students each, **derived**), plus an outreach counselor and an on-call counselor for crises. No source gives a wait time to see a counselor.
- **Main gaps:** any figure on how long Salem takes to finish evaluations (NH DOE's site refused automated requests); how often IEP services are missed or made up; board minutes and presentations, including the September 15, 2026 "Student Services Staff / ABC Update" (the board portal needs a password; only agendas are posted); Fisk's speech and special education staff (Fisk's handbook names only its counselor); any Woodbury summer catch-up program (none found); any Spanish-speaking staff contact named at Fisk or Woodbury; how well 504 plans are followed day to day, which the data can't show.

## school-meals-eligibility: Keeping Free and Reduced-Price Meals Private

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: NH's school meal requirement and the federal income limits and categorical eligibility for free or reduced-price meals for July 2026–June 2027.

1. Federal school meal rules bar schools from publishing, posting or announcing the names of children who get free or reduced-price meals, and from identifying them openly by special tokens, tickets or any other means.
   - citation: 7 CFR 245.8(a)–(b)
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-245.8
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "(a) The names of the children shall not be published, posted or announced in any manner; (b) There shall be no overt identification of any of the children by the use of special tokens or tickets or by any other means". New Hampshire's related rule (no student treated differently from standard cafeteria procedures) is library school-meals-eligibility 11. Raw: raw/support/ecfr-7-245-8.txt
2. Federal school meal rules also bar schools from making children who get free or reduced-price meals work for their meals, use a separate dining area, line or entrance, or eat at a different time, and require that they get the same meal choices as children who pay full price.
   - citation: 7 CFR 245.8(c)–(e)
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-245.8
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "(c) The children shall not be required to work for their meals or milk; (d) The children shall not be required to use a separate dining area, go through a separate serving line, enter the dining area through a separate entrance or consume their meals or milk at a different time; (e) When more than one lunch or breakfast or type of milk is offered ..., the children shall have the same choice of meals or milk that is available to those children who pay the full price". Raw: as fact 1.
