# Salem Residents' Questions

The questions the Salem research is meant to answer. Thirty resident profiles, three questions each (90 in all, half again the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Salem is SAU 57. The Salem School District runs five elementary schools (Barron, Fisk, Lancaster, North Salem and Soule, kindergarten to grade 5), Salem Preschool, Woodbury School (grades 6–8) and Salem High School with its career and technical education (CTE) center. The Birches Academy (K–8) and the Arts Academy of New Hampshire are public charter schools in Salem, and St. Joseph Regional Catholic School is a parochial school. The district votes on its budget by official ballot (SB2), with a default budget if the proposed one fails. Several questions contain premises (school sizes, the former Haigh School, renovation costs, CTE seats, start times, programs) that the research should check rather than assume.
