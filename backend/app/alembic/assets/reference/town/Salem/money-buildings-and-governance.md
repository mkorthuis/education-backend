# Salem: Money, Buildings, Safety and Governance

Part of the Salem research (town/Salem). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 86 facts and 6 number tables on how the Salem School District (SAU 57) spends, borrows, taxes and votes, the state of its buildings, its security and drills, student data privacy, staffing, published results and how residents can volunteer.
- **The budget and what drives it.** The 2026-27 operating budget ($99,125,246, approved 1,907–1,411 on March 10, 2026), its $4.24 million (4.5%) increase and the district's own list of drivers (contract salaries $1.48M, special education programs $0.87M, out-of-district tuition $0.63M, benefits $0.60M), the proposed 2027-28 budget of October 24, 2026 ($101.1 million, +1.9%, with debt payments falling $1.58M), the health insurance line, special education costs, contracts, the autism program's growth, and the district's enrollment count since 2011-12 with projections to 2031-32.
- **Taxes.** The district's own estimate that the local school levy would rise 7.02% in 2026-27 (to $72.7 million) partly because less fund balance and impact fee money was used; its HB 1300 tax cap illustration; school impact fees; Tuscan Village's taxes and the Town's estimate of its school costs; the 2026 revaluation; Salem's elderly exemption and tax deferral.
- **Buildings and debt.** Every bond since 2010 and what it built: the 2010 and 2013 elementary bonds, the 2014 Salem High and CTE bond ($63.97M), the 2020 Woodbury authorization ($48.9M, of which $32.8M was borrowed). $25.5 million of unused borrowing was rescinded in 2025. Principal still owed was $67.3 million at June 30, 2025, with payments falling each year to 2043. No new bond is proposed; repairs in the 2027-28 proposal are small.
- **Governance.** How the default budget is figured and how it compared with the proposed budget line by line, the Budget Committee and the failed 2026 charter change to abolish it, the 2027-28 budget calendar, recent ballot results (including the failed 2025 budget and tax caps).
- **Safety, privacy, staffing, results and volunteering.** Building security features, Run-Hide-Fight drills "in developmentally-appropriate ways", Raptor visitor checks, policies EBCA, EBCB and KI; the district's list of 226 apps and their data privacy agreements (Infinite Campus has one), its technology and records policies, AI working groups; the job board on November 15, 2026; teacher pay against the state and inflation; the district's published scores; the strategic plan's alumni-mentor strategy, the internship mentor drive and volunteer background checks.

**Main sources:** SAU 57's Annual Meeting, Budget & Financials, Finance, HB1300, Reports and Planning, safety, data privacy and strategic plan pages; the 2026, 2025 and 2024 annual reports; the 2026 warrant, MS-27, default budget (MS-DSB), election results and recount; the February 5, 2026 deliberative session presentation; the October 24, 2026 budget presentation, object summaries, detailed budget, enrollment and new-program documents; the FY25 audited financial statements; MS-22s for 2013-14, 2014-15 and 2020-21; SAU 57 policies; the job board; the Town of Salem's 2025 Town Report, charter, assessing pages and news releases on Tuscan Village and the revaluation; RKG's 2023 fiscal impact analysis; RSA 189:64; the site's database.
