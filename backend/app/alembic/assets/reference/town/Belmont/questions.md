# Belmont Residents' Questions

The questions the Belmont research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Belmont's students attend the Shaker Regional School District (SAU 80), shared with Canterbury. Shaker runs Belmont Elementary School (preschool to grade 4), Canterbury Elementary School (K–5), Belmont Middle School (grades 5–8) and Belmont High School (grades 9–12). Belmont High students may take career programs at the Huot Technical Center in Laconia. Several questions contain premises (about 43% of students proficient in math, a tax bill up over 10% with nearly two-thirds going to schools, a vote on a separate ballot or at a Saturday session, homeschool paperwork with SAU 80, the NH Scholar cord) that the research should check rather than assume.
