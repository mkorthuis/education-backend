# Belmont Middle School and School Choices

Part of the Belmont research (town/Belmont). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Belmont Middle School (BMS, grades 5–8, 38 School Street) and school choices for Belmont families in the Shaker Regional School District (SAU 80): the district's math results and what it does about them, extra math help at BMS, the bell-to-bell phone rule, sports, clubs and late buses, IEP meetings and the move from elementary to middle school with an IEP, advanced math, the arts and music, Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs), home education and SAU 80's paperwork, homeschooled students in sports, private school students on district buses, coming back to Belmont High School for grade 9, EFAs at private schools near Laconia and Sanbornton, and substance use prevention and mental health support for teenagers.

- **15 topics, 87 facts, no tables.** Twelve topics are shared district or town slugs with `place: district Shaker Regional` (10) or `place: town Belmont` (`town-school-options-nearby`, `town-recreation-and-summer`). Three add state rules (no place) to existing library topics: `special-ed-iep-meetings-and-consent` (NH's 21-day rule after a parent's written request for an IEP meeting, and copies of the IEP to every teacher), `home-education-rules` (NH DOE's September 2, 2026 advisory on HB 1268) and `efa-uses-and-requirements` (the scholarship organization's fee of up to 10%).
- **Main sources:** the BMS 2026-27 Student & Parent Handbook (board-approved August 25, 2026), BMS athletics and clubs pages, SAU 80 board policies (JICJ, IHBG and its rules and procedures, EEA, IMBD, IF, IK, JFABC, JJD, JJI, IHAMA, JLDBB, IHBA, JLC), SAU 80's home education page and posted forms, its 2026-27 Comprehensive Needs Assessment (a federal Title programs workbook), the district's 2025-26 annual report, school board minutes from September 2025 to November 10, 2026, the Belmont High School 2026-27 Program of Studies and Handbook, BHS's Student Assistance Program page, NH Ed 1100 special education rules, RSA 194-F:4, Sant Bani School's tuition page and the library's EFA marketplace download.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Math:** the district's own needs assessment puts math proficiency at 43% in 2025 (40% in 2024, 35% in 2023) against a state target of 53%. Its response is teacher training (Wurster Consulting, OGAP cohorts, Math Empowered, an instructional coach), Title I teachers and tutors at Belmont Elementary, IXL, Reflex Math and Zearn, and, at BMS, math lab teachers, MTSS small groups, Homework Club, after-school standards recovery with transportation and a July summer program. In November 2026 the BMS principal said BMS "is no longer a school in need of additional support (NH DOE)."
  - **Phones** are banned from first bell to dismissal under policy JICJ (August 2025). At BMS they must be off and in the student's locker. The principal told the board in September 2025 the rule had "not been an issue so far." No counts of violations are published.
  - **Late bus:** the only late bus BMS describes is a 4:50 p.m. bus to Canterbury after Open Library. No late or activity bus for Belmont students was found in the handbook or the 2026-27 route sheets.
  - **Arts and music:** art, chorus, band and music, world language and STEM remain in the 2026-27 program. The handbook lists the band and music teacher as "TBD." In September 2026 the principal was still searching for a music teacher, and in October 2026 the board approved a French and Spanish teacher. The superintendent's list of potential 2027-28 cuts, which the board asked for, includes the BMS world language position, the enrichment teacher, the 7th/8th grade math and ELA lab teachers, BMS B teams and 5th/6th grade sports, and the substance abuse prevention counselor. No votes on them were recorded through November 10, 2026.
  - **Home education:** SAU 80's page still asks families to file an "Intent to Enroll in Home Education Form," and its posted 2020 procedures still describe portfolios and annual evaluations. Since July 1, 2026 state law requires neither, except a notice when a child leaves a public school or uses district programs. SAU 80 also posts NH DOE's September 2, 2026 advisory saying exactly that.
  - **EFAs near Laconia:** Laconia Christian Academy, Holy Trinity, Acton Academy Lakes Region, Sant Bani, Tilton, New Hampton and Holderness are all on the EFA marketplace. Sant Bani's K–8 tuition is $12,800 for 2026-27. A base-only 2026-27 EFA ($4,351, less a fee of up to 10%) covers about 31% of that (derived).
- **Main gaps:** no posted BMS procedure for testing into a higher math grade, and no gifted program description; no Shaker special education procedures plan or written BES-to-BMS IEP transition procedure posted; no sports or club fee amounts; no figures on how often phones are confiscated; no Shaker statement on nurse screenings for private school students; no Belmont youth sports league information on the town site.

## special-ed-iep-meetings-and-consent: IEP Meetings, Notices and Parent Consent

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Notice before IEP meetings, who is on the IEP team, written notice of changes, and when parent consent is required in New Hampshire.

1. Under New Hampshire's special education rules, when a parent, guardian or adult student asks in writing for an IEP team meeting, the school district must agree on a time and date and hold the meeting, or give written prior notice explaining why it refuses, and must do so within 21 days of receiving the written request; the rules also say the IEP team may be reconvened at any time to review the IEP.
   - citation: N.H. Code Admin. R. Ed 1109.06(a)–(c)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rules/state_agencies/ed1100.html
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Rule text: "The IEP team may be reconvened at any time to review the provisions of the IEP. (b) The LEA, upon a written request for an IEP team meeting by the parent, guardian, or adult student shall: (1) Schedule a mutually agreeable time and date for an IEP team meeting; (2) Convene the IEP team on the mutually agreeable time and date; or (3) Provide the parent, guardian, or adult student with written prior notice detailing why the LEA refuses to convene the IEP team that the parent, guardian, or adult student has requested. (c) All activities detailed in Ed 1109.06(b) shall be completed within 21 days following the receipt of the written request for the IEP team meeting." The 10-day meeting notice (fact 1 in the library's topic) still applies unless the parent waives it in writing. Dover's and Milford's procedures plans quote the same rule. Raw: raw/middle/rules-ed1100.html (.txt, search "Ed 1109.06").
2. Under New Hampshire's special education rules, a school district must give each teacher and service provider responsible for carrying out a child's IEP a copy of the complete IEP, and must keep written evidence of the special education and related services, supplementary aids, program modifications and supports for staff that it provides under the IEP.
   - citation: N.H. Code Admin. R. Ed 1109.04(a)–(b)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rules/state_agencies/ed1100.html
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Rule text: "(a) The LEA shall provide each teacher and service provider listed as having responsibilities for implementing the IEP with a copy of the complete IEP for working and monitoring purposes ... (b) The LEA shall maintain written evidence documenting implementation of the IEP, including: (1) Special education and related services provided; (2) Supplementary aids and services provided; (3) Programs modifications made; and (4) Supports provided for school personnel implementing the IEP." That an IEP must be in effect at the start of each school year is in the library (special-ed-moving-with-iep 4). Raw: raw/middle/rules-ed1100.txt (search "Ed 1109.04").
