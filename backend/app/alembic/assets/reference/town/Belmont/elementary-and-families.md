Part of the Belmont research (town/Belmont). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Belmont Elementary School (BES, preschool to grade 4) in the Shaker Regional School District (SAU 80) and the questions of families with young children: before- and after-school care at the school (a Boys & Girls Club program in the BES gym), the school day and 2026-27 calendar, the 2026-27 bus routes (times, Main Street and Mill Street, how delays are announced), speech and special education staff and current openings, ESOL, the preschool application and its terms, how the school reaches families and whom to call, school meals and the yearly application, counselors and the social worker, the homeless liaison and what the district's policies say about foster care, the trauma-related needs the district reports, volunteering and background checks, residency papers at registration, and what bears on whether BES is "a good school" (programs, Title I, parent surveys).

14 topics, 64 facts and 2 number tables (`belmont_elementary_bus_routes_2026_27`, `belmont_elementary_staff_by_role`). Sources are the SAU 80 and BES websites, the BES 2025-26 handbook (the 2026-27 handbook was approved but not posted), the district's full policy manual (posted November 2, 2026) and rules and procedures manual (May 28, 2026), First Student's 2026-27 route sheets (September 14, 2026), the 2026-27 school calendar, the 2026-27 meal application letter, the preschool application, the 2026-27 Comprehensive Needs Assessment, the 2025-26 annual report sections (BES, Student Services, Title I), school board minutes from April 14 to November 10, 2026 (the last a draft), SchoolSpring job postings (read November 21, 2026) and the Boys & Girls Clubs of Central and Northern NH's Belmont Elementary page. No new shared topic is proposed.

Main gaps: no delayed-opening (snow delay) schedule is published anywhere checked; preschool hours and whether it is half- or full-day; the list of documents the district accepts as proof of residency (the registration page returned HTTP 403); a foster care point of contact (none is posted, and NH DOE's lists blocked automated requests); whether any BES student has missed speech or other services because of open positions; any description of trauma-informed training or of how teachers are told about a child's situation.

Privacy note for reviewers: the BES principal's 2025 annual report lists students by name for awards; the raw copy keeps a text extraction with those names removed and the PDF was not kept. Staff named in facts are only the contacts families are told to use.
