# Belmont High School, the Huot Center and the Winnisquam Career Center, and Adult Education

Part of the Belmont research (town/Belmont). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Belmont High School's (BHS) side of career and technical education: how students get to the Huot Center in Laconia and the Winnisquam Career Center (WiCC) in Tilton, the bus runs and car rules, what the school board has said about CTE costs and scheduling, and WiCC's programs; BHS's diplomas, credit rules, block schedule, Early College (formerly Running Start) courses, alternative credit, extended learning opportunities (ELOs) and the Lakes Region High School Consortium; proficiency-based grading, the Habits of Work score and how they appear on transcripts; technology and computer science courses; sports, JV teams, registration and eligibility; counseling contacts, transcript requests and records; businesses, donations and scholarships; the state's NH Scholars pathways; and adult education options for Belmont residents.

**Counts:** 11 topics, 62 facts and 2 number tables.

**Main sources:** the BHS 2026-27 Program of Studies and 2026-27 Student and Family Handbook (Google Docs exports, including the Habits of Work rubric image); BHS counseling, School-to-Career, scholarship and athletics pages; the BHS transcript request form (a scan, read in a browser listing and transcribed); SAU 80 policies IKF, IHBH, IHCD, IMBC, KCD, JJI, JRA and JLCB; Shaker Regional School Board minutes from July 2024 to November 2026 (the current lists load by JavaScript and were read in a browser; the files were downloaded directly); WiCC's site; the NH Adult Education program list; VLACS; the Lakes Region Scholarship Foundation; and Windham High's 2026-27 Program of Studies for the NH Scholars pathway table (nhscholars.org refused automated requests). Huot's own programs, schedule, admission and credentials are in `town/Laconia/high-school-and-huot.md` and are cited, not repeated.

**Main gaps:** BHS doesn't publish how many BHS credits, or which required credits, a Huot or WiCC course earns. No BHS athletic participation fee, Athletic/Co-Curricular Handbook, school profile for colleges or college acceptance list is posted. Nothing found says whether BHS hands out an NH Scholars cord. No count of BHS students at Huot or WiCC is in the minutes (a board member asked for one on October 20, 2026). The BHS "Earning College Credit" and School-to-Career "Job Opportunities" pages are empty.

**Topic note:** the NH Scholars pathway facts sit under `high-school-offerings-and-seals-rules` because NH Scholars is a state designation recorded on the diploma and transcript; it is a state program, not a law or rule, and its `review_by` is one year out.
