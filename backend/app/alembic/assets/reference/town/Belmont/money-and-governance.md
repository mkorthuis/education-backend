# Belmont: Money, Taxes, Governance and Buildings

Part of the Belmont research (town/Belmont). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 71 facts and 3 number tables on the Shaker Regional School District (SAU 80), which Belmont shares with Canterbury: its 2026-27 (FY2027) budget and what drove it, the 2027-28 budget now being built under the Question 2 tax cap, Belmont's 2025 and 2026 tax rates and why they rose, how the district's cost is split between the two towns, board seats and the new separate SAU budget vote, where budget documents, agendas, minutes and meeting video are posted, teacher resignations, the school buildings and capital plan, the Canterbury withdrawal studies, and how Belmont and Canterbury children are assigned to schools.
- **Budget:** the $30,431,829 proposal, amended to $30,546,829 at the February 5, 2026 deliberative session and passed 939–410; a 20.1% health insurance increase ($1,408,422) and other contractual costs of about $1.98 million; the board's cuts; salaries and benefits at 83.31% of spending; the operating budget's growth since 2012-13 (52%, **derived**) while enrollment fell 20% (table); the district's mandatory report on administrator and teacher pay.
- **Taxes:** Belmont's 2026 rate of $19.61 (up 12.77%) and 2025 rate of $17.39 (up 10.48%), the school share (62.32% in 2026), the town's explanation of its own increase, the loss of Belmont's extraordinary need grant, how the state education tax counts in the district's budget, and Belmont's elderly exemption.
- **Cost split:** the 1971 formula (half students, half property value), the 2026-27 shares (Belmont 75.8236%), and **derived** comparisons (tables).
- **The tax cap:** the administration's estimate of $1,144,613 in required cuts from a proposed 2027-28 budget, the potential cut lists (teachers, athletics, support staff, buildings, Chromebooks) and board members' requests to look at administrative costs. No decisions had been made as of the latest posted minutes (November 10, 2026).
- **Buildings:** ages (1936 to 1998), the posted capital plan, HVAC needs, trust fund balances, recent projects, and no bonded debt.

**Main sources:** Shaker Regional's FY2026-27 Deliberative Session Book, public hearing presentation, warrant, voter's guide, ballot results and deliberative session minutes; school board minutes from September 8 to November 10, 2026; the 2027-28 budget calendar; the 2025-26 annual report; the 2026-27 Comprehensive Needs Assessment; the Mandatory Report to Voters; the capital improvement plan and the 2020 facility needs assessment; the FY2023 audit; the 2023 Canterbury withdrawal study reports; the policy manual; the Town of Belmont's tax rate notice, assessing page, voter's guide and calendar; the site's database; and two Concord Monitor articles for facts no primary source posted.
