# Fremont: Money, Governance, the Teacher Contract and Ellis School's Building

Part of the Fremont research (town/Fremont). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics (all local), 75 facts and 4 number tables on the Fremont School District's 2026-27 budget and what the roughly $15 million pays for, the March 2026 votes that rejected both the operating budget and the teacher contract, the June 2026 special election on a revised contract, what the default budget meant in practice, the tax rate and what each cost item means per $1,000, high school tuition rates at Sanborn, Pinkerton and Epping, serving on the School Board and Budget Committee, the 2027 deliberative session and how amendments have gone, renting Ellis School's gym and fields, the building's age, condition, repairs and capital plan, and SAU 83 as a one-district SAU.

- **Budget.** The proposed 2026-27 operating budget was $14,999,581. Voters rejected it 347 to 464 on March 10, 2026, so the district runs on the $14,694,498 default budget. It pays for 507 students (October 2025), not only Ellis's 358: high school tuition is about a third of it (**derived**). The October 2026 draft for 2027-28 is $14,129,295.83.
- **Teacher contract.** A three-year contract (13%, 3.5%, 3.5%) failed 339 to 473 in March 2026; a revised two-year contract (7%, 9%) failed 287 to 578 at a special election on June 6, 2026. No new contract is in effect; negotiations restarted in August 2026.
- **Taxes.** Fremont's 2026 tax rate is $16.52 per $1,000, down from $16.95. At 2025 values, about $946,000 of spending raised from property taxes equals $1.00 per $1,000 (**derived**): the original contract's first year was $0.52, special education transportation ($617,330 proposed) about $0.65 (**derived**).
- **High school tuition.** For 2026-27 Fremont pays $27,329.63 per student at Sanborn, $19,764.55 at Epping and $17,909.87 at Pinkerton. The district has not published a net savings figure; the board asked for a five-year analysis in October 2026.
- **Building.** Ellis School dates from 1950 with five additions; its roofs were replaced in 2019 and 2020. The district has no debt, and the town's 2026-2031 capital plan lists $146,001 of school items, none of them a roof, HVAC system or bond.
- **SAU 83.** It serves only Fremont, as do 85 of 108 SAUs in the site's list (**derived**). Its office cost about 3% of the budget, and Fremont's superintendent salary ($115,000) was the lowest of six area SAUs in Sanborn's comparison.

**Main sources:** SAU 83's budget folders (FY2027 MS-27, warrant, default budget, line-item proposed budget, deliberative session and contract slides), School Board minutes from March to October 2026, the March 17, 2026 board packet (voting results, joint meeting requirements, special meeting plan), the April 14, 2026 Budget Committee hearing minutes, policies KF, BBB, BBBA, BEA, DIA and FA, the facilities folder (2019 Harriman facility analysis, 2021 roof inspection), the 2023 RFP questions and answers; the Town of Fremont's 2025 town report (school district report, voter guide, deliberative minutes for 2025 and 2026, enrollment, special education report, actual expenditures, Budget Committee report, valuation summary, tax rate history, meeting schedules), its 2026-2031 capital plan tables, calendar and news posts; Sanborn's November 2026 budget presentation (Kingston raw); the site's data.
