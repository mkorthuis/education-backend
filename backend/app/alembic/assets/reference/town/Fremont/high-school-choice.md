# High School Choice From Fremont: Pinkerton or Epping, Buses, SST, IEPs and the Sanborn Phase-Out

Part of the Fremont research (town/Fremont). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Fremont's side of high school: how the choice between Pinkerton Academy and Epping High School came about and works, what the two signed agreements say about switching schools, special education, the Seacoast School of Technology (SST) and buses, the 2026-27 high school bus times and the absence of a late bus, the three receiving schools' 2026-27 calendars, the phase-out at Sanborn Regional High School, how many Fremont students attend each school, Fremont's own athletics comparison, the AP and course differences between the two choices, Epping's credit recovery options and a town scholarship fund. The receiving schools themselves are already researched: Pinkerton in `town/pinkerton/`, Epping High and SST access from Epping in `town/Epping/middle-high-school.md` and `support-safety-and-choices.md`, Sanborn and SST from Sanborn in `town/Kingston/middle-high-and-sst.md`, and SST in `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md`. This file cites them and adds Fremont's side.
