# Ellis School's Upper Grades, Support and Choices: Special Education, Middle School Programs, Homeschool Access, Counseling, Devices and Math Results

Part of the Fremont research (town/Fremont). Gathered 2026-11-21; updated 2026-11-22 with the main session's browser pass of sau83.org and documents from SAU 83's online library.

## Summary

Covers Ellis School's middle grades and the support around them: co-teaching, the RISE program, special education and therapy staff, paraprofessional and out-of-district costs, and the Special Education Expendable Trust Fund; the grades 6–8 team and schedule, unified arts (and the absence of a shop or technology education class), media classes, robotics, hands-on science, Modern Band, clubs, high school credit for 7th and 8th grade work, gifted policy and Algebra I (federal data); the five middle school teams, eligibility rules and the activity budget; homeschooled and EFA students' access under policy JJJ, the out-of-date home education policy, and the high school selection form; VLACS; counselors, the psychologist, the new behavioral health coordinator, social-emotional grants, the Getting to Y student group and suicide prevention policy; bullying reports, the bus rules and bus company; Chromebooks, their fee and the phone ban; and the math results, the district's own comparison and its math intervention plan.

12 topics (11 local, plus 1 fact added to the library's `home-education-rules`), 71 facts and 4 number tables (fremont_special_education_spending, fremont_special_education_trust_fund, fremont_ellis_support_staff_crdc, fremont_fy27_budget_selected_lines). Sources: the school district's sections of the Town of Fremont's 2024 and 2025 annual reports; SAU 83 pages read in Chrome by the main session (raw/browser/); documents downloaded directly from SAU 83's document host (the 2026-27 handbook, policies JICJ, JICK, JICC, JJJ, JJJ-R, JJIC, IHBG, IHBB, IMBD, IHBI, JICL-R and JLDBB, the athlete agreement, technology plan, welcome letters, unified arts competencies, the FY27 proposed budget and deliberative session materials, the high school selection form and 15 sets of 2025–2026 school board minutes); federal CRDC rows for Ellis; VLACS's middle school page; RSA 193-A:4. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 19 MB).

**Main gaps.** Not found: middle school team rosters or how many students try out; Ellis's current math course list (whether Algebra I is taught now); whether Chromebooks go home nightly and whether families can opt out; whether grades 6–8 share lunch, recess or hallways with younger grades; whether any IEP services were missed during staff vacancies; the March 2026 results for the trust fund articles; and SAU 83's technology page, which could not be read even in Chrome. ## district-special-education-services: Special Education at Ellis: Co-Teaching, RISE, Staffing, Therapy Spending and the Trust Fund

1. At Ellis School, math and English language arts in grades 6, 7 and 8 are co-taught by a general education teacher and a special education teacher (grades 7 and 8 since 2024-25, grade 6 added in 2025-26), and the middle grades also have self-contained math and English sections with modified instruction for students with greater needs.
   - citation: Fremont School District, 2025 Annual Report, "Special Education"; Report of the Superintendent (2024 town report), "Special Education"
   - source: https://www.fremont.nh.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2580
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Fremont
   - note: 2025 report: "The special education team has successfully expanded the co-teaching model into grade six, after a great many identified and unidentified students benefited measurably from having access to that model of instructional delivery in seventh and eighth grade last year. This model allows Ellis students to receive instruction in math and English language arts from a general education teacher and a special education teacher. Middle school services remain flexible and are adapted to accommodate greater levels of need by providing self-contained sections of math and English designed to close achievement gaps through modified instruction." 2024 report (https://www.fremont.nh.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2667): "successfully implemented a co-teaching model for grades 7 and 8".    - citation: Report of the Superintendent (2024 town report), "Special Education"; Fremont School District, 2025 Annual Report, "Special Education"
