# Fremont Residents' Questions

The questions the Fremont research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Fremont is SAU 83. The Fremont School District runs Ellis School (kindergarten to grade 8). Its high school students attend Pinkerton Academy or Epping High School under tuition contracts, with seniors finishing at Sanborn Regional under the old contract, and may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology. Several questions contain premises (a school budget near $15 million, switching away from Sanborn saving money, about 43% proficient in math, cost per pupil over $22,000, a tiny one-school SAU) that the research should check rather than assume.
