# Epping Middle High School: Schedule, Grading, Diplomas, Advanced Courses, SST and College Help

Part of the Epping research (town/Epping). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Epping Middle High School (EMHS, grades 6–12 in one building at 33 Prescott Road): the A/B block schedule and lunch periods, competency-based grading and semester reporting, the three-tier diploma, AP, honors and Early College courses, electives and clubs, middle school course placement and the move to grade 6, the counseling office's college, FAFSA and scholarship help, and Epping's side of the Seacoast School of Technology (SST). SST itself is in `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md`; this file adds only Epping's rules and numbers. All local facts are placed `district Epping`; two facts about Great Bay Community College have no place.

- **Main sources:** EMHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies (74 pages), 2026-27 Student & Parent Handbook (Google Doc, including its SST weather and in-service table), 2026-27 School Profile, 2026-27 daily schedule, the district's 2026-27 calendar, the counseling office's page, department flyer and career-exploration sheet, the EHS Scholarship Guide, policies IHCD, IK, IKA, IKC and IKF (the graduation policy, revised September 3, 2026), the district's 2023, 2024 and 2025 annual reports, 23 sets of public school board minutes (August 2023 to October 2026), the EMHS online calendar (August–December 2026), the 2023-24 federal Civil Rights Data Collection rows for Epping High, VLACS's tuition page and Great Bay Community College's how-to-apply page.
