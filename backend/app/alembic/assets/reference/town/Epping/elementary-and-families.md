# Epping Elementary School and Young Families

Part of the Epping research (town/Epping). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Epping Elementary School (EES, preschool to grade 5) and the years before it: when children may be dropped off and the town's before- and after-school program, the 2026-27 bus routes and the new bus-tracking app, delays, early dismissals, snow days and remote days, school meals and the free and reduced-price application, changing a child's bus stop, help with coats, field trips and other costs, the district's integrated preschool (how children get a place, what it costs and its hours), the parent portal and the other systems families use, homework help and Title I, whom to tell about bullying at recess or on the bus, English learner services, translation, and volunteering.

- **12 topics, 100 facts and 1 number table.** Places: `district Epping` for the school district's schools, preschool, policies, staff and pages; `town Epping` for Epping Parks and Recreation, the town welfare office and food pantry, and the Community Children's Fund's service area.
- **New data concepts:** the Recreation Department's before- and after-school program at EES (6:45 a.m. to 5:45 p.m., spring 2027 prices, closures); bus ride lengths derived from the six 2026-27 EES route sheets; the FirstView bus-tracking app; the district's rule for moving a child's bus stop (policy EEA, item 15); the remote day plan (8:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.); preschool tuition ($150 a month), its tuition waiver, sessions and location at the SAU 14 office; the Feed it Forward meal account; the Community Children's Fund's 2026 grant to the Epping School District; the volunteer fingerprinting steps and hours; ESOL staffing, service hours and exit score.
- **Main sources:** the 2026-27 EES Parent/Student/Staff Handbook (updated July 10, 2026), the EES FAQ, the 2026-27 preschool handbook and orientation slides, the SAU 14 preschool, food services, transportation, school closings, ESOL, homelessness, counseling, volunteer and bullying pages, the 2026 Lau plan, the Title I plan, the EES remote day guide, the 2026-27 calendar, policies EEA, EBCD, EFAA, EFC, IC, IJOC, IKB, JICC, JICK, JQ and KB, the 2026-27 bus route sheets, the ParentSquare bus post, the FirstView flyer, the Epping Recreation youth programs page and spring 2027 form, the Epping PTO site, the Community Children's Fund site and its October 2026 press release, and the town's welfare and food pantry pages.
- **Derived figures:** bus ride lengths (number table); the Recreation program's daily rates ($12 a morning, $18 an afternoon); that the site's licensed child care data doesn't list the Recreation program.
- **Privacy:** the bus route sheets list stops by house number, with a note naming one rider. Only a redacted copy was kept (house numbers replaced, the note removed), and the facts give route areas and times, not stops.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Drop-off before 7:15 and care until 5:30 or 6:00" (P1.1): partly. EES opens its doors at 8:15 a.m. and doesn't allow earlier drop-off. Epping Parks and Recreation runs a before-school program in the EES gym from 6:45 a.m. and an after-school program until 5:45 p.m., so care reaches 5:30 but not 6:00.
  - "An app to see if the bus is late" (P1.2): correct. The district launched First Student's FirstView app for 2026-27, with live bus location and delay notices.
  - "Remote days now, or days added in June?" (P1.3): both are possible. The district has a remote day plan (8:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., Chromebooks sent home with all K–5 students), and the handbook says remote days "may also be an option." The last day, June 18, 2027, is listed "pending snow days." No posted board policy on remote learning was found, and no record was found of how many remote or snow days were used in 2025-26.
  - "A preschool lottery, or mostly for special needs?" (P10.1): the current SAU page and 2026-27 preschool handbook describe a waiting list with "limited spots for peer model children," filled in late April and early May for the fall. An older EES department page says families "enroll their children in the lottery" and that half the children have identified special needs and half are peer models. No page says how waitlisted children are chosen.
  - "Cost, and half-day or full-day?" (P10.2): $150 a month, September to June, for peer models (children on IEPs pay nothing; a tuition waiver is available). Half-day only: Monday to Thursday, 8:30–11:00 or 12:30–3:00. The preschool meets at the SAU 14 Central Office, 213 Main Street, not in the EES building.
  - "Turns five right around September" (P10.3): the cutoff is five before September 30, with individual exceptions decided by the principal (library).
  - "A dedicated ESL teacher at EES" (P16.1): partly. Epping has one state-certified ESOL teacher, who is also the program coordinator, serving both EES and Epping Middle High School.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** 1 of 15 (P10.3, library `kindergarten-entry-cutoff` fact 24). The library supplied the meal income limits, bullying law, English learner and translation rules, and other towns' files the snow day and meal identification rules; these are pointed to, not repeated.
- **Main gaps:** EES's 2026-27 after-school clubs (the handbook names past ones, such as Homework Club, but lists none for this year); whether the Recreation program takes NH Child Care Scholarships; ride times on Routes 1, 3, 5 and 6 in the afternoon (not printed); whether ParentSquare's automatic translation is turned on and whether report cards are translated; an EES supply list; whether any remote days were used in 2025-26.
- **Proposed shared topic:** none. Every slug here is in the shared list.
- **Spot checks:** three facts were re-checked against the raw copies and matched: the Recreation program's hours and spring prices (6:45–8:15 and 3:15–5:45; $1,245 and $1,854), preschool tuition and sessions ($150 a month; 8:30–11:00 and 12:30–3:00), and policy EEA item 15 on bus stop changes.
