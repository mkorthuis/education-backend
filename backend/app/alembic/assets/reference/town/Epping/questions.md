# Epping Residents' Questions

The questions the Epping research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Epping is SAU 14, a one-district SAU. The Epping School District runs Epping Elementary School (preschool to grade 5), Epping Middle School (grades 6–8) and Epping High School (grades 9–12); the middle and high schools share a building. Epping High students may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (over $23,000 a student, 38% proficiency, a special meeting over a budget form mix-up, an A/B block schedule with semester grades, about 220 high school students, AP courses that change each year, a new state law putting SAU costs on their own warrant article, new roofs, boilers and air handlers, 24-, 27- and 30-credit diplomas) that the research should check rather than assume.
