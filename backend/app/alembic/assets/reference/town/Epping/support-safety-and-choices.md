   - citation: RSA 200:50
   - source: https://www.gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/200/200-50.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15

II. The student-athlete shall also present written permission from a parent or guardian to return to play." The NHIAA reprints this law in its concussion guidelines (By-Law Article III, Sect. 13).
