# Strafford Residents' Questions

The questions the Strafford research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Strafford is SAU 105. The Strafford School District runs Strafford School (preschool to grade 8), and its high school students attend Coe-Brown Northwood Academy under a 2023–2033 tuition contract, with a small Dover High agreement besides. Several questions contain premises ($1.25 million in cuts, a $67,000 boiler repair, playgrounds at the end of their life, teaching positions cut in December, a dedicated bus to Northwood, a December special budget hearing) that the research should check rather than assume.
