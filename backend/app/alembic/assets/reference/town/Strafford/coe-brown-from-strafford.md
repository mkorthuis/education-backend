# Coe-Brown From Strafford

Part of the Strafford research (town/Strafford). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers high school for Strafford students at Coe-Brown Northwood Academy from Strafford's side: the 2023–2033 agreement (admission, reassignment, special education, transportation, career programs, home-educated students, reports and the Education Committee seat), how families register, how many Strafford students attend, Strafford's three Coe-Brown bus runs and the plans to cut one, the lack of a late bus, Coe-Brown's September 2026 survey of Strafford's freshmen, Strafford's special education spending at Coe-Brown, how Strafford's budget limits do and don't reach Coe-Brown's sports and clubs, getting to fall preseason practice before 9th grade, Coe-Brown's trustee reports to Strafford (AP, college credit, certifications, graduates' plans), and what Coe-Brown says about who governs it. Coe-Brown's own programs were researched in `town/Barrington/high-school-choice.md`, `town/Deerfield/high-school-choice.md` and `town/Northwood/coe-brown-from-northwood.md`; this file cites them and adds Strafford's side.

- **Places:** 40 facts use `district Strafford`, 9 `district Coe-Brown Northwood Academy`, and 3 have no place (NHIAA rules).
- **New data concepts:** the agreement's Education Committee seat and its 90% condition; Coe-Brown's position that its trustees and committee minutes are not public records; Strafford's three Coe-Brown runs with start times and ridership, the cost of each run and the options to cut them; Strafford freshmen's own ratings of their preparation, by subject; Strafford's Coe-Brown special education counts, paraprofessionals and the per-student charge; Coe-Brown's graduates' plans for 2023–2025 from its trustee reports; NHIAA fall practice dates for incoming freshmen.

| P4.3 | A dedicated bus to Northwood | **Not dedicated.** Strafford runs three Coe-Brown runs on buses that also run elementary routes; the high school part costs $91.20 a bus a day ($46,904 budgeted for 2026-27). State law makes high school busing optional. |
| P5.2 | Coe-Brown's "Assisted Study program" for special education | **Not a special education program in the sources.** Coe-Brown describes Assisted Study as a smaller, structured study hall for students who need one; its special education services are separate (resource courses and paraprofessionals). The agreement makes Coe-Brown run case management, invites Strafford to every IEP meeting and asks for an agreed IEP before enrollment. |
| P6.2 | Strafford parents have a say at a trustee-run academy | **Through the school board only.** One Strafford School Board member sits as a voting member on the trustees' Education Committee (while Strafford sends at least 90% of its public high schoolers, 95% in September 2026, derived), and the two boards meet twice a year. Coe-Brown says its trustees are not a public body and the committee's minutes are not public records. |
| P12.3 | A late bus to Strafford after sports | **None found.** Each run has one afternoon trip, leaving about five minutes after the 2:24 p.m. dismissal. |
| P14.1 | Strafford students get into Coe-Brown automatically | **Correct.** Coe-Brown must accept "up to all" eligible Strafford residents; families file SAU 105's assignment form and proof of residency, and the board chair signs. |
| P16.1 | A Math Lab and college counseling | **Math Lab is correct** (open during every study hall). No Coe-Brown source read describes FAFSA or essay help; its catalog says counselors help with "financial aid," and it runs a Post-High School Planning Fair. |
