# Strafford: School Money, the Tax Cap, the Building and SAU 105

Part of the Strafford research (town/Strafford). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 66 facts and 2 number tables on the Strafford School District's (SAU 105's) money and governance.
- **The budget and the cap:** the administration's proposed FY28 (2027-28) budget ($15,841,849, up $1,389,109 or 9.6%) and what drives it (Coe-Brown tuition, special education, special education buses); Strafford's 61.8% "yes" on Question 2, which brings the RSA 32:5-i tax cap and 6% SAU cap to Strafford's FY28 budget; the board's $1.25 million reduction from the proposed budget and its November 10 "what-if" list of positions and programs; the state aid the cap can't make up (the hold-harmless step-down, **derived**); tuition under the cap; retaining fund balance; and what the cap could mean on one tax bill (**derived**).
- **Taxes:** the tax year 2026 rate ($14.12 after the 2026 revaluation, not yet in the site's data), the school share of the bill (about 70%, **derived**), and what the town's share pays for (police, fire, highways).
- **Governance:** board meetings and public comment, the December 2 Preliminary Budget Hearing and the 2027 budget calendar, the traditional annual meeting (an SB2 petition failed 17–75 in 2021), how a cap override would be voted, and SAU 105's history and costs since it left SAU 44 in 2021.
- **The building:** the 1966 school (assessor's record), the 2010 renovation bond (paid off in 2030), the October 2026 boiler and water heater failures ($67,210 from the capital reserve), the playground audit and quotes, capital reserve balances, the facility condition assessment and the list of aging systems.
- **Also:** community use of the gym and fields under policy KF (its fee schedule, insurance rules and the board's usual fee waiver for Strafford Recreational Sports), and the town's elderly, disabled and veterans' tax relief.

**Main sources:** the school board's November 18, 2026 packet (minutes of October 14, October 28 and November 10, the superintendent's report, the fund balance memo, the draft FY2025 audit), the FY28 budget planning snapshot (November 17, 2026), the 2026-27 board and budget calendar, the 2025 Town Report's school district section (FY27 budget, warrant, tax impact, revenues, bond schedule), the school district annual meeting minutes of 2020, 2021 and 2026, the Strafford SAU Planning Committee report (October 2019), the 2026 Mandatory Report to Voters, the town's tax rate and revaluation pages, the 2026 town meeting minutes, the town assessor's record for the school, and the site's tax, aid and Question 2 data.
