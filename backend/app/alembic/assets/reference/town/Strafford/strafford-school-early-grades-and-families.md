# Strafford School: Early Grades and Families

Part of the Strafford research (town/Strafford). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Strafford School (preschool to grade 8, 22 Roller Coaster Road) from a young family's side: the seven 2026-27 elementary bus routes and their morning ride times, what the district is weighing about cutting a bus, storm delays shared with Coe-Brown's buses, the school day and the car line, absences and PickUp Patrol, Strafford KinderCampus before- and after-school care at the school and its prices, the Little Spartans Preschool's half-day sessions, tuition and enrollment, homeroom sizes for every grade in October 2026, the school nurse and food allergies, meals and free and reduced-price applications, reading screening and tutoring, Alma and the other parent systems, and the papers needed to register and prove residency.

**Counts:** 9 topics, 56 facts, 2 number tables (2026-27 morning bus runs, derived; homeroom sizes, October 2026 and June 2025).

**Main sources:** the Strafford School website (Arrival & Dismissal with the embedded 2026-27 elementary bus routes, Food Services, School Apps from Home, Literacy Resources, Little Spartans Preschool, Student Registration, Nurse), the SAU 105 School Board and Other Important Information pages, the draft 2026-27 Pre-K–5 Student & Family Handbook from the August 12, 2026 board packet, the adopted 2026-27 calendar, the November 18, 2026 board packet (minutes of October 14, October 28 and November 10, 2026 and the October 2026 enrollment report), the FY2028 budget planning snapshot, the December 2 special budget hearing flyer, board minutes from September 2025 to September 2026, the Transportation Committee minutes and memo, the June 2025 enrollment report, the Proof of Residency Form, the 2026-27 free and reduced-price meal letter, Strafford KinderCampus's website and family handbook, and Coe-Brown's 2026-27 handbook (storm procedure).

**Main gaps:** the published 2026-27 handbooks (the district's handbook page is a script app whose text could not be read; the August draft was used, and the board approved it with changes); afternoon bus times (not printed); any plan for fewer bus routes, or what ride times would be with six buses (none posted); the preschool's current waitlist length and 2027-28 lottery date; whether the nurse is in the building every hour of the school day; the results of the school's exit survey; and whether any plan exists to change the car line (none found in the minutes).
