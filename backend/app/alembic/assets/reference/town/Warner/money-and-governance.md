# Warner: Money, Taxes, Closing Plans, Budget Votes, Staffing and Homeschooling Rules

Part of the Warner research (town/Warner). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 43 facts and 4 number tables on how Warner's schools are paid for and governed: the Kearsarge Regional School District (SAU 65) budget, Warner's tax bill, the closing plans for Simonds and Sutton Central, budget votes and caps, staff and substitutes, vacation weeks, and homeschooling and Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs).
- **Spending per student:** why the district's total budget works out to more than $33,000 a student while NH DOE's cost per pupil is $27,691, where the 2026-27 operating budget goes (by function and, for Simonds, by cost type), and what grew most since 2024-25.
- **Warner's tax bill:** the town's 2026 rates (total $17.81 per $1,000, of which $10.40 is the two school lines), why the rate fell from $30.89 in 2024 to $17.32 in 2025 (the 2025 revaluation roughly doubled the town's assessed value), how Warner's bills are issued, Warner's 15.45% share of Kearsarge's costs, the highest local school rate of the seven towns, what the state pays toward Warner's share, and what a $1 million change or a $3.5 million bond payment would mean for Warner.
- **Simonds and Sutton Central:** the September and October 2026 board votes, the board's October 22 vote to stop planning for Simonds's closing, what the new state closing law means for a Simonds petition, the building-needs figures, Warner's children by school, and what Warner voters, residents and the library and MainStreet Warner groups said.
- **Budget process, votes and caps:** the documents the district posts, the October to January budget cycle, how every operating budget vote since 2022 turned out, the failed 2022 bond, the January 2025 cap petition (rejected 1,435 to 113) and what the superintendent said it would cut, the November 2026 Question 2 result, and the share of the budget that goes to sports and clubs.
- **Staff and calendar:** the teachers' (KREA) contract, the November 22 job board (substitutes, paras, nurse, custodian), background checks, federal teacher-retention counts for Simonds and the high school, Simonds retirements, bus costs, the 2026-27 vacation weeks against Concord's and Hopkinton's.
- **Homeschooling and EFAs:** which Warner providers the EFA marketplace lists, and how the library's rules (home education after July 1, 2026; EFA agreements) answer the testing and rules questions.

**Main sources:** the Kearsarge Regional School District's 59th Annual Report (March 2026), its 2026-27 budget documents (function and object reports, summary by school, revenue estimates, charts, local tax rate chart), 2025 warrant, 2022 to 2026 election results, 2026-27 calendar, mandatory report to voters, and the district's budget page and job board (Frontline); school board minutes of September 3, 10, 17 and 24, October 8 and 22 and November 5, 2026 (the first six approved, the last unapproved); the Town of Warner's tax and assessing pages, 2025 annual report, 2025 revaluation report and tax rate explainer; RSA 189:13-a, 194:3-f and 194:3-g; the Concord Monitor's reporting of the January 2025 cap petition and the September 2026 closing vote; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (2023-24); the EFA marketplace download; and the site's database (cost per pupil, test scores, class size, staff, salaries, Question 2 votes, adequacy aid, EFAs).
