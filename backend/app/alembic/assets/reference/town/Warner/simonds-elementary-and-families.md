# Simonds Elementary and Warner Families

Part of the Warner research (town/Warner). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Simonds Elementary School (kindergarten to grade 5, 14 Church Street, Warner) in the Kearsarge Regional School District (SAU 65) and the questions of Warner families with young children: the Boys & Girls Club's before-school, after-school and vacation program at Simonds; the Warner Early Learning Center on West Main Street (also a Boys & Girls Club program) and other child care near Warner; the Simonds school day and the 2026–27 changes to it; kindergarten; buses, assigned stops and storm calls; Remote Learning Days and devices; picking a child up and who may sign for him; counselors; how the school reaches families; a sick child; and Warner's own roads, sidewalks, community center and library.

12 topics, 36 facts and 1 number table. All topics are shared or library topics.

**Sources.** kearsarge.org's web pages answered automated requests with HTTP 403 on 2026-11-22 (the Simonds school page and policy EEA were tried; see Not retrieved), so Simonds's own web pages and the board policies could not be read. Files the district posts under /sites/g/files/ were reachable, and the main source is Simonds's Family Handbook for 2026–27 (updated August 2026, 30 pages), with the 2021–22 and 2025–26 editions for comparison. The Boys & Girls Clubs of Central and Northern New Hampshire's own pages (nhyouth.org) give the Simonds club's hours and fees and the Early Learning Center's ages, hours and fees. Also used: the state's child care data as loaded in the site's database (NH DHHS Child Care Search); the district's March 2026 annual report (read from New London's saved copy); the Town of Warner's website, 2025 annual report, Class VI road policy and 2009 Safe Routes to School plan; the Pillsbury Free Library's pages; and the library's Head Start file. Earlier files supplied the district's calendar, closing alerts, student support and registration guide (cited by file, topic and fact number).

**Main gaps:** the Simonds bus routes and stops for 2026–27 and policy EEA (how stops are set and whether buses use unsafe roads); how far a child must live from Simonds to ride a bus; whether the Club has a waiting list for Simonds before-school care right now (the state shows openings; the Club's enrollment portal is a script-driven page that couldn't be read); whether the Club opens on Remote Learning Days; what a family without working home internet can do on a Remote Learning Day; any paper-notice option; the Simonds counselor's own page; drive times from Warner to Concord.

Privacy note for reviewers: people are named in no fact. In the saved handbooks the PTO president's name and staff email addresses are left out; the Club's site director and center director names and emails are left out of the saved Club pages; the 2025 town report is saved only as an excerpt (speakers' names left out) because the full report lists residents' births, marriages and deaths by name; the 2009 Safe Routes plan is saved without its page of committee members' names.
