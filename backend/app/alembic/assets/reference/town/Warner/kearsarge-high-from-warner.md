# Kearsarge High and the CRTC, From Warner

Part of the Warner research (town/Warner). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what a Warner family needs to know about Kearsarge Regional High School (KRHS, 457 North Road, North Sutton) and the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC), beyond what New London's research (`town/New London/kearsarge-high-and-career-programs.md`) already holds: the Warner late bus and drive times from Warner, how much of a day the CRTC takes, shop and hands-on classes, certifications, money-skills courses, making up a failed course (KRHS says it has no summer school), financial aid help, Section 504 plans, the cost of sports, work-study partners, the Kearsarge Adult Diploma Program, and scholarships open to Warner graduates (including trade school). Facts that New London's file already states (AP and honors, Early College, the 20 career-center seats, work-study credit, ELOs, the late bus sheet, policy JJIC) are cited, not repeated.

- **10 fact topics, 27 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 0.5 MB): KRHS's 2025-26 and 2026-27 Programs of Studies and excerpts of its 2022-23 and 2024-25 handbooks (`krhs/`), the district's Section 504 Handbook (`krhs/`), excerpts of 15 of the 20 KRHS newsletters read, from September 2023 to August 2025 (`newsletters/`), the 2026-27 late bus sheet (`transport/`), the 2024-25 annual report's athletics and high school pages (`reports/`), the ELO pages and Graduate Portfolio handbook (`elo/`, `capstone/`), the CRTC's 2026-27 Program of Studies (`crtc/`), the KRHS 2025 community scholarship packet and the CRTC's 2026 scholarship list (`scholarships/`), the Warner 2025 annual report's trust funds pages (`town/`), NH Adult Education pages (`adult/`), RSA 193:26-a (`law/`) and route lookups (`geo/`).
- **Places:** 22 facts use `district Kearsarge Regional`, 4 use `town Warner` and 1 uses `district Concord` (the CRTC's scholarship list; the CRTC is run by Concord's school district).
