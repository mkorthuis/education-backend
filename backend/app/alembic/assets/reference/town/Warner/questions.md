# Warner Residents' Questions

The questions the Warner research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Warner's students attend the Kearsarge Regional School District (SAU 65), shared with Bradford, New London, Newbury, Springfield, Sutton and Wilmot: Simonds Elementary School in Warner (kindergarten to grade 5), then Kearsarge Regional Middle and High Schools in North Sutton, with the Concord Regional Technical Center and NHTI nearby. Several questions contain premises (more than $33,000 per student, a Warner Early Learning Center on West Main, a Boys & Girls Club before-school program at Simonds, James House Preschool, a possible closing of Sutton Central or Simonds, a new teachers' contract, Running Start through NHTI) that the research should check rather than assume.
