# Hampton's K–8 Schools and Daily Life

Part of the Hampton research (town/Hampton). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Hampton School District's (SAU 90) three schools as families meet them day to day: the Centre School preschool and its HATCh playgroup, kindergarten aides, before- and after-school care (the Southern District YMCA at Marston School and its Hampton Center), after-school clubs at all three schools, reading and math teaching, devices, counselors, Hampton Academy's advisory, mentoring and sports, registering a new student, the 2026-27 calendar and early releases set beside Winnacunnet's, attendance, the bus company, and the town's flood alerts, flood-prone roads and the Meadow Pond drainage work.

- **13 topics, 56 facts, 1 number table.** All topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; no new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/k8/` (about 37 MB). Large PDFs (the district's 2023-2024 Annual Report, the town's 2025 report, the state's population projections) are saved as excerpts of the cited pages, with the full URL in each `.txt`.
- **The district's website was blocked to the research agents and read later in Chrome.** Every page on sau90.org and its school subdomains (centre-school., adeline-c-marston-school., hampton-academy.) returned a "Client Challenge" bot check on November 16, 2026, On November 17 the main session read the handbook, the storm-closing page and four policies (JEB, EEA, IHBB, JICJ) in Chrome and added eight facts (district-school-day-and-calendar 8–10, district-transportation 4–6, district-technology-and-phones 3, district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners 7); bus route files, newsletters, staff lists and curriculum pages are still unread (listed under "Not retrieved"). The vendor's mirror host was not used. What the district says here comes from its own documents on other hosts: the 2026-27 calendar (via the district's own short link), the 2023-2024 Annual Report (published for the March 2024 meeting, so its school reports describe 2023-24), its Google Sites, district press releases printed on Patch, and two copies of district pages the library saved on November 8, 2026.
- **Sources:** SAU 90's 2026-27 calendar; SAU 21's 2026-27 calendar; the Hampton School District 2023-2024 Annual Report; the Centre preschool, HATCh and SAU 90 counselors Google Sites and the HATCh flyers; the Southern District YMCA's school-age care, locations, vacation and FAQ pages; Patch (March 2026 literacy report; district releases on robotics, February 2025, and Unified Basketball, March 2026); Foster's Daily Democrat via AOL (September 2024); the Town of Hampton's Flood Alerts, Emergency Preparedness and Coastal Resilience pages and 2025 Town Report; NH OPD's 2022 population projections; and the site's database (enrollment, class sizes, staff, child care).
- **Main gaps:** pickup line times; bus route times, late buses and any flood rerouting; the district's attendance and enrollment policies (JH, JEC); Hampton Academy's devices; the number of kindergarten classes and aides; YMCA rates; and any enrollment projection or building capacity figure. Policy JEB, read in Chrome, confirms the library's kindergarten cutoff: "before September 30" (library `kindergarten-entry-cutoff` 33 is right).
- **Spot-checked against raw copies:** the calendar's "Early Release: Elementary - 12noon; Hampton Academy - 11:30am" (`sau90-calendar-2026-27.txt` and `.png`); the Annual Report's "Hampton Academy includes a daily advisory in their schedule" (`sau90-annual-report-2023-24-excerpt.txt`, PDF page 46); and the Public Works report's "Flooding (while it still occurs from Meadow Pond overtopping the properties and roadway from the rear) is quickly alleviated in hours versus days" (`hampton-town-report-2025-excerpt.txt`, PDF page 286). | P12.3 | Bell schedules and early-release days across the buildings may not "line up" | The three K–8 schools share one calendar. It shares four of its five early-release dates with Winnacunnet's (all but March 31), but elementary students leave at 12 noon and Hampton Academy students at 11:30 a.m. Winnacunnet starts a day earlier for ninth graders, closes three days when the K–8 schools are open, and has a tentative last day of June 16 against June 21. The K–8 bell times are 8:20 to 2:52 (Centre and Marston) and 7:20 to 2:20 (Hampton Academy) (district-school-day-and-calendar 8). Winnacunnet runs 7:45 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. and its early-release classes end at 11:56 a.m. (`town/winnacunnet/winnacunnet-high-school.md`, district-classrooms-and-staff 5). |
| P6.2 | Hampton Academy students may rely "on Chromebooks for everything" | Not measurable from the sources. Hampton Academy's device practice was not found. The 2023-24 reports say every Centre School student has a device and Marston's Chromebooks were replaced, while Hampton Academy math classes use group problem-solving at whiteboards and its staff trained in writing instruction. State law requires cursive and the multiplication tables (Londonderry `required-instruction-rules` 1). |
| P20.1 | Centre and Marston may be "crowded" in five years | No enrollment projection or building capacity figure was found. Enrollment has fallen: in the site's data, Centre's K–2 enrollment went from 303 (2019-20) to 282 (2024-25), Marston's from 350 to 303, and Hampton's resident K–8 enrollment from 1,034 to 899 (2025-26). Average class sizes in 2025-26 were 15.8 (grades 1–2) and 17.7 (grades 3–4), below the state's limits. The state projects Hampton's total population to grow about 8% from 2020 to 2030, but it does not project children. |
