# Hampton Residents' Questions

The questions the Hampton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hampton has two school districts. The Hampton School District (SAU 90) runs preschool through grade 8: Hampton Centre School (preschool to grade 2), Adeline C. Marston School (grades 3–5) and Hampton Academy (grades 6–8). Its high school students attend Winnacunnet High School, run by the Winnacunnet Cooperative School District (SAU 21) with Hampton Falls, North Hampton and Seabrook; South Hampton tuitions its students there. Career and technical programs are at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. Both districts vote by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (a $32 million budget, HVAC and auditorium seat articles, $350,000 of maintenance at Centre School and Batchelder Pond, a petition to print the tax impact on a $600,000 home, Hampton's 47% share of Winnacunnet's budget, the Hawley Library redesign, coastal revaluations, buses and services for Sacred Heart students, PreK at Centre School) that the research should check rather than assume.
