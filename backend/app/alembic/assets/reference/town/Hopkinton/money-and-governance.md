# Hopkinton: Money, Buildings, Staff and the School Board

Part of the Hopkinton research (town/Hopkinton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 69 facts and 6 number tables on the Hopkinton School District's (SAU 66) money and governance.
- **Taxes and state aid:** how the school levy grew against enrollment since 2014, why the rate per $1,000 fell in 2024, the FY27 tax estimate and what moved it, how SWEPT and the adequacy grant fit together for Hopkinton (with the FY2023 SWEPT cut as a worked example), the contingency fund used to cut taxes, and what Question 2 (HB 1300) would and wouldn't have done locally.
- **Budget and costs:** the FY27 vote (220–67), what the $29.3 million budget buys, the FY28 proposal ($30,195,892, +3.05%), out-of-district special education (12 placements, $1.57 million budgeted), the CRTC tuition and bus costs and the state's CRTC shortfall, contracts (teachers to 2028, support staff in negotiation, custodians to 2029), health insurance (14% average rate rise for FY28 against a 2.01% budget rise), retirement, and the district's 10-year cost per pupil adjusted for inflation.
- **Buildings:** the capital improvement plan (CIP) as of October 26, 2026, the trust and its deposits, the 2026 high school boiler and oil tank replacement, every scheduled roof, the security redesign, the 2019 building bonds and the projects with no funding yet.
- **Classrooms and staff:** the class size policy, K–6 class sizes, October 1, 2026 enrollment, the 2023 strategic plan's enrollment projections against actual counts, space concerns, and the resignations and principal changes recorded in 2026 board minutes.
- **Board and public input:** meeting times and place, live and recorded video, emailed public comment, the comment policy, the Diligent portal and committees, the budget process and turnout.
- **Calendar, goals, community:** the 2026-27 calendar against Concord's, the calendar policy, the strategic plan's awards and surveys, the board's 2026-27 goals, and the 2026-27 public concert calendar.

**Main sources:** the district's FY27 and FY28 budget pages and their Google Drive files (budget presentation, line-item operations budgets, personnel budget, snapshot, FAQ, special education overview and primer, HB 1300 FAQ and slides, CIP spreadsheet), the 2026 warrant and the March 14, 2026 annual meeting minutes, the Diligent Community board portal (2026 agendas, minutes and attachments: enrollment memo, facilities update, CRTC budget transfer, superintendent's update), the three union contracts, policies BEDH, IIB and IC, the 2023–2028 strategic plan, the Mandatory Report to Voters, the building project pages, the music department's calendar, the board's YouTube playlist, and the site's database (tax, adequacy aid, enrollment, cost per pupil, test scores, class size, salaries).

## district-tax-and-state-aid: Hopkinton's School Taxes and State Aid

From tax year 2014 to tax year 2025, the amount Hopkinton raised in local property taxes for its school district rose from $12,862,749 to $21,447,360, about 67% (**derived**), while its resident students went from 849 in 2014-15 to 877 in 2025-26; the district counted 888 students on October 1, 2026, 23 fewer than a year earlier.
