# Harold Martin School and Young Families

Part of the Hopkinton research (town/Hopkinton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Harold Martin School (HMS, preschool to grade 3, 271 Main Street, Hopkinton) and the things young Hopkinton families ask about: the district preschool and how children get a place, after-school care at HMS and Maple Street, school hours and the staggered dismissals, the shared bus runs and pickup times, snow days, delays and early releases, PickUp Patrol, Chromebook use in kindergarten to grade 3, the reading program and reading screening, volunteering and background checks, school lunch, kindergarten registration, short-term bus changes, help with clothing and meals, and class visits to the Hopkinton Town Library.

- **17 topics, 67 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 24 MB): the district and HMS SchoolBlocks pages (home, hours, district profile, registration, after-school plans, communication, Title I, special education, food service, student services); the 2026-27 HMS Student/Family Handbook; the 2026-27 district calendar; the 2026-27 free and reduced-price meal letter, the "More than a Meal" flyer and the November 2026 HMS lunch menu (image); six 2026-27 HMS "Nest" newsletters and the literacy screening flyer image; the district volunteer and chaperone procedure and the PickUp Patrol parent guide; policies EBCD, EEA, EEAB and IJOC; the preschool's Google site and its 2022-23 handbook; the Boys & Girls Club's Maple Street and Greater Concord pages; the HMS library's About page; Hopkinton Town Library pages and eight director's reports (April 2025 to October 2026); Maple Street School's 2026-27 handbook (literacy section); school board minutes (November 18, 2025; May 7, June 16 and October 6, 2026), three agenda packets (June 2, September 15 and October 6, 2026), page extracts from two more (November 18, 2025 and April 21, 2026) and the March 19, 2026 curriculum committee minutes; and a transcription of the 2026-27 bus route sheets with times and street names only (the original route PDF, which lists stops by house number, was not saved). Residents' home addresses in public comment were blacked out of the saved minutes and packets.
