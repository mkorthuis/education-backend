# Hopkinton Residents' Questions

The questions the Hopkinton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hopkinton is SAU 66. The Hopkinton School District runs Harold Martin School (preschool to grade 3) and Maple Street School (grades 4–6) in Contoocook, and Hopkinton Middle and High School (grades 7–12) in one building on Park Avenue. High school students may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting. Several questions contain premises (full-day public pre-K for everyone, 56% math proficiency, a ban on driving to CRTC, low Niche parent reviews for Maple Street, crowded elementary schools) that the research should check rather than assume.
