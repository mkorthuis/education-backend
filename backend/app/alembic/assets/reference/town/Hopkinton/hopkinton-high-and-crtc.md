# Hopkinton High School and the CRTC

Part of the Hopkinton research (town/Hopkinton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hopkinton High School (HHS, grades 9–12, in Hopkinton Middle High School, HMHS) and Hopkinton's side of the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC): AP and honors courses against Bow and Concord High, VLACS, NHTI and other college-credit routes, graduates' colleges, the CRTC schedule, bus and costs, driving and parking rules, career and apprenticeship guidance, credit transfer, counseling and mental health support, absences for illness, music, theater and art, science labs, the school's rules on artificial intelligence, grading and class rank, and sports (division, cuts, participation, the new cooperative football team).

- **12 fact topics, 82 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 17 MB): HHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies, the 2026-27 HMHS Student and Family Handbook, the 2025-26 school profile for colleges, the bell schedule, the district's 2023–2028 strategic plan, eleven district policies and the policy folder listing, ten sets of approved school board minutes (2025–2026; residents' home addresses in public comment removed) and one agenda, five athletic director reports, the cooperative football memo, the October 2026 student services report, the 2016 NEASC report letter and the 2025 NEASC board and survey presentations, HMHS web pages (home, counseling, staff, unified arts, science, NEASC), the Hawk News newsletter, a Cartwheel family flyer, two CRTC pages, VLACS's home page, and the OpenStreetMap and OSRM files behind a **derived** drive time.
- **Places:** 80 facts use `district Hopkinton`, 2 use `district Concord` (CRTC parking and the automotive program page, which Concord's files lack). No general facts were needed; statewide rules are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **New data concepts:** the CRTC release times in HHS's bell schedule; the principal's "over 95%" CRTC acceptance figure; the district's van purchase tied partly to CRTC runs; HHS's honors sections and AP list set against Bow and Concord; the school profile's no-rank, unweighted average and its list of 79 colleges offering admission; Safe Schools New Hampshire's three-year online therapy pilot and the Cartwheel partnership; the strategic plan's open question on a school social worker; the handbook's AI rules; Division III placement, basketball cuts and participation rates; and a derived 13-mile, 22-minute drive from HMHS to the CRTC.
