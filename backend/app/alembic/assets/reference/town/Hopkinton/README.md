# Hopkinton: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Hopkinton, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the eighth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Hopkinton is SAU 66, a one-district SAU:
- **Harold Martin School** serves preschool to grade 3, and **Maple Street School** grades 4–6.
- **Hopkinton Middle School (7–8) and Hopkinton High (9–12)** share one building on Park Avenue.
- **Career programs:** the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC).

The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting (March 14, 2026). The CRTC was researched in `../Concord/`; Bow and Concord are the usual comparisons.
