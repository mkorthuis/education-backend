# Somersworth Middle School, Somersworth High School and the Career Technical Center

Part of the Somersworth research (town/Somersworth). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Somersworth Middle School (grades 6–8), Somersworth High School (grades 9–12) and the Somersworth Career Technical Center (CTC), which is part of the high school: the move from Maple Wood to the middle school, the CTC's Building Trades program and what it leads to (the carpenters' union apprenticeship, college credit, wages), how CTC programs fit into the 26-credit diploma, Early College (the former Running Start), AP and honors, the arts, sports, vaping and bullying rules, counseling and mental health help, STEM clubs, math placement and acceleration, middle school math help, FAFSA and scholarship help, and what UNH says about how it reviews applications.

- **14 fact topics, 92 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 16 MB): the high school's 2026-27 Program of Studies, 2026-27 and 2025-26 school profiles (the 2026-27 profile is an image, transcribed), the 2026-27 Parent and Student Handbook, the 2026-27 course selection sheets and CTE enrollment sheet, the CTC website's program pages, the middle school's handbook, clubs, athletics and staff pages and its 2024-25 Title I plan (transcribed from images), SAU 56 policies (IKF, IKFG, ADC/GBED/JICG, JICK, JJIB, IHBH), eight sets of board minutes and agendas (July 2025–November 2026), the 2026-27 bus route sheet, CRDC 2023-24 rows for Somersworth and nearby schools, Arbiter's list of active teams, BLS wage data and the carpenters' training fund pages.
- **Places:** 84 facts use `district Somersworth`, 6 use `town Somersworth` (wages and the union apprenticeship) and 2 have no place (UNH's statements on how it reviews applications).
- **New data concepts:** what share of the day a CTE program takes and how it counts toward the diploma; program-by-program college credit, credentials and costs at the Somersworth CTC; the carpenters' union apprenticeship (information sessions, pay as a share of journeyworker pay, hours); carpenter wages in the Boston metro area (which includes Strafford County) and New Hampshire; CRDC sports participation per 100 students and counselor staffing for Somersworth and nearby schools; Algebra I in grades 7–8 at nearby middle schools; the middle school's daily intervention block; the high school's new Blue/White schedule.
