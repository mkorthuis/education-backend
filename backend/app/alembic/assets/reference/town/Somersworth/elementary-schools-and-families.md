# Idlehurst, Maple Wood and Young Families in Somersworth

Part of the Somersworth research (town/Somersworth). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Somersworth's two elementary schools, Idlehurst Elementary School (preschool to grade 2, 46 Stackpole Road) and Maple Wood Elementary School (grades 3–5, 184 Maple Street), and the years before them: the district preschool and the Head Start classroom inside Idlehurst, before- and after-school care, bus eligibility and the 2026-27 routes, school hours and why the two schools start at different times, the calendar, delays and no-school days, meals and summer meals, language help for families, class sizes, reading help and special education referrals, devices, after-school options and child care in the city.

- **13 topics, 75 facts and 3 number tables.** All 13 topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 21 MB), with a source list in `raw/elementary/SOURCES.txt`: `sau56/` (district pages, bus routes, calendar, meal letter, May 2026 enrollment), `schools/` (both 2026-27 handbooks and school pages), `policies/` (EEA, IIB, IFB, JLCF), `board/` (2025-26 minutes and the March 10, 2026 packet), `ymca/`, `capsc/` and `other/`.
- **Main findings.** Maple Wood's school day starts at 8:20 a.m., not 7:30; its buses start picking children up at about 7:32 because the same five route numbers run middle and high school first, then Maple Wood, then Idlehurst (8:55 start). The Granite YMCA runs before-care from 7:00 a.m. inside both schools ($70 a week for members), but its Maple Wood after-school program was wait-listed for every week of 2026-27 when checked. Most children in the district preschool have IEPs (31 of 48 in January 2026); Community Action Partnership of Strafford County (CAPSC) runs a separate, free Head Start classroom inside Idlehurst (18 funded places, 8:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.). The 2026-27 calendar has no early-release days, and the route list shows no late buses for either school. The district's website translates into 11 languages, but not Indonesian; the school board discussed a memorandum of understanding with Indonesian Community Connect in March 2026.
- **Main gaps:** the district preschool's hours, tuition, enrollment steps and waiting list (no page describes them); whether preschoolers ride the bus; what the cafeteria serves (menus sit in an app that couldn't be read by script) and whether pork-free choices are offered; any interpreter or translation procedure for families; minutes of screen time; Somersworth Recreation and library programs (the city's website refused automated requests); and the YMCA's February and April vacation camps and snow-day plans for Somersworth ("stay tuned" when checked).
- **Spot-checked** against raw copies: Maple Wood's "8:20 School day begins with announcements" (`schools/maplewood-handbook-2026-27.txt`, page 13); the YMCA's "Before School - Idlehurst, Idlehurst School, Mon – Fri 7:00 – 8:45 AM" at "$70 per week" for members (`ymca/ymca-sacc.txt`); and "Pre-K (3 & 4) | 48 | 31 | 64%" (`board/materials-2026-03-10-p13.txt`, checked against the page image). All match.
