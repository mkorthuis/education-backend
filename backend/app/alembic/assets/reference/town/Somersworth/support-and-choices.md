# Student Support and School Choices in Somersworth

Part of the Somersworth research (town/Somersworth). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers support and choice questions from Somersworth residents: who handles an IEP when a student moves from Somersworth Middle School to Somersworth High School, and which special education programs the high school runs; what the 2026-27 budget cut after the SchoolCare health insurance assessment, including paraprofessionals, a contracted social worker and counselors; how outside (out-of-district) special education placements are decided and paid for; behavior support for teachers and students; PowerSchool and other ways parents track work; what families pay for field trips, sports, supplies and career center programs; school options across the river in Maine and at Seacoast Charter School, including buses; Somersworth's open enrollment and transfer policies; EFA use and what it means for Somersworth's state aid; and home-educated students' access to classes, sports and the career center, the homeschool notice process at SAU 56, and credit for home education when a student enters Somersworth High.

- **11 topics, 55 facts, 1 table.** Ten are shared district or town topics (`district-homeschool-access`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-budget-and-cost`, `district-student-support`, `district-family-communication`, `district-fees`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `town-school-options-nearby`), and one is the shared rule topic `special-education-placements-across-states` (federal placement rules, no place). Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 18 MB).
- **Places used:** `district Somersworth` for the district, its schools, policies, board and budget; `district Seacoast Charter School` for its bus page; `town Somersworth` for school options near the city (EFA counts and providers, Maine schools, drive distances); no place for federal rules.
- **Main findings:**
  - **The health insurance bill was real.** SchoolCare, the district's health insurance pool, billed Somersworth $672,467.32 in fall 2025, its first assessment in 30 years. The board was told not to freeze spending. The City Council later planned a $450,000 supplemental appropriation for the balance, which let the board add $450,000 of cuts back into the 2026-27 budget.
  - **The 2026-27 cuts came from the city's tax cap.** The proposed budget was about $2.2 million over the tax cap. The cut list included 7 paraprofessional positions ($340,978) and a contracted social worker ($124,495). Neither was on the April 7, 2026 add-back list (**derived**). No school counselor positions were on either list. The district had 6 to 9 open paraprofessional positions during 2025-26.
  - **Somersworth will not pay for students to leave for open enrollment schools.** Policy JFABA (June 9, 2026) and City Council resolution 19-26 (May 4, 2026) set a 0% limit on Somersworth students attending open enrollment schools elsewhere. The district will take up to two students in grades 6–12 from other districts.
  - **Somersworth runs no bus to Seacoast Charter.** Seacoast Charter's page offers a bus only for Dover residents and links a parent carpool group. Somersworth's policy EEA lets charter students ride its own routes inside the city only. The school is about 5.5 road miles from City Hall (**derived**).
  - **SAU 56's homeschool page, intent form and policy IHBG still describe the rules from before July 1, 2026,** including an annual evaluation that state law no longer requires. Policy JJJ, revised October 6, 2026, gives home-educated, EFA, charter and private school residents the same access as enrolled students to classes, activities and sports.
  - **Somersworth High may test or review homeschool work before giving credit.** The Program of Studies says credits from accredited programs are accepted on an official transcript. For homeschool work, the school may require a proficiency test (PLATO or course assessment) or a review of the work. Policy IK (January 2026) lists a home education portfolio as evidence of competencies.
  - **Most Somersworth CTC programs list their costs as grant-funded for 2026-27.** Program fees in the Program of Studies are mostly for partner programs in Dover. No sports participation fee is posted.
- **Main gaps:** NH DOE's count of Somersworth residents in charter schools (not retrieved); any count of how many families apply to charter lotteries; the district's Special Education Policy and Procedures Manual (policy IHBA says it is available from the office; not found online); school supply lists; how many of the 7 cut paraprofessional positions were vacant; the City Council's own record of the April 2026 supplemental appropriation vote; MSAD 60's (Berwick, Maine) terms for out-of-state tuition students.
- **Proposed shared topic:** none.

## special-education-placements-across-states: Who Decides a Special Education Placement, and Who Pays When It Is Outside the District

Under federal special education rules, a child's educational placement is decided by a group that includes the parents and others who know the child and the placement options; it is set at least once a year, based on the IEP, as close as possible to home, and in the school the child would attend without a disability unless the IEP requires something else.
