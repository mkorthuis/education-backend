# Somersworth: Money and Governance

Part of the Somersworth research (town/Somersworth). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics, 58 facts and 5 number tables on how the Somersworth School District's budget is set under the city charter's tax cap, what drove the 2025-26 and 2026-27 budgets, the SchoolCare health insurance assessment and what was cut, special education's share of the budget, pay scales, school debt and building work, the end of SAU 56's service agreement with Rollinsford, the Mayor's Commission to Redesign and Reimagine the Somersworth School District and the strategic plan, and how outside ratings and state percentiles describe the district.
- **The 2026-27 (FY2027) budget from start to finish.** The board's proposed budget ($36,802,805, up 8.9%) was $2,180,527 over the charter tax cap; the district listed $2,180,717 in cuts (20.5 positions, 7 of them paraprofessionals); the City Council passed the budget (ordinance 8-26) and the board approved $34,622,278 on April 7, 2026, restoring $450,000 (5.5 positions) once a separate $450,000 supplemental appropriation for the SchoolCare balance was planned.
- **The SchoolCare bill.** A $30 million pro-rata assessment on all members, the first in SchoolCare's 30-year history; Somersworth's share was $672,467.32. One-third was paid by December 31, 2025, partly with special education aid that came in $448,561.51 above budget; the rest ($448,311.55) was due before July 15, 2026. The council asked the district not to freeze spending.
- **Special education's share.** The district's special education budget was $11,109,594 in FY2026 (32.9% of the approved budget, **derived**) and $12,427,968 proposed for FY2027 (33.8% of the proposed budget, **derived**); 296 of 1,334 students (22%) had IEPs in January 2026, and 18 were placed out of district.
- **The tax cap:** prior levy × national CPI-U plus new construction × last year's rate, with the FY2026 and FY2027 calculations.
- **Governance:** Rollinsford left SAU 56 and now runs SAU 104 on its own; its $238,410 share of SAU 56's costs ended after 2024-25. A joint council-board commission meets quarterly on savings.
- **Plans:** the Mayor's Commission (charge, members, forums, the waivers it wants to seek, a plan due by May 2027), the 2025–30 strategic priorities, a $500,000 Nellie Mae Foundation grant on the November 17, 2026 agenda, and a "$601,000 state revenue shortfall (2026-27)" on the same agenda.

**Main sources:** SAU 56's District Budget folders (FY2027 budget binder, February 5 and April 6, 2026 presentations, March 30 joint workshop, draft reductions, the RSA 189:76 report to voters, FY2026 presentation and the February 20, 2025 memo to the City Council, the SAU 56 FY2025 budget), School Board, committee and agenda documents (October 2024 to November 2026), the SAE (teachers) 2026-27 agreement and the SACA (aides and secretaries) 2024–2027 contract, the City of Somersworth FY2024 financial report, the commission's pages and September 16, 2026 slides, SAU 104's site, GreatSchools, and the site's database (DOE-25 spending, enrollment, tax rates, salaries).
