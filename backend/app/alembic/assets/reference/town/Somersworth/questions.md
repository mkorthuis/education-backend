# Somersworth Residents' Questions

The questions the Somersworth research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Somersworth is a city in SAU 56 with Rollinsford. The Somersworth School District runs Idlehurst Elementary School (preschool to grade 2), Maple Wood Elementary School (grades 3–5), Somersworth Middle School (grades 6–8) and Somersworth High School (grades 9–12), with the Somersworth Career Technical Center. Several questions contain premises (Maple Wood at 7:30 and Idlehurst at 8:55, over a third of the budget on special education, a big health care bill, a Mayor's Commission on redesigning the schools, enrollment around 1,000, low online scores) that the research should check rather than assume.
