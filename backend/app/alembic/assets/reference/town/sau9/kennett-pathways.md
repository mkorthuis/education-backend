# Kennett Pathways: Career Center Credentials, Course Levels, Dual Enrollment, Alternative Programs, Prevention and Outdoor Programs

Part of the Conway research (town/Conway), written for the shared SAU 9 folder (town/sau9) that every SAU 9 town uses. Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file adds to `kennett-and-sau9.md` (Bartlett research) and `town/Jackson/after-jackson-grammar.md`, which already cover Kennett's AP list, the absence of a computer science course, college destinations, credential counts, Running Start, counseling, clubs and athletics. Those facts are not repeated; the question table names them.

- **5 fact topics, 69 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/pathways/` (about 30 MB): Conway School Board minutes (July 2023 to February 2026, several read by OCR from scans), Kennett principal's reports (2024–2026), Conway policies IHBH, IHBI, IHCA and JFCH, the career center's ELO handbook and program pages, ApprenticeshipNH pages, the Kennett student wellness site, NH DHHS's 2023 SAP list, C3PH pages and its 2026–2030 plan, CCSNH's Early College page, two news articles and Kennett's 2026-27 bell schedule.
- **Places:** 64 facts use `district Conway` (Kennett and the career center), 1 uses `town Conway` (a North Conway employer's apprenticeship), and 4 have no place (statewide ApprenticeshipNH, CCSNH and county-coalition context).
- **New data concepts:**
  - Career-center seat caps (16 stations per class), unfilled course requests and total CTE course enrollments.
  - ApprenticeshipNH's listing of the center as a high school partner, and the absence of any named Kennett apprenticeship.
  - The end of Eagle Academy (2007–2021) and the size, staffing and structure of Kennett's current Alternative Program.
  - Conway's ELO and alternative learning plan policies (IHBH, IHBI) and Kennett's credit recovery course.
  - The Kennett Student Assistance Program, the district's substance-abuse policy (JFCH) and the Carroll County Coalition for Public Health's prevention work.
  - A STEM course-level breakdown, the AP computer science history, college lists by location, and 2024–2026 ski results.
- **Premise checks:**
  - "Eagle Academy" (P11.1) no longer runs. It was a night school from 2007 to 2021; Kennett now runs a daytime "Alternative Program."
  - The career center's computer science program was cut in April 2025 (P5.1).
- **Proposed new shared topic:** none needed.
- **Main gaps:**
  - Any named registered or pre-apprenticeship for Kennett students.
  - Written admission criteria for the Alternative Program.
  - Whether the Student Assistance Program is staffed now.
  - A Kennett summer school.
  - Any formal inn or restaurant partnership for culinary internships.
  - The 2026 Nordic state-meet result.
  - Any statement on how HB 1202 affects Kennett students.
