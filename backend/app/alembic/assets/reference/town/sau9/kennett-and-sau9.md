# Kennett High School, the MWV Career and Technical Center, and SAU 9

Part of the Bartlett research (town/Bartlett), written for the shared SAU 9 folder (town/sau9) that every SAU 9 town will use. Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Kennett High School (KHS, Conway School District), the Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center (MWVCTC) at Kennett, and matters that apply across SAU 9. It adds to the Jackson research (town/Jackson), which already covers Kennett's AP list, early credit, college plans, SAT, athletics, counseling, the career center's program list, data portals, SAU 9's English learner program and family notifications. Those facts are not repeated. The last section lists, by topic slug and fact number, the Jackson facts that apply to all of SAU 9 or to Kennett, so they can be folded into this folder later.

- **7 fact topics, 86 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/` (about 80 MB): Kennett pages and 22 principal's reports to the Conway School Board, SAU 9's Fall 2024 Report Card and two newsletters, four Kennett tuition contracts (OCR text), Bartlett and Conway board minutes, two policies, ParentSquare, WMCC, NACAC, CCRC, College Board and Common App sources, and copies of the Jackson raw files cited here (`raw/from-jackson/`).
- **Places:** 59 Kennett and career-center facts use `district Conway`; 9 SAU-wide facts use `sau 9`; 9 facts use `district Bartlett` because they answer assigned Bartlett questions directly (JBES's side of the move to Kennett, Bartlett's language-access policy, and how Bartlett's board and staff discussed test results). Research facts have no place.
- **New data concepts:**
  - Kennett's yearly transition steps: an eighth-grade transition night, Kennett staff visits to sending schools for course registration, eighth-grade tours, and a freshmen-only transition day.
  - The 2027 Kennett contracts' clause giving each sending district "equal access" to all career-center courses.
  - SAU 9's own published counts for 2023-24: 173 Kennett students earning 212 industry credentials; 72 earning Running Start credit; 89 earning 185 articulated credits; 62 scoring 3 or higher on an AP exam.
  - Kennett college persistence for the Classes of 2019–2022, and the Class of 2024's college destinations.
  - A Gallup student-engagement survey result for Kennett.
  - How SAU 9 and Bartlett staff explained test results in public.
  - NACAC's Fall 2023 admission-factor survey and a Florida study comparing AP with dual enrollment.
- **Correction to the Jackson research:** Jackson fact school-programs-after-grade-6 25 in `Jackson/after-jackson-grammar.md` says no step-up day or freshman orientation was found. Kennett's principal's reports, a 2024 letter and SAU 9's newsletters show an eighth-grade transition night, registration visits, tours and a freshman transition day (school-programs-after-grade-6 facts 1–8).
- **Proposed new shared topic:** none needed. P6.2 facts sit under district-curriculum-and-assessment ("its local tests").
- **Main gaps:**
  - Results for Kennett students by sending town, including the quarterly reports to the Bartlett School Board under the current contract. None are published.
  - Career-center capacity, enrollment by program and waitlists. Whether an LNA course runs in 2026-27.
  - Any public statement by SAU 9, JBES or Kennett about online rating portals.
  - Whether ParentSquare translation is turned on for SAU 9 families, and any SAU 9 interpreter service.
  - A written JBES-to-Kennett IEP transition procedure.
  - Any measure of how Bartlett students keep up friendships at Kennett.

## research-ap-and-dual-enrollment: What Research and Admission Surveys Say About AP and Dual Enrollment

- kind: research

Only 1.1% gave considerable importance to AP or IB exam scores; 22.2% gave them moderate importance.
   - citation: National Association for College Admission Counseling (NACAC), State of College Admission, "Factors in the Admission Decision," Fall 2023
