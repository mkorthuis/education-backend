# Laconia Middle, Laconia High and the Huot Technical Center

Part of the Laconia research (town/Laconia). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Laconia High School's block schedule, graduation credits, AP and honors courses, Early College and credit recovery; the J. Oliva Huot Technical Center's programs, schedule, admission, credits and credentials (plumbing and HVAC, automotive, health science and the LNA option, protective services and EMS, personal finance); phone and bullying rules at Laconia Middle and High; attendance and truancy; counseling at LHS; Lakes Region Community College's Early College terms; Plymouth State's transfer-credit rules; state licensing rules for plumbers and nursing assistants; and what trades pay in the Laconia area.

**Counts:** 11 topics, 92 facts and 4 number tables.

**Main sources:** Huot's 2026-27 Program of Studies, 2026-27 Student Handbook, program pages, curriculum guides and 2026-27 college-credit list; LHS's 2026-27 School Profile and Student Handbook and its 2025-26 Program of Studies (the 2026-27 edition was approved on September 1, 2026 but is not posted); Laconia Middle School's 2026-27 handbook (a scanned PDF, read by OCR); SAU 30 policies JICJ, JH, IKF and IHCD; school board minutes from August 2025 to October 2026; LRCC's High School Programs page; the Mechanical Licensing Board's Mec 300 rules and the Board of Nursing's Nur 100-800 rules; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24; and BLS OEWS wages for May 2025.

### Premises checked

| Question | Premise | What the sources show |
|---|---|---|
| P2.1 | Huot's Plumbing and HVAC hours count toward state apprentice hours | **Partly.** Huot says students who finish both levels can enter the NH plumbing apprentice program as second-year apprentices (cte-center-programs 11). The state's journeyman rule asks for 8,000 hours of on-the-job work and 600 hours of education. It says nothing about counting high school lab hours as work hours, and an apprentice license requires age 16 and work under a master plumber (occupational-licensing-rules 1–4). Fuel gas rules do allow hours "in an approved educational setting" (fact 6). |
| P2.3 | Huot runs on a "block schedule" | **Correct.** Huot classes run 90 minutes a day for the full year in one of three blocks (cte-center-programs 3). LHS runs a 4 x 4 block of 76-minute classes, and several Huot programs count toward LHS core credits such as math (school-programs-after-grade-6 1–2, 5–6). |
| P10.1 | An LNA license through Huot's "Health Sciences II" before graduation | **Partly.** The course is "Health Science and Technology II/Nurse Assisting Track" (HTC440). Huot now offers the LNA option through outside ("3rd party") providers and does not guarantee a license (cte-center-programs 19). State law and Board of Nursing rules set no minimum age and no diploma requirement (occupational-licensing-rules 9), but Huot says to "See center" for its own grade and age requirements. |
| P10.2 | "Running Start" | **Renamed.** CCSNH now calls it "Early College at Your High School." LHS's catalog says "formerly Running Start," while Huot's 2026-27 Program of Studies and handbook still use "Running Start" (school-programs-after-grade-6 14; cte-center-programs 26). |
