# Special Education, Student Support, Choices and Adult Learning in Laconia

Part of the Laconia research (town/Laconia). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Laconia residents' questions on special education and 504 plans, staffing of paraprofessionals and speech services, counseling, mental health and substance use support, English learners and translated messages, progress reports at Laconia Middle School, advanced math, school resource officers and building security, private school and tuition options, moving from Holy Trinity into Laconia's schools, EFA students and special education testing, home-educated students at the Huot Center and in LHS sports and band, and adult learning: Laconia Adult Education's evening classes and help with college financial aid.

- **13 topics, 103 facts and 1 table.** All topics are shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`. Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 17 MB), with school board minutes in `raw/support/minutes/`.
- **Places used:** `district Laconia` for SAU 30, its schools, the Huot Center, Laconia Adult Education (a district program at the Laconia High School address) and the district's own resource lists; `town Laconia` for school resource officers (Laconia Police Department), private schools (Holy Trinity, Laconia Christian Academy and others, each named), Lakes Region Community College (named), the Lakes Region Scholarship Foundation and the NH Employment Security office; no place for ParentSquare's product description.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Paraprofessional openings are real; speech openings were not found.** On August 4, 2026 the assistant superintendent reported 18 district-wide paraprofessional vacancies and said recruitment through staffing agencies would begin; on October 6 the Budget and Personnel Committee still listed "ongoing paraeducator needs." In January 2026 the district posted "special education contracted service positions." Woodland Heights' directory lists 7 special education teachers, 13 paraprofessionals and 3 speech staff. No public record shows whether any child's IEP minutes were missed.
  - **Holy Trinity now runs full time only through grade 6.** Its 2026-27 tuition page lists full-time PreK–Grade 6 at $7,900, with grades 7–8 offered only as specialty courses for homeschoolers. A Holy Trinity student would join Laconia Middle School in grade 7, placed under policy JG (revised September 1, 2026) after a records review and a principal–parent meeting.
  - **Laconia does special education testing for students at private schools located in Laconia, whatever their town.** Its procedures plan says it evaluates and may serve them through an Individual Service Plan, with no individual right to services. Policy JJJ (September 15, 2026) sends related-service requests from EFA, home-educated and private school students to the Student Services Department.
  - **Home-educated and EFA students who live in Laconia may join LHS courses, sports and band** under policy JJJ (adopted September 15, 2026), by written request to the principal, with the same eligibility, tryouts and fees. Policy JJIB-P (revised October 6, 2026) says no participation fees are charged to team members. The Huot Center takes home-educated students through LHS's guidance office. It is open to grades 10–12 (sophomores as space allows), lists no welding program for 2026-27, and is not an EFA vendor.
  - **Student support at LHS and LMS:** a director of school counseling and three LHS counselors, an outreach coordinator (social worker), a student assistance counselor who is a licensed alcohol and drug counselor (LMS Monday and Wednesday, LHS Tuesday, Thursday and Friday), a Lakes Region Mental Health clinician, and contracted mental health and substance misuse counseling. Funding includes a $4.59 million federal Project AWARE grant that pays for ten positions, and an $87,259 Student Assistance Program grant for 2026-27.
  - **Laconia Middle School sends a reminder to check grades halfway through each quarter.** Grades in Alma are updated weekly by Friday afternoon.
  - **An SRO is listed at LMS and at LHS**, and the board named the district's SROs truant officers in August 2026. No current source says whether they are in the buildings every school day. No SRO is listed at the elementary schools, where visitors check in at a front-entrance window.
  - **Evening adult classes are run by Laconia Adult Education, not the Huot Center.** It offers electrical and plumbing apprenticeship classes (Monday and Wednesday, 6–9 p.m., $1,450 a year, employer sponsor required), the Laconia Academy evening diploma, and free evening ESL and HiSET classes.
- **Main gaps:** whether IEP service minutes are being missed (no compliance or service-hour record is public); how 504 plans are shared with each LMS teacher; a rule or practice for a 6th grader to take 7th or 8th grade math; whether Laconia's emergency alerts go through ParentSquare and are translated; SRO schedules and the city–school SRO agreement; Laconia Christian Academy's tuition (its site refused automated requests); SchoolSpring's job postings (a JavaScript app behind a bot check); how Holy Trinity students do after moving to LMS.
