# Laconia Residents' Questions

The questions the Laconia research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Laconia is a city in Belknap County and SAU 30, a one-district SAU. The Laconia School District runs three elementary schools for preschool to grade 5 (Elm Street, Pleasant Street and Woodland Heights), Laconia Middle School (grades 6–8) and Laconia High School (9–12) with the Huot Technical Center, a regional career and technical center that also serves students from nearby districts. The school budget is set within the city's budget, which the City Council adopts under the city's tax cap. Several questions contain premises (a preschool packet picked up at the Harvard Street office, the Alma system, a 4.4% budget increase, exemptions to the tax cap, five teaching positions cut through attrition, health insurance up nearly 17%, SAU 30 buses to Holy Trinity, an LNA license through Huot's Health Sciences II, Running Start, Bike Week traffic in June, Huot's Protective Services and Automotive programs) that the research should check rather than assume.
