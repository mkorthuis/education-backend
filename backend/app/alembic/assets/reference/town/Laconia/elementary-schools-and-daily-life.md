# Elementary Schools, the Early Years and Daily Life in Laconia

Part of the Laconia research (town/Laconia). Gathered 2026-11-16.

## Summary

Covers Laconia's three elementary schools (Elm Street, Pleasant Street and Woodland Heights) and family daily life: preschool registration and the Alma system, Project EXTRA (the free 21st Century after-school program, full for 2026-27), school hours and pickup, bus rules and the 2026-27 routes (including a 3:00 p.m. run from Woodland Heights to the Boys & Girls Club), meals on delayed openings and early releases, nurses and 2026 nurse vacancies, counselors and social workers, report cards and paper forms, volunteering, parent groups, kindergarten (full day at all three schools), devices and recess, the elementary zone map and school transfers, how the three schools compare, summer programs, Motorcycle Week traffic and the bus routes at the Weirs, and Holy Trinity School's hours and transport.

19 topics, 92 facts and 1 number table. Sources are the Laconia School District (SAU 30) site, school sites, the 2026-27 elementary handbook, school board policies, school board minutes (November 2025 to October 2026), the three Title I schoolwide plans (2025-2028), the 2026-27 bus route sheets, the Office of Extended Learning, Office of School Wellness, health office and food service sites, City of Laconia pages and City Council minutes, Laconia Motorcycle Week's site, Got Lunch! Laconia, the Lakes Region Chamber's member listing, and Holy Trinity Catholic School's 2026-27 handbook. Three facts add Laconia to the library's `kindergarten-day-length` and `kindergarten-registration` topics, with those topics' own kind, question type and description. No new shared topic is proposed.

Main gaps: why preschool registration uses a paper packet (no source says); whether lunch is served on 1:00 p.m. early-release days; how many hours each nurse works in each building; kindergarten class sizes for 2026-27 (the site's class-size data start at grade 1, and fall 2026 kindergarten counts by school weren't reachable); minutes of screen time in kindergarten; on-time data for buses; any district plan for buses during Motorcycle Week; the Boys & Girls Club's own fees and transport terms (its site blocked automated requests).

Privacy note for reviewers: the "After School Bus Routes" Google Doc linked from the SAU home page, and Woodland Heights' Project EXTRA fall schedule, list children's names. Neither was saved with names; facts cite the school-site PDF bus list (no names) and a copy of the schedule with the roster removed.
