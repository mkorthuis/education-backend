# Gorham: Money, Taxes, Buildings and Governance

Part of the Gorham research (town/Gorham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 15 topics, 94 facts and 11 number tables on the Gorham Randolph Shelburne (GRS) Cooperative School District's budget and cost per pupil, the school tax and state aid, how Gorham, Randolph and Shelburne share costs, SAU 20's office and budget, buildings and repairs, safety, staffing and pay, test results against Berlin, separate districts and consolidation, and attracting families.

- **Why the tax moves.** Gorham's school tax rate fell in tax year 2025 ($10.92 local school rate, down from $12.06) because the town's 2025 statistical update raised its valuation about 35% and reset its equalization ratio from about 75% to 99.5%. The dollars still rose: the local school tax raised was $5,531,626 in 2025, up from $4,659,776 in 2024 (+18.7%) and from $4,230,549 in 2018 (+30.8%, **derived**). For 2026-27 the district expects state aid to Gorham to fall again and to return less fund balance to taxpayers ($249,000 planned against $500,000 once expected), and in January it estimated a school rate of $12.31 for Gorham. In the site's unofficial Question 2 results the school tax cap passed in the GRS district (66.4% yes, 60% needed) and the SAU spending cap passed across SAU 20's six towns (66.7%), so once ratified both would apply from the FY2028 budget.
- **What drives cost.** The state's cost per pupil for the district was $26,560.74 in 2024-25 against $22,699 statewide (site data). Special education is the biggest single line: $2,468,117.70 in FY2025 (24% of general fund spending), up $609,254 (32.8%) from FY2024 (**derived**). The district's average daily membership fell 3.7% from 2022-23 to 2024-25 while its cost per pupil rose about 30% (**derived**). The board's own FY2027 cuts were capital reserve deposits, supplies and one full-time teaching position.
- **SAU 20 against classrooms.** In FY2025, 58.3% of the general fund went to instruction and 7.0% to "general administration, SAU level" (the state form's line, which is the same number as the budget's "School Board" row; **derived**). SAU 20's 2026-27 budget is $1,068,251 (+0.67%); GRS's apportionment of it is $589,544, 62.6% of what the member districts pay (**derived**).
- **Randolph and Shelburne.** The district's own report says Gorham pays 84.3% of the school budget, Shelburne 8.7% and Randolph 7%. The 2004 Articles of Agreement split operating costs 95% by students and 5% by property value, and capital costs 100% by property value; each town's state aid is credited to it. Shelburne and Randolph also pay a fixed "buy-in" ($18,784 and $11,877 a year for 20 years) that ends after FY2027. After state aid, the three towns' local school taxes in 2025 were 81.5% Gorham, 8.2% Randolph and 10.3% Shelburne (**derived**).
- **Buildings.** The Gorham Middle High School boilers were replaced in 2026 under Option 2 for $620,755 (about $294,859 from FY2026 fund balance, up to $250,896 from the Building and Grounds capital reserve and $75,000 in the budget). Edward Fenn (1957) has a 2008 roof rated "good" in the district's own checklist; the district's 20-year plan has roof work at both schools in 2032-2033. An earlier boiler contract lost a $100,000 deposit.
- **Safety.** No school resource officer position is listed by the Gorham Police Department or in the district's staff lists or minutes. A SAFE grant is paying for keyless entry doors at both schools (installation under way in August 2026).
- **Consolidating grades.** The only grade reorganization found is the board's February 17, 2026 vote to move sixth grade back to Edward Fenn for 2026-27. No source shows talk of closing a school; the 2018 Berlin study floated sending Gorham students to Berlin and the 2025 SAU 3 and SAU 20 study kept both districts separate.

**Main sources:** the GRS 2025 Annual Report (2026 warrant, FY2027 budget, MS-25 financial report, principal's report); the GRS School Board's posted minutes for January 2025 to October 2026 (25 meetings read in part); the cooperative's 2004 Articles of Agreement (scanned, transcribed); SAU 20's Reports page, building plan workbooks and the SAU 3 and SAU 20 Collaborative Study summary (April 2025); the Town of Gorham's 2025 Town Report (DRA tax rate calculation of November 20, 2025); the Berlin Sun's report of the March 5, 2026 district meeting; Census Bureau counts; GreatSchools; the site's own database.
