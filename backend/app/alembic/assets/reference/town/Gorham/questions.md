# Gorham Residents' Questions

The questions the Gorham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Gorham's students attend the Gorham Randolph Shelburne Cooperative School District (SAU 20), which runs Edward Fenn School (kindergarten to grade 6), Gorham Middle School (grades 7–8) and Gorham High School in Gorham, with the Berlin career and technical center and White Mountains Community College nearby. Several questions contain premises (an enrollment under 150 at the high school, about 370 students, more than $22,000 per student, a 6th–12th grade "Gorham Middle High" building, Randolph and Shelburne's tax shares, Alma registration, CLiF reading books, co-oping with Berlin in hockey or football) that the research should check rather than assume. Some questions cite Niche, Wikipedia and other websites; treat those as claims to check against NH DOE and the district.
