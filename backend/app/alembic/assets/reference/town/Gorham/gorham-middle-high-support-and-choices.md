# Gorham Middle High School: Support, Choices and Daily Life

Part of the Gorham research (town/Gorham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Gorham Middle High School (Gorham Middle School and Gorham High School, 120 Main Street) and the Gorham Randolph Shelburne Cooperative's support and choices around it: internet and laptops, math results, homeschoolers' access, special education staffing and out-of-district placements, the move from Edward Fenn, welcoming families and bullying, how middle and high school students share a building, phones, winter sports and trails, the Shelburne and Randolph bus runs, athletics travel, mid-year enrollment and new students' social life.

- **Counts:** 12 topics, 88 facts, 2 tables (`gorham_bus_ride_minutes`, `gorham_math_proficiency_vs_state`).
- **Main sources:** the district's NEASC accreditation report for the middle high school (visit November 2–5, 2025), the 2026-27 school profile, student handbook and programs of studies, GRS School Board policies (read as Google Docs from the board's policy page), the board's 2026 minutes (public sessions, names removed), the 2024-25 annual report, the school and SAU 20 websites, the posted bus runs, ArbiterLive team lists, Great Glen Trails' Bill Koch League page, Data USA, and the site's own database (test results, enrollment).
- **Biggest finding:** the "6th–12th grade building" in the questions was right through 2025-26 but is out of date. The board voted on February 17, 2026 to move the sixth grade back to Edward Fenn School, and the 2026-27 profile describes grades 7–12. Some school pages still say 6–12.
- **Main gaps:** Niche's pages (HTTP 403); how the building separates middle from high school students (lunch, halls and bell schedules are in images the handbook text export doesn't carry); how long out-of-district rides are and where students are placed; any athletics transportation budget line; home internet reliability; SchoolSpring job pages (script-only). SAU 20's technology page answered HTTP 406.
- **New data concepts:** posted bus-run times turned into minutes on the bus; the district's device protection plan; the board's reports of out-of-district placement counts; a state-test comparison of the middle school with state grades 6–8 combined.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none. P4.3's numbers are in the site's data, and the library's statewide rules (home education access, bell-to-bell phones, bullying duties, IEP moves) are cited here by file, topic and fact number, but each question also needed local facts from this file.
- **New topics proposed:** none.
