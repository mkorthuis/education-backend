# Gorham: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Gorham, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the fourth ToLoad batch. Gorham is in the Gorham Randolph Shelburne (GRS) Cooperative School District (SAU 20, with Dummer, Errol and Milan):
- **Edward Fenn School** (kindergarten to grade 6 from 2026-27, plus a pre-K class; 152 students, 179 with pre-K, in September 2026);
- **Gorham Middle High School** (120 Main Street; grades 7–12 from 2026-27, grades 6–12 before; the school's own pages still say 6–12), with career programs at Berlin High School's center, one period a day by Gorham's own bus.

The district had no research before this batch; Berlin's files (the career center, the middle high school and Berlin's money) are cited where they apply.
