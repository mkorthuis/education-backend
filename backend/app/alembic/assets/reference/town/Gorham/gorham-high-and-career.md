# Gorham High School and Career Programs

Part of the Gorham research (town/Gorham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Gorham Middle High School (120 Main Street, Gorham; the Gorham Randolph Shelburne Cooperative School District's grades 6 to 12 building) as it bears on 17 resident questions: Berlin's career center and the bus and time it takes, graduates with a trade skill, shop classes and teams as enrollment changes, AP and advanced classes, apprenticeships, seasonal jobs and school-to-career internships, outdoor education, White Mountains Community College (WMCC) credit and tuition, FAFSA help and counseling, local scholarships, arts and theater, what students learn now, how freshmen hear about college and trade options, math before college placement tests, help for graduates, where graduates go, and how businesses can partner.

- **14 fact topics, 73 facts and 6 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 3 MB). Scripts are in the session scratchpad folder `gorham-high/`.
- **Places:** `district Gorham Randolph Shelburne Cooperative` for the school's programs, rules, policies, counseling and outcomes; `town Gorham` for the Gorham Randolph Shelburne Educational Foundation; `town Randolph` for the Randolph Foundation's scholarship; `town Berlin` for WMCC; no place for one CCSNH math fact.
- **Sources:** Gorham's 2026-27 Program of Studies (a Google Doc linked from the student handbook) and the 2025-26 PDF the school's page still links, the student handbook, the Scholarship Handbook 2025-26, the 2026-27 school profile, the November 2025 NEASC accreditation team's report (published January 2026), the middle school Program of Studies, the school's web pages, the Capstone site and the Cabaret site, the Gorham Randolph Shelburne School Board's policies (IHBH, IKFG, IKF, IHCD/LEB, IJO, IJOC, IKG, IHCA), WMCC's and CCSNH's pages, RSA 193:26-a, ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer and NH Education Facts data. No page blocked automated requests. Two items were refused or empty (see Not retrieved).
- **State rules and Berlin's center are cited, not rewritten:** dual and concurrent enrollment, FAFSA information, AP exam fees, youth employment, apprenticeships, the career center's programs and credentials and the state's rules for career center seats and costs come from the library and earlier files, named by file, topic and fact number.
- **Source conflicts:** (1) Gorham's Program of Studies says Early College at Gorham costs $150 a course (with a $75 scholarship), while CCSNH says up to two courses a year are free for grades 10-12, subject to funding (school-programs-after-grade-6 5). (2) The school's pages and the accreditation report say grades 6 to 12; the 2026-27 school profile says "180 plus students in grades 7-12" (district-classrooms-and-staff 1). (3) The profile's Class of 2025 plans (37% four-year, 23% two-year) differ from the accreditation report (34% four-year, 46% two-year) and from the site's NH DOE counts (high-school-outcomes 3). (4) The accreditation report counts both 11 and 15 Early College courses (school-programs-after-grade-6 4). (5) The school's FAFSA letter for the Class of 2024 says students must file or sign a waiver, while RSA 193:26-a, as amended in 2024, says schools must give students the opportunity to receive FAFSA information (district-student-support 3). (6) The 2025-26 book lists AP Biology as "not offered 25-26" but still prints it in its science pathways.

- **Main gaps:** no count of Gorham students who finish Berlin's programs or earn AWS, ASE or LNA credentials; no data on which colleges graduates attend or whether they leave New Hampshire; no college placement-test results for Gorham graduates; no dollar amounts for prom, the senior trip, graduation or cap and gown; no source on whether School-to-Career internships are paid; no page for alumni or graduate career counseling; no application, award list or guidelines for the Gorham Randolph Shelburne Educational Foundation; no source for the arts budget history.
