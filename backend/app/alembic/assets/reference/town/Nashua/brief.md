## What you need to know about Nashua

- **Nashua** is the state's second-largest city, in Hillsborough County on the Massachusetts border, next to Hudson, Merrimack, Hollis, Litchfield and Amherst. Site town id 371. The city is governed by a mayor and the Board of Aldermen under a city charter with a spending cap; the school budget is prepared by the **Nashua Board of Education** and sent to the aldermen, and there is no voter budget vote.
- **Nashua School District** (site district id 371) is its own SAU, **SAU 42** (https://www.nashua.edu/). Use `district Nashua` for SAU-level facts too.
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - **Elementary, kindergarten to grade 5:** Amherst Street (21940, with preschool), Bicentennial (21865), Birch Hill (21895), Broad Street (21925), Charlotte Avenue (21930), Dr. Norman W. Crisp (21835, with preschool), Fairgrounds Elementary (21840, with preschool), Ledge Street (21915, with preschool), Main Dunstable (21935), Mount Pleasant (21885), New Searles (21845, with preschool), Sunset Heights (21850).
  - **Preschool:** Franklin Street School (29242), home of the Nashua Early Education Program (NEEP), and the Purple Panthers Preschool (28610).
  - **Middle, grades 6–8:** Brian S. McCarthy (29274), Fairgrounds Middle (21875), Pennichuck (21825).
  - **High, grades 9–12:** Nashua High School North (27960), Nashua High School South (21860), and Brentwood Academy (29263, 23 students in 2024-25). The Nashua Technology Center is the district's career and technical center; check where its programs run.
  - **Not current:** Elm Street Middle School (21890), the Nashua Title I Preschool and the Phoenix Program aren't on NH DOE's current school list.
- **Charter schools** (use these exact names for places): the Academy for Science and Design Charter School (ASD, grades 5–12, in Nashua); Riverwood Academy Chartered Public School, run by `MicroSociety Academy Charter School Foundation` (preschool to grade 8, in Nashua; the questions call it MicroSociety Academy); and `Gate City Charter School For the Arts` (K–8, in Merrimack).
- **Private schools named in the questions:** Bishop Guertin High School and World Academy (K–8 in NH DOE's list, operator `World Academy`). Others in the city include St. Christopher Academy, Christian Bible Church Academy and Clearway High School.
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - Enrollment: 10,293 in 2015-16, 8,877 in 2023-24 and 9,699 in 2024-25 (NH DOE October counts). In 2024-25, Nashua South had 1,663 students and North 1,526; Pennichuck 768, McCarthy 649 and Fairgrounds Middle 586.
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Nashua $18,376; state $22,699; Bedford $19,650; Hudson $19,704; Litchfield $21,147; Hollis $23,370; Merrimack $23,849; Amherst $23,894.
