# Nashua Elementary Schools and the Early Years

Part of the Nashua research (town/Nashua). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Nashua School District's 12 elementary schools (kindergarten to grade 5) and its preschools: who can attend the Nashua Early Education Program (NEEP) and PlayPals at Franklin Street School, Purple Panther Preschool and the Title I preschool; before- and after-school care (the district's 21st Century Extended Day Program, the Adult Learning Center's School Age Adventures, the Boys & Girls Club and the YMCA); advanced learners, art, music and the 2026-27 STEAM pilot; recess; class-size limits and class placement, including twins; reading instruction, reading help and why reading results differ between schools; kindergarten hours and registration papers; and a comparison of Main Dunstable with the elementary schools in Hollis and Merrimack.

- **10 topics, 76 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 40 MB): district and school pages, Board of Education policies, the 2026-27 elementary handbook, the 2025-2029 teacher contract, the October 21, 2026 Facilities Master Plan workshop deck, Title I documents, 16 sets of Board of Education and Curriculum and Evaluation Committee minutes (`raw/elementary/boe/`), Adult Learning Center, Boys & Girls Club and YMCA pages, and two read-only queries of the site's database.
- **Topics:** seven shared slugs (`district-preschool`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `town-recreation-and-summer`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`) and three library topics with their own kind and description (`kindergarten-day-length`, `kindergarten-registration`, `change-of-school-assignment`, the last for the state's twins placement law, RSA 193:3-a). No new topic is proposed.
- **Places:** `district Nashua` for the district, its schools and the programs run in its buildings; `town Nashua` for the Boys & Girls Club and the YMCA; no place for RSA 193:3-a.
- **Main sources:** nashua.edu and the 12 school sites; franklin.nashua.edu (NEEP); Board of Education policies ILAA, IKE, IMAH, JLCF, JCA, JEB, JFAA; the 2026-27 Elementary Student/Parent Handbook; the Nashua Teachers' Union contract 2025-2029; Board and committee minutes from 2023 to October 2026; the Title I pages and Mount Pleasant's 2023-24 schoolwide plan; nashuaalc.org; bgcn.com; nmymca.org; gc.nh.gov; and the site's database for derived comparisons.
- **Main gaps:** no named gifted program or elementary accelerated math class exists in any source checked; no source states minutes of recess or of art and music for Charlotte Avenue third graders; Purple Panther Preschool and the 2026-27 Title I preschool post no eligibility, tuition or application; no Nashua page says in words that kindergarten is free (none lists a charge either); YMCA after-school rates are only in its registration system; and the web search budget ran out after the first searches, so news coverage could not be used to find more primary sources.

## change-of-school-assignment: Asking to Change Schools Within or Outside the District

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: NH's process for requesting a different school (RSA 193:3, as amended by SB 97 in 2025), manifest educational hardship, and tuition-based enrollment in another district.

1. New Hampshire law forbids any school board from adopting a policy of automatically separating twins or other multiples (triplets or more), or of automatically placing them together.
   - citation: RSA 193:3-a, I
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-3-a.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "No school board shall adopt a policy of automatically separating or placing together twins or other multiples. In this section, 'multiples' means triplets or more." Source: 2007, 309:1, eff. Sept. 11, 2007. Raw: raw/elementary/rsa-193-3-a.txt

2. Under New Hampshire law, a parent of twins or other multiples in elementary school may ask, no later than 60 days before the first day of each school year (or at registration for a new school), that they be placed in the same classroom or in separate classrooms; the request must be granted unless the principal, after meeting with the parents and weighing the reasons and the best interests of these and other children, decides a different placement is necessary, and the parent keeps any right to appeal.
   - citation: RSA 193:3-a, II–III
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-3-a.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "A parent or guardian of twins or other multiples in elementary school may, no later than 60 days before the first day of each school year or upon registration in the case of children enrolling in a new school, request that the twins or multiples be placed in the same classroom or in separate classrooms. This request shall be granted unless the principal, after meeting with the parents or guardians and after careful consideration of the reasons for their recommendation and of the best interests of their children and other children in the school affected by this decision, decides that a different placement is necessary. III. This section is not intended to limit a parent's or guardian's right to appeal." The law covers same or separate classrooms, not a choice of teacher. Raw: raw/elementary/rsa-193-3-a.txt
