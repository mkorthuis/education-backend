# Career Pathways and Adult Learning in Nashua

Part of the Nashua research (town/Nashua). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Nashua Technology Center (NTC) and career and technical education (CTE) at Nashua High School North and South, CTE students and sports, employers and internships, Early College (formerly Running Start), apprenticeships, adult English and HiSET classes, the Nashua Adult Learning Center and the district's adult diploma program, Nashua Community College (NCC) workforce training and transfer, local training grants, the public library's study spaces, and routes from paraeducator to teacher.

- **10 fact topics, 66 facts, no number tables.** Eight topics are shared or library slugs; two are proposed below.
- **Builds on** the library's `early-college-at-your-high-school`, `dual-enrollment-rules`, `ccsnh-tuition-and-aid`, `nh-promise-usnh`, `granite-edvance`, `registered-apprenticeship-basics`, `perkins-nh-indicators` and `cte-centers-overview` topics, Albany's `cte-admission-and-cost-rules` and `youth-employment-rules`, Madison's `educator-licensing-pathways` (paraeducator certificates and the site-based licensing plan) and Tamworth's statewide WIOA facts (`adult-education-and-workforce-training` 8–10). Those are named in the question table, not repeated.
- **Sources:** the NTC's pages and brochure, the 2026-27 Program of Studies for North and South (a Google Doc exported as text), Board policies IHBH and IJO/KA, the paraeducators' contract (2025–2028), the Adult Learning Center's and NCC's pages, the City's Workforce page and its draft Skills Gap Analysis, the Nashua Public Library's pages, UNH and Keene State transfer and tuition pages, RSA 319-C:7 and the Ed 500 rules. Two news articles (2019, 2021) are cited only for NTC employer ties, and say so.

**Premises checked:**
- *"The Nashua Technology Center for HVAC or electrical"* (P15.1): **correct, with a detail.** Both programs exist (HVACR and Electrical Technology), but both run at **Nashua High School South**. North students take the shuttle bus between the two high schools.
- *"If he's taking a CTE program at North"* (P15.2): HVACR and Electrical are **not at North**. North hosts Automotive, Biotechnology, Culinary, Cosmetology, Emergency Services and Graphic Communication.
- *"Running Start classes through Nashua Community College"* (P17.1): the program is now called **Early College at Your High School**. Nashua's high schools work with NCC (NCC's coordinator serves North, South and the NTC).
- *"The Nashua Adult Learning Center ... high school equivalency or a medical assistant certificate"* (P33.2): the Adult Learning Center (a nonprofit at 4 Lake Street) offers **free HiSET classes and testing**, but its pages list **no medical assistant certificate**. The medical assistant route found in Nashua is a **paid apprenticeship** run by Southern New Hampshire Health with NCC. The district's own adult diploma program is a separate program, the Nashua Center for Lifelong Learning (also called Nashua Adult & Community School).
- *"Free evening English classes while someone watches my kids"* (P33.1): **free evening classes exist** at the Adult Learning Center (6:30–8:30 p.m., in person or on Zoom), but its rules bar children from the building during classes, and its child care programs close by 6:00 p.m. **No evening child care tied to English classes was found.**
- *"Local grants or workforce training scholarships"* from Nashua (P34.2): **none from the City was found.** Free or funded training comes from NCC (a free manufacturing boot camp), state and federal programs (WIOA, WorkNowNH) and college scholarships.
- *"Does Nashua South help set up a trade union apprenticeship"* (P39.1): South hosts the NTC's Electrical, HVACR and Advanced Manufacturing programs and a Career Center. No union apprenticeship program tied to South was found.
- The brief's question of **where NTC programs run**: answered (cte-center-programs 3).

**Main gaps:**
- How many NTC completers local employers hire: no count is published. The site's data has each high school's graduates' plans, including "Employed."
- Whether NTC students can make after-school practices: no rule or shuttle timetable was found.
- Union apprenticeship programs (electricians, carpenters, plumbers) near Nashua, Nashua Public Library English conversation groups and test-prep resources, and colleges' para-to-teacher programs: not searched, because the session's web search budget ran out.
