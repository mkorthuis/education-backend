# Rye Residents' Questions

The questions the Rye research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Rye is in SAU 50 with Greenland, New Castle and Newington. The Rye School District runs Rye Elementary School and Rye Junior High School, and its high school students attend Portsmouth High School under an AREA agreement. Several questions contain premises (about 400 students, $470,000 returned to the town, a Rye Elementary building study, a preschool from 8:15 to 2:15 with no bus, a long high school bus ride, a math gap between the junior high and high school) that the research should check rather than assume.
