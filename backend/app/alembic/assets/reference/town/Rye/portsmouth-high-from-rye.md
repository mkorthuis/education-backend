# Portsmouth High School From Rye

Part of the Rye research (town/Rye). Gathered November 21–22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Rye's side of Portsmouth High School (PHS), where Rye's students in grades 9–12 go under the AREA agreement: the size of the move from Rye Junior High (RJH), special education at the handoff, college counseling, career and technical education, whether Rye students are treated differently, the SAU 50 high school bus from Rye, parking, how the RJH and PHS calendars line up, AP and dual enrollment, varsity sports, clubs and the arts, storm delays and closings, and the "math gap" between RJH and PHS. Portsmouth High itself is already researched in `town/Portsmouth/` (middle-high-and-cte.md, student-support.md, getting-to-school-choices-and-staff.md, money-buildings-and-governance.md); those facts are cited in the question table, not repeated. Greenland's Portsmouth High file (`town/Greenland/portsmouth-high-from-greenland.md`) had not been written when this file was finished, so the SAU 50 facts it may hold are not cited.

- **7 fact topics, 46 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 9 MB): the sanitized 2025-26 and 2026-27 SAU 50 bus sheets for Rye and New Castle students to PHS (house numbers removed; originals not kept), SAU 50 policies EBCE, EEA, IC, ICA, IHCD, IMBD and JFAC with the policy index, SAU 50's delayed opening and transportation pages, an excerpt of the PHS 2026-27 Student/Family Handbook (parking, buses, weather), Portsmouth's 2026-27 calendar, the RJH website calendar's 2026-27 events, PHS counseling pages (team, college planning, Futures, college fair, activities), the Futures site, the PHS co-curricular booklet, PHS math and performing arts course documents, RJH guidance, grade 8 math and PHS information pages, SAU 50's special services and grade 8 curriculum pages, Rye School Board draft minutes of October 21 and 26, 2026, the SAU 50 Secondary Advisory/AREA documents and the November 2026 assistant superintendent's report, COAST's route list and Route 41 schedule, CRDC 2023-24 rows for RJH, Greenland Central, Portsmouth Middle and PHS, and an excerpt of the 2025-26 Rye School District handbook.
- **Places:** `district Rye` 30, `district Portsmouth` 13, `sau 50` 2, `town Rye` 1.
- **New data concepts:** the Rye high school bus routes with stop times and derived ride lengths, compared year to year; the SAU 50 rule that a Rye student arriving late at PHS on an SAU 50 delay day isn't penalized; a day-by-day comparison of the 2026-27 RJH and PHS calendars; the share of PHS's ninth grade that comes from each public feeder school; Rye Junior High's report of no Algebra I classes in grades 7–8 (CRDC 2023-24) against PHS's accelerated math track; PHS counselor staffing and how many of PHS's clubs meet during the school day; COAST Route 41 as the only public bus at PHS.
- **Main gaps:** no source reports how Rye students do at PHS (grades, ninth-grade math placement, course failures, activities or sports rosters by sending town); PHS's parking permit price, number of student spaces and any waitlist aren't published; no late or activity bus for SAU 50 students is described anywhere; nothing says what happens for Rye's PHS students when SAU 50 cancels school and Portsmouth stays open; no posted procedure describes the RJH-to-PHS IEP handoff. Most Rye School Board minutes and reports from 2025-26 and 2026-27 couldn't be downloaded (see Not retrieved).
- **Proposed new shared topics:** none.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** 1 of 14 (P7.1, career and technical education, by `town/Portsmouth/middle-high-and-cte.md`). P10.3 is answered almost wholly by Portsmouth's file and the library; this file adds one SAU 50 fact.

| P10.2 | RJH and PHS calendars are out of sync on half-days and conferences | Partly right. In 2026-27 they share every vacation week and four early-release days, but PHS has four more Wednesday early releases, RJH closes two days PHS is open, and PHS closes Columbus Day. SAU 50's policy says calendars are coordinated with Portsmouth "to the extent possible" (district-school-day-and-calendar 1–3). |
| P11.2 | Rye students may have a hard time breaking into Portsmouth Middle friend groups and making varsity | About two in three of PHS's public-school ninth graders come from Portsmouth Middle. No source counts cuts or measures friendships (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 1–2; school-programs-after-grade-6 1). |
| P19.2 | Rye and PHS may not call snow days together | Right: SAU 50's superintendent decides for Rye's schools and Portsmouth decides for PHS. SAU 50 says a Rye student whose bus runs two hours late on an SAU 50 delay day, while PHS is open, isn't penalized (district-transportation 11–13). |
| P20.1 | The bus route from Rye hasn't changed and still takes "forever" | The 2026-27 routes did change from 2025-26. Route 1 went from 14 to 25 morning stops with the same 8:25 arrival, and Route 2 now starts 12 minutes earlier. A student at the first stop rides about 49 or 40 minutes in the morning (district-transportation 1, 3–4). SAU 50's policy keeps high school routing "essentially the same from year-to-year" unless renegotiated, and bus service was going out to bid in fall 2026 (district-transportation 5–6). |
| P20.2 | There's a middle-school-to-high-school math gap for Rye students | No source names or measures one. RJH reported no Algebra I classes in grades 7–8 in 2023-24, and its grade 8 course follows the grade 8 standards, while PHS's accelerated ninth-grade track is for students who took Algebra I in grade 8. SAU 50 received PHS placement and performance data in November 2026 to inform "student readiness" and "course pathways" (district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners 1–5). |
