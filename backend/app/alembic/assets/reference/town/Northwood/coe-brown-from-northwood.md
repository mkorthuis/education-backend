# Coe-Brown From Northwood

Part of the Northwood research (town/Northwood). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers high school for Northwood students at Coe-Brown Northwood Academy, Northwood's school of record: how the assignment works, what the 2025–2035 contract with Coe-Brown says about sending students elsewhere (up to 10%), Northwood's policy JFAC and its own alternative education service; the move from Northwood School's 8th grade (tours, registration, teachers' "Bridges" meetings, course placement, orientation and Coe-Brown's freshman transition survey); Northwood's two Coe-Brown bus runs and their 6:15–6:30 a.m. starts, and the lack of a late bus; Coe-Brown's shop and trade courses and what is known about the Dover and Concord career centers; driver education; special education for Northwood students at Coe-Brown (the contract's terms, counts, contracted therapists and the 2027-28 projections); entering from home education; AP and SNHU credit; college counseling and a college tuition discount; phones; absences; FFA and agriculture; work credit; and fees. Coe-Brown's own programs were researched first in `town/Barrington/high-school-choice.md` and `town/Deerfield/high-school-choice.md`; this file cites those facts and adds what they lack, so Nottingham's and Strafford's research can cite these in turn.

| P2.1 | Coe-Brown is "automatically covered by the town" | **Correct.** Coe-Brown is Northwood's "high school of record," and SAU 44's assignment form says the district pays tuition "as long as student remains a resident of Northwood." Unlike Nottingham, Deerfield and Barrington, Northwood families are not offered a choice of schools. There are exceptions, though. The 2024 contract (read in the 2025 deliberative packet) confirms the `places.yaml` note: Northwood may enroll up to 10% of its students at another high school, but must pay Coe-Brown regular tuition for at least 90%. Hardship placements (jointly approved with Coe-Brown), alternative education, special education placements and parent-paid private, charter or military schools don't count toward the 10%. Under policy JFAC, Northwood pays up to Coe-Brown's rate and the family covers the rest and the transportation. Northwood budgets $5,000 a year for other public high schools (it spent $5,214.96 in 2025-26). |
| P2.2 | Students tour Coe-Brown and pick classes with a counselor | **Correct.** Freshman tours on October 28 and November 4, 2026; the headmaster visits Northwood's 8th grade December 3; registration night March 11; an 8th-grade lock-in March 26; a parent night June 3; an orientation day (August 19 in 2026). Course levels mostly follow 8th-grade teacher recommendations ("the most valuable indicator," from the teachers' Bridges meetings), and Coe-Brown says each student may meet a counselor about course requests. Coe-Brown surveys Northwood's freshmen each fall (all 33 took part in 2024). |

Coe-Brown says supervision ends after the last bus and students must leave by 3:00 p.m. unless in a supervised activity. Northwood's board minutes from 2022 to 2026 and its bus policies mention no late bus. Policy EEA buses students who live 2 miles or more from school. |
| P14.1 | A 6:30 a.m. bus stop time | **Roughly correct, and some stops are earlier.** The CB West run's first stop is at 6:30 a.m. and the CB East run's at 6:22. The last stops are at 7:15, and buses reach Coe-Brown at 7:16–7:19. Classes start at 7:40. |
| P4.1 | Shop, welding and auto classes | **Correct.** Woodworking I–II, Basic Automotive, Construction, Power and Energy, Welding I–II and a senior Capstone Shop with OSHA 10 and automotive A/C certifications. Coe-Brown says it has no "formal Career and Technical Education" program. |

| P11.2 | An IEP carries over smoothly to Coe-Brown | **Northwood stays responsible.** It budgets for 25 students receiving special education at Coe-Brown in 2026-27 ($7,669 each, the gap between Coe-Brown's two tuition rates) and 35 projected for 2027-28, plus aides. Its contracted speech, physical therapy, audiology and behavior specialists also serve students at Coe-Brown. Placement counts were 25, 27 and 21 at the ends of 2023-24 to 2025-26. Northwood's contract covers aides and Medicaid billing but, unlike Barrington's and Deerfield's, names no IEP roles or final say. "Smoothly" can't be measured from these sources. |
| P12.3 | Homeschoolers take placement tests | **No test is named.** Coe-Brown says a student entering from home education "will be assessed ... in order to determine a graduation plan." Honors placement for 9th grade uses 8th-grade teacher recommendations and, for Math I Honors, a math packet. A Reading Inventory decides later reading placements. |
| P13.1 | AP and SNHU credits | **Correct.** Coe-Brown offers 13 AP courses, all for grades 11–12 (8 open to juniors), and about 26 SNHU courses at $100 per 3 credits, which it says add up to "90+ College Credits." No limit on how many a student may take is published. |
| P13.3 | Tuition discounts just for Coe-Brown graduates | **Not just for Coe-Brown.** New England College's $12,000-a-year locked-in tuition is open to graduates of 39 partner schools, Coe-Brown among them. The NH Scholars program lists application-fee waivers and scholarships at some New Hampshire colleges. No other Coe-Brown-specific discount was found, though the web search was cut short (see Not retrieved). |
| P15.1 | An active FFA chapter, and agriculture and forestry classes | **Correct, but forestry has no course of its own.** The "CBNA Much-To-Do FFA" chapter is one of 12 chapters listed by the state FFA association. Forestry is taught as a unit of Agricultural Technology I and through Green Futures and the Agriculture Capstone. |
| P15.3 | Athletic and club fees, and waivers for several children | **No posted fees.** Coe-Brown's handbook, catalog and athletics page list no sports or club fee and no family discount. Listed costs are parking ($80), class dues ($10 a year), a netbook deposit, AP exams, college-credit fees and some course fees. |
