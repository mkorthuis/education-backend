# Northwood Residents' Questions

The questions the Northwood research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Northwood is SAU 44. The Northwood School District runs Northwood School (preschool to grade 8), and its high school students attend Coe-Brown Northwood Academy, a private academy in town, under a 2025–2035 tuition contract. Several questions contain premises (a Champions after-school waitlist, a Northwood School app, a lottery-based pre-K, a 6:30 a.m. high school bus, SAU 44 once shared with other towns) that the research should check rather than assume.
