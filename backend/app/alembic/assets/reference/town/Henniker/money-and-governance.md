# Money and Governance in Henniker: The Henniker School District, John Stark Regional and SAU 24

Part of the Henniker research (town/Henniker). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered.** How Henniker's 2025 local school tax rate of $13.97 splits between the Henniker School District ($9.64) and John Stark Regional ($4.33); the Henniker district's revenue, the $195,265 drop in its state aid for 2026-27 and why, its fund balance returned to taxpayers, and its own rate projections against the rates later adopted; Henniker's share of John Stark's costs and students, and how its cost per student and its John Stark rate on market value compare with Weare's; the 2026-27 Henniker budget ($11,186,252, up 2.98%), its drivers, its default budget, and how the board trimmed a first draft that was up 4.73%; the 2026–2028 teachers' contract and Henniker's place in SAU 24's pay comparison; special education spending and trusts; Henniker's ballot results for 2025 and 2026 (every budget passed from 2023 to 2026); the board's meetings, live stream, records, committees, pay and the 2027 filing period and budget calendar; John Stark's board seats and Henniker's share of its voters; the town's Saturday town meeting, which decides only the town budget; the elderly exemption (from news reports) and what it saves on the school part of a bill; why HCS and John Stark calendars differ; the open enrollment article Henniker's board dropped; and whether Henniker families leave before high school.

**Counts.** 7 topics (all shared), 42 facts, 2 number tables.

**Main sources.** SAU 24's site and the Henniker board's public Google Drive folders: the FY27 public hearing and deliberative slides, revenue sheet, default calculation, object budget, signed warrant and DRA budget form (MS-26), trust balances, the November 2025 tentative teachers' agreement, approved board minutes (November 2025 to April 2026) and the committee schedule; the final 2026–2028 teachers' contract; the HCS and John Stark 2026-27 calendars; SAU 24's 2024 and 2025 annual reports (Henniker and John Stark sections, ballots, proration estimates); the Simbli meeting list and SAU 24 calendar for 2027 dates; RSA 72:39-b, 669:19 and 671:19; and the Concord Monitor for 2026 ballot results, town meeting dates and the elderly exemption, cited as news where it is the only source. John Stark's own budget, votes, contracts and cost formula are in `../Weare/money-and-governance.md` and are cited, not repeated.

**Main gaps.** The town's website (henniker.org) and NH DRA's site refused automated requests, so Henniker's current elderly exemption amounts, income and asset limits and filing details, the 2026 tax rate, any revaluation history and the town's budget committee status come only from news reports or not at all. The 2027-28 budget and warrant articles weren't posted yet. No source tracks where families who leave Henniker move, and the Census API now requires a key, so no mobility estimates were gathered. Simbli agendas and minutes after June 2026 load by JavaScript and weren't read.
