# Henniker Residents' Questions

The questions the Henniker research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Henniker is in SAU 24 with Weare. The Henniker School District runs Henniker Community School (preschool to grade 8); grades 9–12 attend John Stark Regional High School in Weare, run by the John Stark Regional School District of Henniker and Weare and researched in `../Weare/`. Several questions contain premises (a $300 ski club fee, the local school portion near 60% of the tax bill, an 8th-grade math track that locks students in, John Stark's low math proficiency, middle schoolers sharing a building with preschoolers, a town meeting vote on the school budget, New England College courses, White Birch vacation camp, Covered Bridge Montessori) that the research should check rather than assume.
