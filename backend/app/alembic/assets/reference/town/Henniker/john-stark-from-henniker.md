# John Stark Regional High School From Henniker

Part of the Henniker research (town/Henniker). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers John Stark Regional High School (JSRHS) as Henniker families see it: getting into and getting to the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC), apprenticeship and career help, New England College (NEC) courses and NEC's tuition offer for John Stark graduates, the AP courses that actually run in 2026-27 and AP results through 2026, VLACS in summer, what happened to John Stark's alternative program, the minimum diploma, sports at Henniker Community School (HCS) and John Stark, the morning bus times from Henniker, parking, the Tuesday late bus, the gap between John Stark's math and reading results, and college and financial aid help. John Stark itself is researched in `town/Weare/middle-and-high-school.md` (cited below as "Weare file"); this file adds only what that file and the library lack, and Henniker's own side.

- **8 fact topics, 40 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 3 MB): the 2026-27 Henniker bus routes (HCS and John Stark combined), John Stark's NEASC accreditation visiting team report (October 2024), SAU 24's "Our Data Story" AP report (October 2026, transcribed from image) and ESSA indicator report, the Senior Scoop page (parking), NEC's High School Partnerships page and its November 5, 2026 announcement, two VLACS pages, the HCS athletics page, John Stark board minutes of April 8 and May 13, 2026 (not read by the Weare agent), excerpts copied from Weare's raw copies (Program of Studies, handbook, 2025 annual report, strategic plan reports, October 8, 2025 minutes), and an OpenStreetMap geocode and OSRM drive route from HCS to John Stark.
- **Places:** 38 facts use `district John Stark Regional` and 2 use `district Henniker` (HCS sports). Tables carry a `place` line.
- **New data concepts:** morning pickup times by bus for Henniker's high schoolers; the free parking permit for juniors and seniors; the closure of John Stark's "Connections" alternative program; the four AP courses actually running in 2026-27 (down from 12 in 2021-22) and five years of AP results; NEC's tuition-free courses for seniors and $12,000 tuition for John Stark graduates; VLACS summer and partial-course (competency) enrollment; the **derived** reading–math gap at John Stark against the state's grade 11 gap; SAT participation (86.9% in 2026) and SAU 24's SAT goal; and the **derived** number of CRTC student-years that 2026's Article 7 can pay for.
