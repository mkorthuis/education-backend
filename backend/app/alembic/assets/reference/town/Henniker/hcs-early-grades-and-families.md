# Henniker Community School's Early Grades and Young Families

Part of the Henniker research (town/Henniker). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Henniker Community School (HCS, preschool to grade 8) from the youngest children's side: the public preschool, the kindergarten cutoff and day length, school hours and the car loop, before- and after-school care, the 2026-27 calendar and vacation care, storm delays, the four Henniker bus routes and their stops on dirt roads, math grouping and advanced learners, how HCS's early-grade scores compare with Hopkinton's and Bow's, meals and how to apply for free meals, how the school reaches families (ParentSquare, PowerSchool, Pick Up Patrol), Child Find and preschool evaluations, infant care and private preschools, and summer and after-school programs in town.

- **Main findings:**
  - HCS runs a half-day, "fully inclusive" preschool for 3- and 4-year-olds; tuition was $275 a month in 2025-26. Voters approved $150,000 in March 2023 to expand it beyond a special-education-focused model, and it went back to morning and afternoon sessions (23 children in 2023-24). October 1 counts since 2021 run from 7 to 20. No page gives the number of places, how children are chosen or when to register.
  - The kindergarten cutoff is not absolute: Policy JEB admits children who turn five before September 30 and, "in rare instances, space permitting", considers October–December birthdays after a district evaluation the parents arrange and pay for. Kindergarten is full-day.
  - The school day is 7:35–7:45 arrival to 2:25–2:35 dismissal. White Birch Center runs before-care from 7:00 and after-care until 5:30 at HCS ($125 and $175 a week in 2026), and full-day care during HCS vacation weeks and workshop days ($280 a week). The free Youth Hub serves grades 5–8 on Tuesdays and Thursdays to 6 p.m.
  - All four Henniker buses carry HCS and John Stark students together. Morning pickups begin 6:51–7:05 and buses unload at HCS at 7:35, 30 to 44 minutes after the first active stop. Seven active stops are on roads NHDOT lists as entirely unpaved (Baker, Peasley and French roads). In late December and January, 42 of the 82 timed stops come before the 7:16 sunrise. Old Hillsboro Road itself is paved.
  - HCS reported no gifted program to the federal civil rights survey, and no source describes a separate advanced math class in grades K–5. Extension happens through the daily WIN block, i-Ready-based grouping and personalized math pathways. Policy IHCD lets a student who has met grade-level competencies take advanced coursework.
  - Free and reduced-price meals need an application each year (online, on paper or during PowerSchool enrollment). Since fall 2025, ParentSquare carries newsletters, report cards and permission slips, and Pick Up Patrol carries each child's dismissal plan.
- **Main gaps:** how many HCS preschool places there are and how they're filled; 2026-27 preschool tuition and meal prices on the HCS site; any written rule on a regular second drop-off address (such as a relative's house); HCS summer school or tutoring for 2026 or 2027; White Birch's actual waitlist length; Covered Bridge Montessori's tuition; Henniker's town pages (recreation, road plowing), which refused automated requests; Henniker School Board documents after June 3, 2026 (no longer in the Drive folder).

## kindergarten-entry-cutoff: Kindergarten Entry Age Cutoff by District

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: Each district's birthday cutoff for kindergarten entry (New Hampshire law sets no statewide entry age), from its policy or registration page.

1. Henniker's age-of-entrance policy JEB lets a child enter kindergarten if he or she will be five before September 30 (and grade 1 if six before September 30), and "in rare instances," space permitting, considers early entry for a child who turns five or six between October 1 and December 31, after a district evaluation of social, emotional, motor and academic skills that the parents arrange and pay for; the Superintendent makes the final decision.
   - citation: Henniker School District Policy JEB, Age of Entrance (re-adopted 6/6/2018)
   - source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ebB89zzwpdOVeGd4V-iVkWmbE2QFdoLr/view
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Henniker
   - note: Text: "A student may enter kindergarten if his/her chronological age will be five before September 30 of the year of entering school." "In rare instances, the School District will consider parental requests for early enrollment, space permitting, for a child who will not be five or six years old until the period between October 1 and December 31. To determine early enrollment, the child must have completed an evaluation administered by school district personnel. ... must measure the child's social, emotional, motor skills and academic abilities. The parents will be responsible for initiating the scheduling and for related expenses. ... the team will forward a recommendation to the Superintendent of Schools who will make the final decision." The registration page says "by September 30th" (library kindergarten-entry-cutoff 37), so a child born on September 30 is not clearly covered by either wording.
