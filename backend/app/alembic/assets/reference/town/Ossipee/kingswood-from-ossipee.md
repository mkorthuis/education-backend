# Kingswood High and the Lakes Region Technology Center From Ossipee

Part of the Ossipee research (town/Ossipee). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Ossipee's high school students and recent graduates can do at Kingswood Regional High School (KRHS, grades 9–12, site school id 22425) and the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC) in Wolfeboro, both run by the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (site district id 208, SAU 49): when Construction Trades can start, LRTC and sports, AP and Early College limits, college counseling and financial aid help, Health Science placements and Huggins Hospital, personal finance and trades, Kingswood Academy, credits and early graduation, work credit for Culinary students, JROTC and the ASVAB, and, for young adults, nursing programs, adult education, apprenticeship help and scholarships. Wolfeboro's finished `town/Wolfeboro/kingswood-high-and-lrtc.md` already has the shared bell schedule, LRTC enrollment and priority rules, AP and Early College lists, graduates' plans and local scholarship process, all placed with the district; this file cites those facts and writes Ossipee's side.

- **5 fact topics, 50 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 11 MB): the KRHS 2026-27 Program of Studies, Student/Family Handbook (scanned; OCR text) and School Profile, copied from Wolfeboro's raw folder the same day; KRHS counseling pages (college planning, scholarships, gap year, alumni) fetched again; LRTC's 2026-27 handbook and program pages (Construction Trades, Culinary Arts, Health Science, Skylight Dining Room, HOSA, career talks); 8 board policies (IKF, IKFCL, IKFG, IHBH, IHBH-R, IHBI, LEB, JRA-E); the district's 2026 annual report (scanned) and the November 18, 2025 Finance Committee notes; Huggins Hospital's job board and its two student postings; Lakes Region Community College's nursing, admission and scholarship pages and nursing program sheet; White Mountains Community College's nursing and LPN pages; Carroll County Adult Education's pages and NH Adult Education's location list; the district's continuing education page; NH Charitable Foundation's scholarship page; and an OSRM drive-time file from Ossipee Town Hall.
- **Places:** 41 facts use `district Governor Wentworth Regional`, 2 `town Ossipee`, 3 `town Laconia` (Lakes Region Community College), 2 `town Berlin` (White Mountains Community College) and 2 have no place (statewide community college scholarships and the nearest JROTC units). The two Huggins Hospital facts are placed with the district because they describe placements open to LRTC's Health Science students; they name the hospital and its town.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Construction Trades starts in grade 10 at the earliest, not grade 9 or 11.** Construction Trades I is for grades 10–11 (2 credits), Level II for grades 11–12 (4 credits, two blocks a day all year) and Level III is senior-year work-based learning. Grade 10 entry needs related courses first; Wood Technology I and II are open to 9th graders and are the recommended lead-in, while the recommended Electricity course is not offered in 2026-27 (cte-center-programs 1–4).
  - **Health Science and Huggins.** LRTC's Health Science II includes observation and "on-the-job experiences at local facilities," and its Nurse Assisting option has 60 clinical hours at "a local health care facility," but no source names the facility. Huggins Hospital posts a "Student Rotation" in which high school HOSA or school-to-work students "receive school credit," and a no-credit job shadow capped at 12 hours. LRTC has a HOSA chapter (cte-center-programs 7–11).
  - **Kingswood Academy is real and new.** It replaced the contracted Lakeside Academy (Ombudsman, about 40 seats, about 15 attending in early 2025) for 2026-27. It is a partial-day program on the Kingswood campus, by application, with priority to juniors and seniors; families provide transportation one way, which matters for Ossipee. Students must pass the civics test "to ensure alignment" with state graduation rules; no source says which diploma they receive (school-programs-after-grade-6 13–16).
  - **Credits.** 26 credits for the standard diploma, 28 for honors, and 20 for the State Standard Diploma (by application in grade 11). Students must carry 7 credits a year; seniors who already have 20 credits may ask to carry 6. The board "supports early graduation," but the catalog says students who finish in January are "completed early (not graduated)" and get the diploma in June (school-programs-after-grade-6 1–5).
  - **Work hours for credit.** Seniors can earn up to 1 elective credit for paid work (¼ credit per 100 hours, with paystubs, from July 1 of senior year). Culinary Arts III work-based learning (grade 12) and extended learning opportunities are the other routes. No source says whether a parent's business can be the placement (cte-center-programs 5–6, school-programs-after-grade-6 6–7).
  - **No JROTC.** The 2026-27 catalog has no JROTC course. KRHS hosts the ASVAB Career Exploration Program for interested 11th and 12th graders, says taking it creates no military obligation, and gives recruiters names, addresses and phone numbers unless a family opts out (district-student-support 5–7).
  - **Adult nursing isn't offered by the district.** The fall 2026 continuing education list has no health courses, and LRTC's Nurse Assisting program is a high school course. The nearest RN programs are at Lakes Region Community College in Laconia (about 40 road miles from Ossipee Town Hall), which gives admission points for a current LNA license, and White Mountains Community College in Berlin (about 64 miles), which allows part-time study (adult-education-and-workforce-training 1–8).
