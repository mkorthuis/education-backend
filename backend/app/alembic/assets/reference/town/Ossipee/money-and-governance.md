# Money and Governance: Ossipee's Side of the Governor Wentworth Regional School District

Part of the Ossipee research (town/Ossipee). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 5 topics, 39 facts and 2 number tables on Ossipee's share of the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49): what Ossipee raises for the district and why it has grown faster than the district total, its state adequacy aid and property value per pupil, the projected 2026-27 school tax rate and how the local and state school taxes fit together, the new school tax cap; what is and isn't published about spending by school, Ossipee Central's budget, its open jobs and the substitute nurse rate; how Ossipee votes compared with the district result and Wolfeboro, the budget calendar, board meetings at Ossipee Central and the records law; the superintendent change and search; and who pays for the Kingswood campus and Ossipee Central's building work. The Wolfeboro money file (town/Wolfeboro/money-and-governance.md) already has the district budget, its drivers, the 75/25 cost formula, contracts and buildings; this file cites it and adds Ossipee's side.

**Main sources:** GWRSD's FY2027 "Financial Reports" handout (Distribution of School Costs, read from the page image), ballot results for 2023–2026, the 2025 District Report Card, the 2026 Annual Report, board and Finance Committee minutes (April 2025–October 2026) and the November 2, 2026 agenda, the superintendent's page and monthly reports, policy BEDH, the district's job postings (AppliTrack, November 21, 2026), BLS wage data, RSA 91-A:3 and 91-A:4, and read-only queries of the site's tax, enrollment and adequacy data.

**Main findings:**
- **Ossipee's school taxes grew faster than the district's.** From tax year 2014 to 2025 Ossipee's school taxes rose 81.7% while the six towns' total rose 55.4% and Ossipee's resident students fell 2.1% (**derived**). Ossipee's share of the district's students rose from about 25.6% (2015) to 27.7% (2024) as other towns lost students faster, and its state adequacy grant fell by about $947,000 from FY2024 to FY2026.
- **Projected 2026-27:** Ossipee raises $14,352,733 (up 10.66%), at a combined school rate of $9.14 per $1,000 (local $8.08, state $1.06), up $0.80. Both school parts of the bill go to Governor Wentworth.
- **Votes:** Ossipee cast about 17% of 2026 budget ballots; Wolfeboro 43%. On the 18 articles of 2025–2026, Ossipee's majority was on the winning side 14 times, and only 2 results would have changed without Wolfeboro's votes (**derived**).
- **Superintendent:** the superintendent resigned effective July 1, 2026; an interim serves for 2026-27, and a search consultant was to be named in November 2026. No survey or forum had been posted.
- **Substitute nurses:** the board raised the rate from $120 a day to $50 an hour on October 5, 2026, after the Huggins Hospital nursing contract ended and substitutes turned down calls.

**Main gaps:** No school-by-school budget is published, so the split between Ossipee Central and the Kingswood campus can't be given in dollars. The Town of Ossipee's website and its annual reports at UNH, and NH DRA, refused automated requests, so Ossipee's precinct rates, exemption amounts, 2023 revaluation and tax year 2026 rate were not retrieved. The November 2, 2026 minutes (superintendent search consultant) weren't posted.
