# Ossipee Residents' Questions

The questions the Ossipee research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Ossipee's students attend the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (SAU 49), shared with Brookfield, Effingham, New Durham, Tuftonboro and Wolfeboro. Ossipee's elementary children attend Ossipee Central School; grades 7–12 attend Kingswood Regional Middle and High School in Wolfeboro, where the Lakes Region Technology Center offers career programs. The district is researched in `../Wolfeboro/`; the files here add Ossipee's side. Several questions contain premises (a superintendent search, substitute nurse pay of $50 an hour, Kingswood Academy, the School's Out program, nicer facilities in Wolfeboro) that the research should check rather than assume.
