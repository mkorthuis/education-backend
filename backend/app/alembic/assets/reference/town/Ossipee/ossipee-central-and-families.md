# Ossipee Central School and Young Families

Part of the Ossipee research (town/Ossipee). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Ossipee Central School (OCS, preschool to grade 6, in Center Ossipee) in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49) from a young family's side: the district's integrated preschool at OCS and how typical children get its few tuition spots; the School's Out! after-school program (hours, who it serves, fees, late bus, funding and whether it is guaranteed); the school day, kindergarten and buses; reading and math teaching, local test results and the Title I schoolwide program; special education, speech and related-service staff and who to contact about an IEP; art and music; meals and fee help; how the school reaches families and translation; the PTO; summer programs and meals; the Ossipee Concerned Citizens child care center and other licensed programs nearby, with drive times; the Ossipee Public Library's story times; and local holiday help.

16 topics, 71 facts and 2 number tables. Main sources: the OCS 2026-27 Student & Family Handbook, staff list, PTO, preschool, Title I and calendar pages; OCS's Title I Schoolwide Plan and Needs Assessment for 2026-27; the School's Out! website (fees, schedule, late bus, FAQ, funding); GWRSD policies DKB (preschool payment), EBCD and EBCD-R (closings), EEA (transportation), EFAA (meal charges), IMBA (remote learning) and KB (Title I family engagement); GWRSD food service, special education, ESOL, ParentSquare and homeless-information pages; GWRSD board and Finance Committee minutes (October 2024 to October 2026) and the 2025 and 2026 annual reports; the Ossipee Concerned Citizens child care pages and parent handbook; the Ossipee Public Library's pages; the NH Food Bank's 2026 summer meal flyer; NH's Ed 306 rules; the site's child care data with OSRM drive times.

## district-preschool: The Integrated Preschool at Ossipee Central School

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: Whether each district runs a preschool program, where, and any posted tuition.

1. GWRSD policy DKB (revised March 9, 2026) says the district runs an integrated preschool at Ossipee Central School and New Durham School, set up to serve children with educational disabilities, and opens it to "a small number" of children without disabilities as role models "on a space available basis," keeping a list of candidates in each building and notifying parents when openings occur.
   - citation: GWRSD Policy DKB, Pre-School Payment Policy (adopted 2/1/10, reaffirmed 11/06/2023, revised 3/09/2026)
   - source: https://gwrsd.sau49nh.gov/fs/resource-manager/view/3222730b-be76-4349-a1e4-e339cd4b0ca8
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Governor Wentworth Regional
   - note: Policy text: "The Governor Wentworth Regional School District operates an integrated pre-school program in both the Ossipee Central School and the New Durham School. The programs have been established to provide special education opportunities for children with educational disabilities. In order to provide appropriate age and developmental models for these young children, the programs in each school are made available to a small number of non-disabled pre-school aged children on a space available basis. Lists of possible candidates will be maintained in each building and parents/guardians will be notified when openings occur." The number of peer spots and the length of the list aren't published.
