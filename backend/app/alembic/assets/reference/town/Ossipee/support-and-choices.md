# Support, Kingswood Middle and Choices From Ossipee

Part of the Ossipee research (town/Ossipee). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Ossipee's side of the move from Ossipee Central School (OCS, grades PK–6, Center Ossipee) to Kingswood Regional Middle School (KRMS, grades 7–8, Wolfeboro), and the supports and choices around it for Ossipee families in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (SAU 49): how far Ossipee's villages are from Kingswood, the late bus and other ways home, who Ossipee students join at KRMS, orientation, technology at OCS, counselors and mental health and substance-prevention help at OCS and Kingswood, 504 plans moving from OCS to KRMS, attendance and family-vacation rules, moving within the district or to Tamworth, homeschoolers returning for grade 9, EFA providers in Ossipee, weekend food bags, food pantries and holiday drives, and how churches and other groups can partner with the schools.

Much of Kingswood and the district is already researched in Wolfeboro's `town/Wolfeboro/middle-school-support-and-choices.md` (cited below as "Wolfeboro middle file"), which places its KRMS and district-wide facts at `district Governor Wentworth Regional`. This file cites those facts and adds what they lack.

- **12 topics, 71 facts, 3 tables.** All topics use shared slugs. Most facts are placed at `district Governor Wentworth Regional`; Ossipee's recreation department, EFA providers, food pantries, the county public health coalition, the Chamber food drive and Tri-County Transit are placed at `town Ossipee`. No new state-rule facts; state rules are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **Main sources:** Governor Wentworth policies (JH attendance, IHBA and IHBA-R on 504 plans, JCA school changes, JG placement, IK credits, JLDBB suicide prevention, JLCK well-being, IHAMA and JICH on drugs and alcohol, EEB and EEAG on transportation, IJO, IJOC and KCD on partners, volunteers and gifts); the district's Section 504, homeless, drug and alcohol, suicide prevention and Data & Reporting pages; the October 1, 2025 enrollment sheet; Kingswood High's 2023 Youth Risk Behavior Survey report; the 2026 annual report; the OCS and KRMS handbooks, staff lists, calendars and Meet the Teams sheet; federal Civil Rights Data Collection counts; Knights Against Hunger, End 68 Hours of Hunger, the Carroll County Food Access Network list, the Carroll County Coalition for Public Health and Tri-County Transit; the library's EFA marketplace list; and derived drive times (OpenStreetMap).
- **Main findings:**
  - **Who Ossipee students join:** On October 1, 2025 Ossipee sent 78 of KRMS's students (34 seventh graders, 44 eighth graders), second to Wolfeboro's 97, alongside students from New Durham, Tuftonboro, Effingham, Middleton (tuition), Brookfield and Freedom; and 184 Kingswood High students, again second to Wolfeboro. KRMS groups each grade into two teams.
  - **Distance:** Model drives to KRMS with no stops run 25 minutes from Granite, 28 from Center Ossipee, 36 from West Ossipee and 45 from the Conner Pond area (via Pine Hill Road). Bus pickup times and ride lengths are not published.
  - **Counselors:** OCS lists two school counselors, a K–6 outreach counselor, a school psychologist, a Student Support Center staff member and a family liaison; federal data show 2 counselors for about 294 OCS students. The counselor in each building is its suicide-prevention liaison.
  - **504 plans:** OCS and KRMS are both district schools, so the plan stays with one district. The 2026 annual report names the OCS and KRMS assistant principals as 504 coordinators (K–6 and 7–12); the district's website names an SAU Student Success Coordinator.
  - **Food help:** End 68 Hours of Hunger sends weekend meals home with preschool–grade 6 students in Ossipee and the other district towns all year; Knights Against Hunger, a KRHS student club, runs a confidential weekend food bag program for KRMS and KRHS with no eligibility rules. Ossipee has Provisions Food Pantry in West Ossipee, Ossipee Concerned Citizens' meals and the First Congregational Church's Saturday Dinner Bell.
  - **Survey data:** In Kingswood High's 2023 Youth Risk Behavior Survey, 41.3% of students felt sad or hopeless, 23.4% vaped in the past month, 4.4% had unstable housing and 3.5% often went hungry, each a little above the state figure.
  - **Absences:** Family vacations need a note at least five school days ahead and the principal's approval; Kingswood High warns that vacation absences can cost grades or credit.
- **Main gaps:** bus route sheets, pickup times and actual ride lengths; where the late bus stops in Ossipee; any spring visit to KRMS for OCS sixth graders; a written procedure for moving 504 plans from OCS to KRMS; who runs holiday drives for OCS children; homeless student counts for the district; Ossipee Recreation's program details and eligibility (the town site refused automated requests and the RecDesk listings load by script); whether OCS sixth graders' technology skills lag at KRMS (nothing measures it).
