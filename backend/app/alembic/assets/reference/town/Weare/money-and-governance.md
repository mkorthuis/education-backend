# Money and Governance in Weare: The Weare School District, John Stark Regional and SAU 24

Part of the Weare research (town/Weare). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered.** How Weare's $15.00 local school tax rate splits between the Weare School District ($9.77) and John Stark Regional ($5.23), and how John Stark's costs are divided between Henniker and Weare (half students, half property value, under the 1985 Articles of Agreement); the 2026-27 budgets, default budgets and what separates them; the March 2025 and March 2026 votes (both teacher contracts and both budgets failed in 2026, John Stark's teacher contract also in 2025) and the status quo agreements that followed; teacher pay against 10 area districts, coaching stipends and the athletic trainer vote; turnover evidence (Weare Middle's 17% yearly average from 2012 to 2023, John Stark's open positions and lost courses in fall 2026, 20 John Stark job postings); both districts' 2026 open enrollment articles, their reasons and admission rules; SAU 24's makeup, cost split, rented Henniker office and its September 2026 agreement with SAU 34 (Hillsboro-Deering) to explore shared services; the 1985 decision to form a grades 9-12 cooperative instead of K-12; capital reserves, capital plans, the end of the Weare Middle bond, and what is planned for roofs, sprinklers and HVAC; where board meetings stream and how long recordings are kept; where test results are posted; and home sales in Weare against Goffstown, New Boston and Henniker.

**Counts.** 10 topics (all shared), 79 facts, 6 number tables.

**Main sources.** SAU 24's site (board pages, collective bargaining page, live stream pages, calendar, "Our Data Story", John Stark history page) and its public Google Drive budget folders: both districts' FY27 public hearing and deliberative slides, warrants, default budget forms and calculations, revenue sheets, trust balances and contract cost sheets; the 2024 and 2025 SAU 24 annual reports (board and principal reports, the John Stark proration estimate, the SAU apportionment, the March 2025 official ballots); the WEA and JSTA memoranda of understanding; the SAU 24 and SAU 34 memorandum; the 1985 Articles of Agreement; the town's scanned 2026 ballot tallies; the SAU job site; NH REALTORS® market updates; and the Concord Monitor (results totals and the November 20, 2026 report on John Stark's vacancies, cited as news).

**Main gaps.** The town's website refused automated requests, so tax bill due dates, any 2026 revaluation and the 2026 tax rate are missing. Simbli board minutes load by JavaScript and weren't read, so what each board did after the March 2026 votes is known only from the contracts page, the MOUs and news. No turnover rate for John Stark or Center Woods, and no study of the cost of running two districts instead of one, was found. Why the SAU office is in Henniker isn't explained anywhere found. Field and bleacher condition isn't described beyond capital plan entries.
