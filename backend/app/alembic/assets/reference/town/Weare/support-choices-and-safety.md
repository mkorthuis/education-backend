## Summary

Covers special education staffing and openings at Weare Middle School (WMS) and John Stark Regional High School, how therapies fit the WMS day, the move from WMS to John Stark with an IEP; how homeschooled and EFA students can take WMS or John Stark classes, band or sports (Weare and John Stark policies JJJ, John Stark's EFA tuition policy JFAE, the Weare board's EFA vote, the CSF EFA handbook, EFA providers in Weare); fees for middle school sports, band and trips; who gets the WMS gym (policy KF); bullying, cyberbullying and harassment policies and the 2022 racist-threat case at John Stark; counselors and the social worker at WMS; building security, the Weare Police memorandum, state security grants and drills; military families; and the PowerSchool parent portal.

10 topics (one an existing library topic, efa-uses-and-requirements), 88 facts, no number tables. Sources: SAU 24's website (special education, WMS and John Stark pages, staff directories, the 2026-27 Weare K–8 and John Stark handbooks); the SAU 24 job board (36 openings on November 21, 2026); the Weare and John Stark school boards' public Google Drive folders (policies and approved minutes, 2022–June 2026); the SAU 24 2025 Annual Report (saved by the elementary agent); NH DOE's SAFE grant list (via the Legislative Budget Assistant); the Children's Scholarship Fund's August 2026 EFA Parent Handbook; the library's EFA marketplace download; and, for the 2022 case only, NHPR and the Concord Monitor.

Main gaps: no count of open paraprofessional hours or of how often aides are reassigned; no rule on when speech or OT sessions are scheduled; no written WMS-to-John Stark IEP transition procedure; no posted athletic fee amount; no school resource officer found, and the town's police pages refused automated requests; no description of door locks or buzzers; no posted rule on telling parents about drills; no first-responder family program; Weare has not posted an EFA tuition policy; the 2023 court ruling in the John Stark case and the NH DOJ release were blocked.
