# Weare Middle School's Older Grades and John Stark Regional High School

Part of the Weare research (town/Weare). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## district-technology-and-phones: Devices, Technology Rules and Phones

1. The John Stark School Board adopted policy JICJ, "Unauthorized Communication Devices," on September 10, 2025 and revised it October 8, 2025; it says personal devices used "for nonacademic means often leads to disruptions in the learning environment" and strictly prohibits student use of phones, tablets, laptops and smartwatches from the first bell to the dismissal bell, a school day that "includes lunch periods, passing time, and recesses."
