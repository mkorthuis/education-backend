# Weare Residents' Questions

The questions the Weare research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Weare is in SAU 24. The Weare School District runs Center Woods School (preschool to grade 3) and Weare Middle School (grades 4–8); grades 9–12 attend John Stark Regional High School, run by the John Stark Regional School District of Weare and Henniker. John Stark students may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. Several questions contain premises (4th graders at the middle school, a 40-minute rule for competencies, a Tuesday late bus at John Stark, an open enrollment warrant article, talk of joining Hillsboro-Deering, the SAU office in Henniker) that the research should check rather than assume.
