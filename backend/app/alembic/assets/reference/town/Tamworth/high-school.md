# High School for Tamworth Students: Kennett, the Career Center and Moultonborough Academy

Part of the Tamworth research (town/Tamworth). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers high school for Tamworth students. It treats Kennett High School in Conway (SAU 9) with the Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center (MWVCTC), and Moultonborough Academy (MA, SAU 45), where a 2024 contract lets a small number of Tamworth students attend. It adds the Tamworth side of the move from the Kenneth A. Brett School to high school. Most Kennett questions were already answered by `town/sau9/kennett-and-sau9.md`, `town/sau9/kennett-pathways.md`, `town/Jackson/after-jackson-grammar.md` and `town/Conway/schools-k-8.md`. The question table maps them by file, slug and fact number, and this file gathers only Moultonborough Academy facts, Tamworth angles and gaps.

- **10 fact topics, 65 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high-school/` (about 41 MB): Moultonborough Academy's 2026-27 Program of Studies, 2024-25 profile, 2026-27 handbook and counseling pages; the Tamworth High School Options Committee's final report, comparison spreadsheet, FAQ and minutes; Tamworth's 2003 Kennett contract (OCR); Tamworth policies JJJ, IHBH, IHBG and IHBA; 17 sets of Tamworth School Board minutes (2023–2026, out of 51 searched) and two 2026 board packets that contain Kennett principal's reports for September and October 2026; NHIAA's 2026-27 eligibility articles; RSA 193:1-c; NCES school pages; and VLACS, WMCC, LRCC, Chocorua Lake Conservancy and Tin Mountain pages.
- **Places:** 21 facts use `district Moultonborough` (Moultonborough Academy and the career center its students attend), 18 use `district Tamworth` (Tamworth's committee, contract, policies, transition steps and numbers), 14 use `district Conway` (new Kennett and career-center facts), 1 uses `town Tamworth` (the Chocorua Lake Conservancy), 1 uses `town Albany` (Tin Mountain), and 10 have no place (statewide providers, colleges, NHIAA rules and derived comparisons across districts).
- **New data concepts:**
  - Moultonborough Academy's AP, honors, Early College, computer science and career-center options, set side by side with Kennett's (table tamworth_kennett_ma_course_options_2026_27).
  - The Lakes Region Technology Center in Wolfeboro, where MA students take career-tech programs, including Agricultural Science with forestry.
  - The Tamworth High School Options Committee's 2023 findings and resident survey.
  - Tamworth's own transition steps: the joint Kennett and MA information night for Brett eighth graders, and counts of Tamworth students with IEPs at Kennett and MA.
  - How NHIAA's homeschool and below-grade-9 rules apply to a homeschooled Tamworth teen.
  - Derived comparisons built from the site's data and NCES: Kennett and MA against Portsmouth and Oyster River high schools, and students by race and ethnicity.
- **Premises checked:**
  - P2.3 "still getting agricultural, forestry and diesel training": Kennett's career center has no agriculture, forestry or diesel program in 2026-27. Its automotive program names "Diesel & Heavy Equipment Technician" only as a career path. Whether such programs once existed was not checked. MA students can take Agricultural Science, which includes forestry, at the Lakes Region Technology Center in Wolfeboro.
  - P5.2 a homeschooled 14-year-old: at 14 a student may be at the eighth-grade level. NHIAA bars students below grade 9 from Division I and II teams, and Kennett plays in Division II in most sports. No written rule says how a homeschooled Tamworth resident joins Kennett classes, sports or the career center (district-homeschool-access 1–6).
  - P7.2 "IEP handoff ... in 8th grade": this is correct in outline. The Brett School (SAU 13) serves students through grade 8, and Kennett (SAU 9) takes them from grade 9. Under Tamworth's contract, Tamworth's special education representative stays involved. No written handoff procedure was found.
  - P8.3 "a town that is over 90% white": NCES's 2024-25 counts show 91.5% of Brett School students, 90.8% of Kennett students and 91.2% of MA students as White (**derived**). The town's census figure is agent 5's.
  - P9.1 "electrical, plumbing or construction trades": the career center has no stand-alone electrical or plumbing program. Its Construction Trades program covers both, and earlier files already say so.
  - P3.3 "Lakes Region Community College": LRCC's list of partner high schools includes MA and not Kennett. Kennett's college partner is White Mountains Community College. Both colleges offer online Early College courses.
  - P16.2 "December information night" and "~165 students": Brett's Kennett page still shows only a December 5, 2024 information night, which was a joint Kennett and MA night. Kennett held its own eighth-grade family tours and club expo on October 28, 2026. No "shadow day" by that name was found. The site's data has 165 Brett students in 2024-25 and 154 in 2025-26 (see For another file).
- **Proposed new shared topic:** none needed.
- **Main gaps:**

  - Any written path for a homeschooled Tamworth teen into Kennett programs.
  - VLACS's computer science course list, which loads only inside its portal.
  - A high-school internship at the Chocorua Lake Conservancy or Tin Mountain.
  - Results for Tamworth students at either high school.
  - Any measure of how students of color experience Kennett.
