# Tamworth Community, Families, Child Care, School Alternatives and Adult Learning

Part of the Tamworth research (town/Tamworth). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the community side of the Tamworth residents' questions: the town's people, ages, race and ethnicity, income and children; housing and seasonal homes; licensed child care (including the center once called Bearcamp Valley School); recreation, library and summer programs; private schools, homeschool programs and EFA use; food, welfare and mental health help; adult diploma, HiSET and job training; town government; and police, fire and dispatch.

- **9 fact topics, 84 facts and 6 number tables.** On November 14, 2026, four facts that only restated American Community Survey (ACS) measures now in the site's Census data (median age, children and households with children, median household and per capita income, poverty, median home value and rent) were removed, and the ACS figures for housing units, seasonal homes, owners and renters were cut from the housing facts and table. Raw copies are in `raw/community/` (about 41 MB): Census JSON, NH DHHS child care data, the EFA marketplace and statistics feeds, NCES private school searches, OSRM routes, Tamworth's 2025 annual report (cited pages), the 2026 town warrant (OCR), the 2024 build-out analysis, and the pages of the preschool, the Bearcamp Center, Cook Memorial Library, Tamworth Recreation, Carroll County Adult Education, Northern Human Services and workforce programs.
- **Builds on** the Madison research (SAU 13 preschool and home education), the sibling Tamworth files (`brett-school.md`, `getting-to-school-and-the-day.md`, `high-school.md`) and the Jackson and Conway community files. Those facts are named in the question table, not repeated.

**Premises checked:**
- *"Bearcamp Valley School"* (P1.1, P20.1): out of date. The center renamed itself **Tamworth Preschool and Children's Center** in August 2025. It is a licensed child care center (18 months to 12 years, open 7 a.m. to 5 p.m.), not a school with grades.
- *"The Community School in South Tamworth ... high school curriculum"* (P10.1, P5.3, P20.1): out of date. In August 2020 its director said the school "as we know it is closing." The same nonprofit (same federal tax ID) is now **The Bearcamp Center for Sustainable Community**, which runs by-donation meals and K–8 camps. No school, high school or semester program was found, and it has no tuition or financial aid to report.
- *"Tamworth Learning Circles"* (P5.3): **not confirmed active.** It appears only as an EFA marketplace "school" entry with no description, grades or website. It is not in the state child care data, the federal private school survey or the library's homeschool directory.
- *"Carroll County Adult Education ... in Tamworth"* (P18.1): **correct.** Its office is at 680 White Mountain Highway in Tamworth (Chocorua). Some classes meet there; others meet in Conway, in Ossipee or on Zoom.
- *"A town that is over 90% white"* (P8.3): **correct.** The 2020 Census counts 93.7% of residents as non-Hispanic White alone (derived), against 87.2% statewide.
- *"Without requiring parents to drive 45 minutes"* (P19.2): the community mental health center in Conway is about **25 minutes** from Tamworth Village by a derived route in normal conditions. The North Conway and Wolfeboro hospitals are 40–43 minutes away.
- *"Youth sports leagues (soccer, basketball, skiing) ... through the Tamworth Recreation Department"* (P12.3): Tamworth Recreation runs soccer and basketball. Downhill skiing is run through the Brett School's Winter Program, not the Rec Department. The Rec Department offers cross-country skiing at its field.

**Main gaps:**
- How long child care waitlists are: no provider posts one. (NH DHHS's Child Care Search, now in the site's data, gives infant and toddler places and wait-list status.)
- Tuition at Tamworth Preschool and Children's Center, and Tamworth Recreation's camp and sports fees: the fees sit in a script-only registration system.
- Results of the March 2026 town meeting votes on agency funding.
- A count of short-term rentals.
- The Conway NH Works office details (nhes.nh.gov refused access).
- Whether Tamworth Learning Circles operates.

**Proposed shared topics:** two themes had no shared slug, so this file uses two new ones:
- `adult-education-and-workforce-training` (local, after_high_school): "Adult diploma, high school equivalency (HiSET) and English classes near a town, and job-training programs and funds (WIOA, community college workforce courses, apprenticeships) open to its residents."
- `town-government-and-civic-spaces` (local, governance_money): "How a town is governed: its select board, town meeting and town offices, and town funding votes for local agencies and programs."

This file also supports Conway's proposed `town-health-and-therapy-providers`. Mental health facts sit under `town-family-help` here, as in Conway.

**Answered wholly by earlier files:** 1 question (P5.1).
