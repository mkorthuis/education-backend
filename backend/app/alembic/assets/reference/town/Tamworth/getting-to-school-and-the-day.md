# Getting to School, the School Day and Family Support in Tamworth

Part of the Tamworth research (town/Tamworth). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Tamworth students get to the Kenneth A. Brett School (K–8) and to Kennett High School in Conway, and the day-to-day supports around school. It covers:
- who runs Tamworth's buses, the 2026-27 routes and derived ride times, and the Moultonborough Academy bus;
- the Kennett late bus and rides home after activities;
- storm closings, two-hour delays and remote days;
- Brett's school hours, calendar and after-school program;
- school meals, summer meals and weekend food bags;
- fees for damaged devices and field trips;
- moving out of Tamworth mid-year, residency and records;
- support for students without stable housing, and where Brett refers families.

It builds on the Jackson and Conway files and `town/sau9/`, which already cover Kennett's bell schedule, late-bus pass rules, co-curricular eligibility, SAU 9 storm procedures and Conway's remote-day policy. Those are named in the question table, not repeated.

### Premises checked

- **P1.3, care "until 5:00 PM" at Brett.** No school-run care to 5:00 p.m. was found for 2026-27. The handbook's after-school program is seasonal enrichment two to four days a week. In 2025-26 Brett had no before- or after-school programs. In August 2024 the board was told the Bearcamp Valley Children's Center offered care from 7 a.m. and until 5 p.m. Its current status was not found.
- **P4.2, "year-round breakfast, lunch and weekend food backpack support."**
  - During the school year, Brett serves breakfast and lunch free to every student: the board pays for all meals in 2026-27.
  - Weekend food bags are sent home on Fridays.
  - Brett does not run summer meals. In summer 2026 the NH Food Bank delivered meal kits to Tamworth homes.
- **P4.3, the right to finish the year after a move.** RSA 193:12 III defines "legal resident" and gives no right to finish the year. A student who moves out may attend only with the district's consent (RSA 193:12 I). Under Tamworth policy JFAB, that means school board approval and tuition. The exception is McKinney-Vento: a homeless student keeps the school of origin, and one who becomes permanently housed may finish that school year.
- **P9.3 and P17.3, transportation for Tamworth teens.** Tamworth students ride Tamworth's own First Student buses to Kennett, not Conway buses. A Tamworth policy provides a 5:00 p.m. Kennett late bus, but Kennett's website has late-bus sign-up forms only for Freedom and Madison.
- **P12.2, "remote-learning days."** SAU 13's policies allow remote days in bad weather. No remote day was found in Tamworth's minutes. The handbook says missed full days are made up, and the calendar sets make-up days for June 22–25, 2027.
- **P17.3, long rides from South Tamworth.** The earliest Kennett pickup on Route 25 (Bear Camp Highway) is 6:00 a.m., 80 minutes before the 7:20 drop-off. The sheets name roads, not villages.

1. The Tamworth School Board has adopted policy JFABD, shared with Freedom and Madison, on homeless children and youth. Raw copy: raw/daily/policies/tam-policy-JFABD.txt

## school-residency-rules: Legal Residence for School and Moving Between Districts

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Which district is legally responsible for a student under New Hampshire law: domicile, guardians, divided homes and parenting plans, moves between districts, attending a district where a student doesn't live, and who decides disputes

1. New Hampshire law says no one may attend school in a district where the pupil is not a legal resident without the consent of that district or its school board, except as the residency law, RSA 193:3 (change of school assignment) or RSA 193:28 (children in placements) allows.
   - citation: RSA 193:12, I
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-12.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-13
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no person shall attend school, or send a pupil to the school, in any district of which the pupil is not a legal resident, without the consent of the district or of the school board except as otherwise provided in this section, RSA 193:3, or RSA 193:28." Raw copy: raw/daily/moves/rsa-193-12.txt
2. Under New Hampshire law, a minor's legal residence for school is where the parents live. There are exceptions for parents who live apart, court custody orders, guardianships and court placements.
   - citation: RSA 193:12, II(a)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-12.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-13
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "In the case of a minor, legal residence is where his or her parents reside, except that: (1) If the parents live apart and are not divorced, legal residence is the residence of the parent with whom the child resides." The guardianship and relative-placement rules are in library topic special-ed-who-is-parent (fact 6) and the foster care topic. Raw copy: raw/daily/moves/rsa-193-12.txt
3. New Hampshire law defines a "legal resident" of a school district as someone domiciled there who intends to keep a principal home there. A person who moves to another town intending to stay indefinitely loses residence in the old town, even if they plan to return someday. A person may have only one legal residence at a time.
   - citation: RSA 193:12, III
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-12.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-13
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "If a person removes to another town with the intention of remaining there indefinitely, that person shall be considered to have lost residence in the town in which the person originally resided even though the person intends to return at some future time. A person may have only one legal residence at a given time." RSA 193:12 III does not give a student the right to finish the school year in the old district. The law also does not require a district to transport a student beyond its own limits (RSA 193:12, VII; library topic school-transportation-rules 2). Homeless students are covered by McKinney-Vento (district-student-support 1–4). Raw copy: raw/daily/moves/rsa-193-12.txt
