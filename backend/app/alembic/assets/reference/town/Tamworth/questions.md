# Tamworth Residents' Questions

The questions the Tamworth research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Tamworth is in SAU 13 with Freedom and Madison. The Tamworth School District runs the Kenneth A. Brett School (K–8). High school students attend Kennett High School in Conway (SAU 9), and since 2024 a contract lets up to 6 Tamworth students a year attend Moultonborough Academy. Several questions contain premises (enrollments, ratios, rates, programs) that the research should check rather than assume.
