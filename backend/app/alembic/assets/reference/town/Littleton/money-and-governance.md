# Littleton: Money, Buildings, Buses and the School Board

Part of the Littleton research (town/Littleton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 72 facts and 4 number tables on the Littleton School District's (SAU 84) money and governance.
- **Taxes and state aid:** the 2026 tax rate ($15.52; local schools $9.22), the school tax against enrollment since 2014, the 2025 revaluation, fund balance used to cut taxes (FY2024 to FY2026), the FY2027 adequacy aid shortfall and the hold-harmless step-down, Littleton's adoption of the HB 1300 tax cap and the district's cap estimate, and a derived comparison of property value and school tax per student with Franconia, Portsmouth, Bethlehem and nearby towns.
- **Budget and cost:** the FY2027 budget and vote, cost drivers (health insurance up 44.7% from FY2024 to FY2027, derived), the reduction list and the positions cut for 2026-27, ten years of budget and default-budget votes, the FY2028 proposal, the 2019 Cost of Education Study, the teachers' contract and classroom supply lines.
- **Open enrollment and tuition:** the March 2026 open enrollment vote (10 students in, 0% out) and the reasons given, tuition Littleton charges and pays, including St. Johnsbury Academy.
- **Buildings:** the Lakeway project from 2017 to 2026 (three failed votes, the state building aid list, the $43 million 2026 price), Lakeway's structural and roof findings and monitoring, the end of the district's existing debt in FY2027, capital reserves and the 2026 repairs (gym floor, stage lighting, boilers).
- **Buses and storm days:** the 2026-29 Butler's contract, the two 2026-27 routes with derived ride times, the route policy, the two 2025-26 snow days and the remote-day and closing policies.
- **Sharing and staff:** the part-time superintendent, regional course-sharing talks, town-school purchasing, class size policy and stated sizes, staffing cuts, the teacher residency program and what the board says about retention; NESDEC enrollment projections; DonorsChoose totals.

**Main sources:** the district's Drive folders linked from www.sau84.org (2017–2026 election results, 2026 warrant, budget comparative, hearing and deliberative minutes and slides, board, facilities and joint town-school minutes from December 2024 to November 2026, the FY2028 budget slides, the NESDEC projection, the 2026-27 bus schedule, policies IIB, IC, IMBA, EBCD, EEAB and JF-R(1), the 2018 Lakeway building needs assessment, the September 2024 Lakeway presentation, the 2019 Cost of Education Study and the RSA 32:5-d state funding notice); the Town of Littleton's 2026 tax rate press release; NH DOE's building aid files (library raw); DonorsChoose; and the site's database.
