# Daisy Bronson, Student Support and Choices

Part of the Littleton research (town/Littleton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Daisy Bronson Middle School (grades 7–8, "Daisy Bronson Junior High School" in state and federal data), which shares Littleton High School's building at 159 Oak Hill Avenue, and the supports and choices around it: the move up from Lakeway, advanced work in grades 7 and 8, IEPs at Daisy Bronson, part-time access and re-entry for homeschooled and EFA students, local homeschool groups, the phone policy, sports teams and cooperative teams, Littleton Academy (the district's alternative program for grades 7–12), Above the Notch Community School (the district's adult education and evening diploma program), credit recovery, counseling and student assistance, and help for students who arrive mid-year or behind.

- **Counts:** 11 topics, 81 facts, 2 tables (`littleton_special_education_students_nov_2024`, `littleton_student_transfers_nov_2024`).
- **Main sources:** the 2026-27 Littleton High School / Daisy Bronson Middle School Parent & Student Handbook and its linked bell schedule; Littleton High's 2026-27 Career Pathways Guide & Program of Studies; the school counseling website (home, academics, social/emotional, clinical counseling and Student Assistance Program pages); Littleton School Board policies IHBG, IHBI, IHCA, IMBA, IMBD, JF, JF-R(1), JG, JICJ and JJIB; the district's Special Education Procedure Plan (revised August 2021); Littleton Academy's and Above the Notch's web pages; 26 sets of School Board, budget work session and co-curricular committee minutes from August 2024 to November 4, 2026; ArbiterLive's team list; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (2021-22 and 2023-24 rows, from the Concord research's extracts); and Granite State Home Educators' support group list.
- **Main gaps:** no wait times for counselors are published (P16.3); the NHIAA's list of cooperative teams is behind a member login, so a co-op with Profile or Lisbon could not be ruled out from the NHIAA side, only from the board minutes (P15.2); no grade 7–8 course catalog or Lakeway-to-Daisy Bronson transition plan is posted (P4.1, P4.2, P7.3); the administrative procedure IHBG-R, which sets the rules for homeschooled students' part-time participation, isn't posted (P8.1); when the middle school moved into the high school building, and where Littleton Academy meets in fall 2026, weren't found.
- **New data concepts:** a middle school's own bell schedule inside a shared building; the district's special education counts by school and program; student transfers in and out by school; credit recovery bands on a competency scale; an adult education program's fees, evening schedule and enrollment; a district's part-time enrollment definition and capacity-limit policy; Algebra I in grades 7–8 and gifted programs from the federal civil rights data.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files or the site's data:** 0. P8.3 leans most on the library (EFA uses), but the local homeschool groups and the district's EFA provider status are new.
- **New topics proposed:** none.

| P13.3 | The rule on phones, and whether principals back teachers | **Rule confirmed; backing can't be measured.** Policy JICJ (August 13, 2025) bans phones bell to bell; Daisy Bronson students secure phones in homeroom, and Littleton High uses lock boxes (district-technology-and-phones 1–3). Gallen center, serves people 16 and older across Coos and upper Grafton counties, gives HiSET tests, and is a credit recovery option for Littleton High students (adult-education-and-workforce-training 1–9). For a teen who struggles in big classes during the day, the district's alternative is Littleton Academy, grades 7–12 (school-programs-after-grade-6 8–11). |
| P16.2 | Making up credits "over the summer or online without dropping out" | **Yes, several routes.** Credit recovery with the teacher, an after-school "R block," a VLACS course or a retake; referral to Above the Notch's evening program; approved online courses, including in summer; summer activities for credit; alternative learning plans; Littleton Academy part or full time (school-programs-after-grade-6 2–8). |
| P16.3 | A wait to see a counselor | **No wait times are published.** Students book their assigned counselor online, and emergencies are seen right away (district-student-support 2). There is one counselor for grades 9–12 and one for grades 6–8, a social worker and a Student Assistance counselor (facts 1, 3–4); a transition counselor position was cut for 2026-27 (fact 5). |
