# Littleton Residents' Questions

The questions the Littleton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Littleton is SAU 84. The Littleton School District runs Mildred C. Lakeway School (kindergarten to grade 6), Daisy Bronson Junior High (grades 7–8) and Littleton High School (grades 9–12), which houses the Hugh J. Gallen Career & Technical Center. Several questions contain premises (no before-school care at Lakeway, an over-hour bus ride, many snow and remote days, Above the Notch Community School, sports co-ops with Profile or Lisbon, about 170 high school students) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Rachel, 34 — ER Nurse at Littleton Regional Healthcare (2nd grader at Lakeway Elementary)

- **P1.1** My shifts start at 7:00 AM—is there any before-school care at Lakeway, or am I stuck scrambling for a neighbor to put my kid on the bus?
- **P1.2** When the Parks & Rec after-school program closes at 5:00 PM or cancels on snow days, what do hospital and shift workers do for coverage?
- **P1.3** My son is a little behind on reading—will the school catch it early and get him extra help without me having to beg for testing?
