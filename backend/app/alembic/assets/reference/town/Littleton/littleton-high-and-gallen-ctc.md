# Littleton High School and the Hugh J. Gallen Career & Technical Center

Part of the Littleton research (town/Littleton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Littleton High School (LHS) and the Hugh J. Gallen Career & Technical Center (CTC) that it houses: the CTC's programs, who attends, seats, grades, embedded academic credit, credentials, college credit and internships; the White Mountains Regional center's agriculture and digital media courses open to LHS students; LHS's AP, honors and Early College (formerly Running Start) courses, outside courses, graduation requirements, the required Personal Finance course and the honors diploma; arts, music and theater and the 2026-27 budget reductions; the bus schedule (no late bus); counseling, FAFSA help and local scholarships; NEASC accreditation and the school profile colleges see; WMCC's Littleton Academic Center; and what trades pay in northern New Hampshire.

- **12 fact topics, 74 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 25 MB). Scripts are in the session scratchpad folder `littleton-high/`.
- **Places:** `district Littleton` (LHS, the Gallen CTC, SAU 84's board and policies; SAU 84 serves only Littleton), `district White Mountains Regional` (course descriptions for its Arthur T. Paradice center, as printed in LHS's Program of Studies), `town Littleton` (WMCC's Littleton center, area wages, the Chamber's scholarship). No state rules were written; the ones needed already exist and are cited by file, topic and fact number.
- **Sources:** SAU 84's Edlio sites (lhs, ctc, www), the LHS and CTC 2026-27 Programs of Studies (Google Docs), the 2026-27 School Profile, school board minutes July 2025 to November 2026 and board policies (Google Drive), the school counseling Google Site, WMCC, NEASC's directory file, the NH Jump$tart survey, the BLS API and the Littleton Area Chamber. Every page opened without a bot check.
- **Source conflicts:** (1) LHS's Program of Studies says the diploma requires filing the FAFSA or a waiver, but board policy IKF (adopted August 13, 2025) and RSA 193:26-a require only that seniors get FAFSA information (district-student-support 7). (2) The three 2026-27 graduation-requirement sources differ on math (4 or 5 credits) and on whether the Class of 2030 needs 2 CTE credits (school-programs-after-grade-6 9). (3) The 2026-27 School Profile lists one AP course; the Program of Studies also describes an AP American Literature course the profile doesn't list (school-programs-after-grade-6 2).
- **Main gaps:** no count of CTC completers hired by local employers, no LHS AP exam or Early College enrollment counts, the region 03 CTE agreement (how seats are shared among sending schools), the 2027 Littleton Scholarship packet itself (posted February 18, 2027) and town-held scholarship trust funds.
- **Answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 14 questions; state rules on dual enrollment, CTE access, FAFSA information and graduation credits are cited from the library and earlier towns.

### Premises checked

| Question | Premise | What the sources show |
|---|---|---|
| P2.2 | High schoolers are all pushed toward four-year colleges | Not supported. The 2026-27 School Profile expects 44% of the Class of 2027 to attend a four-year college and 23% a two-year college; in the site's data, 14 of the Class of 2025's 40 graduates reported a four-year college. Every LHS diploma requires 2 CTE credits (through the Class of 2029) and a Personal Finance course. |
| P3.1 | Kids from other towns take up the building trades or auto spots | Partly. In November 2026 the CTC had 144 students, 84 from outside Littleton and 60 from Littleton. The 2026-27 capacity report lists building trades at 22 students in rooms rated for 18, and automotive at 15 of 18. How the regional agreement splits seats among towns was not found. |
| P3.2 | A trade program may cost math and English credits needed to graduate on time | Not supported as a rule. Automotive and building construction carry "potential embedded" math credit; business, health science and careers in education carry embedded English; LHS requires English and math every year and counts CTE toward the 2-credit CTE requirement. Whether embedded credit is granted is decided course by course; the documents don't say how. |
| P10.1 | Running Start could have saved money | Correct in principle. Running Start is now "Early College." LHS lists 14 Early College courses (46 college credits, derived); CCSNH covers two a year for grades 10–12, and others are $150, against $238 a credit ($714 for a 3-credit course, derived) at a community college. |
| P10.2 | High schoolers get no real class on budgeting | Out of date. The NH Jump$tart survey (as of June 30, 2024) listed Littleton as offering personal finance only as an elective; LHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies makes Personal Finance (1 credit, grades 10–12) a required course. Its description names spending, saving, credit and debt, income, investing and insurance; taxes and leases aren't named. |
| P12.3 | LHS offers little in forestry, agriculture or heavy equipment | Largely correct for LHS and the Gallen CTC (small engines and diesel introductions only), but LHS students may take Diversified Agriculture at White Mountains Regional's center (greenhouse, livestock and poultry farm, sugarhouse, sawmill), and WMCC's Littleton center has a Diesel Heavy Equipment Technology program. No forestry program was found. |
| P18.1 | Band, chorus and theater are the first cuts when budgets are tight | Not supported for 2026-27. None of the 16 possible reductions or the 7 positions cut named music, art or drama; a retiring music teacher was replaced, drama director posts were filled, and new stage lighting was installed. The board has said no future cuts have been chosen. |
| P18.2 | There may be a late bus for drama or robotics | Not found. The 2026-27 bus schedule lists two buses, with the last afternoon run leaving LHS at 2:42 p.m.; no late or activity run is listed. |
| P20.1 | About 170 students at LHS; few AP classes | Close: 168 in 2024-25 (site data) and 157 in 2026-27 (district capacity report). Only one AP course (English Literature) is in the 2026-27 School Profile; LHS leans on Early College (8 EC courses in the profile, 14 in the Program of Studies list) and online courses. |
