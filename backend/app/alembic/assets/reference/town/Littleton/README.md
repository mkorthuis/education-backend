# Littleton: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Littleton, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the tenth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Littleton is SAU 84, a one-district SAU:
- **Mildred C. Lakeway School** serves kindergarten to grade 6, with a part-day preschool.
- **Daisy Bronson Junior High (7–8) and Littleton High (9–12)** share a building.
- **Career programs:** the Hugh J. Gallen Career & Technical Center at Littleton High, which also serves neighboring districts.

The district votes by official ballot. Berlin's research (`../Berlin/`) has a North Country career center table.
