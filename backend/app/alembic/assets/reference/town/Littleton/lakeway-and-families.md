# Lakeway and Young Families

Part of the Littleton research (town/Littleton). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Mildred C. Lakeway Elementary School (kindergarten to grade 6) in the Littleton School District (SAU 84) and the questions of families with young children: the school day and the lack of before-school care, the Parks & Recreation After School Program and what happens after 5:00 p.m. and on snow days, the Boys & Girls Club in Lisbon, reading screening and Title I help, homework help, school meals and how unpaid or free meals are handled, bus eligibility and walking along Route 302, speech therapists and paraprofessionals, the IEP process, crossing guards, counseling, summer camps, the district's preschool, child care near Lakeway, full-day kindergarten and the entry cutoff, and English learner services and translation.

15 topics, 83 facts and 1 number table (`littleton_child_care_drive_from_lakeway`). Four topics add Littleton to library topics that had no or partial Littleton entries (`district-preschool`, `kindergarten-day-length`, `kindergarten-registration`, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff`).

**Sources.** Every sau84.org and les.sau84.org page answered automated requests normally. Most of what the district says came from (a) Lakeway's 2026-27 Student/Parent Handbook (approved by the board August 17, 2026), (b) board policies EEA, EEAB, EEAE-R, EFAA, IHBA, IKB and KB from the district's policy folders, (c) school board minutes from September 2025 to November 2026 and the November 4 and 16, 2026 FY28 budget presentations, which include a Lakeway slide (enrollment by grade, IEPs, 504 plans, free and reduced lunch, homelessness, staffing) and a special education staffing slide, (d) Lakeway's September and October 2026 principal's reports, the October 2026 student services report, the 2026-27 bus schedule and the November 19, 2026 job openings list, and (e) the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24 file for the district's preschool and kindergarten answers. Also used: Littleton Parks & Recreation's MyRec pages and handbooks, the Boys & Girls Clubs of Central and Northern NH's North Country pages, Shining Lights Learning Center, Tri-County CAP Head Start, White Mountain Science Inc., the site's own child care data (drive times derived with OSRM), OpenStreetMap (which streets carry US 302) and the U.S. Naval Observatory (January sunrise).

**Main gaps:** whether children miss speech or other IEP sessions because of vacancies (no source reports it); the preschool's hours, tuition and how to enroll in 2026-27 (not posted); how long local child care waitlists are (no program posts it); whether Alma messages, report cards and permission slips are offered in Spanish (only the meal application, the Title I compact and the Title I plan are posted in Spanish); which streets the crossing guards cover beyond "the traffic signal" and the Pine Street post; and the 2025-26 Title I plan and engagement documents, which need a Google sign-in.

Privacy note for reviewers: staff are named in no fact or note (names in quoted minutes are replaced with a role in brackets); contacts are given as office phone numbers. The bus schedule lists stops by road or place only (no house numbers), and no home address was sent to any service. The one licensed family (home) child care program in Littleton was left out of the drive-time table.
