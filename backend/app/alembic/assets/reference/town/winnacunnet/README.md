# Winnacunnet High School: Shared Research

Facts about Winnacunnet High School, the high school of the Winnacunnet Cooperative School District (SAU 21) in Hampton. Its member towns are Hampton, Hampton Falls, North Hampton and Seabrook, and South Hampton sends students on tuition. Written by the Hampton research on November 16, 2026 (`../Hampton/`), for any member town to reuse. Each fact is placed in `district Winnacunnet Cooperative`, or `sau 21` when it applies to every SAU 21 district.
