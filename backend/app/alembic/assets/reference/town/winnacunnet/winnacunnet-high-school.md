# Winnacunnet High School (Shared Research)

Shared research on Winnacunnet High School (Winnacunnet Cooperative School District, SAU 21), gathered for the Hampton research (town/Hampton) for every member town: Hampton, Hampton Falls, North Hampton and Seabrook, and South Hampton, which sends students there on tuition. Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Winnacunnet High School (WHS, grades 9–12, in Hampton): AP, honors and dual enrollment; the honors math and science tracks; the move into ninth grade; culinary, business and hospitality-related courses; Winnacunnet's side of the Seacoast School of Technology (SST) in Exeter (how students apply, get there, earn WHS credit, and what WHS pays); the counseling office, college counseling and mental health staff; how families are reached and translation; sports eligibility, registration and season dates; fees; the phone policy; Chromebooks and technology; the Hawley Library and its 2026 redesign article; music and theater; JROTC, extended learning and internships; the evening program; and published outcomes.

- **Places:** 102 facts use `district Winnacunnet Cooperative`; 2 use `sau 21` (ParentSquare and the ESOL fact sheet, which SAU 21 posts for all its districts); 1 has no place (NHIAA season dates).
