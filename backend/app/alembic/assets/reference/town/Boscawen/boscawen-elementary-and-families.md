# Boscawen Elementary School and Young Families in Boscawen

Part of the Boscawen research (town/Boscawen). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Boscawen Elementary School (BES, preschool to grade 5) in the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46) and Boscawen's young families: the 9:00 school day and the YMCA's before- and after-school care in the building, full-day kindergarten, the four BES bus routes and where they run, the district preschool housed at BES, BES's October 1, 2026 enrollment and 2026-27 staff (class sizes worked out per grade, aides, the FACT program and the "What I Need" room), whom to contact about special education and 504 plans, the Title I schoolwide program and reading help, the lead-in-water letter and the state's test results, meals and breakfast timing, storm and dismissal notices, absences and remote days, recess and specials, infant and toddler care and Head Start near Boscawen, and summer options (the town's Summer Parks Program, the library, summer meal sites and the district's restored summer reading line).

**Counts:** 14 topics, 49 facts, 6 number tables (one copied: the October 1, 2026 enrollment; five derived: students per classroom teacher, staff by role, bus ride times, lead results by round, and infant care nearest BES).

**Main sources:** BES's website (home, welcome, Title One, quick links, nurse pages) and the documents it links: the 2026-2027 Student/Family Handbook (with the 2026-27 staff directory), the December 2022 water testing letter, the Title I Schoolwide Plan (2017-18 plan with annual updates through July 12, 2023), the dyslexia screening page, the April 2026 NH SAS letter and the four 2026-27 BES route sheets; district policies IHBAB, IHBAA, IKB, JH and JLCF; the October 5, 2026 board packet (enrollment) and the proposed 2026-27 line-by-line budget (both as extracted by the Loudon research); the NHDES lead-in-schools GIS layer behind the state's 2025 dashboard; the Granite YMCA's school-age care page; NCES's school directory; USDA's 2026 summer meal site layer; the Town of Boscawen's Parks & Recreation page and the Boscawen Public Library's site; and the site's own data (class size, child care, Head Start list).

**Main gaps:** the preschool's tuition, eligibility and openings for children without disabilities; recess minutes and a BES daily schedule; any typical (rather than maximum) time from a speech or reading referral to services; how often BES uses the W.I.N. room or what it is for; whether BES is still on NH DOE's 2026-27 Title I list (ED Data Express and NH DOE were not reached); the Summer Parks Program's dates and fees; a Boscawen library summer reading program; NHDES's own results page (HTTP 403). Web search hit the session's limit partway through, so the preschool and Route 3 questions could not be searched.

## district-family-communication: Storm Notices and Dismissal Changes at Boscawen Elementary
