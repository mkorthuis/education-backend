# Merrimack Valley High School and the CRTC From Boscawen

Part of the Boscawen research (town/Boscawen). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Merrimack Valley High School (MVHS) in Penacook and the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC) as Boscawen families see them. Most of MVHS and the CRTC was researched the same day for Loudon (`town/Loudon/middle-and-high-school.md`, `town/Loudon/support-and-choices.md`) and earlier for Concord (`town/Concord/high-school-and-pathways.md`); this file cites those facts in the question table and adds what they lacked: getting to the CRTC, fitting a CRTC program into a senior year, the CRTC's auto, welding, culinary and computer engineering pages, AP and honors offerings and course cancellations, New England College's partnership with MVHS, how to sign up for Early College, MVHS's personal finance, business, hands-on, computer science and civics courses, Boscawen-only and trade-school scholarships, graduation and FAFSA help, counselor numbers, evening welding training near Concord, Bishop Brady's published results, and **derived** comparisons of Merrimack Valley's test results with the state and of MVHS with Concord High.

- **10 fact topics, 39 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 5 MB): the MVHS 2026-27 handbook, 2025-26 Program of Studies and 2026-27 profile, policy IKF, the MVHS local scholarship page and three Boscawen award applications, four CRTC program pages, New England College's partnership page, CCSNH's Early College pages, Granite Edvance's help page, Bishop Brady's 2026-27 profile, Manchester Community College's welding page, NHTI's workforce course list, the district's transportation page and bus folder listing, and the site database queries.
- **Places:** 27 facts use `district Merrimack Valley`, 3 use `district Concord` (the CRTC's own pages), 8 use `town Boscawen` (Boscawen-only scholarships, Bishop Brady as a nearby option, adult training near Boscawen) and 1 has no place (Granite Edvance's statewide help, added to the library topic `granite-edvance`).
- **New data concepts:** Boscawen-only scholarships and their terms; NEC's tuition-free courses for MVHS seniors and $12,000 tuition for MVHS graduates; the CCSNH Early College sign-up steps; a **derived** count of seven counseling staff behind the site's student-to-counselor ratio; and **derived** comparisons of Merrimack Valley's math and science proficiency with the state, and of MVHS with Concord High, with grade 11 participation shown.
