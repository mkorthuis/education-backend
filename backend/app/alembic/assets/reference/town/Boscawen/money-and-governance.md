# Boscawen: Money, Taxes and Governance in the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46)

Part of the Boscawen research (town/Boscawen). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 37 facts and 6 number tables on how Boscawen pays for and has a say in the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46). The district-wide budget, cost split, state aid, buses, capital plan and board rules are already in `town/Loudon/money-and-governance.md`; this file cites those facts and adds Boscawen's side.
- **Boscawen's school tax:** the town's 2026 rate ($21.80: $12.33 local school, $1.09 state education, $6.37 town, $2.01 county), the town's own tax-rate calculations for 2023–2025 (its share of the cooperative's appropriation rose $1,245,009 while its education grant fell), Boscawen's growing share of the five towns' students (19.1% in 2014-15 to 22.2% in 2025-26, **derived** from site data), the monthly payments to the district, and the gap between the district's March estimate ($14.50) and the actual 2026 school rate ($13.42).
- **The surplus and the tax cap:** why the board kept $500,000 of the $2.3 million balance, the town's separate fund balance, the 2027-28 budget's first draft (−0.49%, or +2.23% with the SAU), and what the Question 2 cap does and doesn't settle for Boscawen.
- **Consolidation:** the October 2026 long-range planning talk and the November 2, 2026 agenda item "Pathways to Potential School Consolidation"; no document names Boscawen Elementary for closing. What the 1965 Articles say about the buildings, and what happened to Boscawen's last retired school.
- **The building:** Boscawen Elementary (1996, 55,000 square feet, 91 acres) and its $1,302,400 ten-year capital plan, with no bond or new building in any plan.
- **Voice and budget documents:** Boscawen's 2 of 11 board seats against its 22.15% student share, recent Boscawen elections and the 2025 vacancy, the SAU board and its $1,748,615 line-item budget, technology spending, where the budget documents are, and the January 2027 budget session at Boscawen Elementary.
- **Relief for seniors and veterans:** Boscawen's elderly exemption ($32,000 to $64,000 off assessed value, income limits $30,000/$40,000), the 9 owners who get it, and veterans' credits.
- **Comparison:** a derived table of 2026 test results, growth, cost per pupil and low-income share for Merrimack Valley, the districts of the towns that border Boscawen, the state and Boscawen Elementary.

**Main sources:** the Town of Boscawen (tax rate history, assessing pages, elderly exemption package, exemptions list of November 5, 2026, 2023–2025 town reports, official tally of November 3, 2026); Merrimack Valley's Articles of Agreement, 2025 and 2026 annual reports, capital plan, line-by-line budget, budget overview, board minutes and hearing minutes, the October 5 and November 2, 2026 board packets, the 2021 Salisbury–Webster study and the SAU 46 board packet of December 3, 2025; the site's database. The Concord Monitor and a 2003 trade-magazine article are cited only where noted.
