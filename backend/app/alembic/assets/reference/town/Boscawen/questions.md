# Boscawen Residents' Questions

The questions the Boscawen research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Boscawen's students attend the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46), shared with Loudon, Penacook (Concord), Salisbury and Webster. Boscawen's elementary children attend Boscawen Elementary School; grades 6–8 attend Merrimack Valley Middle School and grades 9–12 Merrimack Valley High School, both in Penacook, and students may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. The district is researched in `../Loudon/`; the files here add Boscawen's side. Several questions contain premises (elementary consolidation rumors, a $2.3 million surplus, packed and late middle school buses, a water testing letter, 25 children with one teacher) that the research should check rather than assume.
