# Pittsfield: Money, Taxes, the High School's Future and Governance

Part of the Pittsfield research (town/Pittsfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers the Pittsfield School District's (SAU 51) budget and its trend, the FY2024 overspending and FY2025 deficit and how the books are being rebuilt and audited, the 2026 votes, state aid and the school tax, the 2025 revaluation, the school tax cap that Pittsfield voters approved in November 2026, the contract under which Concord's SAU 8 has provided Pittsfield's superintendent and business office since July 1, 2026, public comment rules and the deliberative session, the history of proposals to tuition out Pittsfield's high school students (2020 petition, 2022 study, 2026 Articles 9 and 10 and the High School Options Study Committee), the buildings, capital needs and the absence of bonds, open enrollment tuition bills, and sharing services with neighbors.

**Counts:** 7 topics, 73 facts, 2 tables (`pittsfield_operating_budget_votes`, `pittsfield_school_ballot_votes_2026`).

**Main sources:** SAU 51's Budget Information page (2026 warrant, MS-27, default budget, deficit summaries, deliberative session minutes and presentation, budget committee summary), school board, High School Options Study Committee and Facilities Committee minutes (2025–2026), Policy BEDH, the school district annual reports (2020-21, 2021-22, 2024-25, 2025-26), the Town of Pittsfield's election results (2019–2026) and 2025 annual town report, the Concord–Pittsfield SAU services agreement, and the library's DRA 2026 tax rate list.

**Main gaps:** the High School Options Study Committee's report (due to the board December 3, 2026, not yet posted); the 2022 Team Design Harriman feasibility study itself; the audited FY2024 and FY2025 financial statements; what happened to the $248,054 Prospect Mountain tuition bill after voters rejected it; Pittsfield's 2026 tax rate (not set by DRA as of October 30, 2026); a date for the 2027 deliberative session; and the 2026 March, April and May board minutes (links on sau51.org return 404).

1. Pittsfield's interim superintendent told the school board on March 20, 2025 that the 2023-24 operating budget had been overspent by almost $616,000, that the board first learned of it on February 13, 2025, and that the district had for over 10 years practiced "looping," paying one year's bills with the next year's money; nearly $1.1 million of 2024-25 revenue had paid 2023-24 costs, including $450,000 in summer payroll and vendor payments and $579,000 in NH Retirement System payments.
