# Pittsfield Residents' Questions

The questions the Pittsfield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Pittsfield is SAU 51. The Pittsfield School District runs Pittsfield Elementary School (preschool to grade 5) and Pittsfield Middle High School (grades 6–12) on Oneida Street. Several questions contain premises (talk of closing the high school and tuitioning students out, a superintendent shared with Concord, a past budget deficit, a middle school flagged for low scores, about 60 chronically absent students) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Jenna, 34 – Single mom of a 2nd grader at Pittsfield Elementary, works retail in Concord

- **P1.1** Why does the elementary school let out at 12:35 every Wednesday, and is there any affordable after-school program so I don't have to keep leaving work early?
- **P1.2** My daughter's class feels like it's always shuffling teachers or aides around—are they actually able to fill those open spots?
- **P1.3** Is the school lunch system separate from everything else? I can never tell what I owe on my kid's account.

## P2. Dave, 68 – Retired machinist, lived on Catamount Road for 40 years, no kids in school

- **P2.1** With state aid dropping and health insurance going up, how much more is my property tax bill going to jump next year?
- **P2.2** They just passed that new tax cap law—will Pittsfield actually put it on the ballot so we can keep school spending in check?
- **P2.3** Didn't the district end up with a budget deficit a couple years back? How do we know the books are actually fixed now?

## P3. Ashley, 29 – Stay-at-home mom with a 4-year-old and a toddler

- **P3.1** Does Pittsfield Elementary have a public preschool program, or do we have to pay for private daycare until kindergarten?
- **P3.2** I heard kindergarten numbers are way down this year—does that mean my son is going to be in a tiny class or combined with another grade?
- **P3.3** How safe is drop-off and pick-up on Bow Street? It seems like there's never a good crosswalk when the cars are backed up.

## P4. Marcus, 42 – Commuter who moved from Manchester for a cheaper house, dad of an 8th grader at PMHS

- **P4.1** I keep hearing the middle school got flagged by the state for low test scores—what are they actually doing to help kids catch up?
- **P4.2** Are they really thinking about closing the high school and busing our kids to another town? I need to know before next fall.
- **P4.3** My son wants to take advanced math and science, so can he still get those classes here or would he be better off somewhere else?

## P5. Tammy, 39 – Mom of a 4th grader with an IEP, works part-time at a local diner

- **P5.1** If the elementary school is short on paras and reading specialists, is my son actually getting all the support hours written in his IEP?
- **P5.2** My kid missed speech and reading help a bunch of times this year—does the school have to make those sessions up?
- **P5.3** Who do I even talk to when things fall through the cracks? Sometimes it feels like nobody calls you back.

## P6. Greg, 51 – Self-employed carpenter, dad of a junior at PMHS

- **P6.1** If my son wants to be an electrician instead of going to a four-year college, can he get into a good trades or tech program from Pittsfield?
- **P6.2** If the town decides to send high schoolers to another district, will my kid get a spot in their vocational classes or get pushed to the back of the line?
- **P6.3** Are the school's laptops and shop equipment actually up to date, or are the kids still using broken-down stuff?

## P7. Brenda, 61 – Grandmother raising her 10-year-old grandson

- **P7.1** I'm not great with computers—how am I supposed to check his grades and school messages if everything is online now?
- **P7.2** Does the school have someone who helps kids dealing with family stress or anxiety, or is that all on us?
- **P7.3** If we miss a few days because I have doctor appointments in Concord, are they really going to send the police to our house?

## P8. Tyler, 27 – PMHS alum and young homeowner, no kids yet

- **P8.1** If we shut down the high school and tuition kids out, what happens to the building on Oneida Street—do we still have to pay to heat and fix it?
- **P8.2** Town pride around here has always been tied to Pittsfield High sports and theater—wouldn't losing the high school kill the town's spirit?
- **P8.3** Are property values going to tank if young families realize Pittsfield doesn't have its own high school anymore?

## P9. Rachel, 36 – Homeschool mom of three (ages 6, 9, and 12)

- **P9.1** Can my kids still play middle school sports or join band at PMHS if we homeschool?
- **P9.2** If I use the state's Education Freedom Account money, does that hurt Pittsfield's school budget, or does the town still get by?
- **P9.3** If we decide to put our oldest back into public school for 9th grade, will they make it a hassle to place him in the right classes?

## P10. Kevin, 45 – Remote software worker who bought a lakefront place nearby, dad of two girls (grades 5 and 7)

- **P10.1** How does Pittsfield's academic quality actually compare to neighboring towns like Barnstead, Chichester, or Concord?
- **P10.2** Can we use open enrollment to send our girls to another public high school if we want, or does the town block it?
- **P10.3** Why did the superintendent share an office or contract with Concord—does that mean we're slowly merging with them?

## P11. Diane, 55 – Special education aide / local resident whose own kids already graduated

- **P11.2** Every time a budget gets voted down or goes to default, what actually gets cut first—teachers or supplies?
- **P11.3** Are the new door locks and security upgrades at the schools actually finished and working properly?

## P12. Carlos, 38 – Warehouse supervisor, dad of a sophomore who plays basketball and soccer

- **P12.1** I saw PMHS won a big sportsmanship award—are the sports teams actually staying together, or are we having to co-op with other towns just to field a team?
- **P12.2** If Pittsfield tuitions high schoolers out, will my son even make the varsity team at a bigger school?
- **P12.3** What time do late buses run after practice, or do parents have to figure out rides every single day?

## P13. Melissa, 48 – Mom of a senior applying to colleges

- **P13.1** Does PMHS offer enough AP, honors, or dual-enrollment college credits so my daughter isn't behind when she gets to UNH?
- **P13.2** Is the guidance office actually helping kids with FAFSA and scholarships, or is one counselor stretched across the whole school?
- **P13.3** What happens to local scholarships like the Foss Family one if the town stops having its own high school?

## P14. Herb, 73 – Fixed-income retiree and regular voter at Town Meeting

- **P14.1** Why are we spending so much per student when enrollment keeps dropping every year?
- **P14.2** Can someone explain in plain English if it's cheaper to keep our high school open or pay tuition to send the kids somewhere else?
- **P14.3** Why don't they hold the school deliberative session on a Saturday so more working folks and seniors can actually show up?

## P15. Sarah, 31 – Nurse working 12-hour shifts at Concord Hospital, mom of a kindergartner and a 3rd grader

- **P15.1** When school gets canceled or delayed for snow, how early do they actually send out the alert?
- **P15.2** My kids take the bus from the outskirts of town—how long are they sitting on that bus every morning and afternoon?
- **P15.3** Are the school nurses at both buildings full-time, or are they splitting time between schools?

## P16. Dan, 44 – Local convenience store owner, dad of a 6th grader who struggles with reading

- **P16.1** Now that 6th grade is over at the middle high school instead of the elementary school, are the younger kids separated from the 12th graders?
- **P16.2** If my son is falling behind in reading, can he get real tutoring during the school day, or do I have to pay for someone outside?
- **P16.3** Do kids still get bullied on the buses and in the hallways, and does the school actually do anything when you report it?

## P17. Hannah, 33 – First-time homebuyer, pregnant with her first child

- **P17.1** We're thinking about staying in Pittsfield long-term—will there even be a functioning elementary and high school here in five years?
- **P17.2** Are the school buildings in decent shape, or is the town about to get hit with a huge bond for a new roof or boiler?
- **P17.3** What's the teacher turnover like—do good teachers actually stick around here?

## P18. Pete, 50 – Volunteer youth coach and dad of a 9th grader

- **P18.1** I heard almost 60 kids at the middle high school are missing way too much school—what's going on, and is it hurting the kids who do show up?
- **P18.2** Are the rules on cell phones and behavior actually being enforced in class now?
- **P18.3** How can regular parents get their voices heard by the school board if the board isn't allowed to answer questions during public comment?

## P19. Claire, 40 – Mom of an 11th grader who wants to go into nursing or healthcare

- **P19.1** Does PMHS have decent science labs and health-career electives, or does she have to travel to another town for that?

- **P19.3** Are there enough Chromebooks to go around, or are kids still sharing or waiting on broken ones?

## P20. Ben, 24 – Young tradesperson renting an apartment downtown, no kids
