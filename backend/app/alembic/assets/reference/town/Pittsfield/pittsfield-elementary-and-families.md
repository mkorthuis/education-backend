Part of the Pittsfield research (town/Pittsfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Pittsfield Elementary School (PES, 34 Bow Street) from a family's side: the 2026-27 calendar's weekly Wednesday early releases and why they exist, school hours, the Boys & Girls Club before- and after-school program at PES and its fees and aid, staffing vacancies and the paraprofessional shortage, para pay and hours, special education services and missed IEP services, the federal rules on IEP services and compensatory services, Title I paraprofessional qualifications, the preschool's move to Head Start, kindergarten and school enrollment, the grade 6 move back to PES, drop-off and dismissal at PES and the crosswalk discussion, bus routes and ride times, storm closings and alerts, school nurses, school meals and meal accounts, how families get grades and messages, who to call, and reading help (Title I, Into Reading, the vacant reading specialist post).

**Counts:** 13 topics, 57 facts, 2 number tables (one derived from the 2025-26 bus sheet, one transcribed from the PES principal's September 2025 report). Many facts cite the board's draft minutes (the latest posted), which the loader check flags as "draft"; the minutes had not been approved when checked.

**Main sources:** sau51.org (SAU contacts, PES staff, health, student services and cafe pages, employment page, the 2026-27 district calendar, the bus route sheet), the PES Student and Family Handbook 2024-2025 (the newest PES handbook posted), the PMHS Student and Parent/Guardian Handbook 2026-2027 (for district-wide items: meal prices and charges, storm alerts, Alma), Pittsfield School Board minutes from March 2025 to November 5, 2026, the Pittsfield School District 2025-2026 Annual Report (staff and salary list, student services and principal reports), the Boys & Girls Club of Central and Northern NH's PES and financial assistance pages, and federal regulations (34 CFR 300.151, 300.323 and 200.58). The site's own data (enrollment by grade, licensed child care, class size) is pointed to, not restated.

## title-i-school-programs: Title I Schoolwide and Targeted Assistance Programs

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
