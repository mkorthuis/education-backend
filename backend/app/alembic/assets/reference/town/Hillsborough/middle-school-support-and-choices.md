## Summary

Covers Hillsboro-Deering Middle School (HDMS, grades 6–8) and the support and choices around it: special education staffing, reading intervention and who to call at SAU 34; the after-school day, clubs and the (missing) late bus; the move up from HDES and on to the high school, including homework and grading; homeschooled and EFA students' access and the home education paperwork; bullying reports; counseling, mental health and prevention staff at HDMS and HDHS; arts, music and drama; buses, start times and phones; and local private school and EFA options. Sources are the SAU 34, HDMS and HDHS websites, the 2026-27 HDMS and 2025-26 HDHS handbooks, Hillsboro-Deering board policies (several revised September–October 2026), board minutes and packets from January to November 2026 (most scanned, read as images), the FY27 budget book, HDMS and SAU newsletters, the 2026-27 bus route sheets (times only), SAU 34's job board and Hillsboro Christian School's site.
