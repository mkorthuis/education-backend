# Hillsboro-Deering Elementary and Young Families

Part of the Hillsborough research (town/Hillsborough). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hillsboro-Deering Elementary School (HDES, preschool to grade 5, 4 Hillcat Drive) from young families' side: the integrated preschool and its lottery, before- and after-school care (Kid Adventures), the 2026-27 bus routes and how long the rides are (Emerald Lake in detail), storm closings, kindergarten's day and birthday cutoff, what the 37% math figure is, math and reading programs and Title I help, enrichment and advanced learners, how the school reaches families and language help, school meals, help for grandparents raising children, and how many classroom teachers stayed from 2025-26 to 2026-27.

- **Main sources:** the HDES Student-Parent Handbook (August 2025); SAU 34 and HDES web pages (preschool, Kid Adventures, transportation, closings, food service, registration, Title I); the 2026-27 bus route sheets (scanned; transcribed); Hillsboro-Deering policies JEB, JFAA, IHBB, IHBBA, EEA, EFC and KB; the 2026-27 calendar; the HDES Title I needs assessment (June 2026); school board minutes October 2025–November 2026 (scanned; OCR of cited pages) and the 2026-27 staff nomination list; NHDOT road data; U.S. Naval Observatory sun tables; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection; FRAC; The Grapevine's pages; the site's test-score data.
- **Main findings:**
  - The "37% proficient in math" figure is the **district's** 2024-25 result for all tested grades (3–8 and 11). HDES's grades 3–5 were 43% (2024-25) and 45% (2025-26). Grade 3 is above the state; grade 5 is well below it, with 12% at the top level against 22% statewide.
  - HDES runs no gifted program (federal civil rights survey, both years). Enrichment is through weekly unified arts, trust-funded after-school clubs and a new schoolwide "Einstein hour".
  - HDES is the district's only Title I school and runs a schoolwide program, but it had **no Title I interventionists in 2025-26**; the reading specialist takes K–1 students between the 11th and 25th percentile and grades 2–5 one to two grade levels behind in phonics. 63 children were invited to the 2026 summer programs; 41 enrolled.
  - The preschool is IDEA-funded and mixes children with special education services and lottery entrants, in morning and afternoon sessions; about 45 children (25 three-year-olds, 20 four-year-olds) in fall 2025. No count of lottery places, applicants or a family tuition is published.
  - Kid Adventures, a non-profit, runs before- and after-school care inside HDES under a 2026–2028 agreement (cafeteria from 6:30 a.m. and until 6:15 p.m.); its fees and family hours aren't posted.
  - Emerald Lake's Route 77 stops at 13 corners along Emerald Drive and Gould Pond Road (7:45–8:01 a.m. for HDES, 6:42–6:58 a.m. for the middle and high school). NHDOT lists Emerald Drive and its side streets as private unpaved roads.
  - Morning HDES rides run 34 to 62 minutes from a route's first stop; stops nearest the school (West Main at Wall Street, Mill Street, Bridge Street) are 3 to 6 minutes out.
  - Meals are not free for everyone: lunch is $3.25, breakfast $2.00, and families must apply every year. FRAC lists one Hillsboro-Deering school as eligible for free meals for all (CEP) in 2024-25, but the district didn't take part.
  - 21 of the 23 K–5 classroom teachers in the August 2025 handbook are on the district's April 2026 renomination list for 2026-27.
- **Main gaps:** preschool places, applicants and any family tuition; Kid Adventures' fees, hours and storm-day rules; the town's recreation pages (HTTP 403); a current (2026-27) HDES handbook (the Google Doc version refused access); any HDES statement on interpreters for conferences or translated report cards; written rules on which guardianship papers HDES asks for; the scanned 2024-25 Title I plan (not read).
