# Hillsborough: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Hillsborough, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the eighth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Hillsborough and Deering form the Hillsboro-Deering Cooperative School District, in SAU 34 with Washington and Windsor:
- **Hillsboro-Deering Elementary (HDES)** serves preschool to grade 5, the **Middle School** grades 6–8 and the **High School** grades 9–12, all on Hillcat Drive.
- **Career programs:** the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC).

The district votes by official ballot. Windsor isn't a member; it pays tuition for its students. The CRTC was researched in `../Concord/`; John Stark (`../Henniker/`, `../Weare/`) and Hopkinton are the usual comparisons.
