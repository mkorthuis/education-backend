# Hillsboro-Deering High School and the CRTC

Part of the Hillsborough research (town/Hillsborough). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hillsboro-Deering High School (HDHS, grades 9–12, 12 Hillcat Drive) and its students' use of the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC): the CRTC bus and how CRTC fits the HDHS day and credits; ELOs, paid-work credit and senior privilege; AP and Early College courses taught at HDHS, New England College tuition remission, VLACS and the district's rules on online and outside courses and who pays; course levels, GPA weighting and class rank; the Alternative Program and alternative learning plans; varsity teams and co-ops; access for homeschooled and other non-enrolled students; guidance staffing and college, FAFSA and trade help; local scholarships; the SAU 34 strategic plan's HDHS goals; graduation and graduates' plans against neighboring high schools (**derived** from the site's data); and adult education and HiSET near Hillsborough.

- **9 fact topics, 58 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 32 MB): the HDHS 2026-27 Program of Studies site (`pos/`, 22 pages plus its graduation, grading, crosswalk and course-selection PDFs and the CRTC's 2026-27 Program of Studies for sending schools), the School Counseling Center site (`counseling/`, 10 pages plus the ELO policy, flyers, application and contracts), the 2025-26 HDHS student handbook, the 2026-27 athletic handbook, winter sign-ups and athletics page (`athletics/`), ten district policies (`policy/`), the SAU 34 strategic plan progress report of October 29, 2026 and three sets of 2026 board minutes (`board/`), Project LIFT, National Literacy Directory and NH Adult Education pages (`adult-ed/`), geocoding and routing results (`geo/`), a transcribed excerpt of the 2026-27 bus routes, and the database query behind the comparison table.
- **Places:** 51 facts use `district Hillsboro-Deering Cooperative`, 1 `sau 34` and 6 `town Hillsborough` (scholarships, adult education). No new statewide rules were written; earlier files are cited instead.
