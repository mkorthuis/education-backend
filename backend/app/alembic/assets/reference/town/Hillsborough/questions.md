# Hillsborough Residents' Questions

The questions the Hillsborough research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hillsborough's students attend the Hillsboro-Deering Cooperative School District (SAU 34), shared with Deering: Hillsboro-Deering Elementary (preschool to grade 5), Middle (grades 6–8) and High School (grades 9–12). High school students may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. Several questions contain premises (a preschool lottery, a budget around $24 million, Windsor sharing the cooperative's costs, 37% math proficiency, an annual homeschool assessment, low Niche and GreatSchools ratings) that the research should check rather than assume.
