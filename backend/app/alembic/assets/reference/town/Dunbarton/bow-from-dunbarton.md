# Bow Memorial and Bow High, From Dunbarton: Grade 7, Careers, Rides, Parking, Phones, Arts and Sports

Part of the Dunbarton research (town/Dunbarton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Dunbarton families face at Bow Memorial School (BMS, grades 5–8 in Bow; grades 7–8 for Dunbarton) and Bow High School (BHS, grades 9–12) under the 2024–2039 Bow–Dunbarton AREA agreement: why Dunbarton students join BMS in grade 7, the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC) for welding and construction, Dunbarton's contracted buses and the lack of a late bus, returning from homeschooling, AP and Early College access, counseling and mental health support, student parking, how Bow High's graduates fare, the phone rule, shop and robotics labs, the arts next to Concord High, sports eligibility and rides after practice. Bow's own research (`town/Bow/`) already holds most of Bow's side (AP and dual enrollment counts, the CRTC bus, counselors, phones at BMS, cut and eligibility rules, the AREA's terms). This file cites it by file, topic and fact number and adds Dunbarton's side and the gaps.

- **12 fact topics, 63 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 1 MB): the SAU 67 registration and Dunbarton Routes pages, the Bow–Dunbarton AREA agreement text, the BHS 2026-27 Program of Studies, Alternative Learning Options, athletic handbook, school profile, CRTC dates and parking information and sticker form, the BMS handbook and athletic expectations, BHS web pages (handbook index, personal devices, mental health, clubs, robotics, tips for students), SAU 67's Student Services page, the Concord High and CRTC Programs of Studies, text copies of three Dunbarton School Board minutes (December 3, 2025, January 7, 2026 and April 1, 2026) and an excerpt of Bow's September 4, 2025 minutes (names of residents, students and new hires removed), the June 2026 Bow board packet excerpt, the Dunbarton 2026-27 budget hearing packet, and two extracts from the site's database.
- **Places:** `district Bow` 48, `district Dunbarton` 9, `district Concord` 4, `sau 67` 2. No fact is without a place. The Dunbarton-side facts are the grade layout, Dunbarton's budgeted cohorts and share of Bow Memorial, and its contracted buses and board decisions on stops.
- **New data concepts:** Dunbarton's share of Bow Memorial's grade 7 and 8 students (**derived**); the CRTC's calendar for students entering in fall 2027 (road shows, open house, preview days, "Middle School Visits"); Bow High's 2026-27 student parking rules and fees; counts of arts course titles at Bow High and Concord High (**derived**); the two Bow Memorial documents' different wording of the grade rule for sports; Dunbarton's contracted bus service and the board's stop-by-stop decisions.
- **Proposed new shared topic** (used in this file): `high-school-student-parking` (kind local, question type local_practice): "How a high school handles student driving: who may buy a parking permit and when, the lots, the fee, and the fines and other consequences for parking without one." Several towns' parents ask about parking passes; no existing topic covers them.
