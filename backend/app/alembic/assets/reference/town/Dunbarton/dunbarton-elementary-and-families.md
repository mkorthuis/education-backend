# Dunbarton Elementary: Early Grades, Care, Buses and Families

Part of the Dunbarton research (town/Dunbarton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Dunbarton Elementary School (DES, kindergarten to grade 6) and what young families ask first: the kindergarten day and sign-up, before- and after-school care, preschool options near Dunbarton, the bus (stops, dirt roads, storm days), delays and early release, ParentSquare and Alma, proof of residency for renters, lunch and free or reduced-price meals, class sizes, town recreation and DES clubs, and enrolling a child in foster care.

- **15 topics, 68 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 2.5 MB): the 2026-27 DES Student and Families Handbook, DES and SAU 67 web pages (registration, parking, transportation, school cancellations, food services, McKinney-Vento, special education), the SAU 67 residency declaration, kindergarten questionnaire and free and reduced-price meals FAQ and application (OCR of a scanned PDF, "transcribed from image"), New Morning Schools' before- and after-school page and 2025 parent handbook, the Town of Dunbarton's recreation, children's theater and library pages and its winter operations procedure, excerpts of the town's 2025 annual report (no name lists), excerpts of 23 Dunbarton School Board minutes and packets (July 2025 to November 2026, with residents' and volunteers' names removed), site-database extracts and NH DOE and Head Start extracts.
- **Topics:** shared slugs `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-transportation`, `district-family-communication`, `district-meals`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `district-student-support`, `district-preschool`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `town-child-care`, `town-library-services`, `town-recreation-and-summer`; the library topics `kindergarten-day-length`, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff` and `kindergarten-registration` (same kind, question type and description as the library's); and three statewide rule facts under the existing `school-residency-rules` (no place). ## school-residency-rules: Children in the Department's Custody: Where They Attend School

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Which district is legally responsible for a student under New Hampshire law: domicile, guardians, divided homes and parenting plans, moves between districts, attending a district where a student doesn't live, and who decides disputes (added from the November 13, 2026 towns)

1. Under New Hampshire's residency law, when the Department of Health and Human Services has been appointed a child's legal guardian, the child's residence for school is where the department or the court places the child, and a child placed and cared for in a home for children, or placed by the department or a court with a relative or friend, may attend the public schools of the district where that home is.
   - citation: RSA 193:12, II(a)(3) and V
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-12.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-22

   - citation: RSA 193:12, V-b
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-12.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-22

   - note: Statewide rule, so no place. Text: "the department of health and human services shall request in writing that the superintendents involved resolve the dispute.
