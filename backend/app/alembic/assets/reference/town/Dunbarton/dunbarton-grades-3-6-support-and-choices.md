# Dunbarton Elementary Grades 3–6, Support and Choices

Part of the Dunbarton research (town/Dunbarton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Dunbarton Elementary School (DES) compares with Bow and Bedford on reading and math, how it serves a student who is ahead in math or behind in reading, speech and occupational therapy staffing, the move of a student with an IEP to Bow Memorial School (BMS), who to call at SAU 67, homeschooling and Education Freedom Account (EFA) access to Bow's middle school sports and band, welcoming a Hispanic student, how sixth grade prepares students for BMS, what the PTO pays for and what the school budget covers, art, music and PE, training for staff who work with children who have been through trauma, and police and school resource officers.

- **14 topics, 66 facts and 6 tables.** All 14 topics are shared slugs from `shared-topics.md`: `district-school-results-compared`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-homeschool-access`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `district-student-support`, `district-safety-and-security`, `town-emergency-services`, `district-budget-and-cost`, `district-fees`, `district-parent-rights-and-materials` and `required-instruction-rules`. Library topics `comparing-small-schools` and `research-school-transitions` are cited from earlier towns' files and not rewritten.
- **Places used:** `district Dunbarton` (DES, its budget, board and its side of the Bow AREA agreement), `district Bow` (Bow Memorial School and Bow's school resource officer), `sau 67` (SAU-wide staff and plans), `town Dunbarton` (the Police Department) and no place (state rules). No `town/sau67/` folder exists yet, so the SAU 67 facts are here.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Results:** on the site's state test data, DES is at or above the New Hampshire figure in every grade and subject for 2024-25 and 2025-26 and below Bedford in every one. It is below Bow in most cells but above Bow in 2025-26 grade 5 and 6 math and in 2024-25 grade 5 reading and grade 6 math. Each grade has only about 25 to 50 tested students, so single years swing (grade 5 math: 30% in 2024, 58% in 2025, 47% in 2026). On the Educational Opportunity Project's national scale, Dunbarton's grades 3–6 are 0.64 grade levels above the 2019 national average, against 1.70 for Bow, 2.70 for Bedford and 0.13 for New Hampshire.
  - **Math ahead:** DES has no separate gifted program in anything read. It groups students by need in a schoolwide WINN ("What I Need Now") block that the school says has intervention and extension, runs a PTO-funded after-school Crazy 8s Math Club for grades 3–5 (up to 16 students a session), and takes grade 5 to STARBASE. At BMS, 7th graders are placed in Accelerated Math, 7th Math or Structured Math, and 8th graders can take Integrated Algebra I for high school credit; how students are placed was not found.
  - **Speech and OT:** DES pays for its own speech and OT in its own budget, not through Bow. Speech is a speech aide plus contracted services (no pathologist wage line); OT went from a salaried position in 2025-26 ($65,679) to contracted services in 2026-27 ($59,819), and the SAU says the OT is in the building one day a week with the speech and language pathologist. No document shows Bow's therapists working at DES; the AREA agreement shares Bow's general special education services, including speech and OT, with Dunbarton's grade 7–12 students at no extra charge.
  - **IEP move:** the AREA agreement requires Bow to tell Dunbarton's administration of every special education meeting for a Dunbarton student, and Dunbarton or its agent must approve the services. SAU 67 says that for most students with IEPs a move between buildings is taken up at the annual IEP review. The SAU 67 Director of Student Services (603-224-4728, ext. 214) is the SAU contact; at DES the first steps are the special education teacher and the assistant principal of student services (ext. 204).
  - **Homeschool and EFA:** since July 1, 2026 state law requires no home education notice or yearly evaluation, except that a family that wants its child to use public school classes or activities (or withdraws a child from public school) files a declaration, and state testing is optional (library `home-education-rules` 1–2 and 8). Dunbarton's Policy AB (adopted December 3, 2025) lists the parent's right to district courses and co-curricular programs, and since August 18, 2026 EFA students share that right (library `choice-hb-1817-efa-public-school-courses`). The AREA agreement says Dunbarton's home schooled students take part in Bow athletics and co-curricular activities on the same terms as enrolled students with no extra home-school-only charge, which differs from the practice Bow's superintendent described in March 2026.
  - **Sixth grade to BMS:** the 2025-26 steps were a March 25 visit by BMS administration and counseling, a TEAM Day with BMS 6th graders, a June 3 BMS open house for Dunbarton families and a June 8 BMS 6th Grade Team Day. DES reports on standards-based report cards by semester; BMS grades on a percent scale with marking quarters and late penalties of up to 30%.
  - **PTO and the budget:** the PTO pays for all field trips and gave each certified staff member $250 for classroom needs in 2025-26 after a budget freeze at the start of that year; DES's own annual report says the freeze limited some classroom consumables and professional development. Art, music and PE are not named in any proposed cut: the budget keeps supply lines for each, the board discussed moving PE from 0.87 to a full-time position, and Dunbarton voters passed a local tax cap in November 2026, with the board saying that if the budget is over the cap it "will have to determine where cuts to the budget would come from."
  - **Police:** DES has no school resource officer (SRO) in anything read. Dunbarton police provide a presence at drop-off and pickup, random walkthroughs, safety drills and a 10-week LEAD lesson series in 6th grade. BMS shares an SRO with Bow Elementary and Bow High, and the AREA agreement's cost sheet lists an SRO line in the operating costs counted in Dunbarton's tuition.
- **Main gaps:** Dunbarton's policy manual (Simbli) blocked automated access, so the manual was read only for policy JJJ in a browser (fact 3 of district-homeschool-access); the SAU 67 data dashboard (Canva) returned no text; DES's own benchmark percentages (Acadience and STAR) are not published in anything read; the criteria for BMS's math levels and for honors-type placement were not found; and no source describes trauma-informed training at DES.
