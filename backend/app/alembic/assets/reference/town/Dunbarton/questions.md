# Dunbarton Residents' Questions

The questions the Dunbarton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Dunbarton's students attend Dunbarton Elementary School (kindergarten to grade 6) and then Bow Memorial School and Bow High School under an area agreement with Bow (2024 to 2039), both districts in SAU 67 with a shared office in Bow, with career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. Several questions contain premises (switching to Bow Memorial in 7th grade while Bow students start there in 5th, a March school ballot, a tax cap under HB 1300, Bow charging a per-student rate, no public pre-K because Bow High has the special education preschool, a way to end the arrangement by a Bow vote) that the research should check rather than assume.
