# New Ipswich Residents' Questions

The questions the New Ipswich research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

New Ipswich's students attend the Mascenic Regional School District (SAU 87), shared with Greenville: Highbridge Hill Elementary (preschool to grade 4), Boynton Middle School (grades 5–8) and Mascenic Regional High School (grades 9–12), all in New Ipswich. Students may take career programs at the Region 14 Applied Technology Center at ConVal in Peterborough. Several questions contain premises (Boynton math around 32%, high school math around 25%, 40% of graduates with no college or trade plans, Appleton Elementary's closing, Viking Block) that the research should check rather than assume.

- **P1.3** Are there any before- and after-school childcare programs right at the school, or do parents have to figure out private care?
