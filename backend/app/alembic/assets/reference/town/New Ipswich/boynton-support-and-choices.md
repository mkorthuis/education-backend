# Boynton Middle School, Student Support and Choices for New Ipswich Families

Part of the New Ipswich research (town/New Ipswich). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers Boynton Middle School (grades 5–8, 500 Turnpike Road, New Ipswich) in the Mascenic Regional School District (SAU 87), and the support and choices around it: how Boynton helps students who are behind in math, the school day and what happens after 2:15, busing for private school students, Dublin Christian Academy and High Mowing School's Pine Hill campus, homeschooled and EFA students in Boynton sports and band, IEP transitions and special education staffing, middle school sports and how they were funded after the 2026 default budget cut them, fees, counseling and bullying rules, acceleration and early high school math, clubs, Chromebooks and remote days, and coming back to Mascenic for high school.

**Counts:** 14 topics, 81 facts, 3 number tables (`boynton_fall_2026_sports_rosters_by_grade`, `boynton_athletics_budget_fy27`, `mascenic_special_education_vacancies_fall_2026`). Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 25 MB: `policies/`, `board/`, `minutes/`, `private-schools/`, `route/` and the 2026-27 district handbook).

**Main sources:** the Mascenic Regional School District 2026-2027 Student & Family Handbook (updated September 18, approved September 21, 2026), which has Boynton's own section; 16 district policies from the board's Policies folder on Google Drive (JJJ, IGDA, EEA, EEAG, JICK, JLD, JLDBA, IKE, IMBD, IHCD, IK, JG, JF, JFAA, EBCD, IMBA); Boynton principal's board reports (March, June, August, September, October and November 2026, and May 2025); student services and special education reports (February, May, September and October 2026); the April 2026 middle school sports forum slides and the superintendent's April 20 proposal; the FY27 adjustments to meet the default budget; the 2025-26 Athletic Code of Conduct; Widow Island Fund reports; the speech pathology contracts memo; the draft 2026-27 Mascenic Regional High School Program of Studies; school board minutes (April 6 to November 2, 2026); and Dublin Christian Academy's and High Mowing School's own pages.
