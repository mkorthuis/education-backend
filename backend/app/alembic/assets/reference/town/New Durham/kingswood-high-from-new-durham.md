# Kingswood High and the Lakes Region Technology Center From New Durham

Part of the New Durham research (town/New Durham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Kingswood Regional High School (KRHS, grades 9–12, site school id 22425) and the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC) in Wolfeboro, both run by the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (site district id 208, SAU 49), from a New Durham family's side: when LRTC trades classes start, the early bus and the late bus, shop and building trades for students not headed to a four-year college, dual enrollment and "Running Start," the Nurse Assisting option and an LNA license, college preparation in math and writing, culinary and graphic design in grade 9, transition planning for an IEP or 504, counselors and mental health staff, an alternative or online option, and career readiness. The Wolfeboro and Ossipee research already cover much of Kingswood and LRTC (the bell schedule, LRTC's priority rules, the Early College and AP lists, Kingswood Academy, counselors, career policies); this file cites them and adds what New Durham's questions need.

- **11 fact topics, 40 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 9 MB): the KRHS 2026-27 Program of Studies, Student/Family Handbook (scanned; OCR text) and School Profile, the KRMS 2026-27 handbook, the LRTC 2026-27 handbook and program pages (graphic design, culinary, construction, automotive, precision manufacturing, agricultural science, health science), KRHS counseling and core values pages, Governor Wentworth's special education personnel, transportation and Portrait of a Learner pages, policy EEA, the October and November 2026 superintendent reports, Great Bay Community College's placement policy, Lakes Region Community College's Early College and new student pages, UNH's first-year page, CCSNH's Early College page, the full text of 34 CFR Part 104, Kingswood's rows from the federal Civil Rights Data Collection and the map and route results. Most were fetched on November 21 by the Wolfeboro and Ossipee research and copied here; the rest were fetched on November 22.
- **Places:** 35 facts use `district Governor Wentworth Regional`, 2 use `town New Durham` (Lakes Region Community College's on-campus Early College and VR New Hampshire's office for New Durham) and 3 have no place (two community college placement policies and the Section 504 regulation).
- **Main findings:**
  - **LRTC starts in grade 10 at the earliest.** No LRTC program starts in grade 9, grade 10 entry needs related courses first, and grades 11–12 have priority, so a student now in 8th grade could start a Level I program in 2028-29 (derived). In grade 9 he can take Metal Working Technology I and Wood Technology I and II, which the catalog names as lead-ins. Culinary Arts I is grades 10–11 and Graphic Design I grades 11–12, so a 9th grader can't start either one; Food and Nutrition, Office Software Applications and Computer Visual Design I are the grade 9 lead-ins. The catalog and LRTC's web page disagree on Graphic Design I's prerequisites (cte-center-programs 1–5).
  - **The 6:05 a.m. bus can't be checked.** No source gives any New Durham pickup time; routes are in a system that needs a sign-in. The middle school day starts at 7:24 and a direct drive from New Durham School takes about 23 minutes and from points on New Durham roads 29 to 32 (derived), so a 6:05 pickup would come 79 minutes before the start. The district's transportation line (603-569-2684) is the only source (district-transportation 1–3).
  - **A late bus exists but its New Durham stop and practice end times aren't published.** It leaves Kingswood at 5:00 p.m. Monday to Thursday with a pass; teams ride buses back from games unless a parent asks in writing; a parent can send a note to change a stop. A 5:00 departure could reach New Durham no sooner than about 5:23 (derived) (district-transportation 4–7).
  - **Nurse Assisting is a Board of Nursing–approved course of 106 hours but needs Health Science Technology I first.** It is for grades 11–12, taken with Health Science II, and the license is a separate application after state tests LRTC offers. No source says how many students finish or get licensed (cte-center-programs 7–8).
  - **How many use college-level classes:** for 2023-24 Kingswood reported 42 dual enrollment students (5.8 per 100; the state median is 10.45) and 62 in AP (8.5 per 100; median 9.9), 97 students with IEPs (13.3%) and 31 with 504 plans (4.3%, against a median of 11.6%) (school-programs-after-grade-6 3; district-special-education-services 1).
  - **Math and transition:** the standard diploma names Algebra I and Geometry (not Algebra II), while UNH expects Algebra I and II and Geometry; the 504 regulation has no transition-plan requirement; Kingswood's catalog describes IEP courses (Study Skills, Community Learning Opportunities, life skills) but no transition process or coordinator; New Durham is assigned to VR New Hampshire's Portsmouth office, while Wolfeboro is assigned to Berlin.
  - **Shop math and work habits are built into courses:** Precision Manufacturing I teaches measuring to .001 inch and "basic and applied machining math calculations," LRTC Level I and II courses count as the senior math credit, Wood Technology I grades work ethic, and tardy and attendance rules are stated (district-curriculum-and-assessment 1–2; district-school-day-and-calendar 1–2).
- **New data concepts:** model drive times from New Durham to Kingswood, community colleges, Carroll Academy and VR's Portsmouth office; Kingswood's IEP, 504, AP and dual enrollment counts against New Hampshire medians; Kingswood's grade 9 lead-in courses; Great Bay and Lakes Region Community College placement rules; the April 7, 2027 PSAT, SAT and senior planning day; special education staff counts at Kingswood.
