# New Durham Elementary and Families

Part of the New Durham research (town/New Durham). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers New Durham Elementary School (NDS, preschool to grade 6, 7 Old Bay Road; site school id 22400) in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49) from a young family's side: the school day, arrival and what care exists before and after it; buses, stops, the Stopfinder pilot, cell coverage and snow-day notices; the integrated preschool, kindergarten and Early Supports; licensed child care and Head Start near New Durham, with drive times; New Durham Parks & Recreation (basketball, the Gunstock winter program, summer offerings) and gym use; summer meals and summer learning; speech, occupational therapy and special education staff; reading help; enrollment, class structure and staff cuts; art, music, clubs and winter sports; and how a family that moves in mid-year registers.

17 topics, 55 facts and 3 number tables. Main sources: the NDS 2026-27 Student & Family Handbook, staff list, calendar and forms pages; the GWRSD 2025 and 2026 annual reports (the New Durham School principal's reports and the October 1, 2025 pupil statistics); the district's transportation, enrollment, special education and annual meeting pages; policies DKB, ECB-R, EEA-R and JLIA; the FY2027 new staff request sheet; New Durham Parks & Recreation's website and 2026 commission agendas; the town's 2025 annual report and 2022 hazard mitigation plan; the Community Action Partnership of Strafford County (CAPSC) summer meals and early education pages; the site's child care data with OSRM drive times.

**Main gaps:** bus routes, stop locations and ride times (GWRSD posts none; Stopfinder's own page and Transfinder are closed to automated requests or need a sign-in); whether any New Durham route is in the five-route Stopfinder pilot; the preschool's days, hours and tuition; full-time status or caseloads for speech, occupational therapy and reading staff; the October 1, 2026 count by grade (NH DOE's iPlatform); any written schedule or agreement for Parks & Recreation's use of the NDS gym (the commission's minutes sit in Google Drive folders); Being Kool's own hours, rates and school pick-up; whether Camp Invention or any summer program is set for summer 2027.
