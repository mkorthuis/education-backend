# New Durham Elementary Grades 3–6, Kingswood Middle and Choices

Part of the New Durham research (town/New Durham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers New Durham Elementary School's upper grades (3–6), the move to Kingswood Regional Middle School (KRMS, grades 7–8, Wolfeboro) in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49), and the choices around them: test results against Alton Central, Crescent Lake and the state; a nurse in every building; Infinite Campus, ParentSquare and bus colors; the 6th-grade parent night; the school calendar; phones; behavior and bullying; extra help; moving over the line to Farmington; and Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs) and homeschooling.

- **15 topics, 57 facts, 3 tables.** All topics use shared slugs. District facts have `place: district Governor Wentworth Regional`; New Durham's EFA listings and counts are `town New Durham`; the EFA grant facts are statewide (no place).
- **Main sources:** New Durham School's 2026-27 handbook, staff list, directory and calendar; KRMS's calendar, "Meet the Teams" and 2026-27 handbook (copied from the Wolfeboro research); the GWRSD enrollment, ParentSquare, home education and transportation pages; GWRSD policies JLC, JLDBA, IKB, JFAB, JFAB-R and JCA; the 2026 annual report (OCR excerpts); the January 2026 deliberative session presentation; the May 4 and October 5, 2026 board minutes; the November 2, 2026 assessment report; the district's job postings of November 22, 2026; the Children's Scholarship Fund's EFA FAQ; and the site's own test, meals and bullying data.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Test results:** In spring 2026 New Durham Elementary's tested students were 57% proficient in reading and 60% in math (state, all tested grades: 55% and 44%). Each New Durham grade has only 20–25 tested students, so a grade can swing a lot: the group that was 15% proficient in math in grade 5 (2025) was 76% in grade 6 (2026).
  - **Nurses:** The district hired its own nurses from July 1, 2026 (seven full-time, two half-time) after Huggins Hospital ended its contract. Each of the eight school buildings lists a nurse; Effingham's are two half-time nurses. The school's directory lists three people titled nurse, and substitute coverage was a problem in October.
  - **Bus colors and Infinite Campus:** New Durham School's handbook says to make sure your child "knows the color name of their bus," and gives the transportation office (603-569-2684). Infinite Campus is the parent portal; ParentSquare invitations go to the contact details held there. KRMS grades are in JumpRope.
  - **6th-grade transition:** In spring 2026 there was a 6th-grade field trip to the Kingswood Arts Center (March 20) and a 6th Grade Parent/Guardian Information Night at KRMS (March 31, 6–7 PM). New Durham sent 17 seventh graders to KRMS in 2025-26, about 10% of the grade.
  - **Same calendar:** New Durham School, KRMS and Kingswood High follow one district calendar; school hours differ (New Durham begins at 8:45 AM; KRMS students are due in advisory at 7:24 AM).
  - **Moving to Farmington:** Staying at New Durham School after a move would need the school board's approval, tuition and no school bus; Farmington would hold the responsibility for special education.
- **Main gaps:** Bus routes, stops and times (not published; the transportation department and a sign-in routing system hold them); how often any building has been without its nurse; why New Durham's scores moved; whether New Durham School runs a schoolwide Title I program (NH DOE's files refused automated requests); the 2026-27 dates for 6th-grade night and high school course sign-up; what "Bridges Day" at New Durham School is; New Durham students' own experience of the move to KRMS (no survey or data).
