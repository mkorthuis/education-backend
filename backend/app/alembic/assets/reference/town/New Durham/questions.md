# New Durham Residents' Questions

The questions the New Durham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

New Durham's students attend New Durham Elementary School (preschool to grade 6), then Kingswood Regional Middle and High Schools in Wolfeboro, all in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49), with the Lakes Region Technology Center joined to Kingswood High. Several questions contain premises (a $600,000 salary overage for school nurses, an $8.8 million school appropriation, designated volunteers driving students, a school tax cap on the ballot, a turf field reserve fund, a Stopfinder bus-tracking pilot, a 6:05 a.m. bus stop for a middle schooler, a Strafford County town in a Carroll County district) that the research should check rather than assume.
