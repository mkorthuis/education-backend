# Timberlane Regional Middle School and Student Life

Shared research for every Timberlane Regional member town (Atkinson, Danville, Plaistow and Sandown), written for the Atkinson and Plaistow research (town/Atkinson, town/Plaistow) and kept in the shared folder town/timberlane. Gathered November 21, 2026 by agent T2.

## Summary

This file covers Timberlane Regional Middle School (TRMS, grades 6–8) and student life across TRMS and Timberlane Regional High School (TRHS): the move up from the four towns' elementary schools (orientation, team trips, counselors, the 2026-27 schedule change and its daily WIN block), help for students who struggle (the district's MTSS handbook, i-Ready screening), math acceleration (Pre-Algebra in grade 7, Algebra I in grade 8, high school credit), world languages, middle school band, orchestra, chorus and the musical, athletics (sports offered, tryouts and cuts, eligibility, no participation fee found, wrestling's state titles), the phone and bullying policies, late buses, after-school clubs, what families pay, homeschooled students' access, the Performing Arts Center's age and upkeep, the high school's bathrooms, exterior and air handlers, the fields and gym, and the FY27 budget reductions that touched activity stipends.

- **15 fact topics, 103 facts and 1 number table.** Every fact uses `place: district Timberlane Regional`. Table: `trhs_wrestling_nhiaa_points_2001_2025` (`district Timberlane Regional`).
- **New data concepts:** the TRMS orientation schedule and grade-level team trips; the 2026-27 TRMS schedule (daily WIN, quarter-long unified arts); Pre-Algebra as the route to grade 8 Algebra I; the TRMS ensemble courses; middle school tryout and cut practices; middle and high school academic eligibility rules side by side; NHIAA wrestling results since 2001; the PAC's age, equipment and deferred roof and air handler work; the TRHS bathroom renovation estimate line by line; the TRHS exterior (envelope) and window project; field projects and their CIP status; the reductions to coach, music and theater stipend increases in FY27.

- **Premises checked:**
  - "The PAC is over two decades old" (Atkinson P6.1): **correct.** The PAC was completed in November 2002, about 24 years ago (derived). Its roof and HVAC were judged at "end of life" but not emergencies in 2024; the board moved the $817,000 PAC roof job out of the lease in January 2026, and new PAC air handlers were still to be ordered by Thanksgiving 2026 (district-facilities-and-capital 1–7).
  - "Annual pay-to-play athletic fees" (Atkinson P9.3) and "do kids pay to play, and does it cover away-meet buses" (Plaistow P5.1): **no participation fee was found** in the athletics policies, the middle and high school athletic handbooks, the booster handbook or the athletics pages. Policy JJIB puts athletics in the general budget and has buses scheduled for all games; families pay for physicals and their own health insurance is primary (district-fees 1–2; district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 13).
  - "Powerhouse wrestling reputation" (Atkinson P9.3): **borne out by NHIAA records.** Timberlane won the top-division state title in 2025, was runner-up in 2023 and 2024, and won every year from 2007 to 2014 and 2016 to 2022; 30 titles since 1987 (derived, one shared) (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 11; table).
  - "Free or reduced admission for seniors" (Atkinson P14.3): **no senior discount or pass was found.** Policy DFD has the Superintendent approve all ticket prices; some music events are free, such as the May 2026 commissioned premiere (district-fees 9–10).
  - "Around 40% of middle schoolers proficient in math" (Plaistow P8.1): **correct, from the site's data:** TRMS was 42% proficient in math in 2026, 40% in 2025 and 41% in 2024.
  - "Can middle schoolers still try out for high school productions?" (Plaistow P4.3): **not under current policy.** Policy JJIC says students in grades 6–8 are not eligible for high school extracurricular activities "(clubs, drama, etc.)" unless the Superintendent makes an exception; the policy was last revised in 2014 (school-programs-after-grade-6 4).
  - "Stuck doing pickup at 5:30" (Plaistow P4.2): **consistent with the published schedule.** The only late buses leave about 3:45–4:00 p.m., Tuesday to Thursday (district-transportation 1–2).
  - "The 360-student Atkinson Academy" (Atkinson P16.1): roughly right; the site's data show 364 students at Atkinson Academy and 723 at TRMS in 2024-25 (the October 1, 2024 count).
  - "True acceleration into Algebra I by 8th grade" (Atkinson P16.2): **yes, for selected students:** Pre-Algebra in grade 7 prepares selected students for Algebra I in grade 8, which can count for high school credit (district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners 1–3). But TRMS reported no grade 7–8 Algebra I classes to the federal Civil Rights Data Collection for 2023-24, before the January 2025 curriculum revisions (Danville `middle-and-high-school.md`, district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners 1); how many eighth graders take it now isn't published.
  - "Are the bathrooms still a mess, or did they fix up the high school?" (Plaistow P19.3): **not yet renovated.** Students told the board in December 2024 that the high school bathrooms need improvement; a renovation is designed, estimated at $625,033 (October 30, 2026) and planned for bidding in January 2027 (district-facilities-and-capital 8–11).
