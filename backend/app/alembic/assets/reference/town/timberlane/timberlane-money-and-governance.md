# Timberlane Regional: Money, Taxes and Governance

Shared research for every Timberlane Regional member town (Atkinson, Danville, Plaistow and Sandown), gathered for the Atkinson and Plaistow research (town/Atkinson, town/Plaistow). Agent T3. Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics, 80 facts and 4 number tables on how the Timberlane Regional School District (site district id 534, SAU 106) builds, votes on and pays for its budget, and how its costs are divided among the four towns.
- **How the four towns split the bill.** The Articles of Agreement divide operating costs by each town's resident students (average daily membership two years earlier) and capital costs by equalized property value, and credit each town's own state aid to its share. The district's FY2026 apportionment by town, with **derived** shares and per-student comparisons, a table for FY2022–FY2026, the FY2027 tax impact estimates by town, and Plaistow's 2025 mix of residential and commercial value.
- **Budget, votes and the default budget.** The FY2027 budget ($88,054,569 after the deliberative session), its drivers, where the money goes, the 2025 deliberative session's $6.6 million cut and what was eliminated (37 positions in FY2026, 13 in FY2027), 16 years of budget votes (the default budget was adopted only in 2014 and 2018, and in 2025 and 2026 it was higher than the proposed budget), and the FY2028 outlook.
- **Fund balance.** From $16.4 million (June 2024) to $7.6 million used to cut FY2026 taxes, the 2023 5% retention vote, its 2025 repeal by petition (leaving 0%), the 2026 2.5% article and the board's $500,000 retained in September 2026.
- **Tax caps.** The 2025 and 2026 petitions (2.5%, amended to a study and then to 25%), the Tax Cap Committee's report, and the district's own estimates of Question 2's cap (72.0% yes across the four towns, site data).
- **Buildings and debt.** The March 2023 lease-purchase authorization of up to $25,243,000, its five phases, interest rates, the $11.9 million outstanding and $1.0 million a year in payments to 2044.
- **Staff, contracts and special education.** 49.5 vacant positions whose money pays contracted special education services, the district's own and contracted speech pathologists, psychologists and aides, teachers' and other contracts, Massachusetts salary comparisons, special education costs and aid.
- **Governance.** The 2019–2021 move from SAU 55 to its own SAU 106, the new separate SAU budget (about 3.2% of district operations), the board's makeup, the 2015 Sandown withdrawal study, enrollment from 4,677 (2004-05) to 2,970 (2026-27, grades K–12) and NESDEC's projections, and the 2026 Sandown elementary subcommittee.

**Main gaps:** district-wide totals for the March 2026 ballot (only Plaistow's are posted in readable form); the 2026 Plaistow tax rate (not posted by November 21; NH DRA's files refuse automated requests); any study of Atkinson or Plaistow withdrawing (none found); a published benchmark of central office costs against similar districts (none found; the site's DOE-25 data can compare); Timberlane teacher turnover rates; commercial property shares for Atkinson, Danville and Sandown.

**Topic proposal:** none; the shared topics fit.
