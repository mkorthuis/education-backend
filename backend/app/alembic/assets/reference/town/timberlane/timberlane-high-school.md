# Timberlane Regional High School

Shared research on Timberlane Regional High School (TRHS, Plaistow) for every member town of the Timberlane Regional School District: Atkinson, Danville, Plaistow and Sandown. Written for the Atkinson and Plaistow research (town/Atkinson, town/Plaistow) by agent T1. Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers TRHS's course levels and Advanced Placement (AP) courses, dual enrollment with Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU) and Northern Essex Community College (NECC), online courses (VLACS) and the district's remote learning policy, weighted GPA, class rank and honor roll, how transfer and home-education work is placed, computer science, robotics, engineering, shop and trades classes, career and technical education (CTE) at Salem High and Pinkerton Academy from Timberlane's side (who applies, when, credits, buses), extended learning opportunities (ELOs) such as internships and apprenticeships, the Evening Division and HiSET prep, credit recovery and guided study, special education at TRHS and the diplomas and certificate open to students with IEPs, the diploma tracks and the new requirements for the class of 2030 (including personal finance), counselors and their numbers over four years, the Student Assistance Program, the absence of a high school social worker, community mental health resources the district lists, SAT and college results the school publishes, and a federal comparison of AP and dual enrollment with Bedford and Portsmouth.

## school-programs-after-grade-6: Middle and High School Programs a District's Students Attend

- kind: local

1. Timberlane Regional High School gives each course one of four levels (Advanced Placement, Accelerated, College & Career Prep or Non-Leveled); to be placed in an Accelerated course a student must be recommended by the current teacher and by the counselor, and is expected to have earned 80% or higher in a previous Accelerated course or 90% or higher in a College & Career Prep course.
   - citation: Timberlane Regional High School, Program of Studies 2026-2027 for the Classes of 2027, 2028 and 2029, "Course Levels," p. 6
   - source: https://hs.timberlane.net/fs/resource-manager/view/d0aba7b0-c1f2-46c6-91bc-63299251d7b6
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Timberlane Regional
   - note: Text: "For inclusion in accelerated classes all students: • Must be recommended by current teacher, • Must be recommended by counselor, • Are recommended to have earned a final grade of 80% or higher in previous ACC course, or • Are recommended to have earned a final grade of 90% or higher in previous CCP course, and • Must be in compliance with any additional selection criteria established." In ACC/CCP courses "Students must declare at which level they are taking the class by the 5th day of the course." CCP courses prepare students "for furthering their education in 1, 2, or 4 -year institutions, certificate programs, or immediately entering the workforce." TRHS has no level called "honors"; Accelerated is the level between CCP and AP. The class of 2030 edition has the same text. Timberlane Regional High School students who want credit for an online course (such as VLACS) must meet their counselor and get approval before enrolling; the credit and GPA weight are set during that approval, credit is given only after the registrar receives an official final grade, and the transcript shows the course was taken online.
   - citation: Timberlane Regional High School, Program of Studies 2026-2027 (Classes of 2027–2029), "Online Coursework," p. 15
   - source: https://hs.timberlane.net/fs/resource-manager/view/d0aba7b0-c1f2-46c6-91bc-63299251d7b6
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Timberlane Regional
   - note: Text: "Students must be granted authorization prior to their enrollment. Students must complete an approval form and receive prior authorization from their counselor. Credit and GPA weight for online courses will be determined during the pre-approval process. Credit for an online course will not be granted until an official record of the final grade has been received by the TRHS registrar ... The student's official school transcript will document that the course was taken online. The numerical grade earned will be transferred and converted to the equivalent letter grade in the TRHS grading system." The ELO section adds that "pre-approved online course work (e.g. VLACS)" is graded, unlike other ELOs. No count of TRHS students taking VLACS courses was found. UNH's online courses through VLACS are in the library (unh-early-college-online). Raw: raw/high/trhs-pos-2026-27-classes-2027-2029.txt (PDF pages 15–16).

7.
