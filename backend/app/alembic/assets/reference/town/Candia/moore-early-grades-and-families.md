Part of the Candia research (town/Candia). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Henry W. Moore School (kindergarten to grade 8, Candia) from a young family's side: full-day kindergarten, the cutoff and what happens if a child starts a year later, kindergarten registration and what the school asks about a new kindergartner, public preschool (there is none posted), New Morning's after-school program at Moore, delays and early-release days, the summer program, buses and First Student, meals and help with their cost, specials (art, music, PE), after-school activities, Moore's teams and the CYAA ski bus, the school's size and feel, enrolling with guardianship papers, and child care near Candia. Test scores are checked against the site's data.
