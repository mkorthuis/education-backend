# Candia Residents' Questions

The questions the Candia research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Candia is in SAU 15 with Auburn and Hooksett. The Candia School District runs the Henry W. Moore School (kindergarten to grade 8), and its high school students attend Pinkerton Academy in Derry under a tuition contract from 2018 (it replaced Manchester Central). Jesse Remington High School, a private Christian school, is in Candia. Several questions contain premises (a school tax cap passing, health insurance up 23%, the Budget Committee and School Board disagreeing, Candia's seat on Pinkerton's board, an annual homeschool assessment) that the research should check rather than assume.
