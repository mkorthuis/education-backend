# Candia: Money, the Pinkerton Contract and Governance

Part of the Candia research (town/Candia). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics, 52 facts and 4 number tables on how the Candia School District (SAU 15) builds, votes on and pays for its budget, what drives it, how much goes to Pinkerton Academy against the Henry W. Moore School, the school tax cap Candia adopted in November 2026, the 2026 tax rate, state aid against local taxes, the elderly exemption, the Candia–Pinkerton agreement (transcribed here for the first time) and the Manchester agreement it replaced, what Candia pays SAU 15, the recurring split between the Budget Committee and the School Board, how to follow and speak at board meetings, and the state of the Moore School building.

- **Budget.** Voters approved a 2026-27 operating budget of $11,833,313 on March 10, 2026, 408 to 355, after the deliberative session raised the Budget Committee's $11,585,787; the teachers' contract passed 488 to 274. In March 2025 voters rejected the budget, 472 to 488, and the district ran on its default budget. The board's draft for 2027-28 is $12,563,446 (general fund up 4.20%, 0.89% over the default), sent to the Budget Committee on November 2, 2026.
- **Where the money goes.** In the 2026-27 general fund ($11.71 million), Pinkerton regular tuition is 18.5% (121 students at $17,910), all high school tuition and high school special education 32.1%, buses 8.0%, SAU 15 1.8%, the 2022 energy lease 1.6%, and about 56.5% runs Moore School and the district (**derived**).
- **Tax cap.** Candia adopted the school tax cap (site data). The board's October 2026 update estimated that under the cap the 2026-27 increase would have been held to about $343,828 instead of about $904,000, and listed staffing, buses, activities and field trips, building upkeep and technology as areas that could be affected. No list of cuts had been posted by November 21.
- **Tax rate.** The selectmen set Candia's 2026 rate at $14.84 per $1,000 on November 2, 2026, up $1.87 from $12.97 (**derived**); the parts (school, town, county, state) weren't posted. In 2025 the local school tax paid 80.3% of the school budget's tax-supported cost, the state's adequacy grant 10.6% (**derived**).
- **Pinkerton.** The 2018–2038 agreement sends all Candia high school students to Pinkerton (the board may let up to 5% go elsewhere), sets tuition from Pinkerton's actual costs including its building debt, and requires at least two Candia trustees; Pinkerton's 2026-27 board president is one of them. Pinkerton's trustees make the final budget decisions.
- **Building.** The roof, windows, air handlers, fire alarm and lighting have been replaced, much of it under a 15-year, $2,262,411 energy lease-purchase voters approved in 2022 ($191,645 a year). Still open: heating and plumbing piping "close to 50 years old," the main electrical panels and boilers. The facility capital reserve held $196 at the end of 2025.
