# Moore School's Upper Grades: Support and Choices

Part of the Candia research (town/Candia). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Henry W. Moore School (kindergarten to grade 8, the Candia School District's only school) for students who need extra support or want more, and for families choosing home education, an EFA or a private school: reading and math interventions, special education staff and IEP procedures, paraprofessional, speech and occupational therapy budgets, home-educated and EFA students' access to Moore's classes and activities, homeschool groups, math levels, band, chorus and drama, bullying and phone rules, counseling, help for students who arrive mid-year, the grade 7 curriculum and weekly schedule, military families, Jesse Remington High School, and busing and special education evaluations for private school students. Sources are the 2026-27 Student and Family Handbook, Candia School Board policies (JJJ revised November 2, 2026; JICK adopted September 3, 2026), the district's Special Education Policies and Procedures Manual (January 2024) and Title I Master Plan (2026-27), the board's September–November 2026 packets and minutes (principal's, assistant superintendent's and superintendent's reports, the FY2027-28 budget detail, the October 29 expenditure report), Moore's web pages, SAU 15's competency sheets, the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24, NCES's Private School Universe Survey, the EFA marketplace feed, and the sites of Jesse Remington High School, Smyth Public Library, Granite State Home Educators, Classical Conversations and Pinkerton Academy.
