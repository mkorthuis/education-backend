# Pinkerton Academy From Candia: Buses, IEPs, CTE, Advanced Courses, Teams, Auditions and Life After High School

Part of the Candia research (town/Candia). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Candia's side of high school at Pinkerton Academy in Derry: the 2018–2038 agreement's terms on admission, transportation, special education, career guidance and records; how many Candia students attend; Candia's two Pinkerton bus routes (times, ride lengths, stops), the 2026 stop appeals and the board's decision, storm delays, the absence of a late bus and the drive to Derry; Candia students with IEPs at Pinkerton (counts and the services Candia pays for); what Candia's 8th graders see of the move to Pinkerton; AP and honors in grades 9 and 10; band, jazz and theatre auditions; JROTC; tryouts and cuts; military planning and recruiters; employer connections and work-based learning; dual enrollment costs; and New Hampshire's community college tuition waivers that touch military families. Pinkerton's own programs (Freshman Academy, counselors, CTE programs, AP results, special education procedures, parking) are in the shared Pinkerton files (`town/pinkerton/`) and the Hooksett and Auburn files, which this file cites rather than repeats.

- **8 fact topics, 41 facts and 3 number tables.** Places: `district Candia` 31, `district Pinkerton Academy` 7, no place 3 (state tuition waivers, under the library topic `ccsnh-tuition-and-aid`). Tables: `candia_pinkerton_bus_times_2026_27`, `candia_students_at_pinkerton`, `candia_special_education_students_at_pinkerton`.
- **Main sources:** the Candia–Pinkerton agreement (signed September 2015, approved by the State Board of Education November 18, 2015; scanned, read by OCR and checked against page images); Candia's transportation page, its Pinkerton route sheets as of September 23, 2026, SAU 15's 2026-27 high school storm letter, the 2023–2028 First Student contract (scanned, OCR) and policies EEA, EEAC and EEAF; Candia School Board minutes (October 2025 to November 2026), the September 22, 2026 bus appeal hearing and the December 2025, February 2026 and fall 2026 packets (superintendent's and principal's reports, the proposed 2027-28 budget); the board's October 27, 2026 HB 1300 update; Candia's special education manual (January 2024); Pinkerton's JROTC, Business and Community Connections, Seniors, Assessment and athletics pages, its 2026-27 winter tryouts document, the Pinkerton Players' site and the Pinkerton research's saved catalog; RSA 110-B:63-c, RSA 188-F:15 and CCSNH's tuition waiver page; and a driving route between the two schools.
- **New data concepts:** Candia's Pinkerton bus routes with first and last stops and scheduled ride lengths compared with the contract's and policy's limits; the share of high school stops at intersections; the 2026 stop appeals and the board's cost estimate for an added bus; Candia students at Pinkerton by year and its special education students at Pinkerton over five years; the special education programs and services in Candia's proposed 2027-28 budget; the Pinkerton-wide results shown to Candia's board in February 2026; Pinkerton's ensemble audition calendar; JROTC's after-school schedule; winter tryout times and Pinkerton's statement that some teams make cuts; Pinkerton's military planning materials; employer incentives Pinkerton advertises; state community college waivers for Guard members and children of disabled veterans.

The child of a veteran shall remain eligible for free tuition under this paragraph through the end of the semester in which he or she attains the age of 27"; "(c) All applicants shall complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)."
