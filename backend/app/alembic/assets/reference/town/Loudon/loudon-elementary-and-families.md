# Loudon Elementary School and Young Families in Loudon

Part of the Loudon research (town/Loudon). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Loudon Elementary School (LES, kindergarten to grade 5) in the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46) and the daily life of Loudon's young families: the 9:00 school day and how it fits with the middle and high school day, the YMCA's before- and after-school care at the school, kindergarten size and registration, how bus stops are set and how bus changes work, storm alerts, school meals and the free and reduced-price application, LES's October 1, 2026 enrollment and its 2025-26 staff, the class size policy, paraprofessional and speech staffing, reading screening and intervention, gifted services, test results next to Concord and Bow, library and classroom funding (including DonorsChoose), building security and drills, preschool and Head Start near Loudon, and town, library and summer programs.

**Counts:** 17 topics, 68 facts, 4 number tables (three derived: the 2025-26 staff by role, the 2026 test comparison and the bus ride times; one copied: the October 1, 2026 enrollment by grade).

**Main sources:** LES's website (home, profile, quick links, staff directory, registration packets, dyslexia screening page); the district's food service, transportation, school closings, student services and employment pages; its 2026-27 calendar and the ten 2026-27 Loudon bus route sheets (dated August 25, 2026); board policies IIB, IFA, IHCA, KI, EBCA, EFDA and EEA-R; board minutes (March 2025 to October 2026) and the October 5 and November 2, 2026 board packets (enrollment report, general fund report, LES newsletters); the district's 2026 Annual Report; the MVMS and MVHS schedules; the Granite YMCA's school-age care page; the Town of Loudon's recreation, library and police pages; the Maxfield Public Library; DonorsChoose's LES page; USDA's 2026 summer meal sites; and the site's own data (test results, child care), with library copies of the Civil Rights Data Collection gifted rows and the Head Start and 21st CCLC lists.

**Main gaps:** LES's 2026-27 class lists and number of classes per grade (only an undated profile and the 2025-26 staff directory show them); whether kindergarten rooms have aides; a 2026-27 LES student/family handbook (none is posted); what time storm alerts go out today and whether ParentSquare sends texts; whether the YMCA's before-school care runs on two-hour delay mornings; any LES speech or paraprofessional caseload or missed-session record; tuition and eligibility for the district's preschool at Boscawen and Penacook; what the restored 2026-27 "summer program" is; the Maxfield Public Library's summer reading program; Niche and other rating pages (refused automated requests).
