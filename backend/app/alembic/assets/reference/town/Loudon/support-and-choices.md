# Student Support and School Choices for Loudon Families

Part of the Loudon research (town/Loudon). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers student support and school choice for Loudon families in the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46): homeschooled and EFA students' access to Merrimack Valley Middle School (MVMS) classes, sports and band; returning to Loudon Elementary School (LES) after home education; what EFAs do to the district's state aid; IEPs moving from LES to MVMS and whom to call at SAU 46; Loudon's middle and high school bus routes and winter pickup times; the phone policy at MVHS and MVMS; charter schools near Loudon (Strong Foundations in Pembroke, and Benjamin Franklin Academy in Loudon itself); the district's student makeup, bullying and nondiscrimination policies; counseling and mental health support at MVHS; changing schools within the district after a move; and the Merrimack Valley Learning Center (MVLC). Sources are the district, MVHS, MVMS, LES, MVLC and SAU 46 web pages; the MVHS and MVMS 2026–27 handbooks; the MVHS bell schedules and Pyramid of Student Support; school board policies AC, EEA, IHBA, IHBG, IHBI, IKE, JCA, JF, JFAA, JFAB, JFABD, JICJ, JICK, JLDBB and KE; board minutes from August 2025 to October 2026 and the January 5, 2026 packet; the district's 2026 annual report; the 2026–27 Loudon route sheets; Strong Foundations' and Benjamin Franklin Academy's own pages and Strong Foundations' charter; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (2023–24); and the U.S. Naval Observatory's sun tables. Staff names are left out of the statements.

**Main findings:**
- MVMS's 2026–27 handbook gives homeschooled and EFA students living in the district full access to sports and clubs and up to three class periods a semester, with no fees, under HB 1817 (2026). In January 2026 the board had approved EFA tuition and activity fees, and policy IHBG (2018) still says parents pay all fees and describes the repealed yearly evaluation.
- Loudon's middle and high school buses pick up from 6:20 to 6:29 a.m., mostly at the riders' own addresses, and reach MVHS at 7:20–7:25 for a 7:45 start. On December 21 the sun rises at 7:15; 55 of 175 morning stops come before 6:42, when civil twilight begins (**derived**). From the first stop, two routes are scheduled at 65 minutes to the middle school, over the policy's one-hour limit.
- MVHS phones must be "away (out of view) and powered-off" from first bell to dismissal, not locked in pouches; a phone seen out is confiscated for three school days. For emergencies, families call the main office (603-753-4311).
- Benjamin Franklin Academy, a free charter school for grades 6–12 at 157 Chichester Road in Loudon, needed no lottery for 2026–27 and listed openings in grades 6–11. Strong Foundations (K–8, Pembroke) takes applications January 1–March 31 and holds its lottery in early April; Pembroke residents get preference, and families outside Pembroke arrange their own transportation.
- Moving from Loudon to Boscawen or the Penacook part of Concord keeps a child in the same district. Staying at LES is a change of school assignment: the superintendent decides on room alone, and requests made after July 1 need a meeting.
- The MVLC is the district's alternative middle and high school (grades 6–12, a teacher and a paraeducator in each room, a 20-credit diploma option). In November 2025 the board voted 9–1, with one abstention, to keep it at Washington Street and improve the building.

## district-homeschool-access: Homeschooled and EFA Students in Merrimack Valley's Middle School and Policies

Merrimack Valley Middle School's 2026–27 handbook says resident students who are homeschooled or use an Education Freedom Account (EFA) have equal access to all its curricular, co-curricular and extracurricular programs under RSA 193:1-c (HB 1817): they may take classes for up to 50% of the instructional day (at most 3 class periods a semester), and they have "full access to all MVMS sports teams and clubs."
