# Loudon Residents' Questions

The questions the Loudon research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Loudon's students attend the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46), shared with Boscawen, Penacook, Salisbury and Webster. In Loudon it runs Loudon Elementary School (K–5); grades 6–8 attend Merrimack Valley Middle School and grades 9–12 Merrimack Valley High School, both in Penacook, with the Merrimack Valley Learning Center. Several questions contain premises (a 9:00 start at Loudon Elementary, closing a smaller elementary school, a bell-to-bell phone ban, Strong Foundations charter school, how ESSER money was spent, parking and traffic at the high school, Speedway race weekends, MVHS pushing four-year college) that the research should check rather than assume.
