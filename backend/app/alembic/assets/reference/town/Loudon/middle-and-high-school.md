# Merrimack Valley Middle and High School: Courses, Career Programs, Help, Costs and Outcomes

Part of the Loudon research (town/Loudon). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Merrimack Valley Middle School (MVMS, grades 6–8) and Merrimack Valley High School (MVHS, grades 9–12) in Penacook, which Loudon's students attend after Loudon Elementary, and which Boscawen's research will also cite. It covers the move from the elementary schools to MVMS, after-school buses, MVHS's courses, AP and college-credit options, its engineering sequence, diploma pathways, internships and other extended learning opportunities (ELOs), release time, Flex Block and other free help, career and college advising and scholarships, where MVHS students take career programs (the Concord Regional Technical Center and the Winnisquam Career Center), what the district budget shows about career program spending, what families pay, science and outdoor learning, and MVHS's published plans, test and college-choice figures, with a **derived** comparison to similar-size New Hampshire high schools from the site's own data.

- **8 fact topics, 75 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 13 MB): the MVHS 2026-27 school profile, 2026-27 student handbook, 2025-26 Program of Studies (the 2026-27 edition is sign-in only), diploma pathways chart, bell schedules, Flex Block documents, 2026-27 athletic agreement and Vision of the Graduate slides; the MVHS counseling site's pages; the MVMS 2026-27 handbook, exploratory class list, transition page and newsletters; the CRTC 2025-26 Program of Studies MVHS posts; nine school board minutes (April 2025 to October 2026); eight district policies (IKF, IKFG, IHCD, IHBH, IMBC, IJOA, JQ, JJIB); and a copy of the district's 2026 annual report (also in `additional-data/raw/districts/batch-1/`).
- **Places:** 74 facts use `district Merrimack Valley`; 1 uses `district Concord` (the CRTC's own application process). No new statewide rules were needed; the state rules that apply are named from the library and earlier towns in the question table.
- **New data concepts:** MVHS's Class of 2026 plans and May 2026 AP score distribution (from its own profile); its six diploma pathways, including a Career-ready pathway that requires a career-center certificate and an internship; its rule that students start the CRTC or Winnisquam at 16 as juniors; the district's CRTC tuition budget line and the state's 38% reimbursement in 2025-26; NHTI's six free credits for the Class of 2026; **derived** counts from the Class of 2025 college-choice list; and a **derived** comparison of MVHS with the 15 other New Hampshire public high schools of 600 to 900 students on graduation, college enrollment and the state's College and Career Readiness rating.
