# North Hampton School: Early Grades and Families

Part of the North Hampton research (town/North Hampton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers North Hampton School (NHS, preschool to grade 8) as young children and their families meet it: before- and after-school care (run by the town's Parks and Recreation department in the school), the school day and PickUp Patrol, bus routes and ride times on Route 111 (Atlantic Avenue and Exeter Road), lower-grade class sizes and reading screening, the preschool's design and how families sign up, the kindergarten cutoff and full-day kindergarten, iPads and screen time in the early grades, recess and PE, enrollment papers for a guardian, discreet free and reduced-price meals, ESOL, ParentSquare and the other systems families use, and what the school says about welcoming families of all backgrounds.

**Counts:** 13 topics, 69 facts, 2 number tables (both derived). Eleven topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; one fact each is added to the library's own topics `kindergarten-entry-cutoff` and `kindergarten-day-length`. No new topic is proposed.

**Main sources:** the NHS website (home, At a Glance, New Parents, bus routes, preschool, student services, Title I, ESOL, Child Find, technology, technology and privacy, nutrition, PE, families, curriculum night), the 2025-26 Student & Family Handbook (still the linked handbook on November 21, 2026), the 2026-27 bus route sheet, the 2024–2027 technology plan, wellness policy JLCF, the 2026-27 free and reduced-price meal letter and the 2025-26 lunchroom policies, the 2025-26 school improvement plan, curriculum and assessment reports to the board (November 2024, April 2025), 2026-27 curriculum night slides (preschool, kindergarten, grades 2, 4 and 6), the spring 2026 state test schedule, the district's 2025-26 annual report, SAU 21's staff directory, student services, McKinney-Vento and meal application pages, North Hampton Parks and Recreation's town and RecDesk pages and flyers, NHDOT's 2022 North Hampton road map, and the site's database (class size, English learners, enrollment, licensed child care).

**Main gaps:** the district's entrance-age policy (JEB) and any early-entrance rule (SAU 21's BoardDocs answered "No Access" to the policy list; Simbli was blocked in earlier research); preschool tuition, eligibility and waiting-list rules (the handbook says to call); how often ESOL instruction happens at NHS and who provides it; minutes of PE per week and of device use per day; October 1, 2026 enrollment by grade; the 2026-27 handbook and staff list; whether either licensed child care program in town meets or drops at the school bus.
