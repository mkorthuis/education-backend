# North Hampton Residents' Questions

The questions the North Hampton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

North Hampton is in SAU 21. The North Hampton School District runs North Hampton School (kindergarten to grade 8), and its high school students attend Winnacunnet High School in Hampton as a member of the Winnacunnet Cooperative School District (with Hampton, Hampton Falls and Seabrook), where they may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. Several questions contain premises (about $34,000 per student, about 270 students, a 3.2% cost-of-living raise, an 85-acre campus, a pre-K lottery, tiny classes of 30) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Sarah & Mike Brennan (Late 30s, dual-income commuters to Portsmouth, parents of a 1st and 4th grader at NHS)

- **P1.3** Are class sizes in the lower grades small enough that teachers actually notice if a kid is quietly falling behind in reading?
