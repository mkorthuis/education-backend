# North Hampton: Money, Taxes, Governance and the School Campus

Part of the North Hampton research (town/North Hampton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 66 facts and 4 number tables on the North Hampton School District's budget, what its cost per student covers, the default budget, how North Hampton's school taxes split between North Hampton School, Winnacunnet and SAU 21, staff pay and contracts, the 85-acre campus and its upkeep, senior exemptions, sharing between town and school, school taxes against Rye, New Castle and Stratham, the budget documents, tracking results, the legal routes for leaving Winnacunnet, and home prices. Winnacunnet's own budget, drivers and cost-share table are in `town/Hampton/money-and-governance.md`, and SAU 21's withdrawal study and Joint Board votes are in `town/Seabrook/money-and-governance.md`; they are cited, not repeated.
- **The budget.** The 2026-27 operating budget is $10,567,387, $14,962 (0.14%) less than 2025-26; it passed 489–163. Cuts of 1.0 teacher, 1.5 educational associates, half a speech assistant and part of the curriculum coordinator, and lower out-of-district special education tuition, offset contract raises, a 3.2% raise for non-union staff and a 17.8% rise in the health insurance line. From 2020-21 to 2025-26 the budget rose 13.5% while North Hampton School's enrollment fell 15% (320 to 272) (**derived**).
- **Cost per student.** The ballot printed $34,105.89 for 2024-25. The district's presentation says North Hampton ranks 21st of 161 districts in spending per student but 184th of 259 towns in school tax effort, because its property value per student is about 3.5 times the state average. The figure jumped 13.3% in one year (FY2023 to FY2024) when enrollment fell from 303 to 270 (**derived**).
- **Where the school tax goes.** In tax year 2025, 30.2% of North Hampton's local school tax went to Winnacunnet and 69.8% to North Hampton School (**derived**, site data). SAU 21's central office costs North Hampton $311,217 directly and about $151,000 through Winnacunnet, about 3.1% of its estimated 2026-27 school taxes (**derived**).
- **The default budget.** For 2026-27 the default was $74,364 below the proposal, mostly in buildings, transportation, grounds and the food service transfer. In 2024-25 and 2025-26 the default was higher than the proposal. The budget has passed every year from 2023 to 2026.
- **The campus.** The North Hampton School parcel at 201 Atlantic Avenue is 85.26 acres, owned by the town, with a building listed as built in 1948. Buildings and grounds are 7.5% of the 2026-27 budget (**derived**); repairs come as yearly articles ($230,756 for a 30-year-old gym HVAC unit in 2026). There is no school debt.
- **Seniors.** The elderly exemption is $170,000, $225,000 or $275,000 off assessed value by age, with income limits raised in March 2025 to $50,000 single and $65,000 married; 56 owners had it in 2026.
