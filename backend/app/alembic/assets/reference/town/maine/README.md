## Fryeburg Academy facts in the Jackson files

The Jackson research gathered Fryeburg Academy facts before this folder existed. On November 13, 2026 the Jackson files were folded into the shared topics: the 11 facts about the academy itself now carry `place: maine Fryeburg Academy`, and Maine's definitions behind its data sit under `maine-school-rules` with no place. Facts about Jackson's own agreement stay `district Jackson`. The two Jackson tables of Fryeburg data (`jackson_fryeburg_graduation_rate`, `jackson_fryeburg_chronic_absenteeism`) keep their names. `fryeburg-academy.md` ends with the list, now in the new topic names and numbers.
