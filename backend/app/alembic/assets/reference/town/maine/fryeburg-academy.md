# Fryeburg Academy: Programs, Admission, Costs, Support and Outcomes

Part of the Chatham research (town/Chatham). Gathered November 13, 2026. Shared with the Jackson and Hart's Location research: facts about the academy itself use `place: maine Fryeburg Academy`.

## Summary

This file covers Fryeburg Academy (FA), the private academy in Fryeburg, Maine, where Chatham's town-tuitioned students attend grades 9–12: day and boarding students, admission for Chatham residents and homeschoolers, AP, honors and STEM against Kennett High School, career and technical education, the arts, world languages, outdoor programs, counseling and substance prevention, fees, graduation requirements, credit transfer and dual enrollment, and where to find outcome data. It reuses the academy's 2026-27 school profile saved in Jackson's raw folder and does not repeat the Jackson facts (listed at the end).

- **11 fact topics (10 shared, 1 proposed), 117 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/fryeburg-academy/` (about 29 MB): the academy's web pages, its 2026-27 Parent/Student & Athletic Handbook (Google Docs text export), the 2026-27 Course of Study (36 page images from Issuu with an OCR text file, `fa-course-of-study-2026-27.ocr.txt`), the 2026-27 bell schedule (image) and calendar, staff directory pages filtered by department, Maine statutes (Title 20-A §§2901, 2902, 2951, 2953, 4722, 5806), Maine DOE's 60% publicly funded enrollment file, the Maine CTE center list, Lake Region High School's vocational page, CCSNH's Early College contact list, Maine's Aspirations eligibility rules, NCES's private school record and MSAD 72's FY27 annual report.
- **Places:** 105 facts use `maine Fryeburg Academy`, 2 use `district Chatham` (how New Hampshire's and Maine's dual enrollment rules apply to a Chatham student), 1 uses `maine MSAD 72`, and 9 have no place (Maine law and Maine's early college rules, under the proposed topic).
- **New data concepts:**
  - Maine DOE's count of FA students by who pays (60.9% paid by a Maine school unit in October 2024), which puts FA on Maine's "60% publicly funded" list and under Maine's testing and curriculum rules for such schools.
  - NCES Private School Survey counts for FA (654 students by grade, 59.5 teachers, 2023-24).
  - A derived side-by-side of FA's and Kennett's AP, STEM, arts and world language course lists.
  - FA's posted fees for day students, lab fees and flight-hour costs.
  - Maine's Aspirations early college rules, which exclude non-residents of Maine, and CCSNH's listing of FA among White Mountains Community College's Early College high schools.
  - FA's transfer-credit, homeschool-entry and alternative-program rules (Pequawket Valley Alternative School, Arrow).
- **Proposed new shared topic:** `maine-tuition-academy-rules` (kind rule, question_type rules, no place): "Maine's laws and state programs for private academies that take publicly funded students: approval, state testing, curriculum and diploma standards, records, tuition limits and early college eligibility."
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Large international boarding population" (P11.1): partly right. FA's 2026-27 profile counts 205 international students (29%) and 220 boarders, but 484 of 704 students (69%, derived) are day students.
  - Lake Region Vocational Center in Naples (P7.1): correct. FA is one of its three sending high schools, with priority for 11th and 12th graders. FA's own Course of Study calls it "The Lake Region program in Bridgton, ME"; the center's listed address is in Naples.
  - Region 9 in Mexico, Maine (P7.1): not an option found. Its sending schools are Dirigo, Mountain Valley and Telstar.
  - Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center in Conway (P7.1): it serves Kennett students only, and no arrangement for FA students was found.
  - Running Start at White Mountains Community College (P17.1): CCSNH now calls it "Early College At Your High School." CCSNH does list FA among the high schools WMCC's Early College coordinator serves, but FA's 2026-27 course lists name only Central Maine Community College (CMCC) for dual enrollment.
  - "Just submit the town residency letter, or is there an interview" (P19.3): the residency letter is right. FA's day-student page asks Chatham residents for a letter from the Chatham town office. FA's general commuter-day process includes an interview, but the page does not say whether Chatham residents go through it.
  - MSAD 72 seventh graders starting a world language early (P14.3): MSAD 72 reports world language electives at FA for 8th graders only. Nothing was found for 7th graders.
  - Maine's or New Hampshire's graduation requirements (P17.3): neither directly. FA students follow FA's own 21-credit requirements, set within Maine's rules for approved schools.
- **Main gaps:**
  - Who pays AP exam fees, lab fees, the $500 security deposit and the CMCC course rate for a town-tuitioned Chatham student. Chatham's contract is agent 4's area.
  - Whether Lake Region Vocational Center is free to New Hampshire residents attending FA, and how students travel there.
  - Any registered or pre-apprenticeship tied to FA.
  - FA's Maine state test results. Maine's assessment dashboard blocked automated access.
  - Any outcome data split by day and boarding students; FA does not publish it.
  - The course-selection timeline for incoming 9th graders.
