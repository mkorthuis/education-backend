# Newport High School and the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center in Newport

Part of the Newport research (town/Newport). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Newport High School (grades 9–12, in Newport Middle High School) and the Newport campus of the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center (SRVRTC-Newport). Topics:
- the center's programs and the grades they start in, including the introductory courses freshmen can take;
- the renovation, enrollment and transportation;
- the end of Newport's nursing assistant (LNA) course, and the new Public Safety program;
- graduation credits and pathways, AP, honors, early college and online courses;
- the end of in-person world languages and of driver's education;
- counseling, FAFSA and scholarships;
- sports and the football co-op;
- accreditation and the 2026-27 staff cuts;
- internships, Career Day and work permits;
- what Ruger and Latva Machine ask of new hires, and apprenticeships;
- Colby-Sawyer's credit rules.

**Sources:**
- From the district's document library: the 2026-27 handbook (approved August 13, 2026), the 2026-27 program of studies (approved March 26, 2026), and board policies IKF, IHCD and IHBH.
- Board minutes from July 2025 to September 2026, and the SRVRTC principal's October 15, 2026 budget slides. The library was listed by the main session (raw/browser/sau43-documents-index.tsv); its files download from the district's file host.
- Federal and state data: the College Board's AP Course Ledger; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (2021-22, 2023-24); the Community College System of NH's early college contacts and 2024-25 report; NH DOE's April 2025 capital budget presentation; the NHIAA's 2026-27 handbook.
- Other sources: Stevens High School's 2026-27 program of studies; the Region 10 CTE agreement; Newport High's 2022-23 handbook; Ruger's and Latva's job pages; ApprenticeshipNH; Colby-Sawyer's registrar pages; and Valley News reports.
