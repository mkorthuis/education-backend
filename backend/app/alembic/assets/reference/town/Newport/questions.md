# Newport Residents' Questions

The questions the Newport research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Newport is SAU 43. The Newport School District runs Richards Elementary School (preschool to grade 5), Newport Middle School (grades 6–8) and Newport Middle High School (grades 9–12) on one campus, plus the small SOAR alternative high school. Career programs are at the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center, which has a Newport campus. Several questions contain premises (a $15.4 million career center renovation, tuition students from Croydon or Goshen, an LNA license from Health Science, about 900 students, frequent teacher and principal turnover) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Machine Operator at Sturm, Ruger & Co. (Dad of a 4th Grader & 9th Grader)
Works early manufacturing shifts off Route 11/103 and wants practical, job-ready options for his kids.

- **P1.1** My shift starts at 6:00 a.m.—is there any early morning drop-off at Richards School so I'm not scrambling before work?
- **P1.2** With the $15.4 million tech center renovation, can my freshman actually get into welding or machining right away, or is that only for juniors and seniors?
- **P1.3** Are local shops like Ruger or Latva Machine hiring kids straight out of the SRVRTC programs, or do they still need a two-year degree first?

## P2. First-Time Kindergarten Mom (Moving Up from Local Daycare)
