# Newport: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Newport, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the tenth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Newport is SAU 43, a one-district SAU:
- **Richards Elementary** serves preschool to grade 5.
- **Newport Middle (6–8) and Newport Middle High (9–12)** share one building, under one principal since January 2026; SOAR runs alternative programs.
- **Career programs:** the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center's Newport campus, renovated for $15.4 million.

The district votes by official ballot. The tech center's Claremont campus was researched in `../Claremont/`. SAU 43's website shows a bot check to automated requests, so the agents worked from district documents on its file host, the job board, board packets and Valley News reports.

## Checks

- **Parser:** all four files pass the backend's loader, run against the loaded towns plus Newport. All 11 tables are linked to a topic.
- **Coverage:** 59 of 60 questions have facts: 36 full, 23 partial. One has none (P18.1, a gifted program at Richards; none was found). P18.2 (Algebra I in grade 8) is partial: no course was found, but policy IK allows high school credit for advanced work.
- **Topics:** no new topics.
- **Places:** `district Newport` 337, `town Newport` 21, `district Newport Montessori School` 3, statewide 3, `district Cornerstone Chartered Public School` 1, `district Granite Hill School` 1, `town Claremont` 1.

- **Quotes:** 971 of 1,116 quoted passages found word for word in the raw copies.
- **Sources:** many budget figures and vote counts come from Valley News reports, because the district site and several state pages refused automated requests; each note says so.
