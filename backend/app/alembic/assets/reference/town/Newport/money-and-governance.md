# Money and Governance: The Tech Center Renovation, the Default Budget, Turnover, Tuition Towns and Taxes

Part of the Newport research (town/Newport). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers the Newport School District's money and governance: the $15.4 million renovation of the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center (SRVRTC) in Newport and how it was paid for, the 2026-27 budget that failed and the default budget now in force, the cuts made to reach it, budget votes since 2022, why cost per pupil has risen (including special education costs), state aid and school taxes, superintendent, principal and teacher turnover, tuition students from Croydon and Goshen, bonds, repairs, the proposed closing of the Towle building, savings funds, classroom supplies, DonorsChoose and the PTO, who uses the tech center, and how residents can take part. Main sources: the district's own documents from its document library (the 2026 deliberative session minutes and clerk's ballot results, the 2025 warrant, the 2026 default budget form and tax estimates, the January 2026 budget presentations and October 2025 department presentations, the 2025-2026 annual report, the 2023-24 audit, the trust fund report, the Towle letter and School Board and committee minutes from November 2025 to October 2026); the school board and superintendent letters in the Town of Newport's annual reports (2021–2025) and its assessing pages; NH DOE's capital budget and school building aid handouts; the architect's project page; the Goshen School District's notices; DonorsChoose; and Valley News reports (2019–2026), now used mainly for earlier years and context.

Counts: 10 topics, 114 facts, 7 number tables. Updated November 22, 2026 with the district's documents: every earlier fact keeps its number (several were corrected in place, such as the official vote counts and the audited tech center bond), and new facts were added at the end of each topic.

Main gaps: a teacher turnover rate (none published); the final 2026-27 staffing and class list under the default budget; the EEI energy audit results; the tax year 2026 rate; the terms of the 2026 Goshen agreement and whether Croydon students still attend; a PTO meeting schedule; scanned district documents not transcribed (the 674-page 2027-28 budget book, the 2026 warrant and MS-26 scans). Web search was refused partway through the first pass (shared search budget used up).
