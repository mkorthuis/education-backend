# Mount Lebanon, Hanover Street and Young Families

Part of the Lebanon research (town/Lebanon). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Lebanon School District's two elementary schools, Mount Lebanon Elementary School (MLS, preschool to grade 4, West Lebanon) and Hanover Street School (HSS, kindergarten to grade 4, Lebanon), and what young families ask about them: the Beginnings Preschool lottery, tuition and waiting list; the move from preschool to kindergarten; school hours, morning supervision and after-school care (Immersion Montessori School at both schools, the CCBA Afterschool Program, Lebanon Recreation); storm delays and closings; the two attendance areas; enrichment and the absence of a gifted program; English learner services, translation and interpreting; meal prices, applications and the meal charge policy; the kindergarten day; 4th-grade activities and their costs; homework; the reading curriculum, reading screening (including dyslexia) and Title I reading help; and visiting classrooms. It also has the 2026-27 bus runs for the elementary schools and Lebanon Middle School.
