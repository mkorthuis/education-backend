# Lebanon Residents' Questions

The questions the Lebanon research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Lebanon is a city and SAU 88, a one-district SAU. The Lebanon School District runs Mount Lebanon School (preschool to grade 4) and Hanover Street School (K–4), Lebanon Middle School (grades 5–8) and Lebanon High School (grades 9–12), which takes tuition students from Plainfield, Grantham and other towns. Lebanon High students may take career programs at the Hartford Area Career and Technology Center in Vermont (check). Ledyard Charter School and River Valley Community College are in Lebanon. Several questions contain premises (a 2026 tax rate of $22.16, a school tax cap, a district preschool at Mount Lebanon, free full-day kindergarten) that the research should check rather than assume.
