# Lebanon: Money, Taxes, Tuition Students, Buildings and Governance

Part of the Lebanon research (town/Lebanon). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 101 facts and 9 number tables on how the Lebanon School District (SAU 88) budget, the city's tax rate and the city's own tax cap fit together, why the budget rises while enrollment grows slowly, the Grantham and Plainfield tuition students, the district's bonds, capital plan and buildings, central office costs, and where residents can speak on the budget.
- **Taxes:** the 2026 tax rate set November 2026 ($22.16, of which $12.83 or 57.9% is school taxes, **derived**), the district's own rate projection, how the FY27 levy was built (MS-26), fund balance used to lower the rate, the 2025 revaluation, the elderly exemption, and school impact fees.
- **Tax caps:** Question 2 (the state school cap) failed in Lebanon, so it does not bind the school district; the city's separate charter tax cap, passed March 10, 2026 (1,155 to 854), limits only the city's own levy, starting with the 2028 city budget. The district's "two budgets" were a needs-based and a cap-compliant proposal prepared before the November vote.
- **Budget and enrollment:** the FY27 budget ($59,131,960, passed 1,463 to 622), its drivers (health insurance up 19.6%, special education over $12.8 million), the December 2025 list of possible cuts, the FY28 proposal ($60,830,240, +5.6%), and the district's enrollment counts (1,589 in 2020-21 to 1,699 in 2026-27).
- **Tuition students:** the Grantham–Lebanon–Plainfield AREA agreement (terms, tuition formula and cap, special education, seats on Lebanon committees), ten years of enrollments, rates and payments, 37 other tuition students, and the share of Lebanon High students who live outside the city (212 of 607 in 2024-25).
- **Buildings:** outstanding bonds ($19,869,900 at June 30, 2025; the middle school bond ends 2032, the renovation bond 2043), the payment schedule, the capital improvement plan (no bonds in FY28; $1.4 million of bond-funded projects pencilled for FY2031), capital reserves and recent repairs.
- **Central office and the classroom:** the SAU's own budget (4.99% of proposed FY28 appropriations), administrator salaries, and a **derived** DOE-25 table: 70.3% of Lebanon's school-level spending went to instruction in 2024-25 (state 63.2%), administration 9.5% (state 10.8%).
- **Speaking up:** the FY28 budget calendar, board meetings, Zoom and recordings, public comment, committees and the city's own budget hearings.

**Main sources:** SAU 88 budget pages, the 2025-26 annual report, the 2026 warrant, MS-26 and explanations, the 2027-28 budget presentations and summaries (Google Slides and Docs linked from sau88.net), the AREA agreement, the 2025 demographic study, the FY2025 audit, the capital improvement plan, board minutes (September 23, October 14 and 28, 2026), the City of Lebanon's tax, budget, assessing and election pages, the March 10, 2026 charter amendment and results, and the site's database (DOE-25 lines, read-only).
