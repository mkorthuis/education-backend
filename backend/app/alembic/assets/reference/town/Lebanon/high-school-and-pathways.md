# Lebanon High School and Pathways: The Hartford Career Center, College Courses, Outcomes and Adult Education

Part of the Lebanon research (town/Lebanon). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Lebanon High School (LHS) and the routes out of it: the Hartford Area Career and Technology Center (HACTC) in White River Junction, Vermont, where LHS juniors and seniors take half-day career programs (how they get there, credits, sports, costs); LHS's AP, honors and Early College courses; college courses through River Valley Community College (RVCC), eStart, VLACS and Dartmouth's free Community High School Program; counseling, FAFSA and college help; published outcomes (AP results, college-going, acceptances, the U.S. News ranking the district cites); sports and whether families pay to play; supports for struggling students; and adult education, HiSET preparation and RVCC's evening, online and hybrid classes for adults.

- **8 fact topics, 72 facts and 3 number tables.** All eight topics are shared town topics: `cte-center-programs`, `district-transportation`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `high-school-outcomes`, `district-student-support`, `district-fees`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics` and `adult-education-and-workforce-training`. Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 12 MB): LHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies, Student and Parent Handbook and Athletic and Co-Curricular Handbook (Google Doc text), the 2025-26 school profile, the HACTC letter to LHS students (August 11, 2026), LHS web pages, LHS's November 2025 budget detail for 2026-27, HACTC's 2026-27 handbook, application guide and program sheets, RVCC's pages and Spring 2027 schedule, Lebanon Adult Education's pages, NH Adult Education's program list and Dartmouth's program page. PDFs were read with macOS PDFKit (scripts in the session scratchpad).
- **Places:** 62 facts use `district Lebanon` (LHS, its budget, and HACTC as Lebanon's career center), 10 use `town Lebanon` (RVCC's Lebanon center and classes, Lebanon Adult Education, HACTC's adult courses). No facts are about Hanover, Claremont or the other towns researched this wave.
- **Place type to consider:** HACTC is in Vermont, which the site's tables don't have. Its facts here use `district Lebanon` and name the center in each statement. A `vermont <name>` place like the existing `maine <name>` would let Lebanon, Hanover and other Upper Valley towns share them.
