# Winchester: Money, Taxes, the High School Agreements and School Governance

Part of the Winchester research (town/Winchester). Gathered November 22, 2026; updated the same day with the Winchester School District's own documents (its wnhsd.org document library, listed by the main session's browser pass).

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 66 facts and 2 number tables on how the Winchester School District (SAU 94) is paid for and governed: Winchester's side of its 2026–2033 high school agreement with Hinsdale and its 2026–2031 agreement with Pioneer Valley Regional (terms, cost formula, special education billing, exits, the 2027-28 rate), where the "$500,000 savings" figure came from, Keene's 2026 refusal to stay a choice school, the FY2026 and FY2027 budgets and default budgets and their votes, where the money goes (high school tuition, private special education placements, special programs, classroom pay), teacher and support staff contracts, substitute pay, the 2025 revaluation and why the local school tax rose, state aid, Question 2, the gym's roof, floor and asbestos work, capital reserves and the end of the district's bond, enrollment counts from the minutes, the shared superintendent and staff with Hinsdale, teacher retention against Keene and Monadnock Regional (Swanzey), and how residents can follow, watch and speak at board meetings.

- **The Hinsdale deal:** seven years (July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2033), then ten more, but from June 30, 2033 either district may end it with two years' notice; it can be renegotiated if Hinsdale High loses state approval or NEASC accreditation. The rate was $17,852 for 2026-27 and is $18,780.68 for 2027-28 (+5.2%, **derived**), set at the September 30, 2026 joint board meeting. Special education is billed at actual cost.
- **Savings:** the superintendent told the April 3, 2025 public hearing the district expected "close to a half-million dollar savings to Winchester residents" over four years, comparing Keene's flat special education rate ($33,616) with Hinsdale's billing by need; the board chair said at the deliberative session she couldn't quantify it. No written calculation was posted. Savings phase in: 73 students were still finishing at Keene in December 2025, and private special education placements (about $1.18 million budgeted for high schoolers in 2024-25) aren't affected by the contract.
- **Why taxes rose:** the 2025 revaluation cut the local school rate from $14.60 to $11.11, but the local school tax raised rose from $4,935,028 to $6,851,312. The FY2025 audit shows $1,693,759 of leftover fund balance was used to cut the 2024 school assessment; about $922,000 was returned the next year, about $771,500 less (**derived**).
- **Budget:** FY2027's $13,026,669 passed 349–311; the default was $21,196 lower. FY2026's default differed from the prior budget mainly in Keene tuition and health and dental insurance lines. In FY2026's budget, special programs were 29.2% and regular programs (including high school tuition) 35.6% (**derived**).
- **Buildings:** no bonds were outstanding at June 30, 2025 (the 2003 bond's last payment was in FY2024). The gym roof was modified in summer 2025, its floor resurfaced in summer 2026, and asbestos insulation cleaned up for about $12,000 in January 2026; the facilities manager expects an elementary roof replacement (up to $250,000) within five years. The building reserve held $436,954.50 in June 2025.
- **Pay and staffing:** teachers start at $49,000 (bachelor's, 2026-27) and paraprofessionals at $17.00 an hour; para substitutes earn $16.25 an hour. The board reported "a high retention rate" in 2024-25; federal data show 76.9% of 2023-24 teachers returning, below Keene's 91.3% and Monadnock's 82.4% (**derived**).
- **Meetings:** the board meets monthly at 6 p.m. in the Winchester School library, with a Microsoft Teams livestream link on its agendas since May 2026, some recordings on YouTube, and minutes posted in its online document library (2026 minutes through October 7 by November 22).

**Main sources:** the Hinsdale and Pioneer Valley agreements; Winchester School Board minutes and agendas (2025–2026, including the April 2025 hearing and deliberative session on the agreement, the 2026 deliberative session and the September 30, 2026 joint meeting with Hinsdale); the FY2026 budget (MS-27, default budget MS-DSB and Proposed Budget #1); the FY2025 audit; the WTA (teachers) 2025–2028 and WSSA (support staff) 2024–2027 agreements; board policies BEA, BEDH and GCG; the SAU 94 FY2028 budget notice; Winchester budget committee and selectboard minutes and agendas; the March 10 and November 3, 2026 ballots and tallies; Town Hall Streams; Hinsdale School Board minutes; MyKeeneNow; the federal CRDC 2023-24; NH DRA tax rate data; and the site's database.

1. The Winchester School District's high school tuition agreement with the Hinsdale School District runs seven years, from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2033, and then extends for ten more years, with either district able to end it, from June 30, 2033 on, by giving two years' written notice; students already enrolled may finish at Hinsdale High.
