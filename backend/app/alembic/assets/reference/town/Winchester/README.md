# Winchester: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Winchester, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the ninth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Winchester is SAU 94, which shares its superintendent and staff with Hinsdale:
- **Winchester School** serves preschool to grade 8.
- **High school:** Hinsdale High is the anchor school from the class of 2030, under a 2026–2033 agreement; up to 20% of each class may choose another school (Keene, Monadnock or Pioneer Valley), by lottery if more ask. Keene High is closed to new Winchester students; the classes of 2027–2029 finish there.

The district votes by official ballot. Hinsdale's research (`../Hinsdale/`) has the agreement from Hinsdale's side; Keene's is in `../Keene/`.
