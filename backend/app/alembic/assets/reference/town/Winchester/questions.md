# Winchester Residents' Questions

The questions the Winchester research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Winchester is SAU 94. The Winchester School District runs Winchester School (preschool to grade 8). Its high school students attended Keene High until Keene ended the agreement; from fall 2026 Hinsdale High is the anchor high school, with Monadnock Regional, Pioneer Valley Regional (Massachusetts) and Franklin County Technical (Massachusetts) as choices for up to 20%. Several questions contain premises ($500,000 in savings from the Hinsdale contract, a lottery for the 20%, a Keene bus ending early, an exit from CSI status, a gym bond) that the research should check rather than assume.
