## What you need to know about Seabrook

- **Seabrook** is a seacoast town in Rockingham County on the Massachusetts line, with Seabrook Beach, Route 1 and 1A, and Seabrook Station, the nuclear plant, which pays much of the town's property tax. Site town id 485.
- **Two school districts serve Seabrook, both in SAU 21:**
  - **The Seabrook School District** (site district id 485), preschool to grade 8, on Walton Road: Seabrook Elementary (20810), preschool to grade 4, 302 students in 2025-26; and Seabrook Middle School (27995), grades 5–8, 277 students.
  - **The Winnacunnet Cooperative School District** (site district id 581): Winnacunnet High School in Hampton, for Hampton, Hampton Falls, North Hampton and Seabrook (members) and South Hampton (tuition).
- **Already researched:** Winnacunnet is researched in the shared folder `town/winnacunnet/` (`winnacunnet-high-school.md`, 107 facts). Hampton's `money-and-governance.md` has Winnacunnet's budget, warrant and each town's share. Exeter's `cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md` has the Seacoast School of Technology: its programs, sessions and enrollment by sending school, 79 Winnacunnet students in 2026-27. **Read these first** and cite them. Write only what they lack, such as Seabrook's own students, buses and votes.
- **Starting pages:** SAU 21, https://www.sau21.nh.gov (Seabrook's schools, boards, budgets, policies); Seabrook's schools' pages there; Winnacunnet, https://www.winnacunnet.org (it refuses automated requests; list it as blocked); the town, https://www.seabrooknh.info (tax rate, assessing, exemptions, recreation, the library).
- **Site figures:**
  - Resident public students: 1,016 (2014-15), 958 (2019-20), 807 (2026-27).
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Seabrook $30,121, Winnacunnet $28,650 (P2.1 says "almost $29,000"), Hampton $28,051, state $22,699.
  - 2025 tax rates per $1,000: total $12.40, local school $5.20, state education $1.16, municipal $5.38.
  - Question 2: Seabrook 70.1% yes; Winnacunnet 61.7% across its towns, so the cap is adopted for Winnacunnet (unofficial). Check Seabrook's own district.
  - Math proficiency, 2026: Seabrook 35% (district; P7.1 says "about a third"), Winnacunnet 44% (P9.1 says 33%), state 44%.
  - The site also has enrollment, scores, class sizes, staff, salaries, meals, English learners, safety, graduation and plans, child care, EFAs, Census estimates, adequacy aid, special education aid and taxes (with the cooperative share). Point to these.
- **Saved pages:** `additional-data/raw/districts/*/seabrook-*`: kindergarten information night, Seabrook Elementary's 2026-27 handbook, registration.
