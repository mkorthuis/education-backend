# Winnacunnet and the Seacoast School of Technology for Seabrook Students

Part of the Seabrook research (town/Seabrook). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file adds what Seabrook families need about Winnacunnet High School (WHS, Hampton) and the Seacoast School of Technology (SST, Exeter) beyond the shared research in `town/winnacunnet/winnacunnet-high-school.md` (WHS programs, counseling, fees, phones, outcomes) and `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md` (SST's programs, sessions, admission, certifications and enrollment by sending school). It covers the 2026-27 WHS bus routes through Seabrook and their times, the SST bus and the late bus, WHS's rules on bullying, fighting, vaping and drugs and when parents are told, the trimester schedule and how much room it leaves for electives, "Running Start" and Great Bay's Early College prices, the full class-rank point table and how dual-credit courses are weighted, the social studies and civics courses every student takes, ninth-grade shop courses, SST health science and work placements, Winnacunnet's share of students at SST, the Evening High School's 2026-27 schedule and rules, FAFSA help and the scholarships open only to Seabrook residents, how local businesses can host students, and where the "33% math" on the ballot came from.

- **8 fact topics, 42 facts and 2 number tables.** Every fact is placed `district Winnacunnet Cooperative` except one about Seabrook Middle School's transition calendar (`district Seabrook`). Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 19 MB).
- **Main sources:** WHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies and Student/Parent Handbook (Google Doc text exports, fetched again November 21); the WHS "Bus Routes 2026" spreadsheet (Google Sheets, created August 10, 2026, linked from the WHS home page); Seabrook Middle School's 2025-26 K–8 bus routes (used only to tell which WHS routes run on Seabrook streets); the WHS College and Career Services site and its 2024-25 scholarship list; the WHS ELO site and its Community Partner FAQ; the Winnacunnet Evening High School site and 2026-27 calendar; the Winnacunnet 2025-26 annual report and FY27 budget presentation; SAU 21's K-12 School Counseling Plan; Great Bay Community College's Early College page and participating-schools list; SST's October 2026 report and bell schedules (copied from the Exeter research); one OpenStreetMap route.
- **New data concepts:** WHS bus times on the four routes through Seabrook (table winnacunnet_seabrook_bus_runs_2026_27, **derived** by matching street names); the road distance WHS–SST; the full WHS rank point table (table winnacunnet_rank_grade_points); how many credits the trimester schedule allows against the diploma (**derived**); Winnacunnet's share of students at SST against the region (**derived**); Seabrook-only scholarships; the Evening High School's attendance and nightly schedule; how an employer becomes an ELO host.
