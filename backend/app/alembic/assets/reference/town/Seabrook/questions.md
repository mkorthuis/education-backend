# Seabrook Residents' Questions

The questions the Seabrook research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Seabrook is in SAU 21. The Seabrook School District runs Seabrook Elementary School (preschool to grade 4) and Seabrook Middle School (grades 5–8) on Walton Road; its high school students attend Winnacunnet High School in Hampton, run by the Winnacunnet Cooperative School District, of which Seabrook is a member with Hampton, Hampton Falls and North Hampton. Winnacunnet students may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. Both districts vote by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (an 8:00 to 2:30 elementary day, almost $29,000 a pupil at Winnacunnet, nuclear plant taxes cushioning the budget, Seabrook Elementary's online grades, about a third of middle schoolers proficient in math, 33% math proficiency at Winnacunnet, a 280-student middle school, a Winnacunnet Evening Program, a jump in middle school growth scores in 2024, open enrollment) that the research should check rather than assume.
