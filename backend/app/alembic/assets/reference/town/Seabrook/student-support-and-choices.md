# Student Support and Choices in Seabrook

Part of the Seabrook research (town/Seabrook). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers support for Seabrook students and the choices open to Seabrook families: which school a child attends after a move within town and how bus stops are assigned; storm closings, delays and make-up days; checking grades and missing work on a phone through PowerSchool and ParentSquare; summer camps and summer school; asking for an IEP or 504 plan at Seabrook Elementary and Middle, who to call about bus bullying, and the counselors and social workers in the two schools; the move from Seabrook Middle to Winnacunnet with an IEP or 504 plan; homeschooled students in Seabrook Middle School sports, band and clubs; EFA use by Seabrook families and nearby private schools; and attending Hampton or Exeter schools under today's law.

The Seabrook School District has one school for each grade span, Seabrook Elementary (preschool to grade 4) and Seabrook Middle School (grades 5–8), both at 256 Walton Road, so a family that moves from one Seabrook address to another keeps the same school; Seabrook's high school students attend Winnacunnet High School in Hampton.
