# Seabrook Elementary and Seabrook Middle School

Part of the Seabrook research (town/Seabrook). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Seabrook School District's two schools on Walton Road as families meet them: the school day and after-school care (the Seabrook Adventure Zone and the Recreation Department's program), breakfast and meal applications, buses for siblings, the preschool, kindergarten registration and class sizes, how reading and math help works, what changed in the middle school around the 2024 jump in growth, accelerated math (compressed 7/8 math and Algebra I), phones and Chromebooks, competency-based report cards, language help for Spanish-speaking families, and how Seabrook's eighth graders compare with the other Winnacunnet towns.

- **14 topics, 67 facts, 1 number table.** All topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`, plus the library's existing `kindergarten-registration`; no new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/k8/` (about 69 MB, mostly the district's three annual reports, the two middle school handbooks and the Recreation Department's brochure).
- **Sources:** the SES 2026-27 handbook (the library's copy); the SMS 2026-27 and 2025-26 handbooks; the Seabrook School District annual reports for 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26 (principals' reports, the board chair's report, the February 2026 deliberative session minutes, staff lists and SAU 21 enrollment counts); the district's 2025-26 Title I plan; the curriculum coordinator's monthly board reports (September 2025 to September 2026); SMS math syllabi; SAU 21's grading documents, meal application pages and Child Find notice; the 2026-27 bus routes; the Seabrook Recreation Department's MyRec pages and youth brochure; SAZ's 2024-25 registration form (archived); NH DOE's 21st CCLC list (the library's copy); GreatSchools; and the site's database (scores, growth, class size, child care). - **Main gaps:** SAZ's current hours and any fee (its website is down; the 2024-25 form lists none); whether a late bus means a missed breakfast; preschool eligibility, tuition and application dates; 2027-28 kindergarten registration dates; fall 2026 kindergarten enrollment; how students are chosen for compressed math; any Spanish-speaking staff member or interpreter by name; Niche's and Homes.com's letter grades (both return HTTP 403); SAU 21 board minutes and policies (Simbli bot check, as the Winnacunnet research found).
