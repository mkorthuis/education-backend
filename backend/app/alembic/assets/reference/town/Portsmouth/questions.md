# Portsmouth Residents' Questions

The questions the Portsmouth research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Portsmouth is a city and SAU 52, a one-district SAU (the Portsmouth School Department). It runs the Portsmouth Early Education Program (PEEP) preschool at Little Harbour, three elementary schools for kindergarten to grade 5 (Little Harbour, Mary C. Dondero and New Franklin), Portsmouth Middle School (grades 6–8), and Portsmouth High School (9–12) with its Career Technical Education Center and the Robert J. Lister Academy. Greenland, New Castle, Newington and Rye (SAU 50) send their high school students to Portsmouth High, and New Castle and Newington send middle school students too. The school budget is set within the city budget, which the City Council adopts. Several questions contain premises (elementary redistricting, "Little Clipper" preschool, budget increases "by millions", free lunch for everyone, a local tax cap and override, "random Wednesdays off", a high school bus before 8 a.m., per-pupil spending far above the state's, Rye and Newington's share of the cost, a state ban on DEI programs) that the research should check rather than assume.
