# Getting to School, Choices, Community and Staff in Portsmouth

Part of the Portsmouth research (town/Portsmouth). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Portsmouth students get to school (the 2026-27 bus routes from Student Transportation of America, the Elwyn Park stops, pickup times before the 8:35 a.m. middle and high school start), the school calendar and why it has Wednesday early-release days, the 2016 decision that moved the high school start from 7:30 to after 8:25, the enrollment outlook (NESDEC's November 2025 ten-year projection and the board's housing and enrollment review), private school choices in Portsmouth (Saint Patrick Academy) and what national surveys say about why parents choose schools, students moving from private or charter schools into Portsmouth High, where military members on the Seacoast live, and working for the district: the teachers' and paraeducators' contracts, substitute pay, the job postings open on November 16, 2026, and where people who hold public jobs in Portsmouth live.

No shared topic covers parents' stated reasons for choosing private or chosen schools; `research-private-school-choice` covers effects on results, not reasons.
- **Main gaps:** no on-time or late-bus data for Portsmouth's routes (the bus app's live tracking was paused); no current Coast Guard housing information (uscg.mil pages refused automated requests); no data on where Portsmouth's teachers live beyond a union president's estimate in the news, and no teacher retention or turnover figures (the district's staffing-trends report is due in December 2026); Saint Patrick Academy posts no tuition; no source says where Saint Patrick Academy or Seacoast Charter graduates go for high school, or why Portsmouth families choose private schools.
