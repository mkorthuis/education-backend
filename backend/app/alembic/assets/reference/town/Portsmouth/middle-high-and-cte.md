# Portsmouth Middle School, Portsmouth High School, the CTE Center and Lister Academy

Part of the Portsmouth research (town/Portsmouth). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Portsmouth Middle School (PMS, grades 6–8), Portsmouth High School (PHS, grades 9–12), the PHS Career Technical Education (CTE) Center and the Robert J. Lister Academy (RJLA), Portsmouth's small alternative high school. It answers the questions on trades programs and shipyard hiring, the move from elementary to middle school and from middle to high school, math grouping, AP and dual enrollment, phones, Great Bay Community College classes for credit, financial literacy and work readiness, hands-on learning and internships, Lister Academy, varsity tryouts, and robotics and coding.
