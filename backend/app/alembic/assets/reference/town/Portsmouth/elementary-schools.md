# Portsmouth Elementary Schools, PEEP and the Early Years

Part of the Portsmouth research (town/Portsmouth). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Portsmouth's three elementary schools (Little Harbour, Mary C. Dondero and New Franklin, kindergarten to grade 5) and the years around them: the 2025 elementary redistricting plan and its "transition neighborhoods," the new enrollment-report policy, the school day and dismissal, after-school care (the city's ACE program, New Franklin's MC3, the PEAK programs), summer programs, the kindergarten day, reading instruction and intervention (and the 2026-27 literacy pilot), Title I, special education for preschoolers, PEEP and the separate Little Clipper Preschool at Portsmouth High, art, music, PE and recess, volunteering and the three parent groups, classroom staffing, and what military families arriving mid-year can expect.

- **15 topics, 85 facts, 2 number tables.** All topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md` or existing library slugs (`kindergarten-day-length`, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff`, `district-preschool`, `military-children-compact`, `military-families-nh`); no new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 20 MB, with School Board files in `raw/elementary/board/`).
- **Sources:** the Redistricting Committee's and the elementary redistricting Google Sites, the committee's January 28, 2025 report and street list, School Board minutes (January 2025; March–November 2026) and committee minutes, Policies JF, JCA, JFAA, IJOC, IMAH, JLCF, IHCA and KI, the New Franklin 2025-26 handbook, the Dondero 2024-25 handbook, Little Harbour's online parent handbook, 2025-26 daily schedule and 2026-27 staff directory, the district's school-hours, student-services, Child Find, ESY, Title I, PEEP and Little Clipper pages, the kindergarten learning-targets sheet, the 2026-27 Dondero bus routes, the City Recreation Department's ACE, youth-program and summer-camp pages, the MC3 site, the three parent groups' sites, the library's summer reading page, RSA 110-D:3, Military OneSource, and the site's own database (class sizes, enrollment, child care).
- **Main gaps:** which "transition neighborhood" each listed street belongs to (the posted street list doesn't say); ACE's monthly tuition (not on its page); Camp FunStuff's 2026 weekly price; Seacoast Community School's PEAK page (bot check); Dondero's 2025-26 handbook and daily schedule (only 2024-25 is posted); the Title I plans and the district's 2026 annual report (Google sign-in); how long reading intervention sessions last and how many children get them; the size of PEEP's and Little Clipper's waitlists; whether a JF capacity report was presented in 2026; and any Portsmouth-specific program for military-connected students (the 2021 Purple Star list is in the library, but the NH DOE release returned HTTP 403).

| P1.1 | "Elementary redistricting" may move a New Franklin street "before next fall" | Partly right, and partly out of date. The School Board adopted a redistricting plan on January 28, 2025. | P10.2 | A four-year-old needing speech therapy can enroll in "the Little Clipper preschool program" | Two different programs are mixed up. Little Clipper is a tuition preschool inside Portsmouth High School, run with the high school's Careers in Education program: up to 18 children, $4,770 a year, no special education services mentioned. Speech therapy for a preschooler with a disability comes through the district's preschool special education program (PEEP), free of charge, after a referral and an evaluation. PEEP's paid "peer" seats are only for children who are not on an IEP. |
| P1.3 | Kindergarten may be "pushing screens and testing" | Partly. There are no state tests until grade 3. Devices (iPads, Chromebooks, robots) are used in Little Harbour's library and lab; how much screen time kindergartners get wasn't found. |
| P14.1 | Art, music and outdoor time at Dondero may be "squeezed out for test prep" | Not supported by what's posted. Dondero's handbook gives every class art, music, PE and library once a week, and recess outdoors whenever possible. No source measures time spent on test preparation. |
| P18.2 | Teachers may be "stretched thin" by class sizes | The site's data shows 2025-26 elementary averages from 12.5 to 21.2 students per class, below the state's limits. The 2025 plan aimed for about 17 per class. How stretched teachers feel isn't something the data can show. |
| P1.2 | The school may not have "enough spots for working parents" | Partly right. The city's ACE after-school program at the Community Campus was full on November 16, 2026, with a waitlist. On the site's November 13, 2026 child care snapshot, the PEAK programs at Dondero and Little Harbour reported "Accepting Children on Wait List." |
