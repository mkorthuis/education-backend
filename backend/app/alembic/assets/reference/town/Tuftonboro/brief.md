## What you need to know about Tuftonboro

- **Tuftonboro** is a town in Carroll County on the east shore of Lake Winnipesaukee, with Melvin Village, Center Tuftonboro, Mirror Lake, Neck Road and Tuftonboro Corner. Huggins Hospital is in Wolfeboro. Site town id 537.
- **The Governor Wentworth Regional School District** (site district id 208) is **SAU 49**, shared with Brookfield, Effingham, New Durham, Ossipee and Wolfeboro. It runs Tuftonboro Central School (22410; kindergarten to grade 6; 99 students in 2024-25), Carpenter School (K–2) and Crescent Lake School (3–6) in Wolfeboro, Effingham Elementary, Ossipee Central, New Durham Elementary, and Kingswood Regional Middle (22440; 7–8) and High (22425; 9–12) in Wolfeboro, with the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC). Check how costs are split (the formula is 75% students and 25% property value per Wolfeboro's research), how waterfront values change a town's share, and how many seats Tuftonboro holds.
