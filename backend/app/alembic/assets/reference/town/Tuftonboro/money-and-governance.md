# Money and Governance: Tuftonboro's School Taxes, the Governor Wentworth District's Budget and Who Decides

Part of the Tuftonboro research (town/Tuftonboro). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 11 topics, 49 facts and 5 number tables on what Tuftonboro pays the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49) and why: the 2025 revaluation and its effect on the school rate, the district's projection for 2026-27, 13 years of Tuftonboro's school taxes against its student count, where the state education tax goes, why Tuftonboro's payment per student is higher than Ossipee's or Effingham's, the school tax cap voters approved in November 2026, who may vote, Tuftonboro's school board seat and how to follow candidates, the central office's budget lines, Tuftonboro Central's buildings and repairs, snow-day decisions and notices, how far the school year runs into June, and what a young adult's local pay and rents look like. District-wide facts are placed with the district; Wolfeboro's money file (town/Wolfeboro/money-and-governance.md) already has the district's budget, drivers, bonds, votes and policies, so this file cites it and adds Tuftonboro's side.

**Main sources:** GWRSD's Annual Meeting page (the FY2025, FY2026 and FY2027 "Financial Reports" handouts with the Distribution of School Costs sheets read from page images, the 2026 warrant and final results, the January 31, 2026 deliberative presentation, the 2026 annual report, the 2025 Report Card and the mandatory report to voters); board and committee minutes (July 2024 to October 2026); policies EBCD, EBCD-R and IMBA; the district's ParentSquare, asbestos, SAU office and School Board pages; the Tuftonboro Central School handbook; RSA 75:8-a, 72:38-a, 72:39-a and 72:39-b, 198:57, 654:1 and 671:19; NH Housing's 2024 Rental Cost Survey; BLS wage data (May 2025); and read-only queries of the site's tax, enrollment, adequacy aid, Census and Question 2 data.

**Main findings:**
- **The revaluation is already in the bill.** Tuftonboro's assessed values rose 30.1% in 2025 and the combined school rate fell from $4.37 to $3.46 per $1,000, but the school dollars barely moved (+2.8%; the district's pre-revaluation estimate was within 0.12% of the actual). For 2026-27 the district projects Tuftonboro's school taxes up 8.68% to $9,213,021, a rate of $3.72 (up $0.27). No later revaluation date was found; values jumped in 2019, 2022 and 2025.
- **Fewer children, higher taxes.** From tax year 2014 to 2025 Tuftonboro's school taxes rose 45.4% while its resident students fell 17.0% (**derived**). The district's average daily membership count for Tuftonboro fell 30.3% from 2005 to 2024. The district's total cost rose 15.9% between the 2024-25 and 2026-27 handouts, while Tuftonboro's share of it slipped from 14.46% to 13.71%.
- **Highest payment per student, lowest rate.** Tuftonboro's projected payment is $42,099 per student against $26,114 for Ossipee and $18,602 for Effingham, but its school rate is the lowest of the six towns. The formula gives 25% of costs to property value (Tuftonboro has 21.9% of the value and 11.0% of the students), and Ossipee and Effingham receive adequacy grants while Tuftonboro receives none.
- **Both school lines go to the same district.** The state education tax raised in Tuftonboro ($2.5 million projected) is subtracted from what Tuftonboro owes the district; it is about twice the cost the state calculates for an adequate education for Tuftonboro's students.
- **School tax cap.** Question 2 passed across the six towns with 65.2% (unofficial), so a cap on the local school tax levy and a 6% cap on central office spending apply from the 2027-28 budget.
- **Board seat:** the Tuftonboro seat expires in 2027 and is on the March 9, 2027 ballot; filing opens January 20, 2027 (**derived**).
- **School year:** the last student day was June 18, 2025, June 17, 2026, and is June 18, 2027 (all half days).

**Main gaps:** Tuftonboro's own website (HTTP 403) and the UNH copy of its town report (HTTP 403 on the PDF) were not retrievable, so the town's elderly exemption amounts and limits, its revaluation notice, its tax year 2026 rate and its road agent's plowing practice are missing. No candidate forum or candidate statements were found for the school board seat. The district does not publish actual snow-day notice times, a building condition study, or the tax effect of each warrant article.
