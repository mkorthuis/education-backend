# Tuftonboro Residents' Questions

The questions the Tuftonboro research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Tuftonboro's students attend Tuftonboro Central School (kindergarten to grade 6) in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49), then Kingswood Regional Middle and High Schools in Wolfeboro, with the Lakes Region Technology Center joined to Kingswood High; Brewster Academy (private) is in Wolfeboro. Several questions contain premises (a coming revaluation, waterfront owners paying more per student than Ossipee or Effingham, a school year running into mid-June, a quiet sensory space at Tuftonboro Central, a Kingswood marine tech or welding class for sophomores, snow days across six towns, SAU 49's growing administrative costs) that the research should check rather than assume.

- **P1.3** How are the bus routes out in Melvin Village—are my kids going to be on the bus for an hour each way?
