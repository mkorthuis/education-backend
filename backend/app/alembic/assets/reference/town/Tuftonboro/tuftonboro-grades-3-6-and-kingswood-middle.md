# Tuftonboro Central Grades 3–6, Kingswood Middle and Choices

Part of the Tuftonboro research (town/Tuftonboro). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Tuftonboro Central School's upper grades (3–6), the move to Kingswood Regional Middle School (KRMS, grades 7–8, Wolfeboro) in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, SAU 49), and the supports and choices around them: test results against the state and larger districts, devices and internet on remote days, the late bus and drive times, vaping and bullying, phones, counselors, special education (aides, an autism partner program, out-of-district placements, bus driver training), homeschool and Education Freedom Account (EFA) access, and lake and outdoor learning.

- **13 topics, 61 facts, 5 tables.** Twelve topics use shared slugs. One is a proposed new shared topic, `school-vaping-and-tobacco-rules` (kind rule, question type rules; see the end of this summary). District facts are placed at `district Governor Wentworth Regional`; Tuftonboro's broadband, EFA listings and counts and drive times are `town Tuftonboro`; the state rules (bus driver training, vaping) have no place.
- **Main sources:** Tuftonboro Central's 2026-27 handbook, guidance page, staff list, activity pages and calendar; KRMS's 2026-27 handbook (copied from the Wolfeboro research), calendar and Envirothon page; Governor Wentworth's special education pages (programs, personnel, referral, parental rights), home education and Drug and Alcohol Resources pages, the October 1, 2025 enrollment sheet, the 2026-27 required trainings sheet, the Chromebook agreement and the job board of November 22, 2026; policies IHBA, IMBA, JICH, JICK, JJJ and JJJ-R; the Finance Committee notes of November 19, 2025, the FY2027 new staff requests, the January 31, 2026 deliberative slides and excerpts of the 2026 annual report; the KRHS 2026-27 Program of Studies; NH's school bus rules (Saf-C 1300) and youth tobacco law (RSA 126-K); NH DOE's fall 2025 school designations; federal Civil Rights Data Collection counts; the library's EFA files; NH Broadband's town list and a Laconia Daily Sun report; the Lake Winnipesaukee Alliance's pages; the site's own database (test results, growth, meals, bullying, discipline, restraint and seclusion); and derived drive times (OpenStreetMap).
- **Main findings:**
  - **Test results:** In spring 2026 Tuftonboro Central's tested students were 49% proficient in reading and 42% in math (state, all tested grades: 55% and 44%), down from 62% and 49% in 2025. Each grade has only 10 to 25 tested students, so one child moves a grade by 4 to 10 points: fourth and fifth graders were below the state in both subjects in 2026 (math 33% and 27% against 52% and 43%), third and sixth graders at or above it. Kingswood Middle's seventh and eighth graders (58% and 59% in reading, 41% and 44% in math) were at or above the state except in seventh-grade math, and above Manchester, Nashua, Rochester, Laconia and Concord in every grade and subject. Kingswood High's juniors were 57% proficient in reading and writing and 20% in math on the SAT in 2026, against 62% and 30% statewide.
  - **The jump:** Tuftonboro sent 20 seventh graders and 16 eighth graders to KRMS in October 2025, about 12% of its 168 seventh graders; the Tuftonboro Central sixth grade had 16 students in October 2024 (derived: a move from a grade of about 16 to one of 168). KRMS held a 6th Grade Parent/Guardian Information Night on March 31, 2026; Tuftonboro Central's own calendar lists no sixth-grade visit.
  - **Late bus and drive times:** The KRMS late bus (Monday to Thursday, 5 PM) goes to a "central location" in each town; no page names Tuftonboro's stop. A model drive to KRMS is 8.5 miles and 17 minutes from Tuftonboro Central, 11.7 miles and 24 minutes from Melvin Village (derived).
  - **Vaping and bullying:** The KRMS handbook bans vaping and gives "immediate suspension and possible expulsion" for vape paraphernalia on school property. No KRMS vaping count exists; the state's drug incident count for KRMS was 13 in 2024-25 (11 the year before), and its bullying reports were 2. At Tuftonboro Central the state counts are 0 for drugs and alcohol and 0 bullying reports in 2024-25.
  - **Counselors:** Tuftonboro Central has one full-time counselor for 110 students (derived 110 to 1; the state's limit is 500 to 1), 0.1 psychologist and no social worker (federal data), plus a district-wide K–6 outreach counselor.
  - **Special education:** The district's programs page lists a regular class with supports, a resource room and a self-contained class "available in all district schools". Tuftonboro Central has one special education teacher and a diagnostic prescriptive teacher shared with Effingham, whose time at Tuftonboro Central the 2026-27 budget raised from 30% to 45%. No sensory room or quiet sensory space is mentioned anywhere read. Ossipee Central posts 1:1 aide jobs for students with autism "placed in a Partner Program" supervised by the New England Center for Children (10 miles, 32 minutes from Tuftonboro Central by model drive).
  - **Bus driver training:** State rules require 10 hours of pre-service training and 8 hours a year, with a required topic of "transportation issues involving special needs students."
  - **Homeschool and EFA:** No yearly paperwork goes to the district since July 1, 2026, apart from a written notice when withdrawing a child from a Governor Wentworth school. Policy JJJ gives non-enrolled students equal access to classes, sports and band by written request to the principal. Tuftonboro had 16 EFA students in 2026-27, and the EFA marketplace lists six Tuftonboro providers, four of them summer camps.
  - **Lake and outdoor programs:** Tuftonboro Central has a Hiking Club for grades 4–6; KRMS has an Envirothon club (aquatic ecology among five topics); KRHS teaches Environmental Science (wetland ecology, freshwater resources, outdoor fieldwork). No school lake-ecology program on Winnipesaukee was found; the Lake Winnipesaukee Alliance's Floating Classroom is a public program that says local schools can't yet bring students onto the lake.
- **Main gaps:** KRMS vaping figures (none published), bus routes, pickup times and the Tuftonboro late-bus stop; whether Tuftonboro Central has a sensory space; how many of the district's out-of-district placements are contested (NH DOE's complaint page refused automated requests); how Tuftonboro children do after the move to KRMS (no data); Massachusetts' own test results (not retrieved); the town's own site (403).
