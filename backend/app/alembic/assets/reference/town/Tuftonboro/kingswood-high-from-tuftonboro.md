# Kingswood High and the Lakes Region Technology Center From Tuftonboro

Part of the Tuftonboro research (town/Tuftonboro). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Tuftonboro's high school students and recent graduates can do at Kingswood Regional High School (KRHS, grades 9–12, site school id 22425) and the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC) in Wolfeboro, both run by the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (GWRSD, site district id 208, SAU 49), and how that compares with Brewster Academy (site district id 1008, a private day and boarding school in Wolfeboro). Wolfeboro's `town/Wolfeboro/kingswood-high-and-lrtc.md` and Ossipee's `town/Ossipee/kingswood-from-ossipee.md` already hold the bell schedule, LRTC program list and priority rules, AP and Early College lists, graduation credits, work-credit and early-graduation rules, local scholarships and Brewster's aid; this file cites those facts and adds Tuftonboro's side.

- **13 fact topics, 35 facts and 6 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 13 MB): the KRHS 2026-27 and 2025-26 Programs of Studies, the 2026-27 Student/Family Handbook (scanned; OCR text), School Profile and Counseling Center pages (enrolling, transferring out, college planning); the LRTC 2026-27 handbook and program pages; the district's strategic plan (2024 text with a name table removed, 2025 and 2026 updates), data page, October 1, 2025 enrollment sheet, 2026 annual report (OCR text only; a student's name removed), FY2027 budget form and new staff requests, two board minutes (a student's name removed) and policies IHCD and IIB; Brewster Academy's about, admissions, application, FAQ, college counseling, day student, tuition, Curvey Scholar, local impact and six department pages; GreatSchools' Kingswood page; Lakes Region Community College's Marine Maintenance page; the National Marine Manufacturers Association's copy of ABYC's entry-level certification announcement and ABYC's student page; the College Board's 2025 AP honor roll; and three read-only query extracts from the site's database.
- **Places:** 27 facts use `district Governor Wentworth Regional`, 6 `district Brewster Academy`, 1 `town Laconia` (Lakes Region Community College) and 1 has no place (ABYC's credential).
- **Main findings:**
  - **Welding is not an LRTC program.** Marine Technology and Automotive Technology are open to sophomores (grades 10–11) once related courses are done, but welding is taught inside other courses: Kingswood's Metal Working Technology I (grades 9–12), LRTC's Collision Technology I and, as an introduction, Automotive Technology. Marine Technology II is for grades 11–12 and, like Level II programs, can't be taken in grade 10 (cte-center-programs 1–2).
  - **No seat counts exist.** No source gives seats, waitlists or students turned away by LRTC program. Students request courses each spring, LRTC places second-year students first and ranks oversubscribed courses by grades in related courses, attendance and GPA (cte-center-programs 3).
  - **A trade program fits a 26-credit diploma.** For the Classes of 2027–2029 the named subjects add to 16½ credits and 9½ are electives, a category that names LRTC programs; a two-year LRTC program is 6 credits. A Level II program takes two of four daily blocks, and the required load is seven credits a year, so a half-day program fits without summer school, which Kingswood describes only as a way to repeat a failed course (school-programs-after-grade-6 1–2).
  - **Boat yards:** the catalog says Marine Technology students can move into work-based learning "as an intern, apprentice etc." with partners "such as Goodhue Boat Company," and Marine Technology II's second semester includes a work placement with a local business. Counts of placements, pay and hours aren't published (cte-center-programs 5).
  - **Scores and reputation:** Kingswood's grade 11 results (the SAT since 2016) were below the state's share proficient in 8 of 10 years in math and 9 of 10 in reading; in 2026 the share proficient was 20% in math (state 30%) and 57% in reading and writing (state 62%). GreatSchools gives Kingswood 7 of 10, built only from a college readiness rating that draws on 2018 and 2021 federal data and the 2024 graduation rate (high-school-outcomes 3, school-rating-sites 1; tables kingswood_grade_11_math_proficient_2016_2026, kingswood_grade_11_reading_proficient_2016_2026).
  - **Tuftonboro's students at Kingswood:** on October 1, 2025 the district counted 70 Tuftonboro students at Kingswood High (17, 17, 21 and 15 by grade), almost the same as the 71 Tuftonboro residents in grades 9–12 in NH DOE's town count (derived). No source says how many Tuftonboro students attend Brewster, other private schools or homeschool (district-classrooms-and-staff 1; table tuftonboro_students_at_gwrsd_schools_oct1_2025).
  - **Brewster:** about 350 students from 29 states and 23 countries, 85% boarders and 15% day students, a 6:1 student/faculty ratio, 15 AP course entries (derived count, against Kingswood's 7 AP subjects), and no posted count of where graduates enroll. It was founded in 1820 as "Wolfeborough and Tuftonborough Academy" (town-school-options-nearby 1–4, high-school-outcomes 1–2).
  - **Graduate tracking and job help:** no source shows the district following graduates two years out. LRTC has a Work Based Learning/Career Counselor and coordinator, community partners, advisory committees and career fairs, but no public job board was found (high-school-outcomes 4, district-volunteers-and-partners 1–2).
