# Pelham Elementary School and the Early Years

Part of the Pelham research (town/Pelham). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Pelham School District's preschool to grade 5 years at Pelham Elementary School (PES) from a family's side: why all three schools sit on Marsh Road and what is known about drop-off and pickup traffic, school hours, the car line and PickUp Patrol, before- and after-school care, bus times against sunrise, the preschool (hours, tuition, who it serves), full-day kindergarten, kindergarten and first-grade class sizes, how children are picked for reading help (the third reading specialist, Title I, Walk to Learn, Acadience and i-Ready), the IEP process and special education staff, summer programs and summer reading, recess, unified arts (art, music, STEAM), school meals and food allergies, and building security.

- **20 topics, 71 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 20 MB): the PES 2026-27 Family Handbook; the PES morning and afternoon bus route sheets (updated September 25, 2026); the district's 2026 facility maintenance plan and safety plan; 21 sets of public school board minutes from September 2024 to November 2026 (current ones from pelhamsd.org, earlier ones from the district's public Google Drive folder); the Title I pages, brochure, FAQ and 2023-24 plan; Child Find and PES Student Services pages; the FY19 Special Services Executive Summary; menus, meal prices and the special dietary form; policies IMAH, KI, ECA and form JLCD-F3; grade-level schedule pages; the kindergarten supply list and parent slides; 11 cited pages of the Town of Pelham 2025 Annual Report and 4 pages of the 2024 Master Plan (with full-text extractions of both); the Pelham Public Library's reading challenge pages; the town's 2026 summer camp page; and four U.S. Naval Observatory sunrise files.

| P15.3 | Visitors are buzzed in and show ID | Partly. Doors are locked; visitors ring a buzzer, give their name and reason, sign in and wear a badge. The handbook requires ID to dismiss a student early, but neither the handbook's visitor rules nor Policy KI says visitors must show ID. |

No source measures test preparation time. |
