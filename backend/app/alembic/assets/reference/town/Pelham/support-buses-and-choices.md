# Support, Buses and Choices in Pelham

Part of the Pelham research (town/Pelham). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Pelham High students get into the career and technical education (CTE) programs at Pinkerton Academy, Alvirne High School's Palmer Center and Salem High, and who pays and buses; out-of-district special education placements; school buses, late-bus contacts and storm closings; how the schools reach families who speak Spanish or Portuguese; English learner (ESOL) services for students who arrive mid-year; free and reduced-price meals and their privacy rules; bullying, counseling and 504 plans at Pelham Memorial and Pelham High; the phone rules at both schools; the paperwork a grandparent raising a child needs; homeschooled students in Pelham classes, sports and band; credit for homeschool work on return to Pelham High; and working papers.

- **10 topics, 61 facts and 2 number tables.** Every fact uses `place: district Pelham`.
