# Pelham Residents' Questions

The questions the Pelham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Pelham is SAU 28, a one-district SAU. The Pelham School District runs Pelham Elementary School (preschool to grade 5), Pelham Memorial School (grades 6–8) and Pelham High School (grades 9–12), all on Marsh Road. Pelham High students may take career programs at Salem High, Alvirne High School's Palmer Center or Pinkerton Academy. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (all three schools on Marsh Road, about two-thirds of the tax bill going to schools, middle school math proficiency around 30%, a 40-hour community service requirement, a renovated Pelham Memorial, a reading specialist added at Pelham Elementary, a $400,000 special education capital reserve request, the state's new school tax cap) that the research should check rather than assume.
