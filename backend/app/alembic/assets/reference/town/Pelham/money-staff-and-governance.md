# Pelham: Money, Staff and Governance (SAU 28)

Part of the Pelham research (town/Pelham). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers the Pelham School District's (SAU 28) budget and what drives it: the FY2027 budget and its default, the FY2028 proposal as it stood after the Budget Committee's November 12, 2026 review, health insurance, contracts, the custodial contract, staff reductions by attrition and the district's own enrollment counts; the 2026 tax rate after the town-wide reassessment and the school share of it; what the school tax cap (Question 2, HB 1300), which Pelham adopted, means locally, using the district's own October 21, 2026 hearing figures; SAU 28's central office costs under the HB 1300 and HB 564 definitions; the special education capital reserve and the other reserves, bonds and fund balance; the teachers' and instructional assistants' contracts, staff retention, substitutes and coverage; and the district's own performance and readiness measures.

**8 topics, 73 facts and 8 tables.** All topics are shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`. 71 facts use `district Pelham`; 2 (the town notice's tax example and the town's credits and exemptions) use `town Pelham`. Raw copies are in `raw/money/` (about 12 MB): the March 2026 warrant, ballot results, default budget forms, deliberative slides and minutes; the FY2027 and FY2028 budget books (narrative pages 1–19 only, without the line-item pages that list employees by name); the district's HB 1300 hearing slides; nine sets of 2026–27 board minutes; an excerpt of the November 18, 2026 board packet (agenda, SAU budget memo and summaries, performance memo, Level 4 total, draft default budget and draft 2027 warrant); the teachers' (PEA) and support staff (PESPA) contracts; and the town's 2026 tax notice, assessor and credits pages and the November 2026 election tally.

**Main findings:**
- The school share of Pelham's tax rate is about 65% in 2026 ($8.43 local school plus $0.96 state education of $14.41), as in 2025. The total rate fell 24.5% after the first town-wide reassessment in five years; the town says a property's taxes fall unless its assessment rose more than about 32.5%.
- Pelham adopted the school tax cap and the SAU cap: 4,680 yes to 1,871 no (71.4%, derived) on the town's posted tally. Both begin with the FY2028 budget voted in March 2027. The district's model says its levy would have been about $834,600 over a cap in FY2026, $26,103 in FY2025 and an estimated $99,382 in FY2027; the FY2028 ceiling can't be calculated until spring 2027.
- SAU 28's qualifying central office costs are 4.71% of the FY2028 base by the district's HB 1300 count ($2,116,863 against a $2,698,670 limit). The SAU 28 location budget is $1,544,395 (about 3.3% of the general fund, derived); the broader HB 564 SAU budget is $3,465,182.
- Net teaching positions fell 11.1 from 2023–24 to 2027–28 (projected), mostly by leaving vacancies unfilled; the superintendent said the FY2028 reductions involve no layoffs and that further cuts aren't expected if grades hold at about 100–120 students. Instructional assistants are being added, not cut, in the FY2028 proposal; 16.1 custodial positions moved to a contract.
- The special education capital reserve held $232,109 on June 30, 2025; voters approved adding up to $168,000 (1,383 to 747) to bring it to about $400,000, the district's figure for an average residential placement. The board did not ask for $400,000 itself.
- Staff retention was 88% overall in 2026: 93% for teachers but 72% for instructional assistants. Pelham's starting assistant pay ($17.51 in 2026–27) is below Windham's ($21.00) and Salem's posted $19.56.

**Main gaps:** bus drivers' pay (busing is contracted to Student Transportation of America; the only Pelham driver postings found were on job boards, one blocked); the final FY2028 default budget, which the Budget Committee sets after November 18; the teachers' contract now in negotiation; any district list of what would be cut under a default budget or a cap shortfall; why individual staff leave (no exit-survey results are published); the DRA's 2026 tax rate calculation (dollar amounts); any study of school budget votes and Pelham property values.
