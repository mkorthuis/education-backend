# Mountain View, Goffstown High School and MST

Part of the Goffstown research (town/Goffstown). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Mountain View Middle School (grades 5–8) and Goffstown High School (grades 9–12) for the questions about course levels, math placement, college results, the Manchester School of Technology (MST), credits, personal finance and trade courses, sports costs and eligibility, Early College, college and financial aid counseling, mid-year placement, class sizes, help for students not headed to a four-year college, and what graduates can still use.

- **9 fact topics, 76 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 37 MB): GHS's two 2026-27 Programs of Studies (classes of 2027–2029 and class of 2030, with an OCR of the career pathway pages), the 2026-27 school profile (with OCR of its image pages), the 2026-27 student handbook, the athletics handbook (2019, still posted), the October 2026 "Understanding SAT Benchmarks" handout, the 2026-27 parking contract, the GAP's page and fall 2026 schedule, the counseling, counselor, transcript, Information Center, bell schedule and winter sports pages, the mid-year parking sale sheet and GAP payment instructions; Mountain View's 2026-27 handbook, grade 6 report card guide, home page and registration page; 14 Goffstown School Board policies (IHAH, IHBH, IHCD, IIB, IK, IKF, IKFG, IMBD, JFD, JG, JJ, JJIC, JQ and KJ-R) and the policy index; the FY28 budget with notes (November 8, 2026) and the FY28 presentation packet (October 5, 2026); the cited pages of nine GHS monthly newsletters (November 2025 to October 2026) with full text; SAU 19's bus routes and academics pages; the school board's "Three Things" page; and ProPublica's page for the hockey booster club.
- **Places:** every fact uses `district Goffstown`. No facts were written about MST itself; Manchester's `mst-and-pathways.md` covers it and is cited by fact number.
- **New data concepts:** GHS's course levels and the number of AP, honors and Early College courses; AP prerequisites; the class of 2030's new diplomas (Academic, Career Pathway, Core, Skills) and financial literacy credit; GHS career pathways that join GHS electives to MST in grades 11–12; Goffstown's MST transportation and tuition budget lines and the state shortfall; how many GHS students are in MST sessions; Mountain View's advanced math (Algebra I and Geometry in grades 7–8) and its projected class sizes; the board's class size guidelines; the five-course athletic eligibility rule and when eligibility is lost; what the district budget pays for in athletics (bus trips, ice time); booster club rules and the hockey booster's finances; GHS's college and financial aid calendar (Apply NH Day, Financial Aid Night, Free Money Friday); and the GAP evening program's adult diploma, life credits and tuition.
