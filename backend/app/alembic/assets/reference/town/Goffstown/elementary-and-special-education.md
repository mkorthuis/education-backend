# Glen Lake, the Elementary Schools, Special Education and Family Support in Goffstown

Part of the Goffstown research (town/Goffstown). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Goffstown's youngest students and the supports around them: Glen Lake School's half-day kindergarten and why the district keeps it, kindergarten registration timing, before- and after-school care and how full it is, the Glen Lake Preschool Program and who it serves, Bartlett Elementary (Pinardville) beside Maple Avenue (the village) on test results, low-income share, reading help and Title I, paraprofessionals and Registered Behavior Technicians, the move from grade 4 to Mountain View for students with IEPs, out-of-district special education placements and what they cost, English learner services and interpreters, school meals without stigma, school nurses, and gifted education.

- **New data concepts:** budgeted staff by building (paraprofessionals, Registered Behavior Technicians, special education providers, nurses, counselors) for FY27 and FY28; students with IEPs by school; out-of-district special education tuition by year; the YMCA's kindergarten wrap-around and school-age care rates with their wait-list status on the day checked; the district's own free and reduced-price meal shares by school since 2016; the ESOL program's service minutes by proficiency level; the state's gifted-and-talented reporting law (RSA 189:29-b).

- **Main findings:**
  - Kindergarten stays half-day. In October 2026 the assistant superintendent called Glen Lake "one of very few schools in New Hampshire" with half-day kindergarten and named the reasons: no cafeteria, no gym and too little space. The FY28 plan keeps 4 kindergarten teachers for about 130 children in 8 half-day sessions.
  - The YMCA's three wrap-around programs for Glen Lake kindergartners (morning, afternoon and after care, $65 to $202 a week) were wait-listed for every week of 2026-27 on November 16, 2026. Its before- and after-school care at Bartlett and Maple Avenue was open for registration.
  - Bartlett's lower scores are out of date. Bartlett trailed Maple Avenue through 2024, led in math but trailed slightly in reading in 2025, and led in both in 2026 (math 63% to 57%, reading 60% to 58%), with higher growth both years. NH DOE named Bartlett in its 2026 results release for rising from 34% to 63% proficient in math. Bartlett tests about 85 to 90 students to Maple's 250 or so, and its low-income share has been higher in most years.
  - The district cut its planned paraprofessional staff by 9.3 full-time equivalents for FY28 while adding Registered Behavior Technicians. Administrators said the cut came from reviewing student needs "rather than a reduction in services". Maple Avenue goes from 21.00 to 20.00 paraprofessionals. SAU 19's job board listed no Maple openings on November 16, 2026.
  - Out-of-district special education tuition ran $1.50 million to $1.84 million a year in FY24 to FY26 actual spending, and $1,659,452 is proposed for FY28. Placements include Seven Hills, Lighthouse School, Crest Collaborative, Spaulding Academy and others.
  - English learners are served by pull-out, push-in or sheltered instruction. Minimum service runs from 2 hours a day for beginners to as little as 1 hour a week for advanced students. The district provides an interpreter on request. No source lists a Spanish-speaking staff member at Bartlett.
  - Every school has its own nurse position (1.0 FTE at Glen Lake, Bartlett and Maple Avenue, 2 each at Mountain View and the high school), and the handbooks and Policy JLC say school nurses are registered nurses.
  - Goffstown has no gifted program. Its policy IHBB is one sentence from 2000, and all five schools reported no gifted program to the federal civil rights survey in 2021-22 and 2023-24. A state law, RSA 189:29-b, requires every school to file a yearly gifted-and-talented report, and a 2026 bill to repeal that duty died in the Senate.
- **Main gaps:** the Glen Lake preschool tuition for typically developing peers (not posted) and a 2026-27 preschool program description; Goffstown's own gifted-and-talented report to NH DOE (NH DOE's pages refuse automated requests); any count of how many staff speak Spanish; how many students are in out-of-district placements; written procedures for the grade 4 to grade 5 move for students with IEPs (Mountain View's transition page is a video presentation); whether paraprofessional positions are filled day to day.
