# Getting to School, Safety, Climate and Choices in Goffstown

Part of the Goffstown research (town/Goffstown). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Goffstown students get to school and through the day, and the choices and protections around it: the 2026-27 bus routes and their scheduled ride times, the kindergarten buses to Glen Lake, the late buses, bus eligibility and transportation to private schools, the 2026-27 school hours (including the eight minutes added to Mountain View's day), two-hour delays, early release and workshop days, phones, bullying, counseling and mental health support at Mountain View, guardianship and records paperwork, how elementary schools are assigned by address, and homeschooled and EFA students' access to classes, sports and the Manchester School of Technology.

- **9 topics, 87 facts, 1 number table.** Places: `district Goffstown` for the district, its schools, policies, buses and board; `town Manchester` for one Trinity High School fact. No SAU-wide (`sau 19`) facts: the policies read are Goffstown School Board policies, and SAU pages were cited only where they describe Goffstown's schools.
- **New data concepts:** scheduled ride times on all 2026-27 Goffstown route sheets (derived; table `goffstown_scheduled_bus_ride_minutes_2026_27`), the two-run bus day (high school first, then elementary and middle), the late bus times and sign-up rule, the board's 2026-27 bell-time table, the board's own statements on ride times (up to 45–60 minutes) and driver shortages, and the homeschool course limit at Goffstown High (4 courses a term).
- **Main sources:** sau19.org pages (bus routes, district calendar, school home pages, registration pages, counseling, suicide prevention, text messaging), the 2026-27 handbooks of Glen Lake, Bartlett, Maple Avenue, Mountain View and Goffstown High, the 2026-27 route sheets, Goffstown School Board policies EEA, EEA-R, EBCD, IC, IHBG, JCA, JFAA, JH, JICJ, JICK, JJIC, JJJ, JLDBB-R and JRA, school board minutes (October 2025 to October 2026) and the FY2028 budget packet, the Goffstown High 2026-27 Program of Studies, and the Granite YMCA's school-age child care pages.
