# Money, Buildings and Governance in Goffstown

Part of the Goffstown research (town/Goffstown). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered.** The 2026 tax rate ($21.75) and how much of its rise is the local school tax; how the district's budget, state aid and fund balance feed the school rate, and its projected against actual rates since 2020; elderly exemptions and veterans' credits; the November 2026 school tax cap vote in Goffstown, the district's own cap estimates for FY2028 and FY2029, the $470,417 gap it is trying to close, its stated priorities and the areas it says could be reduced; the failed 2024 local tax cap petition; budget growth against flat enrollment (staffing, special education, contracts, health insurance, the state's career-center shortfall); the 2026–2030 teachers' (GEA) contract, its pay benchmarks against Bedford, Londonderry and other districts, and falling applications for teaching jobs; SAU 19's budget, how it is split, the move of six SAU positions to district budgets, and the superintendent's retirement and named successor; the Goffstown–New Boston AREA agreement's terms (tuition formula, 2.5% rental charge, 90% floor, special education, governance, withdrawal) and New Boston's FY27 tuition; school buildings, the energy lease, direct borrowing (no bonds), the capital reserve fund and the 2026–2031 capital plan, including a $10 million lease or bond and a $70 million new elementary school listed for later years; fields and athletic facilities; building security grants, visitor rules and school resource officers; and how to follow and speak at school board meetings, curriculum decisions and the budget calendar to the March 2027 vote.

**Counts.** 11 topics (all shared), 106 facts, 4 number tables.

**Main sources.** The NH DRA 2026 tax rate breakdown (posted by the town November 5, 2026); the Goffstown School District's FY27 deliberative session slides and handout, the FY28 superintendent's budget packet (October 5, 2026) and line-item budget with notes (November 8, 2026), the October 19, 2026 HB 1300 hearing slides and minutes, and the November 9, 2026 tax cap work session slides and handout; the FY2025 audit; the GEA tentative agreement; the town's March and November 2026 election results, its 2022, 2024 and 2025 annual reports and the 2026–2031 capital improvements matrix; SAU 19's budget hearing notices and superintendent finalist announcement; New Boston's FY27 deliberative session slides; the signed 2024–2034 AREA agreement (scanned; read from page images); and district policies BEA, BEDB, BEDH, EBB, IGA, IGD, IGE, KCB, KF-R and KI.

**Main gaps.** No count of teacher turnover or of teachers leaving for other districts was found. Whether the SAU 19 board approved the new superintendent on November 10, 2026 was not posted. The CIP "project explanation" for the new elementary school was not found. Nothing describes teams competing for practice space at GHS. The School Board had not yet adopted its FY28 budget (due by November 18, 2026). Turnover, home-value effects of the schools and the final Question 2 certification remain open.
