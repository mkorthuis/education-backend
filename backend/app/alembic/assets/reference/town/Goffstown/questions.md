# Goffstown Residents' Questions

The questions the Goffstown research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Goffstown is in SAU 19 with New Boston. The Goffstown School District runs Glen Lake School (preschool and kindergarten), two elementary schools for grades 1–4 (Bartlett Elementary in Pinardville and Maple Avenue in the village), Mountain View Middle School (grades 5–8) and Goffstown High School (9–12). New Boston sends its grades 7–12 to Mountain View and Goffstown High under a 2024–2034 AREA agreement, and Hooksett students may attend Goffstown High by petition. Goffstown students take career and technical programs at the Manchester School of Technology. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (full-day kindergarten at Glen Lake, a $21.75 tax rate, a tax cap on the schools, a new superintendent, Bartlett's lower test scores, a daily bus to MST and two-year MST programs, 28 students to a room, resource officers in the elementary schools, teachers leaving for Bedford and Londonderry, a 3:30 pickup and a late bus for 5th graders) that the research should check rather than assume.
