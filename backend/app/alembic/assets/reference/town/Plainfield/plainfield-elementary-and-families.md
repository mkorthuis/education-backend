# Plainfield Elementary's Early Grades and Families

Part of the Plainfield research (town/Plainfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Plainfield Elementary School (PES; kindergarten to grade 8, Meriden) as young families meet it: preschool and child care, the kindergarten cutoff and day, before- and after-school care, buses (including Meriden Village, mud season and snow), class sizes and combined classes, school lunch, phones and screens, registering mid-year or as a guardian, internet at home, religious and dietary needs, outdoor time, the Friday ski program (FAST) and the school's ties to Kimball Union Academy (KUA).

- **22 topics, 57 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 2.5 MB): the 2026-27 student handbook (scanned PDF, transcribed from the images), the 2026-27 and 2025-26 calendars, the 2026-27 free and reduced-price meals FAQ, the grade 9-12 proof-of-residency form, the bus routes (a cleaned transcript with the house numbers removed; the original sheets are not kept), the district's page texts (registration, about, food service, homeless assistance, library curriculum), excerpts of 19 weekly newsletters (Blackhawks Bulletin, January 2024 and January to November 2026), the PTA page, excerpts of the 2025 annual town report (the full report is not kept because it lists residents by name), the preschool pages of the 2023 community survey, KUA's February 2026 newsletter, Hub66's Plainfield page, Lebanon's December 2025 winter-weather notice, three statutes, and the site-database and routing results behind the tables.
- **Topics:** shared slugs `district-preschool`, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff`, `kindergarten-registration`, `kindergarten-day-length`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-transportation`, `district-meals`, `district-fees`, `district-technology-and-phones`, `district-family-communication`, `district-student-support`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-volunteers-and-partners`, `town-child-care`, `town-broadband`, `town-recreation-and-summer`, `town-streets-and-safe-routes`, `town-community-profile`, `gov-school-transportation` (library topic, same kind and question type) and one proposed new topic, `seasonal-road-posting-rules` (see below).
- **Places:** `district Plainfield` for the school's own practices; `district Lebanon` for Lebanon's winter-weather notice about Plainfield students; `town Plainfield` for child care, broadband and roads; no place for the two state rules. KUA facts are placed with `district Plainfield` because they describe what PES does; the KUA newsletter is cited as the source for one of them.
- **Proposed new shared topic:** `seasonal-road-posting-rules` (kind rule, question type rules): how New Hampshire towns may post seasonal weight limits on class IV, V and VI roads (RSA 231:191). Added here because "mud season" questions recur in small hill towns.
- **Main gaps:** the school board's policies (JEB entrance age, JH attendance, EEA transportation and others) sit in Google Drive folders whose file lists load only in a browser, and the SAU 32 web host (www.sau32.nh.gov, named in the handbook) did not resolve, so no policy text was read; the Town of Plainfield's website (plainfieldnh.org, now plainfieldnh.gov) refused automated requests (HTTP 403), so no road-posting or mud-season notice from the Highway Department was read; no PES or SAU 32 document says what the bus does on a muddy dirt road; no source gives the price of the August and September 2026 menus beyond the $3.80 lunch price, the length of any waiting list, or how often PES closes on days Lebanon stays open; no source states a screen-time limit for the younger grades or whether school devices go home; no source says whether PES classes use KUA's fields.
