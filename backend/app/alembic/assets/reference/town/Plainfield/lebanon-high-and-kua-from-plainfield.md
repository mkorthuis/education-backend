# Lebanon High School and Kimball Union Academy From Plainfield

Part of the Plainfield research (town/Plainfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Plainfield families need to know about the high schools their children use: Lebanon High School (LHS), the assigned school under the Grantham-Lebanon-Plainfield AREA agreement, and Kimball Union Academy (KUA), the private school in Meriden. It covers the jump from Plainfield Elementary School (PES) to LHS, KUA's tuition and aid for Plainfield families, trades programs, buses and late buses, AP and college-course fees, staying informed, sports, enrolling a transfer student, home education and placement, choosing Hanover High, getting around without a car, and scholarships. Lebanon's own research (`town/Lebanon/high-school-and-pathways.md`) already covers LHS and the Hartford center, so this file writes Plainfield's side and points to Lebanon's facts.

- **16 fact topics, 53 facts and 3 number tables.** All topics are shared town topics except `home-education-rules`, which is a library topic (one rule fact the library lacked, with no place). Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 3 MB).
- **Places:** `district Plainfield` (the AREA agreement, enrollment, the district's annual report), `district Lebanon` (LHS pages, handbooks, board minutes, HACTC), `district Kimball Union Academy` (KUA's own tuition, aid and day students), `town Plainfield` (the KUA Study Committee, scholarships, public transit) and no place for state-wide programs.
- **Biggest finding:** the town's 2025 annual report says KUA "continues to consider the establishment of a Plainfield student tuition rate" that needs its trustees' approval, that the town's optional tax exemption ended in 2018, and that "essentially, all students with demonstrated financial need receive assistance," so there is no town discount today, only KUA's need-based aid (town-school-options-nearby 1).
- **Proposed new shared topic:** `town-public-transportation` (public buses, dial-a-ride and volunteer drivers near a town, with fares and which towns they reach). Public transit facts are filed under `district-transportation` with `town Plainfield` until it is added.
- **Main gaps:** the Plainfield School District website (plainfieldschool.org) showed a bot check ("Client Challenge") to automated requests on November 22, so Plainfield's own high school bus route, any late bus, its board policy on home-educated students and its registration page could not be read live (the registration page comes from the library's November 8 copy). The Route 12A morning bus time is not found. Application details for the Friends of the Philip Read Memorial Library scholarships are not found.
