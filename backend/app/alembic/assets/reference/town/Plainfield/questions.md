# Plainfield Residents' Questions

The questions the Plainfield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Plainfield's students attend Plainfield Elementary School in Meriden (kindergarten to grade 8) in SAU 32, whose office is in Meriden, and then Lebanon High School under the Grantham-Lebanon-Plainfield area agreement; Kimball Union Academy, a private boarding and day school, is in Meriden. Several questions contain premises (a September 30 kindergarten cutoff, a superintendent shared with Cornish, an HB 1300 tax cap override, the superintendent's contract extension, KUA's tax-exempt property, a Friday ski bus, a 200-student school) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Hannah Morales, 34 — Nurse at Dartmouth Health; lives near Plainfield Village with a 4-year-old and a 1st grader.

- **P1.1** Does Plainfield Elementary have any pre-K or full-day preschool option, or am I stuck driving up to Lebanon or Hanover for childcare until kindergarten?

- **P1.3** With my 7:00 AM hospital shifts, what does before- and after-school care look like at PES? Do they have an on-site program, or do parents have to carpool?
