# Plainfield Elementary Grades 4–8: Support and Choices

Part of the Plainfield research (town/Plainfield). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers Plainfield Elementary School (PES) for grades 4 to 8 and the choices around it: advanced math and science, special education on site and who runs it, services elsewhere, sharing with Cornish and the plan to combine the two districts, how a one-class-per-grade school handles friendships, test scores against Hanover and Lebanon, Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs) near Plainfield, English learners, art and music, the counselor, welcoming families of color, bullying, anxiety in a small school, Spanish and coding, the outdoors and the Friday winter program, and project-based learning.

- **13 topics, 82 facts and 3 tables.** All 13 topics use shared slugs from `shared-topics.md`: `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-student-support`, `district-conduct-policies`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-school-results-compared`, `district-goals-and-performance-reporting`, `comparing-small-schools`, `town-school-options-nearby`, `district-consolidation-history` and `district-sau-governance`. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 14 MB).
- **Places used:** `district Plainfield` (the school, board and SAU 32 staff), `sau 32` (one fact on staff shared with Cornish), `town Plainfield` (private schools and EFAs) and `district Lebanon` (Lebanon High's language placement). No general rules were written; state rules are cited from the library and earlier towns by file, topic and fact number.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Enrollment is below the "around 200" in the questions.** The principal's report counts 198 students in 2023-24, 183 in 2024-25 and 181 in 2025-26, and the district projected 169 for 2026-27. The school's own About Us page says both "approximately 215" and "200".
  - **Advanced math is an Algebra I class in grade 8, not only worksheets.** The federal 2023-24 count shows one Algebra I class in grades 7–8 with 11 students, all in grade 8, and all 11 passed. No gifted program was reported. The school also has a math specialist, a grades 5–8 math team, weekly "Thinking Playground" math sessions at recess and a daily "Academic Block" in grades 6–8 whose enrichment includes accelerated assignments and independent study.
  - **Who runs special education:** the SAU 32 Director of Student Services, a position the Plainfield and Cornish staff pages both list, as they list the same superintendent. In 2025-26, 20 PES students got speech services at school and 18 got occupational therapy; 17.1% of students had IEPs (the state's October 2024 average was 19.9%); two students were placed out of district. Days and hours per week for the speech and occupational therapists aren't published.
  - **English learners:** the 2026-27 handbook still lists an ELL teacher, but the 2026-27 budget moves English learner services (0.6 FTE) to a contracted model. The site's data shows 5 English learners in 2025-26.
  - **Test scores:** in spring 2026 PES was 53% proficient in math, 64% in reading and 44% in science across grades 3–8, against Lebanon Middle 55%, 58% and 50% and Hanover's Richmond Middle (grades 6–8) 80%, 87% and 74%. PES's own winter 2026 NWEA benchmark puts its median student at the 70th national percentile in math and the 73rd in reading. Each PES grade has only 10 to 35 tested students, so a single grade can swing: grade 6 math went 46%, 65%, 32% in 2024–2026.
  - **The Friday program is FAST, with Whaleback skiing, not a ski bus.** On four Fridays in January and February (January 22 to February 12, 2027, with February 19 as the make-up), students choose downhill skiing at Whaleback (free to $210 depending on pass, lessons and gear), swimming, rock climbing, snow sports or ice skating. Families pick students up at Whaleback by 5 p.m. Suicide Six isn't mentioned, and transportation details are in a registration form that isn't posted.
  - **Combining with Cornish:** Cornish voters rejected a grades 6–8 tuition deal with Plainfield 91 to 25 on March 7, 2026. The two districts' Cooperative Planning Team is studying one shared district and, from the superintendent's newsletters, one shared school; a March 2027 vote is planned if the boards approve the team's Phase I report.
  - **Welcoming families of color:** the federal 2023-24 count shows 19 of 198 students (9.6%, derived) who weren't White (8 Hispanic, 2 Asian, 9 two or more races) and all 24 teachers White. The school ran a diversity, equity and inclusion committee from at least 2023 to January 2025. Its annual Quaglia survey asks parents and students about belonging; the chart shows grade 6–8 students' belonging rising from about 60% to about 69% from 2024 to 2026 (read off an unlabeled chart).
- **Main gaps:**
  - The school board's policy folder (bullying policies JC and JHFE, access policies) is a Google Drive folder that loads by script and wasn't read; the handbook's own summaries are cited instead.
  - No source says how many days a week the counselor, speech-language pathologist or occupational therapist is in the building.
  - No result of the Quaglia survey beyond the unlabeled charts, no bullying-specific parent survey and no figure on how often parents say the principal follows through were found.
  - The FAST "Step 2" form with transportation details was emailed to families and isn't posted.
  - KUA's and Estabrook Christian School's EFA acceptance and Estabrook's tuition aren't posted.
- **Proposed shared topic:** none.
