# Auburn Residents' Questions

The questions the Auburn research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Auburn is in SAU 15 with Candia and Hooksett. The Auburn School District runs Auburn Village School (AVS, preschool to grade 8). Its high school students attend Pinkerton Academy in Derry under a long-term tuition contract, where they may take career programs at Pinkerton's own center. The district decides its budget by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (spending of about $15,135 per student, about 59% proficiency, Pinkerton as a 'private' academy, Auburn students split up or sent elsewhere in SAU 15, a median income over $150,000) that the research should check rather than assume.
