# Auburn: Money, the Pinkerton Contract and Governance

Part of the Auburn research (town/Auburn). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 49 facts and 7 number tables on how the Auburn School District builds, votes on and pays for its budget, what drives it, how much goes to Pinkerton Academy against Auburn Village School (AVS), the school tax cap Auburn adopted in November 2026 and the cuts proposed to meet it, the 2026 tax rate and the yearly tax effect, the Auburn–Pinkerton agreement (transcribed here for the first time) and how Auburn chose Pinkerton in 2010–11, SAU 15's cost split among Auburn, Candia and Hooksett, the SAU board's voting rules, the AVS bond, the board's meetings and votes, and home sales in Auburn and nearby Pinkerton towns.

- **Budget.** Voters approved a 2026-27 operating budget of $21,347,270 on March 10, 2026, 756 to 551. The board says its budget rose about 8.2% a year over three cycles, driven by transportation, Pinkerton tuition, special education, health insurance and contracts.
- **Where the money goes.** Of the 2026-27 general fund ($21.13 million), 29.2% is budgeted for Pinkerton (regular and special education tuition and aides) and 30.4% for all high school tuition; bus service is 6.6%, SAU 15 1.9%, the AVS bond 5.6%, and about 55.6% pays for AVS itself (**derived**).
- **Tax cap.** Auburn adopted the school tax cap (61.8%, site data). The district posted on November 12, 2026 that about $467,000 may need to be cut from the 2027-28 budget, and listed $757,570 of possible cuts, led by high school buses ($207,043), half-time preschool, the SRO, a half-time Spanish teacher and ELA materials. The superintendent brings his recommended cuts on December 8.
- **Tax rate.** The town posted its 2026 rate: $14.93 in all, with the local school rate $10.86, up $0.93 from 2025 (the warrant had estimated $1.17 for the budget and teachers' contract together).
- **Pinkerton.** The 2011 agreement (20 years from July 1, 2013, extending five years at a time) makes Auburn send all its high school students to Pinkerton, except that the board may let up to 5% go to another public high school; Pinkerton keeps at least two Auburn trustees and meets the board three times a year, but its trustees decide its budget. Auburn budgets $17,910 per student in 2026-27 for 281 students; 296 Auburn students were at Pinkerton on November 2, 2026, up from 262 in October 2024.
- **SAU 15.** In 2024-25 Auburn paid 28.45% of SAU 15's administrator salaries (Candia 13.66%, Hooksett 57.89%); Auburn's and Hooksett's SAU payments that year are in exactly that ratio (**derived**), the apportionment the Hooksett file couldn't find.
