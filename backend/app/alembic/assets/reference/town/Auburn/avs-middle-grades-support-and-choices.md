# Auburn Village School's Middle Grades: Support and Choices

Part of the Auburn research (town/Auburn). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Auburn Village School (AVS, preschool to grade 8) for the middle grades and for students who need extra support or want more: special education, paraprofessionals and reading specialists, 504 plans, English learners, shop and life skills, Chromebooks and homework, phones, sports and clubs, home-educated and EFA students, accelerated math and Algebra I, enrichment, outdoor learning, bullying, class placement requests, new students, band, theater and fees. Sources are the 2026-27 AVS Family Handbook, Auburn School Board policies, the board's August–November 2026 packets and minutes (principal's, student services', curriculum and budget reports), the FY2027-28 budget and proposed cuts, AVS's athletics, music and grade-level sites, the Tri-County League handbook, SAU 15's job board, the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24, the town's recreation newsletters and Pinkerton's freshman parent-night slides.

- **11 topics, 105 facts, 3 number tables** (auburn_avs_special_education_paras, auburn_area_algebra1_2023_24, auburn_avs_weekly_minutes_2026_27). Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 22 MB).
- **New data concepts:** budgeted versus actual district-employed and agency-contracted special education paraprofessionals; weekly instructional minutes by subject and grade; a derived comparison of 8th-grade Algebra I across Pinkerton's sending middle schools; the gap between Auburn's posted home education policies and the 2026 state law changes; the FY2027-28 proposed cuts that touch Spanish, music, drama, field trips, athletics and Chromebooks.
- **Main gaps:** NH DOE's pages (gifted-and-talented reports, special education complaints and LEA determinations) refused automated requests. No source describes how the ALPS advanced-learner tier works in practice, whether there is any accelerated math before grade 8, or a buddy or welcome program for new students. Whether IEP accommodations are followed in practice can't be told from public sources.
- **No new topics proposed.**
