# Auburn Village School: Early Grades and Families

Part of the Auburn research (town/Auburn). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Auburn Village School (AVS, preschool to grade 8) from a young family's side: before- and after-school care and early-release days, PickUp Patrol, the school day, buses and the earliest pickups, storm delays, school meals and help with costs, full-day kindergarten, the preschool (PEEP), registering and proof of residency, records from another state, recess, homework, volunteering, how teachers reach families, and building security. Test scores and class sizes are checked against the site's data.

- **15 topics, 85 facts, 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 21 MB): the 2026-27 AVS Family Handbook (Google Doc, text export), the 2026-27 calendar, four board packets and four sets of minutes (August 17 to November 10, 2026), 26 board policies, the 2023–2028 bus contract (scanned; cited passages transcribed), the K–8 storm letter, the May 2026 kindergarten information night slides, the 2026-27 PEEP handbook, the meal application and instructions, New Morning Schools' school-age page and 2025 parent handbook, AVS pages (transportation, PickUp Patrol, nutrition, registration, health office, military families, PowerSchool, Title I, activities, homework club, PEEP, teacher websites), two grade-level Google Sites, three older AVS posts, and a streets-only copy of the 2026-27 K–8 bus routes (`avs-bus-routes-2026-27-streets-only.csv`: house numbers removed; the original sheet was not saved).
- **Topics:** `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-transportation`, `district-family-communication`, `district-safety-and-security`, `district-volunteers-and-partners`, `district-meals`, `district-fees`, `district-school-results-compared`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `kindergarten-day-length`, `district-preschool`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `district-student-support` (all shared or library slugs; no new topics). Tables: `auburn_avs_class_size_2026_11_02`, `auburn_avs_bus_times_2026_27`.
- **Places:** all facts are `district Auburn` (AVS, its policies and buses, and New Morning's program inside AVS). No SAU-wide or town facts; no state-rule facts (state rules are cited from earlier files).
- **Main sources:** auburn.sau15.net, auburnschoolboard.sau15.net (policies, packets, minutes), the Family Handbook Google Doc, newmorningschools.com, sites.google.com/sau15.net grade-level sites, avspta.net, and the site's database for test scores, class size and licensed child care.
- **Main gaps:** the Town of Auburn's website (auburnnh.us) did not resolve, so nothing on town recreation or the Severance School Community Center could be read; no posted PickUp Patrol cutoff time; no posted time of day for storm announcements or whether they go out by text; no posted minutes of recess by grade or temperature for indoor recess; no published processing time for registrations.
