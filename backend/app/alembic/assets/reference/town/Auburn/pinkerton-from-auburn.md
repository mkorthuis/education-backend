# Pinkerton Academy From Auburn: Buses, Admission, IEPs, CTE and the Move to High School

Part of the Auburn research (town/Auburn). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Auburn's side of high school at Pinkerton Academy in Derry: how Auburn students get a place (no application; the 2011 agreement and policy JCB), how many attend, who runs and pays for the Pinkerton buses and whom to call, the 2026-27 routes and times (a third bus added in August 2026), storm delays, the absence of a late or activity bus and the drive to Derry, what high school busing could face under the HB 1300 tax cap, Auburn students with IEPs at Pinkerton, CTE access for sending-town students, the 8th-grade visits and events, shadow days, transfer credits from another state, AP access, and the after-school rehearsals and concerts that fall after the buses leave. Pinkerton's own programs (Freshman Academy, counselors, CTE programs, AP results, parking, special education procedures) are in the shared Pinkerton files (`town/pinkerton/`) and Hooksett's file (`town/Hooksett/high-school-choice-and-pinkerton.md`), which this file cites rather than repeats.

- **6 fact topics, 44 facts and 2 number tables.** Places: `district Auburn` 32, `district Pinkerton Academy` 11 (Pinkerton facts any sending town shares: admission without application, tuition for other towns, detention transportation notice, Electives Information Day, shadow days, transfer students, AP access, after-school arts, the Course Night special education session and CTE seat priority), `town Auburn` 1. Tables: `auburn_pinkerton_bus_times_2026_27`, `auburn_high_school_enrollment_2026_27`.
- **Main sources:** the Auburn–Pinkerton agreement (signed 2011, term from July 1, 2013; scanned, read by OCR and checked against page images) and its 2011 memorandum of understanding; Auburn policies JCB, EEA and EEAF; the 2023–2028 First Student contract (scanned, OCR); AVS's transportation page and its 2026-27 route sheet (Pinkerton tabs); SAU 15's Pinkerton storm letter; Auburn School Board minutes (December 2025 to November 2026) and the September, October and November 2026 packets (superintendent's, special education and curriculum reports, enrollment counts, the proposed 2027-28 budget); the October 20, 2026 HB 1300 information night; Auburn's special education manual (March 2025); Pinkerton's admissions, shadow day, current 8th graders and Stockbridge pages, and the Pinkerton research's saved catalog and handbook.
- **New data concepts:** Auburn's Pinkerton bus routes by bus with pickup and drop-off times; the August 2026 third bus; contract limits on ride length and lateness; Auburn high schoolers by school and grade (October and November 2026); Auburn's special education students at Pinkerton by Pinkerton program; Pinkerton's no-application rule for sending-town residents and its 2026-27 tuition for other towns; Pinkerton's shadow day; Pinkerton's rules for transfer students from outside New Hampshire; after-school and evening rehearsal patterns of Pinkerton's ensembles and the Pinkerton Players; a derived drive time from Auburn Village School to Pinkerton.
