# Pembroke Hill School and Young Families in Pembroke

Part of the Pembroke research (town/Pembroke). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Pembroke Hill School (kindergarten to grade 4) and the daily life of Pembroke's young families: the kindergarten day, before- and after-school care, the 2026-27 elementary bus routes and ride times, the new bus company, delays and closings, dismissal changes and pickup rules, school meals and the free and reduced-price application, class sizes and the district's class-size policy, special education and speech staffing, SAU 53's preschool options, English learner support, summer programs, and what infant, toddler and preschool care costs near Pembroke.

**Counts:** 13 topics, 57 facts, 3 number tables (one derived from the bus route sheets, one derived from the staff directory, one of posted child care fees).

**Main sources:** Pembroke Hill's website (FAQ, bus, food services, special education, Title I, staff directory), its 2026-27 Student/Parent Handbook, the six 2026-27 elementary bus route sheets (dated October 2, 2026), the November 2026 lunch menu, Pembroke policies IIB, JHC, EFAA, EEA and IHCA, Pembroke School Board minutes (August 2025 to October 2026), SAU 53's special education, TLC Preschool and staff pages, SAU 53's job postings on November 21, 2026, Pembroke Hill's Title I plan, the Boys & Girls Club of Central and Northern New Hampshire's Suncook and Concord pages, Pembroke Recreation's summer 2026 page and calendar, the 2024 New Hampshire child care market rate study, and the U.S. Labor Department's National Database of Childcare Prices.

**Main gaps:** what time delay and closing alerts actually go out, and whether parents get texts; any limit on how many adults may be on a child's pickup list, or a late-pickup rule; any public measure of missed special education or speech sessions, or speech caseloads; whether Pembroke Hill has any Spanish-speaking staff or interpreter; the Pembroke Town Library's summer reading program (its website refused automated requests); the outcome of the January 2026 plan to bring Champions or KinderCare care to the school; Pembroke Hill's October 1, 2026 enrollment and current kindergarten class sizes (NH DOE's iPlatform refused automated requests).
