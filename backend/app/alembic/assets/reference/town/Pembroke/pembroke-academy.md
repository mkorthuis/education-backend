# Pembroke Academy: Courses, the CRTC, Support, Outcomes and Other High School Options

Part of the Pembroke research (town/Pembroke). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Pembroke Academy (PA), the Pembroke School District's high school for Pembroke, Allenstown, Chichester and Epsom students: its course levels, AP, honors and college-credit courses, shop and hands-on classes, help for students in the middle and for students who fall behind, counseling and college counseling, phones, parking, tardies and late arrival, sports and what the budget debates said about them, buses after school, PA's side of the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC), its published test means and graduates' plans, and the charter, private and online high school options near Concord with their posted costs.

- **13 fact topics, 71 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 15 MB): PA's 2026-27 Program of Studies (approved February 17, 2026), its 2026-27 Student/Parent Handbook (a scanned PDF; the passages used are transcribed in `pa-student-parent-handbook-transcribed.txt`), its 2025-26 school profile, the April 2025 NEASC visiting team report, the August 2025 phone letter and procedure, the School Counseling and college planning pages, the 2023 incoming-family FAQ, ten Pembroke School Board minutes (January 2025 to August 2026), the FY2026-27 annual meeting budget packet (text), Three Rivers' 2026-27 handbook and bus routes, and the tuition or admissions pages of Bishop Brady, Concord Christian Academy, Trinity Christian School, St. Paul's School and Synergy Academy.
- **Places:** 66 facts use `district Pembroke` and 5 use `town Concord` (private and charter schools in Concord). No new statewide rules were needed; the state rules that apply are named from the library and earlier towns in the question table.
- **New data concepts:** PA's college-credit course list with the college and course number for each; its course levels and the weights behind class rank, and the change to Latin honors with no numerical rank from the Class of 2030; the 2026-27 reassessment and late work rules; PA's own CRTC attendance and transport rules; the published SAT and PSAT means; the counselor caseloads and a **derived** students-per-counselor figure; the phone procedure's consequence steps; parking permit prices; the board's statements about sports in the 2025 and 2026 budget debates; and posted 2026-27 costs for Concord-area private high schools.

1. Pembroke Academy, founded in 1818 on Route 3 in Pembroke, is the public high school run by the Pembroke School Board for students from Pembroke, Allenstown, Epsom and Chichester; its 2025-26 profile says it has about 780 students and also accepts students from towns such as Hooksett and Deerfield under a memorandum of understanding, and its 2026-27 Program of Studies says about 750 students taught by about 90 professionals.

2. Pembroke Academy teaches courses at three levels: Level 0 is AP, dual enrollment and other college-credit courses, Level 1 is honors, and Level 2 is college preparatory, which includes all electives; placement is decided by the student and parents, teacher recommendation and completed prerequisites, and the Program of Studies says college prep courses may be taught "at a pace that enables a student who struggles with learning a particular subject to succeed."
