# Pembroke Residents' Questions

The questions the Pembroke research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Pembroke is SAU 53. The Pembroke School District runs Pembroke Hill School (kindergarten to grade 4), Three Rivers School (grades 5–8) and Pembroke Academy (grades 9–12), which also takes high school students from Allenstown, Chichester, Deerfield, Epsom and Hooksett under tuition contracts. Pembroke Academy students may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC). The district decides its budget at town meeting. Several questions contain premises (a school tax rate up almost $2, state funding cut, the old Pembroke Village School, an annual homeschool filing with SAU 53, central office costs split among SAU 53's towns) that the research should check rather than assume.
