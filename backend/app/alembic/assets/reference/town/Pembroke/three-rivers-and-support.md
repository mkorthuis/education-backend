# Three Rivers School and Student Support in Pembroke

Part of the Pembroke research (town/Pembroke). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Three Rivers School (grades 5–8) and the support around it: the move from Pembroke Hill into Three Rivers and from Three Rivers to Pembroke Academy, counseling and the bullying policy, special education (IEPs moving between schools, out-of-district placements and their transportation), homeschooled and EFA students' access to sports and band, the home education notice, Suncook Village bus stops, after-school activities, how families are notified, the SAU 53 website and Pembroke School Board meetings. Sources are the Three Rivers 2026–2027 Student/Parent Handbook, the Three Rivers pages (FAQ, office, athletics, staff directory, Title I, grade 5), its co-curricular descriptions, Panther Prep notice, bus route list and Title I plan; Pembroke School Board policies IHBG, JJJ, JJIC, JICK, JLD, BEDH, BEDB, EGA and EEA; SAU 53's Home Education, Special Education and FAQ pages and its board meeting calendar; Pembroke School Board agendas, notices and minutes from August 2025 to October 2026; the FY2026–27 proposed budget and budget packet; Pembroke's zoning ordinance; the Boys & Girls Clubs' Suncook Clubhouse page; and Pembroke Academy's Incoming Students page.

**Counts:** 13 topics (all shared slugs), 96 facts and 1 number table (special education placement and transportation budget lines by school).

**New data concepts:**

**Main findings:**
- There is no annual homeschool filing to send to SAU 53: New Hampshire repealed the yearly evaluation and the required notice on July 1, 2026. SAU 53's Home Education page and Pembroke's policy IHBG (December 2020) still describe the older rules (a notice of intent and a yearly evaluation).
- Pembroke policy JJJ (January 2023) gives home-educated, charter and private school students the same access as enrolled students to courses, sports and activities, on the same terms. The Three Rivers handbook and athletics page still say only "current students of Three Rivers School" may join co-curricular activities.
- The bullying policy JICK, adopted August 18, 2026, has these deadlines: parents of both students told within 48 hours of a report, the investigation started within 5 school days and finished within 10 (up to 7 more with the superintendent's approval), and parents told of the outcome within 10 school days. Three Rivers has one school counselor (also the 504 coordinator) and one school psychologist, for about 290 students.
- Pembroke's buses are combined Three Rivers–Pembroke Hill routes, run in 2026–27 by a new company, Butler Bus. The White route's Main Street stops (Kimball and Union Streets) are at 7:17–7:18 a.m., reaching Three Rivers at 7:30. The school day is 7:50 to 2:35.
- The board meets most first and third Tuesdays at 6:30 p.m. in the Pembroke Academy library, with a Webex link for watching remotely. Speaking in person is limited to two 15-minute comment periods, and written comments of up to 425 words are allowed. The board reviews the full 2027–28 budget on November 17, 2026, sends it to the Municipal Budget Committee on December 17, the public hearing is February 4, 2027, and the district meeting is March 6, 2027.
- The proposed FY2026–27 budget moves money from out-of-district tuition into programs run in Pembroke's own schools. Pembroke Hill had 0 out-of-district placements, and the district budgets $959,500 for special education transportation.
