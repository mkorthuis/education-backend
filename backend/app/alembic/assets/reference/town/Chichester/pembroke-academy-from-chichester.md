# Pembroke Academy and the CRTC From Chichester

Part of the Chichester research (town/Chichester). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Chichester's side of high school: the Chichester-to-Pembroke Academy (PA) bus and whether any late bus exists, how a Chichester student gets to the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC) and NHTI without a car, what the CRTC does and doesn't teach (heavy equipment, diesel, agriculture, forestry), whether home-educated teens can attend the CRTC, other options after 8th grade (best-interest and manifest-hardship requests, open enrollment, charters), 504 plans, credit recovery and hands-on options, career counseling, who votes on what for PA, how Chichester students fit in at PA, and scholarships. PA's courses, counselors, CRTC rules, buses and outcomes are in `town/Pembroke/pembroke-academy.md`, the Epsom and Allenstown files and Concord's CRTC facts; this file cites them and adds only what they lack.

- **10 fact topics, 41 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 1.4 MB, 54 files and a `geo/` folder): the current (2026-27) and 2022-23 Chichester bus sheets (house numbers removed), the CCS bus page, Chichester's FY2026-27 voted budget (a scanned PDF, with a transcription of the pages used) and budget excerpts, excerpts of 19 Chichester School Board meetings from May 2023 to June 2026 (names removed), the March 7, 2026 district meeting's Article 9 discussion (transcribed from image), eight Chichester and two Pembroke policies (EEA, IHBA, IHBA-R, IHCD, IKFG, IHBI, IMBD, JCA; Pembroke IKFG and IHBA-R), Pembroke board minutes on IKFG, the CRTC's sending schools, parents, "what is CTE", program-of-studies, Automotive, Construction Trades and application pages and its 2020 program book, PA's Program of Studies, handbook, NEASC excerpts, AREA presentation and 2026 scholarship handout, White Mountains Community College's diesel page, the Winnisquam Career Center's pages, Concord Area Transit's pages and rider guide, RSA 188-E:5, 7 and 8, and derived tables with Nominatim and OSRM responses.
- **Places:** 22 facts use `district Chichester`, 6 `district Pembroke` (PA), 4 `district Concord` (the CRTC), 2 `district Winnisquam Regional`, 2 `town Chichester` (Mid-State Transit and a service club), 1 `town Concord` (Concord Area Transit), 1 `town Berlin` (White Mountains Community College) and 3 have no place (two RSA 188-E rules and the NH DOE program grid).
- **New data concepts:** Chichester's own posted morning bus route to PA (24 stops, 6:02 to 7:15) and the $58,527 high school bus line; the December 2025 proposal to cut that bus; Chichester's policy that its district pays CTE center transportation; the CRTC's missing heavy equipment, diesel, agriculture and forestry programs, and where those are taught (White Mountains Community College and the Winnisquam Career Center); Concord Area Transit and Mid-State Transit eligibility; derived drive times from Chichester Central; the size of Chichester's 8th grade against PA's 9th grade; what Chichester's board was told about SAT scores by town and PA's reports of Top 10 graduates and course counts; Chichester's 504, advanced coursework and career-readiness policies and the date Pembroke adopted IKFG; Chichester's three paths to another high school and its March 2026 open enrollment vote; PA Trustees Scholarship totals and Chichester applicants, and the Melvin Sanborn Scholarship.
