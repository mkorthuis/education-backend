## What you need to know about Chichester

- **Chichester** is a town in Merrimack County north of Concord on Routes 4 and 28, with Horse Corner Road, Webster Mills and Kelly Corner. Site town id 99.
- **The Chichester School District** (site district id 99) is in **SAU 53** (Pembroke) and runs Chichester Central School (22590; kindergarten to grade 8; 192 students in 2024-25). The SAU's other districts are Allenstown, Deerfield, Epsom and Pembroke. Grades 9–12 go to **Pembroke Academy** under an area agreement with Allenstown and Epsom (the places file notes the plan itself wasn't found online and that Chichester adopted open enrollment in March 2026); Concord High and Coe-Brown are other options for some families. Career programs are at the Concord Regional Technical Center.
- **Earlier research to cite:** Pembroke (`pembroke-academy.md`, SAU 53 and Academy money), Epsom (`pembroke-academy-from-epsom.md`, Epsom's AREA facts), Deerfield (`high-school-choice.md`, the three high school agreements side by side) and Concord (the CRTC). Write Chichester's side only.
