Part of the Chichester research (town/Chichester). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Chichester Central School's care after school, the four bus routes and how long the rides are, when routes are posted, PickUp Patrol and the phone, snow-day and delay notices, the school day and the 2026-27 calendar next to Pembroke's and Concord's, building security (doors, cameras, drills), art, music and PE, the library and its librarian, reading help during the year and in summer, the town library, school meals and free lunch, preschool and child care near Chichester, and how a parent can ask that twins be in the same or different classrooms.

**Counts:** 12 topics, 55 facts, 3 number tables (two derived from the 2026-27 bus route sheets and one comparing enrollment counts from several sources).

**Main sources:** Chichester Central's website (After School Care, Bus Information, Food Services, Library Media Center, Our School, School Counseling, staff directory), its 2026-27 Student-Parent Handbook (approved by the board July 22, 2026), the four 2026-27 bus route sheets (cleaned of home stops), the 2026-27 district calendar and Pembroke's, Chichester School Board policies (EEA, EEAA, EBB, EBCA, EBCB, EFAA, IC, ICA, IHCA, JG, JH), the February 2026 public hearing presentation, the 29 sets of school board minutes posted for July 2025 to October 2026 (excerpts from 16 are saved), the school's monthly newsletters (September 2025 to October 2026), the Chichester Town Library's page and November 2026 newsletter, NH DOE's October 1, 2026 preschool enrollment report, the federal Head Start site list, and the site's own data.

**Main gaps:** whether the Learning Tree after-school arrangement and its cost continue in 2026-27 (the school's page still says "For 2025-2026"); what time buses run on a two-hour delay (not posted); any missed-stop procedure; whether the library media specialist holds a state license; how many books are checked out; what the school's summer school ("Pirates Camp") is, who may attend and whether it is for reading; the town library's summer reading program; whether any key-card entry system exists (none is mentioned anywhere); Pembroke Academy's own calendar (Pembroke School District's calendar was used).
