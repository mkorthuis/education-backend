# Chichester Residents' Questions

The questions the Chichester research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Chichester's students attend Chichester Central School (kindergarten to grade 8), part of SAU 53 in Pembroke, and then Pembroke Academy under an area agreement with Allenstown and Epsom, with the Concord Regional Technical Center for career programs. Several questions contain premises (more than $25,000 a student, about half proficient, PickUp Patrol changes by 1:00 p.m., key-card doors and cameras, no vote on the Pembroke school board, an option to leave the Pembroke Academy contract, consolidating K–8 with Epsom or Pittsfield) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Hannah Miller (34) — Mom of a kindergartener and a toddler, just bought off Horse Corner Road

- **P1.1** Does Chichester Central have an after-school program on site, or do working parents have to piece together rides to Concord or Epsom?
- **P1.2** How long is the kindergarten bus ride out on the back roads, and is it really true I have to put every pickup change in PickUp Patrol by 1:00 PM?
- **P1.3** With class sizes so small here, do they still have full-time art, music, and gym, or do those get cut when the budget gets tight?
