# Chichester Central School, Grades 5–8: Support and Choices

Part of the Chichester research (town/Chichester). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Chichester Central School's older grades (5 to 8) and the support and choices around them: state test results and readiness for honors courses at Pembroke Academy, what seventh and eighth graders study (lab science, world language, advanced math), speech and occupational therapy staffing, out-of-district special education placements and the money behind them, the counseling staff, bullying and phone rules, robotics, coding and clubs, who may join sports and band without enrolling, the home education rules, how the school's size and the grade 5 to 6 move work, career awareness, the school's racial makeup and staff training, and private school choices in Concord (tuition, Education Freedom Accounts, rides). Sources are the 2026-27 Student-Parent Handbook, the staff directory and school pages, nine Chichester School Board policies (JICK, JICJ, JJJ, JJI, IHBG, IMBD, EEA, JLD, JLCK), eleven School Board minutes from July 2025 to October 2026, the February 2026 public hearing packet and the November 18, 2026 FY2028 budget packet, the school newsletters, the federal Civil Rights Data Collection, New Hampshire's school approval rules, and the Concord private schools' own pages. Statewide rules are cited from the library and earlier towns' files, not repeated.

**Counts:** 15 topics (all shared slugs), 66 facts and 5 number tables.

**New data concepts:**
- The school's own roster of special education positions (3 certified positions, including the speech-language therapist, and 9.5 aides), its special placement and trust fund figures, and a table of the budget lines for 2025-26 to 2027-28.
- Enrollment by grade for four dates, up to 238 students in August 2026.

- State rule text on what a middle school must teach (Ed 306.21) and that a middle school world language is optional (Ed 306.39).

**Main findings:**
- Chichester Central's test results are above the state's in most grades and years: 44% to 62% proficient in math and 50% to 64% in reading from 2022 to 2026, against 41% to 44% and 52% to 55% statewide. Each grade has only 10 to 30 tested students, so single-grade numbers swing a lot.
- Seventh and eighth graders can take Pre-Algebra/Algebra, which the handbook says prepares them for Pembroke Academy's honors track. No world language class, lab course or lab room is named in any school document, and the board withdrew its old World Languages and Science Curriculum policies on October 21, 2026. State rules make a middle school world language optional.
- The district's own roster has a speech-language therapist (1.00 FTE) among its three certified special education positions; an occupational therapist is on the staff page but not on the salary roster. SAU 53 bills districts for shared staff by caseload. No document gives a caseload per teacher or says what happens when a therapist is out. The federal 2023-24 count was 28 students with IEPs of 189.
- One school counselor (1.00 FTE) serves all 232 to 238 students, and no social worker is listed; the board's policy lists social workers among the staff it endorses employing.
- Policy JICJ bans phones, tablets, laptops and smartwatches from the first bell to dismissal. Policy JICK, revised August 19, 2026, sets 48-hour parent notice and 5- and 10-school-day investigation deadlines; the handbook still prints an older version with a 2-school-day notice.
- Policy JJJ (September 16, 2026) gives home-educated, EFA, charter and private school students the same access as enrolled students. The athletics policy JJI still adds a condition about whether the child's own school has a similar sport, and the 1995 home education policy IHBG still describes the notice and yearly evaluation that the state repealed on July 1, 2026.
- Enrollment is 232 to 238 this fall (35 kindergartners), up from about 192 to 207, so "around 200" is now out of date.

**Main gaps:**
- Chichester Central's special education caseload, the number of students in out-of-district placements and how missed therapy sessions are handled are not published.
- Whether the Robotics Club continues in 2026-27, who takes Pre-Algebra/Algebra and how placement is decided, and who speaks at "Career/Artisan Day."
- Whether the Bishop Brady morning bus stops in Chichester, and the Chichester Central racial makeup after 2023-24.
