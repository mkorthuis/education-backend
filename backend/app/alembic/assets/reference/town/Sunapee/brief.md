## What you need to know about Sunapee

- **Sunapee** is a town in Sullivan County on Lake Sunapee, with Sunapee Harbor, Georges Mills, Wendell and the Mount Sunapee ski area, and Mount Royal Academy (a private school) nearby. Site town id 515.
- **The Sunapee School District** (site district id 515) is **SAU 85**, a single district. It runs Sunapee Central School (22005; preschool to grade 5; 168 students in 2024-25) and Sunapee Middle High School (SMHS; the site lists grades 6–8 as 26965 with 101 students and grades 9–12 as 22000 with 131). Check the buildings, how many students the district has, and the district's votes (deliberative session, then ballot). This is the first research on Sunapee.
- **Career programs:** the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center is researched in `town/Claremont/` (the Claremont campus) and `town/Newport/` (the Newport campus). Cite them and write Sunapee's side (access, the bus, credits). `town/New London/` (Kearsarge) has the closest comparisons.
