# Sunapee Residents' Questions

The questions the Sunapee research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Sunapee runs its own K–12 district, SAU 85: Sunapee Central School (preschool to grade 5) and Sunapee Middle High School (grades 6–12) in the same town, with career programs at the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center in Newport. This is the first research on Sunapee. Several questions contain premises (around 400 students, a separate SAU for a two-school district, tuitioning 150 high schoolers out to Kearsarge or Newport, a bond for the elementary school and a roof at the middle-high school, Sherburne Gym, Running Start, the Statewide Education Property Tax on the bill, a merger below 350 students) that the research should check rather than assume.

- **P1.3** How big are the early elementary classes right now—is my 1st grader getting plenty of one-on-one reading help?
