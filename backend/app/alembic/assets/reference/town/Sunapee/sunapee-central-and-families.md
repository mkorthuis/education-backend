# Sunapee Central School and Young Families

Part of the Sunapee research (town/Sunapee). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Sunapee Central School (SCES, preschool to grade 5, 22 School Street) in the Sunapee School District (SAU 85) and the questions of families with young children: public preschool, after-school care, class sizes and reading help, buses and ride times, snow days and remote days, field trip and ski costs, gifted and accelerated learning, science and coding, new families, infant and toddler child care, kindergarten (entry age, day, registration), registration and proof of residency for renters, the elementary bond and its repairs, and security.

20 topics, 71 facts and 3 number tables (the largest topics are the elementary building and bond history, security, the school day and after care, and the district's early-grades staffing). Two topics add Sunapee to library topics (`kindergarten-entry-cutoff` and `kindergarten-registration`); `district-preschool` is also a library topic whose Sunapee fact 108 (the lottery) this file does not repeat.

**Sources.** The district's own sites worked: www.sau85.org and the SCES site answered ordinary requests on November 22, 2026. The main sources are the district's 2025 and 2023 annual reports (the PDFs are on the district site), approved school board minutes for October 2023, January 2024 and March 2024 (their PDFs are reachable by direct link), the capital improvement committee's December 2025 fund page, the 2026-27 calendar, two district policies (JEB, JFAA), the SCES registration page, SCES's weekly newsletter (the Laker Ledger) for September to November 2026, The Outing Club's page for the after-school program, and the site's own child care and class size data. The Valley News is cited only for the March 2021 and March 2022 bond vote counts and the biomass plant, because no district copy of them was found. The 2022 remote weather notice is a Wayback copy (the live page now returns 404).

**Main gaps:** the bus route times (the routes are a Google Sheet whose tabs could not be read, so there are no pickup times for the Lake and Mountain bus and no ride lengths); how long the school day is and whether kindergarten is full or half day (the SCES family handbook is a Drive file that needs a Google sign-in); the kindergarten registration date; the district's policy lists and board minutes after March 2024 (the lists load by script); the preschool's hours and tuition; the Town of Sunapee's own pages (residency affidavit, recreation after-school), which sit behind a bot check; whether a given infant or toddler program has a waitlist and how long it is (the state's data gives status and vacancies only); SMHS security measures; and Sunapee's own gifted-and-talented report to NH DOE (the DOE page returns HTTP 403).

Privacy note for reviewers: the saved annual report and minutes are excerpts (the PDFs name students and residents); staff names are left out of every fact; the two-page staff rosters are saved as counts of positions only; the SPTO officers' names and all emails are left out; no bus sheet was saved. The one Google Sheet of routes was read by a fetch tool and only its tab names were noted (nothing from it was saved, and no house numbers were seen).
