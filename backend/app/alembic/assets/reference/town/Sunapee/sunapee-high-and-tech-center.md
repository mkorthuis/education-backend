# Sunapee Middle High School (Grades 9–12) and the Sugar River Valley Technical Centers

Part of the Sunapee research (town/Sunapee). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers the high school grades of Sunapee Middle High School (SMHS, 10 North Road; site school "Sunapee Sr. High School," id 22000) and how Sunapee students reach the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center (SRVRTC) in Newport and Claremont. Topics:
- AP, honors, college-credit and online courses, the AP Course Ledger, the Senior Project, graduation rules and grading;
- shop, hands-on, money and elective classes;
- Region 10 career and technical education (CTE) agreement: seats, bus, schedule, credits, students enrolled and the agreement's term;
- SAT, AP and graduates' plans, with a derived comparison against Kearsarge;
- teams, junior varsity (JV) levels, NHIAA divisions and co-ops, and the late bus;
- the guidance office, scholarships, apprenticeships, work credit and teacher tenure.

**Sources:** the district's 2026-27 program of studies, student handbook and undated school profile; the SMHS guidance, athletics and Senior Project pages; the 2023-24 athletic handbook; the district's profile page and 2025 annual report; the College Board's AP Course Ledger; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24; the NHIAA 2026-27 handbook; the Region 10 CTE agreement (read from page images); the site's database; and copies of other towns' research (Newport, Claremont, Kearsarge).

**Limits:**
- The district's board minutes, agendas, enrollment reports, job postings and staff directory load by script ("Documents Loading") and were not read. Teacher turnover, late bus times, team rosters and the SRVRTC schedule are therefore gaps.
- The bus route sheet is a Google Sheet that refuses a download (HTTP 401), and it lists stops by address, so it was not used.
- Several lists differ by source (the undated profile, the athletics page and the annual report); facts say which source each list comes from.
