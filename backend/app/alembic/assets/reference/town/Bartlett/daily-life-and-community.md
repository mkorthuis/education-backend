# Daily Life for Bartlett Families: Care, Buses, Attendance, Meals, Fees, Recreation and Streets

Part of the Bartlett research (town/Bartlett). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the day-to-day side of school for Bartlett families: the Josiah Bartlett Elementary School (JBES) day and before- and after-school care, Bartlett's own school buses and the Kennett late bus, storm days for Hart's Location students, attendance and family travel, school meal accounts, school costs, home education and Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs), Bartlett Recreation and summer programs, infant and toddler care, and walking and biking near the school.

It builds on the Jackson research (November 13, 2026), which already covers SAU 9 storm procedures, Kennett late-bus rules, Bartlett Recreation's Adventure Kids and PLC-day care, licensed child care near Jackson, EFA providers near Jackson, the July 2026 home education law and SAU 9 meal prices. Those are named in the question table, not repeated.

- **10 topics, 84 facts and 5 number tables.**
- **New data concepts:** Bartlett's three 2026-27 bus routes and derived ride times; Bartlett Recreation's funding, fee history and summer program; Bartlett's school lunch program deficit (FY2023–FY2025); EFA program-manager rules for tutors and outdoor programs, with a list of 31 Mount Washington Valley marketplace entries (CSV); derived road distances from JBES to infant and toddler care; Hart's Location's transportation reimbursement.
- **Main sources:** the 2026-27 JBES Parent/Student Handbook (now readable; the Jackson agents could not open the 2025-26 one); Bartlett School Board, transportation, co-curricular and facilities committee minutes (July 2025–October 2026); Bartlett policies EEA, IGBJ, JJJ, JH, DFI, IICA and JN; the 2026 Bartlett and Hart's Location school district annual reports; the Town of Bartlett 2025 annual report and 2016 master plan; Bartlett Recreation's MyRec site and archived bartlettrec.org pages; the Children's Scholarship Fund (CSF) parent and provider handbooks and marketplace feed; NH DHHS child care data.
- **Main findings:**
  - JBES runs 9:00 a.m. to 3:15 p.m., with breakfast from 8:30 and no supervision before 8:45. No before-school program is described. After school, Bartlett Recreation's Adventure Kids runs Monday to Thursday until 4:45 for $8 a day, up from $5 in 2024-25.
  - Bartlett runs its own buses with district-employed drivers. It was short of drivers in 2025-26, started that year with two buses and cut driver positions from four to three in the 2026-27 budget. None of its three route sheets lists a stop in Hart's Location; Hart's Location now budgets $6,125 to reimburse a family's mileage.
  - Bartlett deleted its student fees policy (JN) in December 2025. The 2026-27 handbook lists no fee schedule, supply list or planned-absence procedure.
  - Bartlett's home education policy (IGBJ, 2022) still describes the notice, portfolio and evaluation rules that state law dropped on July 1, 2026. The board began revising its access policy (JJJ) for EFA students in October 2026.
  - Bartlett Recreation is a nonprofit, separate from the town and the school, whose mission covers Bartlett, Jackson and Hart's Location. The school district pays it $24,500 a year, and it gives about $10,000 a year in scholarships.
  - No licensed child care is in Bartlett. The nearest infant programs are in North Conway, about 11.6 to 12.9 road miles from JBES (derived).
- **Main gaps:** NHDOT project and Safe Routes to School pages refused automated access; the 2025 and 2026 B.E.A.R. summer program costs; how long infant and toddler waitlists are (no provider publishes this); a written JBES procedure for one-day bus stop changes; a JBES supply list.
- **Proposed shared topic:** none. EFA program-manager rules sit under `town-school-options-nearby`, following the Jackson file. A general `efa-provider-rules` topic could be added to the library later.
