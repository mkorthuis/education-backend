# Bartlett Residents' Questions

The questions the Bartlett research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026.

Bartlett's school is Josiah Bartlett Elementary School (JBES), which runs from preschool through grade 8. It also takes Hart's Location's K–6 students and Jackson's grades 7–8. Bartlett high schoolers go to Kennett High School in Conway.

## P1. The Attitash Ski Resort Hospitality Worker
Single parent, 32. Works seasonal and year-round shifts at Attitash Mountain Resort; rents an apartment near Route 302 and has a 2nd grader at JBES.

- **P1.1** Before- and after-school care: What extended-day programs or local childcare partnerships exist at Josiah Bartlett Elementary for parents working 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM hospitality shifts?
- **P1.2** Winter sports access: Does the JBES winter ski program at Attitash or Cranmore cover equipment rentals and lift tickets for lower-income local families?
- **P1.3** Transportation reliability: How early are bus routes finalized in the fall, and what happens if a shift change requires an afternoon drop-off adjustment?

## P2. The Second-Home Retiree Turned Full-Time Resident
68. Recently converted a longtime vacation condo in Linderhof into a primary residence; no school-age children; watches property tax bills closely.

- **P2.1** Per-pupil spending vs. outcomes: Why is per-pupil spending at Josiah Bartlett Elementary so high compared to state averages while enrollment sits around 165 students?
- **P2.2** Conway tuition contract costs: How much of Bartlett's school tax rate goes toward the 9–12 tuition contract with Conway for Kennett High School, and how are rate increases capped?
- **P2.3** Capital maintenance: Are there major upcoming bond articles or roof/HVAC repairs planned for the JBES building over the next five years?

## P3. The Remote Tech Worker Relocated from Boston

- **P3.3** AP and STEM tracks: What is Kennett High School's track record for AP course availability, STEM electives, and competitive New England college placements?
