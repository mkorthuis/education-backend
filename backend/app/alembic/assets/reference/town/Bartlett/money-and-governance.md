# Bartlett School District: Money, Tuition Contracts and Governance

Part of the Bartlett research (town/Bartlett). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 94 facts and 6 number tables on the Bartlett School District's money, contracts and governance, and on Hart's Location's school arrangements.
- Why Bartlett's NH DOE cost per pupil is high, what it leaves out, and the budget lines behind it (FY2019–FY2027).
- Kennett tuition as a share of the budget and the tax bill (**derived**), and how the current and 2027 Conway contracts set and cap the charge.
- Every page of the three contracts read from scanned images: Bartlett–Conway 9–12 (2003, current), Bartlett–Conway 9–12 (2027–2032) and Bartlett–Hart's Location (2010).
- State aid as Bartlett's reports show it, and why Bartlett gets no fiscal capacity disparity aid. NH DOE's adequacy aid calculation for Bartlett and Hart's Location, FY2020–FY2027, is in the site's data (adequacy_aid), so this file does not repeat it. Nor does it repeat their NH DRA tax rates (tax years 2014–2025) or NH DOE school taxes (2011–2025), which are in the site's data (property_tax).
- The 2024–2025 propane heating conversion financed by a five-year lease, other building work, the LED and energy studies, and the trust funds.
- Teacher pay (the BEA agreement and its 2026-27 salary schedule) and the 2025 and 2026 votes on staff pay.
- What the Bartlett board decides versus SAU 9, the 2026 SAU 9 withdrawal study committee, high school choice votes, and how residents and Hart's Location families can take part.

**Main sources:** Bartlett warrants (2019–2026), annual reports (2019–2026, scanned; budget pages rendered, rotated and read), annual meeting minutes (2025–2026), school board minutes (July 2024–October 2026), Budget, Facilities, Tuition Negotiations, Alternative Energy and SAU Investigative committee minutes, board policies, the BEA agreement, Hart's Location's reports, warrants and minutes (2023–2026), the library's DOE and DRA town CSVs, and read-only queries of the site database.

**Main gaps:** No 2026 tax rate yet for Bartlett. No MS-26/MS-25 forms retrieved. Conway's November 2026 estimate of Bartlett's 2027-28 Kennett charge was not found. The yearly tuition-rate "riders" for Jackson and Hart's Location are not posted. The sealed nonpublic minutes of the Kennett negotiations were not found. The draft 2027-28 budget was not yet posted.

**Two corrections to the brief:**
- The Bartlett–Hart's Location contract covers grades K–8, not K–6. SAU 9's tuition page files it under "K-6 TUITION CONTRACTS."
- Unlike Jackson's new Kennett contract, Bartlett's 2027 Kennett contract does have a minimum-attendance clause (75%).
