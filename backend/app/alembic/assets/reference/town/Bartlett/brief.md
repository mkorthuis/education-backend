## What you need to know about Bartlett

- **Bartlett** is in Carroll County, in the Mount Washington Valley. Its villages include Bartlett Village, Glen, Intervale and Linderhof, and Attitash ski area is in town. Site town id 35.
- **Bartlett School District** (site district id 35) runs one school, **Josiah Bartlett Elementary School** (JBES, site school id 20325). JBES runs from early childhood education (preschool) through grade 8. Site: https://jbes.sau9.org/
  - Site data: 165 students in 2024-25 (208 in 2019-20); cost per pupil $31,530 in FY2025 against $22,699 statewide.
- **JBES also takes:**
  - Hart's Location's students (site district and town id 236) under a K–6 tuition arrangement;
  - Jackson's grades 7–8 under a contract running July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2029.
- **Grades 9–12:** Bartlett students attend Kennett High School (Conway School District, id 113) under a tuition contract. A new Bartlett–Conway 9–12 contract takes effect in 2027-28.
- **SAU 9** (https://www.sau9.org/, SAU id 9) serves Albany, Bartlett, Chatham, Conway, Eaton, Hart's Location and Jackson. Its tuition contracts are at https://www.sau9.org/tuitions.
- **Other districts:** Conway's elementary schools are now Pine Tree Primary (K–2) and Conway Intermediate (3–5). Conway Elementary no longer exists under that name.
