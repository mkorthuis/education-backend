# Haverhill: Money and Governance

Part of the Haverhill research (town/Haverhill). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers the Haverhill Cooperative School District's budget, local tax and state aid, what the November 2026 HB 1300 tax cap and 6% SAU cap mean for its next budget, its share of SAU 23, its tuition agreements with Bath, Benton, Piermont and Warren, its buildings and bonds, community use of school buildings, budget hearings and meetings, staffing and leadership turnover, the 2026-27 grade realignment, outside ratings, and how resident enrollment has changed. Most facts come from the district's 2025 annual report (FY2027 budget, bonds, SAU budget), the March 16, 2026 annual meeting minutes, and Haverhill and SAU 23 board minutes and packets from December 2025 to November 2026, posted in the SAU's Google Drive folders. Scanned packets were read by OCR and checked against page images where numbers are cited.

12 topics, 96 facts, 6 tables. Statewide rules are cited from the library rather than repeated: HB 1300 and Question 2 (`budget-hb-1300-school-tax-cap`, `budget-question-2-2026-results`), SAU budget votes under HB 564 (`budget-sau-budget-vote`), adequacy aid (`money-adequacy-aid-amounts`, `money-adequacy-grant-calculation`), mid-year overruns (Chatham's `school-budget-overruns-and-reserves` facts 1–6), and rating sites (Manchester's `school-rating-sites`). The site's own data answers the adequacy aid amounts, tax rates, Question 2 votes, enrollment, cost per pupil and teacher pay; this file explains them locally and builds derived comparisons from them.

Main gaps: the FY2028 adequacy aid estimate (NH DOE's page refused automated requests), the SAU 23 FY2028 budget drafts (not posted), the other four districts' appropriations (needed to test the 6% SAU cap), Vermont teacher pay, why the original PreK–3/4–8 split was chosen, and any policy on public use of the track or school libraries.

Several facts cite the first version ("Draft # 1") of the FY2028 budget, which the board will revise before the February 2027 hearing; their notes say "first version". One note (district-board-and-public-input fact 7) quotes the word "draft" from the budget committee's notes, so check_town counts it as a draft note.
