# Woodsville High School and River Bend Career & Technical Center

Part of the Haverhill research (town/Haverhill). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Woodsville High School (WHS) in the Haverhill Cooperative School District and River Bend Career & Technical Center (RBCTC) in Bradford, Vermont, where Woodsville juniors and seniors take career programs: graduation credits and River Bend's embedded credits, shop (technology education) classes, AP, honors and Early College (formerly Running Start) courses, credit recovery and summer school, ELOs, internships and JAG, sports teams and eligibility, the late bus, counseling, college and career help, scholarships, gifts and business partnerships, and a comparison with Blue Mountain Union and Oxbow across the river.

- **9 fact topics, 85 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 18 MB). Scripts are in the session scratchpad folder `haverhill-high/`.
- **Places:** `district Haverhill Cooperative` for Woodsville High, the district's policies and River Bend (the district sends students there; the site has no Vermont districts), and `town Haverhill` for the Vermont high schools across the river and adult training open to residents.
- **Sources:** the WHS website (a WordPress site; pages and posts read through its public API), the 2026-27 WHS Student Handbook and Athletics Handbook, the 2024-25 WHS Academic Catalog (the newest one the site links), SAU 23 policy manuals (Sections I and J saved by the middle-school agent in `raw/middle/`; Section K here), Haverhill Cooperative School Board minutes and board packets (principal's reports in scanned packets were read by OCR and checked against page images), the district's 2025 annual report, River Bend's website, handbook and 2026-27 program overview, NH DOE's April 2025 CTE presentation, NCES, and the NHIAA 2026-27 handbook (saved by the Northfield research).
- **Main findings:** about half of Woodsville's juniors and seniors attend River Bend (51 in October 2025, 53 planned for 2026-27); River Bend's Heavy Equipment and Agriculture programs cover diesel and hydraulic repair, welding, forestry and farm work, and its college courses are free to enrolled students; WHS itself still teaches drafting, architectural design, woodworking and a canoe-building class; WHS taught three AP courses in person in 2024-25 (38 exams by 33 students) plus several Early College courses through White Mountains Community College; Woodsville fields JV and varsity teams on its own (no co-op teams in the NHIAA's 2026-27 alignments).
- **Gaps:** the 2025-26 and 2026-27 course catalogs (none posted), a current WHS late-bus schedule (only the 2024-25 route sheet), Oxbow's 2026-27 Program of Studies (Canva, not readable), Blue Mountain's course list, any published timeline for junior-year college counseling, and River Bend's current student handbook (the posted one is the 2023-24 edition).
