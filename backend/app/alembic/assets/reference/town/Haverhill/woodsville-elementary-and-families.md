# Woodsville Elementary and Young Families

Part of the Haverhill research (town/Haverhill). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Woodsville Elementary School (WES, 206 Central Street, Woodsville) and the young-family questions P1.1–P1.3, P2.1, P3.2, P6.3, P12.1–P12.3, P14.2, P14.3 and P20.1–P20.3: school hours, morning arrival and the before- and after-school program; preschool sessions, cost and sign-up; the 2026-27 calendar, early release days, delays and storm closings; bus routes by area, ride times, Route 10 and Mountain Lakes stops, and the bus policies; school meals, the temporary free meals of fall 2025 and meal debt; the Alma parent portal, ParentSquare, ClassDojo and SchoolMessenger; parent conferences by phone or video; reading help at WES and at the middle school; the papers a guardian brings to enroll a child; kindergarten and first grade counts; Chromebooks and the lack of a hotspot program; and summer camps, summer school, library programs and summer meals.

**Counts:** 14 topics, 89 facts, 2 number tables (`woodsville_elementary_enrollment_2026_10`, `haverhill_wes_bus_ride_minutes_2025_26`).

**Main sources:** the WES Family Handbook 2025-26 (PDF titled "WES Family Handbook 25/26"); the 2026-27 calendar for Benton, Haverhill Cooperative, King Street School and French Pond School (approved April 6, 2026); SAU 23's 2025-26 bus route sheet (created August 15, 2025; saved only as a redacted transcription); SAU 23 board policies (sections E, I and J); Haverhill Cooperative School Board minutes, August 2025 to November 2026, and the WES principal's reports in the May, August, September, October and November 2026 board packets; the district's 2025-26 annual report; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24; the 2025-26 meal letter and 2026 summer meals notice; the HCMS Student & Parent Handbook 2025-26; two fall 2026 issues of the WES newsletter, the *Woodsville Weekly Hoot*; the Town of Haverhill's 2025 annual report; Mountain Lakes District pages; and USAC's Emergency Connectivity Fund data.
