# Haverhill Residents' Questions

The questions the Haverhill research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Haverhill's students attend the Haverhill Cooperative School District (SAU 23): Woodsville Elementary (preschool to grade 3), Haverhill Cooperative Middle School in North Haverhill (grades 4–8) and Woodsville High School (grades 9–12). The district also takes tuition students from SAU 23's other towns (Bath, Benton, Piermont and Warren), and its students take career programs at River Bend Career & Technical Center in Bradford, Vermont. Several questions contain premises (a local budget cap and a 6% SAU administration cap, math proficiency around 17–21%, free lunch for all, Haverhill subsidizing the smaller towns, a two-year budget cap from 2027-28) that the research should check rather than assume.
