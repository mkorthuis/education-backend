# Haverhill Cooperative Middle School, Support and Choices

Part of the Haverhill research (town/Haverhill). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Haverhill Cooperative Middle School (HCMS, 175 Morrill Drive, North Haverhill) and the support and choices around it for Haverhill families: absences for farm and fair days, math help, gifted and advanced learners, the 2026 move of grade 4 back to Woodsville Elementary, special education staffing and programs (including French Pond School), IEPs moving into Woodsville High, homeschooled and EFA students in district programs, River Bend for homeschoolers, the homeschool portfolio question, open enrollment, VLACS, tuitioning elsewhere, behavior and phones.

- **12 topics, 86 facts, 2 tables.** All topics are shared slugs at `district Haverhill Cooperative`, except the North Haverhill Fair dates and two VLACS facts at `town Haverhill`. No new state-rule facts; state rules are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **Main sources:** the HCMS 2026-27 and 2025-26 Student & Family Handbooks, the HCMS Vacation Request Form, HCMS web pages (about, staff, athletics, forms), SAU 23 board policies (sections I and J, plus JCA and IFA from 2022), Haverhill Cooperative School Board minutes (August 2025 to November 9, 2026), the November 9, 2026 board packet (the Special Education Director's report on approved programs, the HCMS principal's report, the October 31 budget report and the FY2028 draft budget), the "2026-27 Open Positions & Hiring" list, the district's 2025 annual report and 2026 warrant, the March 16, 2026 annual meeting minutes, the 2026-27 calendar, the Woodsville High 2026-27 handbook, River Bend's handbook, apply and programs pages, VLACS's pages, UNH Extension's and the fair's pages, and federal CRDC 2023-24 rows.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Grade 4 has moved back to Woodsville Elementary.** On January 12, 2026 the board voted to move grade 4 to the elementary school, with no staffing cuts. HCMS serves grades 5–8 in 2026-27 (175 students on November 2, 2026), with a grade 5–6 team and a grade 7–8 team. A facilities committee is now studying whether HCMS could house grades K–6 instead.
  - **Special education:** NH DOE approves three HCMS programs: a resource room, the self-contained "Next Steps" program and the French Pond School alternative program. French Pond is an SAU 23 program in Woodsville (up to 18 students with full staffing), not an out-of-district school; placement is set by the IEP team. HCMS lists four special education teachers and no speech-language pathologist; speech is budgeted as "speech services."
  - **Absences:** HCMS excuses co-curricular dismissals "such as ... 4H" with prior approval. Family trips are limited to 3 days in the handbook but 5 on the vacation form. The North Haverhill Fair is held in late July, during summer break.
  - **Phones:** banned bell to bell under policy JICJ (August 14, 2025). Phones go into locked cabinets, and a student caught with one faces metal-detector checks on entry.
  - **Choices:** in March 2026, Haverhill voters adopted open enrollment with 0% of resident students allowed to attend open enrollment schools elsewhere. VLACS is free, but credit needs the principal's approval. Policy JCA (2022) sets the best-interest and hardship transfer process.
  - **Homeschoolers:** 2009 policies IHBG and IHBG-R still govern access. They generally grant sports and activities and require a parent's same-day statement of four hours of homeschooling before interscholastic events. IHBG still describes the portfolio and evaluation that state law dropped on July 1, 2026. River Bend admits home-schooled students.
- **Main gaps:** counts of students placed outside the district or at French Pond by grade; who provides speech services and whether those posts are filled; any HCMS gifted program description beyond the policies; the text of policy JICJ (not posted); HCMS phone-violation or referral counts; how Haverhill handles a homeschooler's River Bend tuition and busing; NH DOE's gifted-and-talented reports.
