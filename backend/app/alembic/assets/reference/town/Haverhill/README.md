# Haverhill: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Haverhill, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the tenth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Haverhill is in SAU 23 with Bath, Benton, Piermont and Warren, which send it tuition students:
- **Woodsville Elementary** serves preschool to grade 4 from 2026-27 (grade 4 moved back in January 2026).
- **Haverhill Cooperative Middle** serves grades 5–8, and **Woodsville High** grades 9–12.
- **Career programs:** River Bend Career & Technical Center in Bradford, Vermont, where about half of Woodsville's juniors and seniors go.

The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting.
