# Cooperative Middle School, Support and Choices From Brentwood

Part of the Brentwood research (town/Brentwood). Gathered November 21–22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Brentwood's side of Cooperative Middle School (CMS, grades 6–8 in Stratham) and Exeter High School (EHS), both run by the Exeter Region Cooperative School District, and the choices and supports around them: moving from Swasey Central School to CMS (with an IEP or without), the buses and the late bus, two schools' bus times, homeschool and EFA access, how EFAs touch Brentwood's state aid, children who live in two homes, notices for two households and in other languages, English learners, world languages, the three schools' calendars, team tryouts, vaping and phones, and how parents can see and question what is taught. It builds on `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md` ("Exeter CMS file"), `town/Exeter/exeter-high-school.md` ("Exeter High file"), `town/Stratham/cms-for-stratham.md` ("Stratham CMS file") and `town/Stratham/exeter-high-and-sst-for-stratham.md` ("Stratham EHS file"), and points to this town's other files (`swasey-and-families.md`, "Swasey file"; `exeter-high-from-brentwood.md`, "Brentwood EHS file"). Facts about Swasey and the Brentwood School District's policies are placed `district Brentwood`; CMS, EHS and cooperative facts `district Exeter Region Cooperative`; SAU-wide documents `sau 16`; Brentwood's state aid `town Brentwood`.

- **14 fact topics, 48 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 23 MB, most of it the EHS handbook and program of studies and the cooperative's FY27 budget handout).
- **Main sources:** Swasey's home page, 2026-27 student and family handbook, staff listing, 2026-27 calendar and August 14, 2026 newsletter; the Brentwood School Board's policies (AB, EBCD, EEA, IC, IGD, IGE, IHBA, IHBBA, IHBG, JFAA, JFAB, JJJ, JRA) and its minutes of April 6, May 4, June 1, September 8, October 5 and November 2 (draft), 2026; the cooperative's policies AB, IC, IGD, IGE and IHBA, its board postings page and FY27 proposed budget handout; CMS's daily schedule, curriculum, athletics and late-bus pages, its 2026-27 calendar (a scan), its student/caregiver handbook and sports flyers; the EHS handbook, program of studies and home page; SAU 16's curriculum, world language, student services and transportation pages, its Child Find and IEP notices; RSA 193:12; the site's database (town enrollment by grade, adequacy aid, EFA counts, English learners); OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the OSRM router.
