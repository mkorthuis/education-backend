# Exeter High School and the Seacoast School of Technology From Brentwood

Part of the Brentwood research (town/Brentwood). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Brentwood's students in grades 9–12 attend Exeter High School (EHS), and those who choose a career program attend the Seacoast School of Technology (SST), both run by the Exeter Region Cooperative School District. Exeter's and Stratham's research already covers both schools in depth (`town/Exeter/exeter-high-school.md`, `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md`, `town/Stratham/exeter-high-and-sst-for-stratham.md`). This file cites those facts and adds only what they lack: Brentwood's share of the six towns' high school students; how many EHS students attend SST and SST's summer building camp for middle schoolers; EHS's practical courses (family and consumer science, consumer math, personal finance, driver education) and what it doesn't offer (shop and computer courses are at SST); its grade-point table; the cooperative's alternative learning plan and credit-recovery routes; class sizes and the federal students-per-teacher figure; how its counselor caseload compares with state and national figures; tutoring and life-skills money and programs; Great Bay Charter School's class caps, size and ties to the cooperative; the published outcomes of St. Thomas Aquinas and Berwick Academy; drive times from Brentwood; and fields, gyms and grounds.
