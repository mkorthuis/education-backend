# Brentwood: Money, Governance, Buildings and Taxes

Part of the Brentwood research (town/Brentwood). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 49 facts and 5 number tables on how Brentwood's school taxes split between Swasey Central School and the Exeter Region Cooperative, why they rise while enrollment falls, the 2025 reassessment, the Brentwood School District's FY2027 budget and what drove it, the board's three FY2027 budget options and the unfilled teaching job it dropped, the budget votes of 2024–2026, the 2026–2030 teachers' contract, the solar bond and the stalled federal solar rebate, trust funds, SAU 16's cost to Brentwood, Brentwood's seats and votes in SAU 16 and the cooperative, the new SAU budget vote, Question 2 and the recount, state aid compared with other towns, neighbors' spending and tax rates, housing permits and the enrollment outlook.
- **Taxes.** In tax year 2025 Brentwood raised $13,755,254 for schools: 41.4% for its own district (about $4.73 per $1,000) and 58.6% for the cooperative (about $6.68) (**derived**). From tax year 2019 to 2025 those dollars rose 22.4% while resident students fell 20.0%. The total rate fell from $25.17 to $17.13 in 2025 because assessed values rose 59.3%. For FY2027 the district's own tax estimate rose $486,297 (7.5%, about $0.40 per $1,000) and the cooperative estimated $0.35 more (**derived**, approximate).
- **Budget.** The FY2027 article was $7,858,303; health and dental insurance made up $257,591 of the $263,879 rise before grants (97.6%, **derived**). The board chose the lowest of three options in December 2025, dropping an unfilled teaching job. Motions to cut the budget failed in 2024 and 2025.
- **Governance.** Brentwood pays $195,373 (6.08%) of SAU 16's assessments, plus about $268,800 through the cooperative (**derived**). Brentwood holds one of nine cooperative board seats. From March 2027 the SAU budget is a separate ballot article.
- **Aid and neighbors.** Brentwood's FY2027 state aid is $4,911 per resident student, 14th lowest of 237 towns (**derived**).
- **Housing.** 154 homes were permitted from 2020 to 2024, almost all single-family, while resident students fell (**derived**).

**Main sources:** the Brentwood School District's budget folders on SAU 16's site (2026 and earlier warrants, the MS-27 forms, the FY2027 budget detail, the March 2024, 2025 and 2026 annual meeting minutes, the report-to-voters notice), Brentwood School Board minutes and agendas (September 2025–November 2026), the FY2027 budget options review, the 2026–2030 teachers' contract, the FY2025 annual financial report, the recount notice, NH BEA's housing supply data, NHMA articles, the Exeter and Stratham files' cooperative and SAU tables, and the site's database for derived comparisons.
