# Brentwood Residents' Questions

The questions the Brentwood research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Brentwood is in SAU 16. The Brentwood School District runs Swasey Central School (kindergarten to grade 5), and its students in grades 6–12 attend Cooperative Middle School in Stratham and Exeter High School as members of the Exeter Region Cooperative School District, where they may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology. Several questions contain premises (taxes up every March, a 300-student Swasey, crowded Exeter High classes, a default budget, new housing raising enrollment) that the research should check rather than assume.
