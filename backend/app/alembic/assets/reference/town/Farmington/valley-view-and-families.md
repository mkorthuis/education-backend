# Valley View Community School and Young Families

Part of the Farmington research (town/Farmington). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Valley View Community School (pre-K to grade 3) for the young-family questions P1–P5: the school day, morning arrival and the Tiger BEATS after-school program; meal prices and applications; storm-closing calls; the district preschool and nearby Head Start; kindergarten hours, cutoff and registration; reading instruction, screening and extra help; a grade 3 reading comparison with Dover and Rochester; bus routes and ride times; bus changes; snacks and treats; PowerSchool; after-school clubs and youth sports; moving within or out of town; enrollment papers for guardians; the nurse; and the counselor.

**Counts:** 16 topics, 105 facts, 3 number tables (`farmington_bus_ride_minutes_2026_27`, `farmington_area_grade3_reading_2022_2026`, `farmington_star_midyear_proficiency_2025_26`).

**Main sources:** Valley View's 2026-27 Parent & Student Handbook; the district's 2025-26 Student/Family Handbook; SAU 61 web pages (preschool, transportation, food services, PowerSchool, curriculum, Title I, staff directory); board policies EEA, EEA-R, JLC, JLCD, JLCD-F3, JLCF, JFAA, JCA and JECB; the three 2026-27 bus route sheets; the 2026-2027 meal price sheet; Valley View's 2026-27 Title I schoolwide plan summary (scanned; transcribed); the curriculum director's 2026 board reports; school board minutes from July to November 2026; the Tiger BEATS site; the Farmington 500 and town recreation pages; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24; and the site's own database for the derived comparisons.

**Main gaps:** no before-school program exists or was found at Valley View or in town; the district's storm notices are described as automated calls by about 5:30–6:00 a.m., not texts; no preschool waitlist figure is posted; the school board had not settled the preschool tuition question raised September 14, 2026 (no decision in the October 5, October 19 or November 2 minutes); no rule on birthday treats was found; no source says when a new family receives its PowerSchool Access ID; no policy says whether a child keeps the same teacher after moving within town; whether the nurse and counselor are in the building every day is not stated (federal data show 1.0 FTE each in 2023-24).

**Library gaps filled:** the library's district-practices file has Farmington's preschool drop-off times but "no tuition or enrollment details" (district-preschool fact 30), and lists Farmington's kindergarten day length, entry cutoff and registration as not found. This file adds all four (district-preschool, kindergarten-day-length, kindergarten-entry-cutoff, kindergarten-registration below).

**No new topics proposed.** All facts use existing shared or library slugs.

**Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 15. P5.1 and P3.2 lean on earlier rule topics (school-residency-rules in Nashua's and Hudson's files, the library's special-ed-who-is-parent and kinship-caregiver-support, and dyslexia-screening-and-intervention in Albany's file), which are cited, not repeated.
