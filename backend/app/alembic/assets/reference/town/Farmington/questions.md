# Farmington Residents' Questions

The questions the Farmington research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Farmington is SAU 61, a one-district SAU. The Farmington School District runs Valley View Community School (preschool to grade 3), Henry Wilson Memorial School (grades 4–8) and Farmington High School (grades 9–12). Farmington High students may take career programs at a regional technical center. Several questions contain premises (Valley View starting around 7:30, about a third of Henry Wilson students proficient in math, a $160,000 warrant article, the budget raising the rate by over $3.25 per $1,000, Question 2 lowering taxes, Farmington's ratings below Strafford's, a Special Education Coordinator opening) that the research should check rather than assume.
