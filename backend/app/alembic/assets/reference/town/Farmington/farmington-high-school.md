Part of the Farmington research (town/Farmington). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Farmington High School (FHS, grades 9–12, site school id 22050) for the Farmington School District (SAU 61): the school resource officers and building security for all three schools, the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC) in Wolfeboro where FHS students take career and technical programs, shop and hands-on courses inside FHS, AP and college-credit courses, counselor staffing and college planning, readiness for college, sports teams and fees, tutoring and credit recovery, the phone policy, absence calls and truancy steps, transfers, alternative pathways, mental health supports, work-based learning, and money and life-skills courses.

- **Places:** all 116 facts use `district Farmington`. No new state rules were needed; the state rules that apply are named from the library and earlier towns in the question table.

LRTC lists Farmington among its four sending schools, and FHS's handbook and catalog describe the bus, rules and 13 programs for FHS students.
