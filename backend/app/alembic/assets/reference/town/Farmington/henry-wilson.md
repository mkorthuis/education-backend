# Henry Wilson Memorial School, Special Education and Home Education in Farmington

Part of the Farmington research (town/Farmington). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Henry Wilson Memorial School (grades 4–8) and the district's special education and home education practice: special education staffing and the open coordinator job, case managers and paraprofessionals, paraprofessional training, how IEPs carry from grade to grade, math results and supports, what's offered to students ready to move faster, early high school credit and the "get ready for algebra" course, Chromebooks and phones, how grades 4–8 are grouped, shared bus runs, late buses and after-school help, the new bullying policy and the code of conduct on fights, access to district activities for home-educated and other non-enrolled students, coming back from home education, placement of transfer students, and the move to Farmington High.
