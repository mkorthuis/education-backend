# Pollard Elementary School and Young Families in Plaistow

Part of the Plaistow research (town/Plaistow). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Pollard Elementary School (preschool to grade 5, Plaistow) and the Timberlane Regional School District rules that shape a young family's year there: the half-day early childhood (pre-K) program and its tuition and waivers, kindergarten (half day free, full day by tuition) and the district's class-size policy, Child Find screening and the special education timelines, before- and after-school care (the YMCA runs it inside Pollard), early release days, delays and closings, the bus contractor and the fall 2025 driver shortage, the Pollard PTA and volunteering, how advanced learners are served, English learner services, notices in Spanish, and the make-up of Pollard's and the middle school's students. Main sources: Pollard's and the district's web pages, the 2026-27 elementary Parent & Student Handbook, school board policies, school board minutes (September 2025 to October 2026), the YMCA of the North Shore's pages, NH DHHS Child Care Search listings (from the site's database), NCES and CRDC enrollment by race, and the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 ACS.

**Counts:** 14 topics, 66 facts, 2 number tables.

**Main gaps:** the outcome of the March 2026 vote on tuition-free full-day kindergarten (Article 14); current class sizes in Pollard's kindergarten rooms; how long Timberlane actually takes from a Child Find screening to speech services (NH DOE's district timeliness reports refused automated requests); the elementary early release dismissal time; whether the YMCA opens before school on delay days; any district practice of translating newsletters or permission slips into Spanish; and a survey of how welcome families feel.

## school-transportation-rules: School Bus and Transportation Rules

- kind: rule

- description: When NH districts must provide transportation, and who is responsible when a student attends school outside the assigned school or district.

1. New Hampshire law lets a school district bus pupils between schools and legally operating before- and after-school programs only once the district approves it in the same way it adopts its budget (for an official-ballot district, a ballot vote), and the approval lasts until revoked the same way.
   - citation: RSA 189:6-b
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/189/189-6-b.htm
