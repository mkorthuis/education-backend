## What you need to know about Plaistow

- **Plaistow** is a town in Rockingham County on the Massachusetts line (Haverhill), along Routes 125 and 121A. Site town id 443. It hosts Timberlane Regional Middle School, Timberlane Regional High School and the SAU office at 30 Greenough Road.
- **Pollard Elementary School** (site school 22765): preschool to grade 5; 434 students in 2025-26.
- **Starting pages:** the district, https://www.timberlane.net; Pollard's pages there; the town, https://www.plaistow.com (tax rate, assessing, exemptions, recreation, the library).
- **Site figures:**
  - Plaistow's resident public students: 1,180 (2014-15), 987 (2019-20), 933 (2025-26) and 1,006 (2026-27).
  - Plaistow's 2025 tax rates per $1,000: total $14.44, local school $8.38, state education $1.02, municipal $4.51, equalization ratio 95%. P10.1 says the local school rate rose from $8.38 to $10.54: check the 2026 rate.

  - Timberlane's math proficiency was 42% in 2026; the site has school-level results (P8.1 says "around 40%" in the middle school).
