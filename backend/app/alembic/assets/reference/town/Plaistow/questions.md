# Plaistow Residents' Questions

The questions the Plaistow research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Plaistow's public students attend the Timberlane Regional School District, a four-town cooperative of Atkinson, Danville, Plaistow and Sandown (SAU 106 in the site's data): Pollard Elementary School (preschool to grade 5) in Plaistow, then Timberlane Regional Middle School (6–8) and Timberlane Regional High School (9–12), both in Plaistow, with the SAU office on Greenough Road. Timberlane students may take career programs at Salem High's or Pinkerton Academy's CTE centers. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (about 50 open staff positions and outside staffing agencies, a local school rate that rose from $8.38 to $10.54, a 2.5% tax cap, a 94% graduation rate, about 40% of middle schoolers proficient in math, a 5% unassigned fund balance, an average SAT around 1180, full-day pre-K at Pollard) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Brianna, 32 — Mom of a 4-Year-Old

- **P1.3** What's the kindergarten class size like at Pollard right now—is my kid going to be in a room with 24 other five-year-olds?
