# Allenstown Community School's Upper Grades, Support and Choices

Part of the Allenstown research (town/Allenstown). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Allenstown Community School (ACS, kindergarten to grade 8) from the side of its older students and the families who need extra support or are weighing other choices: the paraprofessional contract votes and what they mean for aides, the new principal and IEP continuity, special education staffing, middle school unified arts (no shop class), music and the open music position, sports and clubs after school, fees, homeschooled, charter and EFA students' access, Strong Foundations Charter School from Allenstown's side, English learner services and translation, counselors, new students, math in grades 7–8 and interventionists, and the bullying policy. Sources are the Allenstown School Board's minutes (August 2025 to September 2026), agendas through November 9, 2026, the January 31, 2026 deliberative session transcript, the March 10 and August 25, 2026 ballot results (handwritten counts read from the images), the FY2026–27 deliberative budget packet and voted budget (read from page images), Allenstown policies (JJJ, IHBG, JICK, JLD, JJIC, JJIB, IJOA, IKE, KA, KB, EEA, IMBD), ACS's website (athletics, clubs, administration, student resources) and linked files (unified arts and grade 7–8 newsletters, eligibility rules, supply lists, the Title I plan, the 2025–26 violence prevention plan, 2024–25 principal reports), and SAU 53's job board as of November 21, 2026.

**Counts:** 11 topics (all shared slugs), 78 facts and 1 number table (student support budget lines, FY2025–FY2027).

**New data concepts:**
- The paraprofessional contract's two failed votes (March 10 and August 25, 2026), the union's size (20.5 paraprofessionals for 119 special education students) and what the "cost items" did and did not fund.
- Student-support budget lines by year (English learner consultant, guidance, special education assistants, field trips, co-curriculars).
- Open positions at one school on one day, as a partial staffing picture.

**Main findings:**
- Voters rejected the paraprofessional contract's cost items twice: 276–290 on March 10, 2026 (three years, $99,535 the first year) and 146–249 on August 25, 2026 (one year, $44,930). The operating budget passed, so current paraprofessional pay (20.50 FTE, $470,941) is funded; only the raises failed. No posted minutes after September 14, 2026 say what happens next.
- ACS has a new principal for 2026–27 (a former assistant principal at Pittsfield Elementary and Alton Central School); the assistant principal (since 2020) and the special education coordinator stay. One special education case manager job was posted October 17, 2026.
- The voted 2026–27 budget funds one school counselor ($60,495, "Includes 1 FTE", down from $114,086); a K–8 counselor posting from June 2026 was still listed. English learner help is bought as a contracted "ELL consultant" line ($76,484, up $23,172); ACS lists no ESOL teacher of its own.
- Middle school unified arts are Technology/Library Skills, Art, PE/Health, Music and Connections: no shop or tech-ed class. The music teacher job and a long-term music substitute were both still posted in November 2026.
- After school: soccer (boys' and girls'), basketball (boys' team, girls' club, spirit team), baseball and softball, robotics, Homework Hour, student council and crochet club; sports need a participation fee (waiver available) and grade checks.
- Allenstown's access policy JJJ (2023) doesn't yet name EFA students, and its home education policy IHBG (2023) still describes the yearly evaluation repealed July 1, 2026.
- Math: the board's curriculum committee found in January 2026 that Eureka Math "is not meeting the needs of a large population of students"; a part-time Title I math interventionist job (posted April 2026) was still listed.
