# Pembroke Academy and the CRTC From Allenstown

Part of the Allenstown research (town/Allenstown). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Allenstown's side of high school at Pembroke Academy (PA) and the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC): who stays responsible for a student's IEP, how Allenstown pays for the CRTC, getting to PA and the CRTC, what the AREA agreement means for Allenstown students and where families take a concern, homeschooled teens and PA classes, PA's AP results and SAT means, summer programs before 9th grade, moving from Allenstown Community School (ACS) teams to PA teams, and JROTC and military families. PA's courses, counselors, CRTC rules, sports and outcomes are already in `town/Pembroke/pembroke-academy.md` and are cited from there, not repeated.

- **10 fact topics, 46 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 16 MB): PA's "AREA Presentation" of October 22, 2025, posted on the Allenstown School Board's page; 12 Allenstown policies (JJJ, IHBG, IHCD, LEB, IKF, IMBD, KE, BEDH, JFAB, IHCA, JJIB, EEA); 15 Allenstown board minutes (December 2024 to September 2026) and the January 31, 2026 deliberative session minutes; Allenstown's FY2025-26 public hearing packet, FY2026-27 Facts at a Glance and the November 9, 2026 FY2027-28 budget information; ACS's 2026-27 bus list (house numbers removed); PA's home page, Exceptional Services page, 2025-26 athletic policy and the Chichester bus sheet; the CRTC's sending schools, parents and welding pages; and RSA 195-A.
- **Places:** 34 facts use `district Allenstown`, 11 use `district Pembroke` (new PA facts that earlier files lack: the AREA presentation's AP, SAT and summer figures, the Exceptional Services page, the counselor and CRTC bus budget lines, the athletic policy, the drop-off advice and the absence of JROTC) and 1 uses `district Concord` (the CRTC's Welding page). There are no `sau 53` or no-place facts. ## district-transportation: Getting From Allenstown to Pembroke Academy and the CRTC

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: Bus service: who runs it, routes and times, eligibility, late and activity buses, storm closings and delays, and transport to schools outside the district

1. Under New Hampshire's AREA law, getting a sending district's students to the area school "shall be governed by the general school laws applicable thereto and shall be the responsibility of each sending district," so busing Allenstown students to Pembroke Academy is up to the Allenstown School District; the general law requires busing only for kindergarten through grade 8 and lets a district bus high school students when it finds it appropriate.
   - citation: RSA 195-A:4; RSA 189:6 (library gov-school-transportation 1–2)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/195-A/195-A-4.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - place: district Allenstown
   - note: Statute text: "Transportation of resident pupils of the sending districts to the area school shall be governed by the general school laws applicable thereto and shall be the responsibility of each sending district." Allenstown's policy EEA says only that "The District will provide pupil transportation services consistent with applicable law." Pembroke's policy JFAB says Pembroke doesn't transport nonresident students (Pembroke money-and-governance district-tuition-contracts 12).
