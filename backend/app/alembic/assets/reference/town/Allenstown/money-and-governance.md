# Allenstown: Money, Taxes, Buildings and Governance

Part of the Allenstown research (town/Allenstown). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 60 facts and 5 number tables on the Allenstown School District's money and governance and the town side of its taxes and old school buildings.
- **Budget and cost:** the FY2027 (2026-27) budget voters passed on March 10, 2026 and what drove its rise (out-of-district special education placements, a new bus contract, health insurance). The FY2028 proposal of November 9, 2026 ($16,615,235, +9.7%) and its default budget ($16,246,832), line by line. The FY2027 default, which was $1,046 *above* the proposal. Spending against budget in FY2026, special education spending, and the trend since 2020-21 against flat K–8 enrollment.
- **The contracts and the August election:** both staff contracts failed in March; voters had authorized one special meeting for each; the one-year versions failed again on August 25, 2026 (146–249 and 128–268), on about 13% turnout (**derived**).
- **Taxes:** the tax year 2026 rate ($26.37; local school $15.75), the school share of a bill, the district's January forecast against the result, revenue, surplus returned to taxpayers, the tax cap's effect on the budget, bill timing and the 2027 revaluation. The China Mill's RSA 79-E tax relief and how its end would work.
- **Buildings:** the 2021 bond vote and the bond's schedule to 2042, trust funds, the sale of Allenstown Elementary to the town and of the Dupont School to the Diocese of Manchester (each for $1.00), what keeping them cost in 2024-25, the town's conversion of the old elementary into a new Town Hall, field upkeep.
- **Governance:** how the board and the Municipal Budget Committee work, the 2026 vacancies and write-in seats, filing for 2027, community use of the gym and fields (policy KF, revised June 2026), and SAU 53's cost formula (half valuation, half pupils), which fills a gap in Pembroke's file.

**Main sources:** the Allenstown School District's pages and Google Drive folders on sau.sau53.org (warrants, signed ballot results, MS-27 and MS-DSB forms, the FY2027 budget binder, Facts at a Glance, the FY2028 packet, board and deliberative session minutes from 2021 to 2026, policy KF, notices); the Town of Allenstown's website and 2025 annual town and school report; the 2021-22 budget presentation (in Pembroke's raw folder); RSA 79-E; the site's database (read only); and, where no primary record could be found, the Allenstown Alt. local news site and the New England Real Estate Journal.
