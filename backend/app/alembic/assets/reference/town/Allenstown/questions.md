# Allenstown Residents' Questions

The questions the Allenstown research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Allenstown is in SAU 53. The Allenstown School District runs Allenstown Community School (kindergarten to grade 8), which replaced Allenstown Elementary and the Armand R. Dupont School. Its high school students attend Pembroke Academy under an AREA agreement, and may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. The district decides its budget by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (about 350 students, an August special election on the paraprofessional contract, the mill's tax break ending, the old elementary school becoming Town Hall and the Diocese buying back Dupont, Pembroke Academy's SAT average near 1180 and math proficiency near 23%) that the research should check rather than assume.
