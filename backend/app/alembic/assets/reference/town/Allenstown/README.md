# Allenstown: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Allenstown, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 21, 2026, in the fourth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Allenstown is in SAU 53 with Pembroke, Chichester, Deerfield and Epsom:
- **Allenstown Community School (ACS)** serves kindergarten to grade 8. It replaced Allenstown Elementary and the Armand R. Dupont School.
- **High school:** students attend Pembroke Academy as an AREA town, and may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC).

The district votes by official ballot. SAU 53 and Pembroke Academy were researched in `../Pembroke/`; these files cite them and add Allenstown's side.
