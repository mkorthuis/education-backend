# Allenstown Community School: Early Grades and Families

Part of the Allenstown research (town/Allenstown). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Allenstown Community School (ACS, kindergarten to grade 8, 171 River Road) from a young family's side: public preschool and the private options in town, the on-site Champions after-school program and the Boys & Girls Club bus, the 2026-27 Butler bus runs and how a bus stop gets added or moved, walking and River Road, state test results before and after the two old schools merged, whether families leave before middle school, homework help, meals and the free or reduced-price application, report cards and how the school reaches families, class sizes, gifted and enrichment offerings, hands-on science in the new building, and weather days.

**Counts:** 18 topics, 61 facts, 3 number tables (test results 2019–2026, derived from NH DOE's files; grade-to-grade changes in resident students, derived from the site's data; 2026-27 bus runs, derived from the route sheets).

**Main sources:** the ACS website (food services, student resources, community links, contact), Allenstown School Board minutes (July 2025 to September 2026) and the principal's 2024-25 monthly board reports, Allenstown policies EEA, EFAA, IIB, IKB, IMBA and IFA, the 2026-27 calendar, the 2026-27 Butler bus route sheets (dated August 20, 2026), the 2026-27 trimester-1 grade newsletters and unified arts schedule, the 2023-24 Title I plan, the 2025-26 free and reduced-price meal forms, SAU 53's TLC Preschool, curriculum and data-dashboard pages, Champions' ACS page, the Boys & Girls Club's Suncook pages, the Allenstown Public Library, the town's 2016 Master Plan, 2026–2031 Capital Improvements Program, 2022 Planning Board minutes and TextMyGov page, The Turner Group's project board for the school, DoD STARBASE and DVIDS, and NH DOE's assessment files as loaded in the site.

**Main gaps:** the 2026-27 handbook (a Canva page) was read in a browser pass for hours, closings, report cards and supervision; it doesn't say how early closings are announced or whether paper report cards are offered; whether River Road has sidewalks near the school; any current gifted program or NH DOE gifted-and-talented report; any lesson that uses the building's solar panels.
