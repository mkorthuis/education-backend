# Northfield: School Taxes, State Aid, the Budget and How to Take Part

Part of the Northfield research (town/Northfield). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 6 topics, 25 facts and 2 number tables on Northfield's side of the Winnisquam Regional School District (SAU 59). Tilton's `money-and-governance.md` (town/Tilton) already has the district-wide money facts: the cost split (72% students, 28% property value in 2025-26), each town's share, the 2026-27 budget and its drivers, the March 20, 2027 district meeting, the roof bond plan and the career center's funding. This file cites those and adds what they lack for Northfield.
- **Northfield's school tax:** why its local school tax rose 18.7% in 2025 ($7.15 to $8.28 per $1,000), split into the bigger district budget, lower state aid and Northfield's smaller share (all **derived**); the district's 2026-27 estimate (up to $9.66); the much smaller year-end surplus this year (about $175,000 against $1.2 million); what the formula change costs Northfield (about $51,000 in 2025-26, **derived**); why Northfield carries the largest share but pays the least per student yet the highest rate; its falling adequacy aid and the hold-harmless grant's phase-down (about $378,000 less in FY2028, **derived**); what renters' landlords pay per $100,000 of assessment; who sets each part of the bill.
- **Budget:** the 2026-27 budget by function (57.8% instruction, 4.1% transportation) and by object (teacher salaries $8,275,405, administrator salaries $1,442,015, health insurance $4,395,941).
- **Union Sanborn:** sold to Compass Classical Academy for $2.2 million in 2023; the district's building costs there fell from $95,729 (2022-23) to $0 since 2024-25; the proceeds went to the career center.
- **Governance:** board meetings rotate to Southwick in Northfield (December 14, 2026 and April 19, 2027); no live stream found; Northfield's three board seats; what voters present did at recent district meetings.
- **Schools:** all Northfield children attend Southwick (K–4), then the middle and high school in Tilton; Northfield had 170 of Southwick's 244 students in October 2025 (**derived**).

**Main sources:** the district's 2026 Annual Report, 2026-27 Budget Committee presentation, MS-27 and proposed budget handout; school board minutes (2023, 2024 and 2026), agendas and the district's public calendar; the Town of Tilton's 2024 and 2025 annual reports (apportionment pages, which list all three towns); RSA 24:1 and 24:13; the site's database (tax rates, equalized rates, adequacy aid).
