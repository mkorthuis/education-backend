# Northfield Residents' Questions

The questions the Northfield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Northfield's students attend the Winnisquam Regional School District (SAU 59), shared with Sanbornton and Tilton. Northfield's elementary children attend Southwick School in Northfield; grades 6–8 attend Winnisquam Regional Middle School and grades 9–12 Winnisquam Regional High School, both in Tilton, where the Winnisquam Career Center offers career programs. The district is researched in `../Tilton/`; the files here add Northfield's side. Several questions contain premises (the school rate rising from $8.28 to $9.30, Northfield paying more than Tilton or Sanbornton, the old Union Sanborn School, a new career center, Compass Classical Academy in Northfield) that the research should check rather than assume.
