# Winnisquam Regional Middle School, Student Support and Choices for Northfield Families

Part of the Northfield research (town/Northfield). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file adds Northfield's side to the Tilton research on Winnisquam Regional Middle School (WRMS, grades 5–8, 76 Winter Street, Tilton) and on student support and school choices in the Winnisquam Regional School District (SAU 59). Most district facts are already in `../Tilton/middle-school-support-and-choices.md` and the other Tilton files; this file cites them and writes new facts only where they lacked something: WRMS's results next to Merrimack County schools (Northfield is in Merrimack County; WRMS is in Tilton, Belknap County), reading results since 2019, Algebra I counts, who to call about an IEP, paraprofessional and teacher openings, teacher retention, phones and reaching a child, sports pickups, the district's mental health contacts (Northfield's community mental health center differs from Tilton's), home education and access rules, Compass Classical Academy's distance from the district's schools, and the policies and data that bear on LGBTQ+ students.

- **12 topics, 39 facts, 2 tables.** Eleven are shared district slugs with `place: district Winnisquam Regional`; one is a **proposed new shared topic**, `lgbtq-students-nh-survey-data` (no place).
- **Main sources:** the site's database (NH SAS results, enrollment by grade, EFA counts); the Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24 (teacher retention, Algebra I); the district's posted policy manual (AC, IHBA, IHBG, JICJ, JICK-R, JJA, JJIC, JJJ-R, JLCK, JLDBB, KE), found on the district's "WRSD Policies" page; the WRMS 2026-27 handbook and the principal's August 21, 2026 letter; the WRHS 2026-27 Comprehensive Guide (the athletic handbook WRMS uses); the district's Student Services, Section 504, Office of Student Wellness and staff pages; board minutes of January 20, February 16, June 15 (draft), August 17 and October 19, 2026; the 2026-27 high school and middle school bus route sheets; CDC's YRBS Explorer (New Hampshire, 2025).
- **Main findings:**
  - **Scores (P4.1):** in 2026 WRMS's math (49% proficient) was higher than 14 of 17 Merrimack County schools that test grades 7 and 8, but its reading (39%) was lower than 13 of them. Its math growth (56) was among the highest; its reading growth (44) among the lowest.
  - **Reading since the pandemic (P13.2):** the district's reading proficiency was 53% in 2019 and 45% in 2026 (state 56% and 55%). Grades 3–4 are back to or above 2019 (Southwick's grade 3 rose from 44% to 57%), while grades 5–8 are 12 to 24 points lower.
  - **Teacher turnover (P10.3):** in the 2023-24 federal civil rights data, 16 of Southwick's 28 teachers (57%) and 26 of WRMS's 38 (68%) had also taught at the school the year before, against about 84% statewide (derived).
  - **Advanced math (P4.2):** honors math in grades 6–7 and Algebra 1 for high school credit in grade 8 (Tilton file); in 2023-24 one Algebra I class enrolled 12 eighth graders, about 12% of the grade (derived), and all passed.
  - **Who to call (P7.3):** the classroom teacher or case manager, then the school's special education coordinator (Southwick and WRMS each list one) and principal, then the Director of Student Services at the SAU office (603-286-4116, ext. 8108); policy KE sets the same order for complaints; state routes are in the library.
  - **Paras (P7.2):** no vacancy count is published; a WRMS paraprofessional position was cut from the 2026-27 budget, and in June 2026 WRMS still had an open special education teacher position (filled in October).
  - **Phones (P9.1):** phones are off and kept in lockers, not locked up; a student with an urgent need may use the office phone, and in a school emergency the district notifies families through ParentSquare.
  - **Mental health (P9.3):** DHHS places Northfield in Riverbend Community Mental Health's region (Tilton and Sanbornton are Lakes Region Mental Health Center's); the district lists both, plus HealthFirst, whose counselors can see children at school.
  - **Compass (P10.2):** Compass (5 Elm Street, Northfield) is about 1.5 miles by road from Southwick and 1.0 mile from WRMS (derived); district buses take charter students on existing routes only.
  - **LGBTQ+ students (P19.1):** policy AC (October 2026) bars harassment based on gender identity and sexual orientation; no GSA or similar club is listed in either school's 2026-27 handbook; the district's athletics policy JJIC (November 2025) sets teams for grades 5–12 by sex at birth, as state law requires. No school-level climate survey is public.
- **Main gaps:** no school-level climate or LGBTQ+ survey; no published paraprofessional vacancy or 1:1 coverage figures (SchoolSpring's search returned no Winnisquam postings); no practice end times posted for WRMS teams; the district's Special Education page has no content; CRDC teacher data are from 2023-24, with no reasons for leaving; Compass's own site (lottery, start time, transportation) was not retried after earlier refusals.
