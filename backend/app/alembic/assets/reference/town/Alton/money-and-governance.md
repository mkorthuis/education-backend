# Alton: Money, Taxes and Governance (Alton School District, SAU 72, and the Prospect Mountain JMA)

Part of the Alton research (town/Alton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 78 facts and 6 number tables on the Alton School District (SAU 72), the Prospect Mountain JMA (SAU 301, run jointly with Barnstead) and Alton's taxes. They cover the 2026-27 (FY2027) budget, what drove it and how voters decided it; the budget's growth since 2023-24 against enrollment (table); the draft 2027-28 budget; the SchoolCare health insurance assessment and how the district paid it; fund balance and what it does to the tax rate; trust funds and capital reserves (table); special education busing (table); the Prospect Mountain budget and how its costs are split between Alton and Barnstead (table); the shared SAU central office and how its costs are split; the SAU merger study and draft plan (table of claimed savings); teacher and support staff contracts and pay; the district's own inflation-adjusted report on cost per pupil and pay (table); the 2026 revaluation and the new HB 1300 tax cap; how meetings, the deliberative session and the ballot vote work; and Prospect Mountain's building and capital plan. Barnstead's research can cite the `district Prospect Mountain JMA` and `sau 72` facts directly; they describe arrangements that apply to both towns.

- **Budget:** $18,792,042 for 2026-27, up 3.84%; passed 572 to 443 on March 10, 2026. It cut two teaching positions and one support position, while special education busing, speech and occupational therapy, elementary busing and health insurance (two plans up 31% and 26%) rose. A draft 2027-28 budget of $19,739,573 (up 4.63%) assumes a 22% health insurance increase.
- **SchoolCare:** in October 2025 the health insurance pool billed Alton $239,047.31 and Prospect Mountain $302,397.40. The Alton board voted in public on October 20, 2025 to pay up to $375,532.44 (its own bill plus its share of Prospect Mountain's) from its unspent 2024-25 fund balance of $328,110.02, money that would otherwise have been returned to reduce the tax rate; about $47,000 came from the 2025-26 budget. Trust funds and capital reserves were not used and grew to $1,980,827.92.
- **Prospect Mountain cost split:** operating costs are split by each town's share of students (Alton 50.64% in 2023-24, 45.55% in 2026-27, 48.53% in the 2027-28 draft); capital fund deposits and draws are split 50/50; SAU central office costs are split one-third each to Alton, Barnstead and Prospect Mountain, whose third is then split by students.
- **SAU merger:** voters created a planning committee 660 to 368. The plan merges the three SAU central offices, not the schools or school boards; it was submitted to the State Board of Education after a November 4, 2026 hearing, and needs 60% in each town in March 2027. Most claimed savings are from shared services since 2020; the new money is a state grant of about $260,000 a year for two years.

**Main sources:** the Prospect Mountain SAU's document folders (Alton warrants, MS-27 and MS-DSB budget forms, deliberative session handouts, ballot results, annual reports for 2023-24 and 2024-25, Alton School Board and JMA board minutes September 2025 to November 2026, SAU merger committee minutes and draft plan, budget drafts and notes for 2027-28, policies BEDH and the PMHS policy manual); the Town of Alton's site (Budget Committee minutes, revaluation notice and FAQ, voting page, tax rate page, 2023 Master Plan); SchoolCare's 2025 assessment list; and the site's database.
