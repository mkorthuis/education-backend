# Alton Central School's Early Grades and Young Families

Part of the Alton research (town/Alton). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Alton Central School (ACS, preschool to grade 8) from a young family's side: classroom placement and what parents may ask about aides, Title I reading help and Extended Learning Time, report cards, enrichment and Algebra I for advanced students, preschool and kindergarten days, school hours and after-school supervision, the 2026–27 calendar and its snow days, delays and closings, buses and the town's winter plowing priorities, cold-weather recess, meal prices, applications and charges, the apps and notices families use, enrollment help for families in housing hardship, visiting and chaperoning, the End 68 Hours of Hunger weekend food program, the town's welfare help and the charities it works with, Parks and Recreation programs for children, the Gilman Library, and the state rule for a grandparent seeking guardianship.

17 topics, 53 facts and no number tables. Main sources: the ACS Student and Parent Handbook for 2026–27 (Google Drive, linked from the school's site), the 2026–27 ACS calendar (approved April 20, 2026), the Town of Alton's 2025 Town Report (scanned; OCR), the town's January 2024 winter road maintenance guidelines, Parks and Recreation and Town Welfare pages, End 68 Hours of Hunger's community page and FAQ, and RSA 193:12.

## school-residency-rules: When a Relative Is Seeking Guardianship

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Which district is legally responsible for a student under New Hampshire law: domicile, guardians, divided homes and parenting plans, moves between districts, attending a district where a student doesn't live, and who decides disputes

1. Under New Hampshire's residency law, when a petition for guardianship or legal custody of a child is filed in court on behalf of a relative other than a parent, such as a grandparent, the child must be allowed to attend school in the district where that relative lives while the court decides; the same law says a guardianship may not be sought only so a child can attend school outside the parents' district.
   - citation: RSA 193:12, II(a)(3)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/193/193-12.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Statute text: "Legal guardianship shall not be appointed solely for the purpose of allowing a pupil to attend school in a district other than the district of residence of the minor's parent or parents. Whenever a petition for guardianship or legal custody is filed in a court of competent jurisdiction on behalf of a relative of a child, other than a parent, the child shall be permitted to attend school in the district in which the relative of the child resides pending a court determination relative to custody or guardianship." Earlier files state this only for one district (Chatham community-and-families.md 14; Farmington valley-view-and-families.md 13); this is the statewide version.
