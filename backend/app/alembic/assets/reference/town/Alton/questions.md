# Alton Residents' Questions

The questions the Alton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Alton is SAU 72. The Alton School District runs Alton Central School (preschool to grade 8); grades 9–12 attend Prospect Mountain High School, run by the Prospect Mountain joint maintenance agreement (JMA) of Alton and Barnstead. Several questions contain premises (a reserve fund wiped out, an unassigned fund balance run down, special education busing costs doubled, an SAU study committee with Barnstead, seasonal waterfront owners' taxes, a full-time SRO at both schools, low para and substitute pay, three different apps) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Sarah Jenkins (34, Marketing Manager & Mom of Two, Alton Village) — Has a kindergartener and a 3rd grader at Alton Central School.

- **P1.3** Are there any reliable after-school care options right at the school, or am I stuck scrambling to leave work early at 2:50?
