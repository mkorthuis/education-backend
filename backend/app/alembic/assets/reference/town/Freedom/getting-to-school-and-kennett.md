# Getting to School and the Move to Kennett: Freedom's Buses, Care, Summer Help, the Grade 7 Move and Kennett for Freedom Students

Part of the Freedom research (town/Freedom). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Freedom children get to school and what happens when they move on: who runs Freedom's buses, the 2026-27 routes to Freedom Elementary, Kennett Middle School (KMS) and Kennett High School (KHS), with **derived** ride times; the route that turns around at the Maine line on Porter Road; storm and delay notices; the late bus from Conway; Freedom Elementary's before- and after-school programs; summer reading packets, extended school year and summer meals; the move to KMS in grade 7 and how many Freedom students make it; how IEPs are handled under the Freedom–Conway contracts; part-time access to Kennett for homeschoolers; the career center's trades programs; what is published about Freedom students' results at Kennett; vaping, searches and bullying rules at Kennett; and enrolling in the Governor Wentworth Regional School District (Effingham, Ossipee) after a move.

It builds on the Conway, SAU 9 and Jackson files, which already cover KMS's grade 6 team and the 2003 Madison and Freedom grades 7–8 contracts, KMS accelerated math and unified arts, Kennett's AP list, college persistence, career center credentials, welding grant, student assistance program, bullying policy JICK, Kennett late-bus rules and SAU 9's storm procedure. Those are named in the question table, not repeated. The Madison research (`town/Madison/`) had no output files yet when this was written; SAU 13-wide items are left to it.

- **Places:** `district Freedom` (Freedom's buses, programs, contracts and board decisions), `district Conway` (new KMS and Kennett facts), `district Governor Wentworth Regional` (6 facts on enrolling in GWRSD, its curriculum documents and its technology center, for P17.2; the name matches the site's district table, id 208), and no place for 1 statewide rule (RSA 188-E:7).
- **New data concepts:** Freedom's own bus fleet and drivers; scheduled 2026-27 ride times for Freedom middle and high schoolers, including a 5:55 a.m. first pickup and a bus change at Cushing Corner; the Freedom late bus sign-up and its five drop-off points; Freedom residents by school and grade on October 1, 2026 (grades 7–12); KMS grade sizes from KMS's own monthly reports; KMS's spring 2026 visit schedule for Freedom sixth graders; GWRSD's enrollment document list; driving distances from Freedom Elementary to KMS, Kennett, Kingswood and Ossipee Central (OSRM, **derived**).
- **Main sources:** Freedom School Board minutes (July 2024 to the October 26, 2026 special meeting; 21 sets cited) and board packets (cited pages saved); Freedom's 2026-27 bus sheets, family handbook and 2025 transportation handbook; SAU 13's transportation page; the Freedom–Conway grades 9–12 contract (OCR) and the grades 7–8 contract saved by the Conway research; KMS and KHS principal's reports to the Conway board, as printed in Freedom's packets; the KHS 2026-27 handbook (saved by the Conway research); the MWV Career and Technical Center and Lakes Region Technology Center program pages; GWRSD's enrollment and curriculum pages and Kingswood's enrollment page; NH Food Bank 2026 summer flyers; RSA 188-E:7 and RSA 193:1-c; the site's database (read-only).
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Freedom 6th graders merge" at KMS (P1.3): not so. Freedom students stay at Freedom Elementary through grade 6 and join KMS in grade 7; on October 1, 2026, 8 Freedom students were in KMS's grade 7 and 6 in grade 8, and none in grade 6.
  - A KMS grade "of over 100" (P15.1): true for grades 7 (126) and 8 (101) in October 2026; KMS's grade 6, which Freedom students don't join, had 85.
  - "A class of 8–10 kids" (P15.1): close. Freedom's grade 6 had 8 students in 2024-25 (site data) and 12 in 2025-26 (principal's reports); in 2026-27 it has 3, in a combined grade 5/6 class of 10.
  - "Up Route 16" (P4.2): Freedom's sheets name Route 153, not Route 16; the afternoon bus leaves KMS with "Right Route 153."
  - Freedom's buses carry grades 7–12 to Conway (P9.3 premise, here for P4.2): confirmed. Freedom owns its buses and employs its drivers, and both contracts make Freedom responsible for transportation.
  - A late activity bus back to Freedom (P4.3): confirmed; it runs daily for KMS and KHS students who sign up by noon.
