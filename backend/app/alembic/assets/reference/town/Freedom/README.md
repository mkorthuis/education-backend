# Freedom: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Freedom, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`; the people are invented. The research is in the reference library's format (`../../additional-data/README.md`) and uses the shared topics in `../shared-topics.md`. It isn't loaded into the Ask yet; see `../recommended-improvements.md`.

Gathered November 13, 2026. Freedom is the third SAU 13 town, after Madison and Tamworth. It runs Freedom Elementary School (K–6) and sends grades 7–12 to Conway's Kennett Middle and High (SAU 9) under 2003 contracts that end in 2027. SAU 13-wide facts were left to the Madison research.

## Notable findings

- **Kennett's contracts end in 2027 with no replacement yet.** Freedom gave notice in February 2024, paused talks with Conway in February 2026, and in October 2026 was negotiating with Governor Wentworth (Kingswood), Moultonborough, Fryeburg and Conway. Under Conway's September 2026 policy, a town without a contract would pay 125% of cost, which board minutes put at about $66,200 per Freedom student, against offers of $25,000–26,000 elsewhere.
- **Why Kennett costs Freedom more:** Freedom has 4.87% of Kennett High's students but 11.05% of the sending towns' property value, so the formula charges it about 7.03% of the high school budget.
- **Freedom gets no adequacy grant** (except FY2023) because the statewide education tax raised in town exceeds its adequacy cost; its 2025 full-value rate (7.20, DRA, in the site's data) is lower than its neighbors'.
- **Where Freedom's children are (October 1, 2026):** 33 at Freedom Elementary, 14 at KMS, 25 at Kennett High, 6 at Kingswood, 12 in charter schools, 8 in VLACS, 5 with EFAs and 5 in the SAU 13 preschool.
