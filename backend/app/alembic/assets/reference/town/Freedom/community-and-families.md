# Freedom Community and Families

Part of the Freedom research (town/Freedom). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the community side of the Freedom residents' questions: who lives in Freedom (population, ages, household incomes, children and births), the enrollment outlook for Freedom Elementary School, housing and seasonal homes, home sales and assessments, licensed child care near Freedom, home education and Education Freedom Account (EFA) use, the food pantry and other local help, scholarships for Freedom graduates, police, fire and ambulance service, the school's role as a polling place and possible shelter, town government, the PTO and community groups, broadband, and the Freedom Town Forest.

- **8 topics, 80 facts and 11 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/community/` (about 45 MB): Census JSON, the Town of Freedom annual reports for 2020–2025 (and OCR text and birth-page images for 2017–2023), town web pages, NH Child Care Search data, NH REALTORS® reports, the state's BEAD broadband files, OSRM road distances, the Town Forest information paper, Freedom's policy JJJ, and Kennett's scholarship lists.
- **It builds on** the Jackson file `community-and-families.md` (EFA rules, Head Start, Carroll County age projections) and the Conway file `community-and-choice.md` (home education law, Kennett scholarships). It also cites raw files gathered today by the other Freedom agents (`raw/money/` and `raw/school/`). SAU 13-wide items (home education notices, meal applications, McKinney-Vento, the SAU preschool) are left to the Madison research.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Freedom is older and more seasonal than most of New Hampshire.** The 2020 Census counted 1,689 residents, 38.5% aged 65 or older (derived), and 1,200 of 2,062 homes (58.2%, derived) held for seasonal use. The median age, income, poverty and other American Community Survey (ACS) estimates for Freedom are in the site's Census data.
  - **Few births, small and uneven classes.** The town clerk's lists show 34 births from 2017 to 2025, between 2 and 7 a year. The district's October 1 counts put Freedom Elementary at 44 students in grades K–6 in 2025, against 52 in 2024 and 54 in 2016. OPD projects total population growth to about 1,852 by 2035, but it doesn't project children for towns. No enrollment projection for Freedom was found.
  - **Fire and EMS are not all volunteer.** Freedom Fire-Rescue has a full-time chief and members paid a stipend for each call. Ambulance transport is contracted to Action Ambulance, a private service with paid crews on duty 24 hours a day at bases in Center Ossipee and Tamworth. The fire department logged 408 calls in 2025, including 11 with no responders available.
  - **The school's shelter role is not settled.** In 2021 the town's emergency management director called Freedom Elementary a "secondary evacuation center." A generator and a kitchen were explored. In December 2023 the school board's minutes recorded that designating the school as an emergency shelter "is not a requirement of this Select Board." The school is the town's polling place (November 3, 2026) and hosts the annual school district meeting. Town meeting is held at the Town Hall.
  - **No licensed child care in Freedom.** The nearest licensed programs are JJ's Playland, a family child care home in Effingham (5.0 road miles), Family Child Care of Madison (8.6) and Ossipee Concerned Citizens in Center Ossipee (8.9) (derived). Programs' places, ages, openings and weekday hours, as listed with NH DHHS, are in the site's data.
  - **Scholarships come from outside the town's trust funds.** The trustees' MS-9 report lists no scholarship fund. Kennett's lists include the Barney Jones Scholarship for Freedom residents, a Freedom Beach Club award and two regional awards that name Freedom.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Volunteer fire/EMS" (P18.2) is partly out of date. Freedom has a paid full-time fire chief and stipend-paid call members. Since April 1, 2020, ambulance service has come from Action Ambulance's paid 24-hour crews. The state's 2021 community profile still listed the fire department as "Volunteer" and EMS as "Part-time."
  - The school's "use as an emergency shelter" (P20.2) is not confirmed. Town and school records call it a secondary evacuation center (2021) and discuss a shelter role, but say a generator would be needed and no designation is required. No source showed a generator installed or a current shelter designation.
  - "Under 60 students" (P9.2, P10.1) is correct. The count was 44 in grades K–6 on October 1, 2025 (52 in 2024).
  - "Town scholarships or alumni trust funds" (P19.2): the town's trust funds include no scholarship fund. The local awards for Freedom residents are run by other groups and listed by Kennett High School.
- **Answered wholly by earlier files:** 1 of 13 assigned questions (P11.2, SAU 13 fact: see Madison research). P1.2, P6.1 and P6.2 rest mostly on library and Madison facts, with Freedom-specific facts added here.
- **Main gaps:** published police, fire or ambulance response times (the 2024 ambulance dashboard in the town report is too faint to read); the town's 2022 hazard mitigation plan and emergency operations plan (not online); any enrollment projection; births from state vital records (blocked, per the Jackson research); the result of the March 2026 open enrollment article.
