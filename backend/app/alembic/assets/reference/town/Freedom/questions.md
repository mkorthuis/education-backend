# Freedom Residents' Questions

The questions the Freedom research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Freedom is in SAU 13 with Madison and Tamworth. The Freedom School District runs Freedom Elementary School (K–6); its older students attend Conway's A. Crosby Kennett Middle School and Kennett High School (SAU 9). Several questions contain premises (enrollments, class sizes, grades, options) that the research should check rather than assume.
