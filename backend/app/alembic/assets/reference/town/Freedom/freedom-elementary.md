# Freedom Elementary School (K–6)

Part of the Freedom research (town/Freedom). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Freedom Elementary School (FES), the Freedom School District's only school (grades K–6, 40 Loon Lake Road): how its few students are grouped into multi-grade classes, staffing and teacher workload, reading and math curriculum and screening, reading intervention, enrichment and the new math enrichment role, STEAM, band and the absence of a world language, special education and related services (who is on staff and who is contracted), counseling and behavior support, outdoor learning with Tin Mountain and the King Pine ski program, devices and internet, meals and weekend food bags, help with fees, building security and emergency plans, and volunteers and community partners.

**Premises checked**
- **"Under 60 students":** true. FES reported 35 students in September 2026 (33 full-time plus 2 part-time) and 33 on October 1, 2026, down from 52 in September 2024 and 44 in September 2025.
- **"Multi-grade" and "multi-age" classes:** true. In 2026-27 FES has four homerooms: grades K–2, two grade 3–4 classes and grades 5–6, plus a K–2 math class.
- **"Class of 8–10 kids":** close. 2026-27 homerooms hold 7 to 10 students; single grades held 1 to 9 students on October 1, 2026.
- **"King Pine" ski program:** true. The 2026-27 winter program runs on eight Thursdays at King Pine (January 7 to March 4, 2027), funded through a $5,000 "KING PINE (ESSC) & ADAPTIVE PROGRAM" line; ski fittings are at Cranmore. No family fee was found.
- **"Band instrument rentals at Freedom Elementary":** band exists (grades 4–6; 13 of 19 eligible students in September 2026) and families returned "instrument paperwork", but no rental cost or rental program was found.
- **"Freedom Town Forest":** the town forest exists, but no FES program in it was found. FES's documented outdoor partners are Tin Mountain and the Green Mountain Conservation Group.
- **Fee help via the Ossipee Children's Fund:** the FES parent page still links its application, but the fund's own site says it has closed and no longer accepts applications.
