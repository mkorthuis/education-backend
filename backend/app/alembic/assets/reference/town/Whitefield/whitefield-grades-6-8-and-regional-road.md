# Whitefield's Grades 6-8 and the Move to Regional Road

Part of the Whitefield research (town/Whitefield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Whitefield families asked about the older grades and the move to White Mountains Regional Middle High School (WMRMHS) on Regional Road: how grades 7 and 8 are kept apart from the high school, the bus to Regional Road, the 7th grade transition, clubs and sports, the phone rule, homework, crisis plans and entrances, a welcoming school, English learner support, newsletters and portals in Spanish, notices when a child misses class, support for IEPs, and substance use and vaping. It writes only Whitefield's side. The district-wide facts (the policies, handbook rules, bus contract, special education process, the phone policy and discipline) are already in the Lancaster files and are cited by file, topic and fact number rather than written again: `town/Lancaster/white-mountains-regional-student-life-and-support.md`, `white-mountains-regional-high-and-career.md`, `money-and-governance.md`, and, for translation and the Alma portal, `lancaster-elementary-and-families.md` and `lancaster-elementary-support-and-choices.md`.

- **Counts:** 10 topics, 62 facts, 5 number tables.
- **Main sources:** the 2026-27 WMRMHS Student and Family Handbook and middle school Program of Studies; the district's 2026-27 special education manual and overview slides; the English learner (ESOL) plan updated July 15, 2026; the Alma parent portal guide; the 2025-26 (scanned) and 2026-27 bus route sheets; the district, WMRMHS and Whitefield School live feeds (read through the site's public API); School Board minutes from September 2025 to November 2026; the 2025 Youth Risk Behavior Survey report for the high school; the Student Services annual report.
- **Biggest findings for Whitefield:** (1) grades 7 and 8 have their own schedule, lunch, dances and wing, but share the building, and the only student view in the minutes (a high schooler) is that integration is going well; (2) Whitefield's 7th graders ride the same buses as elementary riders, 10 to 14 minutes longer, and the 2026-27 sheet gives Pink Line riders the same or earlier pickups than last year and Blue Line (Elm Street) riders later ones; (3) the district has one English learner teacher for all schools, and none of its live-feed posts carries a translation; (4) the high school's 2025 survey shows vaping and early drinking close to the region's and somewhat above the state's, and more students saying they stayed home at least one day because they felt unsafe (16.5% against 8.1% for the state); (5) no survey of grades 7 and 8 was found, so the survey figures describe only the high school.
- **Main gaps:** how hallways, passing time and shared spaces are managed for grades 7 and 8; any survey of 7th and 8th graders or parents on the move; homework minutes; which building the English learner teacher works in and how often; whether Alma's Spanish portal is turned on; where an out-of-district student from Whitefield would go and how that is paid per student; the 2027-28 bus routes; the version of the 2026-27 sheet before the Blue Line was added; the September 24, 2026 board minutes; NH DHHS survey pages (blocked).
- **New topics proposed:** none. The facts use the shared topics, including `district-goals-and-performance-reporting` for the survey results.
