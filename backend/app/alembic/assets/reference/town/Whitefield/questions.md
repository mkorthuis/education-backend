# Whitefield Residents' Questions

The questions the Whitefield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Whitefield's students attend the White Mountains Regional School District (SAU 36), shared with Lancaster, Carroll, Jefferson and neighboring towns: Whitefield Elementary School (preschool to grade 6) and White Mountains Regional Middle and High School (grades 7–12) on Regional Road in Whitefield, with career programs at the Hugh J. Gallen Career and Technical Center in Littleton and White Mountains Community College in Berlin. Several questions contain premises (7th and 8th grade having moved up to the high school building, a culinary arts and criminal justice program, an agriculture and maple sugaring program, Early College classes through White Mountains Community College, a Whitefield Elementary with spare classrooms, SAU 36 board meetings, teams combining with other towns) that the research should check rather than assume.
