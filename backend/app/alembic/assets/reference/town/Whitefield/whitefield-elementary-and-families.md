# Whitefield Elementary and Young Families

Part of the Whitefield research (town/Whitefield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Whitefield Elementary School (preschool to grade 6, in the White Mountains Regional School District, SAU 36) and the town services young families use with it: public preschool and Head Start, the town's after-school program, snow-delay notices, the school day, special education and speech staffing, counseling, bullying and bus reports, meals, fees and help with costs, advanced learners, STEM, arts, class sizes, reading results and whether families can leave the district. Whitefield Elementary is the district's other elementary school; Lancaster's finished files cover the district-wide material and are cited, not repeated.

- **Counts:** 19 topics, 91 facts and 6 number tables. All topics are shared topics; no new topics are proposed.
- **Main sources:** the school's 2026-27 handbook (all 52 pages), the district's FY27 proposed budget detail report (read by account line), the school's and the district's live feeds (2,044 posts from March 2021 to November 2026, read through the website's public data feed), the Town of Whitefield's 2026-27 ASAP packet (a scanned PDF, read by text recognition), board policies IFA, IIB and IMAH, the district's Title I plan and text-message terms, the NH DOE 2026 assessment file, Tri-County Community Action Program's Head Start page, White Mountain Science's pages, the high school's 2025-26 and the middle school's 2026-27 programs of studies, and the site's own database (read-only).
- **Most useful findings:** (1) after-school care to 6:00 p.m. is the town's ASAP program, K to grade 6, $10 a day, with a bus from the school and a sliding fee on request; (2) the district posted its morning-of two-hour-delay notices between 5:09 and 5:55 a.m. (median about 5:32), marked them for text delivery, and families must have opted in to texts; (3) Whitefield's third-grade reading proficiency rose from 17% in 2022 to 44% in 2026 (state 51%); (4) the FY27 budget charges 0.65 of a speech pathologist and 0.53 of a speech assistant to Whitefield, one counselor (down from two) and a general music teacher at half time (down from full); (5) Whitefield's resident students can't tuition out, because the March 2026 open enrollment vote set 0% of residents eligible to leave.
- **Main gaps:** this year's kindergarten and grade 1 headcounts, preschool seats for fall 2027, which days the counselor and speech staff are in the building, field trip costs and any fee waiver, boots or coat help beyond the school's coat drive, and any gifted program details (see Not retrieved).
- **Privacy note for reviewers:** no student, parent or resident is named. Staff are cited by role (the handbook and feeds name them; names are removed from the saved feed copies and from the facts). The board minutes copy is an excerpt that leaves out the roll call and public comment. The ASAP packet is a blank form (no family information). No address was sent to any service; every request was an ordinary page or data-feed request with the identifier `nhfacts-research` and `webmaster@nhfacts.com` in the User-Agent. Nothing personal was sent to any outside service.
