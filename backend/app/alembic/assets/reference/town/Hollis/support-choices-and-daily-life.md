# Support, Choices and Daily Life in Hollis

Part of the Hollis research (town/Hollis). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers special transportation and who to call about it; school buses on narrow, dirt and dead-end roads; religious holidays and absences; what Hollis-Brookline High offers students interested in science, math, engineering and computer science, set beside the Academy for Science and Design (ASD); why the fall calendar has early releases and teacher days; homeschooled, charter, private and EFA students in Hollis and cooperative classes, sports, band, clubs and libraries, and coming back to school; renters' access to buses and fee help; the fees families pay; how newcomers connect with the parent community; and how snow days and delays are decided.

- **11 topics, 86 facts and no number tables.** Places: `district Hollis` (Hollis Primary and Hollis Upper Elementary), `district Hollis-Brookline Cooperative` (the middle and high school), `sau 41` (the shared bus contract, bus barn, registration, meals and volunteering), `town Hollis` (roads and polling places), and no place for state law.

## school-calendar-rules: Aligning School Calendars With Regional Career and Technical Centers

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: New Hampshire rules that shape school calendars, such as aligning sending districts' calendars with regional career and technical education centers

1. New Hampshire law requires each regional career and technical education agreement (RCTEA) to include a calendar agreement aligning sending districts' calendars with the career center's, allowing no more than 10 instructional days, from after Labor Day through the last student day, on which the calendars differ, and encourages aligning teacher in-service days; the commissioner may not approve an agreement that doesn't comply, and annual waivers for districts working toward alignment could not run past July 1, 2026.
   - citation: RSA 188-E:1-a, III
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/188-E/188-E-mrg.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Statute text: "Each RCTEA shall include a calendar conformity agreement to comply with RSA 188-E:5, VII(a) and (b) by aligning the school calendars of sending districts with the school calendars of CTE programs at the receiving district. ... Agreements should address schedule alignment needs such as: disruptions due to differing start/stop times, unscheduled school closures or events, and daily class start/stop times. ... RCTEA sending and receiving districts are encouraged to align teacher in-service days ... There may not be more than 10 instructional days following Labor Day through the last student day of the school calendar year on which one or more of the school calendars of the districts within the agreement are not aligned. ... The commissioner shall not approve a RCTEA that does not comply with this paragraph ... but a waiver for this purpose shall not be extended beyond July 1, 2026." Agreements are renewed every 4 years (RSA 188-E:1-a, I). Pelham's policy IC cites the same rule (Pelham `support-buses-and-choices.md`, cte-center-programs 8, note). Raw: raw/support/rsa-188-E.txt
2. New Hampshire law says all career and technical education students must be given access to their programs for the programs' instructional time, and the commissioner may waive this case by case on a joint application by the student's career center and sending district.
   - citation: RSA 188-E:5, VII(a)–(b)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/188-E/188-E-mrg.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
