# Hollis Residents' Questions

The questions the Hollis research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hollis is in SAU 41 with Brookline. The Hollis School District runs Hollis Primary School (preschool to grade 3) and Hollis Upper Elementary School (grades 4–6). Grades 7–12 attend the Hollis-Brookline Cooperative School District: Hollis-Brookline Middle School (7–8) and Hollis-Brookline High School (9–12). Students may take career programs at the Nashua Technology Center or Hudson's Palmer Center. Both districts decide their budgets at annual meetings (a 2026 petition to move the cooperative to official ballot failed, 216–234). Several questions contain premises (a Hollis Primary renovation voted down in March, HPS at 8:23, HUES at 8:30 and a 7:35 middle school start, Latin Honors replacing class rank, about 675 high school students, a support staff contract through 2028, Hollis Primary at 400 students in a building for 340, Brookline growing faster, extra state adequacy aid) that the research should check rather than assume.
