# Hollis-Brookline Middle and High School: Schedules, Courses, Career Programs, Activities and Rules

Part of the Hollis research (town/Hollis). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hollis-Brookline Middle School (HBMS, grades 7–8) and Hollis-Brookline High School (HBHS, grades 9–12), both run by the Hollis-Brookline Cooperative School District in SAU 41: start times and bell schedules, the shared bus runs, phones, the rules on AI, bullying, math acceleration at HBMS, Latin Honors and grade weighting, AP and honors sections, practical and business courses, work-study and senior schedules, credit for courses from another school, HBHS's side of career and technical education (CTE) at the Nashua Technology Center and Hudson's Palmer Center, sports participation and team rules, drama, counseling and class sizes.

- **Counts:** 12 topics, 98 facts and 6 number tables. Places: `district Hollis-Brookline Cooperative` 92 facts, `sau 41` 3, no place 3 (a proposed research topic on how colleges use class rank).
- **Sources:** SAU 41's FAQ, bussing and math placement pages; HBHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies (107 pages), 2026-27 school profile, student/parent handbook, bell schedules, counseling, CTE, ELO and parking pages; the HBMS 2026-27 handbook and bell schedule; the Hollis cooperative bus routes; the HB Athletics site, the 2026-27 Interscholastic Athletic Agreement and coach handbook; the HBHS Performing Arts site; cooperative board agenda attachments from the SAU 41 Diligent portal (principals' and athletic director's reports, course enrollment reports for 2026-27, the May 2026 class size presentation, CTE counts, the Region 16 CTE agreement, the NEASC visiting team report of March 2026, policies IIB, IKL, IK, JF and the NHSBA sample AI policy EHAG, the 2027-28 Program of Studies change outline); 11 sets of cooperative board and policy committee minutes (May 2025 to November 2026); 2019 Top Ten subcommittee minutes.
