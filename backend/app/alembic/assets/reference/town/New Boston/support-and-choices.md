Part of the New Boston research (town/New Boston). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers how New Boston Central School (NBCS) serves students who need more challenge (no gifted program; school-wide enrichment paid largely by the PTA, music, STEAM plans, Joe's English), special education at NBCS (staffing, the FY28 cuts of one special education teacher and two special education paraeducators, case loads, the state's parent survey and its 2025-26 program review) and how IEPs carry into Mountain View and Goffstown High under the AREA agreement (New Boston pays separately for individual aides there); homeschooled and EFA students' access to NBCS (policy IHBG of 2018 and the draft policy JJJ that adds EFA students, first read November 18, 2026), EFA providers near New Boston, and entering Goffstown High from home education (placement, transfer credits, the 16-course rule for the Academic Diploma); the move from NBCS to Mountain View (step-up activities, teacher meetings, the transfer bus, New Boston students following Goffstown's policies); bullying policy (the 2022 policy JICK and its HB 131 rewrite at first reading); counselors and the nurse; enrollment by race and ethnicity and how the school welcomes families; Nature's Classroom, the ski program and clubs; science materials; the July 2026 lead-in-water results; change-of-school options and charter schools; and attendance rules for anxiety or chronic illness (excused absences, Mountain View's excessive absence indicator, homebound instruction and home tutoring).
