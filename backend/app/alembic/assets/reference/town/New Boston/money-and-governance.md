# Money and Governance in New Boston

Part of the New Boston research (town/New Boston). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered.** How New Boston's school budget divides between New Boston Central School (NBCS) and tuition to Goffstown, and a rough split of the local school tax rate; the district's own tax-rate estimates against the actual rates, the 2026 town-wide assessment update (net valuation up about 48%) and why the 2026 rate is not yet set; revenue trends (local taxes up 4.97% a year, state adequacy 2.29%); the November 2026 school tax cap vote, which fell short in New Boston (59.5%) while the SAU cap passed SAU-wide; the March 2026 school votes; the FY28 request (3.79% lower on a like-for-like basis) and its drivers; cost per pupil over ten years; fund balance, the 5% contingency fund and the summer 2026 septic failure; the NBEA teachers' contract; SAU 19's budget since FY2024 and New Boston's share, New Boston's share of top administrators' pay, and the FY28 move of six SAU positions; the NBCS heating and ventilation project (an EMC study of about $8 million of needs, about $3 million of Priority 1 work, the board's 4–1 vote to pursue a municipal lease rather than a bond, and the October 2026 lease quotes), the capital reserve fund, the town plan's $3.5 million addition bond deferred to 2031 and the absence of bonded debt; AREA tuition budgeted against actual payments, and how New Boston checks them; the STA bus contract and what busing costs; staff years of service and staffing cuts; and the board's meetings, video, public comment rules, the FY28 budget calendar and deliberative session times and turnout.

**Counts.** 9 topics (8 shared town topics and the library's `budget-deliberative-session`), 68 facts, 3 number tables.

**Main sources.** New Boston School District's FY28 requested budget packet and line-item budgets (November 18, 2026); the FY27 deliberative session slides, handout and Mandatory Report to Voters; the 2025 voters' guide; the FY2025 audit; New Boston School Board minutes and packets from January to November 2026, including the September 23, 2026 facilities hearing and the October 28 lease memo; the NBCS building project presentation and EMC estimates; policies BEA, BEDH and IC; the town's 2026 assessment notice, the 2026 MS-1, the 2026–2031 capital improvements plan and the March and November 2026 election results; SAU 19's budget packets and board page; RSA 40:13.

**Main gaps.** No five-year tax-rate projection exists in any district document; the 2026 tax rate was not set by November 21, 2026. No teacher turnover rate is published (the federal 2023-24 data show zeros for NBCS). No bus ridership counts or STA contract terms were posted. No SAU 19 staffing history was found. The SAU 19 board's November 10, 2026 votes (superintendent, SAU budget, how to count Question 2 across the SAU) were not posted. New Boston's vote on the 2022 AREA renewal was not found.
