# New Boston Residents' Questions

The questions the New Boston research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

New Boston is in SAU 19 with Goffstown. The New Boston School District runs New Boston Central School (NBCS); its students in grades 7–12 attend Mountain View Middle School and Goffstown High School under an AREA agreement renewed for 2024–2034. Goffstown's schools are researched in `../Goffstown/`. Several questions contain premises (full-day kindergarten now, a Rec after-school waitlist, the HB 1300 cap vote, a growing SAU office, a new superintendent, water testing at Central School, Wi-Fi on buses) that the research should check rather than assume.
