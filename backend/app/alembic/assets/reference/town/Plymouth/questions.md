# Plymouth Residents' Questions

The questions the Plymouth research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Plymouth is in SAU 48. The Plymouth School District runs Plymouth Elementary School (preschool to grade 8); grades 9–12 attend Plymouth Regional High School, run by the Pemi-Baker Regional School District, which has a career and technical center and takes students from several nearby towns. Mountain Village Charter School is in Plymouth, and Plymouth State University is in town. Several questions contain premises (about half of PES students proficient in math, PRHS reading around 72%, 30 to 55 children a grade at PES, a TV and Media program, an Outdoor Recreation Leadership program, different early-release days at PES and PRHS, a high tax bill because of PRHS's tuition students) that the research should check rather than assume.
