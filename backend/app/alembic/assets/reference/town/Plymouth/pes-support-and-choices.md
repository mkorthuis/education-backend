# PES's Upper Grades, Student Support and School Choices in Plymouth

Part of the Plymouth research (town/Plymouth). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

This file covers Plymouth Elementary School's (PES) grades 7 and 8 (math, Algebra I, Spanish I) and the move to Plymouth Regional High School (PRHS); special education at PES and PRHS (speech evaluations, related services, paraprofessionals); translation for families; homeschooled and Education Freedom Account (EFA) students in PES and PRHS classes and sports; Mountain Village Charter School (MVCS), Holderness School and EFA providers near Plymouth; and parent support for families new to IEPs.

**Counts:** 15 topics, 84 facts, no number tables. One long list: `raw/support/efa-providers-plymouth-area-2026-11-21.csv` (47 EFA marketplace providers within 15 miles of PES, described in `town-school-options-nearby` 11). Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 16 MB).

**Main sources:** the PES 2026-27 handbook; PES grade 7 and 8 team and math pages; PES Student Services pages; Plymouth School District policies JJJ (September 14, 2026), the 1996 Home Education policy, KB (Title I family engagement) and SAU 48 policy IHBAA; Pemi-Baker policy JJJ (October 6, 2026); eight sets of Plymouth School Board minutes (September 2025 to September 2026) and eight sets of Pemi-Baker board minutes (January 2025 to October 2026); the PRHS 2026-27 9th grade program of studies and handbook; SAU 48's Student Services pages, Child Find notice and 2023 LAU (English learner) plan; SAU 48 job openings; Mountain Village's own pages; Holderness School's tuition and aid pages; the EFA marketplace feed; the Parent Information Center's events; Lakes Region Community Services; Whole Village Family Resource Center; Plymouth Recreation.

**Main gaps:** no source says how PES picks students for Algebra I. PRHS does give high school credit for it: its 2026-27 handbook says students who complete "Algebra I or a World Language course at their respective middle schools will receive high school credit for those courses" (town/Thornton/plymouth-regional-from-thornton.md, school-programs-after-grade-6 8). No source measures whether PRHS students with IEPs get all their one-on-one time; the site's staff data and one posted PRHS paraprofessional opening are all that was found. No PES source says when speech or OT sessions are scheduled (whether they fall during art, music or PE). No source translates report cards or permission slips by routine; the LAU plan promises interpretation or translation of required notices on request. No data compare Mountain Village graduates' results at PRHS, and Holderness School doesn't say how much aid Plymouth families receive. No IEP parent support group meets in Plymouth itself in the sources found. Mountain Village's charter (on NH DOE's site) was blocked.
