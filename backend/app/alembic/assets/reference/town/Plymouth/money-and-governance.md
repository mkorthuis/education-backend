# Plymouth: Money, Taxes, Governance and the Two School Calendars

Part of the Plymouth research (town/Plymouth). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 11 topics, 70 facts and 3 number tables on the two districts that serve Plymouth, the Plymouth School District (Plymouth Elementary School, preschool to grade 8) and the Pemi-Baker Regional School District (Plymouth Regional High School), and on SAU 48, which runs both.
- **Taxes and the cost split:** how Pemi-Baker divides its cost among its seven member towns (half by students, half by property value), Plymouth's 24.0064% share for 2026-27 and why it is lower than its share of students; each district's own estimate of its 2026 effect on Plymouth's tax rate; about 74% of Plymouth's local school property tax goes to the Plymouth School District and 26% to Pemi-Baker (**derived**).
- **Tuition students:** PRHS had 27 tuition students of 645 in September 2026; tuition is budgeted at $525,000 of a $19.35 million budget. The other students come from the seven member towns, which pay through the assessment, not tuition.
- **Budgets:** both districts' 2026-27 warrants, what passed, what drove the increases (health insurance and special education for Pemi-Baker; staffing for speech, PE and nursing, administration and transportation for Plymouth), special education spending reports, the 2027-28 work under way, and the new teacher contracts with their 2026-27 and 2027-28 starting pay.
- **Governance:** SAU 48's services, budget and cost split, shared contracts, meeting schedules, Zoom and YouTube access, the 2027 hearing and vote dates, and how often residents speak at meetings (**derived** tallies).
- **Calendars:** the two 2026-27 calendars side by side: PES uses early-release afternoons, PRHS uses two-hour delayed openings for teacher in-service, and the two end on different days.
- **Roads:** the town's school crossing guard, the 2025 repaving of Old Ward Bridge Road and its sidewalks, the Highland Street project and roundabout plan, and a PSU student study of sidewalk gaps.
- **Open enrollment:** PES's 1990 tuition-student policy and the 2026 open enrollment articles in Plymouth and Campton.

**Main sources:** Pemi-Baker's 2025 Annual School District Report (scanned; transcribed from page images), its 2026-27 adopted budget, pie charts, enrollment history and capital plan; the Plymouth School District's 2026-27 adopted budget and the Town of Plymouth 2025 Annual Report (school district report, MS-26, minutes, capital plan, police and highway reports); Plymouth and Pemi-Baker school board minutes August 2025–November 2026; both teacher contracts for 2026–2029; SAU 48's summary of services, 2027-28 budget sheets and allocation; both 2026-27 calendars, PRHS's event list and bell schedules; the 2027 district meeting calendar; select board minutes 2025–2026; PES policy JFAB; the Campton 2026 warrant; the site's database.
