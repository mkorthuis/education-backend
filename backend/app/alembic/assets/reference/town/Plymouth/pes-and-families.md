# Plymouth Elementary School and Young Families

Part of the Plymouth research (town/Plymouth). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Plymouth Elementary School (PES, preschool to grade 8) and young families for questions P1.1–P1.3, P3.1–P3.3, P5.1–P5.3, P9.1, P9.2, P13.1–P13.3, P14.3, P16.2 and P16.3: morning arrival and the ASK before-school program; after-school care (the Pemi Youth Center's PYC Junior at PES, which replaced the Recreation Department's care model in September 2026) and Plymouth Recreation's clubs; storm notices; buses; meal prices, applications and lunch periods; the PES preschool's 2026-27 tuition and admission; kindergarten's cutoff, day length and homerooms; homerooms by grade; Plymouth State University (PSU) student teachers; reading specialists and intervention; counseling and the Student Assistance Program; guardianship papers; Chromebooks; bullying, including on the bus; the PES Winter Program (skiing and other activities) and SAU 48 discounted ski passes; Head Start and the PSU child care center.

**Counts:** 16 topics, 67 facts, 2 number tables (`plymouth_pes_homerooms_2026_27`, `plymouth_pes_winter_program_fees_2027`).

**Main sources:** the PES 2026-2027 Parent-Student Handbook (approved August 10, 2026); PES web pages (busing, meals, counseling, about, administration, approved policies); policies JICK (approved September 2026), Kindergarten Admission (scanned, transcribed), EFAA and the Food Service Payments policy; PES's drop-off and pick-up procedures; the 2026-2027 preschool registration packet (scanned, transcribed); the 2026-2027 Title I schoolwide plan; the PES weekly newsletter of November 20, 2026 (Winter Program pages); the kindergarten and grade 3 schedules; the SAU 48 meals FAQ and residency notice; the student services and Student Assistance Program sites; PES school board minutes, August 2025 to October 2026; the Plymouth Recreation Department site, Rec Club manual and PYC Junior flyers (images, transcribed); the Pemi Youth Center site; Tri-County CAP's Head Start page; the Boys & Girls Clubs page for the PSU center; PSU's clinical-experience page; Pease Public Library pages; Ski NH's passport page.

**Main gaps:** no time of day is posted for SAU 48's storm and delay notices; the ASK before-school program's hours and any fee are not posted; no bus routes, stop lists or ride times are posted (families call the PES office); kindergarten class sizes for 2026-27 aren't published (NH DOE's October 1, 2026 counts were not retrieved, see "Not retrieved"); no current list names PES as a PSU student-teaching site; the PSU child care center's tuition isn't posted; Plymouth Recreation's Fall 2026 booklet was paused by its owner.

## district-transportation: PES Buses, Bus-Stop Rules and Storm Notices

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: Bus service: who runs it, routes and times, eligibility, late and activity buses, storm closings and delays, and transport to schools outside the district
