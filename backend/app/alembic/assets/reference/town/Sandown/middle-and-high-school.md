# Timberlane Regional Middle and High School From Sandown

Part of the Sandown research (town/Sandown). Gathered November 21, 2026 by agent 3. Sandown's grades 6–12 attend Timberlane Regional Middle School (TRMS) and Timberlane Regional High School (TRHS) in Plaistow; most of what these schools offer is already in the shared Timberlane files (`town/timberlane/`), which this file cites rather than repeats.

## Summary

This file adds Sandown's side of the middle and high school years and the district-wide facts the shared files lacked: the morning and afternoon bus times for two Sandown stops and the shared middle and high school runs; driving distances from Sandown to TRHS, Pinkerton Academy, Salem High and Next Charter School; how many Timberlane students attend CTE (career and technical education), seats at the two centers and the mileage repayment for students who drive to Pinkerton; the CTE, athletics and activities budget lines from FY2022 to FY2027; the FY2026 cut of a high school engineering teacher and the culinary course change; the music budget, ensemble participation and the TRHS music and theater courses; SNHU's dual enrollment fee; counselors' daily load; TRHS's 2026 wrestling title, its football and track championship history and the size of its athletic program; what athletes' families pay for; Sandown North and the secondary schools beside Hampstead's and Windham's (site data and GreatSchools); Pinkerton's tuition-student route for Sandown families; how many Sandown eighth graders go on to TRHS's ninth grade; and Sandown's young adults in the 2010 and 2020 Census.

- question_type: local_practice
