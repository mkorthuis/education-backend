# Sandown Central, Sandown North and Young Families

Part of the Sandown research (town/Sandown). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Sandown's two Timberlane Regional School District schools from a family's side: The Learning Center (TLC) at Sandown Central (preschool and kindergarten) and Sandown North Elementary School (grades 1–5). It explains why Sandown children change buildings after kindergarten, the schools' staff and specialists, kindergarten sizes, preschool history, reading and math help, advanced learners, English learner staff, screen time, pickup and building security, meals, and early release days. It also covers care after school and preschools in town, how families hear about board votes and calendar changes, the buildings' age and space, and the enrollment outlook for Sandown. Main sources: the two schools' websites and staff directories, the 2026-27 elementary handbook, school newsletters, school board, Curriculum and Assessment, CIP and Budget Committee minutes and packets (2024–2026), district policies, the September 2017 "Sandown Schools" facilities review, the NESDEC November 2026 Sandown projection, the Town of Sandown's recreation pages, Playmates Learning Center's own site, and NH DHHS child care listings from the site's database.

- **17 topics, 52 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 41 MB), with board and committee minutes in `raw/elementary/board/` and database extracts in `raw/elementary/data/`.
- **Topics:** sixteen shared slugs (thirteen district: `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-preschool`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `district-facilities-and-capital`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-student-support`, `district-technology-and-phones`, `district-safety-and-security`, `district-meals`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-family-communication`; three town: `town-community-profile`, `town-child-care`, `town-recreation-and-summer`) and the library topic `kindergarten-day-length` with its own kind and description. No new topic is proposed.
- **Places:** `district Timberlane Regional` for both schools and district practice; `town Sandown` for the NESDEC town report, child care and recreation.
- **Main gaps:** how early storm closings and delays are usually decided (no source gives a time); the number of children in each Sandown Central kindergarten room and whether each room has its own full-time aide; screen time for kindergartners specifically (the district's survey groups all elementary grades); whether any reading or ESL position at Sandown North was cut (none found, but staffing histories by school aren't published); a school-age rate or Sandown North pickup for Playmates or Sandlot; and the Sandown subcommittee's first findings (its first planning session could be in December 2026).

| P19.1 | Sandown Central might be "packed" | The building is large for its use.
