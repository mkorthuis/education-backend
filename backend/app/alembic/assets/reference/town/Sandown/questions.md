# Sandown Residents' Questions

The questions the Sandown research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Sandown's students attend the Timberlane Regional School District (SAU 106), shared with Atkinson, Danville and Plaistow. In Sandown it runs Sandown Central School (preschool and kindergarten) and Sandown North Elementary School (grades 1–5); grades 6–12 attend Timberlane Regional Middle School and High School in Plaistow. Timberlane is researched in `../timberlane/`, `../Atkinson/` and `../Plaistow/`; the files here add Sandown's side. Several questions contain premises (a Timberlane Learning Center preschool at Sandown Central, a 2.5% tax cap, over a million dollars shifted to outside special education placements, a $25 million lease-purchase, a $2 million budget committee cut, the four-town cost formula, leaving the district, meetings turning into arguments, SAU meetings in Plaistow) that the research should check rather than assume.
