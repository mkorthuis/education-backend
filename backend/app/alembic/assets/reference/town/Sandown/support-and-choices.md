# Special Education, Support and School Choices for Sandown Families

Part of the Sandown research (town/Sandown). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Sandown families ask about special education, staffing and choices in the Timberlane Regional School District (SAU 106): how much special education work Timberlane now buys from contractors and why, the state's 2026 review of Timberlane's IEPs and what the district is doing about it, the special education staff at Sandown North and TLC at Sandown Central, the in-district programs and out-of-district placements, paraeducators' pay and hours under the 2026–28 support staff contract, vacant and eliminated positions, how many teachers stayed at Sandown's schools from one year to the next, the move from Sandown North to Timberlane Regional Middle School (TRMS), the late bus to Sandown, homeless students' right to stay in school, what happens when a family moves out of Sandown mid-year, coming back from home education, and Education Freedom Accounts (EFAs) and EFA providers near Sandown.

- **9 topics, 38 facts and 2 number tables.** All topics are shared topics. Places: `district Timberlane Regional` (Timberlane, Sandown North, Sandown Central, TRMS and their policies) and `town Sandown` (EFA statistics and providers near Sandown). Tables: `timberlane_teacher_retention_crdc_2023_24` and `timberlane_sped_contracted_services`. Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 40 MB).
- **Main findings:**
  - **Contracted special education has grown fast.** Timberlane's contracted special education services rose from $757,285 (FY2023) to $3,530,006 (FY2026); contracted staffing (aides, behavior technicians, psychologists, speech assistants) was $2,133,772 of FY2026's total, up from about $312,000. The district says 69 contracted staff work across its schools, and its own slide calls contracted staffing, not more identified students, the main driver. Identified students fell from 732 (2023-24) to 667 (2026-27).
  - **The state's 2026 review** of 36 IEPs found 18 problems among 792 possible findings (2.3%), mostly documentation, and requires Timberlane to check whether services were delivered or compensatory services are owed. The district reports every flagged IEP amended and a new service-logging system (DASH) in use from October 1, 2026; the work runs to July 8, 2027.
  - **Paraeducator pay and vacancies.** Under the support staff contract approved in March 2026, paraeducators earn $16.84 to $26.98 an hour in 2026-27 (up $1.00 in 2027-28) for a 7-hour day; a full year at step 1 is about $21,690 (**derived**). Of 49 vacant positions in the FY2028 roster, 26 are special education paraeducator positions and 10 special education specialist positions (**derived** from the roster). The district eliminated 26 special education paraeducator positions in FY2025–FY2026, many of them open.
  - **Teacher turnover at Sandown North.** In the federal Civil Rights Data Collection for 2023-24, 19 of Sandown North's 31 teachers had also taught there the year before (61%, **derived**), against about 84% statewide; 7 full-time-equivalent teachers were in their first year of teaching. Sandown Central kept 8 of 10. No newer data were found.
  - **Moving out of Sandown.** Timberlane's policy JFAB lets a student who moves out of the district mid-year finish the year if the new district agrees to pay prorated tuition (free after March 31), but Timberlane won't bus non-residents. Danville is a Timberlane town, so grades 6–12 don't change; elementary children are assigned to the school in the town where they live.
  - **Late bus.** Bus 131 runs Tuesday to Thursday about 3:45–4:00 p.m. through Plaistow and Sandown (Main Street, Royal Range, North Road, Fremont Road, Phillipswood and others); basketball practices at TRMS end between 5:30 and 8:30 p.m., after the last posted bus.
  - **EFAs.** 62 Sandown students were eligible for an EFA in 2026-27 (2 waitlisted). The EFA marketplace lists one provider in Sandown and two "co-op"-tagged providers nearby (in Hampstead and Kingston).
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** 2 of 12 (P9.1, homeschoolers in sports and band; P10.2, bullying and phones at TRMS). The Timberlane shared files (`town/timberlane/`), Atkinson's and Plaistow's files and the library cover most state rules and district policies; this file adds Sandown's side and new district-wide figures.
- **Main gaps:** teacher or paraeducator turnover after 2023-24 (no state attrition data; the next federal collection isn't out); how many TRMS students have paraprofessional support or one-to-one aides; how many Timberlane students are placed out of district (only "<1%"); whether a student moving between Timberlane towns mid-year may finish the year at the old elementary school (no document addresses it); the state's own monitoring report (only the district's presentation of it was found); how the new district (Sanborn Regional, for Kingston) handles tuition for a student finishing a year in Timberlane.
- **Topic proposal:** none; the shared topics fit.
