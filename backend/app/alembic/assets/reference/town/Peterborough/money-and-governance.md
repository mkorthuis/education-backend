# Peterborough: Money, Taxes and Governance of the ConVal School District

Part of the Peterborough research (town/Peterborough). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics, 86 facts and 6 number tables on how the Contoocook Valley (ConVal) School District (SAU 1) is paid for and governed, and what that means for Peterborough taxpayers. District-wide facts are placed `district Contoocook Valley` so the other ConVal towns' research can cite them; Peterborough's own tax bill, revaluation and exemptions are placed `town Peterborough`.

- **The tax bill:** in 2025 Peterborough raised $13,935,239 in local education tax and kept $1,419,713 of state education tax for ConVal, 53.1% of its $28,902,538 tax effort (**derived**), so about 53 cents of each property tax dollar went to ConVal. The 2025 revaluation raised the town's assessed value 49.4% (**derived**) and the total rate fell from $32.50 to $22.35. Peterborough's local education commitment fell 7.6% in 2025 while the town's municipal commitment rose 19.1%. A property's 2025 bill was higher than its 2024 bill only if its assessment rose more than about 45% (**derived**). The 2026 rate had not been set on November 22, 2026.
- **Why the school share is large:** ConVal's spending grew 35.6% from 2015 to 2025 (about inflation), while its district assessment grew 59.2% and total state revenue fell 4.6% (ConVal's Question 2 report). The cost is split 50% by resident pupils and 50% by property value (Articles of Agreement, Article 8); Peterborough's share for 2025 bills was 31.88%.
- **Consolidation:** the Articles require elementary schools in eight towns, and changing that takes two-thirds of district voters. The board's 2024 article to allow closing Temple, Francestown, Dublin and Pierce (Bennington) schools, on a consultant's study with $2.4 million or more in estimated yearly savings, failed 45% to 55%; Peterborough voted 75% yes, the four towns 10–19%. Transportation costs had not been worked out when the savings figure was given. In fall 2026 the board began public input on three middle school options, one of them a single new consolidated middle school.
- **Withdrawal:** Temple dropped its study in August 2025. Francestown's withdrawal passed in March 2026 (77% in Francestown; 46% district-wide, enough under the 3/5 rule then in force) and takes effect in July 2027. ConVal's study estimated Peterborough's school tax would rise about $605,128 (3.83%) if Francestown sends grades 6–12 back to ConVal on tuition, or $1,298,232 (8.22%) if it doesn't; the tuition agreement was still being negotiated.
- **Governance:** 13 seats (Peterborough 4), one member one vote; the board will drop to 12 seats when Francestown leaves and is weighing 11, 13 or an at-large seat. Most board votes in the minutes read were unanimous. On recommending Francestown's withdrawal, Peterborough's three members present voted no, and the Francestown, Dublin and Temple members voted yes.
- **Buildings and bonds:** ConVal has no debt. Roofs and boilers are being paid from the operating budget and a $3.59 million building capital reserve; a December 1, 2026 hearing covers bid specifications for the Dublin Consolidated and ConVal High roofs. The board declined (1–10) to put an $18.7 million CTE/high school bond on the 2025 ballot. The 2023 conditions study found most heating, electrical and fire alarm systems at South Meadow and ConVal High due for replacement.
- **Turnover:** no turnover rate is published. In the federal 2023-24 data, 84.2% of ConVal's teachers had been at the same school the year before (state 83.9%, **derived**); Peterborough Elementary 26 of 27, South Meadow 31 of 33.

**Main sources:** the ConVal School Board site (Articles of Agreement, FAQs, board roster, 2026 vote results, withdrawal reports and SAU governance summary, budget documents and the Budget & Property Committee's February 2026 "Financial Articles"), the October 2026 Question 2 report and slides, board, committee, SAU board, negotiation and deliberative session minutes (December 2023 to November 2026), the November 1, 2026 enrollment report, the October 31, 2026 trust fund summary, the 2023 Existing Conditions Assessment, the 2024 CTE architects' presentation, the Francestown and Temple withdrawal study final reports (October 2025) and the Francestown Independent School District's SAU withdrawal plan (October 2026); the Town of Peterborough's DRA 2025 tax rate breakdown, assessing, property tax FAQ and exemption pages, and the Select Board's 2025 report; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24.
