# Peterborough: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Peterborough, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the ninth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Peterborough is one of nine towns in the Contoocook Valley School District (ConVal), SAU 1:
- **Peterborough Elementary (PES)** serves preschool to grade 4.
- **South Meadow School** serves grades 5–8.
- **ConVal Regional High** serves grades 9–12, with the Region 14 Applied Technology Center.

The district votes by official ballot, each town voting the same day. Region 14 was researched from Conant's side in `../Jaffrey/` and `../Rindge/`; New Ipswich's research (Mascenic), running alongside, cites the Region 14 facts here.
