# South Meadow School, Student Support and School Choices for Peterborough Families

Part of the Peterborough research (town/Peterborough). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers South Meadow School (SMS, grades 5–8, in Peterborough) and the support and choices around it for Peterborough's students in the Contoocook Valley (ConVal) School District, SAU 1: advanced math and the Expanding Horizons Program, special education at Peterborough Elementary (PES) and SMS and who to contact at the SAU office, paraprofessional staffing, the papers a guardian needs to enroll a child and see records, after-school help, the phone and bullying rules, counselors and other support staff, homeschooled and EFA students' access to ConVal classes and sports, EFA providers near Peterborough, the move from PES to SMS, why SMS buses run earlier, and how 5th graders are grouped. District-wide facts are placed with `district Contoocook Valley`, so the other ConVal towns' research can use them; the EFA provider facts are placed with `town Peterborough`.

**Counts:** 14 topics, 88 facts, 1 number table (`conval_peterborough_bus_run_gap_2026_27`). Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 14 MB, most of it board minutes PDFs).

**Main sources:** the SMS Student Handbook 2026-2027 (Google Doc, with district policies JICJ, JICK and JRA in its appendix); the SMS daily schedule (2024-25), "Our School" and attendance pages; the "Puma News" newsletters of May 30, August 3, August 19, September 7, November 1 and November 15, 2026; ConVal's special education, student services, administrators, counseling, social worker, 504, home education, enrollment and records pages; the registration checklist (scanned); ConVal policies JJJ, IHBG, IHBG-R, IMBD, IHBB, JLD, KE, KEB, EEA and EEA-R; ConVal School Board minutes (August 19 and October 7 and November 4, 2025; April 21, May 5, June 2, August 18, September 1, September 15, October 6 and October 20, 2026), the November 17, 2026 agenda, Policy Committee minutes (September 14 and November 9, 2026) and Education Committee minutes (March 16, 2026); the 2026-27 Peterborough bus route sheets (times only); the PES Handbook 2026-2027 (hours and special education only); the PAGS focus group flyer; Student Transportation of America's late bus page; and the EFA marketplace feed (November 22, 2026).

## district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners: Advanced Math and Enrichment at South Meadow School

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice

1. At South Meadow School in Peterborough, a student who wants to take 8th-grade math in 7th grade must finish required summer work and meet three of four criteria: a teacher recommendation (based on habits of work, grades and attendance), an NWEA score above the 85th percentile, a 6th-grade state math test (NHSAS) score of 540 or higher, and at least 80% proficiency in computation fluency.
   - citation: South Meadow School Student Handbook 2026-2027, "Advance Math Placement"
