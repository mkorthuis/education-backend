# ConVal Regional High School and the Region 14 Applied Technology Center

Part of the Peterborough research (town/Peterborough). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers ConVal Regional High School (CVHS, grades 9–12, in Peterborough) and the Region 14 Applied Technology Center (ATC) inside it, for the Peterborough questions on the high school: AP and Early College (dual credit) courses and what they cost, Region 14 programs and credentials, the CTE renovation plan, paid and unpaid work-based learning and apprenticeships, the late bus, sports eligibility, fees and boosters, the arts and partners such as MacDowell, portfolios, counseling for college and other paths, FAFSA help and Peterborough's scholarship trust funds, the AP workload, internships for credit, and Chromebooks and internet access.

- **12 fact topics, 65 facts and 4 number tables.** All facts are new; Region 14 facts already written from Conant's side (`town/Jaffrey/conant-and-cte.md`, cte-center-programs 1–5 and 11, district-volunteers-and-partners 2) are cited, not repeated.
- **Places:** 62 facts use `district Contoocook Valley`, 3 use `town Peterborough` (the Peterborough Players and the town's scholarship trust funds). No state rules were written; the state rules on dual enrollment, AP fees, NHIAA eligibility, youth employment, college transfer and FAFSA are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **Raw copies** are in `raw/high/` (about 18 MB): the 2026-27 Program of Studies, Student Handbook, School Profile and Blended Learning (Chromebook) Handbook; the Nashua Community College Early College flyer; eight ConVal High web pages and the bell schedule; seven Region 14 ATC pages and its work-based learning partner packet; the athletics registration page, boosters page and 2025-26 participation fee sheet; 24 school board and committee minutes (October 2024 to November 2026) and the minutes folder listing; MacDowell in the Schools pages and its site search for ConVal; two Peterborough Players pages; the five late-bus route sheets (text only; they list streets and landmarks, no house numbers); and the Town of Peterborough's June 30, 2025 trust fund statement.
- **New data concepts:** the 2026-27 Early College course list with college credits; AP exam results, SAT means, post-secondary plans and college acceptances for the Class of 2026 (from the school's own profile); the CTE renovation's history, cost, state share and status; work-based learning rules for paid and unpaid placements; late-bus routes and times; athletic participation fees and caps; what the booster club pays for; Chromebook fees and repair costs; scholarship trust funds held by the Town of Peterborough; a derived grade 8 to grade 9 enrollment comparison.
- **Main gaps:** no published measure of Wi-Fi reliability; no ConVal or Peterborough Players page describing a partnership between them; no AP homework or workload guidance from ConVal itself, and nothing that rates teachers; how the town's scholarship trusts are awarded; who pays for team uniforms; whether sports practices end before the late bus leaves; how many ConVal-area students leave for private high schools; a construction start date for the CTE renovation (none set; state funding was undecided in September 2026).
