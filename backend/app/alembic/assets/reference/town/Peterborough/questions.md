# Peterborough Residents' Questions

The questions the Peterborough research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Peterborough's students attend the Contoocook Valley (ConVal) Regional School District (SAU 1), shared with Antrim, Bennington, Dublin, Francestown, Greenfield, Hancock, Sharon and Temple: Peterborough Elementary School (preschool to grade 4), South Meadow School (grades 5–8) and ConVal Regional High School (grades 9–12), home of the Region 14 Applied Technology Center. Several questions contain premises (the SKIP program, First Friends pre-K, closing small elementary schools, towns withdrawing from ConVal, a CTE wing renovation, the ConVal school funding lawsuit) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Hannah, 33 — Nurse at Monadnock Community Hospital, mom of a 4-year-old and a 1st grader

- **P1.1** My shifts start at 7:00 AM—how early can I actually drop my kids off, and does SKIP have spots open before and after school?
- **P1.2** Is it hard to get into the First Friends pre-K program at PES if my younger one doesn't have an IEP, or should I just stick with daycare?
- **P1.3** When there's a two-hour snow delay, how late do the buses run on our side of town?
