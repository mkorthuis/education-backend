# Hudson Residents' Questions

The questions the Hudson research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. The profiles came with descriptive titles rather than names. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hudson is SAU 81, a one-district SAU. The Hudson School District runs the Early Learning Center (kindergarten at Library Street School and grade 1 at Dr. H. O. Smith Elementary, in the site's data), an integrated preschool at Nottingham West, two elementary schools for grades 2–5 (Hills Garrison and Nottingham West), Hudson Memorial School (grades 6–8) and Alvirne High School (9–12) with the Wilbur H. Palmer Career and Technical Education Center, which also takes students from nearby districts. The district votes by official ballot (SB2), with a default budget if the proposed one fails. Several questions contain premises (a 25% enrollment drop, a rejected budget "again", finished Palmer renovations, block scheduling and competency-based grading at Alvirne, "Maroon and Gold days", Alvirne's enrollment then and now, Litchfield's split, Schoology, failed teacher contracts, consolidated bus routes, lower math scores than Windham and Londonderry) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. The Empty-Nest Fiscal Conservative, 68: Retired defense contractor
Lives near Robinson Pond, on a fixed income and closely watches town deliberative sessions and property tax rates.

- **P1.1** Why does the SAU 81 school budget keep increasing every year even though overall student enrollment in Hudson has dropped by roughly 25% over the past decade?
- **P1.2** How much of our local property tax bill goes toward administrative overhead versus actual classroom instruction?
- **P1.3** What happens if voters reject the proposed operating budget again and the district has to run on the default budget?

## P2. The Special Education Advocate Mom, 41: Former paralegal
Lives off Ferry Street, mother of a 4th grader at Hills Garrison Elementary with an Individualized Education Program (IEP) for dyslexia and ADHD.

- **P2.1** Does Hudson have enough certified special education teachers and paraprofessionals to actually fulfill the minutes written into my son’s IEP without constant pull-out cancellations?
- **P2.2** How smooth is the transition process for IEP services when students move from Hills Garrison up to Hudson Memorial School?
- **P2.3** Why are out-of-district special education placement costs eating up so much of the budget, and what are we doing to build stronger in-district programs?
