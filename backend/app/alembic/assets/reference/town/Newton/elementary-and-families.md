# Bakie, Memorial and Young Families in Newton

Part of the Newton research (town/Newton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Newton's side of the Sanborn Regional School District's two elementary schools, Daniel J. Bakie Elementary School (preschool to grade 2, Kingston) and Memorial Elementary School (grades 3–5, Newton): how long Newton children ride the bus to Bakie, whether the Bakie and Memorial buses come at the same times, full-day kindergarten, why the grades are split between two towns (the 2019 change to the Articles of Agreement and the 2020 reorganization), how many Newton children attend each school, care before and after school for Memorial students, the earliest drop-off, bus changes, who may pick a child up, whom the nurse calls, English learner support, Memorial's test results beside Atkinson's and Exeter's grade 3–5 schools, summer options in Newton, and the district preschool, referrals and the move from Early Supports at age 3.

Most of Sanborn's elementary practice is already in `town/Kingston/elementary-and-families.md` (hours, care, calendar, storm delays, apps, meals, nurses, registration, preschool hours) and the library; this file cites those and adds Newton's side. Main new sources: the Memorial 2026-27 bus routes (published sheet, read as CSV), Bakie's 2026-27 routes, the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder (to tell which stops are in Newton), the district's October 1, 2026 enrollment table by town, the Memorial handbook, Bakie's registration page, policies JEB, JHC, JLCE-R, KI, IHBBA and EEA, the special education procedures plan, the 2019 reorganization FAQ (Internet Archive copy), the site's enrollment-by-grade and test data, the YMCA's and YMCA Camp Lincoln's pages, Newton's Recreation Commission, Gale Library and park ordinance pages, the Newton 2025 annual report, CAPHR's summer meals page and federal rule 34 CFR 303.209.

**Counts:** 11 topics, 27 facts, 3 number tables.

**Main gaps:** when 2027-28 kindergarten registration opens; Memorial's delayed-opening start time; the amount of preschool tuition for typically developing children and whether preschoolers get a bus; why the district moved grade 3 to Memorial and grade 6 to the middle school in 2022-23; the full Articles of Agreement; a posted preschool screening day; any free town-run summer camp in Newton (none found); and the sau17.net school pages, which showed a bot check to scripts on November 21 (Kingston's same-day copies were used).

Until 2019-20 each Sanborn Regional town had its own elementary school for preschool through grade 5 (Bakie in Kingston, Memorial in Newton); in 2020-21 and 2021-22 the district grouped its schools by grade instead, with Bakie serving preschool to grade 3, Memorial grades 4 to 6 and the middle school grades 7 and 8, and since 2022-23 Bakie has served preschool to grade 2, Memorial grades 3 to 5 and the middle school grades 6 to 8 (derived from NH DOE enrollment counts).
