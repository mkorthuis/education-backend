# Sanborn Regional Middle and High School, and SST, From Newton

Part of the Newton research (town/Newton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file adds Newton's side to the Kingston research on Sanborn Regional Middle School (grades 6–8) and Sanborn Regional High School (grades 9–12), which share one building at 17 Danville Road in Kingston, and on the Seacoast School of Technology (SST) in Exeter. Most of the school's programs are already in `town/Kingston/middle-high-and-sst.md` (credits, AP, Early College, SST, shop classes, internships, sports, homework) and `town/Kingston/support-and-choices.md` (the phone policy); this file cites them and adds what they lack: the late bus home to Newton, honors courses and how students qualify, a comparison of advanced courses with Exeter High and Timberlane, what is known about high school class sizes, early graduation and VLACS, FAFSA help and scholarships, how local businesses can work with the school, community use of the track, and the board's public comment rules.

- **9 fact topics, 25 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 3 MB): `bus/` (the 2026-27 middle and high school bus and late bus route sheets), `policy/` (board policies BEDH, IHBH-R, IJO, IKB, KF, KF-E, KF-R and KFA), `newsletters/` (eight 2026-27 issues of the middle and high school's weekly newsletter) and `web/` (the honors recommendation and course override forms). Many facts also cite the Kingston research's raw copies (`town/Kingston/raw/`), which hold the program of studies, board and Budget Committee minutes, the welcome-back letter, the live feed and the Newton town report.
- **Main sources:** the district's 2026-27 late bus and bus route sheets; the 2026-27 Program of Studies and its linked honors recommendation and override forms; board policies; the "SRMS & SRHS Campus Connection" newsletters of September 20 to November 15, 2026; Budget Committee minutes of December 1 and 4, 2025 and January 6, 2026; School Board minutes of May 20 and July 22, 2026; the FY28 budget presentation (November 4, 2026); Newton's 2025 town report.
- **The district's website blocks automated requests.** www.sau17.net and the school pages returned a "Client Challenge" page on the first request; they were not retried, and the site's content back end was not used to get around the block. The middle and high school handbook, athletics and guidance pages (with any scholarship list) are therefore unread (Not retrieved). Board documents and newsletters on Google Drive and Smore were reachable.
- **New data concepts:** the Newton late run (time and stops); honors entry criteria and the parent override; a count of honors-level courses (**derived**) and a comparison with Exeter High and Timberlane; the share of small high school classes in fall 2025 from the Budget Committee chair's handout; facility rental fees for the track and fields; the board's public comment rules; scholarship totals at Scholarship Night 2026; Newton's town trust funds.
- **Derived figures:** 20 Sanborn-taught courses with an honors level, 4 AP courses listed and 2 offered, 7 college-credit courses taught at the school (counts from the 2026-27 catalog); about 21 credits after the first half of grade 12 on a full six-credit load, 3 short of the 24-credit diploma; two hours between the 2:10 dismissal and the 4:10 late run; policies IMBA and IKFA absent from the 391-row policy listing.

1. Sanborn's homework guidelines (policy IKB-R, revised August 17, 2022) set no nightly number of minutes for grades 6–8: middle school homework is to count for about 10% of a student's grade, be coordinated by the grade-level teams, vary in type and length, run "typically of a longer duration" than in grades 4 and 5 (where the policy suggests 20 to 40 minutes a day), and include independent reading every day that students record.
