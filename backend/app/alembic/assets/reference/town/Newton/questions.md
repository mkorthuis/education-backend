# Newton Residents' Questions

The questions the Newton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Newton's students attend the Sanborn Regional School District (SAU 17), shared with Kingston. Children attend Daniel J. Bakie School in Kingston (preschool to grade 2) and Memorial School in Newton (grades 3–5), then Sanborn Regional Middle School and Sanborn Regional High School in Kingston. Students may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. Sanborn is researched in `../Kingston/`; the files here add Newton's side. Several questions contain premises (two schools in two towns, Newton's share rising while enrollment falls, empty classrooms at Memorial or Bakie, a new state tax cap, Massachusetts-style calendars) that the research should check rather than assume.
