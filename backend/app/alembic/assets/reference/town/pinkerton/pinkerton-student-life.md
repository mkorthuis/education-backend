# Pinkerton Academy: Student Life and Support

Part of the Derry research (town/Derry), written in the shared Pinkerton folder (town/pinkerton) so every town that sends students to Pinkerton can use it. Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Pinkerton Academy's student life and support for families in any sending town (Auburn, Candia, Chester, Derry, Fremont, Hampstead and Hooksett): how special education works between Pinkerton and the sending districts, transition help for students with disabilities, how freshmen are grouped and supported, counseling, mental health and prevention, the phone and vaping rules, attendance and cutting rules and how families hear about absences, the 7:15 a.m. start and the Start Time Committee's work, student parking, clubs (including the one faith-based club listed), sports and intramurals, and the alternative programs (PACE, the Academy Learning Community, the Adult Diploma Program and HiSET). Facts marked Derry-specific are placed under `district Derry Cooperative`; everything else is `district Pinkerton Academy`.

- **Counts:** 10 topics, 121 facts and 5 number tables.
- **Main sources:** Pinkerton's 2026-27 Student Handbook, its 2026-27 Course Catalog, its Special Education Parent Handbook (revised 2022, still linked), its web pages, the Start Time Committee's documents, the Head of School's monthly updates, and SAU 10's Student Services pages and 2019 special education study.
- **New data concepts:** the split of special education duties between Pinkerton and the sending district; Pinkerton's start-time survey results; student parking eligibility and cost; club dues; alternative program entry rules.
- **No new topic proposed.** Parking facts go under `district-transportation`, as Conway and Jackson did for Kennett. The research on teen sleep and start times is in Nashua's proposed `research-school-start-times` topic and is not repeated here.
- **Main gaps:** what the Derry–Pinkerton agreement says about special education (agent 7 holds the agreement); any decision by the Start Time Committee after its March 2025 survey; the number of student parking spaces; a list of intramural activities; whether Pinkerton uses vape detectors; Pinkerton-level youth risk survey results after 2019; whether Pinkerton sends an automated call for each absence.

## district-special-education-services: Special Education at Pinkerton and the Sending Districts

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice

1. Pinkerton Academy's Special Education Parent Handbook describes Pinkerton as a public academy that helps its sending districts meet their duty to give students with disabilities a free appropriate public education (FAPE); Pinkerton says it voluntarily follows federal special education law and New Hampshire's special education rules except where the contract makes something the sending district's job, and each town's district provides transportation.

Text: "Pinkerton Academy is a public academy that assists the sending districts in meeting their obligation to provide educationally disabled students ... with a free and appropriate education at public expense. Pinkerton voluntarily subscribes to and complies with IDEIA 2004 and the most current NH Rules ... unless otherwise contractually the responsibility of the LEA.
