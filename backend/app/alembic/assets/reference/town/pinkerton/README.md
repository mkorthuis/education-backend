# Pinkerton Academy: Shared Research

Facts about Pinkerton Academy, the private academy in Derry that serves as the high school for Derry and, under tuition contracts, Auburn, Candia, Chester, Hampstead, Hooksett and (from 2026-27) Fremont. Written by the Derry research on November 15, 2026 (`../Derry/`), for any sending town to reuse; each fact is placed in `district Pinkerton Academy` unless it's about a sending district's own terms.

Both files pass the backend's parser (`tools/check_town.sh pinkerton`), and every table is linked to a topic. Spot checks are in `../Derry/README.md`.

**For a sending town's research:** read these files first and add only that town's own terms (its contract, its trustees, its bus routes and its students' counts). The Derry–Pinkerton agreement isn't posted online; Candia's 2018–2038 agreement is saved, scanned and untranscribed, in `additional-data/raw/districts/batch-3/candia-pinkerton-agreement.pdf`.
