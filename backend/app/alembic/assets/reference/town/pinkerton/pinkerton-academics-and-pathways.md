# Pinkerton Academy: Academics, Pathways and Outcomes

Part of the Derry research (town/Derry), written for the shared Pinkerton folder (town/pinkerton) so every town that sends students to Pinkerton Academy can reuse it. Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Pinkerton Academy's course levels and how incoming 9th graders are placed, honors and AP, college credit at Pinkerton and at the community colleges, the graduation requirements (including the new personal finance and civics credits), the Center for Career and Technical Education (CTE) with its trades and agriscience programs, school counseling and college planning, published results (AP, SAT, graduates' plans, the colleges listed in the school profile), adult education in Derry (Pinkerton's PACE adult diploma and SAU 10's Derry Center for Adult Studies), and what trades pay in New Hampshire.

- **7 fact topics, 74 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/academics/` (about 76 MB, most of it three PDFs): Pinkerton's 2026-27 and 2025-26 course catalogs and 2026-27 school profile, the 2026 annual report pages, the CTE pages and presentations, the 2025-26 concurrent credit course list, the counseling pages and 2026-27 Senior Survival Guide, the 8th grade registration timelines and freshman course guide, the PACE pages and 2026-27 adult diploma handbook, SAU 10's adult education and grades 6–8 curriculum pages, NH Adult Education's program list and BLS wage data.
- **Places:** 61 facts use `district Pinkerton Academy`, 6 `district Derry Cooperative` (SAU 10's own curricula and adult education), 2 `town Derry` (statewide wages, for Derry's questions), and 5 have no place (statements of what no source publishes, and a comparison with state rules).
- **New data concepts:** Pinkerton's level system (AP, Honors, A, B, C, Exploration) and its 8th-grade placement guidelines; Honors Algebra 2 placement by teacher recommendation or a Pinkerton math test; the in-district, out-of-district and home-educated registration timelines; graduation requirements by class (personal financial literacy from the class of 2028; Granite State History and Law & Ethics from the class of 2030); the CTE programs that need an application and how students are chosen; area high schools' access to Pinkerton CTE; concurrent credit sections by college (2025-26); the rule that outside college courses earn only elective, pass/fail credit within a three-credit cap; the counselor count and ratio; the colleges listed in the school profile by region; AP mean scores by course; Derry's adult enrichment, HiSET and English classes; New Hampshire wage percentiles for trades.

## school-programs-after-grade-6: Pinkerton Academy's Levels, 9th-Grade Placement, Honors and AP, and College Credit

- kind: local

1. Pinkerton Academy's 2026-27 Course Catalog sorts most academic courses into levels: AP (college-level), Honors, A (preparation for colleges "especially those that are highly selective"), B (preparation for colleges and other post-secondary schools), C (basic skills and fundamentals) and Exploration (C-level expectations in a smaller setting), plus unleveled electives.
   - citation: Pinkerton Academy, 2026-2027 Course Catalog, "Levels" - Course Selection Guide, pp. 26–27
   - source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1D-qc-7DZX72a1yEMdcpyc1z44Nw2q_nl/view
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-11-15
   - place: district Pinkerton Academy
   - note: Catalog linked from https://www.pinkertonacademy.org/academics/course-catalog. Text: "A - Courses labeled 'A' are designed to offer scholastic preparation for colleges and universities, especially those that are highly selective through a competitive admissions process"; "C ... develop basic skills, to stress fundamentals ... scholastic preparation for some two-year post-secondary institutions"; "EXPLORATION ... similar expectations as C level, but in a smaller setting." Raw copy: raw/academics/pinkerton-catalog-2026-27.pdf and .txt (PDF pages 32–33).

2. Pinkerton's catalog says levels for incoming 9th graders are "tentatively assigned" from previous teachers' assessments and standardized tests, and gives guidelines: A level for students with A grades for the past two or three years and NH SAS or NWEA scores at the 80th–90th percentile and "proficient with distinction," B level for B to C grades and 40th–70th percentile, and C level for C- to D- grades and 20th–30th percentile.
   - citation: Pinkerton Academy, 2026-2027 Course Catalog, pp. 26–27; Freshman Course Selection Guide (2026-27)
   - source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1D-qc-7DZX72a1yEMdcpyc1z44Nw2q_nl/view
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-11-15
   - place: district Pinkerton Academy
   - note: Text: "after courses are chosen, scholastic levels in most courses are tentatively assigned by the Academy based on the individual student's abilities and aptitudes as assessed by previous teachers and standardized testing programs ... These levels are neither chosen at random by the student nor made compulsory by Pinkerton Academy; they are recommended." The Freshman Course Selection Guide (https://docs.google.com/document/d/19OwgHs-6gnstQlY0SxihYPzXvDP5VCy9qg7_7WcpVL4) repeats the guidelines and asks teachers to weigh "academic performance, aptitude, work ethic, and standardized testing." Raw copies: raw/academics/pinkerton-catalog-2026-27.txt; raw/academics/pinkerton-freshman-course-guide.txt.

3. At Pinkerton, a change of level needs agreement among the student, parents, school counselor, teacher and associate dean; for 2026-27 the deadlines are November 10, 2026 for first-semester half-year courses, April 8, 2027 for second-semester courses and January 19, 2027 for full-year courses, and grades from the earlier level follow the student.
   - citation: Pinkerton Academy School Counseling, Important Deadlines for 2026-2027; 2026-2027 Course Catalog, p. 26
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1R5nwZKWOOZFaNf5wWU_ngJIo-_9tv7i_jxl4g19te3Q/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-11-15
   - place: district Pinkerton Academy
   - note: Deadlines text: "Change Levels ... Nov. 10th 2026 | April 8th 2027 | Jan. 19th 2027 ... Change of levels will be done if students demonstrate clearly that the level they are seeking is consistent with their documented abilities ... Grades from the previous level will follow the student into the new level." The catalog says level changes come "only through mutual agreement among the student, the parents, the school counselor, any teacher involved, and the Associate Dean of Academics." Raw copies: raw/academics/pinkerton-deadlines-2026-27.txt; raw/academics/pinkerton-catalog-2026-27.txt.

14.
