## kindergarten-entry-cutoff: Gilmanton's Kindergarten Birthday Cutoff

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: Each district's birthday cutoff for kindergarten entry (New Hampshire law sets no statewide entry age), from its policy or registration page.

1. In the Gilmanton School District, a child may enter kindergarten if he or she will be five on or before September 30 of the year of entering school, and grade 1 at six by the same date (Policy JEB, revised April 9, 2024); a birth certificate must be shown at registration as proof of age.
   - citation: Gilmanton School District Policy JEB, Age of Entrance (revised April 9, 2024)
   - source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1P39pRDENExu30hX7vvQJ-V9IKt9aZhL6/view

   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Gilmanton
   - note: Linked from SAU 79's Policies, Section J. Text: "A student may enter kindergarten if their chronological age will be five on or before September 30 of the year of entering school. A student may enter grade one if their chronological age will be six on or before September 30 of the year of entering school. A birth certificate must be presented upon registration as proof of the date of birth." Raw: raw/elementary/policy-jeb.txt
2. Under Gilmanton's Policy JEB, a child moving in who is entering grade 1 is admitted to grade 1 only if he or she turns six before December 31 of that year or was already enrolled in grade 1 in another community and is attending only temporarily (10 months or less); transfers in grades 2–8 are first placed using the records sent by the old district, and every such placement is tentative, with the superintendent's decision final.
   - citation: Gilmanton School District Policy JEB, Age of Entrance (revised April 9, 2024)
   - source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1P39pRDENExu30hX7vvQJ-V9IKt9aZhL6/view

In-coming transfer students in grade 1 will only be initially admitted to grade 1 if their chronological age will be six before December 31 of the year of entering school or if previously enrolled in grade 1 in another community and attending local schools only on a temporary basis (10 months or less). ...
