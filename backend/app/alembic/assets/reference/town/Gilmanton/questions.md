# Gilmanton Residents' Questions

The questions the Gilmanton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Gilmanton is SAU 79. The Gilmanton School District runs Gilmanton School (kindergarten to grade 8), and its high school students attend Gilford High School under an AREA agreement amended for 2023–2035, where they may take career programs at the Huot Technical Center in Laconia. Several questions contain premises (about 350 students, half the budget going to Gilford, a National Blue Ribbon award, driver's ed at Gilford, open enrollment, the town paying for another high school) that the research should check rather than assume.
