# Gilmanton: Money, Taxes, Governance, the Building and Results

Part of the Gilmanton research (town/Gilmanton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 54 facts and 3 number tables on the Gilmanton School District (SAU 79, a one-district SAU) and its side of the Gilford–Gilmanton AREA agreement. They cover how much of the school budget goes to Gilford High tuition and the bond charge, and how that compares with the local school tax; how the tuition line is set and why it is usually over-budgeted; the 2025–26 renegotiation and how it ended; tuition paid to Prospect Mountain; the 2025 and 2026 budget votes and the 2027-28 proposal under the new tax cap; money returned to taxpayers each year; how the school tax is figured; where residents can see what each warrant article costs; the separate SAU budget; the roof, heating, parking lot, trust funds, debt and the draft capital plan; enrollment by grade from 2023-24 to November 2026; the two districts' 2026-27 calendars; and the 2015 Blue Ribbon award and the district's own performance claims.

- **The Gilford share:** regular tuition (almost all to Gilford) plus the bond charge has been 30.3% to 32.5% of Gilmanton's operating budget since 2022-23 (**derived**, table). For 2025-26, the Gilford tuition and bond lines ($4,603,554) equal 31.8% of the operating budget and 42.8% of the local school tax raised in tax year 2025 (**derived**).
- **Tuition per student:** budgeted at $25,435 for 2026-27 (about 177 students, **derived**); Gilford projects $28,190 for 2027-28. Gilmanton usually budgets more than it ends up paying; the AREA formula settles the bill to actual costs after the year.
- **Renegotiation:** after 14 months and eight meetings, no agreement; the subcommittee was dissolved, so the 2020 plan stands to 2035.
- **Votes:** voters rejected the 2025-26 budget, so the default (higher) budget took effect; in 2026 the deliberative session cut $200,000, which the board took from the tuition line. The 2026 ballot counts were not found.
- **Enrollment:** 362 (2023-24), 337 (2024-25), 339 (February 2026), 308 (November 10, 2026).
- **Building:** the gym and middle school roof sections are out to bid (deadline November 20, 2026), to be paid from a roof trust fund holding $560,733 on September 30, 2026; the board says the boilers work; no new bond is proposed.

**Main sources:** SAU 79's Budget Information and Meetings pages (open without sign-in on November 21, 2026): the 2026 warrant, MS-27, default budget form, proof ballot and deliberative session budget book; the 2025 deliberative session handout and draft capital plan; school board, work session, special meeting and AREA subcommittee minutes from February 2025 to November 2026; the November 10, 2026 agenda packet (enrollment by grade, trust funds, superintendent's and business administrator's reports); the AREA subcommittee summary; the Gilmanton School 2026-27 calendar; the Town of Gilmanton's 2025 Annual Town and School Report, 2026 Voter's Guide and tax notices; the old SAU 79 site; and the site's database.
