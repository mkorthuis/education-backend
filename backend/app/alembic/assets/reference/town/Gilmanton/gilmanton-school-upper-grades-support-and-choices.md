# Gilmanton School's Upper Grades, Support and Choices: Grades 5–8 in a K–8 School, Math Challenge, World Languages, Arts, Special Education and 504 Plans, Counselors, Bullying, Phones, Homework, Absences, Homeschool and EFA Access, the Library, Volunteers and the Move to Gilford

Part of the Gilmanton research (town/Gilmanton). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Gilmanton School (kindergarten to grade 8, the district's only school) from the upper grades' side: how grades 5–8 are organized inside a K–8 building; challenge for strong math students; world languages; music, art and clubs; who provides special education and related services, 504 plans and whom to contact in SAU 79; school counselors; the rewritten bullying policy; the phone rule; homework and grading; absences and truancy (including time off for farm work); access for home-educated and EFA students; EFA use and providers near town; the Gilmanton Year-Round Library; volunteering; and the 8th-grade year before Gilford High.

**Sources:** the SAU 79 and Gilmanton School websites (public Google Sites, reachable on November 21, 2026; the library's November 8 note said they required a sign-in), the 2026–27 Student/Family Handbook, the 2026–27 Athletic Handbook, the special education procedures plan ("Special Education Handbook 2024"), 16 Gilmanton School Board policies, school board, policy committee and budget work session minutes from August 2025 to November 10, 2026 (with the January 31, 2026 deliberative session), principal's letters to families, the counseling department, athletics, SEADS and PTA sites, federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) rows for Gilmanton School (2017-18, 2021-22, 2023-24, copied from statewide extracts saved by earlier towns), the Children's Scholarship Fund EFA feeds already in the library, the Gilmanton Year-Round Library's website, and read-only queries of the site's database.

**Counts:** 14 topics, 88 facts, 2 tables.

**Main findings:**
- **Spanish moved online in 2026-27.** The longtime Spanish teacher retired after 2025-26. After posting a world language job twice, the district hired a World Language/World Culture teacher; 7th and 8th graders now choose Spanish online through VLACS (six signed up in September 2026) or a World Cultures class.
- **Related services are contracted.** The proposed 2027-28 budget calls psychological, speech, and PT/OT services "Contracted service to meet the needs of all IEP's." The directory lists a speech pathologist, OT, COTA, psychologist, three case managers and 18 paraeducators. NH DOE found the district in compliance (May 2026 desk audit; November 2026 letter).
- **504/ADA coordinators are the two school counselors;** the Director of Student Services is the Human Rights Officer and Title IX Coordinator. Complaints go teacher → assistant principal or principal → superintendent → board (policy KE, handbook).
- **The bullying policy was rewritten on November 10, 2026** for HB 131: it lists exclusion and rumor-spreading as bullying and drops the "imbalance of power" clause, but the school's About Us page still links the 2010/2015 version.
- **No gifted program; 8th-grade Algebra I.** CRDC 2023-24: one Algebra I class, 8th graders only, 17 students (about a third of 8th graders), all passed.
- **The library has no homeschool program.** The Gilmanton Year-Round Library, next door at 1385 NH Route 140, runs weekday-morning story time and afternoon clubs; school staff walk signed-up students over after school.
