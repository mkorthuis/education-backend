Part of the Hampstead research (town/Hampstead). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hampstead Central School (HCS, preschool to grade 4) and the school day for questions P1.1–P1.3, P4.1–P4.3, P6.2, P9.3, P16.1–P16.3 and P18.1–P18.3: kindergarten in 2026-27 and the entrance screening, care before and after school, class sizes, PickUp Patrol and dismissal, help with costs (meals, care, summer programs), Title I and reading help, the T.E.E.C.H. preschool, building security and the school resource officer, phones and bullying, storm closings and emergencies, buses and records for children with two homes, and attending from a Derry address.

- **17 topics, 89 facts, no number tables.** Every topic uses a shared slug from `town/shared-topics.md` or an existing library slug (`kindergarten-day-length`, `kindergarten-registration`), with that topic's kind and question type. | P18.3 | A child can stay at HCS while living part-time in Derry | Yes, if Hampstead is the child's legal residence under RSA 193:12: for parents living apart, that is the home the child lives with, or the parents' written agreement or court order (library school-residency-rules, Hart's Location fact 2). Hampstead's policies apply that law. A non-resident child needs board approval and pays tuition. |

## kindergarten-day-length: Kindergarten Day Length by District

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: Whether each district offers full-day or half-day kindergarten, from its own pages.

1. Hampstead Central School's 2026-27 Student & Parent Handbook lists five kindergarten classrooms, all full day, and says the kindergarten day is the same as grades 1–4, starting at 9:00 a.m. and dismissing at 3:25 p.m., with a calendar that follows the elementary and middle school schedules.
   - citation: Hampstead Central School 2026-2027 Student & Parent Handbook (updated 8/11/26), "Classrooms" and "Kindergarten"
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aDeiUS2tI2oRHSa7wGQ_s9oBHHQfTItAGSxPkKghwtQ/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Hampstead
   - note: Handbook text: "5 Kindergarten classrooms * 5 full day Kindergarten classrooms"; "The full day session is the same as grades 1-4, beginning 9:00 and dismissing at 3:25." "Hampstead Central School offers Kindergarten for all children who are five years of age on or before September 30 of the current school year." This is the pilot year the library describes (district-practices.md kindergarten-day-length fact 16). Handbook linked from the HCS menu (library raw/districts/batch-8/hampstead-hcs-menu.txt). Raw: raw/elementary/docs/hcs-student-handbook.txt.

2. In Hampstead's 2026-27 kindergarten, children may ride the same buses and routes as grades 1–4, but a parent must be waiting at the bus stop for every kindergarten and first-grade child; if no parent is there, the child (and any sibling on the bus) is taken back to school, and a child may not be released to an older sibling instead.
   - citation: Hampstead Central School 2026-2027 Student & Parent Handbook, "Kindergarten Transportation" and "Kindergarten and First Grade Student Bus Drop Off"
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aDeiUS2tI2oRHSa7wGQ_s9oBHHQfTItAGSxPkKghwtQ/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Hampstead
   - note: Text: "All Kindergarten and First grade students MUST have a parent waiting at the bus stop when the bus arrives. This is a requirement of the bus company First Student and there is NO exception." "Kindergarten and 1st grade students may not be released to a 2nd, 3rd or 4th grade sibling in place of a parent." Policy EEA-R (procedure, revised 04-09-13) says the same: "All kindergarten and first graders must be met by a parent/guardian/designee at the bus stop." Raw: raw/elementary/docs/hcs-student-handbook.txt; raw/elementary/policies/EEA-R.txt.

## kindergarten-registration: Kindergarten Registration by District

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: When and how each district takes kindergarten registration, for the most recent year posted.

1. Hampstead Central School "highly" recommends that every child new to its kindergarten take part in a screening in late spring or summer before school starts; the screening is developmental and includes speech and language, and its results are used to "formulate a recommendation for school placement."
   - citation: Hampstead Central School 2026-2027 Student & Parent Handbook, "School Entrance Screening"
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aDeiUS2tI2oRHSa7wGQ_s9oBHHQfTItAGSxPkKghwtQ/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Hampstead
   - note: Text: "It is highly recommended that all children new to Hampstead Central School Kindergarten participate in the screening process in late spring or summer prior to the start of the school year. Children entering HCS in Kindergarten are given a developmental screening which includes speech and language screening prior to school entrance." "The results of the screening will give an indication of the child's present social, emotional, and academic levels. The information will be used to formulate a recommendation for school placement that will be most suitable for the child." The handbook gives no criteria for holding a child back a year; kindergarten is optional before age 6 (library kindergarten-rules fact 1; district-practices.md kindergarten-day-length fact 16). Raw: raw/elementary/docs/hcs-student-handbook.txt.

2. Hampstead's 2026-27 HCS handbook gives the kindergarten entry age as five "on or before September 30th" of the entering year (six for grade 1), while the board's policy JEB says "before September 30"; the handbook also requires a birth certificate, immunization records (or a notarized exemption) and proof of residency under policy JEB.
   - citation: Hampstead Central School 2026-2027 Student & Parent Handbook, "School Entrance Requirements"; Hampstead School District Policy JEB (in the library)
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aDeiUS2tI2oRHSa7wGQ_s9oBHHQfTItAGSxPkKghwtQ/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Hampstead
   - note: Handbook text: "A child considered eligible to enter school must be five years old for public Kindergarten and six years old for Grade 1 on or before September 30th of the entering year." Policy JEB text (library raw/districts/batch-8/hampstead-jeb.txt, district-practices.md kindergarten-entry-cutoff fact 32): "will be five before September 30." The two differ only for a child whose fifth birthday is September 30; the sources don't say which governs. Raw: raw/elementary/docs/hcs-student-handbook.txt.

3. Hampstead's online registration (PowerSchool Enrollment Express) sends parents a confirmation email and, within about 24 hours, an access ID and password for the parent portal; the HCS handbook asks families not to start an online registration until they are sure the child will enroll, and says a child's start can be delayed if the PowerSchool information is incomplete.
   - citation: Hampstead School District, Enrollment School Guide (linked as "New Student Registration Guide"); Hampstead Central School 2026-2027 Student & Parent Handbook, "PowerSchool"
   - source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1z_2pX_mWbJTfnzM1xmg9-nRUfnIhX_xxpNKyelC5ttQ/edit
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - place: district Hampstead
   - note: Guide text: "After you submit you will receive a confirmation email. It can take up to 24 hours for the next email to arrive. This email will contain your access ID and access password." Handbook: "This should not start until there is certainty that the child will be enrolling at Hampstead Central School." "A students' start of school can be delayed if information in PowerSchool is incomplete." 2027-28 kindergarten registration dates were not found: the district's news pages were blocked (see Not retrieved). Raw: raw/elementary/docs/registration-guide.txt; raw/elementary/docs/hcs-student-handbook.txt.
