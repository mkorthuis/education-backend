# Hampstead Middle School and the Move to Pinkerton Academy

Part of the Hampstead research (town/Hampstead). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hampstead Middle School (HMS, grades 5–8) and Hampstead's side of the move to Pinkerton Academy in Derry: HMS's size, core classes and staff; its math program and the 2024-25 math review; the Ventures program for advanced learners, 8th-grade Algebra I, Spanish, music, art, industrial arts and family and consumer science; high school credit for 7th- and 8th-grade work; how many Hampstead students attend Pinkerton; the Pinkerton bus from Hampstead and getting home late; Hampstead graduates' plans and honors at Pinkerton; and local scholarships. Pinkerton's own programs (counselors, the Freshman Academy, CTE, AP, dual enrollment, parking) are in the shared Pinkerton files (`town/pinkerton/`), which this file cites rather than repeats.

- **8 fact topics, 44 facts and 3 number tables.** 38 facts use `district Hampstead`, 4 use `district Pinkerton Academy` (Pinkerton rules the Pinkerton files lack: middle-school world language placement, credit for middle-school courses, the own-town bus rule and the seniors' scholarship checklist) and 2 use `town Hampstead` (CART rides, the Hampstead Educational Foundation). Tables: `hampstead_students_at_pinkerton`, `hampstead_pinkerton_graduates_plans`, `hms_enrollment_by_grade`.
- **Main sources:** the Hampstead School District's 2023, 2024 and 2025 annual reports (superintendent's, curriculum and HMS principal's reports, and the Pinkerton page with Hampstead's enrollment and graduates' plans); the FY2027-28 budget presentation of November 10, 2026 and the October 23, 2026 HMS, curriculum and technology budget detail; district policies IHBB (Ventures), IMBD (high school credit for grades 7–8), IHCD (advanced course work) and IHAH (world languages); First Student's route lookup for Hampstead's Pinkerton buses; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC); Pinkerton's 2026-27 catalog and handbook; the district's grants site and the Hampstead Educational Foundation's site.
- **New data concepts:** Hampstead students at Pinkerton by grade and over time, with Hampstead's share of Pinkerton; Hampstead graduates' plans by class (four-year college, trade programs, workforce) and NH Scholars counts; Pinkerton bus pickup and drop-off times at central Hampstead stops; HMS core class counts and average sizes against the board's cap; HMS unified arts staffing; the Ventures program's nomination process and services; HMS's 8th-grade Algebra I count; Pinkerton's placement rules for middle-school language study.
