# Hampstead Residents' Questions

The questions the Hampstead research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hampstead is SAU 55. The Hampstead School District runs Hampstead Central School (preschool to grade 4) and Hampstead Middle School (grades 5–8); its high school students attend Pinkerton Academy in Derry under a tuition contract. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (350 students at Hampstead Middle and over 3,200 at Pinkerton, a separate SAU office for one K–8 district, middle school math proficiency under 50%, new tax cap rules, Pinkerton tuition per student, a yearly homeschool notification or evaluation) that the research should check rather than assume.
