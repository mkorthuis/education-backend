# Hampstead: Money, Staff and Governance

Part of the Hampstead research (town/Hampstead). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 68 facts and 7 number tables on how the Hampstead School District (SAU 55) builds, votes on and pays for its budget, what drives it, its staff and pay, its Pinkerton Academy tuition, its one-district central office, its buildings and reserves, the new tax cap, the tax bill and the 2027 vote.
- **The budget and enrollment.** The voted budget rose from $24.1 million (2014-15) to $38.2 million (2025-26), 58.5% (**derived**), while resident K–12 enrollment fell 11.5% (2014-15 to 2024-25); enrollment has since been flat and NESDEC projects growth. The district's drivers: special education, transportation, health insurance and contracts. Its own inflation-adjusted Report to Voters: cost per pupil up 24.8% after inflation over ten years, average teacher pay down 8.9%, administrator pay down 21.2% (all **derived** from its figures).
- **Default budgets.** Voters rejected the operating budget in March 2025 (801–893) and March 2026 (673–729), so 2025-26 and 2026-27 ran on default budgets, which were 1.46% and 0.28% below the proposals and still grew with contracts, Pinkerton tuition, special education transportation and health insurance.
- **The tax cap and the "funding gap".** The October 20, 2026 HB 1300 hearing's $562,804 "gap in funding," the November 10 draft 2027-28 budget ($40,727,385, up 1.01%, no staffing change) and three revenue scenarios running from $933,644 over the cap to $134,473 under it. No cuts had been named as of November 21, 2026.
- **Pinkerton.** A 20-year rolling contract; $17,910 per regular student in 2026-27 (371 students); tuition to public academies at 19.7% of the 2026-27 budget, about $2.80 of the $14.20 local school rate if spread evenly (**derived**).
- **SAU 55.** One district since Timberlane left (state aid records show SAU 106 from FY2022); a separate SAU budget for the first time in 2027-28 ($1,358,366, six staff, 3.34% of the total, below the 6% SAU cap).
- **Staff.** Paraprofessionals (42 special education aides plus kindergarten and intervention aides; the head count of assistants and aides rose from 53 to 62 between October 2023 and October 2024), aide pay up from $1.50 million (2024-25 actual) to $2.17 million requested for 2027-28, two unfilled support positions cut, contracted aides, a 2024-25 mentoring program for alternative-pathway teachers, and substitute lines spent well over budget.
- **Buildings, taxes and voting.** A five-year maintenance plan of $328,000–$414,000 a year, no bonds, a capital reserve fed from surpluses ($1.81 million in expendable trusts, June 2025); the 2026 tax rate ($19.49, 78.6% school, **derived**); the March 9, 2027 vote and absentee ballots.

**Main sources:** the district's 2026-27 and 2027-28 budget sites (Google Sites) and the Drive files they link: the November 10, 2026 budget presentation and object-code budgets, the October 20, 2026 HB 1300 hearing slides, the January 13, 2026 hearing and February 3, 2026 deliberative session slides and supplements, the Mandatory Report to Voters, the 2024 and 2025 Annual Reports, the FY2025 audit, the FY2020–2026 budget recap, the NESDEC projection, the five-year maintenance plan, the October 2026 student services workshop budget and the 2026-27 budget Q&A; the Town of Hampstead's tax rate summary, voting page, election results and absentee form; the LBA's FY2025 state aid report; and the site's database.
