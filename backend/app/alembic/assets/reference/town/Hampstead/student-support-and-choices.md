# Student Support and Choices in Hampstead

Part of the Hampstead research (town/Hampstead). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

This file covers support for Hampstead students and the choices families weigh: special education across the move from Hampstead Central School (HCS) to Hampstead Middle School (HMS), out-of-district placements and who pays for the ride, counseling and mental health at HMS, homework help and free tutoring, help for a grandparent with the parent portal, home-educated and EFA students' access to HMS programs and the home education notice, Hampstead Academy (a private PreK–8 school in town) and moving from it to HMS or Pinkerton, outside school ratings set beside the site's test results, and home sale prices against Windham and Bedford. Pinkerton's own 504, freshman academy and counseling setup is in the shared `town/pinkerton/` files, which answer P20.1–P20.3 for the most part; this file adds only Hampstead's side.

**Counts:** 13 topics, 63 facts, 3 number tables (`hampstead_area_school_ratings_2026`, `hampstead_out_of_district_tuition_2027_28`, `hampstead_area_home_sales_2026`). Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (16 MB).

**Main sources:** the district's policies (IHBA, IHBG, IKB, JFAA, JG, JJIB, JJJ and the November 24, 2026 policy packet) and its 2024 and 2025 annual reports, its draft 2027-28 Student Services and HMS budgets (October 23, 2026 workshop), the District Suicide Prevention Plan (September 24, 2024), the HCS handbook, Hampstead Academy's own pages and handbook, NCES's private school survey, GreatSchools, the NH REALTORS market updates, Tutor.com's New Hampshire page, Lexington Academy's and Seacoast Youth Services' sites, the Hampstead Public Library, eCFR and the RSAs, and read-only queries of the site's database.

## special-education-placements-across-states: Special Education Placements in Other States and Private Schools

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Who is responsible for and pays for special education when a New Hampshire district educates a student in another state or a private school, including approval of out-of-state programs, rates and catastrophic aid

1. Under federal special education rules, a child's placement is decided by a group that includes the parents and people who know the child, the evaluation data and the placement options; it must be set at least once a year, be based on the IEP and be as close as possible to the child's home, and unless the IEP requires another arrangement the child attends the school he or she would attend without a disability.
   - citation: 34 CFR 300.116
   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.116
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15

(b) The child's placement—(1) Is determined at least annually; (2) Is based on the child's IEP; and (3) Is as close as possible to the child's home; (c) Unless the IEP of a child with a disability requires some other arrangement, the child is educated in the school that he or she would attend if nondisabled". Raw: raw/support/ecfr-300-116.txt.
