# Charlestown Residents' Questions

The questions the Charlestown research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Charlestown's students attend the Fall Mountain Regional School District (SAU 60), shared with Acworth, Alstead, Langdon and Walpole: Charlestown Primary School (preschool to grade 4), Charlestown Middle School (grades 5–8) and Fall Mountain Regional High School (grades 9–12) in Langdon. Several questions contain premises (Charlestown paying more than Walpole or Acworth, a Cooperative Review Committee, the closed North Charlestown Community School, free meals for all, a Niche C+ rating, new SAU leadership) that the research should check rather than assume.
