# Fall Mountain Regional High School and Career Programs

Part of the Charlestown research (town/Charlestown). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Fall Mountain Regional High School (FMRHS, in Langdon) for Charlestown families: its shop, agriculture and other career courses, its own career and technical education (CTE) center, the Cheshire Career Center in Keene, the change at the River Valley Technical Center in Springfield, Vermont, apprenticeships, internships and work-based learning, AP, honors and college courses (River Valley Community College's Early College), VLACS, the FFA chapter, the late bus, open enrollment limits and graduates' outcomes. The main sources are the 2026-27 Program of Studies (a Google Doc linked from the SAU 60 site), the 2023-24 Program of Studies, the CTE center's Google Site, the district's budget presentations and budget detail on its Google Drive (FY27 and FY28), the district's 2025 and 2026 annual reports, River Valley Technical Center's annual report and board minutes, Keene Board of Education minutes, NH FFA's chapter list and directory, ApprenticeshipNH, federal civil rights data, and two MyKeeneNow news reports. The SAU 60 and FMRHS websites returned a bot check to automated requests; the main session's browser pass found the high school's late run bus schedule there (a Google Doc), which is used for the late bus.

**Counts:** 8 topics, 44 facts, 2 number tables.
