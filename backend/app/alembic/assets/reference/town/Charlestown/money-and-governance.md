# Charlestown: Money, Taxes and Governance of the Fall Mountain Regional School District

Part of the Charlestown research (town/Charlestown). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 7 topics, 92 facts and 7 number tables on how the five-town Fall Mountain Regional School District (SAU 60) is governed and paid for, and what that means for Charlestown taxpayers: the 1964 Articles of Agreement and the 2002 cost-sharing "methods"; what each town paid for 2025-26 and 2026-27 and why Charlestown's rate is the highest; the failed 2026 budget and the default budget; the new Cooperative Review Committee; the closed North Charlestown Community School; the leadership change from a superintendent to a "Senior Educational Official"; staffing of aides and bus drivers; capital repairs in Charlestown and at the high school; the 2019–2020 Charlestown withdrawal attempt and the 2026 Walpole and Langdon votes; how and when to speak at meetings; and where the budget documents are.

- **The cost split:** under the Articles (amended 2002), each town pays its own elementary schools' direct costs, a set share of district-wide costs by six "methods", and all high school costs by its share of resident pupils. For 2026-27 Charlestown has 46.30% of the pupils, so it pays 46.30% of high school costs; each town's own adequacy aid is credited to it alone.
- **Why Charlestown's rate is highest (derived):** for 2025-26 Charlestown had 45.7% of the five towns' pupils but 29.1% of their equalized property value, and paid 36.9% of their local school taxes. Its local school tax per pupil ($12,824) was the lowest of the five (Walpole $19,202), but its property value per pupil ($995,547) was less than half Walpole's ($2,165,638).
- **2026-27 budget:** voters rejected the operating budget on March 10, 2026 (1,083–1,377; Charlestown 241–631), so the default of $37,218,637 took effect, with $290,920 of capital articles. The board then closed a $550,000 gap with high school position cuts and moves in Walpole; no art or sports cuts were listed. The district's figure for Charlestown's 2026-27 school levy is $8,922,517, up $828,417 (10.2%, derived) from 2025-26.
- **Governance:** the board has 7 members (one from each town, two from any town), all elected by voters of all five towns. The superintendent post was removed from the budget in December 2025; Christopher Young, Charlestown Primary's principal from 2013, was named Senior Educational Official in September 2025 and given the superintendent's authority on August 10, 2026.
- **Change under way:** the board's Cooperative Review Committee (announced July 14, 2026; 12 community members and 3 board members) is reviewing the Articles, the cost formula and the cooperative's structure, with a recommendation due by November 2027. Amending the Articles takes a two-thirds vote.

**Main sources:** the Articles of Agreement; the district's FY2027 and FY2028 budget documents (apportionment, estimated tax rates, default calculation, hearing, deliberative and October 26, 2026 presentations, HB 1300 summary); the 2019–2026 annual reports; 22 sets of 2025–2026 board minutes; board policies BEA, BEDH, DIA, FA and BDF; district news articles; Charlestown's 2019 and 2025 town reports, 2024 Master Plan, 2026 ballot and revaluation notices; RSA 195:16-a.

3. Since fiscal year 2003-04, Fall Mountain's Articles split costs this way: each town pays the direct costs of its own preschool-to-grade-8 schools; district-wide costs are shared by six "methods" (Method 1 fixed fractions for central office and similar costs, Method 2 for middle school sports and coordinators, Method 3 building replacement cost for maintenance, Method 4 bus fleet for transportation, Method 5 computers for technology, and Method 6 pupil counts for special education and similar costs), each with a high school share; and all high school costs are split by each town's average daily membership (ADM) of resident pupils as figured by NH DOE.
   - citation: Fall Mountain Articles of Agreement, Articles 5 and 6 (as amended March 12, 2002), sections I–II
   - source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZRB8vyowR5tWjmFgfasuT4IxvB51SfNV/view

   - note: Text: "All expenses directly attributable to the towns ... shall be budgeted accordingly"; Method 1 "3/9 High School, 4/81 Acworth, 10/81 Alstead, 2/9 Charlestown, 4/81 Langdon and 2/9 Walpole"; Method 6 "1/3 charged to High School, balance by ADM"; "All direct and shared expenses of the Fall Mountain Regional High School ... shall be apportioned annually on the basis of the most currently available Average Daily Membership of pupils residing in each town."
