# Charlestown Middle School: Support and Choices

Part of the Charlestown research (town/Charlestown). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Charlestown Middle School (CMS, grades 5–8, 307 Main Street) in the Fall Mountain Regional School District (SAU 60) and the supports and choices around it: following grades in PowerSchool, special education staffing and who to contact, the move up from Charlestown Primary, homeschooled and EFA students in district classes and sports, help with math, counseling, absences for fairs and farm work, Algebra I in grade 8, enrichment, bullying and phones, building security, and middle school sports.

- **12 topics, 76 facts, 3 tables.** All topics are shared slugs placed at `district Fall Mountain Regional`. No new state-rule facts: the state and federal rules are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **Main sources:** the CMS 2026-27 Student Handbook (a Google Doc, with OCR of its image pages), Fall Mountain policies JH, JICK, IKE, EEAA and JLDBA (linked from the handbook), the district's 2024, 2025 and 2026 annual reports, the November 9, 2026 version of the FY28 Charlestown budget, the October 26, 2026 FY28 budget presentation, the SAU's FY28 budget, the February 2026 deliberative session slides, the FMRHS 2026-27 program of studies and 2025-26 athlete handbook, the Fall Mountain Athletics site, the district's 2022-23 copy of policy IHBG, and a 2022 Keene Sentinel report on school security grants.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Special education staffing at CMS:** the November 9, 2026 version of the FY28 budget lists 3 special education teachers and 6 special education paraprofessional positions (0.8125 FTE each), one marked vacant. The October 26, 2026 presentation proposes cutting 2.0 FTE paraprofessional positions at CMS that are "currently vacant," and the CMS special education aide wage line falls 30.94% from FY27. The district adds a speech-language pathologist in FY28 because of a "significant increase" in students' communication needs. Its own reports describe trouble recruiting related-service staff and the use of remote speech therapists and psychologists.
  - **Grades:** PowerSchool (grades 5–12 only; app code DTPG). Teachers are to update grades at least weekly. Progress reports and report cards come by email each trimester.
  - **Algebra I:** the program of studies says Algebra I Honors "may be offered to eighth grade students who meet the prerequisites," on a middle school teacher's recommendation. If it isn't offered, students can double up later to reach calculus.
  - **Phones and bullying:** phones, earbuds and smartwatches are stored away for the whole school day, with escalating turn-in periods. The bullying policy JICK (2015) still includes a definition clause the 2026 state law repealed.
  - **Security:** CMS is "under video surveillance at all times," visitors report to the office, and late arrivals sign in at the front door. The district got $227,300 in state security grants across 10 schools in 2022 and has applied again for surveillance and access control upgrades.
  - **Sports:** no participation fee appears in the CMS handbook or the athlete handbook. The district's own report names "transportation logistics and low participation rates in certain sports" as challenges; CMS teams are for grades 7–8, with 6th graders only by exception.
- **Main gaps:** whether any CMS student has missed IEP services; a written process for IEPs moving from CPS to CMS; the district's current home education page and administrative procedure IHBG-R; which sports CMS fields this year (soccer); whether Algebra I runs at CMS in 2026-27; any gifted program; after-school tutoring at CMS (none found); FMRHS's current phone rule; the per-school list of 2022 security grants.

| P17.2 | CPS and CMS doors, cameras and check-ins may be outdated | **Can't be judged from documents.** CMS has cameras throughout, a front door installed in 2023, office check-in for visitors and late arrivals, and supervised entry. The FY28 proposal includes a "door monitoring system update" at CPS. The district won security grants in 2022 and applied again for surveillance and access control upgrades. |
| P17.3 | A participation fee may hold families back | **No fee found.** Neither the CMS 2026-27 handbook nor the 2025-26 athlete handbook lists one, and no fee revenue appears in the budgets checked.
