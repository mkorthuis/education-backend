# Charlestown Primary and Young Families

Part of the Charlestown research (town/Charlestown). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Charlestown Primary School (CPS, pre-K to grade 4) and young families' daily life: pre-K and kindergarten, the school day and drop-off, buses (who runs them, changes, ride lengths, the bus app, storm and bad-road procedures), meals and summer meals, how the school reaches families, reading help and outside ratings, mid-year enrollment, child care and private school options near Charlestown. Sources are the Fall Mountain Regional School District (SAU 60) site, the CPS 2026-27 handbook, the district's transportation policy, handbook and 2026-27 route sheets, board minutes (July 2024 to October 2026), the district's 2024 and 2025 annual reports and FY2028 budget presentation, the town's 2025 annual report, Green Mountain Children's Center's and Meeting Waters YMCA's own pages, and GreatSchools.

**Counts:** 18 topics, 65 facts, 1 number table (`charlestown_bus_ride_minutes_2026_27`). Four topics fill gaps listed in the library's district-practices coverage table (`district-preschool`, `kindergarten-day-length`, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff`, `kindergarten-registration` for Fall Mountain).

**Main findings:**
- CPS pre-K is half-day (8:30–11:00 or 12:30–3:00), for Charlestown children who are 4 by September 30; pre-registration opened January 5, 2026, and the school says it fills fast with a waiting list. The district closed its Langdon Early Learning Center on June 30, 2026.
- Fall Mountain runs its own buses. Each of Charlestown's four buses runs a high school trip to Langdon first, then a trip to CMS and CPS; scheduled morning K–8 runs last 46 to 74 minutes from the first stop (derived). The transportation director plans to cut Charlestown from 4 routes to 3 for 2027-28 and reports a severe driver shortage.
- Meals are not free for everyone: families must apply every year. Summer 2026 meals were free to anyone 18 and under, with pickup at the Silsby Public Library in Charlestown.
- Green Mountain Children's Center moved from Claremont to 509 River Road, Charlestown in fall 2026 (ages 6 weeks to 5); the board chair said in May 2026 that the closed North Charlestown Community School was becoming a child care center.
- No after-school program at CPS was found.
