# After Jackson Grammar: JBES, Kennett, the Career Center, Fryeburg Academy and Comparing Small Schools

Part of the Jackson research (town/Jackson). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the schools Jackson students attend after Jackson Grammar School (JGS): Josiah Bartlett Elementary School (JBES) for grades 7–8, Kennett High School and the Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center (MWVCTC) for grades 9–12, and Fryeburg Academy, which Jackson high schoolers may attend from 2027-28. It also covers how to compare small schools: state test standards, staffing ratios and online school-rating sites.

- **14 shared-topic sections (16 topics before the November 13, 2026 fold), 113 facts and 10 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/next-schools/` (about 300 files, including CSV sheets extracted from Maine's spreadsheets).
- **New data concepts:**
  - NCES's preliminary 2023-24 mapping of state proficiency standards (New Hampshire at NAEP Basic, Massachusetts at NAEP Proficient).
  - Kennett's 2026-27 course levels, AP and dual-enrollment lists.
  - Kennett's school-profile SAT averages, AP score spreads and post-secondary plans.
  - NHIAA divisions for every Kennett sport, and Kennett's ski titles from 2003 to 2025.
  - The MWVCTC program and credential list.
  - Fryeburg Academy's Maine DOE graduation and chronic-absenteeism rates, and its 2026-27 profile.
  - Teacher counts from NCES's Common Core of Data.
  - How GreatSchools and Niche treat a 37-student school.
  - The clauses in Jackson's tuition contracts on orientation and on reports about Jackson students.
- **Derived tables** built from the site's database: cohort sizes by grade (JGS, JBES, Kennett, and Jackson residents), and teachers and class sizes across JGS, JBES and the Conway schools.
- **Main gaps:**
  - Kennett's chronic absenteeism rate. NH DOE's iPlatform blocks automated access, and no other public source was found.
  - Any public data on how Jackson students do at JBES or Kennett. The contracts require reports to the Jackson School Board, but none were found published.
  - JBES's middle-school structure (teams, advisory), which isn't described on its site or in its handbook. Kennett's transition night and freshman transition day were later found by the SAU 9 research (see school-programs-after-grade-6 25).
  - U.S. News pages, which block automated access.
  - MWVCTC enrollment and the share of Kennett students in CTE.
  - School-based clinicians from Northern Human Services.
