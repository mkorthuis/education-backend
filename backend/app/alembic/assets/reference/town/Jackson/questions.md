# Jackson Residents' Questions

The questions the Jackson research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026.

## P1. The Remote Tech Executive Transplant
42, moved from Boston during the remote-work boom, lives near Black Mountain, parent of a 2nd grader and a 5th grader.

- **P1.1** How do Jackson Grammar School's test scores and curriculum rigor compare to top Massachusetts suburban elementary schools?
- **P1.2** What advanced math, STEM, and enrichment options exist in a tiny K–6 school with multi-grade classrooms?
- **P1.3** Are there private day school alternatives within a reasonable winter driving radius if we want a more competitive college-prep track?
