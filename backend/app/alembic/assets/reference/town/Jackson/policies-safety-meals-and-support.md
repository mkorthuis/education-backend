# Policies, Safety, Meals, Fees and Family Support for Jackson Students

Part of the Jackson research (town/Jackson). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the written rules and services that apply to Jackson students at Jackson Grammar School (JGS, Jackson School District), Josiah Bartlett Elementary School (JBES, Bartlett School District, grades 7–8) and Kennett High School (KHS, Conway School District, grades 9–12). All three districts are in SAU 9, but each district has its own school board and its own policy manual. Under the Bartlett–Jackson tuition contract, JBES rules apply to Jackson students who attend JBES.

**Counts:** 9 shared-topic sections (22 topics before the November 13, 2026 fold), 125 facts and 3 number tables (JGS lunch program money for FY2022–FY2025, SAU 9 meal prices for 2026-27, and state SAFE school security grants for the three districts).

**New data concepts:**
- School security grants by district (NH DOE SAFE grants).
- School lunch program money.
- Meal prices.
- The Farm to School Census. The 2023 Census public file has no row for any SAU 9 district.
- Written policy texts with their adoption dates: phones, bullying, buses, meals, fees, drills and building security.

**Main findings:**
- All three districts adopted a bell-to-bell personal device ban between September 2025 and February 2026.
- All three revised their bullying policy (JICK) between August and October 2026.
- JGS serves lunch only, with no breakfast price listed, under a food service agreement with the Bartlett School District.
- Jackson's lunch program has run a growing deficit, from $8,964.93 in FY2022 to $28,289.67 in FY2025.
- Jackson received state SAFE security grants: 3 projects ($83,000) in the 2022 rounds, plus a 2026 grant for extra cameras.
- No pay-to-play fee for Kennett sports appears in any Kennett or Conway document found.

**Main gaps:**
- The name of SAU 9's McKinney-Vento homeless liaison.
- Whether JGS lunches are cooked at JGS or delivered from JBES.
- Ski team equipment costs.
- The dollar amount of Jackson's 2026 SAFE grant.
- NH DOE's own CEP and liaison lists, which block automated access.
- Any written JGS-to-JBES IEP transition procedure.
