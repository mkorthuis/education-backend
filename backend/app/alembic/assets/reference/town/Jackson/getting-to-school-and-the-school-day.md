# Getting to School and the School Day

Part of the Jackson research (town/Jackson). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Jackson students get to and from Jackson Grammar School (JGS, K–6), Josiah Bartlett Elementary School (JBES, grades 7–8, in Bartlett) and Kennett High School (KHS, grades 9–12, in North Conway). It covers bus routes and ride times, who runs the buses, late buses, storm closings and winter roads, school hours, before- and after-school care, the school calendar against the valley's busy tourist months, and attendance rules for family travel and ski racing.

16. New Hampshire law lets a school board require students who are entitled to busing to walk up to one mile to a school bus stop. In areas the district's buses cannot reach, it can be up to 1½ miles if the commissioner of education approves. Districts must make sure the walk is safe and the stop is in a safe place.
   - citation: RSA 189:8
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/189/189-8.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-13
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Statewide rule. Statute text: "Pupils entitled to transportation in accordance with RSA 189:6 may be required to walk a distance not to exceed one mile to a school bus stop established by the local school board." At the January 24, 2025 Bartlett/Jackson transportation meeting, SAU 9's coordinator described "the distance between bus stops (1 mile)" as a state standard. Raw copy: raw/school-day/rsa-189-8.txt
