# Jackson Grammar School: Learning and Programs

Part of the Jackson research (town/Jackson). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Jackson Grammar School (JGS) itself: how its multi-grade classrooms and staff are set up, the school's own description of its culture, its curriculum and local tests, enrichment and STEM, how it serves advanced learners, special education and related services, reading instruction, skiing, PE and outdoor learning, science outdoors, devices and technology rules, access for homeschooled children, and public preschool.

- **8 shared-topic sections (12 topics before the November 13, 2026 fold), 96 facts and 3 number tables.**
- **Main sources:** the school's own web pages (jgs.sau9.org and its retired Google Site), the 2026-27 Student Handbook, the principal's newsletters (the Jaguar Journal), the Jackson School District annual reports for 2019 to 2026 (scanned, so pages were rendered and read as images), Jackson School Board minutes and agenda packets from 2023 to November 2026, and Jackson's policy manual on SAU 9's Google Drive.
- **Main gaps:**
  - No written gifted or talented policy appears in Jackson's policy list.
  - The school does not publish weekly PE minutes or screen-time amounts.
  - No source links JGS lessons to the White Mountain National Forest by name.
  - The speech and occupational therapy providers are not named. They are paid as contracted services.
  - No public source names Orton-Gillingham or Wilson. The programs named are Fundations, Fountas & Pinnell Leveled Literacy Intervention, Arts & Letters and Geodes.
