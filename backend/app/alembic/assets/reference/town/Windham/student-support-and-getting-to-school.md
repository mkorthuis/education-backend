# Student Support, Choices and Getting to School in Windham

Part of the Windham research (town/Windham). Gathered November 20, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Windham's side of career and technical education (CTE) at Salem High and Pinkerton Academy (the application, the 6:45 a.m. bus, programs open to Windham students, what the district budgets, and the schedule arithmetic); Windham Woods School, the private day school in Windham, and how its tuition works; finishing the school year after moving out of town, tuition students and the 2026 open enrollment vote; how families get notices and what translation exists; home-educated students' access to classes, sports and band, the district's home education policy and coming back for 9th grade; bus eligibility, routes, school hours, snow delays and closings, and the lack of a late bus; the 2026-27 calendar's early releases and workshop days; counseling and mental health staff; and how 504 plans are run.

- **10 topics, 62 facts, 3 number tables.** Places: 56 facts use `district Windham` (SAU 95, its schools, policies, buses, budget and board), 5 use `district Windham Woods School` (the site has a district record for it, id 1138) and 1 uses `town Windham` (the community mental health center). Tables: `windham_cte_tuition_and_transport_budget`, `windham_school_hours_2026_27`, `windham_counseling_staff_2026_27`.
- **New data concepts:** the CTE bus's 6:45 a.m. pickup and the block-schedule arithmetic against a driving route (OSRM, derived); the district's CTE tuition and transportation budget lines; the "last 60 / last 90 school days" rule for finishing a year after a move, with 2026-27 dates (derived); Windham's 2026 open enrollment terms (no Windham students out, up to 4 non-residents in, no CTE seats); machine translation on the district website and school newsletters; the 2025 (December 2025 edition) 504 coordinators by school; Windham Woods School's tuition, funding routes, transportation rates and IRS 990 figures; counselor and mental health counselor counts by school; two-hour-delay and early-release times for every school.
- **Main sources:** windhamsd.org pages (transportation, school closures, calendars, student support, school counseling, student services, Section 504, ESOL, open enrollment FAQ and WHS open enrollment process, withdrawal form); school board policies EEA, EEA-R, EEAB, EEAB-R, EBCD, IC, IMBA, IHBA (with the 2025 Section 504 Handbook), IHBG, IK, JFAB, JFAB-R, JG, JJJ, JJJ-R and KB-R (read through the policy page's own document list); the 2026-27 handbooks of all four schools; Windham High's 2026-27 Program of Studies and daily schedule; the Communications Guide; the 2026-27 calendar and monthly event calendars; school board minutes July 2025 to November 2026 (37 public sets searched; 2 cited); the FY27 budget documents already saved in the library; Windham Woods School's own pages and its FY2025 IRS 990; WMS athletics pages and handbook; WCS and WMS club pages; a WCS newsletter on Smore.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Getting into Salem High or Pinkerton's CTE programs, or waitlisted" for "electrical or plumbing" (P2.3): **partly answerable.** Seats are "competitive and limited," and the board says Windham has "a limited number of spots allocated." **No count of Windham applicants, admissions or waitlists is published** by Windham, Salem or Pinkerton. Electrical is open to Windham students at Pinkerton (from grade 11); **plumbing is not offered** to Windham students at either school.
  - "Pinkerton or Salem CTE engineering" (P15.1): **only Salem** offers engineering to Windham students; Pinkerton's list for Windham is Architecture, Animal Science, Electrical and Welding. Bus ride times aren't published; the derived arithmetic says a morning Salem program takes the place of Windham High's first block.
  - "Windham Woods School" (P5.2): it is a private day school in Windham for grades 1–12 serving students with learning disabilities such as dyslexia; 2026-27 tuition is $54,500. It is **not on NH DOE's June 2025 list of approved private special education providers**, though it describes itself as an approved nonpublic school.
  - "Finish out the school year in Windham" after moving to Derry or Pelham (P6.3): only if the move falls in the **last 60 school days** (grades K–8; last 90 for high school), or with the board's case-by-case approval; the family drives. For 2026-27 that window opens March 18, 2027 for K–8 if no snow days are used (derived).
  - "Annual homeschool paperwork or portfolio reviews" (P13.2): **out of date** under state law since July 1, 2026 (library `home-education-rules` 1–2, 5), but Windham's posted policy IHBG (reviewed March 17, 2026) still describes a participating agency, a yearly evaluation and a portfolio.
  - "Different start and dismissal times … tiered busing" (P14.1): **different times are correct** (Golden Brook 8:40–2:50; Windham Center 7:40–2:20 with homeroom from 7:30; Windham Middle 7:30–2:20; Windham High 7:52–2:17). No district document uses the word "tier" or says buses run more than one route.
  - "A late bus" after a club (P14.3): **none found.** Policy says a missed bus is the parent's responsibility, and Windham Middle's handbook says students take their regular bus at dismissal.
  - "A nearly 900-student building" (P16.1): **correct.** Windham High's 2026-27 profile lists 873 students (Salem file `high-school-and-cte.md`, school-programs-after-grade-6 13); the district's October 1, 2026 count is 863 (`money-buildings-and-staff.md`); the site's most recent school enrollment figure is 940.
  - "Everything English-only" (P9.2): **not quite.** The district website and Smore newsletters have Google Translate buttons (machine translation), and the ESOL plan promises an interpreter or translator on request; no source says SchoolMessenger or MyPowerHub messages are sent in other languages.
