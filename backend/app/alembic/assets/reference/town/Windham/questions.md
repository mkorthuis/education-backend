# Windham Residents' Questions

The questions the Windham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 20, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Windham is SAU 95, a one-district SAU. The Windham School District runs four schools:
- Golden Brook School: preschool to grade 4, with the district's Early Childhood Program;
- Windham Center School: grades 5–6;
- Windham Middle School: grades 7–8;
- Windham High School: grades 9–12.

Windham High students may take career and technical programs at Salem High's CTE center or at Pinkerton Academy. The district votes by official ballot.

Several questions contain premises the research should check rather than assume:
- a Golden Brook preschool lottery and a 2:50 dismissal;
- a budget over $72 million with enrollment near 2,900;
- the HB 1300 tax cap;
- nearly $39 million spent renovating Golden Brook and the middle school;
- $19,690 spent per pupil;
- moving Windham High's graduation off-site in 2028;
- Tutor.com for 4th graders;
- 30 varsity teams;
- a nearly 900-student high school;
- about 65% of students proficient in math;
- $730,000 homes;
- annual homeschool paperwork;
- full-time school resource officers and marshals at Golden Brook.
