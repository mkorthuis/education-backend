# Windham Middle School and Windham High School

Part of the Windham research (town/Windham). Gathered November 20, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Windham Middle School (grades 7–8) and Windham High School (grades 9–12): how the middle grades handle students who are ahead in math (Accelerated Math, 8th-grade Algebra I and its high school credit), the move from Windham Center School (homework, grading and retakes), Windham High's course load, AP and dual enrollment rules, homework by course level, science, engineering and computer science courses, FIRST Robotics, the college counseling and financial aid help the school offers, extended learning opportunities (ELOs), internships and the career diploma, phones, laptops and screen time, sports, team levels, tryouts and cuts, what families pay (and what the district budget pays) for athletics, the bell schedule and late arrival, and the 2026 decision to move graduation to the Tsongas Center from 2028.

- **12 fact topics, 84 facts and 4 number tables.** 81 facts use `district Windham`; the 3 facts in the proposed topic `college-credit-for-ap-and-dual-enrollment` (UMass Amherst's rules) have no place. Tables: `windham_area_algebra1_grades_7_8_2023_24` (places `district Windham`, `district Londonderry`, `district Salem`, `district Pelham`, `district Bedford`), `whs_ap_scores_3_or_higher_2017_2026`, `whs_yrbs_2023_selected_measures` and `whs_athletics_budget_lines_fy24_fy27` (all `district Windham`).
- **New data concepts:** Windham's Accelerated Math track and 8th-grade Algebra I, with federal counts of 8th graders in Algebra I at Windham Middle and four nearby middle schools (derived shares); the rule for high school credit for middle school courses; homework minutes by course level at Windham High and by department at Windham Middle; AP and dual enrollment tuition and exam costs; FIRST Robotics team 3467's 2025 and 2026 results; computer science students per 100 at area high schools (derived from the Salem file's federal table); Windham High's college and career readiness shares for 2026; 2023 Youth Risk Behavior Survey results on mattering, trusted adults, bullying and screen time; the district's budget lines for high school athletics; the graduation venue decision; UMass Amherst's rules for AP and transfer credit.
- **Main sources:** the Windham High 2026-27 Program of Studies, school profile, student handbook, student-athlete handbook and FY28 school improvement plan (October 2026); the Windham Middle 2026-27 handbook, FY28 improvement plan, incoming 7th grade parent night slides, athletics and club pages; the Windham Center 2026-27 handbook; the district's K–8 math curriculum and Math and Business page; board policies IFA, IHBH, IHCD, IKFB, IMBD, JICJ, JJIB and JQ with their procedures; board minutes July 2023 to November 2, 2026 (public sets searched; 6 cited); the principal's Smore newsletters for 2026-27; the 2023 Youth Risk Behavior Survey report for WHS; FIRST's team pages; the athletic boosters' site; the FY27 proposed budget already saved in the library; UMass Amherst's transfer credit pages; federal civil rights data rows already extracted by the Concord research.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Windham Middle School is ranked near the top in New Hampshire" (P3.1): **correct as the district reports it.** The district announced on October 20, 2026 that U.S. News ranked Windham Middle 4th among New Hampshire middle schools (district-goals-and-performance-reporting 1). U.S. News's own page, read in Chrome on November 21, confirms 4th of 140 in its 2027 rankings (4th in math proficiency, 5th in reading).
  - "Moving Windham High's graduation off-site in 2028" (P7.2): **correct, and now decided.** The board voted at its November 17, 2026 meeting to hold graduation at the Tsongas Center in Lowell, MA, starting in 2028 (district-school-day-and-calendar 6). **"Is the turf field really out of space" is not the reason given:** the stated reason is limited on-site options in bad weather (heat and thunderstorms were also raised). No source mentions seating capacity. The turf itself was approved for replacement in May 2026 because it was past its useful life (district-facilities-and-capital 1).
  - "1:1 Chromebooks" at WHS (P11.2): **partly wrong.** The district gives one-to-one Apple laptops in grades 9–12 and Chromebooks in grades 1–8 (district-technology-and-phones 1).
  - "30 varsity sports teams" (P12.1): **roughly right, with different counts in different sources.** The 2026-27 profile says "30 Varsity Sports Teams"; the improvement plan says "58 Athletic teams in 30 sports"; the boosters' site says 32 sports on one page and 29 on another (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 2).
  - "Athletic fees" (P12.2): **no athletic participation fee was found** in Windham's policies, handbooks, program of studies, newsletters or FY27 budget (district-fees 1).
  - "Be in the building so early" (P15.3): Windham High starts at **7:52 a.m.**, later than Windham Middle (7:30) and Windham Center (7:40, homeroom 7:30) and earlier than the 8:30 a.m. pediatricians recommend; juniors and seniors with a first-block study hall may apply for late arrival (district-school-day-and-calendar 2).
  - "Do colleges like UNH and UMass accept the credits?" (P7.1): **depends on the college, the course and the grade or score.** UNH takes dual enrollment courses with a C- or better and AP scores by subject; UMass Amherst takes comparable college courses with a C- or better and AP scores that vary by subject; both cap transferred credit (college-credit-for-ap-and-dual-enrollment 1–3; Exeter and Dover files).

- **Main gaps:** how many Windham students were admitted to their first-choice colleges, and how many families hire private college counselors (no source); how many students are cut from teams each season; a four-year cost of sports (no fees, uniform costs or booster dues are published by team); the laptop fee amount; how Windham Middle decides who takes Accelerated Math (no placement criteria posted); why the high school's start time is 7:52 (no source gives a reason).
