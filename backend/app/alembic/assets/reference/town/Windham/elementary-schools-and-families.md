# Windham Elementary Schools, the Preschool and Family Support

Part of the Windham research (town/Windham). Gathered November 20, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Windham School District's preschool to grade 6 years from a family's side: Golden Brook School (preschool to grade 4, with the Early Childhood Program) and Windham Center School (grades 5–6). It covers the preschool lottery and waitlist, full-day kindergarten and what families pay for, school hours, drop-off and dismissal, the Granite YMCA's before- and after-school care at both schools and its current prices, reading instruction (the 2024-25 move to Wit & Wisdom, the phonics and intervention programs), the 2026 math adoption, Title I and Response to Intervention, IEP progress reporting and extended school year services, the ESOL program, Tutor.com for grades 4–6, specials (unified arts), STEAM and clubs at Windham Center, the move from grade 4 to grade 5, K–4 class sizes, school meals and help for families short of money.
