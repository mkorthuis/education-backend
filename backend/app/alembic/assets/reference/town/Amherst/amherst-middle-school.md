# Amherst Middle School, Choices and Home Education

Part of the Amherst research (town/Amherst). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Amherst Middle School (AMS, grades 5–8) and the choices around it: buses and the question of late buses, special education staffing, outside evaluations and reading help, how 5th and 8th graders are grouped, the phone rules, hands-on and tech classes, how AMS's curriculum compares, charter school busing, sports and classes for charter, private and home-educated students, EFAs and private high school admission, home education paperwork, enrolling mid-year, and AI rules.

- **11 topics, 57 facts, 1 table.** All topics are shared (`district-transportation`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-technology-and-phones`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-homeschool-access`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `town-school-options-nearby`). No new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 33 MB). The loader check passed. It flags notes containing "draft", but in this file that word only names the FY28 budget draft presentations (proposals dated October 22 and November 19, 2026); no fact is pending.
- **Places used:** `district Amherst` (AMS), `sau 39` (SAU-wide policies, the special education manual, screening, registration, the bus contract), `town Amherst` (EFA counts and listings), `town Nashua` (Bishop Guertin, following the Nashua research), `district The Derryfield School`, `district Academy for Science and Design Charter School`.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Phones are locked away all day, lunch included.** Since 2026-27 every AMS student locks phones, earbuds and smartwatches in a Yondr pouch from 8:30 a.m. to 3:24 p.m.
  - **AMS and Souhegan share buses.** All 16 posted 2026-27 middle school routes are joint "AMS/SHS" routes, so 5th graders ride with 8th graders and high schoolers. Inside the school, grades 5–6 (Lower School) and 7–8 (Upper School) have separate assistant principals, counselors, teams and lunch periods.
  - **No late bus is posted for 2026-27.** Clubs run 3:45–4:45 p.m. A 4:45 p.m. late bus existed in November 2021, when the school said it might be cut for low ridership. No later source mentions it.
  - **Special education at AMS:** a learning specialist for each grade, Life Skills and My Time programs, and related services including speech therapy, occupational therapy and behavior analysts. SAU 39's manual promises an independent evaluation at public expense or a due process hearing, and SAU 39 says many of its educators have Orton-Gillingham and LETRS training. Budget drafts cut 4 AMS paraprofessional jobs in FY27 and propose 3 more for FY28 "based on student need".
  - **Home education:** SAU 39's website has no home education page. The only policy copies that could be reached (2018) already said no annual notice was required. They still describe the yearly evaluation that state law repealed on July 1, 2026.
  - **EFAs:** 130 Amherst children were EFA-eligible for 2026-27 (7 on the waitlist). Bishop Guertin and Derryfield are both on the EFA marketplace. Both have January 15 application deadlines and require entrance tests (HSPT; SSAT or ISEE).
- **Main gaps:**
  - SAU 39's current policies and board minutes are on Simbli, which uses a bot check and loads by script, so they weren't read. That leaves out the current home education policies IHBG and IHBG-R, any AI policy, the placement policy JG and the grading policy.
  - No count of speech-language pathologists at AMS.
  - No current (2025-26 or 2026-27) elective list.
  - No source on teacher AI training, on how a mid-year transfer is placed in a math level, on whether AMS offers Algebra 1 in grade 8, or on whether AMS's 5th grade has its own wing.
  - The AMS clubs flyers and grading handbook need a Google sign-in.
