# Amherst Residents' Questions

The questions the Amherst research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Amherst is in SAU 39 with Mont Vernon. The Amherst School District runs Clark-Wilkins School (preschool to grade 4) and Amherst Middle School (grades 5–8); Mont Vernon sends its middle schoolers to Amherst. Grades 9–12 attend Souhegan High School, run by the Souhegan Cooperative School District of Amherst and Mont Vernon. Souhegan students may take career programs at Milford's Applied Technology Center or the Nashua Technology Center. Both districts vote by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (the Wilkins project and 5th grade moving back in 2028, a $52.9 million bond voted down in 2025, early-release Wednesdays, 42 Souhegan students in CTE, a 2026 tax rate of $17.76 after revaluation, three school boards, a 2026 Attorney General letter about electioneering, 30% state building aid, an annual homeschool notification) that the research should check rather than assume.
