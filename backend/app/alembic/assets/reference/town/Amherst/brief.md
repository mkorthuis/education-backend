## What you need to know about Amherst

- **Amherst** is a town in Hillsborough County west of Nashua and Merrimack, along Routes 101, 101A and 122, with Baboosic Lake. Site town id 17.
- **Two school districts serve Amherst, both in SAU 39** (with Mont Vernon):
-   - **The Amherst School District** (site district id 17), with Clark-Wilkins School (21750), preschool to grade 4, and Amherst Middle School (21745), grades 5–8, which also takes Mont Vernon's middle schoolers (`places.yaml`).
-   - **The Souhegan Cooperative** (site district id 493): Souhegan High School (26860), grades 9–12, for Amherst and Mont Vernon.
- **Career and technical education:** Souhegan students attend Milford's Applied Technology Center and the Nashua Technology Center. Both are researched (`town/Milford/middle-high-and-atc.md`, `town/Nashua/pathways-and-adult-learning.md`); write only Souhegan's side.
- **Starting pages:** SAU 39, https://www.sau39.org (both districts, boards, budgets, the Wilkins project, policies); the town, https://www.amherstnh.gov.
- **Site figures:**
-   - Resident students: 1,903 (2014-15), 1,844 (2019-20), 1,816 (2026-27).
-   - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Amherst $23,894 (K–8), Souhegan $25,841, Hollis $23,370, Milford $22,084, state $22,699.
-   - 2025 tax rates per $1,000: total $23.98, local school $16.24, municipal $4.92, equalization ratio 66% (P15.1 says $17.76 after revaluation, so check 2026).
-   - Question 2: Amherst 56.7%, Souhegan 56.3%, so no cap (unofficial).
-   - Math proficiency, 2026: Amherst 67%, Souhegan 46%, state 44%.
- **Shared cooperative:** Souhegan and SAU 39 serve Mont Vernon too. Write their facts here with `district Souhegan Cooperative` or `sau 39`; Mont Vernon has no research yet.
- **The site's own data** has every school's enrollment by grade, test scores, class sizes, staff and salary schedules, free and reduced-price meals, English learners, safety counts, graduation and plans, licensed child care, EFAs by town, Census estimates, adequacy aid, special education aid, tax rates and school taxes.
