# Thornton Central School Operations and Families

Part of the Thornton research (town/Thornton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Thornton Central School (TCS, kindergarten to grade 8) day to day, for questions P1.1, P1.3, P2.3, P4.2, P4.3, P5.2, P6.1, P6.2, P6.3, P7.2, P7.3, P11.1, P11.3, P15.1 and P16.1: preschool and the nearest alternatives; kindergarten entry; after-school care and the new on-site program; the 2026-27 calendar and how it compares with Plymouth Regional High School (PRHS); buses and a late bus; snow-day decisions and notices; where families find the calendar, menus and news; internet, cell service and remote learning; school meals, free and reduced-price applications and summer meals; fees and help with costs; music, art and foreign language; the Winter Activities program and outdoor time; volunteering, the library and community use; and enrolling a grandchild.

**Counts:** 19 topics, 65 facts and 4 number tables (`thornton_drive_to_preschool_options_2026`, `thornton_after_school_monthly_rates_2026_27`, `thornton_winter_activity_costs_2027`, `thornton_school_days_by_month_2026_27`).

**Main sources:** the TCS Parent and Student Handbook 2026-2027 (September 2026 draft, 117 pages); TCS web pages (home, meals, winter activities, staff directory, approved policies, board agendas); the TCS Happenings newsletter of November 20, 2026; the 2026-27 calendar (an image, read by eye); the 2026-27 winter activity letters and forms (images, transcribed); the SAU 48 free and reduced-price meals FAQ for TCS; Thornton School Board minutes from March 2025 to November 2026 (scans transcribed by OCR where they have no text layer); Innovation Learning's Thornton page, flyer and rate sheet; the TCS PTO site; Thornton Public Library pages; the Town of Thornton's 2023 Master Plan and 2022 survey; NH Broadband's Thornton page; and the site's own data (enrollment, licensed child care).

**Main gaps:** bus times and stops are not posted (families call the bus company or the office), so no Route 3 pickup time; no late or activity bus is posted; no time of day or process is posted for the snow-day decision beyond "SAU 48 Administration"; no standing program for winter gear, and no fee help is posted for Winter Activities or field trips (the PTO and the library's Friends group have funds); no policy on community use of the gym; no measured cell coverage (the town's own survey says it is poor in many places); the lunch menu app (FD MealPlanner) and the school's Instagram page could not be read; the parent resources page needs a sign-in.

**Library gaps filled:** the library's coverage table lists Thornton's kindergarten day, kindergarten cutoff, kindergarten registration and preschool as "not found" (district-practices coverage table; additional-data/raw/districts/not-found-notes.csv), although the Thornton brief says they were loaded. This file adds all four.

**No new topics proposed.** All slugs are shared topics or the library's kindergarten slugs.

**Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 15. Several lean on earlier files, which are cited and not repeated: the Campton research for the Campton preschool and Got Lunch, Plymouth's files for the SAU 48 residency notice, the PRHS calendar and the SAU 48 ski passes, Rochester's file for New Hampshire's snow-day and remote-learning rules, Concord's for the federal summer meal rules, and the library for Summer EBT.
