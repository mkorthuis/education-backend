# Thornton Central School: Learning, Support and School Choices

Part of the Thornton research (town/Thornton). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers Thornton Central School (TCS, kindergarten to grade 8, Thornton School District, SAU 48): how big its classes are and how a parent can ask for a class change, test results next to the state's and nearby schools' (including the "57% and 62%" and "7 to 1" figures in the questions), special education staff, speech therapy, the cost of out-of-district placements and the move of an IEP to Plymouth Regional High School (PRHS), the school counselor, bullying, behavior and phone rules, sports (including the field hockey team TCS shares with Campton), access for homeschooled and Education Freedom Account (EFA) students, returning to school after homeschooling or moving in from a larger school, how TCS and PRHS compare for math and science, and how Campton Elementary and Russell Elementary (Rumney) compare with TCS.

**Counts:** 11 topics, 81 facts and 6 number tables (facts by place: district Thornton 72, district Rumney 5, SAU 48 3, district Pemi-Baker Regional 1). Raw copies are in `raw/learning/` (the TCS handbook, seven board policies, ten sets of cleaned Thornton School Board minutes from January 2026 to November 2026 and excerpts of the 2025 minutes, the 2026-27 budget form and budget hearing presentation, the special education enrollment chart, Russell Elementary's pages and handbook, SAU 48 pages and forms, and the site-database queries with derived CSVs).

**Main sources:** the TCS Parent and Student Handbook 2026-27 (revised August 10, 2026); Thornton Central School's staff directory, athletics and policy pages; policies JJJ (access for non-enrolled students, approved September 28, 2026), JCA (change of class, SAU 48, 2022), JICK (bullying, updated September 28, 2026), JICJ (phones, August 25, 2025), JKAA (restraint and seclusion) and JLCK (physical and emotional well-being, November 17, 2025); Thornton School Board minutes (January 2025 to November 2026); the adopted 2026-27 budget form and the February 5, 2026 budget hearing presentation; the 2026-27 basketball schedule draft and sports permission letter; SAU 48's homeschooling page and notification form and K-8 math and counseling competencies; Russell Elementary's website and 2026-27 handbook; the site's own data (test scores, enrollment, class size, staff, discipline and bullying counts); and earlier files for Plymouth, Campton, Nashua, Epping and Moultonborough.

**Main gaps:** no source says how many days a week the speech pathologist or the Student Services staff are in the building, who the current Section 504 coordinator is (the handbook names a counselor who retired in June 2025), which math program TCS uses or how 8th graders are placed in math, or what TCS does to hand an IEP to PRHS beyond what SAU 48 and PRHS post. No source compares how Thornton's graduates do in PRHS classes. TCS posts no gifted and talented plan (not asked here) and no data on how many students are new to the school each year. The state's own report card (https://jwt.nh.gov/ and the school's iReport) is a dashboard that automated requests can't read. Whether Thornton's students could join other Campton teams beyond field hockey isn't covered by any policy found.

## district-conduct-policies: Bullying, Behavior and Discipline at Thornton Central

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice

Thornton's bullying policy JICK (updated and approved September 28, 2026) lets a student report to the principal or to any school employee or volunteer, requires staff to tell the principal by the end of that school day, requires the principal to notify the parents of both the student reported as bullied and the student reported as bullying within 48 hours, start an investigation within 5 school days, finish it in most cases within 10 school days and tell both families the remedies within 10 school days, and bars retaliation.
