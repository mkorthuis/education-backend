# Thornton: Money, Taxes, the Building and Governance

Part of the Thornton research (town/Thornton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 49 facts and 4 number tables on how Thornton pays for Thornton Central School (kindergarten to grade 8) and, through the Pemi-Baker Regional School District, Plymouth Regional High School; on SAU 48's cost to Thornton; on the 2024 energy bond and the school building's condition; on school board meetings; on pay for teachers, support staff and substitutes; and on housing.
- **Taxes and the split:** Thornton's own books show $5,816,981 (70.4%) of its 2025-26 school appropriations going to Thornton Central and $2,442,558 (29.6%) to Pemi-Baker (**derived**); the two districts' own 2026-27 figures put the total at $8,689,564, up $430,025 (5.21%, **derived**). The state education property tax is inside these totals. Thornton Central's 2026-27 assessment rose 5.70% ($0.33 per $1,000), driven by health insurance, transportation and a falling state adequacy grant ($624,971 in 2021-22 to an estimated $377,280).
- **Why bills change:** Thornton sends two bills a year (June, based on half of last year's rate; December, final). The town's April 1, 2024 revaluation reset assessed values after a 2023 state ratio study put assessments at 56.5% of sales prices. Enrollment at Thornton Central fell from 215 (2018-19) to 172 (2024-25), not "flat", while cost per pupil rose from $18,696 to $33,346 (site data, **derived**).
- **The building:** voters approved a $4.5 million energy project (new boiler room, lighting, ventilation, envelope) on March 7, 2024, 201 to 89, with up to $4,007,118 in bonds; debt service is $286,107 in 2026-27. The school's capital plan (updated June 23, 2026) lists $2.95 million over ten years, including a $1.05 million cafeteria in 2029-30, and no roof or new school.
- **Governance:** SAU 48 charges Thornton 10.69% of the SAU's assessment ($252,587 in 2026-27, 3% of the school budget); each district board adopts its own budget. The five-member board meets monthly, mostly on Mondays at 5:30 p.m.; 2026 minutes show 2 to 9 named people present and a speaker or question in 7 of 20 public comment periods (**derived**). Voters rejected a school budget cap 60 to 150 and official ballot voting 55 to 158 in 2025.
- **Pay and staffing:** Thornton Central pays substitutes $100 a day (teachers and support staff, 2026-27), tied with Wentworth for the highest rate in SAU 48 (Pemi-Baker's teacher rate is also $100). The support staff contract (2026-2029) has a 4% yearly adjustment; no turnover counts are published.
- **Housing:** the 2023 master plan reports 37.1% seasonal homes in 2010, a $375,000 year-to-date median sale price in June 2023 and a resident survey calling for more affordable housing for young families.

**Main sources:** Thornton School District's 2026-27 adopted budget, warrant, budget hearing deck, pie chart, capital plan, energy performance plan deck and "Building Issue Journey"; ten sets of 2026 school board minutes plus 2024 and 2025 excerpts; the March 2024, 2025 and 2026 district meeting minutes; both Thornton collective bargaining agreements; the FY2026 board report; the Town of Thornton 2025 Annual Report, 2023 Master Plan, 2024 revaluation manual and tax pages; SAU 48's substitute pages and 2026-27 and 2027 calendars; BLS wage data; the site's database; Plymouth's money file for Pemi-Baker and SAU 48 figures.
