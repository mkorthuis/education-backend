# Thornton Residents' Questions

The questions the Thornton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Thornton's students attend Thornton Central School (kindergarten to grade 8) in the Thornton School District and then Plymouth Regional High School, run by the Pemi-Baker Regional School District, all in SAU 48 with its office in Plymouth. Several questions contain premises (about 20 kids per grade, a separate SAU office in Plymouth for a school of about 170, scores of 57% in math and 62% in reading, a 7-to-1 class size, Owl's Nest's expansion and the tax rate, Applied Technology Center programs at PRHS, ParentSquare and Google Classroom, a 40-minute bus ride) that the research should check rather than assume.

- **P1.1** Does Thornton Central do public pre-K, or are we stuck driving down to Campton or Plymouth for daycare another year?

- **P1.3** Is the internet and cell service reliable enough on snow days for remote learning if school gets called off?
