## What you need to know about Thornton

- **Thornton** is a town in Grafton County on Route 3 and I-93 near Waterville Valley and Lincoln, with Owl's Nest Resort, Mad River Road, Mill Brook Road and Route 175. Site town id 531.
- **The Thornton School District** (site district id 531) is in **SAU 48** and runs Thornton Central School (22380; kindergarten to grade 8; 172 students in 2024-25). Grades 9–12 are the **Pemi-Baker Regional School District** (site district id 428): Plymouth Regional High School (PRHS; 22350) and its career and technical center. SAU 48's other districts are Campton, Ellsworth, Holderness, Plymouth, Rumney, Waterville Valley and Wentworth. Check how Pemi-Baker's costs are split.
