# Money and Governance in Raymond (SAU 33)

Part of the Raymond research (town/Raymond). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered.** The 2026-27 operating budget ($32,121,915) and why its default budget ($32,285,799) was higher; the 2025 vote that left the district on its default budget; where the money goes (salaries and benefits, tuitions, transportation, benefits growth); the teachers' (REA) and support staff (RESS) contracts and the district's own salary comparisons; the district's estimated tax impacts, its year-end surplus returned to taxpayers, revenue estimates and the November 2026 worksheets for fitting the 2027-28 budget under the new school tax cap; the town's tax billing schedule, revaluation notice and elderly and veterans' relief; grants (federal formula grants, 21st Century after-school, Student Assistance, Promising Futures, a workforce grant, a biomass energy grant and higher special education aid); the end of the middle school bond in 2025, the capital improvement plan and capital reserve funds, the $250,000 elementary school capital reserve; the Lamprey River Elementary building project (why the district says the building must be replaced, the 2021 and 2025 studies, the $47,435,000 estimate, the 40% aid rate and the likely 30%, the June 2027 state decision and a bond vote no earlier than 2028); enrollment since 2007-08 and its projections; how school board meetings, public input, agendas and the deliberative session work, with what the 2025 and 2026 sessions did; the strategic plan.

**Counts.** 8 topics (all shared), 83 facts, 3 number tables.

**Main sources.** SAU 33's posted PDFs (the 2026 warrant article slideshow, the January 8, 2026 budget hearing slideshow, the February 2026 voter newsletter, the MS-27 and MS-DSB forms, the 2026-27 capital improvement plan, the 2025 and 2026 election minutes, salary comparison, budget committee responses), 2026 School Board working agendas, minutes and backup (year-end surplus, capital reserve balances, grants, revenues, tax cap worksheet, educational specifications, principals' reports), the district's building project site and its linked Google Drive documents (2021 Report of Findings, March 2025 Elementary Committee report, October 2025 public forum minutes, cost worksheets), policies BEDH and BEDB, and the Town of Raymond's assessing, exemption, tax collector and revaluation pages.

**Main gaps.** www.sau33.com's HTML pages (minutes index, policies index, report card to the community, human resources, annual report) refuse automated requests; its PDF files could be read. No count of teacher turnover, or of where departing teachers go, was found. The district has not published an estimated tax rate for the new elementary school; the tax impact depends on building aid and bond terms not yet known. NH DOE's building aid rank order list refused the request, so Raymond's place on it is not confirmed. The 2026 tax rate had not been set by November 18, 2026.
