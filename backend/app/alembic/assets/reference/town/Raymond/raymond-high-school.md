# Raymond High School and Raymond's Side of the Seacoast School of Technology

Part of the Raymond research (town/Raymond). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Raymond High School (RHS, grades 9–12, 45 Harriman Hill Road): its size, courses, shop classes, AP and Early College courses, graduation and personal finance requirements, the move into 9th grade, counseling and mental health support, sports, ways to catch up or finish a diploma, adult education near Raymond, and Raymond's side of the Seacoast School of Technology (SST) in Exeter. SST itself (programs, sessions, admission, credentials, college credit, enrollment by sending school) is in `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md`, cited by topic and fact number. Facts about RHS and the district are placed `district Raymond`; adult education near the town `town Raymond`; Manchester Community College's admission rules have no place.

- **Premises checked:**
  - "Raymond High only has around 330 kids" (P7.1): **out of date.** RHS counted 329 high school students in 2024-25 and 315 in June 2026, but 295 on its September 16, 2026 opening-day report; SST's October 2026 report counts 289 in grades 9–12. The site's data shows 441 in grades 9–12 in 2015-16 and 321 in 2024-25.
  - "Anything like JROTC" (P18.1): **none.** No JROTC course or unit appears in RHS's 2026-27 program of studies or its list of activities. RHS offers a "Selective College and Military Academies" course plan, visits by military representatives, the Raymond Police Explorers and SST's Health Science program.
  - "Welding or carpentry at SST" (P5.1): **confirmed.** SST runs Welding Technologies and Building Construction Technologies, and RHS's program of studies lists both. No acceptance rates by program are published.
  - "Hands-on shop classes right here in town" (P5.2): **confirmed.** RHS offers Woodworking I and II, Fine Woodworking, a full-year Wood & Construction Technology course, Introduction to Electricity and Electronics, Robotic Design and Introduction to Advanced Manufacturing; from fall 2026 these are being realigned, course by course, with Manchester Community College's advanced manufacturing certificate.
  - "Running Start classes" (P10.1): **renamed.** The program is now Early College; RHS lists two Early College courses taught at the school (Honors/CCSNH Pre-Calculus and CCSNH Quantitative Reasoning), plus the new Manchester Community College robotics course.
  - "A Freshman Academy" (P9.2): **not found.** RHS runs Jumpstart, a summer orientation for incoming freshmen; no freshman academy, team or separate 9th-grade program appears in its documents.
  - "No mandatory personal finance class" for a 2025 graduate (P19.1): **correct, with a qualification.** The class of 2025 had to take a half-credit Economics course whose topics include personal finance; a separate Personal Finance course and Consumer Math (taxes, credit cards, loans) were electives. From the class of 2030, Economics is a one-credit course that includes half a credit of financial literacy.
  - "The school's career center" (P19.3): **not found.** RHS describes career services inside its school counseling office; no separate career center, and no services for graduates, appear in its documents.
