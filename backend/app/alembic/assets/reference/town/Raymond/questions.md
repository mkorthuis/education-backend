# Raymond Residents' Questions

The questions the Raymond research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Raymond is SAU 33, a one-district SAU. The Raymond School District runs a preschool program, Lamprey River Elementary School (kindergarten to grade 3), Iber Holmes Gove Middle School (grades 4–8) and Raymond High School (grades 9–12). Raymond High students may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (a preschool at the high school, a $32.1 million operating budget with a higher default, $250,000 of surplus for a Lamprey River reserve, a Lamprey River renovation or new building, about 330 high school students, an open enrollment vote in March) that the research should check rather than assume.
