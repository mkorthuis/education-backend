# Support, Choices and Daily Life in Raymond

Part of the Raymond research (town/Raymond). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

This file covers student support, school choices and daily life in the Raymond School District (SAU 33): paraeducator staffing and one-on-one aides, special education communication at Iber Holmes Gove Middle School (IHGMS), out-of-district placements and their transportation, outside ratings of the schools, Education Freedom Account (EFA) and home-educated students in district classes, sports and band, VLACS online courses for Raymond High (RHS) credit, translation and how families are told about closings, middle school teams and rides after practice, athletic fields and gyms, snow days and remote learning days, the bullying policy, the March 2026 open enrollment vote, manifest educational hardship transfers and military-connected families.

**Counts:** 12 topics, 85 facts, 4 number tables (`raymond_paraeducator_fte_2022_2026`, `raymond_special_education_report_fy2023_2025`, `raymond_cip_athletic_facilities_2027_2041`, `raymond_greatschools_ratings_2026`). Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 31 MB).

**Main sources:** Raymond School Board policies IC, JFAM, JFBC, JICC, JICK (August 2026), JJIB, JJJ (September 2026), EEA and IMBC; the IHGMS 2026-27 and 2025-26 handbooks, the RHS 2026-27 handbook and program of studies, the RHS athletic eligibility handbook; the 2025 district annual report; 18 sets of approved school board minutes (April 2025 to October 2026); the 2026 Deliberative Session minutes, ballot results, voter newsletter and budget committee materials; the 2026-27 calendar and capital improvement plan; VLACS's pages; GreatSchools; and 34 CFR 300.320.

**Main gaps:** every HTML page on sau33.com (Student Support Services, Transportation, Handbooks, the bullying forms page, the policy index) returned a Cloudflare bot check, so this file relies on the district's PDFs, which were reachable. Policy JCA (manifest educational hardship, revised January 2026) could not be found as a file, so Raymond's own transfer procedure is not quoted. No source says whether Raymond translates newsletters or emergency alerts, whether IHGMS teams cut players, how late any activity bus runs (none is described), how often Raymond families' hardship transfers are approved, or how satisfied parents are. The NH Department of Education's September 2026 advisory on EFA access and fees was blocked.
