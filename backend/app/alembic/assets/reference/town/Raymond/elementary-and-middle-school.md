## Summary

- how families get a 4-year-old into the preschool at Raymond High School, and what it costs;
- Lamprey River Elementary's classes and the plan for a new preschool-to-grade-4 building;

- arrival times, before-school care and the after-school program (LEAP);
- intervention and enrichment time, i-Ready screening, grade 8 acceleration and homework limits;
- the phone and smartwatch policy, Chromebooks and fees;
- bus times, one-day bus changes and stop changes;
- meal prices and applications;
- counselors, social workers, the Student Intervention Team and English learners;
- town help with costs.

- **Main findings:**
  - The preschool is at the high school ("Lambs to Rams"), with half-day sessions four days a week. It costs **$150 a month** plus a $75 deposit and usually fills. Child Find screenings are on November 6, 2026 and March 26, 2027. (The library's `district-preschool` fact 95 says no tuition is posted; see For another file.)
  - **IHGMS:** classrooms open at **7:35 a.m.**, there is no supervision before then, and school runs 7:55–2:35. No before-school program is listed there. Lamprey River has a district before-school program from 7:00 to 8:30 for **$150 a month**, and LEAP runs after school at both schools until 6:00 (Lamprey River) or 5:45 (IHGMS).
  - Buses: IHGMS morning routes start picking up at 7:05–7:12. For a one-day change, a parent sends a note, and the office approves afternoon changes; a full bus takes no extra riders.
  - Meals aren't free for all. Lunch costs $3.60 at Lamprey River and $3.75 at IHGMS ($0.40 reduced price), and families reapply each year.
  - Lamprey River's classes average about 16 children, and the board plans a new preschool-to-grade-4 school. At IHGMS each grade keeps its own lunch and recess times.
  - Reading and math help: Lamprey River sets aside daily intervention and enrichment time. IHGMS runs six-week RtI groups for intervention or enrichment, screened with i-Ready, and offers grade 8 Algebra I with Physical Science for high school credit. No Raymond school reported a gifted program.
  - The phone policy (JICJ, revised August 20, 2025) bans phones and smartwatches bell to bell. IHGMS students have assigned Chromebooks, and the handbooks list no Chromebook fee.
