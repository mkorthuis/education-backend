# Nottingham: SAU 107, School Taxes, Tuition, the Building and the Board

Part of the Nottingham research (town/Nottingham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 51 facts and 7 number tables on the Nottingham School District's (SAU 107's) money and governance.
- **Leaving SAU 44:** the 2020 plan's reasons, its projected $516,754 single-district SAU and "at least $157,891" a year of savings; what the SAU office and business office actually cost in FY2025 to FY2027 (about $713,000 a year, **derived**); the 2024-25 superintendent's salary against the planned $75,000 part-time role; the leadership changes since 2025 (a part-time superintendent, a contracted business office, a Chief Education Officer); and the payroll error from the separation that affected 66 employees' retirement accounts.
- **Taxes:** the local school levy against flat resident enrollment (**derived**), the 2025 revaluation, the district's own FY27 tax estimates, the 2026 petition for a school tax cap, and Question 2 (57.2% yes, short of 60%).
- **Tuition:** the new 2026–2036 Coe-Brown contract (no percentage cap; Nottingham's rate may not exceed Strafford's or Northwood's), Dover's 2026-27 rate estimate ($22,720.93, up 11.12%), the high school budget lines, the October 2026 split (137 at Coe-Brown, 79 at Dover) and what a shift between schools costs (**derived**).
- **Budget and staff:** the FY27 budget and its drivers, the FY26 surplus and its uses, what cost per pupil leaves out, the 2026–2029 teachers' contract and what the public record shows about staff turnover.
- **The building and crowding:** the 1994–95 building, capital reserve funds and why repairs come as separate articles, the repair history, the draft 2027–2036 capital plan, the space study and NESDEC's enrollment projection.
- **The board:** meeting times, the YouTube channel, public comment and the FY28 budget calendar.

**Main sources:** the district's MS-27 (2026-27), 2026 warrant and sample ballot, 2026 Mandatory Report to Voters, school board minutes (February 2025 to October 2026) and packets (October 2025 to November 2026, including the Coe-Brown contract, Dover's rate sheet, NESDEC's projection and the draft capital plan), the facilities committee's documents, Nottingham's 2020 SAU planning report and 2021 mailer (saved by the Northwood research), RSA 35, and the site's tax, enrollment, adequacy aid, DOE-25, cost per pupil, test score and Question 2 data.

Nottingham's SAU planning committee, created by voters on March 10, 2020, recommended in November 2020 that Nottingham leave SAU 44 and become a single-district SAU from 2022-23, citing local control over the community's spending, administrators focused only on Nottingham's students, faster business office work and audits, and lower cost, while saying "projected financial savings alone would not necessarily support becoming a single district SAU."
