# Nottingham Residents' Questions

The questions the Nottingham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Nottingham is SAU 107, formed when Nottingham left SAU 44. The Nottingham School District runs Nottingham School on Stage Road (preschool to grade 8), and its high school students choose Dover High School or Coe-Brown Northwood Academy under tuition contracts. Several questions contain premises (promised savings from leaving SAU 44, a preschool lottery, Oyster River as an option, annual homeschool portfolios, caps on tuition increases) that the research should check rather than assume.
