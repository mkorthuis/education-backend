# Nottingham School: Early Grades and Families

Part of the Nottingham research (town/Nottingham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Nottingham School (preschool to grade 8, 245 Stage Road) for young children and their families: the preschool's enrollment rules, drop-off and pickup, and what happens on storm days; the kindergarten day; school hours, car-line drop-off and pickup with Pick Up Patrol; before- and after-school care (none run by the school) and early-release days; the 2026-27 calendar, delays, closings and make-up days; the seven bus routes, ride times, the "Plan B" routes used when a bus has no driver, the board's bus stop rules and which route roads are unpaved; class sizes in the October 2026 enrollment report and the board's class-size guideline; how kindergarten teaches reading, the reading program, screening and Title I reading help; meal prices, free and reduced-price applications and meal charging; English learner policy and staffing; translation and paper notices; non-resident attendance after a move; and summer reading, library programs and the library's computers and printing.

**Counts:** 12 topics, 54 facts, 1 number table (derived from the 2026-27 bus route sheets).

**Main sources:** the Nottingham School website (Pre-K, kindergarten, bus routes, food services, Title I, student support services, curriculum, staff directory, parent portal, calendars, news, policies), the 2026-27 Student and Family Handbook, the 2026-27 school calendar, the Nottingham Preschool Google site and its "What to Know" sheet, the kindergarten Google site and its 2026 "Welcome to Kindergarten!" presentation, the 2026-27 bus route and Plan B sheets (Dail Transportation), school board policies EEA, EBCE, IC, IDA, EFAA, EFC, IHBBA, IHCA, IKACA, JFAB and KF, board minutes of August 5, October 7 and October 21, 2026 and packet pages for August 19 and November 18, 2026, the dyslexia screening notice, the Blaisdell Memorial Library website, the site's own data, and OpenStreetMap road-surface tags (derived use only).

**Main gaps:** whether any child care program runs before or after school in the building or serves Nottingham School on early-release days (the school names none; the town's recreation pages could not be read); 2026-27 meal prices (the page still shows 2024/2025 prices); whether the school offers translation or interpreting beyond the meal program's civil rights notice; whether buses enter unpaved side roads in mud season or winter (no policy says; only corner stops on the route sheets and OpenStreetMap tags suggest it); the town recreation department's summer camp and vacation programs (town website refused automated requests); and the board's general tuition rate for a non-resident child.
