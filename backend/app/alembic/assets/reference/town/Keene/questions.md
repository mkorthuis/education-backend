# Keene Residents' Questions

The questions the Keene research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Keene is a city in Cheshire County and the largest district in SAU 29, which also serves Chesterfield, Harrisville, Marlborough, Marlow, Nelson and Westmoreland. The Keene School District runs Jonathan Daniels School (preschool), four elementary schools for kindergarten to grade 5 (Franklin, Fuller, Symonds and Wheelock), Keene Middle School (grades 6–8) and Keene High School (9–12) with the Cheshire Career Center. The other SAU 29 towns and Stoddard send students to Keene Middle and Keene High under AREA agreements. Keene has two public charter schools, Gathering Waters and Making Community Connections (MC2), and votes by official ballot (SB2). Several questions contain premises (a Question 2 tax cap and the "4% of the budget that isn't locked in", "Benjamin Franklin Elementary" and its 2:40 p.m. pickup, "Cheshire Center Preschool", KHS safety renovations, one elementary Spanish teacher for 400 students, a remote evaluation idea for special education, tuition towns not covering the cost, Career Center access in grade 9, Keene Community Education's offerings, annual homeschool evaluations) that the research should check rather than assume.
