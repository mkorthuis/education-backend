# Special Education, Student Support and Staff in Keene

Part of the Keene research (town/Keene). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how the Keene School District (SAU 29) handles special education evaluations and staffing, paraprofessionals at Wheelock, the move from an elementary school to Keene Middle School for students with IEPs, teacher and support staff hiring, pay terms and mentoring, substitutes, teacher collaboration time, translation for families, bullying notification, bus service and bus drivers, and support for staff dealing with student behavior.

- **6 topics, 87 facts and 2 number tables.** All six are shared district slugs: `district-special-education-services`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-student-support`, `district-family-communication`, `district-conduct-policies` and `district-transportation`. Most facts are `place: district Keene`; SAU-wide items (the Student Services office, the substitute pay sheet, SEAC) are `place: sau 29`. No new topic is proposed.
- **New data concepts:** special education evaluation loads by school (September 2026); the district's caseload range and how it is applied; certified and support staff openings, November 2025 against November 2026; positions vacant in winter 2024-25 by school and type (table); students with disabilities by school, October 2022–2024 (table); substitute daily and hourly rates and bonuses; paraprofessional and classroom assistant benefit thresholds by scheduled hours; the paraprofessional contract's last posted wage schedule and the 2025 successor's cost items; the mentoring and induction plan; the 2026-27 professional development calendar; the bus contractor and its posted driver pay.
- **Main sources:** Keene Board of Education, Education Committee and Personnel Committee minutes (January 2025 to November 2026); the superintendent's monthly reports (June and October 2026); the FY2026 budget book (Wheelock, Special Education, ELL, Personnel, General District and the question-and-answer section); SAU 29's Student Services, Special Education, ELL, HR benefits and substitute pages; SAU 29's job board (November 16, 2026) and the district's posting lists; Keene policies IHBAA, IHBBA, JICK, JICC, JLDBA, GCG and EEA; the Wheelock, Fuller and Keene Middle School handbooks; the 2026-27 calendar; the Keene Paraprofessionals Association agreement (2021–2025); Student Transportation of America's Keene posting.
- **Main findings:**
  - In September 2026 the Director of Student Services reported about 47 evaluations under way at Keene High and about 10 at Keene Middle, and said a psychologist-only evaluation model would need two more full-time positions. The district has a contract with Antioch University evaluators. In October 2026 the Education Committee heard of "a new program" that "may cut down time on evaluations." No minutes found describe it as remote.
  - The district's own posting lists show 9 certified openings in November 2026 against 17 in November 2025 (derived). The board said vacancies had "almost halved." Most open certified jobs are in special services: a school psychologist, two BCBAs, speech and occupational therapy, and special education teachers at Wheelock and for charter schools.
  - Wheelock had 3 vacant paraprofessional positions in winter 2024-25. Its 2025-26 budget removed one vacant special education paraprofessional position and moved two from Symonds. Its 2026-27 handbook lists five special education paraprofessionals and a WIN paraprofessional. In November 2026 its special education teacher job (Learning Center) had been posted since October 6.
  - Keene Middle School runs three collaborative programs: Bridges, Horizons and Vistas. Each acts as its own cluster, and Vistas serves students on the autism spectrum. The district says transition meetings are held with families.
  - Keene's bullying policy JICK has the principal tell both families within 48 hours, and it counts school buses as school property. A revised policy was at first reading in October 2026.
  - Keene's buses are run by a contractor. Student Transportation of America's Keene driver posting lists a pay range of "30.00-31.00", paid training, and a program for drivers to bring their own child along.
- **Main gaps:** any count of late evaluations or the average time from consent to evaluation (NH DOE's Indicator 11 and district data pages refused automated requests); what the October 2026 "new program" for evaluations is; whether any IEP service hours have gone undelivered at Wheelock; the current paraprofessional wage schedule (the 2025 successor agreement isn't posted); the teachers' (KEA) contract text, including prep and collaboration time and any mentor stipend (the state labor board's copy refused automated requests); any district statement on translating IEP documents into Spanish or who arranges interpreters.
