# Keene: Money, Taxes, SAU 29 and Buildings

Part of the Keene research (town/Keene). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 70 facts and 6 number tables on the Keene School District's 2026-27 (FY2027) budget and what drove it, the school tax and what the district said about the "4% discretionary" share, Question 2's result in Keene, the Keene High School safety projects and how they are being paid for, the AREA tuition agreements and how the tuition rate is figured, SAU 29's budget and how it is split, where the budget documents are and what the district publishes, the City of Keene's 2026 revaluation, the city's own tax limit, exemptions, and Keene rents.
- **The FY2027 budget:** $82,426,334, up 3.4%, approved March 10, 2026; the school tax up 12.74% ($5,010,440) because non-tax revenue fell $2,279,642 (adequacy aid down $1,676,987 and a smaller surplus). Drivers: out-of-district special education placements and transportation, contracted pay and a 10.2% health premium rise. About 13 positions cut and 8 added.
- **Discretionary spending:** the district's own breakdown puts $3,365,992 (4.1%, **derived**) in five discretionary lines (field trip and athletic transportation, books, supplies, equipment); staff pay is classed as non-discretionary.
- **Keene High safety work:** a $400,000 first phase of a mass notification and fire alarm system (estimated at about $2.5 million in all) was moved out of the budget and paid from the building maintenance trust fund (up to $650,000 approved April 14, 2026). A larger security and locker room plan (Banwell Architects, October 6, 2026) has no board action; the capital plan pencils in $2,357,941 a year from 2028-29 to 2031-32, the same yearly amount now spent on elementary life safety renovations. No bond is proposed.
- **Tuition:** the 20-year AREA agreements (from July 1, 2026) set tuition at Keene High's net operating cost per student plus a building charge; 2026-27 rates are $18,842 (KHS) and $16,182 (KMS), with special education billed separately. The CFO calls it "break even."
- **Taxes and housing:** Keene's Question 2 vote was 4,247 yes to 5,049 no, so no school tax cap applies. The city's 2026 revaluation raised preliminary single-family values 67% and apartment buildings 49%; the 2026 rate is set by NH DRA in November.

**Main sources:** the district's FY2027 budget books (introduction, revenues, debt, maintenance, KHS and elementary justifications, the Q&A section), the January 20, 2026 public hearing presentation, Finance Committee, Facilities Committee and board minutes (November 2025 to October 2026), the 7-year capital improvement plan (July 31, 2026), the KHS safety plan presentation (October 6, 2026), the 2026-27 tuition rate memo and worksheet, the three AREA agreements, SAU 29's budget hearing minutes, the superintendent's October and November 2026 reports, City of Keene assessing, finance and election pages, the city's revaluation presentation and Citizen's Budget Guide, NH Housing's 2024 rental survey, and the site's database (Question 2, tax rates, school taxes, enrollment).
