# Keene Middle School, Keene High School and the Cheshire Career Center

Part of the Keene research (town/Keene). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Keene Middle School (KMS, grades 6–8), Keene High School (KHS, grades 9–12) and the Cheshire Career Center (CCC) attached to KHS: course levels and placement, AP and honors STEM courses, Early College and Keene State courses, computer science, the CCC's programs, starting grades, credentials and work-based learning, behavior rules at KMS, homework and extra help, late buses, smaller settings inside KHS, counseling and scholarships, day-to-day safety measures, who attends, transfer credits and sports eligibility for students who move in, and what trades pay in the Keene area.

- **13 fact topics, 91 facts and 4 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 42 MB): the 2026-27 KHS and CCC Program of Studies (PDF, 93 pages), the KHS school profile, the 2026-27 KHS handbook and the 2025-26 KMS handbook (Google Docs exported as text), the 2026-27 KMS Program of Studies, the CCC pathway guide, the CCC's program, work-based learning and contact pages, the KHS counseling, registration, scholarship, ELO, athletics and anonymous-tip pages, the 2026-27 KHS-CCC scorecard and KMS strategic action plan (Google Slides, text taken from the slides' accessibility labels), the 2026-27 KMS bus routes, ten Keene School Board policies (IHCD/LEB, IK, IKB, IMBC, IHBI, JJIC, JLDBA, EBCA and others), the 2024-25 KHS athletics parent handbook, the NHIAA's 2026-27 eligibility by-law, Keene State's Accelerated High School page, federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24 extracts for KHS, KMS and 15 other New Hampshire high schools (filtered from `town/Concord/raw/high/crdc-2023-24/`), and BLS wage estimates for the Southwestern New Hampshire nonmetropolitan area (`raw/high/wages/`).
- **Places:** 87 facts use `district Keene`, 1 uses `town Keene` (wages) and 3 have no place (the NHIAA's statewide eligibility rules). The CRDC comparison table has a row for each school's district.
