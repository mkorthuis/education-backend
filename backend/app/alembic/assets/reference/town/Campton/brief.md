## What you need to know about Campton

- **Campton** is a town in Grafton County in the Pemigewasset Valley, with Waterville Estates, West Campton and Campton Hollow, on Routes 3 and 175. Site town id 75.
- **The Campton School District** (site district id 75) is in **SAU 48** and runs Campton Elementary School (CES; 22335; preschool to grade 8). Grades 9–12 are the **Pemi-Baker Regional School District** (site district id 428): Plymouth Regional High School (PRHS; 22350) with its career and technical center. SAU 48's other districts are Ellsworth, Holderness, Plymouth, Rumney, Thornton, Waterville Valley and Wentworth. Check which towns are in Pemi-Baker and how its costs are split.
