# Campton Elementary School Operations and Families

Part of the Campton research (town/Campton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Campton Elementary School (CES, preschool to grade 8) operations and young families for questions P1.1, P1.3, P4.1, P4.2, P4.3, P7.3, P8.2, P10.2, P10.3, P12.1, P12.2, P15.1, P19.2, P20.1, P20.2 and P20.3: the part-day preschool and its tuition; school hours and the lack of before-school care; the Campton Recreation after-school program (kindergarten to grade 5, until 5:00 p.m.); the five bus routes and their posted times; storm closings and delays and how families are told; the Winter Program (skiing and other activities) and other low-cost sports; community use of the building, gym and fields; the school nurse (a position contracted through Speare Memorial Hospital); enrolling a grandchild and homeless-student rights; free and reduced-price meals and lunch debt; security drills, door and camera work and the Department of Homeland Security walk-through; Internet permission and fiber service; volunteer, gift and donation rules; the Got Lunch summer program, the town's welfare application and other help; and truancy steps.

**Counts:** 16 topics, 69 facts, 2 number tables (`campton_ces_bus_route_times`, `campton_ces_winter_program_fees_2027`).

**Main sources:** the CES 2026-2027 Parent/Student Handbook (dated August 11, 2026); the Campton Elementary Preschool Parent Handbook (revised March 2024) and the August 2026 preschool welcome letters; CES's Busing, Mealtime, Athletics and home pages; the 2027 Winter Program letter and price sheet; the CES nurse's pages and the Speare Memorial Hospital job posting; CES policies JH, JFABD, JLIA, K (scanned, transcribed), KCD and IJOC (scanned, transcribed); SAU 48's homeless-student procedures and liaison sheet and its Internet permission form; Campton School Board minutes, August 2025 to October 2026 (excerpts); the Town of Campton's Recreation pages, Places of Worship page and welfare application; Campton Recreation's 2024-25 flyers; the Waterville Estates Village District, Campton Mountain and Waterville Valley Resort pages; the Got Lunch Campton-Thornton site; NH Broadband's Campton page; the site's database.

**Main gaps:** CES's Pre-K tuition for 2026-27 isn't posted separately (the handbook is dated March 2024); no bus stop lists or stop-level times are posted and Upper Mad River Road and Chickenboro Road aren't named on the route list; no page says how far ahead of a storm families are told except the 2024 preschool handbook's "usually sent by 5:15-5:30 AM"; no before-school care was found; no count of Campton children who are homeless or short of food is published (NH DOE's homeless-education page returned HTTP 403); no primary page was found for Campton's food pantry; school Internet reliability isn't reported anywhere found; the Recreation Department's program manuals and Fall 2026 booklet aren't public.
