# Campton: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Campton, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the third ToLoad batch. Campton is in SAU 48:
- **The Campton School District** runs Campton Elementary School (preschool to grade 8; 242 in kindergarten to grade 8 in October 2026, 267 with preschool).
- **The Pemi-Baker Regional School District**, a cooperative of the SAU's towns, runs Plymouth Regional High School and its career and technical center (the Plymouth Applied Technology Center).

Plymouth's research (Plymouth Elementary, PRHS and the career center, and SAU 48 and Pemi-Baker money) was already done; these files cite it and write Campton's side. Thornton's research follows in the same batch.

## Premises checked

| Question says | What the sources show |
|---|---|
| A full-day Pre-K | Part-day: sessions of 2¾ hours at $15 a day (March 2024 handbook) |
| Around 250–300 kids in K–8 | Slightly high: 242 in October 2026 (265 in 2024-25 on the site) |
| A 3:25 pickup | Dismissal is 2:35–2:40; 3:25 is the last afternoon stop on three bus routes |
| Early drop-off for a 7:30 shift | None found; families can't arrive before 7:45 |
| A full-time nurse | The handbook says yes; the nurse page says no; substitutes cover, and the post is contracted through Speare Memorial Hospital |
| Trouble keeping paraprofessionals | Partly supported: the board reopened the support staff contract for low pay; no CES opening on SAU 48's October 2 list |
| A shop class in middle school | None found: a boat-building page (newest build 2019), the Technology Student Association and a class called "Skills" |
| A "Plymouth Area Technical Center" with welding and automotive | NH DOE calls it the Plymouth Applied Technology Center, inside PRHS; automotive is offered; no welding course is named |
| A CNA program | The license is LNA |
| Unified Basketball at Plymouth Regional | Exists at PRHS and at Campton Elementary |
| Lakes Region Community College as a PRHS path | One of several college partners; its Laconia campus is about 41 road miles from Campton |
| "New state ballot questions" capping school taxes | Question 2 was on the November 3, 2026 ballot and failed locally (unofficial): Campton 56.5%, Pemi-Baker 51.6%, SAU 48 51.2%; the town's own 2.5% petition reached only the town budget |
| Seven town schools under SAU 48 | Nine districts and eight schools |
| Short-term rentals and the funding formula | The formulas count students and property value, not rentals |
| A huge bond coming | None proposed: year 6 of a 20-year bond ($72,480 a year); roof and door repairs come from the capital reserve |
| Special buses and out-of-district placements costing a fortune | Special bus and out-of-district costs are 2.5% and 1.6% of the budget |
| Combining with Thornton or Waterville Valley | No merger study found |
| A diverse student population | Both areas are about 80–91% non-Hispanic White |
| Got Lunch's "50% in Campton" lunch eligibility | The site shows 25.2% (62 of 246, derived) |
