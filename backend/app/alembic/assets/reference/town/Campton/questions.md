# Campton Residents' Questions

The questions the Campton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Campton's students attend the Campton School District's Campton Elementary School (preschool to grade 8) and then Plymouth Regional High School, run by the Pemi-Baker Regional School District, all within SAU 48. Several questions contain premises (a "Plymouth Area Technical Center", Lakes Region Community College as a PRHS path, seven town schools under SAU 48, a state ballot question capping school tax increases, combining elementary schools with Thornton or Waterville Valley, Unified Basketball at Plymouth Regional) that the research should check rather than assume.
