# Antrim Residents' Questions

The questions the Antrim research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Antrim's students attend the Contoocook Valley School District (ConVal, SAU 1), a cooperative of nine towns: Antrim Elementary School (preschool to grade 4) and Great Brook School (grades 5–8) are in Antrim, and ConVal Regional High School and its Region 14 Applied Technology Center are in Peterborough. Several questions contain premises (a nine-town cooperative since 1967, a school tax cap vote, a state special education review, ConVal's lawsuit over school funding, Bennington, Francestown and Hancock students merging at Great Brook, Student Transportation of America's buses) that the research should check rather than assume.
