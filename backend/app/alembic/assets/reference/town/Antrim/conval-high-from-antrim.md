# ConVal High and the Region 14 Center, From Antrim

Part of the Antrim research (town/Antrim). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers ConVal Regional High School (Peterborough) and its Region 14 Applied Technology Center from Antrim's side, for 15 Antrim questions: how an Antrim student applies to the automotive and welding classes and whether the credits work; the 2026-27 morning and afternoon high school bus times by Antrim route, the late bus and why the day starts early; work readiness and hosting interns; moving from home education to ConVal High; AP and Early College listings; college counseling and scholarships; SAT results and graduates' plans against Keene and Milford; the move from Great Brook and honors and support; independent study and trade classes against the schedule; and counselor staffing.

- **9 fact topics, 60 facts and 6 tables.** All facts are new. Peterborough's ConVal High file (`town/Peterborough/conval-high-and-region-14.md`), the New Ipswich Mascenic file and the library are cited, not repeated. No question was answered wholly by earlier files.
- **Places:** 58 facts use `district Contoocook Valley`, 1 uses `district Mascenic Regional` (Mascenic's own catalog) and 1 uses `town Antrim` (the Antrim Scholarship Committee). No state rules were written.
- **Raw copies** are in `raw/high/` (about 40 MB): ConVal High's 2026-27 catalog, handbook, profile, bell schedule and ten web pages; the freshman course registration sheet and the 2025 rising-ninth-grader deck; Region 14's course guide, application, About Us and Student Opportunities pages and work-based learning packet; the Region 14 agreement; Mascenic's catalog; thirteen school board and committee minutes (student representatives' names removed); the five STA late-bus sheets; a cleaned CSV of Antrim's three 2026-27 high school routes (times and street names only); news and Phaze pages; scholarship pages; and CSVs from the site's database and the federal CRDC.
- **New data concepts:** Antrim's high school bus times and streets by route; where the automotive and welding classes are held and how a student is placed; credit arithmetic for a two-block trade class; the board's busing and start-time record since 2016; ConVal, Keene and Milford grade 11 SAT results and graduates' plans side by side (derived from the site's data); counselor FTE from the CRDC; Antrim and ConVal scholarship funds.
- **Main gaps:** how ConVal students get to Mascenic and when the automotive class meets; the Region 14 application deadline for 2027-28 and the application form's contents; what a student pays for the welding option; practice and game end times; any counseling waitlist; any summer internship program; start-time survey results; how a home education portfolio is judged at ConVal High; any college acceptance rate for the three high schools.
