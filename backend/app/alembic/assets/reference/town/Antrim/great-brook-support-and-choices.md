# Great Brook School, Student Support and School Choices for Antrim Families

Part of the Antrim research (town/Antrim). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers Great Brook School (GBS, grades 5–8, in Antrim) in the Contoocook Valley School District (ConVal, SAU 1), and the support and choices around it for Antrim families: how GBS compares with South Meadow School (SMS) in Peterborough, math and music options, sports and clubs for 6th graders, phones and bullying, the move from Antrim Elementary School (AES) and which towns' students attend GBS, the late bus and the morning bus runs, free and reduced-price meals, weather delays and snow days, homeschooled and EFA students' access, counselors and trauma-informed practice, school ratings, private and charter options near Antrim, and whether families are leaving. GBS and ConVal-wide facts are placed with `district Contoocook Valley`; Antrim-only facts (the after-school club at Avenue A, EFA providers, private schools, resident student counts) are placed with `town Antrim`. District rules that Peterborough's research already wrote (the SMS handbook's bullying timelines, JJJ and IHBG-R, the district's meal rules and calendar) are cited, not repeated.

**Counts:** 17 topics, 63 facts, 4 number tables (`gbs_morning_bus_runs_2026_27`, `conval_middle_school_sections_oct_2026`, `greatschools_antrim_area_ratings_2026_11_22`, `gbs_drive_to_nearby_schools_2026_11_22`). Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 7 MB).

At Great Brook School (GBS) in Antrim, a student may move up only one grade level in math: the 2026-27 handbook names four criteria for taking 8th-grade math in 7th grade or Algebra I in 8th grade (a teacher recommendation, NWEA scores, NH SAS scores and computation fluency) without giving cutoffs, and Algebra I, Parts I and II, finished with a B or better earns two high school math credits that count in the high school GPA.
