## What you need to know about Antrim

- **Antrim** is a town in Hillsborough County on Route 202, with Gregg Lake and Clinton Village. Site town id 21.
- **The Contoocook Valley School District (ConVal)** (site district id 112) is **SAU 1**, a cooperative of nine towns (check the list and the year it formed: Antrim, Bennington, Dublin, Francestown, Greenfield, Hancock, Peterborough, Sharon and Temple). It runs Antrim Elementary (22215; preschool to grade 4) and Great Brook School (22210; grades 5–8) in Antrim, ConVal Regional High School (22290; Peterborough), South Meadow School (22295; grades 5–8, Peterborough), and elementary schools in Bennington, Dublin, Francestown, Greenfield, Hancock, Peterborough and Temple. Check how costs are split among the towns and how the district votes (deliberative session, then ballot).
- **Career programs:** the Region 14 Applied Technology Center at ConVal High is researched in `town/Peterborough/conval-high-and-region-14.md`. Cite it; write Antrim's side (how Antrim students apply and ride, internships with Antrim employers).
