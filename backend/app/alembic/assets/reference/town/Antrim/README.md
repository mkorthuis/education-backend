# Antrim: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Antrim, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the third ToLoad batch. Antrim is in the Contoocook Valley School District (ConVal, SAU 1), a cooperative of nine towns, which runs:
- **Antrim Elementary School** (preschool to grade 4) and **Great Brook School** (grades 5–8), both in Antrim;
- **ConVal Regional High School** and the **Region 14 Applied Technology Center** in Peterborough, with elementary schools in the other towns.

Peterborough's research (ConVal High, South Meadow and ConVal's money) was already done; these files cite it and write Antrim's side.

## Premises checked

| Question says | What the sources show |
|---|---|
| Over half of the $23.11 rate goes to school taxes | Right: school taxes are 61.3% of Antrim's dollars |
| $1.19 state education tax on top of $12.89 local | Not on top of ConVal's bill: the state tax is credited against Antrim's $8,241,728 share |
| A nine-town cooperative from 1967 | The 1967 vote was real (1,278–450); ConVal began operating July 1, 1968 |
| A school tax cap vote | Two cap votes; neither passed |
| ConVal's lawsuit over state school funding | Real, but the order to pay was reversed; Antrim's estimated state aid is falling |

| Dismissal at 3:25 p.m. | Correct |
| After-school care at AES | None confirmed; the 2026 care pilot is at Francestown and Greenfield |

| Student Transportation of America runs the buses | Correct; no page explains the winter combining |
