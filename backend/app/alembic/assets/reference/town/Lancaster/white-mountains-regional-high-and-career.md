# White Mountains Regional High School and Career Programs

Part of the Lancaster research (town/Lancaster). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers White Mountains Regional Middle High School in Whitefield (grades 7–12; the district's only high school, which Lancaster, Whitefield, Jefferson, Dalton and Carroll students attend) and its Arthur T. Paradice Career and Technical Education (CTE) Center: the career programs (welding and diesel, culinary and hospitality, criminal justice), credit toward White Mountains Community College (WMCC), the Lancaster buses, AP and Early College courses, counseling and college planning, dropping or switching classes, extended learning opportunities (ELOs), work permits and paid work programs, adult education near Lancaster, and public safety training. Whitefield's research will cite this file.

## youth-employment-rules: New Hampshire's Rules for Youth Training and Work in Firefighting

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: Federal and state rules on the ages, hours and kinds of work teenagers may do, including in a parent's business and in work-based learning

1. New Hampshire law bars anyone under 16 from working in firefighting except in an explorer program approved by the Department of Labor, requires youths to finish basic wildland fire training and then the Fire Standards and Training Commission's Firefighter Level 1 course before doing any task in support of firefighting, and certifies as "junior firefighters" those who pass Firefighter Level 1 with at least 70 percent in practical and academic testing.
   - citation: RSA 276-A:23, 276-A:24, 276-A:25 and 276-A:26
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XXIII/276-A/276-A-23.htm
   - as_of: 2026-11-22
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: RSA 276-A:23, I: "Except when enrolled in an explorer program approved by the New Hampshire department of labor ..., no youth under 16 years of age shall be employed or permitted to work in firefighting."; IV: "Youths shall not be employed at any task or duty in support of firefighting prior to completing training pursuant to RSA 276-A:24, I." 276-A:24: basic wild land fire training (National Fire Protection Association outline) plus communications equipment and fire extinguisher training, then "the fire standards and training firefighter level 1 course"; the training qualifies a youth for non-hazardous duties such as scouting, fire line construction, mopping up and use of hand tools, hoses and radios, but not operating motor vehicles, pumps or directing traffic. 276-A:25: "Youths who have completed this course with at least 70 percent in practical and academic testing shall be certified by the commission as junior firefighters." 276-A:26: a youth employed in support of firefighting carries an identification card signed by the fire organization's chief authority.
