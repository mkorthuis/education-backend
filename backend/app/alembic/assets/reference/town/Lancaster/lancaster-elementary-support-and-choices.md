# Lancaster Elementary: Learning, Support and Choices

Part of the Lancaster research (town/Lancaster). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Lancaster Elementary School (preschool to grade 6) in the White Mountains Regional School District (SAU 36): how its reading and math results compare with the state and with southern New Hampshire districts, what the district says it is doing about them, special education and counseling staffing, translation, bullying policy, the school's technology and coding offerings, re-enrolling from homeschooling, homeschooled and Education Freedom Account (EFA) students' access to sports and band, EFA use, a new student's welcome, after-school clubs and youth sports, and whether to raise a child in Lancaster.

- **Counts:** 16 topics, 87 facts, 5 tables (`lancaster_elementary_proficiency_by_grade_2026`, `nh_district_proficiency_and_low_income_share_2026`, `lancaster_elementary_group_proficiency_2026`, `lancaster_and_littleton_resident_students`, `lancaster_elementary_staff_directory_titles_2026`).
- **Main sources:** the 2026-27 Lancaster Elementary and White Mountains Regional Middle High School handbooks; the district's board policies JJJ (August 13, 2026), IHBG, JG, JF, JICK (August 13, 2026), JJB and JICL; the 2026-27 Special Education Policies and Procedures Manual and the ESOL (English learner) plan updated July 15, 2026; the December 18, 2025 School Board minutes with the fall 2025 performance data presentation (transcribed from images); 20 other School Board minutes from July 2025 to November 2026; the district's April 15, 2024 Strategic Action Plan and Title I plan; the school's public staff directory, Choice Block letters and sports flyers; NH DOE's 2026 test results file; the site's database (free and reduced-price meals, resident students); Colonel Town Recreation's registration pages; UNH Extension's Coös 4-H page; Granite State Home Educators' group list; the EFA marketplace list in the library; a GreatSchools profile; RSA 194-F:2.
- **Main gaps:** no published count of students per special education case manager or per counselor; no school-level interpreter list or policy on interpreting at conferences; the staff roster Google Doc linked from the handbook asked for a sign-in; no published internet speed for the school; the 2026-27 EFA tuition rate for district classes and the 2026-27 team lists for grades 5 to 8 were not found; the district's special education audit findings are not in the minutes; the district's procedure for homeschooled students' participation (the "IHBG-R" appendix) is not posted; no recent resident survey of how welcoming the school feels exists, and the data cannot show it.
- **New data concepts:** the move of grades 7 and 8 to Whitefield in 2026-27 and what it did to the school-level test data; the district's own list of improvement actions; the district's Education Service Provider tuition for EFA students; Choice Block rotations in social studies and science; Colonel Town Recreation's programs and scholarship fund; derived comparisons of district test results with the share of low-income students.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files or the site's data:** 0. P4.2 and P17.1 lean most on the site's test score, growth and meal data, which the Ask can read; the derived comparisons here add the southern-New-Hampshire view.
- **New topics proposed:** none.
