# White Mountains Regional: Student Life and Support

Part of the Lancaster research (town/Lancaster). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers student life and support at White Mountains Regional Middle High School (WMRMHS) in Whitefield, where Lancaster's students go after Lancaster Elementary. It covers a student with an IEP moving up to the high school, sports rules and costs, the bus after activities, the bell-to-bell phone rule, mental health and counseling support, the move from the elementary school, making up missed work, and substance use and discipline. Everything here is district-wide (SAU 36 is a single district), so Whitefield can cite it.

- **Counts:** 9 topics, 90 facts, 0 number tables.
- **Main sources:** the district's 2026-27 WMRMHS Student and Family Handbook; school board policies JICJ, JICG, JIC, JICD, JICDD, JH, JJB, JJIC, JLCA, JLCK, JLDBA, JLDBB, IHAMA, IK, IKB and EEA; the district's 2026-27 Special Education Policies and Procedures Manual; the school's live feed and staff directory (read through the website's public API); the 2026-27 bus route schedule and earlier route sheets; school board minutes (July 2025 to May 2026); job descriptions; the 2024-25 Program of Studies and Title I plan; the 2026-27 middle school Program of Studies.
- **Biggest finding for Lancaster families:** the district reorganized on July 1, 2026. Grades 7 and 8 left Lancaster Elementary and Whitefield Elementary (both were PK-8) and now attend the new "Middle High School" building in Whitefield. Lancaster Elementary is now PK-6. Two persona questions (P5.2, P19.2) assume the older K-8 setup.
- **Main gaps:** the amount of the athletic participation fee (the policy says there is one, with a fee waiver, but no page shows the amount); any statement that a mental health clinician is at the high school every day; whether a late bus runs after practices in 2026-27 (none is listed; a late bus is described only in the 2019-20 handbook); costs of physicals, uniforms and cleats; the 2026-27 daily schedule and the grade 9-12 Programs of Studies (Google Docs that require sign-in).
- **New topics proposed:** none. Number tables: none.
