# Lancaster: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Lancaster, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the five files below, in the reference library's format. These files are the first research on the White Mountains Regional School District (SAU 36); Whitefield's research follows and cites them.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the third ToLoad batch. Lancaster is in the White Mountains Regional School District (SAU 36), a five-town district (Carroll, Dalton, Jefferson, Lancaster and Whitefield) that runs:
- **Lancaster Elementary School** (preschool to grade 6; it was preschool to grade 8 until grades 7 and 8 moved on July 1, 2026) and **Whitefield Elementary School**;
- **White Mountains Regional Middle High School** (grades 7–12) on Regional Road in Whitefield;
- the **Paradice** career center inside the high school, with White Mountains Community College (Early College) and the Gallen center in Littleton as nearby options.

The district's cost formula is 60% students and 40% equalized value, and its board meets in Whitefield.

## Premises checked

| Question says | What the sources show |
|---|---|
| Lancaster Elementary is preschool to grade 6 with 308 students (the brief) | The site's 308 for 2024-25 includes 25 seventh and 39 eighth graders (derived); the school was PK-8 then and is PK-6 since July 1, 2026 (board vote February 4, 2026) |
| Public pre-K at Lancaster | It exists, but the 8 seats for new students in 2026-27 were at Whitefield |
| "New school cell phone rules" | Not new: policy JICJ was adopted July 17, 2025; Lancaster Elementary's PK-6 procedure began August 31, 2026 |
| A diesel or welding certification at the high school | No diesel course; only a half-credit intro with basic welding; WMCC offers the college welding course |
| Running Start at the high school | Renamed Early College in 2023; the school lists 12 entries |
| A late bus to Lancaster | None listed for 2026-27; only the 2019-20 handbook describes one |
| Bus routes changed again | Yes: Notchview replaced W.W. Berry, and the sheet differs each of the last three years |
| A 4-H or Lancaster Fair excuse | None in the attendance policy |
| A property tax cap vote | Right; it starts with the FY2028 budget |
| The central office and big budget meetings in Whitefield | Right: all 32 agendas that name a place are in Whitefield |
| A huge bond for a roof or boiler | None; the boiler was a lease-purchase |
| Low state scores in math and reading | Math 41% against 44%, reading 45% against 55%; growth above the state's |
| Families move to Littleton for better schools | Not supported: Lancaster's K–6 resident students fell 12% from 2015 to 2026, Littleton's 21% |
