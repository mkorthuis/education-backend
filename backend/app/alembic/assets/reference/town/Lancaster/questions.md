# Lancaster Residents' Questions

The questions the Lancaster research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Lancaster's students attend Lancaster Elementary School (preschool to grade 6) in the White Mountains Regional School District (SAU 36), which also runs Whitefield Elementary and the White Mountains Regional middle and high school in Whitefield, with career programs, Running Start and White Mountains Community College in Berlin nearby. This is the first research on SAU 36. Several questions contain premises (new cell phone rules, a bell-to-bell phone ban, a central office in Whitefield, a property tax cap vote, bus routes changing again, Running Start classes at the high school, Lancaster Fair week absences, a culinary arts program) that the research should check rather than assume.
