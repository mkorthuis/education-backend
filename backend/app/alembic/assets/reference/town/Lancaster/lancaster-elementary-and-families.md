# Lancaster Elementary Operations and Families

Part of the Lancaster research (town/Lancaster). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers how children get to and from Lancaster Elementary School (LES, preschool to grade 6) in the White Mountains Regional School District (SAU 36), and the family-facing rules around it: the school day and the 2026-27 calendar, buses (the new contractor, the 2026-27 Lancaster routes, ride lengths, the Middle Street stops, snow-day and delay calls), after-school care (the town's Safe Haven program), the cell phone rules, public preschool, kindergarten and new-student registration, free and reduced-price meals, bus conduct rules, the Alma parent portal, volunteering and background checks, coats and backpacks, and the Lancaster Fair.

14 topics, 74 facts and 1 number table (`lancaster_green_line_ride_minutes`). Two topics add White Mountains Regional to library topics (`district-preschool`, `kindergarten-registration`); the rest use shared topics. No proposed new topics.

**Sources.** Every sau36.org, les.sau36.org, core-docs and files-backend page answered automated requests normally; the district's site is a Nuxt app whose page text sits in the page's own data block, which was read from the downloaded HTML (no browser). The main sources are (a) the LES Student and Family Handbook 2026/2027 (updated 8/17/2026), (b) board policies JICC, JICJ, IJOC, GBCD, EBCD, JH, EEA, EFAA and JLCB, (c) the district's live feed posts (each fetched on its own dated page), (d) the 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27 bus route sheets, (e) the 2026-27 district calendar, (f) the preschool flyer of April 29, 2026 (transcribed from image), (g) Colonel Town Recreation's 2026-27 Safe Haven page and handbook, (h) the 2026 Lancaster Fair program and UNH Extension's 4-H page, and (i) school board minutes of October 8 and 22, 2026.

**Main gaps:** why the district changed bus companies and routes (no notice gives a reason); how a kindergartner is assigned to a bus and whether any 2026-27 elementary rider actually has an hour-long ride (the sheet lists stops, not riders); preschool tuition, if any (none posted); any local coat, backpack or holiday gift help for Lancaster families (none found; the town website returned HTTP 403 and two community guides need a sign-in); an official LES bell time (the handbook gives 7:40 and 7:45 as cut-offs); and the reason the Safe Haven page lists November 5–12 as closed days.
