# Paul A. Smith School and Young Families in Franklin

Part of the Franklin research (town/Franklin). Gathered November 21, 2026; updated the same day from the main session's browser pass of the SAU 18 website.

## Summary

This file covers Paul A. Smith School (Franklin's one elementary school, preschool to grade 4, 41 Daniel Webster Drive) and the years before it: the school's special education preschool, staff (nurse, speech and other therapists, reading and Title I teachers, counselor), what the 2026-27 budget kept and cut there, reading instruction, buses and storm notices, enrollment papers, English learner help, meals, the city's before- and after-school program and other recreation, Head Start and child care in the city, and volunteering and substitute background checks.

- **10 topics, 54 facts, no number tables.** All 10 topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 25 MB):
  - `city/`: Parks & Recreation and library pages, and the City's FY2027 adopted budget with page images of every page cited;
  - `district/`: the June 8, 2026 school budget presentation, the January 2022 reopening plan, policies JEB, JFAA, JLC and JLCA, and the NCES school record;
  - `browser-pass/`: SAU 18 pages saved by the main session in Chrome, plus a times-and-streets summary of the 2026-27 bus routes (the PDF was deleted);
  - `childcare/`: CAPBM Head Start and Tiny Twisters;
  - `other/`: the NRPA case study, the Franklin Outing Club's ski area and a Concord Monitor article;
  - `rules/`: RSA 189:13-a.
- **Main findings.**
  - **After-school care.** The City of Franklin's Parks & Recreation Department runs Franklin's before- and after-school care at the Bessie Rowell Community Center. Three of the four afternoon Paul Smith bus trips stop at or end at that center (2:12–2:49 p.m.), and one morning trip picks up there at 7:04. The City's FY2027 fee schedule lists $20 for before school, $30 for after school and $50 for both, without a unit; the site's state child care data lists $30 a week.
  - **Buses.** All four 2026-27 bus routes reach Paul Smith at 7:15 a.m. and leave at 2:00 p.m., and no late bus is listed. The district sends closing and delay notices through its Thrillshare/Apptegy app by text and phone call.
  - **Preschool.** Paul Smith's preschool is a special education program only, four days a week, for 3- and 4-year-olds who qualify through an evaluation. CAPBM's Head Start in Franklin is free to eligible families.
  - **Meals.** Meals are not free for everyone: lunch is $3.25 from July 1, 2026, and families must file a free and reduced-price meal application each year.
  - **2026-27 cuts.** Grade 4 moved into Paul Smith in 2025-26. The City Council approved the tax-cap school budget for 2026-27, which removes Paul Smith's 0.75 reading specialist, its art teacher and one classroom teacher. It keeps one nurse, four grant-funded Title I teachers in a schoolwide Title I school, and 1.5 speech-language pathologists with 1.5 assistants.
