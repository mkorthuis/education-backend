# Franklin Residents' Questions

The questions the Franklin research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Franklin is a city and SAU 18, a one-district SAU. The Franklin School District runs Paul A. Smith School (preschool to grade 4), Franklin Middle School (grades 5–8) and Franklin High School (grades 9–12). Franklin High students may take career programs at the Huot Technical Center in Laconia. Several questions contain premises (very low math and reading scores, students leaving for Winnisquam or private schools, a federal school safety and mental health grant pulled, yearly budget fights between the city and the school board, Franklin Continuing Education night classes, free LRCC courses) that the research should check rather than assume.
