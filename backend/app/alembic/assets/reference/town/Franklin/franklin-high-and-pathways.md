# Franklin High School, the Career Centers, College Courses and Adult Education

Part of the Franklin research (town/Franklin). Gathered November 21, 2026; updated the same day from the main session's browser pass (the 2026-27 FHS Student/Parent Handbook and two SAU 18 pages read in Chrome).

## Summary

Covers Franklin High School's (FHS) 2026-27 schedule, course load, We CONNECT advisory and Tornado Time; its Lakes Region Community College (LRCC) Early College courses, the free-class limits and the district's own $400 scholarship, and the 2026 early childhood certificates; honors, honors contracts, AP and the weighted GPA; competency-based grading, credit and recovery; graduation requirements, the Diploma with Distinction and the NH Scholars pathways; extended learning opportunities, internships and early graduation; counseling services; sports eligibility and attendance rules for athletes; what the school reported when it was named New Hampshire's 2026 High School of Excellence; Franklin students' side of the Huot Career and Technical Center (Laconia) and the Winnisquam Career Center (WiCC, Tilton); the NHIAA rule on counting off-site learning; the Franklin Falls Academy Adult Diploma Program and HiSET testing; and Tilton School's tuition and aid.

**Which career center:** both. Huot lists Franklin among its seven sending high schools and has a Franklin-only application form; WiCC lists Franklin among its eight eligible schools; FHS's handbook has students sign out "for HUOT or Winnisquam" (cte-center-programs 1, 3).

**Counts:** 9 topics, 46 facts and 2 number tables.

**Main sources:** the FHS 2026-27 Student/Parent Handbook and SAU 18's counseling and adult diploma pages (read in Chrome by the main session because sau18.nh.gov shows automated requests a bot check); LRCC's and CCSNH's May 2026 articles on the FHS certificates; LRCC's High School Programs page; FHS's 2022-23 School Profile; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24; Huot's application page, Franklin form and 2026-27 handbook; WiCC's 2025-26 Program of Studies and its web pages; the NHIAA 2026-27 eligibility by-law; NH Adult Education's program list, catalog and program pages; the National Literacy Directory's HiSET listing; hiset.org's New Hampshire page; Tilton School's tuition and aid pages; the EDies 2026 award page; and, where they are the only source, Concord Monitor and NHPR articles (said so in each note).

**Main gaps:** no FHS Program of Studies or course list was found (the counseling page links none), so the honors and AP courses actually offered are unknown; no count of FHS students at Huot or WiCC; no Franklin bus times to the centers or LRCC; the adult diploma fee for adults not enrolled in school isn't posted; the FHS athletic handbook and scholarship page weren't read. School-reported results (attendance, discipline, dropout, credits) come from the award text and news, not NH DOE data.

**New topics:** none proposed. Two facts use existing shared rule topics with no place (`school-sports-rules`, `adult-diploma-and-equivalency-rules`).
