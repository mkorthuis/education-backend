# Chesterfield Residents' Questions

The questions the Chesterfield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Chesterfield's students attend Chesterfield School (the site's Chesterfield Central School, kindergarten to grade 8), part of SAU 29 with Keene and five other towns, and then Keene High School under a long-standing area agreement (in place since 1966, amended in 2024-25). Several questions contain premises (a "Chesterfield Middle School", a 1,400-student Keene High, a school tax cap, full-time math and reading specialists, Chesterfield sharing a middle school with Westmoreland, a 2:20 dismissal, Keene Middle School tryouts for Chesterfield 7th graders) that the research should check rather than assume.

- **P1.3** How long is a little kid sitting on the bus if we live over on the far side of Spofford Lake?
