# Keene High School and the Cheshire Career Center From Chesterfield

Part of the Chesterfield research (town/Chesterfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file is Chesterfield's side of Keene High School (KHS, grades 9–12) and the Cheshire Career Center (CCC), which the Keene research already covers in depth (`town/Keene/middle-high-and-career-center.md`, cited below as "Keene file", and `choices-and-adult-learning.md`, `money-sau29-and-buildings.md`). New here: Chesterfield's own 2026-27 bus sheet for KHS (pickup times, transfers, afternoon departures), what the Keene AREA plan promises Chesterfield students (equal access, the same rules, counseling in grade 8, a yearly status report), how big KHS is by its own different figures, Chesterfield students' 2025 SAT scores at KHS (from the Chesterfield School principal's report), KHS's counseling, crisis and attendance rules from its 2026-27 handbook, Chesterfield's own bus, change-of-school and attendance policies, Chesterfield Parks & Recreation and library programs open to private-school students, the town's scholarship trusts, and a comparison of an EFA with Our Lady of Mercy Academy's tuition.

- **12 fact topics, 43 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 3 MB): Chesterfield's 2026-27 bus sheet and Keene's KHS bus sheet (cleaned of house numbers), the Keene AREA plan text, Chesterfield policies EEA, JCA and JH, Keene policy IKFB, excerpts of the Chesterfield and KHS 2026-27 handbooks, excerpts of the Chesterfield 2025 annual town report (with the SAT chart image) and of three 2026 Happenings newsletters, the KHS About, activities and Need Help pages, the KHS school profile, the Keene activity-bus page, the Chesterfield Parks & Recreation pages, the CCC pages of the 2026-27 Program of Studies, Keene Community Education's electrical page, and the site-database queries, and Saint Joseph Regional School's and Our Lady of Mercy Academy's 2026-27 tuition page.
- **Places:** 19 facts use `district Keene` (KHS, its counselors, attendance rules, programs), 17 use `district Chesterfield` (Chesterfield's buses, policies, AREA side, SAT results) and 7 use `town Chesterfield` (Parks & Recreation, library, scholarship trusts, private-school costs).
