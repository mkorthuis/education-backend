# Chesterfield: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Chesterfield, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the third ToLoad batch. Chesterfield is in SAU 29 (with Keene, Harrisville, Marlborough, Marlow, Nelson and Westmoreland):
- **Chesterfield School** (the site's Chesterfield Central School; kindergarten to grade 8 in one building; 248 students in March 2026) is run by the Chesterfield School District;
- grades 9–12 go to **Keene High School** under the Keene AREA (in place since 1966, amended for a 20-year term from July 1, 2026), with the Cheshire Career Center for career programs.

Keene's research (the four Keene schools, the Career Center, support, choices and the AREA tuition formula) was already done; these files cite it and write Chesterfield's side.

## Premises checked

| Question says | What the sources show |
|---|---|
| Public pre-K at the school | None; the nearest are in Keene and Marlborough |
| Dismissal at 2:20 | 2:25 p.m.; early release is at 11:55 a.m. |
| Full-day kindergarten and exact windows | Same K–8 schedule, 7:55 to 2:25; class sizes aren't posted |
| A "Chesterfield Middle School" | There is one K–8 school, not a separate middle school |
| A 1,400-student Keene High | 1,164 in October 2026 and projected to fall (the AREA boards' minutes); other sources say 1,400, 1,350, 1,331 or 1,280 |
| The high school bus in West Chesterfield | Chesterfield runs its own Keene High buses, first stops from 7:43; the sheet doesn't say which stops are in West Chesterfield |
| Welding or electrical at the Cheshire Career Center | No welding; the only electrical "waitlist" found is Keene Community Education's adult class |
| A late activity bus from Keene High | None found; buses leave at 3:38 p.m. |
| Full-time math and reading specialists | The handbook says so; the staff list showed the math post as "TBD" until a specialist started October 19, 2026 |
| A school tax cap vote | Already held: Chesterfield 57.3% yes (unofficial) against 60% needed; the SAU 29 cap got 47.8% (derived); no cap applies for FY2028–29 |
| "Fewer than 300 kids" and a rising budget | True for the building (248 in March 2026, 239 in November), but 25.4% of the budget is Keene High tuition for about 130 students |
| Who negotiates Keene High's tuition | Nobody each year: a formula in the 20-year agreement, set by Keene's board by November 15; Chesterfield has an advisory voice only |
| A roof or HVAC bond | None found; roof and siding work was reported complete in September 2025 |
| Sharing a middle school with Westmoreland | No study found; each town runs its own K–8 school |
| A yearly homeschool assessment | No longer required since July 1, 2026; the district's policy IHBG (May 2024) still describes it |
| SAU 29 closing together on snow days | The superintendent decides; "always" can't be shown |
| Track isn't offered at Chesterfield | Wrong: the school runs cross country, soccer, basketball, track, baseball and softball (no wrestling) |
| A buzzer at a locked front door | Locked all day with sign-in; no buzzer is mentioned |
