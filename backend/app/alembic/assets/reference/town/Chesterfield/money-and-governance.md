# Chesterfield: Money, Taxes, Tuition to Keene, the SAU and Governance

Part of the Chesterfield research (town/Chesterfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 62 facts and 5 number tables on the Chesterfield School District's 2026-27 (FY2027) budget and what moved it, Question 2 and the school tax cap, the Keene High tuition Chesterfield pays and who sets it, SAU 29's cost split and what it provides, the School Board's meetings and public comment, the town's Budget Committee, the school building and its reserves, teacher and staff turnover, homeschool and EFA students' access to school programs, and whether Chesterfield has looked at sharing a school with Westmoreland.

- **The FY2027 budget:** the School Board and Budget Committee proposed $10,856,036 (up 3.95%); voters at the March 14, 2026 district meeting added $120,000 on a 58-54 vote and passed $10,976,036 (78-30). The local property tax line rose 9.0% in the proposal because adequacy aid, fund balance, a capital reserve transfer and catastrophic aid all fell. About a quarter of the budget (25.4%, the district's own percentages) is tuition to Keene High for about 130 students, so a school with fewer than 300 students in the building (248 in March 2026, 239 in November 2026) is paying for roughly 370 to 380 students (derived).
- **School tax cap:** Question 2 got 57.3% yes in Chesterfield (unofficial), short of the 60% needed, so no school tax cap applies to Chesterfield for FY2028 and FY2029. The SAU 29 central office cap also failed (47.8%, derived).
- **Tuition to Keene:** the 2026-27 rate is $18,842 for a regular education student and $37,150 for a student with an IEP. It comes from a formula in the 20-year AREA agreement (from July 1, 2026) and is set by Keene's Board of Education each November. It is not negotiated each year. Chesterfield has an advisory voice (a representative on Keene Board committees, two joint board meetings a year), not a vote on Keene's budget.
- **SAU 29:** Chesterfield's budget line for SAU 29 is $553,694 in 2026-27 (up 10.56%, 5.1% of the budget). The Chesterfield School Board chair is also chair of the SAU 29 board. From August 18, 2026 the SAU budget goes to voters as a separate warrant article.
- **The building:** opened in 1951; roof and siding work was reported complete in September 2025; no bond or building article is in the 2026 warrant and the capital reserve was not drawn in 2025-26. Open items are a 20-year-old generator that powers only part of the building, back steps, a septic evaluation and playground planning.
- **Meetings:** the next regular School Board meeting is Monday, December 14, 2026 at 6:30 p.m. at the school; a policy sets aside the first 15 minutes for residents (extendable by vote) and recent public input items ran well over an hour.

**Main sources:** the district's January 2026 public hearing handout, March 14, 2026 district meeting handout, warrant, MS-27 budget form, district meeting minutes and election results; School Board, Finance Committee and Safety and Facilities Committee minutes (December 2025 to November 2026) and the 2026-27 meeting schedules; board policies BEDH, BEA, DBC, IHBG, IIB and JJJ; the town Budget Committee's January 2026 minutes and hearing notice; the Keene AREA agreement and the joint AREA boards' minutes; SAU 29 board minutes; the Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24; the Chesterfield Historical Society; and the site's database (tax rates, Question 2, EFA counts).
