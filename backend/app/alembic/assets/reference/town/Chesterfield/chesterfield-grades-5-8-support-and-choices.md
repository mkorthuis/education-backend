# Chesterfield School Grades 5–8: Support and Choices

Part of the Chesterfield research (town/Chesterfield). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers Chesterfield School's grades 5 to 8 (the school is one K–8 building in the Chesterfield School District, SAU 29): how its state test results compare with the other SAU 29 districts, challenge and honors, class size and homerooms, the math and reading specialists, support for children who need more help, phones, bullying reports, local trades, music, band, chorus and drama, school and Parks & Recreation sports, welcoming new families, placing a new sixth grader, Keene Middle School tryouts and how grade 8 prepares students for Keene High.

**Counts:** 13 topics, 75 facts and 3 number tables. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 13 MB): the 2026-27 Family Handbook (`school/`), 16 pages of the school's website with HTML and text, the federal COVID relief plan, the sports forms and the flyer folder listing (`site/`), nine board policies (`policies/`), nine sets of Chesterfield School Board minutes from September 2025 to November 2026 with residents' and students' names removed (`minutes/`), the town's Parks & Recreation pages and two text excerpts from its newsletters (`town/`), three outside-program flyers (`eflyers/`), copies of Keene Middle School's athletics pages, Keene's non-resident policy and a Keene High catalog excerpt (`keene/`), and CSV exports of the site's test-score, class-size and enrollment queries (`data/`).

**Main sources:** the school's 2026-27 handbook (Google Drive PDF linked from its Families page) and website (che.sau29.org); the Chesterfield School District's policy folder (JICJ, IIB, IMBD, JJIC, JG, JFAA, JICK and others); School Board minutes; the town's website (chesterfield.nh.gov); and the site's own database for test results, class size and enrollment. Where a Keene file already covers a Keene fact, this file cites it by file, topic and fact number.

**Main finding on each question:**
- **Test scores (P4.1, P16.2):** the site's data answers it. In spring 2026 Chesterfield was 53% proficient in math and 62% in reading across grades 3 to 8, between Westmoreland (63% and 71%) and Keene (41% and 58%) and above the state (44% and 55%), but the grade 5 class was at 19% in math (state 43%) while grades 7 and 8 were at 88% and 63%. Each grade has only 20 to 30 students tested, so grade figures swing.
- **Challenge and honors (P4.2):** no honors or accelerated middle school track is described. What is written: a policy letting grade 7 and 8 students earn high school credit with the high school principal's approval, a half high school credit for grade 8 STEM, "intervention and enrichment blocks" at the middle school level from 2026-27, and clubs.
- **Phones (P8.1):** policy JICJ (May 11, 2026) says phones stay off in a locker, backpack or handbag; no locked pouches are described.
- **Specialists (P16.2):** the handbook says the district employs full-time math and reading specialists; the math post was "TBD" in the handbook's staff list and a new math specialist was due to start October 19, 2026.
- **Sports (P17.1, P20.2):** the school runs cross country, soccer, basketball, track, baseball and softball but not wrestling; the town's Parks & Recreation pages list basketball and soccer with no 2026-27 sign-up dates; Keene Middle School says a student who isn't one of its own must call its athletic office, and no document says Chesterfield students may try out.

**Main gaps:** no posted daily schedule, honors or Algebra course list, or math and reading placement process for a new transfer student; no 2026-27 school or town sports sign-up dates or fees; the school's monthly newsletters (a Google Drive folder that returned no listing) and Parks & Recreation's social media were not read; NH DOE's home education enrollment report (for P12.2) was not retrieved; no source says how specialists are paid or evaluates whether they helped students catch up.

**Proposed new shared topics:** none. Existing slugs fit.

**Spot checks:** four facts were re-checked against the raw copies and matched: the JICJ sentence on keeping devices "with the power turned off in a student's assigned locker, backpack, or handbag" (raw/middle/policies/JICJ.txt); "Our new math specialist is scheduled to start on 10/19" (October 12, 2026 minutes); the handbook's "full-time certified Reading Specialist" and "TBD" lines (pp. 5 and 16); and all three number tables against the CSV exports of the database queries.
