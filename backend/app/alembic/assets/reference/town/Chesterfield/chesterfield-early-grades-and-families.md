# Chesterfield School's Early Grades and Families

Part of the Chesterfield research (town/Chesterfield). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what a Chesterfield family with a young child needs day to day: whether the district runs a public pre-K, the school day and drop-off windows, kindergarten hours and class size, child care after school, the bus (including ride times from the lake roads and the runs that end at a child care center and the YMCA), delay and snow-day calls, the lunch account and free and reduced-price meals, speech, aides and special education contacts, art, music and Spanish, recess and outdoor learning, door security, and how the school reaches families.

- **14 topics, 54 facts and 3 number tables.** Places: `district Chesterfield` for Chesterfield School and its board; `sau 29` for SAU 29's hours, closing notices, food service, Child Find and registration; `town Chesterfield` for Parks and Recreation and drive times; `town Keene` for the Keene Family YMCA; no place for the state's special education help lines.
- **New data concepts:** the 2026-27 bus run times (the route sheet gives times, not ride lengths, so ride minutes are derived); the arrival and car-line windows; early release dates and the 11:55 a.m. release; the meal-charging rules in the board's own policy EFAA; open staff postings at Chesterfield on November 22, 2026; drive times from the school to the nearest preschools and child care; the Chesterfield kindergarten supply list's winter gear; the school's 23-acre outdoor classroom; board minutes on aide vacancies.
- **Main sources:** the Chesterfield School Family Handbook 2026-27 (fresh copy of the PDF linked from che.sau29.org), SAU 29's 2026-27 school hours, early release times and registration notice, the Chesterfield 2026-27 calendar, the district's Bus Routes document for 2026-27, the school's supply list, Chesterfield's board policy EFAA on meal charging, SAU 29's food services pages, SAU 29's Child Find notice, the NH special education Procedural Safeguards Handbook, the SAU 29 job board, Chesterfield School Board minutes (April to November 2026), MyKeeneNow's closing lists, the Keene Family YMCA's page and flyer, Chesterfield's Parks and Recreation pages, and OpenStreetMap routing.
- **Derived figures:** ride and wait minutes from the bus sheet; the average kindergarten class if the 2024-25 count were split between the two kindergarten teachers listed for 2026-27; the length of the school day (6 hours 30 minutes); the drive times and miles.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "A public pre-K at the school" (P1.1): no. Chesterfield School runs kindergarten to grade 8, its handbook lists no preschool class, and the state's October 1, 2026 preschool count lists no Chesterfield school. The nearest public preschools are Jonathan Daniels in Keene (about 22 minutes by car) and Marlborough School (about 28 minutes).
  - "School letting out at 2:20" (P1.2): the day ends at 2:25 p.m. in 2026-27 (SAU 29's hours list and the handbook). Early release days end at 11:55 a.m.
  - "Full-day kindergarten every day" (P7.1): the schedule lists one set of hours, 7:55 a.m. to 2:25 p.m., for kindergarten through grade 8, and no shorter kindergarten day is posted. Kindergarten starts on August 31, five days after the other grades (August 26). "Drop-off and pickup windows" exist: the handbook's no-vehicle windows are 7:40 to 8:15 a.m. and 1:50 to 2:45 p.m.
  - "A kindergarten class of 12 or 25" (P12.1): neither is posted. State rules cap kindergarten to grade 2 classes at 25 students per educator (schools must try for 20); the site's data show 30 kindergartners on October 1, 2024, and the handbook lists two kindergarten teachers (about 15 each if split evenly, derived).
  - "Aides and speech therapists" (P5.1): the handbook lists one speech therapist, two special education teachers and 13 paraprofessional entries (12 different names). The board was told in May 2026 that para vacancies were hard to fill; on November 22, 2026 the district had open postings for a behavior analyst and an 80% occupational therapist at Chesterfield, and none for a paraprofessional or speech-language pathologist.
  - "Regular art, music and Spanish" (P14.1): yes, all three are listed for kindergarten to grade 8 as part of six specials classes. The handbook says Spanish is "currently funded at 50%" and meets one day a week in the elementary grades; the K-3 music teacher left in May 2026.
  - "The front door locked all day with a buzzer" (P17.3): the handbook says all doors are locked on school days and visitors sign in at the main entrance, where the receptionist calls their destination. It does not mention a buzzer or intercom.
  - "SAU 29 closing together or Chesterfield deciding" (P7.2): the handbook says the Superintendent of Schools decides after hearing from town road agents and the National Weather Service. In two published cases (December 23, 2025 early closing and the January 27, 2026 delay) SAU 29 listed Chesterfield with the other member towns, but nothing says it always does.

- **Main gaps:** Chesterfield's two-hour-delay start time is not posted; no kindergarten class sizes or class lists are posted; no recess minutes by grade or cold-weather rule; Village Children's Center's school-age hours and tuition are not posted; Jonathan Daniels' pages don't say whether children from other towns may enroll; the handbook's meal-charge wording ("one lunch") differs from the board's policy (meals up to a negative balance of $25); the "Parents Night Out" page on the town site is gone (HTTP 404); the FY2025 school district report PDF at UNH Scholars refused automated requests (HTTP 403).
