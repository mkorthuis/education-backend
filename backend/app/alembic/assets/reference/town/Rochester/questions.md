# Rochester Residents' Questions

The questions the Rochester research is meant to answer. Thirty resident profiles, three questions each (90 in all, half again the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. The profiles came with first names only. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Rochester is a city and SAU 54. The Rochester School District runs five elementary schools (Chamberlain Street, East Rochester, Gonic, McClelland and the new Salmon Falls School, kindergarten to grade 5), REACH Preschool at East Rochester, Rochester Middle School (grades 6–8), Spaulding High School with the Richard W. Creteau Technology Center, and Bud Carlson Academy. Wakefield sends its high school students to Spaulding under an AREA agreement. The City Council approves the school budget under the city charter's tax cap. Several questions contain premises (budget totals, a tax cap exception, building costs, programs and schools) that the research should check rather than assume.
