## What you need to know about Rochester

- **Rochester** is a city in Strafford County, next to Dover, Somersworth, Barrington, Farmington, Milton and Lebanon, Maine. Site town id 461. The City Council approves the school budget under the city charter's **tax cap**; the council held its FY2027 school budget hearing on May 19, 2026.
- **Rochester School District** (site district id 461) is **SAU 54**. Use `district Rochester` for SAU-level facts too.
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
