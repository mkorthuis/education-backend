# Spaulding High School, the Creteau Technology Center and Bud Carlson Academy

Part of the Rochester research (town/Rochester). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Spaulding High School, the Richard W. Creteau Regional Technology Center (Rochester's career and technical center, beside Spaulding) and Bud Carlson Academy, Rochester's alternative high school: which trade programs Creteau runs, when students can start them, its automotive, machining and networking programs, equipment, partners, credentials and college credit, the programs Rochester students can take at Dover and Somersworth instead (welding, culinary arts, electrical, building trades), AP and honors science, Early College and dual enrollment fees, graduation requirements and the move of graduation to UNH's Whittemore Center, Air Force JROTC, extended learning opportunities and paid work-based learning, counseling, college planning, financial aid and scholarships, the cell phone policy, the school resource officer agreement and cameras, substance-misuse programs, Bud Carlson Academy's pathways and flexible hours, New Hampshire's work-hour limits for 16- and 17-year-olds, and the federal rule on military recruiters' access to students.

- **9 fact topics, 92 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 21 MB): Spaulding's 2026-27 Program of Studies (a public Google Doc linked from the school board's June and July 2026 packets, exported as text and HTML), 14 school board agenda packets and minutes from September 2025 to November 2026 with the policy back-ups they link (JICJ cell phones, the school resource officer agreement for KLGA-R, the 3rd Millennium and HAVEN memo, policies IK, IKF, IHBH and IHBI), 14 posts from the City of Rochester's news site (news.rochesternh.gov), pages from the SHS Counseling site, the older Creteau ("CTE") Google site and the automotive program's site, the Spaulding ELO site, UNH's first-year admission page, 20 U.S.C. 7908, RSA 276-A:4, and Spaulding's and Bud Carlson's rows from the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24 (filtered from Concord's statewide extracts).
- **Places:** 85 facts use `district Rochester`; 7 have no place (RSA 276-A:4, 20 U.S.C. 7908 and UNH's admission expectations).

The facts here come instead from documents the district posts elsewhere (the catalog, board packets) and from the city's news site. See *Not retrieved*.
- **New data concepts:** Creteau program-by-program starting grades, college credit and certifications (table rochester_creteau_programs_2026_27); exploratory courses open to 9th graders; the Tri-City programs Spaulding students may apply to; Early College, SNHU and articulation fees; Spaulding's 26-credit diploma against the state's 20; the graduation venue change and its stated cost; Air Force JROTC's courses and flight training; extended learning opportunity (ELO) rules; Bud Carlson Academy's pathways and flexible attendance; JUUL-settlement-funded substance-misuse courses; the school resource officer agreement; New Hampshire's 35-hour school-week limit for working 16- and 17-year-olds; military recruiters' access and the parent opt-out.
