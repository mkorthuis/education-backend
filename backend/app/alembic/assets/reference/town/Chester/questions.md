# Chester Residents' Questions

The questions the Chester research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Chester is SAU 82. The Chester School District runs Chester Academy (kindergarten to grade 8), and its high school students mainly attend Pinkerton Academy in Derry under a tuition contract, where they may take career programs at Pinkerton's own center. Several questions contain premises (lower teacher salaries than nearby towns, almost no commercial tax base, high administration costs for one school, school taxes rising while enrollment falls) that the research should check rather than assume.

- **P1.3** How do snow days and two-hour delays work when both parents work out of town?
