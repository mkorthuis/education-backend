# Chester Academy's Upper Grades: Support and Choices

Part of the Chester research (town/Chester). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Chester Academy (preschool to grade 8, the Chester School District's only school, SAU 82) for students who need extra support or want more, and for families weighing home education, an EFA, a charter or a private school: special education procedures and staff, paraprofessional openings, home-educated and EFA students' access, coming back to public school, clubs and sports, bullying and phone rules, the middle school math pathways and Algebra I, enrichment and advanced learners, Spanish, volunteering, private and charter high school options and their distances, charter busing, and what changes between grade 8 and Pinkerton. Sources are the 2026-27 Chester Academy Student/Parent Handbook, the district's Local Education Agency Plan and Special Education Policies and Procedures Manual (updated August 2026), Chester Academy's Middle School Math Pathways sheet, the school's department and activity pages, SAU 82's Home Education page, SAU 82's job board (AppliTrack), the school board's 2026 agendas as posted on the town's Agenda Center, the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) 2023-24, the federal Private School Universe Survey, the live EFA marketplace feed, the Town of Chester's recreation and library pages, the admissions pages of The Derryfield School and Trinity High School, and drive routes computed with OpenStreetMap tools.

- **13 topics, 90 facts, 2 number tables** (chester_academy_crdc_2023_24, chester_high_school_options_drive_distances). Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 10 MB).
- **New data concepts:** the district's own special education timelines (15 business days to meet on a referral, 60 days to evaluate, 30 days to write an IEP, 21 days to answer a meeting request); three routes to 8th-grade Algebra I (at Chester Academy, online through VLACS, or before school at Pinkerton) and what each means for Pinkerton placement and credit; a summer route from grade 7 math into Algebra I; EFA marketplace providers with Chester addresses; a private high school inside Chester (Busche Academy); drive times from Chester Academy to 13 high school options; private schools' entrance requirements that draw on grade 8 math and English.
- **Main gaps:** SAU 82 loads its policy lists, board minutes, monthly updates and budget documents through a Google Apps Script that needs a browser, so the text of policies JJJ (access for home-educated and EFA students), JICK, JICJ, IHBG and the transportation policies, the June 2026 "Enrichment and Acceleration Programming Update" and the board's staffing reports were not read. No fall or spring sports list or Athletics Handbook was found. NH DOE refused automated requests (special education complaint and determination data, gifted reports). The grades and hours of Spanish weren't found. Web search became unavailable partway through (the session's shared search budget ran out), so no further searching was done.
- **No new topics proposed.**
