# Pinkerton Academy From Chester: Buses, the Move to a Big School, IEPs, Fees, Math Pathways, Teams and Chester's Say

Part of the Chester research (town/Chester). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Chester's side of high school at Pinkerton Academy in Derry: how many Chester students attend (by grade, 2018-19 to 2026-27), Chester's Pinkerton buses (who runs them, the drive, storms and getting home late, the bus consortium talks and what the HB 1300 tax cap could touch), the move from Chester Academy to a school about 17 times its 8th grade, Chester Academy's middle school math pathways into Pinkerton's honors math, the board's Pinkerton student representative, Chester students with IEPs at Pinkerton and what Chester pays for Pinkerton's special education programs, CTE access and what Chester's middle school offers on careers, what families pay at Pinkerton (class dues, sports, band, AP exams, CTE lab fees), team levels and cuts, who to call at Pinkerton, and Chester's say through trustees, sending-district meetings and the 2025 contract talks. Pinkerton's own programs (Freshman Academy, counselors, CTE programs, AP results, dual enrollment, special education procedures) are in the shared Pinkerton files (`town/pinkerton/`) and the Hooksett, Auburn and Candia files, which this file cites rather than repeats.

- **9 fact topics, 40 facts and 2 number tables.** Places: `district Chester` 31, `district Pinkerton Academy` 9. Tables: `chester_students_at_pinkerton_by_grade`, `chester_pinkerton_bus_times_2026_27`.
- **Update, November 22, 2026:** Chester's 2026-27 Pinkerton route sheets, opened in Chrome by the main session (raw/high/browser/), are now folded in: district-transportation 4–7 and the bus times table replace the earlier statement that the times couldn't be read.
- **Main sources:** SAU 82's district, board and Pinkerton bus pages and headlines; the Chester School Board's minutes (January 2025 to February 2026) and agendas (through November 18, 2026) from the Town of Chester's Agenda Center; the Chester School District 2025 Annual Report (Pinkerton enrollment by grade, the Pinkerton class of 2025, the Special Services Director's report, the FY27 budget detail and Pinkerton's audited 2024-25 cost letter and itemized accounting, the last three transcribed from page images); Chester Academy's Middle School Math Pathways, Integrated Arts, School Counseling and Grade 8 pages and its 2026-27 handbook (agent 1's copy); the October 21, 2026 HB 1300 presentation; Pinkerton's Class of 2030 Freshman Parent Night slides, its CTE pages, and the Pinkerton research's saved catalog, handbook and athletic handbook; Pinkerton's 2026-27 afternoon bus lineup (Derry research); Chester's 2026-27 Pinkerton route sheets (updated 9/4/26; browser copies); OpenStreetMap and OSRM for one school-to-school drive.
- **New data concepts:** Chester's Pinkerton bus routes with first and last stops and scheduled ride lengths compared with Auburn's, Candia's and Hooksett's; Chester's Pinkerton enrollment by grade over nine years and the Pinkerton class of 2025 count; Chester Academy's three routes to Algebra I in 8th grade and the Pinkerton freshman math each leads to; Pinkerton's 2024-25 audited per-student costs by program, including athletics within regular tuition; Pinkerton's 2026-27 team levels by sport; Pinkerton's 2026-27 freshman support team and contact list; the 2025 Chester–Pinkerton contract talks.
