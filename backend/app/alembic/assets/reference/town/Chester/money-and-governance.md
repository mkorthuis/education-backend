# Chester's School Money and Governance: Taxes, Pinkerton Tuition, the District Meeting, Pay, the Building and the Board

Part of the Chester research (town/Chester). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how the Chester School District (SAU 82) is paid for and governed: how the school tax rate is set and why the school tax has grown while the number of resident students has fallen, the 2026 tax rate, the town's almost all-residential tax base, the new school tax cap, how much of the budget goes to Pinkerton Academy against Chester Academy, the teachers' and support staff contracts, the traditional school district meeting (Chester has no deliberative session) and its recent votes, the budget calendar for 2027-28, what the one-district central office costs and how its administration spending compares with neighbors, the state of the Chester Academy building and its seven-year capital plan, staff turnover, why Chester Academy's and Pinkerton's calendars differ, community use of the school, how open the board is about curriculum and test results, how young residents can be heard, and the town's tax relief for seniors.
