# Chester Academy: Early Grades and Families

Part of the Chester research (town/Chester). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Chester Academy (preschool to grade 8, the Chester School District's only school, SAU 82) from a young family's side: before- and after-school care, the preschool, storm days, the 2026-27 calendar, test results and class sizes, registration, reading help, bus stop rules, meals, special education and speech services for young children, kindergarten, welcoming new families, arrival and pickup, recess, and child care and summer programs in town.
