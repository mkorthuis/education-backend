# Winnisquam Regional High School and the Huot Center, From Sanbornton

Part of the Sanbornton research (town/Sanbornton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Winnisquam Regional High School (WRHS, grades 9–12, 435 West Main Street, Tilton), the Winnisquam Career Center (WiCC) next door and the Huot Center in Laconia, as seen from Sanbornton, for the questions assigned to agent 3: shop and agriculture classes (P2.2); how Sant Bani School graduates fit in (P6.1); AP and dual enrollment (P6.2); Tilton School and other private high schools (P6.3); school safety and the resource officer (P8.2); a late bus (P8.3); internships (P10.1); where graduates go (P10.2); the Huot Center and class time (P10.3); a comparison with Gilford and Laconia (P13.1); counseling and mental health (P14.1); making up a failed math course (P14.2); the phone rule (P14.3); help with community college, NHTI and trades (P18.1); scholarships (P18.2); and FAFSA and resumes (P18.3).

The Tilton research (`town/Tilton/high-school-and-wicc.md`) and the Northfield research (`town/Northfield/high-school-and-career-center.md`) already cover WRHS and WiCC in depth: programs, credits, the Huot bus and schedule, AP, honors and Early College, extended learning opportunities (ELOs), credit recovery, counseling and FAFSA events, the phone policy and the shared resource officer. Those facts are cited, not repeated. This file adds Sanbornton's side and what those files lacked:
- how far Sanbornton is from WRHS, the Huot Center, the community colleges, Gilford High and eight private or charter high school options (two **derived** tables);
- what WRHS's own course list does and doesn't hold for shop and agriculture, the WiCC's shop equipment and FFA, and a **derived** count of how much of the school day a morning Huot class takes;
- how ninth graders from outside WRHS's middle school are welcomed and given credit, how WRHS promotes students by credits, and its reassessment and extra-help rules (for the math make-up question);
- building security, drills and the resource officer's role; the athletic handbook's ride rules and the district's afternoon bus sheet, which lists no late bus and shows five afternoon routes ending at Sanbornton Central School;
- the district's youth suicide prevention plan, the mental health center the plan names for Sanbornton, the counseling office's fall 2026 sessions, and the guide's admission table for two-year colleges;
- scholarships: the WRHS scholarship matrix (updated November 10, 2026), the Lakes Region Scholarship Foundation's eligibility and its funds that name WRHS, and that no Sanbornton-only fund was found;
- Sant Bani's own statement on where its graduates go, the district board chair's 2025 comments on students leaving the district, and the site's data on graduates' plans and a **derived** comparison of WRHS with Gilford High and Laconia High.

**Counts:** 10 topics, 30 facts and 7 number tables.

**Main sources:** the WRHS 2026-27 Comprehensive Guide, the WRHS 2025-26 School Profile and the counseling office's September–November 2026 newsletters (copied from the Tilton research's November 21 downloads); the WRSD Youth Suicide Prevention & Response Plan and the district's wellness pages; the WRSD scholarship matrix and the Lakes Region Scholarship Foundation's pages and donor fund list; the October 19, 2026 board packet and the 2025 annual meeting minutes (in the 2026 Annual Report); the 2026-27 afternoon bus sheet (from the Northfield research's OCR copy); Sant Bani's website; WiCC's program pages; the site's database; and OpenStreetMap Nominatim and OSRM routes.

**Main gaps:** no count of Sanbornton students at Tilton School or other private high schools; no list of the colleges or trade schools WRHS graduates attend; no WRHS FAFSA completion rate (the federal data page timed out); no source that mentions resume help at WRHS; no named internship hosts; no Sanbornton-only scholarship (the town's annual report PDFs returned HTTP 404); no source on how WRHS places a ninth grader who comes from a private K–8 school; and no measure of whether students pay attention in class since the phone ban.

**Topic note:** no new topics proposed. Local scholarships go under `town-family-help`, as in the Antrim, Belmont and Boscawen files.

**Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** 3 of 16 (P6.2, P10.1 and P14.3). For each, this file adds only a pointer.

### Premises checked

| Question | Premise | What the sources show |
|---|---|---|
| P2.2 | Kids may "have to go over to the Huot Center in Laconia for trades" | **Partly.** WRHS's own course list has no welding, machining, woodworking, construction, automotive or agriculture course, but the WiCC, next door in Tilton, teaches welding, machining, CNC and CAD (Design, Create and Fabricate) and agriculture business, animal care and outdoor resources, with FFA. | P6.1 | Sant Bani graduates "switch over" to WRHS | **Some do.** Sant Bani says its graduates go to "hometown public schools, local private secondary schools, and private boarding schools" and names none (town-school-options-nearby 1). The site counts 10 Sant Bani eighth graders in 2025-26. |
| P6.2 | AP and dual-enrollment classes "without having to leave campus" | **Yes, but few AP.** WRHS lists two AP courses (both English, one per year), honors courses in five subjects and six Early College courses taught on campus (Tilton file school-programs-after-grade-6 4–7). |
| P6.3 | Many Sanbornton families pay for Tilton School | **Not countable.** No count of Sanbornton students at Tilton School was found. The school board chair said in 2025 that about 99 students change in and out of the district in a year and that 81 EFAs were being drawn in the three towns (town-school-options-nearby 2). Tilton School's day tuition is $47,000 (the site's tuition data; Franklin file town-school-options-nearby 1). |
| P8.2 | The high school may have a "full-time resource officer" | **Yes, shared.** One full-time Tilton Police Department officer serves WRHS and the middle school (Tilton file district-safety-and-security 1; district-safety-and-security 3). |
| P8.3 | A late bus back to Sanbornton | **None found.** The WRHS guide has no late or activity bus, and the district's 2026-27 afternoon route sheet lists ten routes, all leaving at 2:31–2:39 p.m., none labeled late (district-transportation 1–2). Belmont High and Gilford High each run a late bus, but only for students from a tuition or AREA town (Belmont and Gilford files). |
| P10.3 | A Huot student may "miss half their regular classes just riding the bus" | **The ride is small, the class takes a large part of the morning.** The driving is about 47 minutes round trip, about 11% of the school day, but a morning Huot class has the student away about 2 hours 39 minutes, 38% of the day (**derived**, cte-center-programs 3). |
| P14.2 | A failed math class could mean "repeating the whole grade" | **No.** WRHS moves students up by credits (6 for sophomore, 12 for junior, 18 for senior), reassesses, offers Bear Block help, summer recovery "when available" and online credit recovery (school-programs-after-grade-6 3–4; Tilton file school-programs-after-grade-6 13–14). |
| P14.3 | "Are kids actually paying attention in class?" | **Not measured.** The phone rule is bell to bell (Tilton file district-technology-and-phones 1). No WRHS source reports attention or learning since the ban. |
| P18.2 | Local scholarships "just for Sanbornton or Winnisquam kids" | **Winnisquam yes, Sanbornton-only no.** The WRHS matrix lists WRHS Local Scholarships, a Tilton-Northfield Rotary award and others, and ten Lakes Region Scholarship Foundation funds name WRHS. No fund restricted to Sanbornton was found (town-family-help 1–4). |
| P18.3 | The school helps seniors with FAFSA and resumes "during the school day" | **Partly.** FAFSA and aid help comes from counselors, a Granite Edvance webinar (an evening, 5:30–6:30 p.m.) and ApplyNH Day; no WRHS source mentions resume writing (district-student-support 5; Tilton file district-student-support 2–3). |
