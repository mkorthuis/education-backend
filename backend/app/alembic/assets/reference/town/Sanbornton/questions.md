# Sanbornton Residents' Questions

The questions the Sanbornton research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Sanbornton's students attend the Winnisquam Regional School District (SAU 59), shared with Tilton and Northfield: Sanbornton Central School (kindergarten to grade 4), the Winnisquam Early Learning Center in Tilton (preschool, special education only), Winnisquam Regional Middle School (grades 5–8) and Winnisquam Regional High School in Tilton, with the Huot Career and Technical Center in Laconia. Sant Bani School (a private K–8 school in Sanbornton) and Tilton School (a private high school) are local choices. Several questions contain premises (a full-day Pre-K at Winnisquam, a bus to the Early Learning Center, math and reading scores under 50%, SAU 59's share of the tax bill, a merger with Franklin or Gilford, a full-time resource officer, state reading screener rules) that the research should check rather than assume.
