## What you need to know about Sanbornton

- **Sanbornton** is a town in Belknap County on Route 3 and Lake Winnisquam, with Hunkins Pond, Hermit Lake, Cawley Pond, Steele Hill, Bay Road and Sanbornton Square. Site town id 477.
- **The Winnisquam Regional School District** (site district id 582) is **SAU 59**, a cooperative of Sanbornton, Tilton and Northfield. It runs Sanbornton Central School (22925; kindergarten to grade 4; 177 students in 2024-25), Southwick School in Northfield (K–4), the Winnisquam Early Learning Center in Tilton (29247; preschool), Winnisquam Regional Middle School (22955; grades 5–8; 361) and Winnisquam Regional High School (22950; grades 9–12; 356), all in Tilton.
