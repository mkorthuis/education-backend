# Winnisquam Middle, Support and Choices: Sanbornton Families

Part of the Sanbornton research (town/Sanbornton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Sanbornton families ask about the step from Sanbornton Central School to Winnisquam Regional Middle School (WRMS, grades 5–8, 76 Winter Street, Tilton) and about support and choices: how the move compares with Sanbornton Central, test scores, advanced math and early high school credit, behavior support, free lunch and organization help, homeschooled and EFA students at band and sports, returning to public school, local groups for homeschoolers, Sant Bani School's tuition aid and calendar next to the district's, robotics, coding and STEM, VLACS and other online courses, Sanbornton Central's art, music and outdoor time, and summer reading with the town library.

- **14 topics, 63 facts and 4 tables.** All 14 topics are shared slugs (school-programs-after-grade-6, district-school-results-compared, district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners, district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics, district-conduct-policies, district-student-support, district-meals, district-homeschool-access, district-school-day-and-calendar, district-transportation, district-curriculum-and-assessment, town-school-options-nearby, town-recreation-and-summer, town-library-services); facts about Sant Bani School use `place: district Sant Bani School`. The tables are winnisquam_math_proficiency_2026, winnisquam_reading_proficiency_2026, santbani_winnisquam_school_days_2026_27 and winnisquam_ms_hs_bus_sanbornton_routes_2026_27.

- **Main sources:** the WRMS 2026-27 Student and Parent Handbook (which holds the school's 2025-2026 Title I schoolwide plan) and the principal's August 21, 2026 letter; the 2026-27 Sanbornton Central and Southwick handbook; the district's 2026-27 calendar, policy manual (IC, IHBG, IHBH, IHCA, IHCD, IMAH, IMBA, IMBD, IMBD-R, EFC, JJJ), November 1, 2026 enrollment report, 2026-27 budget handout, 2026 annual report and 2026-27 middle and high school bus route sheets; Sant Bani School's tuition, FAQ, handbook and 2026-27 important dates; Sanbornton Public Library and town Recreation pages; Prescott Farm's homeschool series; and the site's own database (state test results, enrollment, free and reduced-price meals, EFA payments, Sant Bani's tax data).
- **Citation convention:** "Tilton district-…" and "Northfield district-…" cite those towns' `middle-school-support-and-choices.md` unless another file is named; "library" means `additional-data/`.
- **Main findings:**
  - **The move:** Sanbornton Central had 155 students on November 1, 2026 (two classes a grade) and WRMS 355. About half of Sanbornton Central's children live outside Sanbornton (Tilton east of I-93; derived), so the new mix at WRMS is mostly Northfield and the rest of Tilton. WRMS's day starts about 70 minutes earlier than Sanbornton Central's (homeroom 7:34 against 8:45; derived), and the earliest Sanbornton route to WRMS picks up at 6:05 a.m. (a 73-minute ride; derived).
  - **Scores:** the "under 50%" claim fits WRMS (2026: math 49%, reading 39%) and the district's reading (45%), but not Sanbornton Central (math 67%, reading 53%); the state's all-grade math was 44%. The state results are by school, grade and student group, not by teacher.
  - **Advanced work:** honors math in grades 6 and 7, high school Algebra 1 for high school credit in grade 8 (12 of 98 eighth graders took Algebra I in 2023-24, Northfield file; derived), and board policy IMBD-R sets how that credit is recorded. WRMS's science sequence has no biology; credit can be earned by competencies, online courses or ELOs with approvals.
  - **Behavior and organization:** WRMS uses a three-tier behavior framework (MTSS-B), a Student Support Center, a Student Support Coordinator and a Target Team; the 2026-27 focus is student agendas, and the guidance office, Homework Club and after-school teacher help are the organization supports.
  - **Free lunch:** board policy EFC says only the point-of-sale system can identify children on free or reduced-price lunch and the information is confidential; the handbook says students who owe money are told at the register, and a balance of $15 or more brings a sun butter and jelly sandwich.
  - **Homeschoolers and EFA students:** policy JJJ (approved November 16, 2026) lets resident homeschooled and EFA students request WRMS band, clubs and sports on the same terms as enrolled students; the district's band and school sports start in grade 5 at WRMS. A returning student is placed after the principal's evaluation (policy IHBG, which predates the July 2026 law).
  - **Sant Bani and the district:** Sant Bani's 2026-27 tuition is $12,800 (kindergarten to grade 8) with need-based aid applied for by February 15; no total aid figure is published. Counting weekdays, Winnisquam is open on 15 days when Sant Bani is closed and Sant Bani on 4 days when Winnisquam is closed (derived), and each school decides its own snow days.
  - **Summer reading:** the district budgets an "SCS summer reading program" (2026-27: $2,500 pay, $1,200 books); the library runs its own program and is about 0.1 mile from the school (derived). No source describes a joint plan.
- **Main gaps:** no Sant Bani aid totals or share of families, and nothing on how its aid combines with an EFA; no WRMS club schedule for 2026-27 or confirmation that a robotics club meets this year; no co-op for homeschoolers found in Sanbornton, Tilton or Laconia (the nearest nature series is Prescott Farm in Laconia); no art or music minutes at Sanbornton Central; no joint school and library summer reading plan; no teacher-level results (not published).
