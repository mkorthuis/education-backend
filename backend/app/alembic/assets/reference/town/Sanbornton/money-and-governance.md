# Sanbornton: School Taxes, Budget, Buildings, Pay and Governance in the Winnisquam Regional School District

Part of the Sanbornton research (town/Sanbornton). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 12 topics, 43 facts and 5 number tables on Sanbornton's side of the Winnisquam Regional School District (SAU 59), which it shares with Tilton and Northfield. Tilton's `money-and-governance.md` (town/Tilton) and Northfield's (town/Northfield) already hold the district-wide money facts: the 2026-27 budget and its drivers, the cost formula and each town's 2025-26 shares, the March 20, 2027 district meeting, the $7 million roof bond plan, the career center and the tax cap. This file cites them and adds only what Sanbornton's questions need.
- **Sanbornton's tax bill:** the 2025 rates and what share is school tax (52.8% of the tax raised, **derived**); the rate history since 2023 (its total rate $12.04, $12.81, $13.21); why its local school tax rose 12.6% in 2025 (the district's bigger bill, less state aid, a slightly smaller share; **derived**); why its state aid fell (fewer resident students and a hold-harmless step-down; **derived**); its share of costs (26.49% of costs with 23.4% of students and 34.4% of value); what the 2024 formula change saves it; the district's 2026-27 estimate for Sanbornton ($6.16 at most, before any surplus) and its estimate-versus-actual record; the tax base (95.3% residential), the elderly exemption and veterans' credits.
- **The rate's steadiness (P13.3):** against eight nearby towns, Sanbornton's yearly swings in the equalized school rate rank seventh steadiest of nine; its 2025 rise (+11.7%) was smaller than Tilton's and Northfield's but larger than Gilford's, Franklin's and Laconia's; what one warrant article adds (about $0.26 per $1,000 for each $1,000,000, **derived**); the 2026 rate was still not set on November 18, 2026.
- **Budget and enrollment (P4.2):** district enrollment down 16.2% since 2020-21, teachers down 16.0%, cost per pupil up 30.6% (FY2021 to FY2025); Sanbornton Central's own cost center per student; Sanbornton's resident students down 19.2% since 2014-15 while its local school tax rose 36.7% (table).
- **Pay and staff (P7.1):** the March 2025 meeting's account of why teachers leave and the contract that followed; the site's 2025-26 pay comparison with Laconia and Gilford (Winnisquam 3.7% below Laconia and 9.1% below Gilford on average, after a 14.2% jump; higher starting pay, lower top pay; table); 2026 resignations, open jobs and 17 new teachers; paraprofessionals' union talks, credentials and a cut position. No turnover rate or paraprofessional wage was found.
- **Buildings (P2.3, P12.1):** the 2022 bond that failed at 55% (60% needed) and the sprinkler comment; Sanbornton Central's flat roof replaced in 2026; doors, bathrooms and windows at the middle and high school; the roof bond for them planned for March 2027; what the bond would cost Sanbornton (about $62,900 a year at the board's figure, **derived**).
- **High school value and skills (P4.3, P12.2):** how credits and diplomas are earned (competency rule, "NC"), the 24-credit diploma and its personal finance and economics requirements, the board's February 2026 talk of grade 11 math (10% proficient on the SAT-based test).
- **Say, mergers, waterfront and young adults (P15.1-P15.3, P12.3):** Sanbornton's three board seats and what its voters' share of the ballots is; the budget calendar and the February 5, 2027 petition deadline; no merger or withdrawal study found in the district's records, with what neighbors are doing and the state's rules; no source separates waterfront from other property, and one quarter of Sanbornton's homes are seasonal; ages 20 to 34 are 21.7% (+/-6.2) of residents, not fewer than the state's 18.7%, and what jobs and housing look like.

**Main sources:** the Town of Sanbornton's 2025 Annual Report (tax rate breakdown, MS-1, budget narrative), 2026 warrant and 2026 town meeting minutes, NH Employment Security's community profile, Master Plan 2016, 2026 zoning ordinance and news page; the district's 2026 Annual Report, 2026-27 budget presentation, central office budget hearing slides, school board minutes (January to November 2026) and September 2026 facilities update and budget calendar (all from town/Tilton's saved copies); the Town of Tilton's 2024 and 2025 annual reports (the three towns' apportionment); WRHS's 2026-27 comprehensive guide and school profile; a Laconia Daily Sun article on the 2022 vote; Marinace Architects' project page; NH REALTORS' October 2026 market update; the Census Bureau's ACS 2020-2024 age table; the site's database (tax rates and school taxes, adequacy aid, cost per pupil, teacher pay and schedules, staff counts, enrollment, test scores).
