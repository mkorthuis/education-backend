# Dover High School and the Career Technical Center

Part of the Dover research (town/Dover). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Dover High School and the Dover Regional Career Technical Center (CTC): the CTC's programs, admission, credentials and college credit (welding, construction, health science, EMT, biomedical science, culinary arts, pre-engineering, computer networking, NJROTC), the Tri-City partnership with Rochester and Somersworth, Dover High's AP and dual enrollment courses, graduation requirements (including the social studies and financial literacy changes for the class of 2030), work-based learning, internships and paid work for credit, how employers and restaurants can partner with the CTC, college planning help, the Dover School of Technology's evening apprenticeship classes, and UNH's admission rules.
