# Getting to School, School Zones and the School Day in Dover

Part of the Dover research (town/Dover). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Dover students get to school and how the school day and school zones work: who gets a bus, the First Student contract and its rates, the three-tier bus schedule, late buses, buses to Seacoast Charter School and to private schools (St. Thomas Aquinas, Portsmouth Christian Academy), what transportation costs and how special education transportation compares, school hours at each level, two-hour delays and the 2026-27 calendar, before- and after-school care (the Granite YMCA at Garrison and Horne Street, the Seymour Osman Community Center's program at Woodman Park), after-school clubs at Dover Middle School and the city's Teen Center, how students are assigned to elementary schools and can ask to change, the district's capacity report and its redistricting history, and traffic, drop-off and walking routes at Horne Street, Garrison, Woodman Park and Dover Middle School, with the city's planned Horne Street reconstruction, Shaw's Lane parking and Dover Point Road roundabout.

  - "Are we paying for half-empty routes and extra special ed vans?" The SOCC's fee isn't posted.
  - "Morning cut-through traffic" at Horne Street (P15.3): the city's capital plan calls Horne Street "an arterial road that also services" the school; no traffic counts were found. The school's drop-off pattern was set with the Dover Police Department.
  - "Seaborne Drive" (P25.3): consistent. Portsmouth Christian Academy is at 20 Seaborne Drive, and Garrison's handbook describes PCA as "just down the road" with overlapping drop-off times.
