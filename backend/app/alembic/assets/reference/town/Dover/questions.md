# Dover Residents' Questions

The questions the Dover research is meant to answer. Thirty resident profiles, three questions each (90 in all, half again the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Dover is SAU 11. The Dover School District runs three elementary schools (Frances G. Hopkins Elementary School at Horne Street, Garrison and Woodman Park, kindergarten to grade 4, with the district preschool at Woodman Park), Dover Middle School (grades 5–8) and Dover High School with the Dover Career Technical Center, which also takes high school students from Barrington and Nottingham under tuition contracts. Seacoast Charter School (K–8) is in Dover, and Saint Mary Academy and St. Thomas Aquinas High School are Catholic schools in the city. The school budget is set through city government under the city charter's tax cap. Several questions contain premises (budget and cap figures, a ballot question, program names, a school's planned move, state requirements) that the research should check rather than assume.
