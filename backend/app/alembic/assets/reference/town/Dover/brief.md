## What you need to know about Dover

- **Dover** is a city in Strafford County on the Seacoast, next to Durham (Oyster River), Rochester, Somersworth, Madbury and Portsmouth across the bay. Site town id 141. The city council sets the city budget under a charter **tax cap**; the school budget runs through it.
- **Dover School District** (site district id 141) is **SAU 11**. Use `district Dover` for SAU-level facts too.
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - **Elementary, kindergarten to grade 4:** Frances G. Hopkins Elementary School at Horne Street (20445), Garrison (20430), Woodman Park (20460, also home of the district preschool).
  - **Dover Middle School** (20435, grades 5–8), where the three elementary schools' students come together.
  - **Dover High School** (20455, grades 9–12) and the **Dover Career Technical Center** (CTC), which also serves sending schools. Barrington (2025–2034) and Nottingham (2025–2032) send high school students to Dover under tuition contracts (`places.yaml`).
- **Seacoast Charter School** (site school 28400, K–8) is in Dover. **Saint Mary Academy** (K–8) and **St. Thomas Aquinas High School** are Catholic schools in Dover (operator `Diocese of Manchester` in the site's data). **Portsmouth Christian Academy** (K–12) is in Dover.
- **Starting pages:** the district, https://www.dover.k12.nh.us (school pages, board, policies, budget); the CTC, http://ctc.dover.k12.nh.us; the city, https://www.dover.nh.gov (council, budget, tax cap, planning, police, recreation, the library). Board materials and policies are linked from the district site.
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - Enrollment by town of residence (the site's town enrollment): 3,686 Dover students in 2013-14, 3,371 in 2023-24 and 3,261 in 2025-26. In 2024-25 Dover's schools had Dover High 1,352 (including tuition students), Dover Middle 941, Horne Street 412, Woodman Park 421 and Garrison 353.
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Dover $19,315; state $22,699; Rochester $19,176; Somersworth $21,989; Oyster River $23,921; Portsmouth $27,609.
  - The site also has every school's enrollment by grade, test scores (with growth where NH DOE reports it), class sizes, staff and salary schedules, free and reduced-price meals, English learners, safety counts, graduation and plans, licensed child care in the city, EFAs by town, Question 2 results, Census estimates, adequacy aid, special education aid, tax rates and school taxes.
