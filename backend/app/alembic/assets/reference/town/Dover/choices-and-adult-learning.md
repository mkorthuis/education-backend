# Choices, Private, Charter and Home Education, and Adult Learning in Dover

Part of the Dover research (town/Dover). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the choice side of the Dover residents' questions: what private, charter, home-educated and EFA students who live in Dover may take or join at Dover's schools (classes, the Career Technical Center, sports), Dover's own home education and EFA participation policies, how homeschool and other credits count at Dover High, Seacoast Charter School's lottery and the move from it to Dover High, Saint Mary Academy's planned move to Somersworth, services for private school students, Tri-City CTE access for private school students, and the Dover Adult Learning Center (DALC): its counselor's funding, hours and evening options, HiSET, the adult diploma, and the step from a high school equivalency to the evening electrician and plumbing classes. It ends with help for graduates applying for financial aid.

- **8 topics, 63 facts, no tables.** Seven are shared town topics (`adult-education-and-workforce-training`, `district-homeschool-access`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `cte-center-programs`, `district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics`, `district-special-education-services`, `town-school-options-nearby`); one adds a fact to the library topic `community-colleges-nh`. Raw copies are in `raw/choices/` (about 31 MB), with geocoding and routing results in `raw/choices/geo/`.
- **Places used:** `district Dover` for the district, its schools, the CTC, the Dover School of Technology and DALC (DALC's 2026-27 staff handbook calls it "a department of the Dover School Department", so it is not filed under `town Dover`); `district Seacoast Charter School`; `district Diocese of Manchester` for Saint Mary Academy; `district Portsmouth Christian Academy`; no place for the Great Bay Community College fact.
- **Main findings:**
  - **The DALC counselor premise is partly right.** The counselor was grant-funded. With "significant reductions in state and federal funding," the school board's FY2027 budget (adopted January 12, 2026) moved the position into the district budget, and DALC's 2026-27 staff handbook (adopted August 5, 2026) lists a counselor for Rochester and Dover as a full-time, salaried position. DALC's pages describe the counselor appointment, diagnostic testing and HiSET classes as free.
  - **DALC is open Monday to Thursday evenings (5:30–8:00 p.m.) during the school year.** It also offers free tutoring at times set with the tutor, online adult diploma courses ($60 a credit) and daytime and evening classes at its Rochester site. Its fall 2026 HiSET testing dates include two Saturdays in Dover.
  - **The evening electrician and plumbing classes are the Dover School of Technology at the CTC.** The 2026-27 registration form asks for a high school diploma or equivalency, an employer sponsor and an apprentice ID card; the cost is $1,450 a year. DALC's transitions advising gives "information about job training courses" to HiSET graduates. No source describes a referral from DALC to the evening program.
  - **Dover's policy JJJ (2022) gives Dover-resident private, charter and home-educated students the same access as enrolled students** to courses, activities and sports, by written request to the principal, with an appeal process. A separate policy, JFBC (May 2026, revised September 2026), covers EFA students: up to half of regular instruction, existing class and practice times only, full cost of fee-based clubs, and "not entitled to special education services."
  - **Dover's posted home education policy (IHBG, 2023) and intent form still describe the rules from before July 1, 2026,** including the annual evaluation that state law no longer requires.
  - **Seacoast Charter places siblings of admitted students and children of staff before its lottery.** For 2027-28, applications are open October 1, 2026 to March 19, 2027, and the lottery is March 24, 2027. All 2026-27 classes are full.
  - **Saint Mary Academy plans to move to a 10.25-acre site shared with St. Ignatius of Loyola Parish in Somersworth.** If completed as planned, it would open in 2028. The project costs an estimated $20 million, including a $15 million anonymous gift. From Garrison Hill Park the drive grows from about 1.6 miles to about 4.2 miles (derived, free-flow).
  - **Dover manages the Title I grant for qualifying students at Saint Mary Academy.** The FY2027 budget added a school psychologist to evaluate and serve students at private and out-of-district schools.
- **Main gaps:** any measure of how Seacoast Charter students adjust at Dover High; Dover's equitable services plan or amount for private school students with disabilities; whether Dover has revised IHBG or its intent form since July 2026 (board minutes are behind a JavaScript records viewer); the HiSET test fee; the Tri-City online application itself (a JavaScript app); the Somersworth CTC's site (404); NH DOE's November 5, 2025 technical advisory on access for EFA students (education.nh.gov blocks automated requests); any source saying Dover High or DALC helps graduates who are not DALC students with financial aid forms.
