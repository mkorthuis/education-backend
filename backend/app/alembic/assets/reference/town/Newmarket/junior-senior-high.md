# Newmarket Junior-Senior High School: Courses, College Credit, SST, Counseling and the Arts

Part of the Newmarket research (town/Newmarket). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Newmarket Junior-Senior High School (NJSHS, grades 6–12, one building at 213 South Main Street): its middle school and high school schedules, AP and honors courses, advanced math, college credit through UNH, Great Bay Community College, SNHU and VLACS, extended learning, work study, graduation requirements, Newmarket's side of the Seacoast School of Technology (SST), counseling for college, trades and financial aid, scholarships, and music, theater and clubs. SST itself is in `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md` and is cited, not repeated. Facts are placed `district Newmarket`, except one local scholarship (`town Newmarket`).

- **8 fact topics, 63 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 27 MB), with school board documents in `raw/high/board/`.
- **Main sources:** NJSHS's 2026-27 Program of Studies (Classes of 2027–2029) and its separate 2026-27 Program of Studies for the Class of 2030; the 2026-27 and 2025-26 school profiles; the 2026-27 Student Handbook; the 2026-27 bell schedule; the counseling department's pages and documents (year-by-year counseling plan, "Senior Year… What's Next?" slides, steps for applying, College Admission Handbook, 2026-27 counseling calendar, Early College guide, VLACS guide and distance-learning forms, Work Study contract, ELO packet); the FY2027 approved budget report; the 2026-27 NJSHS bus routes; school board agendas, minutes and packets on the district's Diligent portal (November 2025 to November 2026); UNH's 2026-27 catalog; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24 (filtered from the Concord research's extracts); and SST's October 2026 report (Exeter's raw copy).
- **New data concepts:** in-person AP courses actually running against those in the catalog; students per counselor (**derived**); how SST's sessions fit NJSHS's periods (**derived**); the SST bus run; work-study and ELO hours per credit; the middle school and high school separation in one building; the music staffing cut; school-reported graduates' plans for the Class of 2026.
- **Derived figures:** about 139 high school students per high school counselor and 101 per budgeted guidance position, 119 junior high students per budgeted guidance position; 42 AP students per 290 high school students in 2023-24 (about 14 per 100); 52 Newmarket SST students, 19% of Newmarket's 272 high school students in October 2026; SST session overlap with NJSHS periods; 21 donated instruments; the Class of 2027's 24 credits summed.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Head start on college classes at UNH" (P2.2): **possible, at the family's cost.** The handbook lets students apply to take UNH or Great Bay classes from the end of junior year (or sooner with permission), with tuition and transportation the student's responsibility; UNH's catalog offers juniors and seniors its CATS program; UNH Early College Online runs through VLACS. No UNH course is taught at NJSHS.
  - "Middle schoolers have their own wing" (P2.3): **not found.** No source describes a separate wing. The middle school has its own bell schedule, lunch and recess, trimesters, grade-level teams, dances and open house, and in September 2026 the principals described a "full separation of the two schools," but both share one building, library and cafetorium.
  - "How many AP classes are taught in person" (P12.1): **five in 2026-27** (AP Calculus AB, Statistics, Biology, Environmental Science and Literature), per the school profile; the catalog lists seven (adding AP Chemistry and AP U.S. History), and 2025-26 ran six.
  - "Kids per counselor" (P12.3): two high school counselors split the alphabet for 277 students (about 139 each, **derived**), well under the state's limit of 300 per counselor.
  - "Music teaching time got trimmed" (P19.1): **confirmed as staffing.** The FY2027 budget cut music from 3.0 to 2.5 full-time positions districtwide; the half-time middle school music job had gone unfilled in 2025-26. In 2026-27 the school still runs a 6th grade band, a 7th/8th grade band, a chorus and a high school band; no source says when the 7th/8th grade band rehearses.
  - "Senior project" (P20.1): **not found.** No senior project appears in the Program of Studies, handbook or profile. The Class of 2027 needs 24 credits, the state civics test and the required senior exit survey.
  - "Work-study at local shops for credit" (P17.1): **confirmed.** Juniors and seniors may earn 0.5 credit per 120 hours worked, up to 1.0 credit a year.
  - "Cheap college credit through Great Bay or Running Start" (P20.2): **confirmed with limits.** NJSHS's Early College guide says the first two community college courses a year are free and later ones half price; the high school itself teaches one Great Bay course (Hospitality Management) and one SNHU course (College Composition).
  - "Evening FAFSA workshops" (P20.3): the 2026-27 FAFSA Information Night was a virtual event on October 6, 2026; no in-person evening FAFSA workshop was found. Local scholarship applications usually come out in early February and are due in early April.
