# Newmarket Residents' Questions

The questions the Newmarket research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Newmarket is SAU 31, a one-district SAU. The Newmarket School District runs Newmarket Elementary School (preschool to grade 5) and Newmarket Junior-Senior High School (grades 6–12). Newmarket students may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (a $39 million renovation still being paid off, about a thousand students, a $100,000 building reserve, unfilled teacher and tutor positions, trimmed music time, trouble filling paraeducator jobs, a yearly homeschool filing with SAU 31) that the research should check rather than assume.
