# Student Support, Daily Life and Choices in Newmarket

Part of the Newmarket research (town/Newmarket). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how the Newmarket School District (SAU 31) reaches families in other languages and through the Infinite Campus parent portal; paraeducators, related services (speech, occupational and physical therapy) and the move from Newmarket Elementary (NES) to Newmarket Junior-Senior High (NJSHS) for students with IEPs; storm delays, how families are told, the First Student bus contract and the 2026-27 routes; access to classes and activities for home-educated, charter, private and EFA students, the district's home education policy and placement on returning; the phone policy, bullying and counseling; sports teams, JV levels and co-ops; the lack of a late bus; and drop-off and pickup traffic at the two schools on South Main Street (NH 152).

- **9 topics, 77 facts, 2 number tables.** Places: 74 facts use `district Newmarket` (SAU 31, its schools, policies, buses and board) and 3 use `town Newmarket` (the 2026 Master Plan, the Highway Safety Committee and a town-side gap). Tables: `newmarket_bus_ride_minutes_2026_27` and `newmarket_counseling_staff_2026_27`.
- **New data concepts:** the open "Paraeducator – Special Education" postings and how long they have stayed open (derived), paraeducator starting pay and the contract's intensive-needs differential; the paraeducator job description's duty to go with assigned students to related services and to PE, art and music; the First Student renewal (2026–2031), its rate increases and driver wages; scheduled ride times on every 2026-27 route (derived); the policy committee's finding that the transportation policies are out of date; the approved JJJ-R regulation for non-enrolled students (September 17, 2026) and the committee's view that JJJ already complies with HB 1817; the junior high's "Off and Away" rules; the junior-senior high's 2026-27 team list with JV levels and co-op partners; the elementary school's one-way car loop; the athletic field study's traffic scoping.
- **Main sources:** newmarket.k12.nh.us pages (transportation, closings and delays, registration, student services, counseling, related service providers, ESOL, human resources, school hours, families toolbox); the 2026-27 NES and NJSHS handbooks; the 2026-27 bus route sheets; the Infinite Campus Parent Portal Guide; school board policies IHBG, JJJ, JJA, JJIB, JICJ, EEA, EEA-R, IJO, KB-R, JG and IK (from the district's BoardDocs site); Diligent Community board agendas, minutes and attachments from December 2025 to November 2026 (35 agendas and 21 sets of minutes searched; 12 sets of minutes cited); the Newmarket Support Staff Association contract (2026–2030); the Frontline job board; the middle school counseling page and newsletter; the athletic contract and the Arbiter team list; the town's Master Plan transportation chapter (adopted August 11, 2026) and Highway Safety Committee page.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Trouble filling paraeducator jobs" (P6.1): **partly supported.** On November 21, 2026 the district's job board listed two "Paraeducator – Special Education" openings, posted August 10 (NJSHS) and August 26 (NES) and open "when filled," about three months later (derived). No district document calls it a shortage. The FY2026 year-end report credits savings to "unfilled vacancies" without naming the positions.
  - "A yearly homeschool filing with SAU 31" (P10.2): **out of date.** Since July 1, 2026 state law requires no declaration unless the family uses district classes or activities or withdraws a child from public school (library `home-education-rules` 1–2, 5). Newmarket's own policy IHBG (last revised 2019) already said "No Annual Notice Required"; it still describes the repealed yearly evaluation and portfolio, and the policy committee recommended no changes to IHBG on August 27, 2026. An EFA student is not a "home educated student" under state law (library `home-education-rules` 4).
  - "Two-hour snow delay … how early do they send the text or call" (P9.2): **delays are two hours** (NES starts 10:30, NJSHS opens 9:15). Policy EEA-R says announcements begin "at approximately 6:00 a.m."; the policy committee found that policy out of date in fall 2026, and no log of actual send times is published.
  - "Routes get canceled when they're short on drivers" (P9.3): **no record found.** No 2026 minutes, notices or posts mention a canceled route; the superintendent called First Student "very dependable" (October 2025) and reported no major bus issues at the 2026-27 opening.
  - "Enough kids for JV and varsity" and "co-op for hockey or football" (P15.1, P15.2): **JV teams exist** in football, boys' basketball, boys' and girls' soccer and golf. **Co-ops are correct:** football with Epping, hockey with Portsmouth (listed as Portsmouth-Newmarket-Sanborn), lacrosse with Oyster River, and unified soccer and basketball with Epping. The only cuts found were to the middle school boys' soccer team in fall 2026, because of roster limits, not low numbers.
  - "A huge waitlist" for a counselor (P13.3): **no waitlist data is published.** The middle school has one counselor for 208 students (derived), plus a student support counselor, a grades 6–12 psychologist and a social worker, with online appointment booking that is new in 2026-27.
  - "Drop-off and pickup traffic back up all the way through downtown" (P17.2): **no source links downtown backups to school traffic.** Both schools sit on South Main Street (NH 152), which the 2026 Master Plan names as a main route drawing traffic into the village core. No school traffic study was found. A traffic and access review is planned only for the proposed athletic fields.
  - Translation (P4.1): **no translated report cards, forms or portal messages were found.** The website has a machine-translation menu, and policy IJO promises "reasonable efforts" to communicate in a parent's primary language.
