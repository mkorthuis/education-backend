# Bedford High School and Pathways

Part of the Bedford research (town/Bedford). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Bedford High School (BHS): how the IB Diploma, IB courses and AP courses fit together, course levels and grade weighting, AP and IB exam sign-up dates, fees and the help available, dual enrollment and other outside credit, the Manchester School of Technology (MST) from Bedford's side, hands-on courses, the Adult High School Diploma, counseling, mental health support and the 2025 Youth Risk Behavior Survey, help with schoolwork (the Academic Center, peer tutoring and Tutor.com), the district's rules on generative AI, sports commitments, fee help at Lurgio, and Bedford's test scores set beside the state's by family income.

- **11 fact topics, 84 facts and 3 tables.** Ten topics are shared town topics; one is proposed (below). Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 18 MB, 110 files): BHS's IB and AP sites, the 2026-27 student handbook and Athletic Teams and School Organizations Handbook, the exam registration documents, counseling, social work and peer tutoring pages and the grade-level counseling curricula, the course-level and pathways documents, the curriculum guide list, district policies IHCD, IKL, IKB, IKFG and JQ, the district AI statement (April 2026) and 2024 parent letter, the Tutor.com guide (OCR), the FY27 BHS budget detail and executive summary, the 2025 YRBS memo and results, the November 9, 2026 board agenda and brief, the BHS school profile (scanned; OCR), Lurgio's handbook and athlete handbook and the elementary handbook, College Board fee pages and the 2026-27 AP Coordinator's Manual, CCSNH's 2024-25 Early College report and UNH's catalog page on IB credit.
- **Places:** `district Bedford` for BHS, Lurgio, the elementary handbook and district policies. No place for College Board, CCSNH and UNH facts.
- **New data concepts:** AP and IB exam fees, deadlines and aid at one school, with New Hampshire's AP fee assistance; IB Diploma rules and pass rate; how a school with both IB and AP lets students mix them; the share of IB Diploma candidates; a district's generative AI statement and academic honesty policy; YRBS results for one high school against the region and state; proficiency by family income against the state (derived); a high school's CTE tuition budget against actual spending.
- **Proposed topic:** `ap-and-ib-exam-fees-and-deadlines` (kind how_it_works, question type rules): "When students sign up for AP and IB exams, what the exams cost, late and cancellation fees, fee reductions and New Hampshire's AP fee assistance." No existing topic covers College Board deadlines and fees, which apply to every New Hampshire high school.
- **Main gaps:** how many Bedford students attend MST; which Early College (CCSNH) courses BHS runs (the Program of Studies database is built in a script and could not be read); costs of senior week, prom and any team or club dues; the College Board's own explanation for fall exam ordering; IB's own fee and deadline pages (blocked); any measure of outside tutoring.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 14. The Londonderry (BHS profile, counselors, CRDC), Manchester (MST programs), Albany (CTE cost law), SAU 9 (admissions research), Nashua (grade 11 results by income) files and the library fill in parts, as the table shows.

| P13.2 | AP and IB exam sign-ups happen "so early in the fall before we even know how hard the class is" | Mostly accurate.    - citation: Bedford High School IB website, "The IB Diploma"
