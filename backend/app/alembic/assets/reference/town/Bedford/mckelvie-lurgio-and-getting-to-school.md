# McKelvie, Lurgio, Getting to School and Safety in Bedford

Part of the Bedford research (town/Bedford). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Bedford's grades 5–8 schools and how students get to them: the move from three K–4 schools into McKelvie Intermediate (grades 5–6), McKelvie's math placement and enrichment, special education as students move up, the 2026-27 bus routes and staggered bell times, bus problems and whom to call, drop-off on the shared Lurgio–Bedford High campus on Nashua Road, the phone policy and social media rules, choosing ninth-grade courses, returning from a charter school, how McKelvie compares with The Founders Academy and Manchester's Catholic schools, translation, what students learn about AI, data and engineering, bullying procedures and counts, and the emergency plan.

- **13 topics, 124 facts, 6 number tables.** Places: `district Bedford` for SAU 25, its schools, policies, buses and board; `town Bedford` for the Catholic schools families may compare (named in each statement).
- **New data concepts:** scheduled ride times and the two-wave bus schedule for all 116 posted 2026-27 route runs (58 morning, 58 afternoon) (derived; table `bedford_bus_ride_minutes_2026_27`); the district's own monthly bullying, suspension and threat-assessment reports to the school board, by school; special education caseloads by school on October 1, 2026; NCES student-to-teacher ratios for McKelvie, Lurgio, Founders and five Manchester Catholic schools; the August 2026 phone-policy survey results; the reunification site at Saint Anselm College; the police–school agreement on school resource officers.
- **Main sources:** the district's Google Sites (transportation, enrichment, AI, bullying, special services, policy manual), the McKelvie, Lurgio and Bedford High 2026-27 handbooks, McKelvie's FAQ and math placement site, the 2026-27 bus route sheets and transportation handbook, policies EEA, EEAB, JICJ, JICK, EBCA, EBCB, JLDBC, IIB, IHBB, IK, IK-R, IMBD, JFAA, JG and EHAG, the police memorandum of understanding (JIC-R), the parents' emergency operations guide, school board minutes and briefs (April–November 2026), the board's monthly bullying, suspension and enrollment reports, the October 1, 2026 special education staffing sheet, the Special Education Parent Advisory Council's October 2026 minutes, McKelvie's and Lurgio's public calendars, the ELL program's family presentation, NCES's public and private school data, and the site's own enrollment, class size and bullying tables.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Every kid in town suddenly merges together at McKelvie in 5th grade" (P2.1): **correct.** McKelvie is the district's only grades 5–6 school. On November 2, 2026 the three K–4 schools had 282 fourth graders and McKelvie had 296 fifth graders.
  - "McKelvie's later morning schedule" (P2.3): **correct.** McKelvie's tardy bell is 8:50 a.m. (drop-off from 8:20); K–4 schools start at 8:00 and Bedford High at 7:55. McKelvie buses pick up from 7:51 a.m., about 45 minutes or more after the elementary buses (derived).
  - Elementary, McKelvie and Lurgio start times "all staggered" (P7.1): **partly correct.** There are two waves, not three: K–4 (8:00) and Bedford High (7:55) first, then McKelvie and Lurgio together (both 8:50 tardy bells, both 3:15 dismissal). The district's pages don't give a reason; policy EEAB aims for "maximum service with a minimum fleet of buses," and every McKelvie and Lurgio route number also runs an earlier route (derived).
  - Lurgio and the high school "right next to each other" (P9.1): **correct** (47A and 47B Nashua Road, one campus). Their arrival times are about an hour apart: BHS buses arrive by 7:35 for 7:55 classes; Lurgio drop-off begins at 8:30.
  - "Thousands of middle and high schoolers" at Nashua Road (P17.2): **overstated.** About 1,950 students (622 at Lurgio and 1,328 at Bedford High on November 2, 2026, derived).
  - "The much bigger McKelvie building" (P5.3): **correct.** McKelvie had 596 students on November 2, 2026; Memorial had 355.
