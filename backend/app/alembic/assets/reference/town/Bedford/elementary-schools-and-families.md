# Bedford Elementary Schools, BEEP and Family Life

Part of the Bedford research (town/Bedford). Gathered November 16, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Bedford School District's three kindergarten-to-grade-4 schools (Memorial, Peter Woodbury and Riddle Brook) and its preschool, the Bedford Early Education Program (BEEP), from a family's side: finding which school serves an address and checking the town line; full-day kindergarten, how it was approved and what it costs families; before- and after-school care; kindergarten registration and after-school waitlists; home-educated and EFA students' access to district classes and activities after the July 1, 2026 law change; English learner and reading support; the Bedford PTG, school councils and volunteering; pickup and visitor rules; PowerSchool, ParentSquare and Google Classroom; and K–4 class sizes against enrollment.

- **13 topics, 66 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 22 MB): the 2026-27 elementary and BEEP handbooks, eleven board policies (IHBG, JJJ and JJJ-R, IHBBA, IIB, IJOC, JHC, JFA, JCA, KBBA, KI), ten sets of school board minutes (April to October 2026) and two agendas, district, ELL, PTG, town assessing and GIS pages, the after-school providers' pages and flyers, Census ZIP-code files, the site-database queries, and transcriptions of the scanned 2025 annual report pages.
- **Topics:** eleven shared slugs (`district-attendance-zones-and-transfers`, `district-preschool`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `town-recreation-and-summer`, `district-homeschool-access`, `district-student-support`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-volunteers-and-partners`, `district-safety-and-security`, `district-family-communication`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`) and two library topics with their own kind and description (`kindergarten-day-length`, `kindergarten-registration`). No new topic is proposed.
- **Places:** `district Bedford` for SAU 25, its schools, BEEP, policies and programs run inside its buildings; `town Bedford` for the town's assessing and map tools, Census ZIP-code facts and private programs off school grounds (New Morning's IMAGINE campus and Elite Plus).
- **Main sources:** the district's Google Sites pages (sites.google.com/bedfordnhk12.net/…), its policy, budget, agenda and minutes folders on Google Drive, the 2025 annual report, bedfordptg.org, bedfordnh.gov and gis.vgsi.com/bedfordnh, the providers' own sites, the Census Bureau, and the site's database for a derived class-size comparison.
- **Main gaps:** the School Finder street table could not be read (it opens a Google sign-in for automated requests); the district posts no rule on how a grandparent or other caregiver is added as an authorized pickup person or given portal access; no library or cafeteria volunteer roles are described; no 2027-28 kindergarten registration date is posted yet; providers post no waitlist lengths, Elite posts no prices, and no before-school program inside a Bedford school was found; Bedford Parks and Recreation's program portal and the Bedford Citizen (after one search) refused automated requests.

The district does report families moving in during the school years (its "Bedford Effect"). The data can't say how much any one development adds; housing is agent 4's area. |
| P10.2 | Homeschool "paperwork" is filed with SAU 25 | Since July 1, 2026 New Hampshire requires a declaration only when a family wants district classes or activities or withdraws a child (library home-education-rules fact 1). The board's minutes of September 8, 2026 say families no longer have to inform the district. Bedford's posted policy IHBG (revised 2022) still describes the older notice and evaluation rules. |
| P14.3 | "Everything is on PowerSchool and Google Classroom now" | Partly. The elementary handbook lists ParentSquare for assignments, grades, attendance, announcements and homework; PowerSchool for report cards; and Google Classroom for assignments and resources. ## kindergarten-day-length: Kindergarten Day Length by District

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: Whether each district offers full-day or half-day kindergarten, from its own pages.
