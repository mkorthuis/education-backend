# Bedford Residents' Questions

The questions the Bedford research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. The profiles came with first names only. Received November 16, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Bedford is SAU 25, a one-district SAU. The Bedford School District runs three elementary schools for kindergarten to grade 4 (Memorial, Peter Woodbury and Riddle Brook), the Bedford Early Education Program (BEEP) preschool, McKelvie Intermediate School (grades 5–6), Ross A. Lurgio Middle School (grades 7–8) and Bedford High School (9–12), which offers the IB Diploma and AP courses; Lurgio and the high school share a campus on Nashua Road. Bedford students may take career and technical programs at the Manchester School of Technology. The district votes by official ballot (SB2), with a default budget if the proposed one fails. Several questions contain premises (free full-day kindergarten, McKelvie's "later morning schedule", staggered start times, "thousands" of students at the Nashua Road campus, a move away from commercial textbooks, a cut to the technology budget, aging technology and building repairs and a coming bond, early AP and IB exam sign-ups, BEEP being only for special education, new houses raising K–4 class sizes) that the research should check rather than assume.
