# Bethlehem: Money, Taxes, Governance and Buildings

Part of the Bethlehem research (town/Bethlehem). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 12 topics, 83 facts and 4 number tables on the Bethlehem School District's and Profile School District's money, SAU 35's governance, and the White Mountain School.

- **Taxes and state aid:** how Bethlehem's school tax splits between the Bethlehem School District and Profile (about half and half in 2025), the 18% rise in the local education tax from 2014 to 2025 against a 20% fall in resident students, the 2023 jump in assessed value, how Profile's cost is shared with Franconia, Sugar Hill and Easton (by students and valuation; dollars by town for 2025 and FY27), fund balances, the hold-harmless step-down and the 2026 tax-cap slides.
- **Budgets:** Bethlehem's 2025 and 2026-27 warrants, Profile's 2026-27 budget line by line, enrollment against budget, transportation cost sharing, the FY28 start, tax-cap cut scenarios, and Grafton County cost per pupil (27 districts).
- **SAU 35 and boards:** four boards today, five (29 members) until Lisbon left on July 1, 2026, the executive board, how SAU costs are divided, the FY27 and FY28 SAU budgets, Profile's structure as a cooperative of the Bethlehem and Lafayette districts, and consolidation talks (a dropped 2025 study article, shared superintendent talks with Littleton).
- **Meetings and where to see budgets:** board schedules, Profile's FY28 budget calendar, hearing notices, and the gaps in plain-English summaries.
- **Buildings and security:** the 2026 energy and ventilation leases at all three schools ($1,328,737 Bethlehem; $2,670,253 Profile), financing, progress, Profile's $130,000 Safe Schools Grant cameras, drills, Bethlehem's safety policies, elevator and lead work, and what is not known about code inspections.
- **Buying:** SAU 35's joint bids, Bethlehem's purchasing policies, and how contractors were chosen.
- **Staff, housing and the White Mountain School:** retirements and recruiting, the contracts, enrollment trends, and the school's June 30, 2024 closing and auction.

**Main sources:** school board and executive board minutes posted on the Bethlehem Elementary, Profile and SAU 35 sites (January 2025 to November 2026; 16 Bethlehem, 22 SAU 35 and about 20 Profile files), Profile's March 19, 2026 annual meeting minutes (a scan, read from images), SAU 35's October 27, 2026 tax-cap slides, SAU 35's capital project page, bid notices, the site's database (taxes, enrollment, cost per pupil), and two news reports on the White Mountain School.
