# Bethlehem Elementary: Learning, Class Groupings and School Choices

Part of the Bethlehem research (town/Bethlehem). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

This file covers what Bethlehem Elementary School (BES, preschool to grade 6) publishes about how it groups classes and assigns students, class size and capacity, benchmark testing, state test results and how they compare with Lafayette Regional School, homework, enrichment, art and music, anti-bullying and online-safety lessons, volunteers and substitutes, how a child coming from a private school is placed, and how homeschooling and Education Freedom Account (EFA) families deal with SAU 35. State rules are not repeated: it points to the library and to earlier towns' files.

**Counts:** 15 topics, 76 facts, 1 number table (`bethlehem_lafayette_grades_3_6_tests_2022_2026`). Raw copies are in `raw/learning/` (about 47 MB).

**Main sources:** BES's 2026-27 Student & Parent Handbook and its July 2022 Policies & Procedures Handbook; BES's staff and registration pages; 19 SAU 35 board policies (exported from the Google Docs linked on sau35.org); 14 sets of Bethlehem School Board minutes (January 2024 to June 2026) and 7 principal's reports (August 2024 to February 2025); the SAU 35 Superintendent's monthly reports (November 2025 to August 2026); BES's ESSER II and III pages; Lafayette Regional School's staff and report-card pages; the EDies award page; the EFA marketplace and statistics lists in the library; Granite State Home Educators' support groups page; the site's own database (class size, enrollment, test results).

**Main gaps:** no source names BES's reading or math programs; no source says how many minutes a week students have art, music or library; no BES document describes a digital-safety lesson for grade 5 or a way to move a student up in math; no volunteer reading-tutor program is posted; the March 17 and April 14, 2026 board minutes (which would show the March vote on open enrollment) need a Google sign-in; several NH Department of Education pages refused automated requests.
