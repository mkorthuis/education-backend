# Bethlehem Residents' Questions

The questions the Bethlehem research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Bethlehem's students attend the Bethlehem School District's Bethlehem Elementary School (preschool to grade 6) and then Profile School, a junior and senior high (grades 7–12) in Bethlehem that serves several towns, all within SAU 35 (Bethlehem, Lafayette Regional, Landaff and Profile). Several questions contain premises (about 200 students in grades 7–12, four school boards under SAU 35, The White Mountain School's closing, a share of Profile's cost for Franconia, Sugar Hill and Easton, a tech center in Littleton) that the research should check rather than assume.
