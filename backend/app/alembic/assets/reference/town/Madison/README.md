# Madison: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Madison, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`; the people are invented. The research is in the reference library's format (`../../additional-data/README.md`) and uses the shared topics in `../shared-topics.md`. It isn't loaded into the Ask yet; see `../recommended-improvements.md`.

Gathered November 13, 2026. Madison is the first town researched outside SAU 9. It is in SAU 13 with Freedom and Tamworth, runs Madison Elementary School (K–6, with the SAU 13 preschool) and sends grades 7–12 to Conway's Kennett Middle and High (SAU 9). Its 47 SAU-wide facts are placed `sau 13` for Freedom and Tamworth to share.

## Notable findings

- **Default budgets two years running:** voters rejected the operating budget in 2025 and 2026, and the district returned surpluses of $551,566 (2024-25) and a projected $886,221 (2025-26).
- **Kennett's future is open:** the board voted in February 2024 not to renew the 2003 contracts, asked in October 2024 about Governor Wentworth and Moultonborough, and had no new Conway contract by November 2026.
- **A closure study may go to voters:** a draft 2027 warrant article would set up a committee to study closing Madison Elementary.
- **Madison gets no adequacy grant for FY2027:** the statewide education tax raised in town now exceeds its adequacy cost (site data). The 2025 revaluation set a base waterfront value of $800,000 on Silver Lake.
- **Tax cap:** Madison passed the HB 1300 school tax cap with 60.6%; the three SAU 13 towns together reached 60.9% (derived).
