# Getting to School, the School Day and SAU 13 Policies for Madison Families

Part of the Madison research (town/Madison). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Madison students get to school and the rules around the school day. Madison Elementary (K–6, SAU 13) and the students Madison sends to Kennett Middle and High (grades 7–12, SAU 9 / Conway) both ride the same three First Student buses, which became Madison's contractor in 2026-27. The file gives the 2026-27 route sheets and derived ride times, the Route 113 and Silver Lake (East Shore Drive) stop times, the late bus and early-release buses, and the bus policies. It also covers Madison Elementary's hours and its fee-based before- and after-school program; Madison's 2026-27 calendar side by side with SAU 9's (derived); storm closings and the remote-instruction policy; and where extended school year (ESY) is held. On policy it covers the home education policy and how it compares with the July 2026 law, access for home-educated students (policy JJJ), free and reduced-price meal applications, the AP exam fee waiver tied to meal eligibility, and the SAU 13 homeless and foster care policies, contacts and transportation costs.

It builds on the Conway and Jackson files, which already cover Kennett's late-bus rules, the SAU 9 calendar, SAU 9 meal applications, Kennett's lack of a sports fee, Conway's foster care policy and the shared topic `foster-care-school-stability`. Those are named in the question table, not repeated.

**Premises checked:**
- P2.3 "the morning school bus … along Route 113 and East Shore Drive": the sheets label Village Road and Conway Road as Route 113, and Madison's buses stop along both (6:28–7:02 a.m. for Kennett, 8:34–8:58 a.m. for Madison Elementary). No stop is listed on East Shore Drive. That road meets Plains Road (Route 41) near Fawn Drive, where the Red bus stops (derived). Kennett and Madison Elementary riders don't have separate buses: the same three buses run a Kennett trip first and then an elementary trip.
- P4.3 "do students have to travel to Tamworth or Conway?": SAU 13's 2026 ESY was held at Madison Elementary (July 6–August 5, Monday–Wednesday, 9:00–12:00).
- P5.1 "annual homeschool notifications and end-of-year portfolio/testing evaluations": out of date. Since July 1, 2026 New Hampshire requires neither, except a declaration when a child leaves a public school or joins public school courses. Madison's posted policy IHBG (2014) still describes the earlier rules, though even it said no annual notice was needed.
- P6.3 "fees for Kennett High athletics": no Kennett sports participation fee was found (Jackson file). AP exams do carry a fee ($99), and for students eligible for free or reduced-price meals, College Board and the state together bring it to $0 for May 2027.
- P17.2 "Madison/SAU #13 … school vacations": SAU 13 has no single calendar. Freedom, Madison and Tamworth each adopt their own. Madison's 2026-27 vacations match SAU 9's week for week.
- P19.2 "winter weather forces remote learning days": SAU 13 policy allows remote instruction on bad-weather days. No remote day was found in Madison minutes from July 2025 to November 2026, and the 2025-26 snow day was made up at the end of the year.
- P6.2: late buses from Kennett to Madison exist, but only on request (sign up by 1:00 p.m. the same day).
