# Madison Residents' Questions

The questions the Madison research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Madison is in SAU 13 with Freedom and Tamworth. The Madison School District runs Madison Elementary School (with the SAU 13 preschool); its older students attend Conway's A. Crosby Kennett Middle School and Kennett High School (SAU 9). Several questions contain premises (ratios, enrollments, rates, contract lengths, laws) that the research should check rather than assume.
