# Jaffrey: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Jaffrey, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 21, 2026, in the second wave of the second ToLoad batch. Jaffrey's students attend the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District (SAU 47), shared with Rindge:
- Jaffrey Grade School: preschool to grade 5;
- Rindge Memorial School, in Rindge: preschool to grade 5;
- Conant Middle High School, in Jaffrey: grades 6–12.

Since December 2025, Conant students take career programs only at the Region 14 Applied Technology Center (ConVal in Peterborough; Mascenic for automotive and construction trades). The district decides its budget by official ballot. These files are the first research on Jaffrey-Rindge; Rindge's research follows and cites them. District facts are placed `district Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative` and serve both towns.
