# Jaffrey Grade School and Young Families

Part of the Jaffrey research (town/Jaffrey). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Jaffrey Grade School (JGS, preschool to grade 5) in the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District (SAU 47) from the side of young families: the free preschool lottery and what the 2026 budget did to it, the staff-only Little Orioles child care center, Head Start and other care, the school day, after-school care and clubs, buses and driver shortages, storm days and remote learning, class sizes and class-size targets, staffing (paraprofessionals, reading and speech staff), reading instruction and how parents see progress, meals and the free and reduced-price application, the Alma portal and SchoolMessenger, English learners and Spanish-language materials. District-wide facts are placed with `district Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative` so the Rindge research can reuse them; town programs are placed with `town Jaffrey`.

- **13 topics, 76 facts and 3 number tables.** Twelve topics are shared slugs (`district-preschool`, `town-child-care`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `town-recreation-and-summer`, `district-transportation`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-meals`, `town-family-help`, `district-family-communication`, `district-student-support`); one is a library topic (`kindergarten-day-length`). Tables: JGS bus runs 2026-27 (derived), elementary enrollment and homerooms 2020-21 to 2026-27, and JGS and Rindge Memorial class-size projections for 2027-28.
- **New data concepts:** preschool lottery dates, sessions and the waitlist after the cut; Little Orioles tuition and hours; the district's own class-size projections and its unwritten "17" target against written Policy IIB; the paraprofessional model (IEP-only paras since about 2023); the Rindge driver-shortage route plan; each Jaffrey bus's first pickup and its earlier Conant run; storm-day and remote-learning policies adopted in 2026; elementary early-release dates; Got Lunch.

### Premises checked

| Question | Premise | What the sources show |
|---|---|---|
| P1.1 | The Little Orioles child care program and pre-K slots are being cut | **Partly wrong.** The Little Orioles Center serves only the children of district staff and was not cut: in July 2026 the board kept it, made it fully tuition-funded and cut one full-time staff member. **Pre-K was cut:** after the default budget, four pre-K teaching positions were cut and two brought back with grant money; Jaffrey has one full-day class of 14 with about 11–12 children waitlisted (June 2026). A November 2026 tax-cap scenario for 2027-28 lists two more pre-K teachers and two paras as possible cuts; nothing is decided. |

Current 2nd-grade counts weren't found. |
| P1.3 | School lets out at 3:30 | **Slightly off.** JGS's day for grades K–5 is 8:30 a.m. to 3:15 p.m.; car-rider dismissal begins at 3:15 (2025-26 handbook). |
| P4.2 | JGS can't keep paraprofessionals | **Partly supported.** A board member said in November 2025 that the district typically has 5–10 open para positions, and Student Services reports "continued difficulty filling open internal positions." | P7.3 | Kindergarten may not be full-day every day | **It is full-day:** kindergarten follows the K–5 day, 8:30 a.m.–3:15 p.m. Registration for 2026-27 opened April 1, 2026; 2027-28 dates weren't posted. |
| P11.1 | Jaffrey buses are being combined with Rindge routes, arriving 25 minutes late | **Not what the posted plan says.** The district's driver-shortage plan combines two Rindge buses (43 and 45) with each other. | P11.2 | Snow days may be remote days | **Possible but not yet used, as far as found.** Policies adopted in January and August 2026 let the superintendent call a remote learning day in bad weather; in 2025-26 the district's three snow days were made up by moving the last day of school. |
| P13.3 | The district aimed for 17 students per elementary class | **Partly.** Written Policy IIB (2018) aims for no more than 20 in K–2 and 25 in grades 3–5. In December 2025 the superintendent described a board target of 17 or fewer in K–3 and 20 in grades 4–5. JGS's average class size is about 16; its 2027-28 projections run from 11 to 18. The board chair said kindergarten classes were "over 20" in October 2026 (school not named). |
| P16.1 | An ESL teacher may or may not be at JGS daily | **No ESL teacher found.** No English learner (ESOL) teacher appears on the district's October 2026 organizational charts. The district had 3 English learners in 2025-26 (site data). |
