# Jaffrey Residents' Questions

The questions the Jaffrey research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Jaffrey's students attend the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District (SAU 47), shared with Rindge. It runs Jaffrey Grade School (preschool to grade 5) in Jaffrey, Rindge Memorial School (preschool to grade 5) in Rindge, and Conant Middle High School (grades 6–12) in Jaffrey. Students may take career programs at the Region 14 Applied Technology Center (check where). Several questions contain premises (the Little Orioles child care program, one school psychologist, paraprofessional turnover, a new CTE wing at Conant, about 30% proficient, the default budget again, cuts to sports, electives and the library media associate, a free SAU 47 preschool lottery, buses combined with Rindge's, a 17-to-1 ratio, open enrollment) that the research should check rather than assume.
