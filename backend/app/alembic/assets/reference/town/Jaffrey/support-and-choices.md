# Student Support and School Choice in the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District

Part of the Jaffrey research (town/Jaffrey). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

This file covers student support and school choice for Jaffrey's students in the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District (SAU 47): who does special education evaluations and how the psychologist is paid for, 504 plans and the 504 coordinator, homeschooled and EFA students in Conant's sports, band and classes, what home education and EFA families file, how 6th graders are grouped at Conant Middle High School (CMHS), the phone and bullying rules and how they are enforced, counseling, regulation rooms and suicide prevention, online courses, alternative learning plans and credit loads, building security and drills, the district's March 2026 open enrollment vote, the change-of-school process, and the open enrollment and charter options nearby (ConVal, Mascenic, Lionheart Classical Academy in Peterborough). Facts about the whole district are placed with `district Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative` so the Rindge research can cite them.

**Counts:** 11 topics, 70 facts, 2 number tables (`jrcsd_psychological_services_budget`, `jrcsd_2026_open_enrollment_vote`). Raw copies are in `raw/support/` (about 39 MB, most of it the scanned deliberative session minutes and the ConVal annual report).

**Main sources:** the CMHS 2026-27 Learner & Guardian Handbook, Discipline Handbook, Program of Studies and Activities/Athletics Handbook; the district's 2025-26 organizational charts (September 2025); the FY27 Student Services line-item budget (draft 3, January 2026); 21 district policies from the board's policy list (JJJ, IHBG, JICJ, JICK, JLD, JLDBB, JLCK, ACE, IHBA, IHBAA, IHBH, IHBI, IMBA, JFAB, JCA, ECA, KI, EBB, EBCA, EBCB, JJIB); the 2026 warrant, the district clerk's March 10, 2026 results and the February 4, 2026 deliberative session minutes; the November 22, 2025 board minutes and the October 19, 2026 agenda; the SAU 47 home school page; ConVal's 2026 warrant (in its annual report); Lionheart Classical Academy's and VLACS's own pages; and the JGS 2025-26 handbook for elementary security.

**Main gaps:** no source gives how long Jaffrey-Rindge evaluations take (NH DOE's Indicator 11 data refuse automated requests); no Jaffrey-Rindge 504 procedure says how plans pass from middle to high school teachers; no source says whether 6th graders ride separate buses or use separate hallways; no source measures how consistently the phone and bullying rules are enforced; Mascenic's own open enrollment documents sit in a Google Drive folder that requires sign-in, so its article is known only from local news; ConVal's and Mascenic's tuition policies for paying families were not found; the November 16, 2026 student services budget presentation exists only as a video.

**Proposed new shared topic:** none. All 11 topics are existing shared topics.

**Spot checks:** four facts were re-checked against the raw copies and matched: the open enrollment article's wording and its 1,416–1,168 result (warrant p. 4 and clerk's report, both transcribed); the psychological services lines of $195,930, $210,000 and $213,120 (student services budget, p. 1); the phone policy's exemption for IEP and 504 devices (policy JICJ, section C); and the 8-credit minimum for freshmen (Program of Studies, "Minimum Credits").
