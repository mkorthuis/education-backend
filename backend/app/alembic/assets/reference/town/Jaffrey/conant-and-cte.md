# Conant Middle High School and Career and Technical Education

Part of the Jaffrey research (town/Jaffrey). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Conant Middle High School (CMHS, grades 6–12, in Jaffrey), the Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative School District's only secondary school, and how its students reach career and technical education (CTE): the Region 14 Applied Technology Center (ATC) at ConVal Regional High School in Peterborough, Mascenic Regional High School in New Ipswich, and the Cheshire Career Center in Keene. It covers the defeated 2025 CTE addition at Conant and the end of Conant's own construction trades program; Region 14's programs, credentials, application deadlines and seat sharing, and what CTE tuition and buses cost the district; the 2025 and 2026 budget cuts to athletics and activities and what runs in 2026-27; sports fees and fundraising; AP, honors, Early College, ELOs, work release and early high school courses for middle schoolers; music, art, drama and clubs; counseling, college and FAFSA help and the library; grading and work habits; outside recognition the school cites; and business partnerships. Rindge's research follows and will cite this file, so district-wide facts are placed with the district.

- **13 fact topics, 86 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 37 MB): 25 sets of school board and hearing minutes from January 2025 to November 2026 (scanned PDFs, with text made by optical character recognition; three passages checked against the page images), four district newsletters on CTE (July 2023 to September 2024), the CMHS 2025-26 and 2026-27 Programs of Studies, the CMHS CTE and career-pathway pages, the 2026-27 Activities/Athletics and Parent-Learner Handbooks, the 2022-23 school profile, the ELO handbook, the FAFSA letter, the 2025-26 clubs list, the staff directory, ConVal's 2025-26 Region 14 Program of Studies, four Region 14 ATC pages, the College Board's 2025 New Hampshire AP School Honor Roll, the EDies award pages, RSA 193:26-a, a Ledger-Transcript article with the 2025 CTE vote, and the three schools' rows of the federal 2023-24 Civil Rights Data Collection.
- **Places:** 80 facts use `district Jaffrey-Rindge Cooperative` and 6 use `district Contoocook Valley` (Region 14 ATC facts). No state rules were written; the state CTE, dual enrollment, graduation and EFA rules are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **New data concepts:** the 2025 CTE addition's cost, state share and vote by town; Conant becoming a Region 14 "sending school only" (December 2025) and CTE "open access" tuition; ConVal's 25% seat reserve for Conant; CTE tuition and bus costs (about $900,000 in 2026-27; about $40,000 a bus run) and the minibus purchase; welding certification through Region 14's Manufacturing III; what was cut from and restored to athletics in 2025 and 2026; the 2026-27 fall sports list; the board's rules for donations and fees; course weighting; the College Board AP Honor Roll and the EDies award; a three-school CRDC comparison of AP and dual enrollment.
