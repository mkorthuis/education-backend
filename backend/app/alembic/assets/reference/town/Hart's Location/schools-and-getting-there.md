# Schools and Getting There: JBES, Kennett and the Road Through Crawford Notch

Part of the Hart's Location research (town/Hart's Location). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the schools Hart's Location students attend and how they get there: the contract that assigns kindergarten to grade 8 to Josiah Bartlett Elementary School (JBES) in Bartlett, the high school pattern (Kennett High School or Fryeburg Academy, case by case), transportation through and below Crawford Notch, storms, the ski program, homeschool access, the school resource officer at Kennett, special education counts at JBES, how a rating site scores Kennett, and volunteering and substitute teaching in SAU 9.

Most assigned questions were already answered by the Bartlett, Jackson, Conway and SAU 9 research. The question table names those facts by file, slug and number. New facts here are Hart's Location angles or fill earlier gaps.

- **11 topics, 50 facts and 1 number table.** All topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`. Raw copies are in `raw/schools/` (about 13 MB).
- **Places:** 18 facts use `district Hart's Location`, 12 `district Bartlett`, 12 `district Conway`, 5 `sau 9` and 2 `town Hart's Location`; 1 fact (an NHIAA by-law) has no place. The backend parser (`parse_file`) currently rejects `sau 9` places; they follow this town's brief.
- **Main sources:** Hart's Location School Board minutes (21 sets, September 2022 to the September 9, 2026 draft), its 2026 annual report and its posted policies; the 2010 Bartlett–Hart's Location tuition agreement (read from page images); SAU 9's transportation, cancellation, tuition and volunteer/substitute pages; Bartlett and Conway substitute handbooks for 2026-27; Bartlett policies EEA and JJJ; Conway School Board minutes and its FY2026 budget; Kennett's 2026-27 Program of Studies and handbook; SAU 9's Fall 2024 Report Card and Spring 2025 newsletter; the Eastern Slope Ski Club's pages; NHIAA's 2026-27 eligibility by-laws; an archived NHDOT news release; GreatSchools.
- **New data concepts:** derived road distances and drive times from Hart's Location to JBES, Kennett and Fryeburg Academy; the daily mileage implied by Hart's Location's 2026-27 reimbursement plan; Bartlett's busing rule for children whose parents live apart; substitute day rates; Kennett's school resource officer agreements and budget line.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "SAU #9 bus transportation through Crawford Notch to Bartlett" (P1.2): no SAU 9 route has a stop in Hart's Location. The contract makes Hart's Location responsible for transportation. Hart's Location spent $0 on pupil transportation in 2024-25, when it had one student at JBES; how that student traveled is not recorded. For 2026-27 it budgeted mileage for a family driving to Fryeburg Academy.
  - "Thursday afternoon Ski & Ride program" (P11.2): partly right. In 2024-25 JBES grades 1–6 skied with the Eastern Slope Ski Club (ESSC) on Thursdays, leaving after lunch. ESSC offers snowboarding only in grades 5–6. No 2026-27 day or fee is published.
  - "Hart's Location tuition them to JBES or directly to Kennett Middle School" for grades 7–8 (P5.1): the contract covers grades K–8 at JBES, and Hart's Location has no grade 7–8 contract with Conway. SAU 9's tuition page files the contract under "K-6."
  - "Kennett High School, where Hart's Location high schoolers go" (P8.1): Hart's Location has no high school contract. Its board says it has sent students to both Kennett and Fryeburg Academy, and its 2026-27 budget plans for Fryeburg.
  - "Parent-to-staff ratio for special education" at JBES (P13.3): no such ratio, or any caseload figure, is published.
  - "Single route all the way to Redstone/Conway" (P17.3): Kennett is in the Redstone area of Conway. Bartlett's afternoon Kennett runs include a transfer at the Intervale overlook (Bartlett file).
  - "Does SAU #9 offer high-speed broadband" (P8.3): no SAU 9 broadband, hotspot or home-internet program was found (Conway file).
- **Main gaps:** how Hart's Location's JBES student travels; any Hart's Location storm procedure; ESSC fees and the 2026-27 ski day; current Niche and U.S. News pages for Kennett; any Kennett emergency-plan link with the Carroll County Sheriff or State Police; special education caseloads; whether RSA 193:1-c access reaches a tuition district's school for a Hart's Location homeschooler.
