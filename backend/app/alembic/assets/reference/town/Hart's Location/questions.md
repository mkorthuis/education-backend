# Hart's Location Residents' Questions

The questions the Hart's Location research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hart's Location runs no schools. Its K–8 students attend Josiah Bartlett Elementary School (JBES) in Bartlett under a tuition contract; its high school arrangements are less clear (the Bartlett research found a high school tuition line, mileage paid for one student at Fryeburg Academy, and no posted high school contract). Several questions contain premises (grades, meeting times, programs, figures) that the research should check rather than assume.
