# Hollis-Brookline High School and Career Pathways for Brookline Students

Part of the Brookline research (town/Brookline). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Brookline students find at Hollis-Brookline High School (HBHS, grades 9–12, run by the Hollis-Brookline Cooperative in SAU 41): STEM courses set beside one large Massachusetts high school, the seven HBHS courses that can earn college credit, how a two-year career program fits the 23-credit diploma, the drive to the Nashua and Hudson career centers, how counselors work with each class and what they cover (trade schools, two-year colleges and the military among them), students per counselor against state and national figures, attendance and tardiness rules, the "Portrait of a Cavalier" and HBHS's accreditation review, how local businesses take part (ELOs, work-study, robotics sponsors), the FIRST robotics team, the turf field, the unusable track and the cooperative's capital plan, Brookline's afternoon bus runs (no late bus), local scholarships for Brookline and HBHS seniors, and why New Hampshire's public colleges cost more and graduates borrow more.

- **Counts:** 13 topics, 42 facts and 3 number tables. Places: `district Hollis-Brookline Cooperative` 34 facts, `town Brookline` 2, no place 6.
