# Richard Maghakian Memorial School, Captain Samuel Douglass Academy and Young Families

Part of the Brookline research (town/Brookline). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Brookline School District's two schools: Richard Maghakian Memorial School (RMMS, preschool to grade 3, 22 Milford Street) and Captain Samuel Douglass Academy (CSDA, grades 4–6, 24 Townsend Hill Road), and the daily life of young Brookline families.
- **What it covers:**
  - school hours, arrival and dismissal, absence reporting, delays, early releases and the 2026-27 calendar;
  - the Brookline Afterschool Program's before- and after-school care at both schools (hours, 2026-27 rates, delay and early-release rules);
  - the preschool's waitlist, services and drop-off;
  - kindergarten placement and registration answers;
  - the 2026-27 Brookline elementary and cooperative bus routes, ride lengths and how the two morning waves are staggered;
  - CSDA's carpool changes;
  - class sizes by grade in November 2026, enrollment history and NESDEC projections, staffing and FY28 draft changes;
  - building space: the 2022 building aid applications, CSDA's portables and the 2025-26 Facilities Committee;
  - reading and math programs, the switch from Aimsweb to Star, reading specialists, homework limits and accelerated grade 6 math;
  - special education leadership and related-service staff, counseling and social-emotional lessons;
  - Chromebooks, screen time and phone rules;
  - meal prices, the free and reduced-price application and the district's rules against singling children out;
  - state test results compared with Hollis, Amherst and the state.
