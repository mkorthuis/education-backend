# Money and Governance in Brookline

Part of the Brookline research (town/Brookline). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered.** Brookline's school taxes and state aid: the 2026 tax rate ($25.56, with the local school rate down to $17.52 from $17.81 while the town rate rose), how the Brookline School District turns its budget and aid into a tax effort, the new fiscal capacity disparity aid in FY2027 and the smaller FY2028 aid estimate, the district's own 8% tax cap (2019), the failed 2025 attempt to repeal it, the new HB 1300 cap Brookline adopted in November 2026, and the FY2028 cap estimates; the FY2027 budget (the operating budget defaulted, so a teacher, an interventionist, a half-time case manager and the RMMS roof were cut, and the roof and paving were later paid from fund balance); the FY2028 drafts (Round 1 $15.8 million, Round 2 $14.45 million with four position cuts to fit under the HB 1300 cap); special education costs and the move from contracted to district-employed paraeducators; health insurance (a 15.1% rate rise for 2026-27, no guaranteed maximum rate, new lower-premium plan options, and the Brookline health line rising 26.9% in the FY2028 draft); the 2026–2029 teachers' contract, its new salary schedule, turnover and the district's comparison with Amherst, Bedford, Hollis and Milford; the support staff contract; the board, Finance Committee, SB2 votes and what the 2025 and 2026 deliberative sessions changed; the RMMS and CSDA buildings, the energy lease, the maintenance trust and the Facilities Committee; the 2026-27 calendars; the elderly exemption; the commercial share of the tax base and the commercial construction exemption; and the 2023 revaluation.

**Counts.** 8 topics (all shared), 74 facts, 5 number tables.

**Main sources.** SAU 41's Brookline budget pages (FY2027 hearing and deliberative presentations, warrant, district reports, default budget detail, mandatory report to voters); Diligent agendas and attachments (FY2028 Rounds 1 and 2, tax cap estimates, revenue and expense reports, default budget cuts, the special education overview, a business office memo); the Brookline Teachers' Association (2026–2029) and support staff (2026–2028) contracts; SAU 41 health insurance MOUs; cooperative board minutes of November 12, 2025 and October 21, 2026 (the HB 1300 hearing); the Town of Brookline's 2025 annual report (2025 school ballot results, the 2025 deliberative session minutes, the MS-1 summary, special education costs), its 2026 tax rate sheet, tax rate history, elderly and commercial exemption sheets, and the 2023 revaluation manual; the 2026-27 calendars and the 2026-27 Brookline handbook; the Brookline Facilities Committee's charter, agendas and minutes.

**Main gaps.** The official results of the March 10, 2026 Brookline school ballot (the operating budget's failure is shown by district documents, but the votes on the SB2 repeal, board size, fund balance, budget cap and tax cap petitions were not found); minutes of the 2026 deliberative session, of 2026 Brookline board meetings and of the cooperative budget committee's fall 2026 meetings; the $33.11 million cooperative figure; how Brookline's 2026 local school rate splits between its own district and the cooperative (DRA's sheet); Brookline home sale prices; Merrimack in the district's pay comparison.
