# Brookline Residents' Questions

The questions the Brookline research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Brookline is in SAU 41 with Hollis. The Brookline School District runs Richard Maghakian Memorial School (preschool to grade 3) and Captain Samuel Douglass Academy (grades 4–6); grades 7–12 attend the Hollis-Brookline Cooperative's middle and high schools in Hollis, researched in `../Hollis/`. Several questions contain premises (extra state adequacy aid for Brookline, two separate school budgets, a $33.11 million co-op placeholder budget, health insurance jumping, about 750 students at HBHS, Latin Honors, an 8:25 start, the Route 130 drop-off line, 50+ clubs) that the research should check rather than assume.
