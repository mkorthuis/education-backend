# Hinsdale: Money, the School Tax Cap, Buildings and SAU 92

Part of the Hinsdale research (town/Hinsdale). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 11 topics, 64 facts and 5 number tables on the Hinsdale School District's 2026-27 budget and what changed in it, the 2027-28 budget now being built under the school tax cap Hinsdale voters adopted in November 2026, what an override would mean for a tax bill, cost per pupil, unpaid meal charges, assessed value, building repairs and trust funds, bus costs, SAU 92's administrators and what it shares with Winchester, where the budget documents are and when residents can speak, high school accreditation, tuitioning, consolidation, and whether families leave before middle school.

- **The budget.** Voters passed a $15,967,262 operating article on March 14, 2026 ($15,068,262 before $899,000 of grant and food service transfers), 2.59% below 2025-26. Most of the drop is the last payment on the 2005 middle-high school bond; the 2026-27 budget cut one elementary teaching position and replaced a retiring speech pathologist with an assistant, but added sports teams, tech ed supplies, career center seats and a Dean of Students.
- **The cap.** Question 2 passed in Hinsdale, so the 2027-28 budget (voted March 2027) is the first capped one. The district's October 27 briefing estimated the 2026 local school tax at $7,602,352 and, with hypothetical 3.6% inflation, a cap allowing $273,685 more, against $821,832 of contractual increases (health insurance up 26%, at least $639,601), an example gap of $548,147. An override needs a 60% ballot vote at the annual meeting. Each $434,977 raised moves the tax rate $1.00 per $1,000.
- **Lunch debt.** The district's "bad debt" balance was $54,234 on November 15, 2025, up from $0 in June 2022; the general fund (mostly property tax) has covered part of it ($11,606 in 2024-25).
- **Buildings.** All 12 middle-high HVAC units (about 20 years old) and 6 of 8 elementary units need replacing, some roof sections are "in poor shape," and the gym windows are 60+ years old; the maintenance trust held $110,338.50 in June 2025.
- **SAU 92.** A single-district SAU. Its superintendent, Dr. David Ryan, is a 0.6 position in Hinsdale's budget and also Winchester's superintendent; the business administrator and technology director are shared too. Central office spending was 4.11% of appropriations in 2026-27, under the new 6% cap.

**Main sources:** Hinsdale School District's Financial Information page (2026-27 budget book, transcribed from scanned pages; 2024-25 audit; DRA's 2025 tax rate), school board minutes and packets January 2022 to November 10, 2026, the October 27, 2026 HB 1300 community briefing, the 2023, 2025 and 2026 district meeting minutes, the Winchester tuition agreement committee page, the Town of Hinsdale Budget Committee's 2026 minutes and the Select Board's October 26, 2026 minutes, NEASC's directory data, and the site's database for derived comparisons.
