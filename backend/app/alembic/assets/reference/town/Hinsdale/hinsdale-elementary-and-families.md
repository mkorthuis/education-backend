# Hinsdale Elementary and Young Families

Part of the Hinsdale research (town/Hinsdale). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hinsdale Elementary School (HES, preschool to grade 5) and the questions young families asked: the principal changes, staff and class sizes, school hours, the Hinsdale After School Program (HASP) before and after school, the drive to Brattleboro, kindergarten's day, elementary buses, meals and the meal application, special education, speech and paraprofessionals, reading help, counseling, the PowerSchool parent portal and alerts, the preschool and its waitlist, walking home, building security and visitor rules, and summer programs in town.

**Counts:** 13 topics (11 shared slugs and 2 library slugs: `kindergarten-day-length`, `district-preschool`), 68 facts, no number tables.

**Main sources:** the HES Student/Caregiver Handbook 2026-2027; the district's "District Information for Parents" page; the HES home page and staff list (November 2026); the September 11, 2026 interim principal posting; Hinsdale School Board minutes from January 2024 to the November 10, 2026 draft (public minutes, plus excerpts of four posted non-public minutes); the 2026 annual district meeting minutes; the HASP brochure (Fall 2024, transcribed from image), registration form, 2021-22 parent handbook and policy guide; the 2026-27 calendar and free and reduced-price meal application; the 2025–2030 strategic plan; the HES Title I schoolwide plan (2022-23); the HES PowerSchool parent guide (2013-14); the 2022 emergency letter to parents; the Town of Hinsdale's Parks and Recreation and library pages, three 2026 Recreation Committee minutes and the March 2026 library newsletter; NH DOE's September 2026 21st CCLC list (library raw copy); and OpenStreetMap routing for the drive to Brattleboro.

**Main gaps:** no 2026-27 class sizes are published (the site's data has 2025-26); no elementary bus schedule for 2026-27 is posted; no "$1.6 million" cut appears in any board minutes through November 10, 2026 (the 2027-28 budget is built in December 2026); the public minutes give no reason for the principal change; HASP's 2026-27 fee and summer 2026 schedule are not posted; the district's policies (class size IIB, transportation, visitors) sit on a SharePoint site that could not be read; nothing was found on private reading tutors in Keene or Brattleboro, on NH DOE's special education determination for Hinsdale, or on Vermont's pre-K rules for New Hampshire children.

**No new topics proposed.** All facts use shared or library slugs.

**Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 16. P4.3 and P12.2 lean most on the library (school-meals-eligibility 1–11; district-preschool 45), with local facts added here.
