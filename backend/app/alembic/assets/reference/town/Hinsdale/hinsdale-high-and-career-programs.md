# Hinsdale High School and Career Programs

Part of the Hinsdale research (town/Hinsdale). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Hinsdale High School (part of Hinsdale Middle High School, HMHS) and the career programs its students use: where Hinsdale students take career and technical education (the Windham Regional Career Center in Brattleboro, Vermont, since 2025-26, after the district left Keene's Cheshire Career Center), how they get there and how it fits with sports; HMHS's AP courses (on site and through the AP Rural Collaborative Network), River Valley Community College courses taught at the school, VLACS and other online and college options; pathways, extended learning opportunities (ELOs) and local work placements; early graduation and alternative credit; world languages, arts and computer science; graduation requirements for the class of 2030; sports teams and their NHIAA divisions; the school resource officer; fees; buses after school; college and financial aid help; graduates' reported plans; and the 2026–2033 Winchester tuition agreement from Hinsdale's side.

## cte-admission-and-cost-rules: Out-of-State Career Centers and Transportation in New Hampshire Law

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: State rules on who may enroll in a regional career and technical education center (including home-educated, charter and private school students), space, prerequisites and age, and who pays tuition and transportation

1. New Hampshire law lets the education commissioner designate "an out-of-state school or program ... when it is in the best interest of the state, as a part of the New Hampshire regional career and technical education plan," and makes the sending district responsible for providing and paying for transportation to regional career and technical centers, which the state then reimburses.

RSA 188-E:8: "The sending district shall be responsible for providing transportation and paying the transportation costs and shall then be reimbursed from state funds" (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/188-E/188-E-8.htm).
