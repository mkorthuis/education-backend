# Hinsdale: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Hinsdale, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the ninth wave of the second ToLoad batch. Hinsdale is SAU 92, which shares its superintendent, business administrator and technology director with Winchester:
- **Hinsdale Elementary** serves preschool to grade 5.
- **Hinsdale Middle High** serves grades 6–12 in one building. Under a 2026–2033 agreement it is Winchester's high school from the class of 2030.
- **Career programs:** since 2025-26, the Windham Regional Career Center (WRCC) in Brattleboro, Vermont, instead of Keene's Cheshire Career Center.

The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting. Keene was researched in `../Keene/`; Winchester's research, running alongside, cites the agreement facts here.
