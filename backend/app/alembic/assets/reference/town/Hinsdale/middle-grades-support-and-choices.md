# Hinsdale Middle Grades: Support and Choices

Part of the Hinsdale research (town/Hinsdale). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers the middle grades at Hinsdale Middle High School (HMHS, grades 6–12, one building) for agent 2's questions: math results and the help offered, the school's new federal improvement status, comparisons with Winchester and Brattleboro, access for homeschooled and EFA students, speech evaluations for children not enrolled, moving up or returning in 6th grade, what sharing a building with high schoolers means for lunch and the day, storm closings and delays, teacher turnover and pay, the PowerSchool parent portal, band, chorus and art, the bullying policy and parent notice, and laptops, online programs and phones.

**Counts:** 15 topics (14 local topics with `place: district Hinsdale` and 1 rule topic with no place, added to the library's `special-ed-evaluation-request`), 93 facts, 4 number tables.

**Main sources:** the HMHS 2026–2027 Student Handbook (updated August 27, 2026), the 2026–27 district calendar, the HMHS 2026–27 School Profile and Program of Studies, the district's "District Information for Parents," curriculum (arts, digital literacy, advisory) and Middle School HASP pages, the HMHS website and staff list, Hinsdale School Board minutes (February 2025 to the November 10, 2026 draft) and board packets (September 2025 to November 10, 2026: superintendent's, principal's, technology and student services reports; policies JJJ, JICK, JG, JF, IMBD, IC, JICL and EHAG; the 2025–2030 strategic plan), NH DOE test results as loaded in the site's database, Vermont's 2025 and 2026 assessment datasets (data.vermont.gov), and New Hampshire's special education rules (Ed 1100).

   - citation: N.H. Code Admin. Rules Ed 1105.01(b), (c)(2)
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rules/state_agencies/ed1100.html
   - as_of: 2026-11-22

"(2) The LEA, using the special education process, shall find, identify, and evaluate all children suspected to be children with disabilities who are 2.5 years of age or older but less than 22 years of age." The rule speaks of children "residing within its jurisdiction," not only enrolled students.
