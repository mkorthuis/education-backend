# Hinsdale Residents' Questions

The questions the Hinsdale research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Hinsdale is SAU 92. The Hinsdale School District runs Hinsdale Elementary School (preschool to grade 5) and Hinsdale Middle High School (grades 6–12), and from fall 2026 its high school is Winchester's anchor high school too. The district decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting. Several questions contain premises ($1.6 million in cuts, a tax cap allowing about $228,000 more, $66,000 in unpaid lunch debt, a risk to accreditation, a $2.5 million drop in assessed value, a new school resource officer) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Sarah, 34 — Nurse commuting to Brattleboro, mom of a 2nd grader at Hinsdale Elementary

- **P1.1** If the district has to cut $1.6 million next year, are my kid's class sizes going to blow up?
- **P1.2** Who's actually going to be the permanent principal at the elementary school, and why has there been turnover?
- **P1.3** Are there reliable before- and after-school care options in town for parents who have to be across the bridge in Brattleboro by 7:00 AM?
