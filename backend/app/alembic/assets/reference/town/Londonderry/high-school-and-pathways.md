# Londonderry High School and Pathways

Part of the Londonderry research (town/Londonderry). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Londonderry High School (LHS): its AP, honors and college-credit courses and how many students take them, computer science and engineering courses and the 2026-27 catalog changes, course cancellations, career and technical education (CTE) at the Manchester School of Technology (MST) and the Palmer center at Alvirne High School in Hudson, hands-on courses before high school at Londonderry Middle School (LMS), published results set beside Pinkerton Academy and Bedford High School, school counseling and college planning, excused absences and college visits, the Open Campus privilege and how the school tracks students, and the charter and private high schools near Londonderry.

- **7 fact topics, 79 facts and 6 tables.** All topics are shared town topics. Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 20 MB): LHS's 2026-27 and 2025-26 Programs of Studies and student handbooks (Google Docs, exported as text), the 2026-27 LHS school profile, the district's 2025 "A Year in Review," policies JH-R, IHCD and IMBC, 15 school board minutes from 2025–2026 (`raw/high/minutes/`), the LHS clubs list and 2026-27 announcement feed (staff email column removed), the school counseling site, LMS's Program of Study and technology page, the Palmer CTE admissions page, Bedford High School's 2026-27 profile (scanned; transcribed), an archived LHS home page (Wayback, March 8, 2026) used only to identify the 2025-26 handbook, and private and charter school pages (`raw/high/choices/`).
- **Places:** most facts use `district Londonderry`. Others use `district Bedford`, `district Hudson` (Palmer center), `district Pinkerton Academy`, `district Academy for Science and Design Charter School`, `district Next Charter School`, `district Spark Academy of Advanced Technologies`, `district The Founders Academy Charter School`, `district Diocese of Manchester` (Trinity, Holy Family), `district The Derryfield School` and `town Londonderry` (the nearby-schools list, and the Arts Academy of New Hampshire, whose `district` name has a trailing space).
- **New data concepts:** LHS's AP exams and pass rates by year (2020-21 to 2025-26), AP courses that ran against those listed; Early College and SNHU dual-credit course lists and fees; course changes between the 2025-26 and 2026-27 catalogs (robotics, digital electronics and civil engineering courses dropped); the CTE partner programs open to LHS students and the application timeline; LMS hands-on courses and clubs; counselor caseloads at LHS, Pinkerton and Bedford; LHS, Pinkerton and Bedford school-profile results side by side, plus the federal Civil Rights Data Collection's (CRDC) AP and computer science counts; the excused-absence list and the senior college-visit allowance; the 2025-26 and 2026-27 Open Campus wording; and tuition and lotteries at nearby private and charter high schools.
