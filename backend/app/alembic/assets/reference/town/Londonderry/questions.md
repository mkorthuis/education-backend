# Londonderry Residents' Questions

The questions the Londonderry research is meant to answer. Thirty resident profiles, three questions each (90 in all, half again the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. The profiles came with first names only. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Londonderry is SAU 12. The Londonderry School District runs Moose Hill School (the LEEP preschool), three elementary schools for kindergarten to grade 5 (Matthew Thornton, North Londonderry and South Londonderry, with full-day kindergarten from 2026-27), Londonderry Middle School (grades 6–8) and Londonderry High School. Hooksett students may attend Londonderry High by petition. The district holds a deliberative session and then a ballot vote, with a default budget if the proposed one fails, and a school tax cap question went to voters on November 3, 2026. Several questions contain premises (budget totals, contract costs, a failed tax cap, district offices at Moose Hill, modular classrooms, an Open Campus change, i-Ready results) that the research should check rather than assume.
