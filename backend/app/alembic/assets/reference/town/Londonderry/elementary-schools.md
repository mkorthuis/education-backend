# Londonderry's Elementary Schools and the Early Years

Part of the Londonderry research (town/Londonderry). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Londonderry School District's three kindergarten-to-grade-5 schools (Matthew Thornton, North and South) and its preschool, the Londonderry Early Education Program (LEEP) at Moose Hill School: where full-day kindergarten went in fall 2026 and what happened to the portable classrooms, class sizes and the kindergarten cap, before- and after-school care and its waitlists, enrichment and advanced math, how the three schools' test results compare over five years, i-Ready and early reading screening, Chromebooks and screen time in the early grades, recess, summer programs and help for students who struggle, and LEEP's admission, evaluations (Child Check), hours, tuition, transportation and a proposed full-day preschool.

- **9 topics, 63 facts and 2 number tables.** Eight topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; one new shared topic is proposed: `extended-school-year-rules` (rule, rules: "What New Hampshire's special education rules say about extended school year services: who decides, and that they are not limited to summer"), with one fact. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 44 MB): district, school and Moose Hill pages and handbooks; the curriculum hub and master schedule outline; the full-day kindergarten maps; LEEP's tuition sheet, application and calendar; the YMCA's program page and rate sheet; policies IHCA, IFA and IMAH; the 2025–2028 teachers' agreement (scanned; read by OCR); `minutes/` (11 school board minutes, 2025–2026); `docs/` (the 2022 and 2023 kindergarten committee documents and the district's 2025 Year in Review); and `data/` (NH DOE test-result rows, 2022–2026, for the three schools, the district and the state).
- **Sources.** Nearly everything comes from the district's own sites (londonderry.org, the school and Moose Hill sites, the Family Curriculum Hub, news.londonderry.org) and the Google Drive files they link, plus school board minutes. Before- and after-school care facts come from the Granite YMCA's own registration page and rate sheet. Test results come from NH DOE's files as copied in the site's backend. No news source was needed.
- **Main gaps:** the district publishes no i-Ready results by grade or school after spring 2025, so whether full-day kindergarten has changed 1st-grade results can't be measured yet (the first full-day class reaches 1st grade in 2027-28); how many LEEP seats go to peers without IEPs, and how many families were on the lottery list; total daily screen minutes in grade 2; whether South's recess has changed with kindergarten in the building; which private preschools prepare children "best" (no source rates that). Two SharePoint letters (the 2026 kindergarten and 1st grade info night letter, the 2025 LEEP application notice) and the Family Resource Guide's before- and after-school page could not be opened (sign-in).
- **Spot-checked** against raw copies: the kindergarten cap ("The kindergarten classrooms are capped at 18 students", `minutes/7-27-2026_Minutes.txt`); South's after-school status ("23 weeks open, 4 wait-listed", `ymca-school-age-child-care.txt`); LEEP's 4-day tuition ("$210.00 ($168.00)", `leep-tuition-2026-27.txt`); and South's October 31, 2026 grade 1 row ("SS 1 3 25 74 68", `additional-data/raw/districts/batch-4/londonderry-enrollment-2026-10-31.txt`). ## district-curriculum-and-assessment: Reading and Math Programs, Early Screening and i-Ready in Londonderry's Elementary Schools

- kind: local
- question_type: local_practice
- description: The curriculum programs a district uses (math, reading, science), how reading is taught and supported, its local tests, and how teachers fit local content into lessons

1. Londonderry's elementary schools screen every child in kindergarten through grade 4 with Acadience Reading, a short one-on-one test given by the classroom teacher or reading staff at the beginning, middle and end of the year to find children at risk for reading difficulties; when a result shows risk, the teacher and the school's interventionists decide next steps, and children receiving intervention or special education are checked more often.
   - citation: Londonderry Elementary Schools Family Curriculum Hub, "Assessments, Diagnostics & Screeners"
   - source: https://sites.google.com/londonderry.org/familycurriculumhub/assessments-diagnostics-screeners
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-11-15
   - place: district Londonderry
   - note: "Acadience Reading Online is a universal screener. We use Acadience in grades K-4 to identify students who are at risk for reading difficulties ... Students complete the benchmark assessments at the beginning, middle and end of the year, these are very quick and skill focused and complete 1 on 1 with the classroom teacher or our reading department ... After a benchmark assessment, if there is an indicator of risk, the classroom teacher and interventionists would determine next steps." ## extended-school-year-rules: Extended School Year Services for Students With IEPs

- kind: rule
- question_type: rules
- description: What New Hampshire's special education rules say about extended school year services: who decides, and that they are not limited to summer

1. Under New Hampshire's special education rules, a school district must provide extended school year services, special education beyond the regular school year, when a child's IEP team decides they are needed for the child's free appropriate public education, and these services may not be limited to the summer months or to a set program design.
   - citation: N.H. Admin. Rules, Ed 1110.01(a)–(b); 34 CFR 300.106
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rules/state_agencies/ed1100.html
   - as_of: 2026-11-15
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: "The LEA shall provide extended school year services at times during the year when school is not in session, if determined by the IEP team to be necessary for the provision of FAPE. Extended school year services shall not be limited to the summer months or to predetermined program design." Proposed new shared topic. Only students with IEPs are covered; Londonderry's own summer offerings are in district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners. Raw: raw/elementary/nh-rules-ed1100.txt (lines near "Ed 1110.01").
