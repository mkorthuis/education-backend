## What you need to know about Londonderry

- **Londonderry** is a town in Rockingham County, between Derry, Hudson, Litchfield, Manchester, Auburn and Windham, along I-93 (Exits 4 and 5) and next to the Manchester-Boston airport; Woodmont Commons is its large mixed-use development. Site town id 319. It has a town council and a town manager; the school district is separate.
- **Londonderry School District** (site district id 319) is **SAU 12**. Use `district Londonderry` for SAU-level facts too. It holds a deliberative session (Friday, February 6, 2026) and then a ballot vote (March 10, 2026), with a default budget if the proposed one fails. A school tax cap question went to voters on November 3, 2026: 6,138 yes to 5,678 no (51.9%), short of the 60% it needed (the site's Question 2 data; unofficial).
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - **Moose Hill School** (27905): the Londonderry Early Education Program (LEEP) preschool. Kindergarten was here until 2025-26. Find out whether the district offices have moved into the building.
  - **Kindergarten to grade 5:** Matthew Thornton (20485), North Londonderry (20470) and South Londonderry (20475), with full-day kindergarten from 2026-27 (voters approved it 3,255 to 1,369 on March 10, 2026).
  - **Londonderry Middle School** (20480, grades 6–8) and **Londonderry High School** (20490, grades 9–12; the site calls it Londonderry Senior High School). The district's enrollment report also lists **Lancer Academy** and 18–22-year-olds.
- **Hooksett** students may attend Londonderry High by petition, under a 2023–2028 memorandum: up to 40 a grade, with Hooksett paying Pinkerton's rate (in `places.yaml`; Hooksett's school of record is Pinkerton).
- **Starting pages:** the district, https://www.londonderry.org, and its news site https://news.londonderry.org (superintendent's newsletters, the budget, the tax cap, board news); the town, https://www.londonderrynh.gov (elections, the town budget, the tax rate, recreation, the library, police).
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - The district's October 31, 2026 enrollment report (saved, below): Matthew Thornton 643 (six kindergarten classes averaging 18), North 498, South 481, Moose Hill 121 (preschool), the middle school 870, the high school 1,208 (plus 20 aged 18–22), 22 students placed out of district, 3,843 in all (down 0.6%), and 129 home-educated students.
  - Enrollment by town of residence (the site's town enrollment): 4,408 Londonderry students in 2013-14, 3,848 in 2023-24 and 3,714 in 2025-26.
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Londonderry $21,196; state $22,699; Litchfield $21,147; Hudson $19,704; Windham $19,690; Bedford $19,650; Hooksett $17,848.
  - The site also has every school's enrollment by grade, test scores (with growth where NH DOE reports it), class sizes, staff and salary schedules, free and reduced-price meals, English learners, safety counts, graduation and plans, licensed child care in the town, EFAs by town, Question 2 results, Census estimates, adequacy aid, special education aid, tax rates and school taxes. Point to these; don't restate them.
