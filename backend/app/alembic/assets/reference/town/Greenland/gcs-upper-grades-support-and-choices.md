# Greenland Central's Upper Grades, Support and Choices

Part of the Greenland research (town/Greenland). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Greenland Central School (GCS, kindergarten to grade 8) from grade 5 up, and the support and choices around it: how the school is grouped and how the move into the middle grades works, advisory and class placement, the math program and what is posted about advanced math, Encore electives and other hands-on classes, robotics, music and band, sports and away-game buses, special education practice, counselors and the social worker position, bullying procedures, devices and phones, homeschool access, and EFAs, Seacoast Charter and other nearby options. It also adds two facts on Portsmouth High's band that the Portsmouth files lack.

- **11 topics, 80 facts and 3 number tables.** Places: `district Greenland` for Greenland Central; `sau 50` for SAU-wide policies, the special education procedures plan and SAU reports; `district Portsmouth` for Portsmouth High's band.
- **Main sources:** the GCS website (home, About Us, Athletics, Robotics, Mathematics, Unified Arts and Encore, Science, staff page); the 2026-27 Student and Family Handbook (revised May 26, 2026); the 2024-25 annual report; the grade 6, 7 and 8 websites and the "Transition to Middle School at GCS" family presentation; the 2026-27 Encore class list and Encore FAQ; the GCS Counseling Department website; the August 2026 Fall Sports Summary and athletic contract; principal's newsletters (August to November 2026); principal's, special education, technology and assistant superintendent reports to the Greenland School Board (September to November 2026); the FY2028 proposed budget (October 2026); SAU 50's policy list, Home Education page, Special Services page and Special Education Procedures Plan (revised June 2026); SAU 50 policy JICK as posted by GCS; federal Civil Rights Data Collection rows; the EFA program's statistics feed; and OSRM drive times between school addresses.
- **New data concepts:** the grade 5 to grade 6 change in schedule, grading and responsibilities as the school itself describes it; counselor caseloads by grade span; the 2026-27 Encore list; away-game buses and the athletic trips transportation budget; EFA applicants for Greenland in 2026-27; drive times from GCS to nearby charter and private schools.
- **Main gaps:** how well IEP accommodations are carried out (no outside measure found; NH DOE's district determinations page refused the request); full texts of SAU 50 policies IHBG-R, IHBG, JJA and IMBD (the policy page shows summaries only); the time students spend on screens (not published); FY2026 board minutes (downloads disabled by the owner); whether GCS ever offers Algebra I or other accelerated math in grade 8 (no page says so; federal data show none in 2023-24).
- **Proposed new topic:** none.
