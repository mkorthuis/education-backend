# Portsmouth High School From Greenland

Part of the Greenland research (town/Greenland). Gathered November 21–22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Greenland's side of Portsmouth High School (PHS), where Greenland's students in grades 9–12 go under the AREA agreement: the move from Greenland Central School (GCS), the Greenland high school bus and the lack of a late bus, storm delays, access to honors courses and career programs, getting to career and internship placements, IEPs and one-on-one aides at PHS and who pays, readiness for advanced courses, homeschooled teenagers and PHS courses, how PHS reaches families, and dual enrollment. Portsmouth High itself is already researched in `town/Portsmouth/` (middle-high-and-cte.md, student-support.md, getting-to-school-choices-and-staff.md, money-buildings-and-governance.md), Rye's side in `town/Rye/portsmouth-high-from-rye.md`, and Greenland's money, early grades and upper grades in this folder's other three files; those facts are cited in the question table, not repeated. Facts any SAU 50 sending town shares are placed `sau 50` (or `district Portsmouth` for PHS itself).

- **10 fact topics, 29 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 14 MB): the sanitized 2026-27 SAU 50 bus sheet for Greenland students to PHS (house numbers removed; the original wasn't kept) and the route folder listing; SAU 50's transportation, delayed-opening, special education and data dashboard pages; SAU 50 policies BA, EEA, IHBG, IHBG-R, JFAC and JJJ with the policy index; the Class of 2025 "District High School Report" for Greenland and Rye from SAU 50's dashboard; SAU 50 Secondary Advisory/AREA folder listings, the February 4, 2025 agenda and the December 2024 PHS principal's report; SAU 50's special education procedures plan (June 2026) and its March 2024 special education report; Greenland School Board documents (pages of the August 10 and October 27, 2026 minutes, the November 16 draft minutes, the October superintendent's, November assistant superintendent's and September special education reports, the October 2026 special education expense report, and the proposed 2027-28 budget's text with pages 8, 53 and 100); the GCS 2024-25 handbook (counseling excerpt), its grade 7–8 math overview and 8th Grade Information Station; PHS pages fetched November 21, 2026 (Current 8th Graders, 8th Grade Transition, home, Back to School, Equity & Access) and its course-selection, math, CTE, honors and transition documents; Portsmouth policies IHBA, IHBG, JFAB and JJJ; COAST's route and Connector pages; Great Bay's Early College page; and read-only queries of the site's database.
- **Places:** `district Greenland` 19, `district Portsmouth` 4, `sau 50` 4, `town Greenland` 1, `town Portsmouth` 1. The Class of 2025 table also has rows for `district Rye` and `district Portsmouth` (see "For another file").
- **New data concepts:** the Greenland high school bus with stop times and derived ride lengths; what Greenland budgets for high school buses (including a bus to Lister Academy shared with Rye) and for its high school students' IEP services outside tuition; SAU 50's Class of 2025 reports of how Greenland's PHS graduates did (plans, top-ten ranks and SAT means beside the PHS-wide and Rye figures); a grade 8 test comparison of PHS's three public feeder schools; the SAU 50 and Portsmouth access policies that leave a homeschooled Greenland teenager's access to PHS courses unsettled; the two school systems' different parent portals and alert systems.
- **Main gaps:** no late or activity bus exists in any source; the AREA agreement, including its 2009 special education terms, isn't posted, so whether Portsmouth bills Greenland separately for a one-on-one aide can't be confirmed; no source reports how Greenland students are placed in PHS honors or math courses (SAU 50 received PHS placement data in November 2026 but hasn't published it); no posted procedure describes the GCS-to-PHS IEP handoff; no policy says whether a homeschooled Greenland teenager may take a single PHS course; Greenland School Board minutes from 2025-26 can't be downloaded.
- **Proposed new shared topics:** none. One fact is added to the library's `early-college-on-campus` topic with its own kind, question type and description.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** 1 of 12 (P17.3, dual enrollment with Great Bay, by `town/Portsmouth/middle-high-and-cte.md` and the library).
