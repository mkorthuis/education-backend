# Greenland Residents' Questions

The questions the Greenland research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Greenland is in SAU 50 with New Castle, Newington and Rye. The Greenland School District runs Greenland Central School (kindergarten to grade 8), and its high school students attend Portsmouth High School under an AREA agreement, where they may take career programs at the Portsmouth Career Technical Center. Several questions contain premises (about 54% proficient in math, a new superintendent after a search, a 360-student school, an annual homeschool letter to SAU 50, open enrollment in Portsmouth) that the research should check rather than assume.
