# Rollinsford Grade School's Early Grades and Families

Part of the Rollinsford research (town/Rollinsford). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Rollinsford Grade School (RGS, kindergarten to grade 6) from a young family's side: whether the school has public pre-K, kindergarten (day length, entry age, waiver, registration papers), the school day and the Horizons before- and after-school program (hours, fees, discounts, rules), First Student buses (who to call, routes, eligibility, changing a drop-off), school meals and the yearly free and reduced-price application, English learners and translated information, military and mid-year new students, the residency rules and open enrollment (a grandparent's house, a child who lives in another town), reading and math help, 504 plans, conferences, the PTO and volunteering, and the nearest licensed child care, camps and local help with costs.

17 topics, 72 facts and 3 number tables. Main sources: the RGS Handbook for 2026-27 (a Google Doc linked from the school's site), the Horizons parent packet, the kindergarten letter and registration packets, the 2026-27 calendar and bus sheet, Rollinsford School Board policies (JEB, JFAA, JFAB, JFABA, EEA, ACE), SAU 104's Student Services and Food Services pages, the 2026 Voter Information Guide and ballot results, the district's 2026-27 federal grants (ESEA) plan, two School Board newsletters, and the site's own child care data. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 10 MB; see `raw/elementary/SOURCES.txt`). No file was blocked.

**Main findings:**
- **RGS has no general public pre-K.** The school is kindergarten to grade 6. The district describes its budget as for "grades K-12 and Special Education Pre-K," and MSAD 35 (the Maine district that runs Rollinsford's special services under a contract) oversees preschool special education. The nearest licensed programs, including a Head Start classroom, are in Somersworth and Dover, 9 to 13 minutes by road (derived).
- **Kindergarten is full day, with a September 30 age cutoff.** The principal's February 2026 letter says "full-day program." Policy JEB sets the cutoff and a waiver process (written notice by March 1, a $150 assessment).
- **Horizons opens at 7:00 a.m. and closes at 5:30 p.m.** It is run at RGS for grades K to 6, mostly by RGS staff, and says it is "not registered as a daycare." Fees run from $100 to $285 a month per child by days a week; families who qualify for free or reduced-price lunch get 30% off, if they apply early.
- **Changing a bus drop-off goes through the Superintendent's office, not First Student.** Policy EEA requires a written request for an alternative route home for child care, granted if there is room; First Student's number (603-692-4406) is for route questions.
- **Lunch is not free for everyone.** Hot lunch is $3.35 and breakfast $2.10; families apply for free or reduced-price meals each school year.
- **A child's legal residence is where the parents live, not where the child spends days.** Policy JFAA and the registration affidavit follow RSA 193:12. RGS became an open enrollment school for 2026-27 (voters said yes, 455 to 223), with room for up to two New Hampshire students in grades 1 to 6 from other districts.
