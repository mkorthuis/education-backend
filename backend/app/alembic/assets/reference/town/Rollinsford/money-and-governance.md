# Rollinsford: Money and Governance

Part of the Rollinsford research (town/Rollinsford). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 10 topics, 78 facts and 12 number tables on why Rollinsford's school taxes rose in 2024 and 2025 and then held in 2026, the cost and terms of the Marshwood (MSAD 35) tuition contract, what the single-district SAU 104 costs, the grade school building's age and condition, how the deliberative session and warrant work, what the district publishes to explain its warrant, and what the district has and hasn't said about a new school, a merger or closing the grade school.

- **Taxes.** Rollinsford's school tax (local plus state education) was $4,871,631 in 2023, $5,500,904 in 2024 (up 12.9%), $5,930,640 in 2025 (up 7.8%) and $5,955,681 in 2026 (up 0.4%); the 2026 school rate of $12.04 per $1,000 is a cent above 2025's $12.03. The 2024 jump came from a $404,935 larger budget while the surplus used to cut taxes fell from $385,410 to $61,250; the 2025 jump from a $678,224 larger budget (Marshwood tuition and special education, a new SAU model, insurance). In 2026 a $335,054 surplus held the rate.
- **Who the tax pays for.** About 277 students in September 2026: 147 at Rollinsford Grade School and 130.25 at Marshwood. Of the 2026-27 budget, 57.6% is the grade school, 36.7% Marshwood Middle and High and 5.3% the SAU and school board (**derived**).
- **Marshwood.** The tuition agreement was approved October 10, 2024 for 2025 to 2030. The 2026-27 base rate is $14,143.19 a student (131 students, $1,852,758), plus special education and other charges, $2,231,035 in all; the rate for 2027-28 is up 6.3%. A separate two-year special education services contract is up for renewal in 2026.
- **SAU 104.** The district's own December 2024 figures put its own SAU about $87,909 above staying with SAU 56 (**derived**); its stated reasons are more hours, control and a direct line to MSAD 35.
- **Building.** Built in 1936 and on the National Register; windows (1997-98 vinyl) and roof are planned work; no item rated "poor" in October 2026; reserve funds hold $317,202 for the building.
- **Calendar.** Public budget hearing January 12, 2027; deliberative session Tuesday, February 2, 2027 (snow date February 6); vote March 9, 2027.

**Main sources:** the SAU 104 and Rollinsford School District site (sau104.org) budget pages and board folders (FY2026-27 budget book, presentations, MS-27 and default budget forms, warrant, voter guides, ballot results, FAQs, mailers, newsletters, minutes and committee summaries from October 2024 to November 2026, the capital plan), the town website's 2026-27 schedule and December 2025 newsletter, the National Register nomination for the school, NH DRA tax rate sheets (in the budget book), and the site's database.
