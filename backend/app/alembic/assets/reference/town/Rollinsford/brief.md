## What you need to know about Rollinsford

- **Rollinsford** is a town in Strafford County on the Maine line along the Salmon Falls River, with its village, the Salmon Falls Mills and Locust Street. Site town id 463.
- **The Rollinsford School District** (site district id 463) is **SAU 104**, a single-town SAU, and runs Rollinsford Grade School (RGS; 22810; kindergarten to grade 6; 154 students in 2024-25). Grades 7–12 go to **Marshwood Middle School and Marshwood High School** (MSAD 35: Eliot and South Berwick, Maine) under a tuition contract (the places file has it since 2015-16, renewed unchanged in October 2024 and approved by the State Board on November 14, 2024; its term wasn't found). Earlier, the town sent students to Somersworth. Check the tuition per student, the contract's end date, the special education arrangement, and how the NH State Board and Maine rules interact.
- **Earlier research to cite:** Somersworth (`town/Somersworth/`; the former receiving district and NH comparisons), Dover (`town/Dover/`; Seacoast Charter, St. Thomas Aquinas, the closest Dover options) and `town/maine/` (Maine's MSAD 72 and Fryeburg Academy; for how a Maine school treats New Hampshire tuition students). Marshwood and MSAD 35 are new: this is the first research on them.
- **Site figures:** Resident students (the site's town enrollment; its "2026" row is the October 1, 2025 count, i.e. 2025-26): 262 (2014-15), 158 (2019-20), 154 (2025-26); school enrollment in 2024-25 (the site's "2025" row, the October 1, 2024 count): Rollinsford Grade School 154; cost per pupil FY2025 Rollinsford $24,597, state $22,699; 2025 tax rates per $1,000 (Rollinsford): total $17.90, local school $10.85, state education $1.18, municipal $3.88, county $1.99; Question 2: Rollinsford 53.4% yes (unofficial).
