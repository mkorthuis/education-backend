# Rollinsford Grade School: Support, Choices and Ratings

Part of the Rollinsford research (town/Rollinsford). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Rollinsford Grade School (RGS, grades K–6, 154 students in 2024-25 and 147 in September 2026) from the side of support and choices: gifted and advanced math, who handles special education and the speech and occupational therapy staff, the school nurse, allergies and medicine, counseling, bullying rules in a small school, homeschool and EFA access to RGS, busing for private and charter students, EFA use at schools in Dover and South Berwick, Seacoast Charter School, homeschool groups, VLACS, and the RGS ratings on outside sites.

- **13 topics, 68 facts and 1 number table.** Twelve topics are shared topics (`district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-curriculum-and-assessment`, `district-special-education-services`, `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-student-support`, `district-conduct-policies`, `district-homeschool-access`, `district-transportation`, `town-school-options-nearby`, `town-recreation-and-summer`, `school-rating-sites`, `district-goals-and-performance-reporting`); one adds a fact to the library topic `vlacs-online-courses`. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 10 MB).
- **Places used:** `district Rollinsford` for RGS, its board, policies and staff; `maine MSAD 35` for Marshwood's curriculum page; `town Rollinsford` for the town's recreation page, EFA counts, homeschool groups, Maine private schools and the mental health center; `district Seacoast Charter School` and `district Portsmouth Christian Academy` for those schools. No new places.
- **Main findings:**
  - **RGS reports no gifted and talented program** to the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (2021-22 and 2023-24). Its multi-age classes (1+2, 3+4, 5+6) are its stated way to give "an extra challenge", and in 2026-27 it pays for a math interventionist (Title I and IV) and a math consultant (Title II). Marshwood says it lets students "access accelerated math courses as needed", without saying how students are placed.
  - **MSAD 35 does oversee special education for Rollinsford, but RGS has its own staff.** SAU 104's Student Services page says that, under a contract with MSAD 35, Marshwood "provides SAU 104 with oversight" of special education, 504 plans, English as a second language and homeless-student support, preschool to grade 12. RGS's own 2026-27 staff directory lists two special education teachers, a speech pathologist and an occupational therapist, and the board's staffing overview shows speech and language at 1.00 full-time equivalent (FTE). The board was told in August 2026 that the special education contract with MSAD 35 is set to expire after 2026-27.
  - **RGS lists one nurse (1.00 FTE) and one guidance counselor (1.00 FTE),** and the federal data for 2023-24 show 1 FTE of each and 0.16 FTE of a psychologist. No source says the nurse is in the building every day.
  - **Policy JJJ (August 13, 2026) gives homeschool, EFA, charter and private school residents the same access as RGS students** to courses and activities, by written request to the principal and at the same fees. The principal told the board on September 10, 2026 that RGS has a drama club, band, chorus and intramural sports. Policy IHBG (2019) still describes the annual homeschool evaluation that state law dropped on July 1, 2026.
  - **Rollinsford's busing policy (EEA) covers charter and private school students only "within the District"** on the regular routes and stops. Nothing in it buses a student to Dover.
  - **RGS shows 8/10 on GreatSchools (test scores 7, student progress 8) and 4/10 on Public School Review.** The two sites use different test years: the state's data for grades 3–6 show math proficiency of 34% in 2023 and 45% in 2024, and reading 39% and 60% (derived). A C+ could not be checked (Niche and Homes.com refuse automated requests).
  - **Seacoast Charter School calls itself arts-integrated** (every student plays a stringed instrument from grade 3), and none of its saved pages mentions Montessori. All its classes were full for 2026-27.
  - **The Granite State Home Educators group list, which names the nearest co-ops and park-day groups, stopped being updated after September 15, 2026.**
- **Main gaps:** RGS's own gifted report to NH DOE (NH DOE refuses automated requests); the RGS Math and Reading Intervention slides (not readable); any posted nut-free rule or district allergy plan; RGS band, chorus and intramural details and fees; recreation league listings; any parent survey of RGS; Niche's grade; where Rollinsford homeschool families actually go for co-ops.
