# Marshwood High School and Career Programs, From Rollinsford

Part of the Rollinsford research (town/Rollinsford). Gathered November 22, 2026. Marshwood High School and MSAD 35 had no earlier research, so facts about them use `place: maine MSAD 35`.

## Summary

This file covers what a Rollinsford student at Marshwood High School in South Berwick, Maine can reach: the career and technical centers Marshwood partners with in Maine and New Hampshire, the late bus from the school to Rollinsford, how Marshwood's AP and college results compare with St. Thomas Aquinas and Berwick Academy, its diploma options, outside and summer credit, driver education, college courses through Great Bay and UNH, in-state tuition and scholarship residency, and who helps with the FAFSA and applications.

- **Premises checked:**
  - Tech centers "in Maine or New Hampshire" (P6.1): correct. Marshwood partners with SRTC in Sanford, Maine (24 programs) and Dover CTC in New Hampshire (3 programs for Marshwood students); both need an application in February of sophomore year.
  - A late bus "back to the American Legion" for robotics or drama (P6.2): partly. MSAD 35 runs a late bus Monday to Thursday, and its 2026-27 run lists a "Legion Turnaround (Rollinsford)" stop at about 4:48 p.m. Drama exists (Marshwood Theatre). No robotics club appears in the 2026 clubs catalog or the school profile, only a half-credit "Robotics and Drones" course. MSAD 35 also says high school students may not ride across state lines on district transportation except with residency in both districts, and does not explain how that fits the Rollinsford stop.
  - "Berwick Academy" as an AP comparison (P10.1): Berwick Academy does not label its courses AP. Its 2017 post describes a shift to "Pinnacle" courses. St. Thomas Aquinas lists 13 AP-labeled courses; Marshwood's pages give 15, 12 and 13.
  - "An alternative diploma track" (P17.1): none found. Marshwood has one credit-based diploma, with a 3-year graduation option, extended study to age 20, extended learning opportunities, career technical programs and hands-on electives.
  - "Free summer school" (P17.2): no Marshwood summer school or credit recovery program is described on any page read.
  - Driver's ed "through Marshwood in Maine" (P17.3): no Marshwood driver education course is listed anywhere read. New Hampshire's rule for a license before 18 is in the Gilmanton file.
  - Dual enrollment "through Great Bay or UNH ... or only Maine community colleges" (P20.1): not only Maine. Marshwood lists Great Bay (up to two free courses a year for New Hampshire residents, taken on campus or online), eStart and VLACS for New Hampshire residents, and a Husson program free to Maine and New Hampshire students; UNH credit comes through UNH Early College Online and VLACS, not at Marshwood. Marshwood's own guide says New Hampshire students get the 18 free credits only through York County Community College, which conflicts in part with Maine DOE's Maine-resident rule.
  - "New Hampshire in-state scholarships and UNH tuition breaks" for a Maine graduate (P20.2): UNH's in-state status follows the parent's New Hampshire residence, and its New Hampshire Promise needs New Hampshire residency; neither page names the high school's location. No state scholarship tied to graduating from a New Hampshire school was found.
  - "Marshwood guidance office, or an NH-specific advisor" (P20.3): both exist. Marshwood has five counselors and a financial aid folder that includes a "NH Financial Aid Insider" guide; Granite Edvance is a free New Hampshire nonprofit that offers FAFSA appointments.
  - First Student's phone number: MSAD 35's page says (603) 692-4406 and Marshwood's handbook says 603-694-4406.
