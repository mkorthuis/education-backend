# Marshwood Middle School and the Move Up From Rollinsford Grade School

Part of the Rollinsford research (town/Rollinsford). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

What's covered: how Rollinsford Grade School's (RGS) and Marshwood's (MSAD 35) 2026-27 calendars, school days and snow-day decisions compare; the buses and the late bus for Rollinsford students at Marshwood Middle School (MMS); how the move from RGS to MMS in grade 7 is handled; class and grade sizes; middle school sports; how special education and Section 504 work between SAU 104 and MSAD 35; and what the sources do and don't say about an IEP moving from New Hampshire to Maine rules.

- **10 fact topics, 62 facts and 4 number tables.** Four web searches were used (none blocked). Raw copies are in `raw/marshwood-middle/` (about 12 MB: calendars, SAU 104 and MSAD 35 pages, policies, cleaned minutes and newsletter excerpts, statutes). Bus route sheets and minutes were saved only as cleaned excerpts (no house numbers, no student names).
- **Main findings:**
  - The two calendars are almost the same. For 2026-27 they share the first day (August 31), both vacation weeks, the holidays and the last day (June 11, 2027), and each has 175 school days. They differ on December 23 (RGS open, Marshwood on winter recess) and January 15 (RGS teacher workshop day, a school day at Marshwood). In 2025-26 the last days differed: June 16 at RGS and June 18 at Marshwood. RGS says its snow day decisions "align with MSAD 35".
  - Marshwood's own students reach MMS in grade 6 (from Great Works School, grades 4–5). Rollinsford students stay at RGS through grade 6 and enter MMS in grade 7, so they join a school the others have attended for a year. RGS lists a "6th Grade Step Up Day at MMS" (June 5, 2026).
  - Rollinsford's First Student buses take students to MMS (arriving 7:10 a.m., pickup 2:30 p.m.). For after-school activities, Rollinsford children ride the South Berwick late bus to the "Legion stop" in Rollinsford (about 4:48 p.m. on the sheet), Monday to Thursday. Rollinsford pays for the late bus and for Thursday morning supervision (late-start Thursdays at MMS start at 9:00).
  - SAU 104 says MSAD 35's Office of Special Services oversees special education, 504 plans and English learner services for Rollinsford students under a contract. Rollinsford's side cites New Hampshire rules and forms; MSAD 35's own page cites Maine's rules and forms. No page says which state's rules govern a Rollinsford student's IEP at Marshwood. The special education contract with MSAD 35 is up for renewal this year.
  - MMS had 490 students in 2024-25 (149, 169 and 172 in grades 6, 7 and 8). In November 2026 RGS's grade 6 had 21 students in two combined 5–6 classes of 19 and 18; Rollinsford had 26 students in grade 7 and 22 in grade 8 at MMS. MSAD 35's class size guideline for grades 6–8 is 18 to 25.
- **Main gaps:** which state's special education rules apply to a Rollinsford IEP at Marshwood (no page says; Maine's Chapter 101 returned HTTP 403); any dated schedule or written plan for Rollinsford's 7th graders' entry to MMS beyond the June 5 Step Up Day listing; MMS results for Rollinsford students compared with students from Eliot and South Berwick (none published; Maine DOE's school-level results were not retrieved); how a 504 plan is handed from RGS to MMS; class sizes by classroom at MMS; MMS's own clubs list (a Canva page refused non-browser access).
