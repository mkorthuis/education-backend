# Rollinsford Residents' Questions

The questions the Rollinsford research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Rollinsford's students attend Rollinsford Grade School (kindergarten to grade 6) in the town's own single-town SAU 104, and then Marshwood Middle and High Schools in South Berwick and Eliot, Maine, under a tuition agreement with MSAD 35. Several questions contain premises (public pre-K at RGS, MSAD 35 handling the town's special education, a school board newsletter, an "8/10" rating next to a "C+", Seacoast Charter School in Dover, in-state scholarships for a Maine graduate, driver's ed through Marshwood, a Horizons before-school program) that the research should check rather than assume. Marshwood and MSAD 35 have no research yet.
