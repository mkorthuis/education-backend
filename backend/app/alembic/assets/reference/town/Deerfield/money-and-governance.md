# Deerfield: Money, Taxes, Tuition, Buildings and Governance

Part of the Deerfield research (town/Deerfield). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 67 facts and 10 number tables on the Deerfield School District's money and governance.
- **Budget:** the proposed operating budget has failed at the ballot every year from FY2022 to FY2027 (and FY2019), so a default budget has set spending. The 2026 vote (684 yes, 837 no) put the $20,776,836 default in place, $15,706 below the proposal (**derived**). What drove FY2027 and the FY2028 proposal ($21,403,950, +1.8%; default $21,292,673), the FY2026 year-end surplus, and where the plain-language budget papers are.
- **Taxes:** tax year 2026 rates from the district ($15.44 total; school local $10.69 and state $0.99, 75.6% of the rate, **derived**). The 2025 revaluation that cut the rate while the levy rose 8.4%. The levy's growth since 2014 against flat resident counts (**derived**, site data). Surplus returned to taxpayers, 2017–2026.
- **High school tuition:** per-student rates and budgeted students at Coe-Brown, Concord High and Pembroke Academy from FY2022 to the FY2028 estimate. Actual tuition spending rose from $2.70 million (FY2023) to $4.15 million (FY2026). Each agreement's cost formula and term (all end June 30, 2027) and renewal talks in fall 2026. Special education costs budgeted at each school. The 2023, 2024 and 2026 votes on choice, including the 2026 advisory vote to keep it (765–750).
- **SAU 53:** Deerfield's share (21.5% for FY27, 22.2% proposed for FY28), its trend, Deerfield's portion of the top administrators' pay ($109,972.20 in 2025-26, **derived**), and the planning committee voters created in March 2026 (812–648) to study reorganizing or leaving SAU 53.
- **Building:** the 2025 space study (room for about 446 to 496 students), the purchased modular classrooms, the failed 2022 bond ($45.5 million addition and renovation), trust funds ($1.61 million) and no debt.
- **Staff, ratings, home values:** what is and isn't known about teacher turnover, the district's salary comparison, DCS's GreatSchools rating (7/10), and Zillow home values for Deerfield, Candia and Northwood.

**Main sources:** the Deerfield School District page and its Google Drive folders on sau.sau53.org: the 2026 deliberative session packet, budget information packet, handout and minutes, the 2026 election results, the FY2027-28 budget summary and the November 4 and 18, 2026 board packets, the RSA 189:76 voter report (March 2026), the 2025 space limitations presentation, budget packets from 2020 to 2025, the three high school agreements (OCR copies from agent 3's `raw/high/`), and board minutes from 2022 to November 2026. Also GreatSchools, Zillow's ZIP code home value file, the SAU 53 towns' money files (Allenstown, Epsom, Pembroke), and the site's database (read only).
