# Deerfield Residents' Questions

The questions the Deerfield research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Deerfield is in SAU 53. The Deerfield School District runs Deerfield Community School (DCS, preschool to grade 8). For high school, families choose Concord High School, Coe-Brown Northwood Academy or Pembroke Academy under tuition contracts, and students may take career programs at the Concord Regional Technical Center. The district decides its budget by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (about half of DCS students proficient, a cap on Coe-Brown seats, school ratings, the choice being locked in, DCS near capacity, a building bond) that the research should check rather than assume.
