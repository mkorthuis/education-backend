# Deerfield Community School: Upper Grades, Support and Choices

Part of the Deerfield research (town/Deerfield). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Deerfield Community School's (DCS) middle grades and the supports and choices around them: state test results and how the school checks progress, special education and speech staffing and who decides special education, advanced math and Algebra in grade 8, VLACS and online courses, hands-on classes, fees and help with costs, homeschooled and EFA students' access, middle school sports and clubs, practical skills, counselors, bullying and phones, math intervention, world languages, attendance, and how DCS graduates do in high school math. Sources are the DCS website pages (special education, speech, OT, PT, counseling, WIN and Title I, athletics, music, art, technology, curriculum, math, world cultures, policies, food services), the DCS math website, the 2026–2027 DCS Student/Parent Handbook, the September 9, 2026 Middle School Community Meeting slides, 17 Deerfield School Board policies, Deerfield School Board minutes from August 2022 to November 2026, the May 6, November 4 and November 18, 2026 board packets (the state's special education determination, the draft 2027–28 staff roster, co-curricular and technology budgets, and a special education cost handout), SAU 53's October 2024 special education presentation, Concord High School's 2023–2024 report to the Deerfield School Board, the 2026–27 calendar, The Bridge (November 20, 2026), SAU 53's special education and home education pages, its job board, and VLACS.

**Counts:** 14 topics (all shared slugs), 101 facts and 2 number tables (special education and support staff on the draft 2027–28 roster; Deerfield students' grades in 9th and 10th grade math at Concord High, 2023–24).

**Main findings:**
- DCS students' 2025–26 state test proficiency was 52% in math and 59% in reading (site data), above the state's 44% and 55%. The 2026 ballot printed 2024–25 results: ELA 58%, math 52%, science 46%.
- Special education staffing on the draft 2027–28 roster (October 30, 2026): 10.4 full-time equivalents of special education teachers, speech-language pathologists (1.4) and an occupational therapist, plus 23 special education aides, one of them a 1:1 aide position marked "OPEN". The school's speech page lists the preschool–grade 1 speech-language pathologist as "TBD". On November 21, 2026 the SAU 53 job board listed no special education, speech or aide opening at DCS.
- Grade 8 has two math courses, "Grade 8 Math" and "Grade 8 Algebra." Concord High's report says many Deerfield students arrive having completed algebra and start in Geometry or Advanced Geometry.
- DCS has offered no world language since about 2020; the board chose a World Cultures class instead in March 2023 because world language teachers were hard to find. Students start level 1 in high school or may take VLACS courses on their own.
- The Deerfield Fair has its own no-school day (October 2, 2026). Family trips need the principal's advance approval, with a note to the teacher two weeks ahead.
- Deerfield's access policy JJJ (April 2026) opens courses, sports and activities to home-educated residents, but says EFA students lack the same right and charges EFA students in grades 7–8 for academic courses. HB 1817 added EFA students to the access law on August 18, 2026; no revised policy was posted.
