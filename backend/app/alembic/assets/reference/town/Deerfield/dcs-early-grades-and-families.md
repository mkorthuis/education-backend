# Deerfield Community School: Early Grades and Families

Part of the Deerfield research (town/Deerfield). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers Deerfield Community School (DCS) for young children and their families: the preschool's spots, tuition, hours and registration; the kindergarten day and kindergarten class size; recess; the school day, before- and after-school care, dismissal and pickup when a child is sick; bus routes and ride times for 2026-27, the bus driver shortage and the bus policy for roads a bus can't use; storm closings and delays; Alma, One Call Now and the other tools families use; report cards and conferences; reading help and how teachers meet different levels; Chromebooks and borrowing a hotspot; the school nurses, medicines, inhalers and allergy plans; building security; and the town library as a place to work after school.

**Counts:** 14 topics, 62 facts, 1 number table (derived from the bus route sheets).

**Main sources:** the DCS website (bus, policies, health, technology, Alma, food services, enrichment, Ways to Connect, curriculum and grading, English language arts, Title I, library, staff directory), the 2026-27 Student/Parent Handbook, the DCS Preschool Google site (information, tuition and registration pages), the 2026-27 bus route sheets (August 20, 2026) and the updated Orange and Purple routes (from November 30, 2026), the 2026-27 academic calendar, Deerfield School Board policies (EEA, EEAL, EBB, EBCA, EBCB, IIB, IMAH, IFA, IKB, IC, JHC, JLC, JLCD, JLIA, KF-A), Deerfield School Board minutes from December 2025 to November 4, 2026 and board packets from September to November 2026, the New Hampshire General Court's bill status pages for SB 578 and HB 1507 (2026), and the Philbrick-James Library's website.

**Main gaps:** what time the district usually decides on a delay or closing, and whether DCS runs a delayed-opening bus schedule (neither the handbook nor the website says); how many minutes of recess kindergartners and other elementary grades get; kindergarten enrollment and class sizes on October 1, 2026 (only the March 2026 planning count of 43 was found); whether the November 2025 school safety grant was awarded; DCS's visitor check-in procedure (the handbook section reads "To be updated"); whether any child care program drives children to or from DCS; which Deerfield roads are gravel and how the buses handle them in mud season (the town website could not be used); and whether children with IEPs pay preschool tuition (the posting doesn't say).
