# Kearsarge Regional High School and Career Programs

Part of the New London research (town/New London). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Kearsarge Regional High School (KRHS, grades 9–12, 457 North Road, North Sutton), where New London's high school students go: AP and honors courses and how they compare with bigger high schools; Early College (formerly Running Start) courses taught at KRHS and college courses for seniors at Colby-Sawyer, New England College, Dartmouth and the community colleges; credit transfer to UNH; graduation requirements (including those planned for the Class of 2030), registration, grading and moving in from a private school; extended learning opportunities (ELOs), work-study and internship credit, the LNA program at New London Hospital and the Graduate Portfolio capstone that replaced the Senior Project; career courses at KRHS (culinary, trades, engineering, the kit car build, Ford ACE) and the two career centers KRHS students can attend (the Concord Regional Technical Center and Sugar River Valley's Newport campus), with 20 budgeted seats in 2026-27; the 2026-27 late buses, driving and parking; sports, the hockey co-ops, eligibility and the participation fee; theater, band and arts courses; and fees.
