# Kearsarge Middle School, Support and Choices: The Bus to North Sutton, Phones, Math, Climate, Reading Help, IEPs, Homeschool and EFA Access, Co-ops, Proctor, Music and Theater, and the Move to Grade 6

Part of the New London research (town/New London). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Kearsarge Regional Middle School (KRMS, grades 6–8, North Sutton) for New London families: New London's 2026-27 bus routes, the transfer at KRMS and the late bus; phones (board policy JICJ and the 2026-27 handbook); Algebra I and other high school courses taught at KRMS (policy IMBD); the school's social climate, the principal's March 2026 letter about racist conduct and the revised code of conduct; counselors and social workers; the move from KRES at New London to grade 6 and the KRMS school day; reading specialists and special education (evaluations, staffing and out-of-district placements); homeschooled and EFA students' access (policies JJJ and IHBG); middle school sports by grade; the Tracy Memorial Library's card rules; homeschool co-ops near New London; what Proctor Academy publishes about aid; music and drama in grades 6–12; and youth theater and music lessons nearby.

13 topics, 75 facts, no number tables. Facts 1–54 were gathered first; 21 more (appended at the ends of topics, with earlier notes corrected where they had said a source couldn't be read) come from the main session's browser pass of kearsarge.org on November 22, 2026 (raw/browser/): the KRMS 2026-27 Student-Family Handbook, board policies JICJ, JJJ, IHBG, IMBD and JJIC, the 2026-27 late-run sheet and Bus Transfer Guide, and the route sheets for Buses 32, 34 and 35 (scanned; read in the scratchpad, with only times and street names transcribed). Other sources: the district's 2023, 2024 and 2026 annual reports (the 56th, 57th and 59th; the 2025 report wasn't found); the FY2024-25 Article 1 budget information and the December 14, 2023 board minutes; KRES at New London's 2025-26 monthly newsletters and 2026-27 handbook; a KRMS grade 8 cluster's 2026-27 parent letter; the KRSD social workers' page; the KRHS 2025-26 and 2026-27 Programs of Studies; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24 (Kearsarge rows); the principal's March 2, 2026 letter (as reproduced by the Concord Monitor); a 2022 bus bid notice; Proctor Academy's tuition and financial aid pages; the New London Barn Playhouse, Gear Up Homeschoolers and Tracy Memorial Library pages; the library's EFA marketplace download; and OpenStreetMap/OSRM model drives between school and town-building addresses.

Main gaps: no source says what reading programs Kearsarge's reading specialists are trained in; no source says whether 8th graders may audition for the high school musical or join high school ensembles (the KRMS handbook and both KRHS Programs of Studies are silent); the route sheets print no arrival times at school; how students are placed in KRMS's high school courses isn't stated; Proctor doesn't publish what day students pay after aid; NH DOE's special education monitoring page refused automated requests; policy JJA and KRMS's Daily Schedule page (sign-in required) weren't read.
