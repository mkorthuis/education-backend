# New London Residents' Questions

The questions the New London research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

New London's students attend the Kearsarge Regional School District (SAU 65), shared with Bradford, Newbury, Springfield, Sutton, Warner and Wilmot: Kearsarge Regional Elementary School at New London (kindergarten to grade 5, with a preschool), and Kearsarge Regional Middle and High School in North Sutton. The district holds a two-session annual meeting. Several questions contain premises (James House Preschool, New London paying a big share, about 1,680 students, remote snow days, early-release Wednesdays, Colby-Sawyer's Live & Learn Local program) that the research should check rather than assume.
