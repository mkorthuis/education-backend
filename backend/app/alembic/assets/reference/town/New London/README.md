# New London: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering New London, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 22, 2026, in the tenth wave of the second ToLoad batch. New London is one of seven towns in the Kearsarge Regional School District, SAU 65:
- **Kearsarge Regional Elementary at New London** serves kindergarten to grade 5, with the district's preschool in town.
- **Kearsarge Regional Middle (6–8) and High (9–12)** are in North Sutton.
- **Career programs:** the Concord Regional Technical Center (district bus) and Sugar River Valley's Newport campus (students drive).

The district votes by official ballot. kearsarge.org's web pages show a bot check to automated requests (its PDFs still download), so the agents also used the schools' Google Docs and Sites.
