# Grantham Residents' Questions

The questions the Grantham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Grantham's students attend Grantham Village School (preschool to grade 6) in Grantham's own single-town SAU 75, and then Lebanon Middle and High Schools under the Grantham-Lebanon-Plainfield area agreement, with the Hartford Area Career and Technical Center across the river in Vermont. Several questions contain premises (a tuition of $20,000 to $21,000 a student, an HB 1300 tax cap forum, a pickup cutoff of 2:00 p.m. on PickUp Patrol, Before School Program hours, a bus driver who won't release K–3 children without an adult, Grantham representation on Lebanon's board, Eastman as mostly year-round families now) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Jenna, 36 — Nurse at Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center, lives in Eastman
Mom of a 1st grader and 3rd grader; leaves the house before 7:00 AM three days a week.

- **P1.1** If the Before School Program at GVS doesn't open until 7:30, what do hospital parents who have 7:00 AM shifts actually do?
- **P1.2** When GVS has a two-hour delay, they cancel the morning program completely—how much notice do we usually get before I have to scramble for a sitter?
- **P1.3** Are there reliable carpools from Eastman up I-89 to Lebanon, or do most parents just rely on the bus?
