# Lebanon Middle School From Grantham: Support, Choices and Getting There

Part of the Grantham research (town/Grantham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers what Grantham families of 6th to 8th graders need to know about Lebanon Middle School (LMS): how Grantham's classes and test results compare with Hanover's and Lebanon's, math enrichment, the move of an IEP from Grantham Village School (GVS) to LMS and who checks in, homeschoolers and Education Freedom Account (EFA) students, Ledyard Charter School's bus, the transition and making friends, the Grantham buses to LMS and the 7:30 start, getting sick at school in Lebanon, the two school schedules, fees, and what changes for a family moving from Lebanon to Grantham. Lebanon's own facts on LMS (phones, bullying, counselors, the late bus, clubs) are in `town/Lebanon/middle-support-and-choices.md`, and the AREA agreement's terms on equal access, counseling, reports and Grantham's homeschool policies JJJ and JJJ-R are in this research's `lebanon-high-from-grantham.md` (agent 3); they are cited, not repeated.

- **14 topics, 40 facts and 1 table.** All 14 are shared slugs from `shared-topics.md`: `district-classrooms-and-staff`, `district-school-results-compared`, `district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners`, `district-special-education-services`, `school-programs-after-grade-6`, `district-student-support`, `district-transportation`, `district-school-day-and-calendar`, `district-fees`, `district-homeschool-access`, `town-school-options-nearby`, `district-tuition-contracts`, `district-attendance-zones-and-transfers` and `town-recreation-and-summer`. Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 5 MB).
- **Places used:** `district Grantham` (GVS, SAU 75 and the Grantham board), `district Lebanon` (LMS and the AREA agreement's grade ranges), `district Ledyard Charter School`, `town Grantham` and `town Lebanon`. No general rules were written; state rules are cited from the library by file, topic and fact number.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Bus to LMS:** Grantham runs its own buses to Lebanon. Three routes pick up between 6:17 and 6:55 a.m., reach Lebanon High at 7:05–7:07 and LMS at 7:15–7:16, and then return for GVS students (8:28). A student's scheduled ride to LMS is about 21 to 58 minutes in the morning (**derived**). In the afternoon the buses leave Lebanon at 2:33, transfer riders at Exit 14, and reach the first Grantham stops at 2:53–2:55.
  - **Two schedules:** the same three buses make both morning runs, so a family's stop has two times about an hour apart: for example Rt. 10 & Smith-Hastings Rd. at 6:55 for LMS and 7:55 for GVS. LMS runs 7:30–2:30 and GVS 8:30–3:15, and the calendars differ.
  - **The 7:30 start:** correct. Lebanon began a study in fall 2026 of changing LMS and Lebanon High start times; no decision had been made on October 16, 2026.
  - **IEPs:** Grantham stays the legal "LEA" (the district responsible for the IEP) for its students at LMS. Its representative sits on the IEP team, Lebanon sends quarterly progress updates, and the two districts' coordinators may meet monthly. A new SAU 75 Director of Student Services began July 1, 2026.
  - **Transition:** LMS held a "New to LMS" parent night on June 2, 2026 and a Step-Up Day for GVS 6th graders on June 9, 2026, and a parent orientation night on September 3, 2026. Grantham students are about a quarter of LMS's 7th and 8th grades (**derived**). The AREA agreement's promise of counseling for Grantham's 6th graders is in agent 3's file.
  - **Math:** GVS runs a take-home Math Challenge for all K–6 students; LMS lists Algebra/Geometry 1, a high-school-level course, for students with an NWEA score of 240 or higher and a teacher's recommendation.
  - **Ledyard:** Grantham's transportation policy gives charter and private school pupils the same transportation "within the District"; Ledyard is in Lebanon. Grantham's buses reach Lebanon High (195 Hanover Street) at 7:05–7:07; Ledyard (39 Hanover Street) is about 1 mile away on foot (**derived**). Ledyard's website posts nothing about transportation.
  - **Homeschoolers and EFA students:** Grantham's policy IHBG (adopted September 1, 2026) has the principal evaluate a returning home-educated child for placement, and still describes notices and evaluations that state law no longer requires; its policy JJJ (agent 3's file) gives access to GVS courses and activities on the same terms and fees as enrolled students. Lebanon's own policy texts weren't readable.
- **Main gaps:**
  - Lebanon's policy texts (JJJ, IHBG, JICK, JICJ) load only by script, so what Lebanon requires of a Grantham homeschooler or EFA student at LMS is known only from its board minutes.
  - Nothing published says whether the monthly AREA coordinator meetings on IEPs happen, or how an LMS transition meeting for a child with an IEP is scheduled.
  - Ledyard's transportation plan for students from other towns wasn't found; NH DOE's transportation page refused automated requests.
  - No survey shows whether Grantham students feel included at LMS, and no field trip fee is listed for LMS or GVS.
- **Proposed shared topic:** none.
