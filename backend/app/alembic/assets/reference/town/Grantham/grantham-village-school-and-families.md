# Grantham Village School and Families

Part of the Grantham research (town/Grantham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Grantham Village School (GVS, preschool to grade 6) and what young families ask about it: the Before School Program (hours, delays, fee, who gets in, help with the cost); after-school care at the school (The Outing Club Village and Grantham Children's Academy); the school day, calendar and early-release days; the kindergarten day; preschool places and the interest list; PickUp Patrol, the 2:00 p.m. change cutoff and the rule for kindergarten to grade 3 riders; the 2026-27 bus run times and ride lengths; storm-day notice; Alma, registration help and in-person contact; free and reduced-price meals and their privacy; counseling and social-emotional support; reading support; homework by grade and recess; and playgroup and early-childhood options before preschool.

- **14 topics, 53 facts and 1 number table.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 13 MB): the GVS Back to School, registration, preschool, reading, counselor, free and reduced meals, bus, calendar, policies and school board pages; the 2026-27 family handbook, Before School Program guidelines, district calendar, PickUp Patrol instructions, Alma instructions and the "Grantham-area Preschool Options" sheet; school board policies EBCD, EEA, EEA-R, EEAE-R1, EFAA, IKB and JLD; the 2026-27 free and reduced-price meal letter, application and instructions; cleaned text copies of the 2026-27 AM and PM bus route sheets (house numbers and driver names removed; the original PDFs are not kept); excerpts of six GVS and superintendent newsletters; The Outing Club and Grantham Children's Academy pages; Dunbar Free Library, Grantham Recreation and TLC Family Resource Center pages; an excerpt of the 2026 annual report's enrollment table; and the results of read-only queries of the site's own database.
