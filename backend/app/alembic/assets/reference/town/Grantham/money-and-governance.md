# Grantham: Money, Tuition, Taxes, SAU 75 and Governance

Part of the Grantham research (town/Grantham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 12 topics, 85 facts and 6 number tables on how the Grantham School District pays Lebanon for grades 7–12, what its budget and tax rate look like, the October 20, 2026 HB 1300 hearing and how Question 2 came out, the single-town SAU 75, room at Grantham Village School, the school board's meetings and public input, the bus contract, Eastman's share of the town and its fees, and the teachers' contract.
- **Tuition to Lebanon:** the AREA agreement's formula as it bills Grantham, the 2026-27 budget lines ($1,604,411 for Lebanon Middle and $2,473,688 for Lebanon High, 31.4% of the operating budget, **derived**), the first FY2028 draft (+$417,453), the 0% open enrollment vote of March 3, 2026, and the legal route out of an AREA plan (RSA 195-A:14).
- **Taxes:** the final 2026 rates (local school $12.45 and state education $1.39, $13.84 together, below the $14.39 projected in March; the whole town rate is $21.40), the school share of a bill (64.7%, **derived**; 58.3% on Eastman property, **derived**), the 2027 revaluation, and the board's own HB 1300 numbers.
- **Governance:** SAU 75's three positions and $489,101 budget (3.8% of the operating budget, **derived**), the fall 2026 votes (all unanimous), who may speak and vote at the district meeting, and the non-voting committee seats at Lebanon.
- **Room and growth:** capacity 378, enrollment 235 in October 2026, class sizes against the board's new caps, and what the sources say about Eastman's year-round population (about 65% full time).
- **Eastman fees:** the Eastman Community Association's FYE2027 assessment ($4,501 for a house or condo) and the village district rate ($2.34 per $1,000).

**Main sources:** the Grantham School District's 2026 annual report, MS-26, warrant and budget slides; the board's budget, hearing and meeting minutes (February to October 2026); the board's HB 1300 slides, handout and hearing minutes; the November 17, 2026 superintendent report and FY2028 draft budget; the AREA agreement of 2021; the teachers' contract (2025-2028); the town's 2026 Master Plan and assessing page; the Eastman Community Association's fee pages; NH DOE's cost per pupil report; RSA 195-A; and the site's database (read-only).

## gov-area-agreements: AREA Schools (Authorized Regional Enrollment Areas)

- kind: term
- question_type: governance_money

   - citation: RSA 195-A:4

   - as_of: 2026-11-22

   - citation: RSA 195-A:14, I and II
   - source: https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/195-A/195-A-14.htm
