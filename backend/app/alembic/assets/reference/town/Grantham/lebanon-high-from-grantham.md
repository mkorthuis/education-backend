# Lebanon High School and the Hartford Center, From Grantham

Part of the Grantham research (town/Grantham). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Grantham's side of high school at Lebanon High School (LHS) and the Hartford Area Career and Technology Center (HACTC): the 2026-27 Grantham bus times to Lebanon (by route, including the Eastman route), whether any late bus or carpool is documented, what happens when Grantham closes for snow and Lebanon doesn't, how a Grantham student reaches the Hartford center, AP and college courses, a homeschooler's single classes, varsity tryouts, Lebanon High against Kimball Union Academy (KUA), SAT and college plans against other Upper Valley high schools, LHS's attendance and make-up rules, and its mental health and substance-use supports. Lebanon's own file (`town/Lebanon/high-school-and-pathways.md`), its middle file, the AREA agreement text saved in Lebanon's raw folder and the library are cited, not repeated.

- **14 fact topics, 52 facts and 4 number tables.** Thirteen topics are shared topics from `shared-topics.md`; one is proposed (`nh-youth-risk-behavior-survey`, below). Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 3.5 MB): Grantham's 7-12 bus page, cleaned copies of the 2026-27 AM and PM route sheets (streets and times only), four Grantham policies (JJJ, JJJ-R, JCLF, JCA-R), an excerpt of Grantham's 2026 annual report, LHS's 2026-27 handbooks and Program of Studies (copied from Lebanon's folder), the AREA agreement, KUA's tuition page and 2026-27 profile, ApprenticeshipNH's pages, Dartmouth's rideshare page, LHS's mental health page, a NH DHHS release and CSVs read from the site's database.
- **Places:** `district Grantham` (buses, policies, the AREA agreement, enrollment), `district Lebanon` (LHS rules, courses, athletics, support, outcomes), `district Kimball Union Academy` (KUA), and no place for two regional or statewide facts.
- **New data concepts:** Grantham's morning and afternoon bus times by route to Lebanon High and Middle with ride times (**derived**); the records Lebanon must send Grantham under the AREA agreement; a side-by-side of LHS, Hanover High, Mascoma Valley, Stevens and Newport on the grade 11 SAT, graduation, graduates' plans and habitual truancy (**derived** from the site's data); Grantham students as a share of LHS (**derived**); KUA's 2026-27 day tuition and aid.
- **New topic proposed:** `nh-youth-risk-behavior-survey` (kind how_it_works, question type comparison): what the state's Youth Risk Behavior Survey shows and what it can't show about one school. A library table set on the same survey exists in `national-comparisons.md`, but no topic.
- **Questions answered wholly by earlier files:** none of the 13. Lebanon's high school file answers parts of P2.3, P4.1 to P4.3, P17.1 and P20.3; each is cited.
- **Main gaps:** no late or activity bus and no carpool is documented; no source says whether a student kept home by a Grantham closure may practice that day, or when the Hartford buses return to LHS; Lebanon's policy JJJ (access for home-educated students) could not be read; no school-level survey data for LHS; KUA publishes no SAT average in its profile; the U.S. News page and NH DHHS regional survey reports were not reachable.
