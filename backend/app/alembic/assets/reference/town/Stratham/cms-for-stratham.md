# Cooperative Middle School for Stratham Students

Part of the Stratham research (town/Stratham). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Cooperative Middle School (CMS, grades 6–8, at 100 Academic Way in Stratham) from Stratham's side: how Stratham Memorial School (SMS) students move up, Stratham's share of each grade, the morning and late buses for Stratham, grading and the parent portal, math placement into Exeter High, counselors, vaping and phones, and the Stratham School District's own home education and access policies. It builds on `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md` (cited below as "Exeter CMS file") and `town/Exeter/exeter-high-school.md` ("Exeter High file"), which already cover CMS's teams, 6th-grade transition, Encore, clubs, sports, late-bus stops, bus rules, phone and vaping policies, the cooperative's home education policies (IHBG, JJJ) and Exeter High's course levels. Facts about CMS and Exeter High are placed `district Exeter Region Cooperative`; the Stratham School District's own policies, board minutes and SMS events `district Stratham`; SAU-wide documents `sau 16`.

- **9 fact topics, 32 facts and 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/middle/` (about 42 MB, most of it three cooperative board packets).
- **Main sources:** the Stratham School Board's policies IHBG, JJJ and JFAA and its minutes of June 3, September 16 and October 21, 2026 (draft); SMS's home page and June 1, 2026 newsletter; CMS pages (daily schedule, late-bus routes, incoming 6th grade, 8th graders preparing for EHS, counselors, seven team pages, curriculum, athletics, the Alma and Infinite Campus pages) and the CMS student/caregiver handbook; SAU 16's K–8 grading guidelines and news posts; CMS principal's reports in the cooperative board packets of September, October and November 2026; Exeter High's 2026-27 program of studies and November 2026 board report; the site's database (enrollment by town and school; spring 2026 state test results by grade); CDC's YRBS Explorer (New Hampshire and U.S. high school vaping, 2019–2025); OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the OSRM router.
- **New data concepts:** Stratham residents' share of the six towns' 5th graders, 6th graders and grades 6–8 (**derived**); grade 5 state test results at the six feeder schools side by side (**derived** tables); the SAU 16 K–8 grading scale and CMS's 80/20 grading weights; the Exeter High math prerequisite chain toward AP Calculus (**derived**); the road distance from CMS to the late bus's Stratham stop (**derived**); New Hampshire high school vaping rates as the nearest public measure.
- **Derived figures:** Stratham's 85 of 314 5th graders (about 27%), 64 of 335 6th graders (about 19%) and 219 of 965 grade 6–8 students (about 23%) in 2025-26; CMS about 1.9 times SMS's enrollment; CMS homeroom 65 minutes before the 8:30 a.m. start pediatricians recommend; CMS to Stratham Municipal Center 3.68 road miles and about 11 minutes. Only two public addresses (100 Academic Way and 10 Bunker Hill Avenue, Stratham) were sent to Nominatim and one route to OSRM, as `nhfacts-research`.
