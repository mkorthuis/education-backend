# Stratham Memorial School and Families

Part of the Stratham research (town/Stratham). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Stratham Memorial School (SMS, preschool to grade 5) for families: the school day, before- and after-school care, buses and dismissal changes, the Infinite Campus portal and other ways the school reaches families, the reading and math programs, report cards and tests, reading help and the Child Study Team, special education staffing and contacts, counseling and homework help, Responsive Classroom and what research says about it, meals and the free and reduced-price application, help with fees, the preschool, kindergarten orientation, and walking and biking on Gifford Farm Road.

- **15 topics, 80 facts, 1 number table.** Thirteen topics are shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; `kindergarten-registration` is the library's own topic (same kind, question type and description). One new shared topic is proposed: `research-classroom-behavior-approaches` (research, research): "What research finds about classroom approaches that join social-emotional learning with classroom management, such as Responsive Classroom, and their effects on achievement and behavior." Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 22 MB).
- **Sources:** the SMS 2026-27 Family Handbook; SMS web pages (home, registration, dismissal changes, counseling, special education, health, food services, technology, Safe Routes, staff listing); SMS's 2025-26 preschool flyer, 2026-27 calendar, before- and after-school list, welcome flyer and Tutor.com flyer; SMS newsletters of June 1 and October 13, 2026; Stratham School Board minutes and presentations from August 2025 to October 2026 and the November 18, 2026 agenda; Stratham policies EEA, EEAE, EFAA, EFC, IKB, JEB, JICC and JLD; SMS's meal letters; SAU 16's Child Find notice, K–8 report card letter, 2026-27 assessment letter, Infinite Campus, transportation, curriculum and communications pages; the YMCA's and Stratham Circle Learning Center's pages; PickUp Patrol's and ParentSquare's own pages; the town's public assistance and recreation pages, its 2019 Safe Routes to School Action Plan and 2023–2028 Capital Improvement Plan; the AERA abstract and IES award page for the Responsive Classroom trial; and the site's own data.
- **Main gaps:** prices and waitlist lengths for school-age care at SMS; whether any SMS buses run late or are combined (no record either way); how often reading help happens in the classroom versus pulled out; one-on-one speech and OT minutes or caseloads; picture day; what happens when no adult meets a young child at an afternoon stop; whether a recurring stop at a second address is allowed; the 2026-27 preschool tuition; and which Safe Routes improvements near SMS have been built.
- **Spot-checked against raw copies:** the handbook's "Changes to a child's dismissal plan must be communicated in PickUp Patrol no later than 1:30pm" (`sms-family-handbook-2026-27.txt`); the preschool flyer's "Three (3) Year Old Program: $2,700 / Four (4) Year Old Program: $3,800" (`sms-preschool-flyer-2025-26.transcription.txt`, checked against the rendered page); and the October 21, 2026 minutes' "SMS has 70 special education students in grades pre-K to 5, 3 students in out of district placements and 1 student attending a charter school" (`ssb-2026-10-21-minutes-draft.txt`). ## research-classroom-behavior-approaches: What Research Says About Classroom Behavior and Social-Emotional Approaches

- kind: research
- question_type: research
- description: What research finds about classroom approaches that join social-emotional learning with classroom management, such as Responsive Classroom, and their effects on achievement and behavior

1. Responsive Classroom, as described in a federally funded study, gives teachers a set of practices meant to create classrooms "with fewer behavior problems and more opportunities to learn": regular structured class meetings, a set approach to rules and consequences, academic choice, teacher language that focuses children on the process of learning, and set ways of introducing new material.
   - citation: Institute of Education Sciences, award R305A070063, "The Efficacy of the Responsive Classroom Approach for Improving Teacher Quality and Children's Academic Performance" (University of Virginia, 2007–2012)
   - source: https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/awards/efficacy-responsive-classroom-approach-improving-teacher-quality-and-childrens-academic-performance
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - note: Page: "The Responsive Classroom approach offers teachers a set of strategies designed to create efficient classroom environments with fewer behavior problems and more opportunities to learn. This is accomplished through: regular structured class meetings designed to be fun and intellectually engaging; an established approach to handling rules and consequences for behavior; procedures that offer academic choice to children; specific recommendations for teachers that focus children's attention on the process of learning, problem-solving, and reflecting on their work; and methods for introducing new academic material to students." Award $2,814,668. Raw: raw/elementary/ies-responsive-classroom-award.txt (.html)
2. In a randomized trial of 24 schools and 2,904 children followed from the end of grade 2 to grade 5, published in 2014, students at schools assigned to Responsive Classroom did not outperform those at comparison schools in math or reading, but classrooms that used the practices more showed higher achievement (effect sizes of 0.26 in math and 0.30 in reading, and 0.89 in math for students who started low), so the authors stressed how faithfully schools carry it out.
   - citation: Rimm-Kaufman, S. E., et al. (2014), "Efficacy of the Responsive Classroom Approach: Results From a 3-Year, Longitudinal Randomized Controlled Trial," American Educational Research Journal 51(3); abstract as posted by AERA
   - source: https://www.aera.net/Newsroom/Recent-AERA-Research/Efficacy-of-the-Responsive-Classroom-Approach-Results-From-a-3-Year-Longitudinal-Randomized-Controlled-Trial
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-11-21
   - note: Abstract: "Schools (n=24) were randomized into intervention and control conditions; 2,904 children were studied from end of second to fifth grade. Students at schools assigned to the RC condition did not outperform students at schools assigned to the control condition in math or reading achievement. Use of RC practices mediated the relation between treatment assignment and improved math and reading achievement ... ES relations between use of RC practices and achievement were .26 for math and .30 for reading. The RC practices and math achievement relation was greater for students with low initial math achievement (ES = .89). Results emphasize fidelity of implementation." An effect size is the difference measured in standard deviations. The trial was in a large urban Virginia district (IES page). Raw: raw/elementary/aera-responsive-classroom-rct.txt (.html)
3. The published abstract of the 2014 Responsive Classroom trial reports only reading and math results, so it does not say whether the approach changed classroom behavior; no school-level measure of behavior under Responsive Classroom at Stratham Memorial School was found.
   - citation: AERA posting of the Rimm-Kaufman et al. (2014) abstract; Stratham School Board minutes, August 2025 to October 2026
   - source: https://www.aera.net/Newsroom/Recent-AERA-Research/Efficacy-of-the-Responsive-Classroom-Approach-Results-From-a-3-Year-Longitudinal-Randomized-Controlled-Trial
