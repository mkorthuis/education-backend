# Stratham Residents' Questions

The questions the Stratham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Stratham is in SAU 16 with Exeter, Brentwood, East Kingston, Kensington and Newfields. The Stratham School District runs Stratham Memorial School (preschool to grade 5); grades 6–12 attend the Exeter Region Cooperative School District: Cooperative Middle School (in Stratham) and Exeter High School, which hosts the Seacoast School of Technology. The Stratham School District decides its budget at a traditional annual meeting; the cooperative votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (an 8:25 to 3:05 day at Stratham Memorial, a new special services wing and a renovation finishing in summer 2027, an $11.96 million bond, a 2026 tax rate of $14.50, a 7:25 start at Cooperative Middle School, a 500-student elementary school, Infinite Campus and Canvas portals, a new Electrical Technologies program at SST, a yearly homeschool filing) that the research should check rather than assume.
