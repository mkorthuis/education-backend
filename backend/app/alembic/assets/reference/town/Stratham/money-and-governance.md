# Stratham: Money, Governance, the School Renovation and Taxes

Part of the Stratham research (town/Stratham). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 80 facts and 8 number tables on Stratham's 2026 tax rate and what drives its school part, how the tax splits between the Stratham School District and the Exeter Region Cooperative, the Stratham Memorial School renovation and its $11,961,745 bond (including the leftover money now going to windows, flooring and a skylight in summer 2027), the Stratham School District's FY2027 budget and its drivers, the teachers' contract, enrollment and staffing trends, Stratham's state aid compared with other towns, the senior exemptions and deferral, HB 1300, how Stratham votes on two school budgets, the SAU 16 central office's cost to Stratham, who decides contracts and class sizes, renters' access, school capacity, and the private schools families use.
- **Taxes.** Stratham's 2026 rate is $14.50 per $1,000 (local school $10.55, state education $1.09, town $2.22, county $0.64), up $0.98, of which the local school part is $0.83 (84.7%, **derived**). In tax year 2025 the local school tax split 51.4% to the Stratham School District and 48.6% to the cooperative, about $5.00 and $4.72 per $1,000 (**derived**). The district's own FY2027 worksheet estimated a $0.77 rise for its articles.
- **The renovation.** Voters approved the bond by 3/5 ballot at the March 4, 2025 district meeting, with the board and its Financial Advisory Committee both recommending it 5–0; a 20-year bond sold in July 2025, with $1.12 million due in FY2027, the first year of principal. The project ran under budget (a projected $1.8 million surplus in April 2026), and on May 13, 2026 the board voted 5–0 to spend part of it (about $854,000–$860,000) on windows, flooring and a skylight, scheduled for summer 2027; about $500,000 was still unspent in October 2026.
- **Budget.** The FY2027 operating article was $17,498,091; the bond (+$821,409), health insurance (+$374,947) and special education tuition and transportation (+$305,000) drove the increase; the four-year teachers' contract ($380,684 in FY2027) passed on a 175–104 ballot. Stratham Memorial's enrollment fell from 600 (2014-15) to 501 (2025-26), and staff from 128.6 to 122.8 full-time equivalents (**derived**).
- **State aid.** Stratham's FY2027 state aid equals its cost of an adequate education, $4,873 per resident student against $7,078 statewide; it gets none of the extra grants that go to towns with less property wealth per student and more low-income students (**derived**).
- **Governance.** Each of SAU 16's seven boards hires and negotiates for its own schools; the joint board of all seven governs the superintendent's office, which cost Stratham $404,947 in FY2027 (12.60% of SAU assessments).

**Main sources:** the Stratham School District's 2026-27 and 2025-26 budget folders (warrants, MS-26, annual meeting presentation and minutes, report to voters), Stratham School Board minutes, agendas and presentations (October 2025–November 2026, including the February 2026 tax "fireside chat" slides), Stratham policies JF, JFAA, EEA and DB, the 2026–2030 teachers' contract, the Town of Stratham's tax rate, property tax, exemption, revaluation and recreation pages, its 2025 Town Report and 2026 Voter Information Guide, Phillips Exeter Academy's and St. Thomas Aquinas's tuition pages, and the site's database for derived comparisons.
