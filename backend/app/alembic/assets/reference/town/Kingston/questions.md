# Kingston Residents' Questions

The questions the Kingston research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Kingston's students attend the Sanborn Regional School District (SAU 17), shared with Newton. Sanborn runs Daniel J. Bakie School (preschool to grade 2) and Memorial School (grades 3–5), Sanborn Regional Middle School (grades 6–8) and Sanborn Regional High School (grades 9–12) at 17 Danville Road in Kingston. Students may take career programs at the Seacoast School of Technology in Exeter. Several questions contain premises (state test scores around 44%, falling enrollment, the Kingston–Newton cost split, a budget survey asking what to cut, teacher turnover, Pickup Patrol and Alma, a new state budget cap) that the research should check rather than assume.
