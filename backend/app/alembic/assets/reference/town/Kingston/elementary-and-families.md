# Bakie and Memorial Schools and Young Families in Kingston

Part of the Kingston research (town/Kingston). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

Covers the Sanborn Regional School District's two elementary schools, Daniel J. Bakie Elementary School (preschool to grade 2, 179 Main Street, Kingston) and Memorial Elementary School (grades 3–5, 31 West Main Street, Newton), and the questions of families with young children: Bakie's half-day preschool; school hours, drop-off, pickup and dismissal changes; the YMCA's before- and after-school care inside Bakie; which child care centers the buses stop at; the 2026-27 calendar, early release days and no-school days; storm delays and how alerts go out; the apps families use (Alma, PickUp Patrol, MySchoolBucks and, since fall 2026, the Apptegy "Sanborn Regional Schools" app with classroom Rooms); registration papers; school meals and the meal application; reading screening and help (WIN blocks, Title I, the reading specialist); report cards and conferences; kindergarten and first-grade class sizes and staffing; speech and occupational therapy providers; school nurses; English learner support and translation; and nearby care on days school is closed (Kingston Children's Center, YMCA Camp Lincoln).

Main sources: the 2026-27 Bakie Family Handbook and Memorial Student Handbook; the schools' and district's Apptegy web pages, staff directories and live feeds; the superintendent's August 21, 2026 welcome letter; the 2026-27 calendar and bus routes; school board policies (IIB, EBCE, EEA, EFAA, IHBBA, JLC, IKB-R); school board minutes (September 2025 and May–October 2026); the district's special education procedures plan and program descriptions; school newsletters (Smore); the Southern District YMCA's, YMCA Camp Lincoln's and Kingston Children's Center's own pages; NH DOE's 2024-25 Title I school list; federal Civil Rights Data Collection 2023-24; and vendor help pages (Apptegy, PickUp Patrol).

**Counts:** 10 topics, 60 facts, 2 number tables.

**Main gaps:** the October 1, 2026 kindergarten and first-grade enrollment at Bakie (NH DOE's iPlatform refused automated requests; the district's summer registration counts are used); whether every first-grade room has an aide; any record of how reliably IEP speech and OT minutes are delivered; past examples of when storm alerts actually went out; whether report cards or Alma can be translated; whether Sanborn voters ever approved busing to child care programs under RSA 189:6-b; and the Kingston town website (HTTP 403).
