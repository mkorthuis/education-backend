# Sanborn Regional Middle and High School, and Sanborn's Side of the Seacoast School of Technology

Part of the Kingston research (town/Kingston). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Sanborn Regional Middle School (grades 6–8) and Sanborn Regional High School (grades 9–12), which share one building at 17 Danville Road in Kingston, and Sanborn's side of the Seacoast School of Technology (SST) in Exeter. It answers the Kingston questions on test scores, SST, college credit, advanced math, computer science, devices and homework, fees, AP and Early College, college planning, career preparation, sports and the gym, shop classes, and music and drama. SST itself (programs, sessions, admission, credentials, college credit) is in `town/Exeter/cms-sst-and-getting-to-school.md`, cited by topic and fact number. Every fact here is placed `district Sanborn Regional`, because Newton shares the district and its research will cite this file.

- **10 fact topics, 61 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 25 MB): `board/` (board, committee and budget documents, the program of studies, the professional staff contract's stipend pages), `policy/` (17 board policies and the policy listing), `crdc/` (federal Civil Rights Data Collection extracts), `web/` and `geo/`.
- **Main sources:** Sanborn Regional High School's 2026-27 Program of Studies (approved by the board April 1, 2026); school board minutes from October 2025 to October 2026, the September 16, 2026 principals' and superintendent's reports, the April 15, 2026 board presentation on high school scores, the October 2026 Sanborn Seminary grant list, the 2026–2031 district goals and the November 4, 2026 FY28 budget presentation; the October 1, 2026 enrollment sheet; the 2026-27 fine arts calendar; the professional staff contract's 2024–2027 stipend schedule (scanned, transcribed from image); board policies IHCD, IMBD, IK, IKF, ILBAA, IMBC, IHBH, JJIB, JQ, JQ-R, IKB-R, JICL, JLD, IFA, IJOA, EEA and EEAG; the federal Civil Rights Data Collection for 2023-24; CCSNH's 2024-25 Early College report; SST's own documents as saved by the Exeter research.
