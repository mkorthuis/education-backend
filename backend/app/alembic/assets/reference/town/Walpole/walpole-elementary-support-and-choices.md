# Walpole Elementary School, Grades 2–8: Support and Choices

Part of the Walpole research (town/Walpole). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Walpole Elementary School (WES, 8 Bemis Lane) in the Fall Mountain Regional School District (SAU 60) and the supports and choices around it: 8th grade math, test scores against Keene and Hanover, advanced classes, special education staffing and therapy, counselors and calming supports, bullying and phones, sports and clubs, a late bus, homeschooled and EFA students, one-on-one reading help, Chromebook help, field trips, band and the 8th-grade trip.

- **16 topics, 81 facts, 9 tables.** All topics are shared slugs placed at `district Fall Mountain Regional` (one general fact has no place). No new state-rule facts: the state and federal rules are cited from the library and earlier towns.
- **Main sources:** the Walpole Schools website (walpole.sau60.org) and its feeds; the Walpole Elementary Student Handbook (a scan made August 22, 2024, so it describes the school before the 2026 consolidation); the Walpole Attendance Area Summer 2026 Newsletter; the November 9, 2026 version of the district's FY28 Walpole budget; the October 26, 2026 FY28 budget presentation; the district's 2024, 2025 and 2026 annual reports; 2026 school board minutes; the district's home education page; the SAU staff directory; and the site's own database (read-only) for test scores, lunch eligibility, bullying counts and enrollment.
- **Main findings:**
  - **The school changed in 2026.** North Walpole School's grades 2–4 moved into Walpole Elementary for 2026-27, so the school now has grades 2–8 (it was grades 5–8 in 2024-25 and 2025-26). There is no school named "Walpole Middle School", though the school board's 2026 donation lists use that name.
  - **Test scores.** In spring 2026, 41% of Walpole Elementary's grade 8 students were proficient or above in math, against 32% at Keene Middle School, 68% at Frances C. Richmond School (Hanover's middle school) and 40% statewide. Across all its grades Walpole's math share fell from 45% (2024) to 35% (2026) and its reading share from 70% to 51%. The principal's summer 2026 newsletter says middle school math proficiency "declined" over several years.
  - **Special education staffing.** The FY28 proposal lists 2 special education teachers (1.5 FTE) and 6 paraprofessionals (about 4.7 FTE) at Walpole Elementary, none marked vacant. Speech, occupational therapy and psychology are budgeted district-wide, not by school; the district reports trouble hiring related-service staff.
  - **Counselors.** The FY28 proposal lists a 1.0 FTE counselor at Walpole Elementary plus a second counselor listed at 0.5 FTE under both Walpole Elementary and Walpole Primary. No quiet room or sensory room is described in any document found.
  - **Sports and activities.** Soccer, basketball, volleyball, softball, band, chorus, drama, math team and clubs are named in the handbook, annual reports or budget. No late bus for these activities was found: the only late run the district posts is the high school's.
  - **The 8th-grade trip.** In April 2026 the school board recorded community gifts toward "the Washington DC trip for the 8th grade students" and 8th-grade trips; what families pay is not stated.
- **Main gaps:** what the LEAP program (a district special education program housed at Walpole Elementary) serves; whether Walpole Elementary offers Algebra I or any gifted program; the 2026-27 student handbook; who handles Chromebook problems for Walpole families; per-school speech, OT and psychologist time; whether any child has missed services; the district's current home education procedure (IHBG-R); the math improvement section the summer newsletter promises.
