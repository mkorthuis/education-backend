# Walpole Residents' Questions

The questions the Walpole research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Walpole's students attend the Fall Mountain Regional School District (SAU 60), a cooperative of Acworth, Alstead, Charlestown, Langdon and Walpole: Walpole Primary School (preschool to grade 1) and Walpole Elementary School (grades 2–8) in Walpole, and Fall Mountain Regional High School in Alstead and Langdon, with the Cheshire Career Center in Keene and the River Valley Technical Center in Springfield, Vermont nearby. Several questions contain premises (a North Walpole School on Bemis Lane and an October community forum about it, Walpole pulling out of the Fall Mountain cooperative, the Cooperative Review Committee's plan, a "Walpole Middle School", a Hooper Institute garden program, superintendent turnover, meetings out in Langdon or Charlestown, a Len-Tex plant) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. Sarah, 34 — Nurse at Cheshire Medical Center, mom of a 1st grader at Walpole Primary School and a 3-year-old

- **P1.2** How hard is it to get a Pre-K spot at Walpole Primary, and does it fill up by lottery or first-come, first-served?
- **P1.3** When there's a snow day or an early release, how much notice do parents usually get before the buses roll?
