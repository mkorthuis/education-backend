# Fall Mountain Regional High School and Career Programs, From Walpole

Part of the Walpole research (town/Walpole). Gathered 2026-11-22.

## Summary

Covers Fall Mountain Regional High School (FMRHS, 134 FMRHS Road, Langdon) and the career programs Walpole students can reach: the Cheshire Career Center in Keene, River Valley Technical Center (RVTC) in Springfield, Vermont, and Fall Mountain's own career and technical education (CTE) center. Charlestown's file (`town/Charlestown/fall-mountain-high.md`) already covers the high school's catalog, CTE, AP and college courses, the late bus and River Valley budget cuts, so this file writes Walpole's side only: how far Walpole is from each site, who in Walpole moved to keep River Valley open, the high school's board policies on credit for returning homeschoolers, tuition students, athletics, advanced coursework and devices, the Walpole withdrawal study's plan for high school tuition, the board's 2026 meetings on freshmen, Chromebooks, repairs and vacancies, the Fall Mountain Scholarship Fund, the district's budget lines for agriculture, a derived comparison of graduates' plans over 14 years, teacher pay schedules at Fall Mountain, Keene and a Springfield, Vermont employer, and Walpole's capital reserve and FY2027 tax estimate. The main sources are Fall Mountain's board policies (the District Policies folder on Google Drive), its board minutes and budget papers (saved by the Charlestown research), the 2026 annual report (Acworth's copy), the Walpole withdrawal study reports and the district's withdrawal Q&A (Walpole Clarion, February 2026), River Valley's calendar and teacher agreement, the National FFA's About page, ProPublica's copy of the Fall Mountain Scholarship Fund's IRS Form 990 data, and the site's own database. The SAU 60 and FMRHS websites returned a bot check to automated requests (not retried); the district's Google Drive documents and the Walpole Schools site (walpole.sau60.org) could be read.
