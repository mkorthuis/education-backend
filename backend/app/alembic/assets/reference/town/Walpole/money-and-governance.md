# Walpole: Money, Taxes and Governance of the Fall Mountain Regional School District

Part of the Walpole research (town/Walpole). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 8 topics, 66 facts and 10 number tables on how Walpole's schools are paid for and governed inside the five-town Fall Mountain Regional School District (SAU 60), written from Walpole's side of the cooperative: the North Walpole School building and the move of grades 2 to 4 to Walpole Elementary (8 Bemis Lane); Walpole's 2025-26 withdrawal plan, its cost estimates, the minority report and the March 10, 2026 vote that rejected it; the Cooperative Review Committee's work through October 5, 2026; what Walpole is charged and why its school tax per pupil is higher than Charlestown's; where Walpole's property tax and state aid come from; how the district's budget splits between classrooms, buildings and buses; who has led the district and its high school; and how Walpole parents can reach a board that meets in Langdon. Charlestown's file (`town/Charlestown/money-and-governance.md`) already holds the Articles of Agreement, the failed 2026 budget, the Senior Educational Official and the district-wide budget; this file cites it and adds only Walpole's side.

- **North Walpole School:** grades 2 to 4 already moved to Walpole Elementary for 2026-27 after the board's unanimous March 23, 2026 vote. The district says the building "has not been formally closed." Since House Bill 1374 took effect October 8, 2026, closing or relocating it would need a vote of all five towns, expected in March 2027. The board's two-year plan set a decision date of November 9, 2026; that meeting's outcome was not posted by November 22. The building's own costs are $66,828 (FY2027) and $72,156 (draft FY2028).
- **Withdrawal:** Walpole voters rejected leaving, 336 to 440 (district-wide 580 to 1,929). The study committee's own budget table does not add up to its printed total (lines $8,577,839, printed $7,947,217), it could not get Fall Mountain's high school tuition rate, and its tuition guess was $20,000 a student; Walpole's current share of the high school's costs is about $3.3 to $3.4 million a year (about $28,000 a student, derived).
- **Cooperative Review Committee:** meeting since August 3, 2026; members called the status quo unacceptable, are studying three options in parallel (revised cost sharing, town-run pre-K to 8 schools with a shared high school, moving grades 7 and 8 to the high school) and expect up to 18 months, with no article for March 2027.
- **Taxes (derived):** Walpole's local school tax rose 58% from 2014 to 2025 while resident students fell 15%; for 2025-26 78% of its school funding was local tax, 14% state adequacy aid and 8% the state education tax kept in town. Its per-pupil local assessment is higher than Charlestown's mainly because Walpole's adequacy aid is $3,213 a pupil against Charlestown's $8,571.

**Main sources:** the district's North Walpole School plan and October 26, 2026 forum minutes; 19 sets of board minutes (July 2025 to October 2026); the 2016 to 2026 annual reports; the FY27 and FY28 budget documents (apportionment, by function, by location, deliberative slides, October 26, 2026 presentation, Walpole budget lines); the Walpole withdrawal plan and minority report, the Langdon study and the Selectboard letters; the Cooperative Review Committee's minutes; RSA 194:61 and the 2026 school closure and withdrawal rules; the site's database.
