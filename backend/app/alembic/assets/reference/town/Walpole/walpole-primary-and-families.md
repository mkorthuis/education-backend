# Walpole Primary and Elementary: Early Grades and Families

Part of the Walpole research (town/Walpole). Gathered November 22, 2026.

## Summary

Covers the early grades and young families at Walpole Primary School (WPS, pre-K to grade 1) and Walpole Elementary School (WES, now grades 2 to 8): care before and after school, pre-K, snow days and early release, the bus ride from North Walpole and from Drewsville, bus stops, free and reduced-price meals, public preschool and playgroups, class size, the Hooper Institute's school programs, summer camps and meals, how announcements reach families, building safety, and what the move of North Walpole School's students to WES means for reading help and specials. Sources are the Walpole Schools site (news posts, flyers, documents), the Walpole Attendance Area principal's Summer 2026 newsletter, Fall Mountain's board minutes, 2024 and 2025 annual reports, 2026-27 calendar, storm procedures, transportation handbook and Walpole bus route sheets, the Hooper Institute's, Walpole Village School's, Walpole Town Library's and Southwestern Community Services' own pages, and the site's database.

**Counts:** 17 topics, 58 facts, 1 number table (`walpole_bus_ride_minutes_2026_27`). No proposed topics: the shared slugs fit.

**Main findings:**
- North Walpole School's students (grades 2 to 4) already moved into Walpole Elementary for 2026-27 (the board voted March 23, 2026; by September 14 the administration called the move successful and said the building was to be unoccupied). Route 4 now carries them: the scheduled morning ride is up to 35 minutes from the first stop and the afternoon ride up to 27 minutes (derived). The school board's community forum on the North Walpole building was held October 26, 2026; the building's future (a decision "by November 9, 2026" under the board's two-year plan) is in agent 4's `money-and-governance.md`.
- Drewsville is part of the Walpole Attendance Area, not a separate zone; its children attend WPS and then WES. Route 3 serves Drewsville and is scheduled at 40 minutes from its first stop in the morning (derived), against about 10 minutes by direct drive.
- No before- or after-school care run by the school was found. The only licensed Walpole program for school-age children in the site's data (Home Away From Home) posts no hours or rates; Walpole Village School's 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. "Full Day Extended" schedule is for ages 2 to 5.
- Walpole Primary added a morning Preschool (3-year-olds) alongside Pre-K (4-year-olds) for 2026-27; no lottery, first-come rule or waiting list is posted. The Walpole Town Library holds Wiggle Time (Mondays 10 a.m.) and Story Time (Wednesdays 10 a.m.).
- The Hooper Institute, a Town of Walpole program, says it teaches in all classes from pre-K through grade 8 this school year, runs a garden at each school, and ran 2026 summer camps for grades 1 to 8 that are "not a school sponsored event". No Walpole site was listed for 2026 summer meals; the nearest were Vilas Middle School in Alstead (7.2 miles) and the Silsby Library in Charlestown (11.1 miles).
