# Concord Residents' Questions

The questions the Concord research is meant to answer. Forty resident profiles, three questions each (120 in all, twice the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. The profiles came numbered 1–40 in four groups; the numbers and group headings are kept. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Concord is SAU 8. The Concord School District enrolled 3,832 students in 2024-25 in five elementary schools (Abbot-Downing, Beaver Meadow and Christa McAuliffe; Mill Brook for preschool to grade 2 and Broken Ground for grades 3–5), Rundlett Middle School (grades 6–8) and Concord High School, home of the Concord Regional Technical Center. The district is governed by a charter: its Board of Education adopts the budget, with no district meeting or ballot vote. A new middle school to replace Rundlett is planned. Penacook, a village of the city, is in the Merrimack Valley School District (SAU 46) in the state's district list, not the Concord School District. Several questions contain premises (the new school's cost and site, Penacook's schools, walk zones, a building's age, comparisons with Nashua and St. Paul's School) that the research should check rather than assume.
