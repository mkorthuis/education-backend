# Concord Elementary Schools and the Early Years

Part of the Concord research (town/Concord). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Concord's five elementary schools and the years before them: gifted programs and math acceleration toward grade 8 Algebra, after-school and no-school-day care for Mill Brook families, reading assessments and how parents hear about them, phone and smartwatch rules, kindergarten class sizes, iPads, Chromebooks and home internet, public preschool and the idea of universal pre-K, handoffs from preschools to kindergarten, the 2026-27 calendar, and outdoor science at White Farm.

- **10 topics, 62 facts, 2 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 36 MB, 119 files). Eight topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; one adds a state-law fact to the library's own `parental-bill-of-rights` topic. No new topic is proposed.
- **Sources:** the district's own pages and 2026-27 handbooks, its posted policies, the Board of Education's 2025 and 2026 minutes and packets (including the Great Schools Partnership opportunity audit of March 2026, the monthly enrollment reports with every class's size, and the instructional committee's iPad review), the updated strategic plan (January 2026), the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (2021-22 and 2023-24), NCES's Digest of Education Statistics, RSA 189-B, the Granite YMCA's program pages, the Concord Public Library and one Concord Monitor article.
- **Main gaps:** the hours, eligibility and waitlist size of the 21C after-school program at Mill Brook; how students are chosen for Rundlett's grade 8 Algebra I, and any elementary math acceleration; the preschool's tuition amounts; any process for private preschools to hand off information to kindergarten teachers; what CRDC's internet-access columns mean (the data element list returned HTTP 403); whether any school uses White Park or Oak Hill. The web search budget ran out partway through, so a few community checks (for example the Boys & Girls Club, whose site didn't connect) were not made.
- **Spot-checked against raw copies:** Policy 641's "If class size exceeds 20 students in kindergarten" (`policy-641-class-size-guidelines.txt`); the October 2026 kindergarten sections 14, 14, 14, 14, 14, 14 at Mill Brook and 17, 17, 17 at Christa McAuliffe (`boe-packet-2026-11-02-p70-enrollment-oct26.png`); and the YMCA's "After School - Mill Brook ... Mon – Fri 2:30 – 6:00 PM ... All 27 weeks wait-listed" (`granite-ymca-school-age-child-care.txt`).
