# Concord: The New Middle School

Part of the Concord research (town/Concord). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

**The main finding: the new middle school is being built at the current Rundlett site on South Street, not at Broken Ground.** The Board of Education chose Broken Ground in December 2023, reversed that 6-3 on January 22, 2025, approved the South Street project 8-1 on October 23, 2025, and authorized borrowing 9-0 on January 28, 2026. Construction started in 2026, a groundbreaking ceremony was held on September 26, 2026, and the district's timeline has students moving in at the 2028-29 holiday break. The architect's page says the school opens in 2029. The old 1957 building is to be demolished after the move, and new fields will go where it stands. Many of the residents' questions assume Broken Ground, so the answers below start from the actual site.

**What's covered:** 5 topics, 62 facts and 2 number tables:
- the current site, status, design, size, schedule and construction progress;
- costs from the 2024 cap to the October 2026 Guaranteed Maximum Price (table), the district's line-by-line site comparisons (table) and its own comparison with Nashua's McCarthy Middle School;
- the votes and process: the 2023, 2025 and 2026 board votes, the charter's borrowing rule, the 2024 charter amendments requiring a public vote to relocate a school or sell land, public hearings, staff and student input, the building committee and the naming survey;
- outreach and translation: the 2023 hearing invitations in French and Swahili, and project updates;
- construction effects at the South Street site (noise, hours, athletics moved) and what the Broken Ground land studies found (no wetlands or vernal pools; city trails), with the land's possible sale;
- energy: ground-source heat pumps (84 wells), solar, the utility energy target and LEED;
- the old building: its age, the historical review, why the district rejected renovation, demolition and fields.

**Sources:** the district's middle school project page and the files it links (HMFH Architects and Harvey Construction presentations, cost estimates, studies, the 2023 Broken Ground wetland and wildlife reports, timelines), Board of Education and committee minutes and packets from December 2023 to November 2026, the district charter (July 1, 2025) and November 2024 ballot, the City of Concord's planning staff report (January 21, 2026), a 2022 NH Division of Historical Resources review form and the architect's project page.
