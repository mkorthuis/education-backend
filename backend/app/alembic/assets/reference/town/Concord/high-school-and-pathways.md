# Concord High School: Academics, the CRTC and Pathways After High School

Part of the Concord research (town/Concord). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Concord High School's academic program and the Concord Regional Technical Center (CRTC): career and technical education (CTE) programs, credentials and college credit; AP courses, course levels and grading; dual enrollment with NHTI, SNHU and Manchester Community College; extended learning opportunities (ELOs) and internships; computer science and the district's artificial intelligence policy; graduation requirements, financial literacy and civics; AP participation by race and ethnicity; the Seal of Biliteracy and the new Seal of Civic Excellence; remedial math placement at NHTI; St. Paul's School's approach to AP; and adult diploma, HiSET, English and job-training options in Concord.
