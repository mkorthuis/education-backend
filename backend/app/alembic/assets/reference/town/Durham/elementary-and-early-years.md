# Mast Way, Moharimet and the Early Years in Oyster River

Part of the Durham research (town/Durham). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers the Oyster River Cooperative School District's two K–4 schools, Mast Way School (in Lee) and Moharimet School (in Madbury), and the years around them, for Durham families: how children are assigned to a school and how to ask for a change, proof of residency for renters, buses and drive times, before- and after-school care, the Preschool Education Program, meal prices and the privacy of meal applications, field trip and other fees, class-size guidelines and October 2026 enrollment, the new K–5 reading curriculum and the screeners the district uses (including its dyslexia screener), progress reports, how schools reach families and translate, counseling, English learner services and grade placement for children arriving from abroad.

- **13 topics, 75 facts, 3 number tables.** Twelve topics use shared slugs from `town/shared-topics.md`; one (`school-meals-eligibility`) is the library's own topic, used with its kind, question type and description for two federal rules on who may see meal-application information. No new topic is proposed. Raw copies are in `raw/elementary/` (about 46 MB): district pages, the 2026-27 handbooks, 13 board policies (`policies/`), nine board minutes and packets (`board/`), the Infofinder bus lookups (`infofinder/`), drive-time files (`drive/`) and page images (`img/`).
- **Sources:** orcsd.org pages (registration, transportation, student services, ESOL, Title I, child nutrition, ParentSquare, academics and assessment, the preschool, both schools' counseling and staff pages); the 2026-27 Mast Way and Moharimet handbooks; Policies IIB, IIB-A, JCA, JEB, EEA, EFC, JQ, IKA, IKA-R, IKE, IKE-R, IHB, IHBAA; the LAU (English learner) plan; Mast Way's 2026-27 Title I plan; the registration and meal forms; School Board minutes of May 6, June 3, July 22, August 5, August 19, October 7 and November 4, 2026, with the October 1, 2026 enrollment report and the November 2026 assessment slides; the December 2024 facilities assessment; the district's Infofinder bus lookup; Growing Places' and Durham Parks and Recreation's pages and the Seven Rivers program guide; 7 CFR 245.6; OSRM and Nominatim (under `nhfacts-research`) for drive times; and the site's own database (class size, enrollment, child care) to check premises.
- **Main gaps:** the scale used on elementary progress reports (the 1–4 marks in P13.1 are not described in any public document found); how many minutes a week ESOL students are served; how the preschool chooses children without disabilities and whether it charges them; rates for the Live and Learn program at Mast Way and Growing Places' "Our Time" (the Seven Rivers rates are posted); whether buses stop at child care centers; enrollment projections; and street-by-street school assignments for most of Durham (the bus lookup returned stops only for Packers Falls Road and Mast Road).
- **Spot-checked against raw copies:** Policy IIB-A's "a reasonable assignment process to maintain parity in grade levels between schools supersedes zone assignments" (`policies/IIB-A.txt`); the June 3, 2026 minutes' "Matt Bacon made a motion to approve the adoption of the Core Knowledge Language Arts Program as presented ... ## school-meals-eligibility: Free and Reduced-Price School Meals: Who Qualifies

- kind: rule

1. Under federal school meal rules, a school may share a child's name and free or reduced-price meal status without the parent's consent only with people who run a federal education program, a state health or education program run by the state or local education agency, certain low-income nutrition programs, or verification contractors, and those who receive it may use it only for that program.

   - source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-245.6
   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15
   - note: Text: "the State agency or local educational agency, as appropriate, may disclose, without parental consent, children's names and eligibility status ... to persons directly connected with the administration or enforcement of: (i) A Federal education program; (ii) A State health program or State education program administered by the State or local education agency; (iii) A Federal, State, or local means-tested nutrition program with eligibility standards comparable to the National School Lunch Program ...; or (iv) A third party contractor assisting in verification" and "Individuals and programs to which children's free and reduced price meal eligibility information has been disclosed under this section may use the information only in the administration or enforcement of the receiving program.    - citation: 7 CFR 245.6(i) and (k)

   - as_of: 2026-11-21
   - review_by: 2027-08-15

(3) There must be a statement informing parents and guardians that failing to sign the consent will not affect the child's eligibility" and "(k) ... any individual who publishes, divulges, discloses or makes known in any manner, or to any extent not authorized by statute or this section, any information obtained under this section will be fined not more than $1,000 or imprisoned for up to 1 year, or both."
