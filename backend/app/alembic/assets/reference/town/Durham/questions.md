# Durham Residents' Questions

The questions the Durham research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Durham's public students attend the Oyster River Cooperative School District (SAU 5), shared with Lee and Madbury: the Oyster River Preschool Education Program, Mast Way School (in Lee) and Moharimet School (in Madbury), both kindergarten to grade 4, Oyster River Middle School (grades 5–8) and Oyster River High School (grades 9–12), both in Durham. Oyster River students may take career programs at Dover's career center. The district votes by official ballot. Several questions contain premises (both elementary schools outside Durham, a $9.8 million elementary bond that failed by 41 votes, Question 2 voted down, 77% of the budget on salaries and benefits, a paraeducator contract, a turf replacement fund, an 8:15 high school start, a superintendent search, an open enrollment article, $519,000 added at the deliberative session) that the research should check rather than assume.
