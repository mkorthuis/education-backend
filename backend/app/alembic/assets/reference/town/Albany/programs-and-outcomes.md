# Programs, Outcomes and Policies at Conway's Schools for Albany Students

Part of the Albany research (town/Albany). Gathered November 13, 2026.

## Summary

Albany runs no schools, so its children attend Conway's Pine Tree Primary, Conway Intermediate, Kennett Middle (KMS) and Kennett High, with the Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center at Kennett. Most school-side questions were already answered in `town/Conway/schools-k-8.md`, `town/sau9/kennett-and-sau9.md`, `town/sau9/kennett-pathways.md` and the Jackson files; the question table maps each question to those facts first. This file adds what was missing or Albany-specific: what Albany's contracts and board record say about outcome data for Albany students, whether Albany students can be separated in test results, outdoor programs at KMS, heavy equipment and family-business work for credit, seat rules at the career center, students' race and ethnicity, the school resource officer and emergency procedures, staffing shortages, Conway's curriculum, library and survey policies, and alumni transcripts.

- **15 fact topics, 84 facts and 3 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/programs/` (about 13 MB): Albany's five tuition contracts (current K–6, 7–8 and 9–12, and the 2027 7–8; OCR text), Albany board minutes, agendas and the 2026 annual report, 20 Conway policies, two KMS newsletters, NCES school pages, RSA 188-E:5–7, U.S. Department of Labor Fact Sheet #43, WMCC program pages, Kennett's alumni transcript form, saved site-data queries, and text copies of the earlier-research files cited here (`raw/programs/from-earlier/`).
- **Places:** `district Conway` for Conway's schools, `district Albany` for Albany's contracts, board, students and policies, `sau 9` for SAU-wide staffing, `town Berlin` for one WMCC fact, and no place for state and federal rules.
- **New data concepts:** the reporting clauses in all five Albany contracts; the absence of Albany-specific outcome data in Albany's minutes, packets and annual report; Albany's share of each Conway school and why a town-level result would be suppressed; state CTE admission and cost rules; federal hazardous-occupation limits on teenagers working in a parent's business; Conway's SRO contract and budget by school; and Conway's policies on objections to course and library materials and on non-academic surveys.
- **Proposed new shared topics (not yet in `shared-topics.md`):**
  - `district-parent-rights-and-materials` (local, local_practice): "How a district lets parents see and object to curriculum, library and health materials, opt children out of lessons, surveys and state tests, and publishes the Parental Bill of Rights."
  - `cte-admission-and-cost-rules` (rule, rules): "State rules on who may enroll in a regional career and technical education center, space and prerequisites, and who pays tuition and transportation for students from other districts."
  - `youth-employment-rules` (rule, rules): "Federal and state rules on the ages, hours and kinds of work teenagers may do, including in a parent's business and in work-based learning."
- **Premises checked:**
  - P6.1 "heavy equipment programs at the career center": no such program exists. The center has no heavy equipment or diesel program; welding is taught inside Advanced Manufacturing; the nearest diesel and heavy equipment program found is White Mountains Community College's in Berlin.
  - P4.2 "gifted and talented ... at Kennett Middle": Conway has no gifted program or policy (earlier Conway file); acceleration starts with grade 8 Algebra 1a.
  - P6.3 "equal priority for limited CTE slots": seats are limited (16 stations a class), but no rule or practice found gives Conway residents priority; Albany's contracts promise "the same opportunities" (2003) and "equal access" (2027).
  - P4.3 "buried inside Conway's aggregate reporting": public data is by school, not by town, and Albany's grade groups are too small to report. Albany's contracts do require reports to the Albany board, but none appears in its minutes or posted packets from 2019 to November 2026.
  - P6.2 "paid work-study apprenticeship with my excavation company": paid co-ops and ELOs exist, but no named Kennett apprenticeship exists (earlier file); federal rules bar workers under 18 from backhoes, skid-steers and most trenching even in a parent's business.
  - P16.3 "resource officers ... across Conway's school buildings": Conway contracts one SRO arrangement with the Conway Police Department; the budget charges most of the cost to Kennett High, with small amounts at KMS and the two elementary schools.
  - P18.1 asks about Kennett High; the policies that apply are Conway's. Albany's own posted policy folders have no curriculum, library or opt-out policies.
  - P12.1 and P14.3: no premise problems found.
- **Main gaps:**
  - No public results for Albany students by grade, and no posted copy of any quarterly report under Albany's contracts.
  - No teacher or paraprofessional turnover rate for Pine Tree or Conway Intermediate.
  - No data on how Kennett graduates place into non-credit courses at WMCC or elsewhere.
  - No written Conway form or procedure for exempting a child from the state test.
  - No KMS partnership with the White Mountain National Forest or forestry course.
  - No rule on whether a parent's business can host an ELO or co-op.

## youth-employment-rules: Federal Rules on Teenagers Working, Including in a Parent's Business

2. Federal Hazardous Occupations Order 7 bars workers under 18 from operating, riding on or helping operate most power-driven hoisting equipment, including forklifts, skid-steers, skid-steer loaders, backhoes, manlifts, scissor lifts, boom trucks and cranes.
   - citation: U.S. Department of Labor, Fact Sheet #43, "HO 7. Power-driven hoisting apparatus"
   - source: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/43-child-labor-non-agriculture
   - as_of: 2026-11-13

   - citation: U.S. Department of Labor, Fact Sheet #43, "HO 17. Trenching and excavation operations"
   - source: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/43-child-labor-non-agriculture
