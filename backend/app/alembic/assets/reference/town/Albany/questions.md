# Albany Residents' Questions

The questions the Albany research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 13, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Albany runs no schools. SAU 9 lists tuition contracts between Albany and the Conway School District for grades K–6, 7–8 and 9–12, so Albany students attend Conway's schools: Pine Tree Primary (K–2), Conway Intermediate (3–5), A. Crosby Kennett Middle (6–8) and Kennett High (9–12), with the Mount Washington Valley Career and Technical Center. Several questions contain premises (school names, contract lengths, policies, figures) that the research should check rather than assume.

## P1. The Route 16 Hospitality Worker & Single Mom
Age 32. Single mother of a 1st grader and 4th grader; front-desk supervisor at a North Conway hotel; rents an apartment near the Conway town line; household income about $44,000.

- **P1.1** Since Albany tuitions K–6 kids into Conway, do I get any say in whether my kids go to Pine Tree Elementary or Conway Elementary, or is it strictly assigned by bus route?
- **P1.2** What before- and after-school care options exist at the Conway elementary schools for parents whose shifts start at 7:00 AM?
- **P1.3** How do free and reduced-price lunch applications transfer between Albany residency and Conway schools, and does our town's high poverty rate bring extra Title I support to my kids' classrooms?
