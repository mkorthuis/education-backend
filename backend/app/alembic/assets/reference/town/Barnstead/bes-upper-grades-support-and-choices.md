# Barnstead Elementary's Upper Grades, Support and Choices: Math Results, Staff Turnover, Special Education and Behavior, Bullying and Rules, Counseling, Advanced Learners, Activities, Home Education and EFAs, and the Building Project

Part of the Barnstead research (town/Barnstead). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Barnstead Elementary School's (BES, preschool to grade 8) middle grades and the help around them: math results and what the district is doing about them; how many teachers, paraeducators and counselors stay; special education, behavior support and where to take an IEP disagreement; bullying and the rules for 7th and 8th graders; counseling, social work and trauma training; acceleration, algebra and the BRIDGE enrichment program; sports, clubs, music and drama; access for home-educated and EFA students and EFA use in town; and what the BES expansion and renovation adds.

**Sources:** the BES 2026–27 Student/Parent Handbook (main session browser copy); the Barnstead School District annual reports for 2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24 and 2024-25 (staff rosters, administrators' reports, the 2026 warrant); Barnstead School Board minutes from June 2025 to October 2026; 2026 principal, special education, personnel, curriculum and buildings reports to the board; 16 Barnstead board policies; the June 2024 special meeting warrant; NH DOE's February 2025 school building aid handout to the House Finance Committee; federal Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) rows for BES (2017-18 to 2023-24); the CSF NH EFA feeds already in the library; Prospect Mountain High School's 2026–27 Program of Studies; and read-only queries of the site's database. The SAU website (pmsau.nh.gov) shows scripts a bot check; its posted PDFs were downloaded from their own file addresses (see raw/middle/SOURCES.txt).

**Counts:** 11 topics, 77 facts, 2 tables.

**Main findings:**
- **Math is low and the district says so.** In spring 2026, 13% of BES students were proficient in math (state 44%), with 97% tested; the district's own report shows 60% below proficient. BES is designated ATSI because student groups fall below the state's bar. The district adopted OpenUp math (grades 5–12) and OpenSciEd for 2026-27, gives math the intervention blocks, and uses STAR tests three times a year.
- **Turnover is real at every level.** CRDC shows 18 of 38 BES teachers in 2023-24 had also taught there the year before (47.4%, against 83.8% statewide; 80.9% in 2017-18). Of the 20 paraeducators and behavior technicians on the 2024-25 roster, 11 are on the 2026-27 list. None of the 2026-27 counselors or social worker appears by name on the 2024-25 roster. The principal, special education director, superintendent and assistant principal all changed between 2024 and 2026, and paraeducator posts were still open in fall 2026.
- **Behavior support was rebuilt for 2026-27:** a full-time behavior specialist (paid from a special education teacher post the district couldn't fill), two more behavior technicians, a crisis interventionist, Capturing Kids' Hearts training for nearly all staff (June 2026), and written levels for physical contact. BES had 94 students in special education in September 2026; in 2023-24, 26.4% of its students had IEPs (state 17.8%).
- **Bullying policy predates HB 131.** The JICK policy the board reviewed on September 23, 2026 (adopted 2021) keeps the "imbalance of power" wording the 2026 law removed; boards have until about February 1, 2027 to adopt a new one. The assistant principal's February 2026 report said bullying and harassment incidents were declining.
- **The building project is real and under way:** a $25.8 million expansion and renovation (55% state aid) begun in June 2025, about two-thirds done in June 2026, adding a gym, locker rooms, science labs, a STEM lab, art, music and health rooms. The state's project description says BES lacked the space for "an approved middle school program." No source names which rooms grades 5–8 will use.
