# Barnstead Residents' Questions

The questions the Barnstead research is meant to answer. Twenty resident profiles, three questions each, numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. Received November 21, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Barnstead is SAU 86. The Barnstead School District runs Barnstead Elementary School (BES), and its high school students attend Prospect Mountain High School in Alton, run jointly by the Alton and Barnstead school boards. Prospect Mountain is researched in `../Alton/`; the files here add Barnstead's side. Several questions contain premises (the BES expansion and renovation, 55% state building aid, open enrollment at Prospect Mountain, rough math scores, high staff turnover, sharing a superintendent with Alton, spending below the state average) that the research should check rather than assume.
