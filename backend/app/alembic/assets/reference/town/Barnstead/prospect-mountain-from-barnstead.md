# Prospect Mountain From Barnstead

Part of the Barnstead research (town/Barnstead). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Prospect Mountain High School (PMHS, site school id 28215, run by the Prospect Mountain JMA, site district id 970) from a Barnstead family's side: the Barnstead high school buses and ride times, getting home after practice, the career center in Wolfeboro for carpentry and welding, co-op sports, moving from home education into PMHS, honors levels, music, drama and robotics, internships and ELOs, counseling for college, class sizes and open enrollment, adult classes near Barnstead and how freshmen start. It builds on the Alton research (`town/Alton/prospect-mountain-and-cte.md`), which already covers PMHS's AP and college-credit courses, its LRTC program list, counselors, clubs, schedule and graduates' plans; those facts are cited in the question table rather than repeated.

- **10 fact topics, 62 facts and 5 number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/high/` (about 13 MB): JMA board minutes (September 2025 to June 2026) and September 2026 board packets (athletics report, participation analysis, principal's and superintendent's reports, Dean of Students presentation), the 2026-27 Prospect Mountain Athletics Handbook, three Barnstead School Board minutes, LRTC's 2026-27 student handbook and program pages, NHIAA 2026-27 alignments, and OpenStreetMap/OSRM route files. Facts also cite the browser-pass copies in `town/Alton/raw/high/browser/` (PMHS handbook, Program of Studies, Barnstead bus routes), Alton's `raw/money/` copies (PMHS policy manual, budgets, JMA minutes) and this town's `raw/money/` and `raw/elementary/` copies (Barnstead budget-cut letter and minutes, annual reports, 2025 Master Plan).
- **Places:** 49 facts use `district Prospect Mountain JMA`, 9 `district Barnstead` and 4 `town Barnstead`. No state rules were written; the ones that apply (busing, co-op teams, home education, dual enrollment, adult diplomas) are named from the library and earlier towns.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Barnstead nearly dropped high school busing for 2026-27.** After the March 2026 district meeting cut $447,052, the board planned to reduce or eliminate it (over $100,000); it ended up running two routes. Both stop only at intersections and at Barnstead Elementary, and reach PMHS at 7:35 and 7:40 (about 48 minutes from the first stop). There is no late bus, and athletes must find their own ride home after practices (district-transportation 1–7).
  - **Hockey is already a co-op.** NHIAA's 2026-27 alignment lists boys "Kingswood-Prospect Mtn." and girls "Kingswood-Prospect Mtn.-Plymouth" teams. PMHS fields no field hockey, lacrosse, gymnastics, Nordic skiing or swimming team (district-pe-outdoor-and-athletics 1–4).
  - **Carpentry is LRTC's Construction Trades program; welding has no program of its own.** Welding is taught in Collision Technology and introduced in Automotive. LRTC's A block starts at 7:25, before Barnstead's buses reach PMHS; the LRTC bus times aren't published (cte-center-programs 2–7).
  - **Honors levels:** 52 course entries carry an Honors, Early College or AP option for grades 11–12, but English 12 and the regular U.S. History course have none. In 2025-26, 12% to 29% of freshmen earned honors distinction, depending on the subject (district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners 1–5, table).
  - **Open enrollment and class size:** enrollment was 427 in September 2026, up from 387 in 2022-23 but still below 500 in 2015-16. Open enrollment seats were capped at 60 for 2026-27, and 48 were filled. Board policy caps classes at 30 (district-classrooms-and-staff 1–4).
  - **No cuts to drama, music or robotics stipends were found.** The co-curricular budget held at about $71,000–$73,000 from FY2025 to FY2027; the drama stipends were restructured lower and robotics kept its $6,600 advisor. The cut in these years was two teaching positions when enrollment fell below 400 (district-enrichment-and-advanced-learners 7–10, table).
