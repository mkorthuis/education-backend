# Barnstead: Money, Taxes and Governance (Barnstead School District, SAU 86, and Its Share of Prospect Mountain)

Part of the Barnstead research (town/Barnstead). Gathered November 21, 2026.

## Summary

**What's covered:** 9 topics, 47 facts and 5 number tables on the Barnstead School District (SAU 86) and its side of the Prospect Mountain JMA. They cover the Barnstead Elementary School (BES) addition and renovation (cost, state building aid, the two 20-year bonds, construction status and the tax effect); the 2026-27 (FY2027) budget, the $447,052 cut voters made in March 2026 and how the board absorbed it; the budget by function (table); special education and health insurance costs; Barnstead's growing share of Prospect Mountain's costs and why it moves (two tables); open enrollment money; teacher pay and turnover as the district reports them (inflation-adjusted table); sharing a superintendent with Alton and the SAU merger; the 2023 revaluation; how much of the budget comes from local taxes against state money; the HB 1300 tax cap; the move to SB2 ballot voting in 2027; and why BES is a single K–8 school.

Alton's research (`town/Alton/money-and-governance.md`) already covers the Prospect Mountain budget, its contracts, the enrollment-based split and its history, the 50/50 capital split, the shared central office and its one-third cost split, the SAU merger plan and committee, and open enrollment billing. This file cites those facts and adds Barnstead's own figures and documents.

- **BES project:** $25,805,258 gross, with $12,849,760 of state aid (55% of $23,363,200 eligible cost) and a $11,820,700 20-year bond (2025 Series A). The owner's project manager reported it on time, on budget and about 66% complete on June 24, 2026; the district expects the whole school done in 2027.
- **Tax effect:** budgeted debt service rose from $158,146 (2025-26) to $1,279,751 (2026-27), an increase of about $1.03 per $1,000 of assessed value, or about $413 a year on a $400,000 assessment (**derived**). The addition bond's payments fall each year to $614,935.50 in 2044-45.
- **FY2027 budget:** $20,109,458 proposed; the March 21, 2026 annual meeting cut it by $447,052 (2.2%). The board cut high school busing (later two buses were planned), a dean of students, a second teaching position and support positions. Debt service (+$1,121,605), the Prospect Mountain share (+$506,632) and transportation (+$474,716) drove most of the 11.9% rise in budgeted spending (**derived**).
- **Prospect Mountain share:** Barnstead's share went from 48.36% (2024-25) to 54.45% (2026-27) because the split follows students, and its dollar share rose 30.6% in two years while Alton's rose 2.3% (**derived**). Alton's 2027-28 draft uses 48.53% for Alton, so Barnstead's share would fall to 51.47% (**derived**).
- **Governance:** voters adopted SB2 ballot voting from 2027 (a 2024 attempt failed 32–113). The district pays one-third of the shared superintendent's salary.
