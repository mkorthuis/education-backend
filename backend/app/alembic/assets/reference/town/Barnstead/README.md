# Barnstead: Research for Ask NH Education Facts

Cited facts and figures for answering Barnstead, NH residents' questions on the Ask page (nhfacts.com/ask). The questions are 20 resident profiles with 3 questions each, numbered P1.1 to P20.3 in `questions.md`. The people are invented. The research is in the four files below, in the reference library's format.

Gathered November 21, 2026, in the third wave of the second ToLoad batch. Barnstead is SAU 86, part of the Prospect Mountain SAU with Alton (SAU 72) and the Prospect Mountain JMA (SAU 301):
- **The Barnstead School District** runs Barnstead Elementary School (BES), preschool to grade 8.
- **Prospect Mountain High School**, in Alton, is run by a joint board of the Alton and Barnstead school boards. Its students take career programs at the Lakes Region Technology Center (LRTC) in Wolfeboro.

Barnstead decided its budget at a traditional meeting until 2026 and votes by official ballot (SB2) from 2027. Prospect Mountain was researched first in `../Alton/`; these files cite Alton's facts and add Barnstead's side.

## Library notes (for review, not applied)

- **`kindergarten-entry-cutoff` 4** says "before September 30" (from the registration page); the 2026-27 handbook says "on or before".
- **`budget-form-and-vote-dates` 7 (Barnstead)** describes a traditional meeting. Voters adopted SB2 in March 2026, starting in 2027.
- **`high-school-tuition-options` 8** calls Barnstead's 54.45% a share "of new high school contract costs". It is that year's enrollment share of all of Prospect Mountain's operating costs.
