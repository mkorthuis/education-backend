# Barnstead Elementary's Early Grades and Young Families

Part of the Barnstead research (town/Barnstead). Gathered 2026-11-21.

## Summary

Covers Barnstead Elementary School (BES, preschool to grade 8) from a young family's side: the 2026–27 bus routes and times (including the streets near Locke Lake), the FirstView bus app, storm closings and how families hear of them, drop-off and pick-up, the school day and calendar, the after-school program's history (the Barnstead Adventure Zone), the integrated preschool and who gets a seat, full-day kindergarten and the entry age, health papers needed to enroll, Title I and reading and math help, the district's move to the science of reading, take-home devices and the phone ban, meal prices and free or reduced-price meals, the apps and notices families use, the town's Parks and Recreation programs, the town's roads, winter plowing and gravel bus routes, and whether BES classes stay together through grade 8.

13 topics, 58 facts and 2 number tables (BES bus ride times, 2026–27; BES class sizes followed from their first year in the site's data to grade 8). Main sources: the BES Student/Parent Handbook 2026–27 (board-approved 8/19/26; the main session's copy in `raw/browser/`), the 2026–27 BES bus route sheets (`raw/browser/`), the 2026–27 BES calendar, the Barnstead School District's annual reports for 2021–22, 2023–24 and 2024–25, the Town of Barnstead's 2025 Annual Report, its 2025 Master Plan (approved March 5, 2026) and its Highway Department responsibilities sheet, the Barnstead Parks & Rec website, NH DOE's September 2026 list of 21st Century Community Learning Centers (from the library), and the site's own enrollment data.

| P5.3 | A 2nd grader who falls behind in reading may get extra help during the day | Yes. | P13.1 | Kindergarten may be full-day; the cutoff is unknown | Full-day: confirmed by the handbook (kindergarten-day-length 1). Cutoff: age five "on or before September 30" in the handbook, but "before September 30th" on the registration page (kindergarten-entry-cutoff 1). |
| P19.2 | Buses may not come down narrow dirt camp roads in winter | 51 of Barnstead's 91 miles of road were gravel in 2019, and 48.9 of 78.1 miles were unpaved in the 2026 Master Plan (town-streets-and-safe-routes 1, 3). The route sheets show a bus on "Beauty Hill Road (dirt)" and many stops at side-road corners (district-transportation 5). Whether buses enter private camp roads isn't stated. State law lets a board require a walk of up to one mile to a stop (Jackson `district-transportation` 16). |
