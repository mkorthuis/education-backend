## What you need to know about Manchester

- **Manchester** is the state's largest city, in Hillsborough County. Site town id 335. The city is governed by a mayor and the Board of Mayor and Aldermen under a city charter with a tax cap; the school budget is adopted by the **Board of School Committee**, and there is no voter budget vote.
- **Manchester School District** (site district id 335) is its own SAU, **SAU 37** (https://www.mansd.org/). Use `district Manchester` for SAU-level facts too.
- **Its schools** (site school ids):
  - **Elementary, kindergarten to grade 4, most with preschool:** Bakersville (21545), Beech Street (21600, K–4), Gossler Park (21530), Green Acres (21610), Highland-Goffes Falls (21560), Jewett (21590), McDonough (21565), Northwest Elementary (26435, K–4), Parker-Varney (21540), Smyth Road (21605), Webster (21635), Weston (21515).
  - **Middle, grades 5–8:** Hillside (21520), Henry J. McLaughlin Jr. (27595), Middle School at Parkside (21525), Southside (21575). The site's 2024-25 counts show grade 5 in the middle schools and the elementary schools ending at grade 4.
  - **High, grades 9–12:** Central (21510), Memorial (21505), West (21555), and the Manchester School of Technology (MST) high school (29085), the city's career and technical center.
  - **Not current:** Hallsville School (21625) and Henry Wilson Elementary School (21640) aren't on NH DOE's current school list. Questions P4, P22 and P23 still name them.
- **Charter schools in the city**, each its own district record in the site's data (use these exact names for places): Mill Falls Montessori Chartered Public School (K–6), Polaris Charter School (K–5), Kreiva Academy Public Charter School (6–12), The Founders Academy Charter School (5–12), Spark Academy of Advanced Technologies (9–12) and NH Career Academy Chartered Public School.
- **Private schools named in the questions:** The Derryfield School (6–12, operator `The Derryfield School`); Trinity High School, Mount St. Mary Academy and Cardinal Lacroix Academy (operator `Diocese of Manchester`). Other private schools in the city include Holy Family Academy, St. Joseph Junior High, St. Benedict Academy, St. Catherine School, Mount Zion Christian Schools and North End Montessori.
- **Places students come from:** `places.yaml` lists Hooksett sending grades 9–12 to Manchester under a tuition contract, July 2019 to June 2029. The library has Hooksett designating Pinkerton Academy as its school of record; check how the two fit if a question needs it (P25.1).
- **Site figures that frame the questions:**
  - Enrollment: 13,526 in 2015-16, down to 11,463 in 2023-24 and 11,865 in 2024-25 (NH DOE October counts).
  - Cost per pupil, FY2025: Manchester $18,020; state $22,699; Goffstown $17,811; Nashua $18,376; Bedford $19,650; Londonderry $21,196; Concord $24,351.
  - Licensed child care in the city: 47 programs with 4,116 places (NH DHHS, November 13, 2026), in the site's child care data.
