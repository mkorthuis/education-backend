# Manchester Middle and High Schools

Part of the Manchester research (town/Manchester). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers Manchester's four middle schools (grades 5–8) and its three comprehensive high schools (Central, Memorial and West): class sizes and staffing, advanced math, bullying and conduct rules, clubs and STEM activities, safety at Central, AP, honors and college credit, West's STEAM program and size, counseling and college-application help, VLACS credit, the phone policy and long-term substitutes. MST's own programs are in `mst-and-pathways.md`.

- **10 topics, 68 facts, no number tables.** 8 topics use shared slugs with `place: district Manchester`; 2 add state-rule facts (no place) to existing library topics (`school-staffing-and-class-size`, `graduation-requirements`).
- **Main sources:** the district's 2026-27 High School Handbook, 2026-27 Middle School Handbook and 2026-27 Middle School Course Description Guide (all in the district's public Google Drive), the district's 2023 course-catalog FAQ, Central's 2025-26 school goals and 2022-23 school profile, Memorial's Class of 2027 Common App and Xello sheet and its counseling pages, the 2022–24 teachers' contract (NCTQ's copy), VLACS's pages, UNH Manchester's Tech Camp page, NH rules Ed 306 and Ed 503, and the site's own enrollment data. Raw copies are in `raw/secondary/` (about 18 MB).
- **The district's website could not be read.** www.mansd.org and every school subdomain answer scripts with a "Client Challenge" bot check, and the pages are built by JavaScript. The current district policies (the phone policy Students 166 and the bullying policy Students 526), the 2025-26 and 2026-27 high school course catalogs, West's program pages and the schools' club lists all sit there. The handbooks, the course guide and several Google Docs and Sheets that the schools link were reachable through Google Drive and Docs, and the schools' older Google Sites (2022-23) are still online.
- **News is the only source** for five facts: the 2025-26 opening vacancies (one at Central), the August 2025 phone-policy vote, two budget statements about class size, and the May 2026 vote to avoid staff cuts. Each says so in its note.
- **New data concepts:** middle school math acceleration (Pre-Algebra in grade 7, Algebra 1 for high school credit in grade 8); the 2023 high school "de-leveling" into three course levels; the teachers' contract rules on a sixth class and on long-term substitutes; the state's 20-day limit for an uncredentialed substitute in one position; Memorial's transcript facts that seniors give colleges (unweighted 4.0 GPA, exact weighted rank, class size); the district's bell-to-bell phone rules and penalties; the bullying levels in the code of conduct.
