# Manchester's Charter Schools: Admission, Buses, Special Education, Buildings and Rules

Part of the Manchester research (town/Manchester). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers four public charter schools in Manchester that residents asked about: Mill Falls Montessori Chartered Public School (K–6), Polaris Charter School (K–5), Kreiva Academy Public Charter School (6–12) and The Founders Academy Charter School (5–12). It gathers each school's own admission, lottery, sibling and waitlist rules and its latest published numbers; how Manchester School District buses carry Manchester-resident charter students, and the free city-bus rides for district high school students; hours and before- and after-school care; how special education works when the resident district stays responsible; recess, gym and sports; lunch; buildings; high school graduation requirements; and the state rules on testing, graduation and teacher certification that apply to charter schools.

- **11 topics (10 shared local topics plus the library's `charter-schools` rule topic), 84 facts and no number tables.** Raw copies are in `raw/charters/` (about 28 MB): each school's pages, handbooks, policies, programs of studies, Mill Falls' 2025 annual report to NH DOE, 2026 board minutes of Mill Falls and Polaris (`raw/charters/minutes/`), the district's transportation pages, the Manchester Transit Authority's fare pages, RSA 194-B and RSA 189:11 (`raw/charters/rules/`), and a copy of the NHIAA eligibility by-law.
- **Places:** each school's own district name for its facts; `district Manchester` for the district's buses; `town Manchester` for the Manchester Transit Authority; no place for state rules, which go under the library's `charter-schools` topic with its own kind, question type and description.
- **No earlier town file covers these schools**, so no question was answered wholly by earlier files. State rules already in the library (`charter-schools`, `school-transportation-rules`, `graduation-requirements`, `choice-charter-district-special-education`, `kindergarten-entry-cutoff`, `kindergarten-registration`) are named, not repeated.
- **Main findings:**
  - **Mill Falls draws one number per family.** All of a family's children in the lottery are drawn together and admitted grade by grade as seats allow. Siblings of enrolled students go on a priority sibling wait list. Its waitlist carries over from year to year and stood at 385 for 2025-26. The 2026 lottery had 130 applicants for a school of 168, full again in September 2026.
  - **Polaris's waitlist lasts one year and never closes.** It always takes waitlist applications. Its 2026 lottery was April 3. In September 2026 it had 122 students and was still filling open places.
  - **Kreiva admits first come, first served** (no lottery while it has room; space in grades 6 and 11 for 2026-27). **Founders runs a lottery** (2027-28 deadline March 12, 2027) and had waitlists in grades 6–12 for 2026-27.
  - **Manchester buses its own residents to all four schools.** It uses separate charter routes and transfer shuttles through Cardinal Lacroix and the high schools. Mill Falls' board heard of "chaos around the bussing" in September 2026, and Kreiva families saw morning bus changes in October 2026.
  - **District high school students ride MTA buses free.** No source says whether Kreiva's high schoolers qualify. The regular adult fare is $2.00, or $60 a month.
  - **Special education stays with the resident district** (Manchester for city children). Mill Falls bills districts for services its own staff give; Polaris relies on district staff or providers, at Polaris or at the district school.
  - **Graduation and testing.** Charters must give the state tests and the civics naturalization test, but set their own graduation requirements: 21.5 credits at Kreiva and 26 at Founders, against the state minimum of 20. At least half of a charter's teachers must be certified or have three years' experience.
  - **Buildings.** Mill Falls leases a building it has outgrown. Polaris bought its building (celebrated 2022). Kreiva leases a 55,000-square-foot building with a gym and is raising $5 million to buy it. Founders bought its building near the airport in 2014. Families pack lunch at Mill Falls (no cafeteria) and Polaris (no meal program); Kreiva has no lunch program and relies on donated food.
- **Premises checked:**
  - P31.1 "Kreiva … right downtown on Pine Street": correct, 470 Pine Street. "Without a traditional school yard": its building has a gymnasium, which hosts sporting events, and an auditorium. No source describes an outdoor yard or outdoor time.
  - P10.3 "Polaris … right here on the West Side": the address, 100 Coolidge Avenue (ZIP 03102), is confirmed. No source read names the neighborhood, so "West Side" is neither confirmed nor disputed.
  - P8.2 "charter schools don't have the same city bus routes": partly. State law says Manchester must bus its resident charter students on the same terms and regular routes. In practice the district runs separate charter routes and shuttles, and only Manchester residents ride.
  - P31.3 "If Kreiva doesn't have a school bus": Kreiva has no buses of its own, but Manchester-resident students ride district buses and shuttles (2022 schedules; October 2026 changes to the morning bus).
  - P8.1 "entered together as a pair": at Mill Falls, yes: one family number draws both twins, then each is admitted only if its grade has space. Its pages don't address the case where one kindergarten seat is left.
  - P30.2 "the Polaris kindergarten waitlist": Polaris keeps a waitlist only for the year applied for, and in fall 2026 it still had openings and was taking applications.
  - P31.2 "exact same … testing and graduation rules": the state tests and the civics test apply to charters too. Graduation credit requirements are set by each charter, not by the district or the state minimum.
  - P13.1, P20.1, P20.2 and P30.1: no other premise problems found. Founders runs through grade 12, and Kreiva and Founders both enroll 8th graders moving into grade 9.
