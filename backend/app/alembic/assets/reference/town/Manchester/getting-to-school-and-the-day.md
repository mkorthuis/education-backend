# Getting to School and the School Day in Manchester

Part of the Manchester research (town/Manchester). Gathered November 15, 2026.

## Summary

This file covers how Manchester students get to school and what surrounds the school day: who gets a bus (the 1.4-mile rule and its exceptions), the district's own bus department since 2024-25, how families hear about delays and closings, start times and the 2026 bell schedule proposal, buses for private and charter school students, buses to daycares and after-school programs, split-week addresses, crossing guards and winter sidewalks, before- and after-school care with 2026-27 prices, and school meal applications.

- **6 fact topics, 41 facts, no number tables.** Places: `district Manchester` for the school district's buses, schedule, programs and meals; `town Manchester` for city sidewalks and snow and for community after-school programs (Boys & Girls Clubs, YMCA, New Morning Schools, Girls Inc.); no place for the state law on private school pupils' transportation (added to the library's existing `school-transportation-rules` topic, rule/rules).
- **New data concepts:** the district's walk radius and its exception rules; 2026 budget options to widen it (1.1/1.5/2.0 miles or 2 miles for all) and the June 2026 decision to keep 1.4 miles; fleet, route, driver and rider counts for the district-run bus department; the reordered bell schedule proposed in 2026 and set aside for 2026-27; after-school providers' 2026-27 hours, weekly fees and which schools they serve or transport from; unpaid meal debt.
- **Main sources:** Board of School Committee minutes (September 9, 2024; May 26, 2026) and budget packets (February 18 and March 25, 2026), read from the district's document host; the district's transportation regulations (Transportation 100R, dated June 2009, the newest copy reachable); the district's older Google Sites pages (transportation, private and charter routes, before- and after-school programs, 21st CCLC), still online but last updated 2021–2023; the district's closings and delays page (saved November 8, 2026 in the library); provider pages (Boys & Girls Clubs of Greater Manchester, Granite YMCA, New Morning Schools, Girls Inc. of New Hampshire); City of Manchester Highway Division pages; RSA 189:9; and news (NHPR, CBS Boston, WZID, the Union Leader via Yahoo) where no primary source could be reached.

The current transportation page, current Policy Transportation 100, food services and meal prices, the 21st Century program page and the 2026-27 handbooks are listed under "Not retrieved" for the main session to open in a browser. The session's web search budget also ran out near the end.
- **Premises checked:**
  - "Hallsville School" (P4): out of date. The Board of School Committee voted in 2021 to close Hallsville Elementary and send neighborhood children to the Jewett Street School; a closing ceremony was held September 25, 2021.
  - "A 5th grader at Wilson" (P22): out of date. Wilson School closed after 2023-24; its students and staff were relocated within the district in summer 2024. Grade 5 is now in the middle schools (site enrollment data).
  - "We live about a mile from school, so we don't get a bus" (P4.1): consistent with the district's rule. Students within 1.4 miles provide their own transportation, and the board kept the 1.4-mile radius for 2026-27 after considering 2 miles.
  - "Elementary and high school start times are staggered" (P16.3): correct. Middle schools start first (7:25 a.m.), high schools at 7:38 and elementary schools last (8:20). A 2026 proposal to put elementary first was not adopted for 2026-27.
  - "Early drop-off at 7:00 AM" (P22.3): no district-run early drop-off was found. Elementary arrival is 8:20; the Boys & Girls Club opens at 6:15 a.m. and transports to Beech Street, and YMCA and New Morning programs open at 7:00 at certain schools.
  - "The city keeps dropping or delaying bus routes for private and charter school kids whenever there's a driver shortage" (P27.1): partly. Buses are run by the school district, not city government. In September 2023 the district suspended charter school routes after a new contractor said it lacked drivers. Since 2024-25 the district has run its own buses; no later suspension of private or charter routes was found in the sources read, though the search could not cover 2024–2026 news fully.
  - "The Boys & Girls Club bus from Northwest" (P11.3): consistent. The Club's 2026-27 registration offers transportation to and from its Union Street Clubhouse, and its before-school program lists Northwest among the schools it drives students to. No sign-up deadline or waitlist is posted.
