# Manchester Residents' Questions

The questions the Manchester research is meant to answer. Forty resident profiles, three questions each (120 in all, twice the usual twenty profiles), numbered P<profile>.<question> so the research files can say which facts answer which question. The profiles came grouped by part of the city; the group headings are kept. Received November 15, 2026. The people are invented profiles, not real residents.

Manchester is its own SAU (SAU 37) and the state's largest district: the Manchester School District enrolled 11,865 students in 2024-25 in 14 elementary schools (kindergarten through grade 4, most with preschool), four middle schools (grades 5–8: Hillside, McLaughlin, Parkside and Southside), three high schools (Central, Memorial and West) and the Manchester School of Technology (MST). The city has public charter schools (Mill Falls Montessori, Polaris, Kreiva Academy, The Founders Academy, Spark Academy and NH Career Academy) and private schools including The Derryfield School, Trinity High School, Mount St. Mary Academy and Cardinal Lacroix Academy. The Board of School Committee adopts the budget, and the Board of Mayor and Aldermen sets the city's appropriation under a charter tax cap. Several questions contain premises (school names and grades, Hallsville and Wilson schools, budget totals and cuts, spending comparisons, a lawsuit, start times and programs) that the research should check rather than assume.
